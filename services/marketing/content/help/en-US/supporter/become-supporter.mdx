An active Supporter subscription gives you a few extras as a thank-you. Everything core stays free.

## What Supporters get

- The Supporter badge.
- Custom accent colors.
- Plan Today's Route, which puts the day's follow-ups and plans in the shortest driving order.
- Alternate app icons on iPhone and iPad.
- Choosing each buddy's color.
- Much higher Scribe AI limits. See [How do Scribe AI limits work?](/help/scribe/scribe-usage).

## Good to know

- Contact filters, sorting, saved views and Buddies stay free for everyone.
- A one-time Tip gives Donor recognition, but it doesn't turn on Supporter features.
- Thank you to everyone who supports WitnessWork. Your support built Cloud Sync and WitnessWork on the web.
