---
id: signInOnComputer
title: How do I sign in on a computer with my phone?
category: account
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Use your phone to approve the computer. This works with an iPhone or an Android phone that's signed in to your WitnessWork account, and it needs the internet on both. Your phone needs WitnessWork 1.47.0 or later.

1. On the computer, open [app.witnesswork.app](https://app.witnesswork.app) and choose Use my phone. The browser shows a QR code.
2. On your phone, open WitnessWork and go to Settings → Account → Sign In on a Computer, then scan the code. Scan it inside WitnessWork; the code doesn't open from your camera app.
3. Check the screen on your phone: it shows the browser, the system, and roughly where the browser is, and a six-digit code. The same code must be showing in the browser. If the phone warns that the browser seems far away, only continue if you're sure it's your own computer.
4. Tap YES, then confirm it's you with Face ID, your fingerprint, or your passcode.

The browser signs in, shows "Approved by" and your phone's name, and offers to add a passkey on this computer so next time you can skip the phone.

Only approve a computer in front of you that shows app.witnesswork.app. If you didn't start this, tap NO. If the browser shows "Approved by" a phone that isn't yours, choose That Wasn't Me to sign out and erase this browser. Then see [What if my phone or computer is lost or stolen?](/help/account/lost-device).

Each code changes about every 60 seconds and works once. If it expires or a different device already scanned it, the browser or phone tells you; if the browser stops offering codes, choose Show a New Code. Only a phone can approve a browser; a signed-in browser can't approve another one. If you don't have your phone, use a passkey or your recovery key instead ([What if I lose access?](/help/account/lost-access)).
