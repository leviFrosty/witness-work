title: How long does a browser stay signed in, and how many can I use?
category: account
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

A browser stays signed in to your account while you use it, so WitnessWork on the web opens straight to your data. Its sign-in ends after 180 days without use, or when you sign out ([Signing out on the web](/help/account/web-sign-out)).

You can sign in up to 3 browsers at once. Browsers have their own limit, separate from your phones. If you sign in a fourth, the browser you used least recently is signed out and shows the sign-in screen; sign in again there to carry on.

To see and sign out your browsers and devices, go to Settings → Account → Signed-In Devices, on a phone or on the web. Browsers are listed by name and system, such as "Chrome on macOS". Tap one to sign it out. A browser you sign out from another device is erased the next time it connects to WitnessWork.

Each new sign-in, on a phone or in a browser, also shows in the bell on Home on your other devices and in Settings → Account → Account Activity, along with passkeys added and browsers approved from a phone. If a sign-in isn't yours, see [What if my phone or computer is lost or stolen?](/help/account/lost-device).
