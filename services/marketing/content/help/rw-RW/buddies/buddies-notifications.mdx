title: Amamenyesha ya Buddies avuga iki, kandi ni nde ushobora kuyabona?
---

Amamenyesha ya Buddies avuga uwakoze iki, akoresheje izina ry'irihimbano wahaye mugenzi niba warariteguye: urugero, “Anna yagutumiye muri Gahunda” hamwe n'umunsi n'igihe byayo, “Joe ntashobora”, “Levi yifuza kwifatanya nawe” cyangwa “Levi yabonye ikimenyetso” hamwe n'izina ry'ikimenyetso. Amenyesha ya Gusubirayo yerekana gusa umunsi n'igihe byayo, ntiyerekana izina rya nyir'inzu, aderesi cyangwa ingingo, naho amenyesha ya Gahunda ntiyerekana umutwe wayo, ahantu cyangwa icyitonderwa. Seriveri ya WitnessWork ntibona na rimwe aya mazina cyangwa amakuru: buri menyesha rigera ubutumwa bwa mugenzi wawe bugihishe, kandi igikoresho cyawe kirabufungura kikandika amenyesha ubwacyo. Ako kanya nyuma yo gutangiza igikoresho bundi bushya, mbere y'uko ugifungura bwa mbere, cyangwa iyo ubutumwa budasomeka, ubona amenyesha rusange nka “Ubutumire bushya” aho kubona izina; fungura inzogera y'amamenyesha yo ku Ahabanza kugira ngo urebe uwabukoze. Niba amamenyesha atigeze yerekana izina ry'umuntu, vugurura WitnessWork. Amamenyesha ashobora kugaragara kuri ecran ifunze; kugira ngo uhishe ibyo avuga kugeza ufunguye, hindura igenamiterere rya ecran ifunze mu igenamiterere ry'amamenyesha ry'igikoresho cyawe. Kugira ngo uhitemo amamenyesha ya Buddies iki gikoresho kibona, koresha Amamenyesha ya Buddies, Imenyesha ryo Saba kwifatanya, na Imenyesha ry'ibimenyetso muri Igenamiterere rya Buddies, cyangwa uzimye Imenyesha ryo Saba kwifatanya kuri mugenzi umwe ku rupapuro rw'amakuru ye. Byose biracyagaragara mu nzogera y'amamenyesha. Kuri Android, amamenyesha ya Buddies akenera serivisi za Google Play. Niba areka kugera, reba ko amamenyesha yemerewe WitnessWork mu igenamiterere ry'igikoresho cyawe, hanyuma ufungure Buddies kugira ngo ivugurure. Niba akomeje kutagera, menyesha ikibazo uciye mu Kigo cy'ubufasha utashyizemo amazina cyangwa andi makuru bwite.
