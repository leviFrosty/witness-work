title: Why are you accepting donations?
category: supporter
---

As a brother in the hall, I consider it a privilege to be able to use my skills to help others in the congregation. Although free to everyone, developing, running, and maintaining this application, unfortunately costs a significant amount of money and time. I view donating as similar to how one might pay for another's gas after going in service with them - it's a loving gesture that goes greatly appreciated.
