A browser stays signed in while you use it, so WitnessWork on the web opens straight to your data. The sign-in ends after 180 days without use, or when you sign out.

You can have up to 3 browsers signed in at once, separate from your phones. If you sign in a fourth, the one you used least recently is signed out.

## See and sign out your browsers

1. Open **Settings → Account → Signed-In Devices**, on a phone or on the web.
2. Tap a browser, such as "Chrome on macOS".
3. Choose **Sign Out**.

A browser you sign out from another device is erased the next time it connects. Anything it hadn't synced is lost. To sign out the browser you're using, see [How do I sign out of WitnessWork on the web?](/help/account/web-sign-out).

## Good to know

- A new sign-in shows in the bell on Home on your other devices, and in **Settings → Account → Account Activity**. If one isn't yours, tap it, sign that device out, and change your account key. See [What should I do if my phone or computer is lost or stolen?](/help/account/lost-device).
- For a few minutes after you sign in, you can add or remove a passkey or view your recovery key without confirming. After that, WitnessWork asks you to use a passkey.
- No passkey on this computer? Choose **Sign In Again** and sign in with your recovery key or your phone. Use the same account. Your data stays in the browser.
- To hide WitnessWork when you step away, turn on App Lock. See [How do I lock WitnessWork?](/help/general/app-lock).
