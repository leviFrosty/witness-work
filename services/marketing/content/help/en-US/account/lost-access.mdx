---
id: lostAccess
title: What if I lose access to my WitnessWork account?
category: account
---

Try these in order. Most people get back in with one of the first three.

## Ways back in

1. **A new phone that already has your key.** An iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Keychain on finds it by itself. On Android, setting up the new phone from your Google backup can bring it back.
2. **A passkey.** Open app.witnesswork.app and choose **Sign in with a passkey**. On a new phone, choose it in setup (**I already use WitnessWork**) or in **Settings → Account → Sign In with a Passkey**. If it can't finish on its own, WitnessWork asks for your other phone or your recovery key.
3. **Another phone that's still signed in.** Show a **Set Up Another Device** code for a new phone, or approve a computer with **Settings → Account → Sign In on a Computer**. A browser can't do either. See [How do I move to a new phone or switch between iPhone and Android?](/help/account/account-handoff) and [How do I sign in on a computer with my phone?](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer).
4. **Your recovery key.** Choose **Use a Recovery Key**. See [How do I use my recovery key on a new phone?](/help/account/account-recovery-key-use).

## If none of these work

Without a recovery key, the account can't be recovered. No one at WitnessWork can read or reset it, and support can't change that. You can start a new account on a new device, but your old records can't be brought back from the server.

## Stay safe next time

- Save your recovery key in a password manager, or print it. See [What is my recovery key, and how do I keep it safe?](/help/account/account-recovery-key).
- Consider saving a passkey too. See [What is a passkey?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
