---
id: stillFree
title: Is WitnessWork still free?
category: supporter
---

Yes, and it always will be. Every core feature stays free for everyone, including time and reports, goals, contacts and return visits, plans, Buddies, and the Apple Watch and Wear OS apps. From version 1.47.0, syncing your devices is included for everyone too, with Cloud Sync, iCloud Sync or Google Drive Sync. Supporter is an optional subscription that helps fund development. As a thank-you, it adds Plan Today's Route, higher Scribe AI limits, and personal touches like accent colors, app icons on iPhone and iPad, and buddy colors. Thank you to everyone who supports WitnessWork: your support built Cloud Sync and WitnessWork on the web.
