---
id: petBuddyRooms
title: Can I see my buddies' pets and their rooms?
category: pet
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Yes. Open Buddies and tap a buddy. If they share their pet with you, their page shows it in its room, the way they decorated it: walls, floor, bed, shelf, lamp, study corner, and the hat their pet is wearing. You can tap and rub their pet just like yours. It's only for fun: nothing you do there reaches your buddy, and it doesn't change their pet's hearts.

A buddy's pet shows when:

- They have a pet and share it with you. They choose this with Pet under How buddies see you in their Buddies Settings, for everyone or just for you.
- Your own pet section is on Home. If you removed it from Home, buddies' pets are hidden from their pages too. To bring it back, tap Edit Home Screen after the last section on Home, then Add Sections, then Pet.

Their room shows only how it looks. Their sign stays blank, because it would show their service time. You never see their hearts or coins, and their pet always looks happy on your screen. If a buddy's app is older, their pet shows in the default room until they update. Changes they make show up the next time WitnessWork refreshes Buddies.

If you share your pet, your buddies see your room the same way. To stop, turn off Pet in Buddies Settings, or for one buddy, open their page, tap Edit Buddy (the pencil at the top), then Sharing & Alerts.
