id: territoriesLimits
title: What are the limits on sharing a territory, and why does it say it's too large to share?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
platforms: [ios, android]
---

"This territory is too large to share." usually means the territory has reached one of these limits:

- **10 people per territory.** That counts you, everyone it's shared with, open invitations, and the people a buddy you handed a street to shared it with. People using a guest link don't count. To make room, open the territory and, under Shared With, tap Stop beside someone who has finished, or beside an invitation nobody accepted.
- **10 open guest links per territory,** and up to 30 new links a day. End links nobody needs anymore: tap the link under Shared With, then End Link Now. Every link ends by itself after 24 hours.
- **A very large territory.** Rarely, a territory with a great deal of history is too large to share at all. It stays on your device as before.

Other limits you might notice:

- **A guest link covers up to 30 streets.** For a territory with more, make a link for the first streets and another for the rest.
- **Invitations last 7 days.** After that, share the territory again.
- **Guest links last 24 hours** and can't be extended. Make a new link if someone is still working.
- **Notes for Scribe AI** can be up to about 8,000 characters or 300 lines. Longer notes are read on your device instead; split them to have Scribe AI read them.

Territories themselves are free, with no limit on how many you add.
