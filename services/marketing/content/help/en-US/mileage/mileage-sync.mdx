---
id: mileageSync
title: Does my mileage sync, and does it work on Android?
category: mileage
---

Mileage works on iOS and Android. Cars, fuels, prices, and trips sync between your devices with Device Sync, which is included for everyone from version 1.47.0 (earlier versions need Supporter access), using the option you choose in Settings → Device Sync: Cloud Sync, which also syncs between iPhone and Android, or iCloud (Google Drive on Android). They're included in WitnessWork Backup (Settings → Account) and in backups from Settings → Backup & Restore on both platforms. Mileage is never shared with Buddies and doesn't appear on your calendar or Service Report.
