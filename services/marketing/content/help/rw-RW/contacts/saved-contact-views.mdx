title: Nshobora kubika umuyunguruzi w'abantu nkawuhindukiraho vuba?
---

Yego, hamwe n'uburyo bubitswe, ikiranga cy'Umuterankunga kuri iOS na Android. Muri Abantu, kanda buto y'umuyunguruzi iruhande rw'ishakisha (cyangwa ••• → Tondeka & Shungura…), hitamo imyunguruzi n'uburyo bwo gutondeka, hanyuma ukande Bika nk'uburyo hejuru, ubuhe izina (kugeza ku nyuguti 30), nka Ibyigisho bya Bibiliya cyangwa Ifasi y'Icyesipanyoli. Uburyo bwawe bugaragara nka buto munsi y'aho gushakisha, hejuru y'uturango tw'igihe cyo gusura bwa nyuma, no hejuru y'ikarita: kanda ubumwe kugira ngo ubugaragaze, hanyuma ububukande nanone kugira ngo usubire ku myunguruzi yawe bwite. Uburyo bukoreshwa ku rutonde no ku ikarita kimwe. Inyandiko zishakishwa ntizibikwa mu buryo. Mu gihe uburyo bugaragara, impinduka ukora muri Tondeka & Shungura cyangwa ukoresheje uturango tw'igihe cyo gusura bwa nyuma zishyirwaho Byahinduwe; kanda Vugurura uburyo kugira ngo uzibike, Bika nk'uburyo bushya kugira ngo ubike byombi, cyangwa Subizaho kugira ngo uzireke. Kugira ngo utondeke uburyo bwawe, kanda ubumwe, utegereze gato, hanyuma ububukurure ku murongo. Kanda ufate uburyo utimuye kugira ngo ubuhindure izina cyangwa ubusibe, cyangwa uhitemo ••• → Hindura uburyo bubitswe kugira ngo uhindure amazina, utondeke kandi usibe uburyo. Gusiba uburyo ntibihindura abantu bawe. Guhindura izina cyangwa kubika mu bubiko umwanya w'umuntu bituma uburyo bukomeza gukora; niba usibye umwanya burundu, imyunguruzi iwuriho irirengagizwa. Uburyo bubikwa ku gikoresho cyawe, bushyirwa muri backup, kandi buhuzwa na iCloud Sync kuri iOS na Google Drive Sync kuri Android; vugurura WitnessWork kuri buri gikoresho kugira ngo ubibone aho. Uburyo bugaragara bukomeza kuba kuri buri gikoresho. Kubaka imyunguruzi utayibitse bikomeza kuba ubuntu. Niba uburenganzira bw'Umuterankunga burangiye, uburyo bwawe buhishwa muri Abantu kandi ntibugikoreshwa, ariko burabikwa kandi bugaruka uburenganzira bugarutse. Niba uburyo bugaragaza abantu utari witeze, fungura Tondeka & Shungura urebe imyunguruzi yabwo, cyangwa ukande Subizaho kugira ngo ukureho impinduka zitabitswe. Ku bindi, vugana n'ubufasha uciye mu Kigo cy'ubufasha kandi udashyiramo amazina, aderesi n'inyandiko.
