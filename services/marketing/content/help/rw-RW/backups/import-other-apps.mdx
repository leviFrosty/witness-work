title: Nshobora kwinjiza amakuru kuri MyTime, Ministry Assistant, cyangwa indi porogaramu?
---

Backup za MyTime zirashyigikiwe. Fungura Igenamiterere → MyTime Import, uhitemo backup y'umwimerere ya .mytimedb iva kuri MyTime, hanyuma usuzume incamake mbere yo kwinjiza. Imiterere ya backup z'izindi porogaramu ntishyigikirwa ubwayo. Niba ushobora kohereza inyandiko isomeka, Kwinjiza inyandiko bishobora gufasha kuyinjiza ukoresheje Scribe AI ku bikoresho bishyigikiwe iyo iboneka. Suzuma inyandiko n'imiburo bitangwa mbere yo kubika. Backup za WitnessWork za JSON zijya muri Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura.
