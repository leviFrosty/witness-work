---
id: planJustHours
title: Can I plan hours without picking a time?
category: plans
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Yes. A new Plan has no set time. Its **Start time** row reads **Anytime**, and you only choose how long.

## Plan a length of time

1. Open the Plan form.
2. Set how long with the **−** and **+** buttons or the quick amounts under the number. Tap the number to type an exact length.

## Add a start time

1. Tap **Start time**.
2. Pick when you start and finish. The hours add up for you.

To take the time off again, edit the Plan, tap **Start time**, and choose **No set time**.

## Good to know

- A Plan with no set time shows **Anytime** on your Schedule and counts toward your goals like any other Plan.
- With **Notify Me** on, it reminds you at 8:00 AM that day.
- Buddies you invite see the day and how long, with no time.
- New Plans open the way you planned last, so your usual way is ready next time.
