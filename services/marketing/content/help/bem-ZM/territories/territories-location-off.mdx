---
id: territoriesLocationOff
title: Mulandu nshi WitnessWork tailasunga apo imiseko ili?
---

WitnessWork isunga apo umuseko uli fye nga ingabomfya location yenu, **Sunga Apo Umuseko Onse Uli** yaiwashiwa pa musebo, elyo muli pa musebo. Ukwabula location, fyonse fimbi fibomba.

## Ukulungika

Moneni limo pa mashiwi ayo mu cilembelo ca miseko nelyo pa mapu ya territory:

- **"Location is off for WitnessWork."** Tinikeni **Isula Ifyo Mwasala** elyo musuminishe location ku WitnessWork. Pa Android akatini kangalanda **Suminisha Location**. Pa web, suminishani icibombelo ici ukubomfya location yenu mu fyo mwasala fya browser.
- **"WitnessWork only has your approximate location."** Tinikeni **Isula Ifyo Mwasala** elyo mwashe precise location.
- **"Location is turned off on this device."** Tinikeni akatini mu cebo ukuti mwashe location pa foni yenu.
- **"You're away from this street, so no pins for now."** Nga mwaba ku miseko, tinikeni icikope ca pin mu cilembelo ca miseko, elyo **Ndi ku Miseko**.

## Imilandu imbi

- **Sunga Apo Umuseko Onse Uli** yashimiwa pa musebo uyu. Washeni switch mu cilembelo ca miseko, nelyo mu **Lungika Umusebo**.
- Territory privacy mode yaishiba. Tasunga ama pin ya miseko. Moneni [Territory privacy mode ni nshi?](/help/territories/territories-privacy-mode)
- Nga takuli cebo, foni yenu kuti tayasanga signal apo muli, nga mukati mu ng'anda.

## Ukubika pin pa museko ne ntambi

1. Isuleni umusebo elyo mutinike ing'anda.
2. Tinikeni **Lundako Pin**.
3. Tinikeni pa mapu apo umuseko uli.

Ici cibomba ukwabula location.
