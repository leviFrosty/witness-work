id: mileageTurnOff
title: Nzimya nte cyangwa nfungura nte kurikirana ibirometero?
---

Koresha akuma Kurikirana ibirometero muri Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Umubwiriza. Kuzimya bihisha ikarita yo ku Ahabanza, igikorwa Andika urugendo, na ecran y'Ibirometero, ariko bigakomeza ingendo zawe; fungura nanone byose bigaruka. Niba warahisemo Oya, murakoze ku ikarita yo ku Ahabanza, aka kuma kayifungura igihe icyo ari cyo cyose. Kugira ngo usibe ibirometero byawe, fungura Ibirometero → Igenamiterere → Siba amakuru yose y'ibirometero. Ibi bisiba ku bikoresho byose bihuza n'iki kandi ntibishobora gusubirwamo. Ushobora kandi guhisha ikarita yo ku Ahabanza gusa ukoresheje Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Ekara y'ahabanza.
