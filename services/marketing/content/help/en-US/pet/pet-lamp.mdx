---
id: petLamp
title: What does the lamp in the room mean?
category: pet
---

From version 1.46.0, the lamp follows your pet's hearts. It burns one level brighter for each whole heart, from unlit below one heart (a sleeping pet is at half a heart) to its brightest at five hearts, and it goes out if your pet died. It's the same whether or not you show your streak, and it burns the same whichever light you picked in the Light tab.

Before version 1.46.0, the lamp was your Service Streak instead. It burned brighter as your streak grew, from unlit at 0 to its brightest once your streak reached 30, or 12 for Kingdom Publishers, whose streak counts months. If you turned off Show streak in Settings → Preferences → Streaks & Badges, it kept a steady medium glow. From 1.46.0, your streak no longer lights the lamp.
