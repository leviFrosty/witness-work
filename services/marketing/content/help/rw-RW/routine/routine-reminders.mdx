title: "WitnessWork yanyibutsa Spiritual Routine yanjye?"
---

Yego. WitnessWork ishobora kohereza inyibutsa imwe ku munsi kuri iPhone na Android. Izimije kugeza uyifunguye.

## Ifungure

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Amamenyesha**.
2. Fungura **Daily Routine Reminder**.
3. Hitamo isaha. Ni saa 7:00 z'umugoroba keretse uyihinduye.
4. Emera amamenyesha niba WitnessWork ibikubajije.

## Izimye

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Amamenyesha**.
2. Zimya **Daily Routine Reminder**.

Kuzimya Spiritual Routine na byo bihagarika inyibutsa.

## Byiza kumenya

- Inyibutsa yerekana gusa ibikiri bifunguye uwo munsi, nka Bible reading na Midweek meeting prep.
- Niba byose byabaruweho cyangwa nta kintu gitegerejwe, nta nyibutsa ica.
- Kanda inyibutsa kugira ngo ufungure WitnessWork ubarureho ibintu.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta nyibutsa ihageze:** amamenyesha ashobora kuba azimije kuri WitnessWork mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe. Yongere uyafungure aho.
