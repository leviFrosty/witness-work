title: "Ibirometero byanjye bihuzwa, kandi bikora kuri Android?"
---

Yego. Ibirometero bikora kuri iPhone na Android, kandi imodoka zawe, lisansi, ibiciro n'ingendo bihuzwa hagati y'ibikoresho byawe iyo guhuza bifunguye.

## Byiza kumenya

- Guhuza birimo kuri bose. Hitamo uburyo muri **Igenamiterere → Sync**: Cloud Sync (ikora kandi hagati ya iPhone, Android na murandasi), cyangwa iCloud (Google Drive kuri Android).
- Ibirometero biri muri Backup ya WitnessWork (**Igenamiterere → Konti**) no muri backup ziva muri **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura**.
- Ibirometero ntibigaragara kuri kalendari yawe cyangwa Raporo y'umurimo.
- Ntibisangizwa na Buddies, keretse imodoka wahisemo gusangiza. Reba [Imodoka zisangiwe ni iki, kandi nasangiza nte imodoka n'umuryango wanjye?](/help/mileage/shared-cars).
- Imodoka isangiwe ivugururwa ubwayo kandi ntiri muri guhuza cyangwa backup. Reba [Imodoka zisangiwe zigaragara ku bindi bikoresho byanjye, ku isaha yanjye no muri backup?](/help/mileage/shared-cars-devices).
