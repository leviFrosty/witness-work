id: territoriesUndoDelete
title: I deleted or returned a territory by mistake. Can I get it back?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

**Returned by mistake:** yes. In the Territories tab, tap Show returned territories, open it, and tap Move Back to Checked Out. The territory, its streets, which streets were worked, and its do-not-calls come back, but its not-at-homes and other house-by-house records were deleted when you returned it and can't be recovered. If it was shared, sharing ended when you returned it; share it again.

**A door or street removed by mistake:** right after you remove a door, tap Undo in the door recorder. A deleted street can't be brought back; add it again.

**A deleted territory:** Delete Territory can't be undone, and it deletes the territory on all your synced devices. Add the territory again from the card. If it was shared, deleting it also stopped sharing it, so share it again too.

**A territory someone shared with you:** if you left it or removed it from your list, ask the owner to share it with you again.
