title: How do I use Scribe AI?
category: scribe
pinned: true
---

On Home, tap the glowing microphone in Quick Actions, or tap + and choose Speak an update. Say what you did or plan to do, like “Two hours this morning, and I talked with John at 42 Oak St, follow up next Tuesday,” then tap Done. To type instead, tap the keyboard at the top of the screen while it listens. You can also scan handwritten notes on iPhone and iPad, or read photos of paper pages; check the text before you send it. Scribe AI shows what it understood as cards: time, plans, and contacts with their visits and follow-ups. Uncheck anything you don’t want, pick the right person when it asks something like “Which Maria?”, and tap Add. Nothing is added until you do. Tap an added contact or plan to open it. Right after, Undo in the message at the bottom of the screen takes it back, and for 30 days you can open the conversation from History (the clock in Scribe AI) and tap Undo there. If Quick Actions isn’t on Home, hold any Home section, tap Edit Home Screen, and add it back.
