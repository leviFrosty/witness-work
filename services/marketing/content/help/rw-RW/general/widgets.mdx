id: widgets
title: "Hari widgets zo kuri ecran y'ahabanza cyangwa yo gufunga?"
---

Yego, kuri iPhone. Fata ku ecran y'ahabanza cyangwa yo gufunga, kanda **+**, ushakishe WitnessWork uhitemo widget.

## Widgets zihari

- **Raporo y'umurimo** (ecran y'ahabanza cyangwa yo gufunga): amasaha y'uku kwezi yerekeza ku ntego yawe n'ayo ukeneye buri munsi kugira ngo uyigereho. Niba utandika amasaha, yerekana ibiganiro n'ibyigisho by'uku kwezi.
- **Kalendari**, **Ibyateganijwe**, **Contacts** na **Inshuti y'itungo**: ecran y'ahabanza.

## Byiza kumenya

- Widgets zijya ku munsi mushya saa sita z'ijoro nubwo utafunguye WitnessWork.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Widget isa n'iyashaje:** fungura WitnessWork.
