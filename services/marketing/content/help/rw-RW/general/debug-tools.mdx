title: "Debugging Tools ikora iki?"
---

Debugging Tools ifasha wowe n'ubufasha gushaka no gukosora ibibazo. Igenewe gukoreshwa igihe ubufasha bubigusabye.

## Ifungure cyangwa uyihagarike

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Ibyerekeye → App Info**.
2. Kanda inshuro 7 nimero ya verisiyo iri munsi y'izina rya porogaramu. Ikimenyetso cyo kwemeza kigaragara iruhande rwa verisiyo igihe ifunguye.

Ikora kuri telefoni wayifunguyeho gusa. Iyo ifunguye, hiyongeraho akabuto ka **Ibikoresho**, ecran zimwe zigaragaza amakuru y'ubuhanga y'inyongera, kandi hakandikwa ibisobanuro birenzeho ku bw'ubufasha. Kuyifungura cyangwa kuyihagarika nta cyo bihindura ku makuru yawe. Kugira ngo uhishe akabuto n'amakuru, zimya akabuto kari hejuru y'akabuto ka **Ibikoresho**, cyangwa wongere ukande verisiyo muri **App Info** inshuro 7.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ibikorwa bimwe muri **Ibikoresho** byongeramo amakuru y'ubugerageza cyangwa bisiba amakuru burundu. Ubikoreshe gusa igihe ubufasha bubigusabye.
- Banza ukore backup muri **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura**.
