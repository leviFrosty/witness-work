title: "Kuki gahunda cyangwa ubutumire bya mugenzi bibura cyangwa bishaje?"
---

Gahunda za mugenzi zishobora kuba zibura kuko ubusabe butaremezwa, gusangiza kwe kuzimije, cyangwa telefoni yawe nta murandasi ifite. Manura kuri **Buddies** kugira ngo uvugurure.

## Ibyo kureba

1. Reba ko ubusabe bwemejwe muri **Buddies → Ibisabwa**.
2. Kuri **Gahunda**, kanda umunsi. Werekana gahunda za buri mugenzi z'uwo munsi.
3. Fungura **Buddies** umanure kugira ngo uvugurure. Mwembi mukeneye murandasi.

## Byiza kumenya

- Utudomo munsi y'iminsi twerekana gusa bagenzi bafite **Erekana kuri kalendari yanjye** ifunguye. Itangirana izimije kuri mugenzi mushya.
- Mugenzi wawe ashobora kuba yarahagaritse gusangiza cyangwa yarahagaritse gusangiza gahunda nawe. WitnessWork ntibikubwira.
- Mugenzi usangiza igice cy'igihe gusa yerekana **Mu gitondo**, **Nyuma ya saa sita**, **Nimugoroba** cyangwa **Byateganyijwe** mu mwanya w'isaha.
- Ubona gahunda z'ibyumweru umunani biri imbere.
- Gahunda wamaze gukurura zigumaho nta murandasi. Impinduka nshya zikeneye murandasi.
- Kubona gahunda za mugenzi ntibizongera mu zawe.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta menyesha ry'ubutumire:** fungura amamenyesha ya Buddies muri WitnessWork no mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe. Inzogera y'Ahabanza ikomeza kwerekana ubutumire. Niba ivuga ko itabashije kureba, subira kuri murandasi ukande **Ongera ugerageze**.
- **Android:** amamenyesha ya Buddies akeneye Google Play services. Nta byo, impinduka zigera ufunguye WitnessWork.
- **Buddies ivuga ko idashobora kwemeza iki gikoresho:** reba [Kuki WitnessWork ivuga ko idashobora kwemeza iki gikoresho?](/help/account/account-verification).
