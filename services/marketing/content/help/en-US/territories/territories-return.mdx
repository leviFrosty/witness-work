title: How do I return a territory, and what happens to its records?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

When you hand the card back, open the territory, tap **Return Territory**, and confirm. The territory moves to your returned territories.

## What happens to its records

- Deleted: the house-by-house records (not-at-homes, **Spoke** results, and door pins).
- Kept: the streets, which streets were worked, and the do-not-calls with their address or pin and date.

If some streets aren't marked worked, the confirmation says how many, so you can mark them first. When every street is worked, the page asks "Is that the whole card?" and offers **Return Territory** or **Add Another Street**.

## Pass the do-not-calls on

1. Open the returned territory.
2. Tap **Copy List** under **Do Not Call**.
3. Paste the list wherever your congregation keeps them.

## Find it again

In the **Territories** tab, tap **Show returned territories** and open one.

- **Move Back to Checked Out** reopens a territory you returned by mistake. Its house-by-house records don't come back.
- **Check Out Again** is for when you're given the territory again. It starts fresh from today. Streets and do-not-calls are kept.

## Good to know

- If it was shared, returning stops sharing for everyone, ends its guest links, and brings handed-off streets back to you. People you shared it with keep a read-only copy until they remove it.
- To remove a territory completely, open it, tap **Edit**, then **Delete Territory**. This deletes its streets, records, and do-not-calls from your phone.
