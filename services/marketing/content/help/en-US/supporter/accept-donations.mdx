WitnessWork is free to everyone, but building, running and maintaining it takes a lot of money and time. As a brother in the hall, I consider it a privilege to use my skills to help others in the congregation. I see donating as similar to paying for another's gas after going in service with them. It's a loving gesture, and it's greatly appreciated.

## What donations are used for

- Real phones and tablets for testing.
- Fees for map and location services.
- Fees for building the app and for the App Store and Google Play.
- The many hours spent developing the app.

Every bit of support helps keep the app running.
