title: "Urufunguzo rwanjye rwo kugarura ni iki, kandi nza kurubika nte?"
---

Urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura ni kode ndende itangira na WW1-. Ni rwo rukugarura muri konti yawe, bityo urubike ahantu hizewe.

## Reba urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti**.
2. Kanda **Erekana urufunguzo rwo kugarura**.
3. Emeza ukoresheje Face ID, Touch ID, kode ya telefoni cyangwa gufunga ecran.
4. Rukopore mu bubiko bw'amajambobanga, cyangwa urucape.

Kuri murandasi, WitnessWork ibanza kugusaba kwemeza ukoresheje passkey. Konti nshya ya murandasi kandi yerekana urufunguzo ako kanya umaze kuyikora, bityo urubike icyo gihe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ufite urufunguzo wese afite konti yawe, harimo na Buddies yawe. Ntuzirwohereze cyangwa ngo urushyire ku mbuga.
- Warutakaje? Ongera ururebe kuri telefoni cyangwa mushakisha iyo ariyo yose ikiri yinjiye.
- Ukeka ko hari undi muntu urufite? Hindura urufunguzo rwa konti yawe. Reba [Nahindura nte urufunguzo rwa konti yanjye?](/help/account/change-account-key).
- Niba utakaje ibikoresho byose byinjiye n'urufunguzo rwawe, ntawe ushobora kugarura konti, harimo natwe. Reba [Nakora iki niba ntagishoboye kwinjira muri konti yanjye ya WitnessWork?](/help/account/lost-access).
- Kugira ngo urukoreshe kuri telefoni nshya, reba [Nakoresha nte urufunguzo rwanjye rwo kugarura kuri telefoni nshya?](/help/account/account-recovery-key-use).
