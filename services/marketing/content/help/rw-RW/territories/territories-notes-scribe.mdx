title: "Scribe AI isoma inyandiko zanjye z'agace, kandi ni iki cyoherezwa?"
---

Yego. Iyo Scribe AI ifunguye kandi ihari, isoma inyandiko wongeraho ukoresheje **Ongeraho ibyavuye mu nyandiko**. Nta kintu cyongerwa kugeza igihe urebye ibyanditswe ugakanda **Ongeraho ibyanditswe**.

## Ibyoherezwa

- Inyandiko zawe, amazina y'imihanda y'agace, n'ururimi rwa porogaramu yawe.
- Ntibyoherezwa: nomero y'agace, "Ntibasurwe", n'izindi nyandiko zako.

Scribe AI ntibika inyandiko zawe kandi ntizikoresha mu kwigisha, kandi WitnessWork ntizibika. Ibika gusa umubare w'ibyo wakoresheje.

## Ibigaruka

Gusa nomero z'amazu, imihanda, n'ibyabaye (**Abatari mu rugo**, **Twaganiriye**, **Ntibasurwe**). Amazina, impamvu, nomero za telefoni, n'andi magambo birasibwa, bityo "Ntibasurwe" ibika aderesi yayo gusa.

## Igihe telefoni yawe isoma inyandiko

Iyo Scribe AI izimye, nta murandasi ihari, igeze ku mupaka, itaboneka, cyangwa inyandiko ari ndende cyane, WitnessWork izisomera kuri telefoni yawe. Ekara ivuga impamvu. Nta kintu kiva kuri telefoni yawe, ariko isobanukirwa gusa ibimenyetso by'Icyongereza bisanzwe. Reba [Kuki Scribe AI itasomye inyandiko zanjye?](/help/territories/territories-notes-trouble)

## Byiza kumenya

- Gusoma inyandiko bibarwa mu mipaka yawe ya Scribe AI, ariko imirongo mike ikoresha bike cyane. Reba [Imipaka ya Scribe AI ikora ite?](/help/scribe/scribe-usage)
- Ku nshuro ya mbere, Scribe AI igusaba kwemera amabwiriza yayo, nk'uko bimeze hose muri porogaramu.
