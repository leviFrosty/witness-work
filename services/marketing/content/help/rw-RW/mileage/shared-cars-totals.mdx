title: "Ibiteranyo bikora bite ku modoka isangiwe, kandi mbona nte ingendo zanjye gusa?"
---

Ibiteranyo Ahabanza, kuri Aho bigeze no mu Birometero bibara ingendo za buri wese ku modoka isangiwe. Kugira ngo ubone ingendo z'umuntu umwe gusa, koresha menu ya **Whose Trips**.

## Reba ingendo zawe gusa

1. Fungura **Ibirometero**.
2. Kanda menu ya **Whose Trips**.
3. Hitamo **Everyone**, **Me**, cyangwa umuntu umwe.

Incamake, igishushanyo, urutonde rw'ingendo, **Sangiza raporo** na dosiye y'urupapuro rw'ibarura byose bikurikiza ihitamo ryawe. Raporo yatoranyijwe ivuga ingendo z'uwo ari we, nka "Logged by: Anna."

## Reba uwanditse urugendo

- Mu rutonde rw'ingendo, urugendo rwanditswe n'undi ruvuga izina rye.
- **Trip Details** yerekana **Logged By**, na **Edited By** iyo undi yarwihinduye.
- Inyandiko yo gusangiza y'urugendo na dosiye y'urupapuro rw'ibarura na byo bivuga uwarwanditse.

## Byiza kumenya

- Iyo abantu babiri bafite izina rimwe, izina ry'imodoka ryongerwaho, nka "Ben · Family Minivan."
- Ibiciro biteganyijwe bikoresha lisansi n'ibiciro by'imodoka ubwayo, bityo buri wese abona igiteganyijwe kimwe.
