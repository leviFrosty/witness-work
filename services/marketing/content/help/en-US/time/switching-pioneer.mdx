Change your status in **Settings → Profile → Status**. Goals and totals adjust to match.

## Switch your status

1. Open **Settings → Profile → Status**.
2. Choose what fits right now: Kingdom Publisher, Regular Auxiliary, Regular Pioneer, Special Pioneer, Circuit Overseer, or Custom.

![Profile settings with the Status menu](switching-pioneer.webp)

Switch back when you're done. Past months keep the time you entered, so your history stays accurate.
