title: Nzimya nte amajwi y'ibyishimo n'ibyibutso?
---

Fungura Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Amajwi n'Ibinyeganyega ukazimya Vuza amajwi. Ibi bigenzura amajwi y'ibyishimo n'ibyibutso WitnessWork ifunguye; ibyibutso biracyagaragara, ariko nta jwi (kuri Android, ni ubutumwa bugufi muri porogaramu). Ibyibutso bigeze porogaramu ifunze bikurikiza igenamiterere ry'amamenyesha ry'igikoresho cyawe; ugahindure ukwabyo nibiba ngombwa.
