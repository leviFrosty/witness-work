title: "Itungo ryanjye rirahuzwa, kandi bagenzi banjye babona iki?"
---

Yego. Itungo ryawe rihuzwa hagati y'ibikoresho byawe, kandi bagenzi babona gusa ubwoko, ibara, izina n'ingofero yaryo.

## Ibihuzwa

Izina ry'itungo ryawe, ingofero, icyumba, ingofero n'ibintu ufite, n'ibyibutso bihuzwa n'uburyo wahisemo muri **Igenamiterere → Guhuza**. Binaboneka muri backup zawe.

Imitima n'ibiceri ntibibikwa. Buri gikoresho kibibarira mu nyandiko zawe, bityo ibikoresho byawe birangana. Niba ibikoresho bibiri bikoresha ibiceri nta murandasi, bishobora kwerekana 0 kugeza ubonye ibindi. Nta kintu waguze gitakara.

## Ibyo bagenzi babona

Bagenzi babona ubwoko, ibara, izina n'ingofero by'itungo ryawe. Nta kindi kintu cy'itungo ryawe, kandi nta nyandiko zawe, zisangizwa.

Kugira ngo uhagarike, zimya **Itungo** mu Igenamiterere rya Buddies. Akabuto kagaragara umaze kugira itungo.
