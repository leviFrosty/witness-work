title: "Nakoresha nte trackpad, mouse cyangwa Apple Pencil kuri iPad?"
---

Huza trackpad cyangwa mouse na iPad yawe, cyangwa ufate Apple Pencil ikoreshwa hejuru gato ya ecran. Utubuto, imirongo n'iminsi ya kalendari bimurika uko indanga ibicaho.

## Ibyo wakora

- Reka indanga ku kabuto kagaragaza igishushanyo gusa, nk'igishushanyo cy'umurongo w'ibumoso wagabanyijwe, ibikoresho by'ikarita cyangwa inzogera, kugira ngo ubone izina ryako.
- Tereka indanga ku mbonerahamwe y'ibikorwa, ku gishushanyo cy'umuvuduko w'Umwaka w'umurimo, cyangwa ku nkingi y'ibirometero kugira ngo ubone itariki n'agaciro byayo.
- Ku ikarita, tereka indanga ku kimenyetso kugira ngo ubone izina ry'umuntu, cyangwa ku muntu uri mu rutonde rw'uruhande kugira ngo ubone ikimenyetso cye.
- Kanda n'intoki ebyiri kugira ngo ufungure menu imwe n'iyo ubonesha gufata.
- Koresha gukurura n'intoki ebyiri kugira ngo wimure ifoto y'umuntu mu gihe uyikata.

## Byiza kumenya

- Byose bikomeza gukora ukoresheje gukoraho.
- Gutereka indanga nta cyo bihindura ku makuru yawe.
- Kuri Android, mouse imurika imirongo kandi yerekana amazina n'ibiri ku bishushanyo, ariko ibimenyetso by'ikarita ntibyerekana amazina.
- iPhone nta gutereka indanga igira.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta kintu gisubiza:** reba ko trackpad, mouse cyangwa Pencil bihujwe, hanyuma uvugurure porogaramu uyifungure nanone.
- **Biracyanze:** koresha ihuza ry'ubufasha ry'ibanga ryo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha, kandi ntushyire amakuru y'abantu muri raporo rusange.
