title: Bushe ama motoka yasabankanywa yaboneka pa fibombelo fyandi fimbi, wachi, na mu backups?
---

Ee. Motoka iyasabankanywa iboneka pa cibombelo cili conse cenu icibomfya Buddies identity imo. Ifyo e nshila na ibwelela pa foni ipya.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ama motoka yasabankanywa yaluka pa lwayo kuli bonse abali pa motoka. Tayaba mu sync (Cloud Sync, iCloud, nelyo Google Drive), WitnessWork Backup, nelyo backups ukufuma ku **Ukusunga & Ukubwekeshepo**.
- Pangeni icibombelo cimbi na icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha nelyo **Pangeni Icibombelo Cimbi**, elyo cilanga ama motoka yenu ayasabankanywa.
- **Lemba Ulwendo** na Siri pa iPhone na Apple Watch ibikako ama motoka yasabankanywa. Siri taiba pa Android, elyo Wear OS tayingalemba ifyalweendo.

## Nga cabipa

- **Icakwishibisha cilanda "A shared car isn't ready on this device yet":** ifyo mwacinja fyashala pa cibombelo ici elyo fisabankanywa ilyo yaba ukuti yalungama.
