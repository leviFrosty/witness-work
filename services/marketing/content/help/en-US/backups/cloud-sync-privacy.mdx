title: Is Cloud Sync more private than iCloud or Google Drive sync?
category: backups
---

They're equally private. With all three, your records and photos are locked on your device before they're uploaded. Only your own devices, signed in to your WitnessWork account, can read them. WitnessWork, Apple and Google can't.

## How they differ

- **Cloud Sync:** your copy is stored on WitnessWork's server. It works on iPhone, Android and the web.
- **iCloud:** your copy is stored in your iCloud Drive. It works between iPhones and iPads.
- **Google Drive:** your copy is stored in a hidden folder in your Google Drive. It works between Android devices.

## Good to know

- The server, Apple or Google only know things like how many devices sync and how big each copy is. They never see what's in it.
- With iCloud or Google Drive, the lock starts once every device you use has the latest WitnessWork. **Settings → Sync** shows **End-to-end encryption: On** when it has.
- If you lose every device and your recovery key, nobody can read your copies, including us.
