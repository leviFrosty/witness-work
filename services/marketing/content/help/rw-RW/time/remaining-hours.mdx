id: remainingHours
title: Ni gute namenya umubare w'amasaha nsigaje muri uku kwezi cyangwa mu mwaka?
---

Open the Progress tab from the bottom bar. Igishushanyo mbonera cy'ukwezi kigaragaza igihe n'amasaha asigaye muri uku kwezi. Abapayiniya barashobora kwimukira kuri sub-screen y'umwaka kugira ngo barebe iterambere ry'intego y'umwaka, igihe kugeza ubu, amasaha asigaye, hamwe n'isesengura rya buri kwezi ugereranije amasaha nyayo na teganyo.
