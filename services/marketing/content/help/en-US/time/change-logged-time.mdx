title: How do I change or delete time I already logged?
category: time
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Open **Add Time**, pick the day, and tap the entry you want to change or delete.

## Change an entry

1. On **Home**, open **Add Time**. You can also use the **+** button.
2. Tap the day in the row of days at the top. To pick another day, tap the date above them.
3. Tap the entry in the list under the days.
4. Change the time, Type or note. To move it, tap another day.
5. Tap **Save**.

To leave an entry without saving, tap it again. If you changed something, WitnessWork asks before it throws the change away.

## Delete an entry

1. Open **Add Time** and pick the day.
2. Touch and hold the entry, then choose **Delete**. On the web, right-click it.

Deleting can't be undone.

## Good to know

- A day with more than three entries shows the first two. Tap **Show more** to see the rest.
- To log time on several days, add the first entry, then tap **Add Another** on the message that confirms it.
- Logging time shows up only if your status logs hours. Kingdom Publishers turn on **Log my hours** in **Settings → Profile** first.
- You can also change an entry from its day. See [How do I log time or plan a specific day?](/help/time/log-time-for-day)
