title: Bushe amakilomita yandi yalalunganikanya, kabili yabomba pa Android?
---

Amakilomita yabomba pa iOS na Android. Motoka, mafuta, imitengo, ne ndwendo shilalunganikanya pakati ka fibombelo fya iOS na iCloud Sync, icibombelo ca Supporter, kabili shaba mu mabackup ya Settings → Ukusunga no Kubwesha pa fibombelo fibili. Pa Android shilalunganikanya pakati ka fibombelo fya Android na Google Drive Sync, na yene icibombelo ca Supporter. Pakati ka iPhone na cibombelo ca Android, bomfyeni backup. Amakilomita tayatuma kuli Buddies kabili tayamoneka pa kalenda yenu nelyo Lipoti ya Mulimo.
