title: Nkurikirana nte ibirometero?
---

Kurikirana ibirometero ni ubushake kandi bihari ku bwoko bwose bw'ababwiriza. Subiza Yego ku ikarita "Gukurikirana ibirometero?" yo ku Ahabanza, cyangwa ufungure Kurikirana ibirometero muri Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Umubwiriza. Hanyuma ongeraho imodoka yawe ya mbere; izina ni ryo ryonyine rikenewe. Andika urugendo ukoresheje Andika urugendo ku ikarita y'Ibirometero yo ku Ahabanza, kuri buto Ongeraho, cyangwa ku ecran y'Ibirometero. Andika intera (fungura Kugenda no kugaruka kugira ngo yikube kabiri) cyangwa uhindukire kuri Igipimo cy'ibirometero wandike ibipimo byo gutangira no kurangira. Igipimo cyo gutangira cyuzuzwa kuva ku gipimo cya nyuma ry'iyo modoka. Ingendo ntizishobora gushyirwaho amatariki yo mu gihe kizaza. Kanda urugendo kugira ngo urebe amakuru yarwo, cyangwa urukande igihe kirekire kugira ngo urihindure, urwandike nanone uyu munsi, urusangize cyangwa urusibe.
