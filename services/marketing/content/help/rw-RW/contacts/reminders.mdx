title: "Inyibutsa za gusubirayo, gahunda, n'umuntu zikora zite?"
---

Fungura **Menyesha njyewe** iyo wongeyeho cyangwa uhinduye gusubirayo cyangwa gahunda, hanyuma uhitemo igihe mbere yo gutangira inyibutsa igomba kuziraho.

## Shyiraho inyibutsa

1. Ongeraho cyangwa uhindure gusubirayo cyangwa gahunda.
2. Fungura **Menyesha njyewe**.
3. Hitamo igihe mbere yo gutangira.

Niba amamenyesha atemewe, kuyifungura bisaba uburenganzira. Niba warigeze kuvuga oya, ifungura igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gahunda isubiramo ikwibutsa igihe kimwe mbere ya buri tariki. Amatariki wasibye, n'amatariki nyuma y'itariki yayo yo kurangira, ntakwibutsa.
- Telefoni itegura inyibutsa za gahunda isubiramo ibyumweru bibiri imbere, bityo fungura WitnessWork nibura buri byumweru bibiri.
- Niba igihe cy'inyibutsa cyamaze kurenga, urupapuro rubivuga kandi nta nyibutsa yoherezwa.
- Kuvanaho umuntu na byo bikwibutsa iyo abonetse bundi bushya.
- Buri nyibutsa inagaragara mu nzogera ya Ahabanza, kandi kuyikanda bifungura gusubirayo, gahunda, cyangwa umuntu.
- Nyuma yo guhuza cyangwa kugarura, fungura WitnessWork rimwe kuri iyo telefoni kugira ngo ishyireho inyibutsa zayo.
- Kuri murandasi, nta mamenyesha yoherezwa. Gahunda na gusubirayo ushyiraho aho bigera ku matelefoni yawe, abakwibutsa umaze gufungura WitnessWork kuri buri imwe.
- Gusiba isura, gahunda, cyangwa umuntu bikuraho inyibutsa zayo.
- Iyo uburyo bwo kurinda amakuru bufunguye, inyibutsa zisiga amazina n'insanganyamatsiko.
- Kuri Android, inyibutsa ishobora gutinda iminota mike kugira ngo ibike umuriro.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Inyibutsa ntizigera:** reba ko amamenyesha yemerewe WitnessWork muri **Igenamiterere → Amamenyesha** no mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe, kandi ko Focus cyangwa Do Not Disturb bitayacecekesha.
- **Kuri Android:** reba ko icyiciro cya **Reminders** gifunguye mu igenamiterere ry'amamenyesha rya WitnessWork.
- **Biracyari ubusa:** kimenyeshe ukoresheje Ikigo cy'Ubufasha, nta mazina cyangwa andi makuru bwite.
