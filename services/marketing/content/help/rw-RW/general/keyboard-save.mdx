title: "Nabika ifishi nkoresheje akayoboro ka keyboard?"
---

Yego. Mu gihe wandika mu ifishi, kanda Command-Return kuri keyboard ya Mac cyangwa iPad, cyangwa Ctrl+Enter kuri Windows, Linux na ChromeOS. Bikora ibyo akabuto k'ingenzi k'ifishi gakora: **Bika**, **Ongera**, **Byarangiye**, **Shyiraho**, **Komeza** cyangwa **Ohereza** muri Scribe AI.

## Byiza kumenya

- Niba akabuto kagaragara kijimye, urugero kubera ko umwanya ngombwa utuzuye, akayoboro nta cyo gakora.
- Mu mwanditsi w'inyandiko, akayoboro gafunga umwanditsi kagakomeza inyandiko yawe, nka **Byarangiye**. Ntikabika gusura, igihe cyanditswe, gahunda cyangwa urugendo biri munsi yawo. Ongera ukande ako kayoboro aho.
- Return yonyine itangiza umurongo mushya.
- Mu rupapuro cyangwa idirishya rito, akayoboro gakanda akabuto k'urwo rupapuro, si ak'ecran iri inyuma.
- Ntikigera gisiba, gukuraho, gusohora cyangwa kugarura ikintu, kandi ntikigera gisubiza icyemezo.
- Kuri iPhone na iPad gakenera keyboard nyayo, indanga iri mu mwanya wo kwandika. Kuri murandasi gakora mu mafishi yose.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta kintu kibaho:** reba ko indanga iri mu mwanya w'ifishi kandi ko akabuto k'ingenzi katijimye. Ushobora buri gihe gukanda akabuto.
- **Biracyanze:** koresha ihuza ry'ubufasha ry'ibanga ryo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha, kandi ntushyire amakuru y'abantu muri raporo rusange.
