title: "Nafungura aderesi isangiwe cyangwa ubutumire bwa Buddies kuri murandasi?"
---

Yego. Niba udafite porogaramu ya WitnessWork kuri icyo gikoresho, paji y'ihuza itanga **Komeza kuri murandasi**. Ifungura WitnessWork kuri [app.witnesswork.app](https://app.witnesswork.app).

## Fungura aderesi isangiwe

1. Kanda ihuza uhitemo **Komeza kuri murandasi**.
2. Injira niba bikubajijwe.
3. WitnessWork iyo ibajije **Injiza aderesi?**, hitamo **OK** kugira ngo uyongere, cyangwa **Bireke** kugira ngo uyireke.

Nta kintu kibikwa kugeza uhisemo.

## Fungura ubutumire bwa Buddies

1. Kanda ihuza uhitemo **Komeza kuri murandasi**.
2. Injira niba bikubajijwe.
3. Reba uwagutumiye, hanyuma wemere.

## Byiza kumenya

- Niba WitnessWork ifunze cyangwa ifunguye kuri indi tab, ifungure cyangwa ubanze uhitemo **Use Here**. Aderesi cyangwa ubutumire bifunguka ako kanya nyuma yaho.
- WitnessWork ibika ihuza utari wafungura igihe cy'isaha imwe. Nyuma yaho, ongera ufungure ihuza.
- Gukora upakira paji cyangwa gusangiza ifoto yayo ntibyerekana aderesi cyangwa ubutumire.
- Ubutumire bwa Buddies bukora rimwe kandi burarangira nyuma y'iminsi irindwi.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ihuza ntirikora:** ryaciwe cyangwa ryahindutse igihe ryakoporowaga. Saba uwarisangije akongera kurisangiza. Reba [ibibazo by'ubutumire bwa Buddies](/help/buddies/buddies-invite-trouble).
