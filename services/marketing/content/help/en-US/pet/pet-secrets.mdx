id: petSecrets
title: What are the secret hats and room items?
category: pet
---

Besides the hats and room items you buy in the Shop (before version 1.46.0, the ones that opened up with your streak), every tab but Window and Sign has secret ones. Until you find one, it shows only as a dark shadow with ??? for its name, and tapping it says only that it's found along the way. From version 1.46.0, it shows only in the Shop, and your pet's details list it once you've found it; before, it showed in the pet's details too, with a question mark. Secrets are never sold, and coins can't get you one. Each opens up on its own milestone as you keep going in the ministry and care for your pet; which one is part of the surprise. None depend on hours, none need a buddy, and none can be missed for good, so you can find any of them later. A few take well over a year of going out; they count the months in which you went out at least one day since you adopted your pet, not months in a row. From version 1.46.0, no secret depends on your Service Streak or on Plans you keep.

The first time you see one after it opens up, it says New in your pet's details; then pick it like any other item. Secrets you've found stay open, sync with your pet like other items, and come back from a backup. If a secret you expected hasn't shown up, open your pet's details again after your records sync; secrets are worked out on each device from what's stored there.
