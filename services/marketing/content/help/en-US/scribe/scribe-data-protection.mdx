---
id: scribeDataProtection
title: Can Scribe AI add contacts in data protection mode?
category: scribe
minAppVersion: "1.46.1"
---

Yes. With data protection mode on (Settings → Preferences → Privacy), tell Scribe AI about someone new as usual, such as "I talked with Daniel at 14 Birch Lane, he wants me to come back Thursday." Their card shows "Daniel agreed I may keep their details" with a switch. Ask the householder first; the info button beside it shows what to say. Turn the switch on once they agree, then tap Add. Until then, Add stays off. If they'd rather you didn't keep their details, uncheck that card and add the rest.

The contact is saved with the date and time they agreed, just as when you add someone in the contact form. Edit the contact to see or withdraw that agreement. If the name matches someone you already have, the card asks which person you mean, and choosing an existing contact doesn't ask again.

In data protection mode, Scribe AI doesn't send your contact list or visit notes, and it doesn't log Not at Home visits, since nobody was there to agree.
