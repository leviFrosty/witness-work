id: followUpReminders
title: Nshobora kubika inyandiko z'gusura nta gutegura gusubirayo cyangwa urwibutso?
---

Yego. Iyo wongeraho cyangwa uhindura gusura, zimya Gusubirayo hanyuma ubike. Gusura n'inyandiko zako bikomeza, ariko uko gusura nta gusubirayo cyangwa urwibutso rwateganyijwe kandi ntikuzagaragara nk'icyatinze. Ushobora kongera gufungura Gusubirayo nyuma ugahitamo itariki n'igihe. Kugira ngo uhagarike urwibutso rwa gusubirayo rwa kera, hindura uko gusura uzimye Gusubirayo.
