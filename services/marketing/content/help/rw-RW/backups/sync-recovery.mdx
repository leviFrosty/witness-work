id: syncRecovery
title: Ni iki kiba ku byo nahisemo mu gutegura, abantu basibwe n'inyibutsa mu gihe cyo guhuza?
---

Gutangira bushya gutegura bigira ingaruka kuri iki gikoresho gusa. Guhitamo Tangira bushya bihagarika guhuza na iCloud kugeza ubyemereye mu Igenamiterere; hanyuma uhitemo Huza byose cyangwa Garura uvuye kuri iCloud bijyanye n'ibyo ushaka. Garura uvuye kuri iCloud isoma amafoto y'amakuru y'ibindi bikoresho kandi igasimbuza amakuru yo kuri iki gikoresho nyuma yo kwemeza. Mu gihe cyo gutegura, igikoresho gisanzwe gifite inyandiko kikubaza mbere yo kuzisimbuza, kandi Abaterankunga bashobora guhitamo Huza. Gumana amakuru y'iki gikoresho bituma ibindi bikoresho byawe bihindura bigakoresha amakuru y'iki gikoresho ubutaha bihuza, bigasimbuza impinduka byakozwe mbere. Kugarura amakuru ntibyohereza mu Buddies kwanga gahunda zitari mu bubiko. Amakuru y'abantu basibwe ashobora kugarurwa abikwa kugeza ku minsi 90, hanyuma agakurwaho hagasigara gusa ibigaragaza ko yasibwe. Inyibutsa zongera kubakwa kuri iki gikoresho nyuma yo guhuza no kugarura, zigumana igihe cyo kwibutswa cyahiswemo kuri buri gusubirayo na gahunda; hakenewe uruhushya rw'amamenyesha kandi hategurwa inyibutsa z'ahazaza gusa. Kugira ngo ugarure, gumana igikoresho gifite inyandiko za vuba kandi ubike ububiko mbere yo gusimbuza amakuru. Koresha ubufasha bwa bwite bwo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha niba ukeneye ubufasha.
