title: Which languages does WitnessWork support, and how do I change or report a translation?
category: general
---

WitnessWork comes in 19 languages. It follows your phone's language unless you pick another.

## Languages

Arabic, Bemba, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Kinyarwanda, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Russian, Spanish, Swahili, Ukrainian, and Vietnamese. If your language isn't on the list, WitnessWork shows English.

## Change the language

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Language** at the top and choose one.

WitnessWork restarts in that language. **Device Default** follows your phone. A language you pick inside WitnessWork wins over your phone's setting.

You can also set a language for WitnessWork alone in your phone's settings. On iPhone and iPad, open **Settings → Apps → WitnessWork → Language**. It shows once your phone has more than one preferred language. On Android 13 or later, open **Settings → Apps → WitnessWork → Language**.

## Good to know

- Some text comes from your phone, so it follows your phone's language: alert buttons, date pickers, share sheets, and the widget gallery.
- On iPhone and iPad, the explanations in permission requests, such as location or camera, are always in English.
- Translations are made with AI, so a word or phrase may be off, especially terms Jehovah's Witnesses use in a particular way.

## Report a translation

Use the email link under **Still need help?** and include your language, the screen, the text you see, and what it should say. A screenshot helps. Cover names, addresses, and notes first. Corrections arrive in a later update.
