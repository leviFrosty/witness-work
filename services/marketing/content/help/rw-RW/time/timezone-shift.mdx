---
id: timezoneShift
title: Will my time entries or plans shift days if I travel between timezones?
---

Oya. Kuva mu ivugurura rya Mata 2026, igihe cyose kwinjira na gahunda bihujwe n'itariki ya kalendari yigenga, bityo kugenda cyangwa kwambuka umupaka w'izuba ntibizamura kwinjiza ku wundi munsi. Older entries created before that update are migrated automatically the first time you open the new version. Niba ubonye inyandiko ku munsi utari wo nyuma yo kuvugurura, nyamuneka wohereze raporo ya bug hanyuma tuzareba.
