Check the message you see below. If the card's code won't scan, you can enter the link or use a different map option instead.

## Scanning the card's QR code

This is in the phone apps only. On the web, paste the link with **Enter Map Link**.

- **"WitnessWork needs the camera to scan a QR code."** Tap **Open Settings**, allow the camera for WitnessWork, and try again.
- **"That QR code has no web link."** The code holds something other than a web address. Use **Pin on a Map** or **Outline the Area**, or take a photo of the card.
- **Nothing happens:** hold the card flat and steady in good light, with the whole code in view. Tap **Stop Scanning** to enter the link instead.

## A map link that isn't working

**Enter Map Link** saves any link you paste. Open it from the territory's **Map** button.

WitnessWork also tries to pin the spot for **Directions**. This works for links to a place in Google Maps, Apple Maps, or OpenStreetMap. A short link, such as maps.app.goo.gl, needs a connection while "Finding the location…" shows.

If no pin appears, the link still opens. Tap **Pin on a Map** and tap where the territory is. A pin you placed yourself isn't moved by a link.

## Scanning notes

On iPhone and iPad, "No writing was found in the scan" means you should scan again in better light with the page flat, or type the notes. On Android, copy the text from a photo with Google Lens, then paste it.
