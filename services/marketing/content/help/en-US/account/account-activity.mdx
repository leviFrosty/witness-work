title: How do I see which devices use my account?
category: account
---

Go to **Settings → Account → Signed-In Devices**. It lists every phone and browser signed in to your account, with when each was last used. Your own is marked **This device**.

## Sign out a device

1. Open **Settings → Account → Signed-In Devices**.
2. Tap the phone or browser you want to remove.
3. Choose **Sign Out**.

## See what happened on your account

Open **Settings → Account → Account Activity**. It shows the last 400 days: sign-ins and sign-outs, passkeys added or removed, browsers approved from a phone, backups downloaded or deleted, and when your account key was changed.

When another device or browser signs in, or a passkey is added, the bell on Home tells you. WitnessWork checks when you open the app and when you open **Settings → Account**.

## Good to know

- A phone renews its sign-in about every three months. That shows in Account Activity as a renewal, not as a new device. A phone you haven't opened for a while may sign in again and look new, so check the device name first.
- WitnessWork doesn't keep where you were or your internet address.
- You need an internet connection to see these screens.

## If you don't recognize a device

1. Sign that device out in **Signed-In Devices**, or remove the passkey in **Settings → Account → Passkeys**.
2. Change your account key. See [How do I change my account key?](/help/account/change-account-key).

A phone that still has your account on it can sign back in, so changing the key matters. For a lost phone, see [What should I do if my phone or computer is lost or stolen?](/help/account/lost-device).
