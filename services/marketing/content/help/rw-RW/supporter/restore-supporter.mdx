title: "Maze kwishyura, ariko ibiranga Umufasha cyangwa ibiciro birabura. Nakora iki?"
---

Uko uri Umufasha kwanditse kuri konti yawe ya WitnessWork. Kugaragara ku gikoresho cyose cyinjiye muri konti imwe, harimo WitnessWork kuri murandasi.

## Ibyo kureba

1. Reba murandasi yawe. Ibiciro n'uko uhagaze bikururwa ubwabyo usubiye kuri murandasi.
2. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti** urebe ko winjiye muri konti wagurishijeho.
3. Reba ko ububiko bwa telefoni yawe bukoresha konti wishyuriyeho.
4. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Ba umufasha** ukande **Garura ibyigeze kugurwa**. Kuri murandasi nta kabuto ka **Garura ibyigeze kugurwa**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Impano imwe (Tip) ntifungura ibiranga Umufasha.
- Niba waraguze winjiye muri indi konti ya WitnessWork, injira muri konti ushaka, hanyuma ukande **Garura ibyigeze kugurwa**.
- Kugarura ibyo waguze bigarura uburenganzira bw'Umufasha, si amakuru y'umurimo wawe.
- Guhuza ibikoresho byawe ntibishingira ku kuba Umufasha.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **"Purchases Aren't Ready Yet":** reba murandasi yawe wongere ugerageze. Ishobora kugaragara akanya gato nyuma y'uko igikoresho cyinjiye muri indi konti.
- **Nta byo waguze bibonetse, ibiciro ntibikururwa, cyangwa biracyatamenyekana:** andikira ubufasha mu ibanga ushyiremo verisiyo ya porogaramu n'ibyabaye.
- Niba ubufasha busaba indangamuntu ya konti yawe, fungura **Igenamiterere → Ibyerekeye → App Info** ukande **Copy All Details**. Yisangize mu ibanga.
