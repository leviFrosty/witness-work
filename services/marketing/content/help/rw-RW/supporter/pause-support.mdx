title: "Nahagarika inkunga yanjye by'igihe aho kuyihagarika burundu?"
---

Yego. Iyo utangiye guhagarika, WitnessWork ibanza kugutanga guhagarika by'igihe.

## Hagarika kuri iPhone cyangwa iPad

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Gucunga ifatabuguzi**.
2. Kanda **Cancel support**.
3. Hitamo guhagarika kwishyura amezi 1, 3 cyangwa 6.

Guhagarika gutangira igihe igihe cyawe cy'ubu kirangiye. Ntiwishyurwa muri icyo gihe, kandi ibiranga Umufasha biguma. Kwishyura bisubukura nyuma ku mubare umwe ku itariki yerekanwe.

## Hagarika kuri Android

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Gucunga ifatabuguzi**.
2. Kanda **Cancel support**.
3. Hitamo **Hagarika by'igihe** muri Google Play, uhitemo amezi 1 kugeza 3. Ibi bikora ku ifatabuguzi rya buri kwezi.

Ibiranga Umufasha na byo birahagarara, hanyuma bigaruka ubwabyo igenamiterere ryawe rikomeje. Ushobora gusubukura hakiri kare muri Google Play.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ushobora guhagarika rimwe mu mezi 12.
- Ushobora gukomeza guhagarika burundu igihe icyo ari cyo cyose mu mafatabuguzi y'ububiko bwawe.
- Guhagarika by'igihe ntibitangwa mu igeragezwa ry'ubuntu, hari ikibazo cy'ubwishyu, ku ifatabuguzi ryo gusangira mu muryango, cyangwa mu mezi 12 nyuma y'igihe cya nyuma wahagaritse.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Guhagarika by'igihe biranga:** nta kintu gihinduka. Ongera ugerageze nyuma, cyangwa ucunge ifatabuguzi ryawe mu bubiko bwawe.
- **Ikindi cyose:** koresha **Uracyakeneye ubufasha?** kugira ngo uvugane n'ubufasha mu ibanga.
