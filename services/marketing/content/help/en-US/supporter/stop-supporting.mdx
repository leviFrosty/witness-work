title: What happens if I stop supporting?
category: supporter
---

You keep Supporter access until the end of the period you've paid for. After that, you keep all your data and syncing, and only the Supporter extras end.

## Stop supporting

1. Open **Settings → Manage subscription**.
2. Tap **Cancel support**.
3. Optionally, answer the short survey about why. It's anonymous and never includes your records.

You can also cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Cancel where you subscribed. If this device can't manage your subscription, use the link in your receipt email.

## What stays

- Your records and local backups.
- Syncing, with Cloud Sync, iCloud Sync or Google Drive Sync, and your photos on Cloud Sync.

## What ends

- Your accent color and app icon go back to the defaults. Your choices are saved for when you support again.
- **Shortest Order** and **Start Route** in Plan Route need Supporter again.
- Scribe AI goes back to the free weekly limit.

If you only need a break, **Cancel support** offers a pause first on iPhone, iPad and Android. See [Can I pause my support instead of cancelling?](/help/supporter/pause-support).

Thank you for supporting WitnessWork.
