---
id: calendarDisconnect
title: Nshi cicitika ilyo nashimya Calendar Sync nelyo nafumya WitnessWork?
---

Mu Settings → Ifyo Mwasala → Calendar Sync, salenipo Shimya Calendar Sync, nelyo Shimya pa cishibisho ca Ukukusha kwa kalenda kwaleka. Pa cibombelo ca ntanshi, icilekusha kalenda yenu, kuti mwasunga ifiputulwa ifyalundwako kale nelyo ukufifumyapo. Fumyapo Ifiputulwa kufumyapo ifiputulwa ifyalembwa ukufuma pa kalenda iyasalwa, pamo ne fyapita ifyatumwa; ifiputulwa ifyashikwatanako na kalenda yene fisala. Ukushimya pa cibombelo ca ntanshi kuleka ukukusha ukufuma pa fibombelo fyonse. Pa cibombelo cimbi conse, Calendar Sync yashimya pa cibombelo ico fye kabili ifiputulwa fisala. Ifiputulwa ifyo musunga, nelyo ifyashala panuma ya kufumya app, tafikushiwa nakabili ukufika mwapanga Calendar Sync nakabili. Ukufumya WitnessWork tekufumyapo ifiputulwa fya kalenda. Kuti mwafumyapo kalenda iyapangilwa iyatabomfiwa mu app yenu ya kalenda panuma ya kumona ukuti tapali icintu mulefwaya ukusunga. Ukushimya Calendar Sync tekufumyapo Abantu benu ba Kulanshanya, Intandalilo, nelyo Ukukonkapo muli WitnessWork.
