title: What is my WitnessWork account?
category: account
---

It's an anonymous account that lets Buddies, Scribe AI, Backup and signing in on other devices work. There's no email, no phone number and no password.

Your phone makes it for you the first time you use one of those features, or when you view your recovery key.

## Where to find it

Open **Settings → Account**. The status there tells you how it's doing:

- **Offline:** this device has no internet. Everything still works, and the status updates when you're back online.
- **Not responding:** WitnessWork couldn't be reached. Wait a few minutes and tap **Try Again**.

## Good to know

- Your service records, contacts and notes stay on your device. If you turn on **Backup**, a copy is kept that only you can read.
- Buddies never sees your name or anything you haven't shared.
- You can also sign in with a passkey, or use your account in a web browser at app.witnesswork.app once it's set up on your phone. See [What is a passkey?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
- Your recovery key is the way back into your account. See [What is my recovery key, and how do I keep it safe?](/help/account/account-recovery-key).
- If a device can't prove it's the real app, see [Why does WitnessWork say it can't verify this device?](/help/account/account-verification).
