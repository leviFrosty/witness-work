title: Territory privacy mode ni nshi, elyo data protection mode icinja cinshi?
---

Territory privacy mode tasunga ifyalembwa fya ng'anda ing'anda. WitnessWork isunga fye imisebo iyabombelwa na Tamutandalile, ne adiresi nelyo pin ne bushiku.

## Ukuwasha nelyo ukushimya

1. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Ifyasuma**.
2. Washeni nelyo mushimye **Territory privacy mode**.

Ukuwasha kufumyapo ifyalembwa fya ng'anda ing'anda ifili kale pa foni yenu, pa numa ya kusumina, na pa fibombelo fyenu fimbi ilyo fyasync. Tamutandalile yashala. Ukushimya tekubwesha ifyalembwa.

## Ico cicinja

- Takuli Not at Home, ifyalembwa fya **Nalanshanya**, nelyo ama pin ya miseko ayasungwa.
- **Lundako Ifyo Mwafikapo Ukufuma ku Malembo** ifisama.
- Peji ya territory ilanda "Territory privacy mode is on · no house-by-house records". **Ifyo Mwasala fya Ifyasuma** isula switch.

## Data protection mode

Territory privacy mode ikonka data protection mode ukufika ilyo mwacinja lwa mwine. Ukuwasha data protection mode mu **Ifyo Mwasala → Ifyasuma** kuwasha na territory privacy mode. Ukusunga ifyalembwa fya ng'anda ing'anda, shimyeni territory privacy mode.

## Territory shasabankanishiwa

- **Nga mwe mwine wa territory,** ico mwasala cibomba kuli bonse abali pa yo, pamo na bamwenyi. Takuli uulemba imiseko. Abantu kuti balemba fye ukuti imisebo yabombelwa no kulundako Tamutandalile.
- **Nga umbi asabankanyeko na imwe,** ico mwasala cibomba fye pa foni yenu. Tamusunga nelyo ukumona imiseko ya bambi, lelo kuti mwalemba ukuti imisebo yabombelwa no kulundako Tamutandalile.
