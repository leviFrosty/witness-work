id: webTabs
title: "Kuki WitnessWork ivuga ko ifunguye kuri indi tab?"
---

WitnessWork kuri murandasi ikora kuri tab imwe ya mushakisha icyarimwe, kugira ngo tab ebyiri zidasimburana amakuru. Tab ya kabiri ivuga **WitnessWork Is Open in Another Tab**.

## Komeza gukorera kuri iyi tab

1. Hitamo **Use Here**.
2. WitnessWork irangiza kubika kuri indi tab, hanyuma igafungurwa hano.

## Subira kuri indi tab

Hindukirira kuri indi tab uhitemo **Use Here** aho.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gufunga tab urimo gukoresha birayigombora. Tab ikurikira ufunguye cyangwa wongeye gupakira ifunguka nk'ibisanzwe.
- Ibi bireba mushakisha imwe gusa. Izindi mushakisha na telefoni yawe ntibyahungabana. Zigumana guhuzwa binyuze kuri Cloud Sync.
