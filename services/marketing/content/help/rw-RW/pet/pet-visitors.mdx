title: "Kuki andi matungo ari mu cyumba cy'itungo ryanjye, kandi nyahisha nte?"
---

Ayo ni amatungo ya bagenzi bawe, asura uwo munsi. Itungo risura iyo nyiraryo ariyisangiza nawe kandi riri mu kintu urimo uyu munsi, nka Gahunda cyangwa Gusubirayo musangiye. Buri tungo risura rimwe maze rikagenda umunsi urangiye.

## Menya itungo ry'nde

- Kanda itungo ryawe. Buri musuye avuga uwo asuranye na we.
- Kanda ufate itungo risura kugira ngo ubone **Reba gahunda** (cyangwa **Reba gusubirayo**) na **Reba mugenzi wawe**.
- Kanda ikarita y'itungo ryawe, hanyuma umurongo wa **Abasuye uyu munsi** ubone urutonde rwose.

## Hisha amatungo asura

1. Kanda ikarita y'itungo ryawe, hanyuma akabuto k'igenamiterere gaherereye hejuru iburyo.
2. Zimya **Amatungo ya bagenzi asura** kugira ngo uyahishe yose, cyangwa uzimye mugenzi umwe.

Ushobora kandi gukanda ufate itungo risura ugahitamo **Hisha**, hanyuma ugakanda **Subiramo** kugira ngo uryagarure. Akabuto kamwe, **Amatungo yabo asure iryanjye**, kari mu Igenamiterere rya Buddies, no munsi ya **Hindura mugenzi** hanyuma **Gusangiza n'imenyesha** kuri mugenzi umwe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Bagenzi ntibabimenyeshwa, kandi bakomeza kubona itungo ryawe niba uriryasangiza.
- Usura yambara ingofero nyiryo yahisemo niba porogaramu ye ivuguruye. Ingofero y'usura ntihindura icyumba cyawe.
- Abasura baba inyuma y'itungo ryawe. Niba itungo ryihishe mu mbaga, koresha urutonde rwa **Abasuye uyu munsi** ahubwo.
