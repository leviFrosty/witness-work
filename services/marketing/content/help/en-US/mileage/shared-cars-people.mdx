Everyone on a shared car can log and change trips. Only the owner, the person who shared the car, can remove people or stop sharing.

## See who is on a car

1. Open **Mileage**, then **Mileage Settings** (the gear).
2. Tap the car.
3. Look under **People**. It lists everyone on the car and its open invitations.

A car holds up to 10 people, counting open invitations.

## Everyone on the car can

- Add, edit, and delete trips, fuel setups, fuels, and prices.
- Rename the car, or archive it, which hides it from new trips for everyone and keeps its trips in reports.
- Invite their own buddies with **Invite Buddy…**. The people on a car don't all need to be each other's buddies.
- Cancel an invitation they sent with **Cancel Invitation**.
- Leave with **Leave Car…**.

## Only the owner can

- Take someone off the car with **Remove from Car…**.
- End sharing for everyone with **Stop Sharing…**.
- Cancel anyone's invitation.

When the owner leaves, the person who joined first becomes the owner.

## Good to know

- A shared car can't be deleted. Archive it for everyone, or leave it yourself. See [What happens when I leave a shared car, I'm removed, or sharing stops?](/help/mileage/shared-cars-leave).
- A shared car has its own fuels, copied from the owner's when it was shared. Changing the price of one of your own fuels doesn't change the shared car's fuel, so update both if you use both.
