title: How do I plan a route with buddies?
category: plans
---

A route's buddies are the buddies on its plan. You invite them to the plan, and they say whether they're going.

## Invite buddies

1. Open the route and tap **Details**.
2. Tap **Invite Buddies**.
3. If the route already has a plan, tap a buddy to invite them. Tap again to take them off, which cancels their invitation.
4. If it has no plan yet, a new plan opens with the route's day, start time, and starting place. Choose who to invite and save.

You can also start from the plan: choose **Plan Route** in its menu.

## Good to know

- Buddies get the plan's date, time, title, place, and note, and any changes you make. Their answers show in **Details**.
- They don't get the route's stops, order, or notes.
- **Invite Buddies** doesn't show for recurring plans or plans you joined through a buddy's invitation.
- If you have no buddies yet, **Invite Buddies** opens Buddies so you can set them up.
