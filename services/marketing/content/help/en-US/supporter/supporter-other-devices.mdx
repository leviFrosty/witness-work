title: Why am I a Supporter on one device but not another?
category: supporter
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Your Supporter status belongs to your WitnessWork account, so every device signed in to the same account shares it, including WitnessWork on the web, whichever device you bought on. Devices that sync through Cloud Sync, iCloud or Google Drive agree on one purchase record by themselves, usually within a minute of syncing.

If one device still shows you as not a Supporter:

- Open WitnessWork on it while it's connected to the internet.
- Check that it's signed in to the same account in **Settings → Account**.
- Check that sync is on in **Settings → Device Sync**.

WitnessWork also checks your status with your account on our server, so your Supporter features can show before the store app catches up. If it still doesn't show after a day, use Restore Purchases on the phone or tablet you bought on (WitnessWork on the web doesn't have it), or contact support.
