category: routine
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"

Only you. What you check off in Spiritual Routine is private, on iPhone and Android.

## Good to know

- It's never shared with your buddies, never shown on your Buddy Card, and never included in your Service Report.
- It has no streaks and no rankings, and it isn't compared with anyone.
- It's kept with your other records, so it's on your other devices and comes back when you restore a backup.
- WitnessWork's usage counts may note how often features are used, but never what you read or your notes. You can turn them off in Settings.
- If you turn Spiritual Routine off, nothing is deleted. Your history returns when you turn it on again.
