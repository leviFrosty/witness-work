title: Nshobora gukoresha Siri cyangwa Shortcuts na WitnessWork?
---

Yego, kuri iPhone na iPad zifite iOS 17 cyangwa irenzeho, no kuri porogaramu ya Apple Watch. Fungura WitnessWork rimwe nyuma yo kuvugurura, hanyuma uvuge "Ongeraho igihe muri WitnessWork" (cyangwa "Andika igihe muri WitnessWork"), "Tangiza igihe cyanjye kibarwa muri WitnessWork", "Hagarika by'agateganyo igihe cyanjye kibarwa muri WitnessWork", "Hagarika igihe cyanjye kibarwa muri WitnessWork", "Andika urugendo muri WitnessWork" cyangwa "Andika urugendo rwo kugenda no kugaruka muri WitnessWork". Siri yumva mu rurimi rwa Siri rwawe, rushobora gutandukana n'urwo wahisemo muri WitnessWork. Siri ibaza igihe kingana iki cyangwa intera ingana iki. Intera ikoresha igipimo cyawe cy'intera y'Ibirometero; ku rugendo rwo kugenda no kugaruka, vuga intera y'inzira imwe kandi irikuba kabiri. Guhagarika igihe kibarwa byongeraho iminota yacyo yuzuye nk'inyandiko y'uyu munsi hanyuma kikongera gutangira kuri zeru; niba kitageze ku munota umwe, nta kintu cyongerwaho. Igihe n'ingendo byongerwaho ku munsi w'uyu munsi, nk'igihe gisanzwe kandi n'imodoka wakoresheje bwa nyuma. Kugira ngo uhitemo Ubwoko cyangwa imodoka, ongeraho igikorwa muri porogaramu ya Shortcuts, aho ushobora no kugikoresha ukoresheje Action Button cyangwa shortcut yo kuri Home Screen. Kongeraho igihe no gukoresha igihe kibarwa bisaba ko wandika amasaha (Ababwiriza b'Ubwami babanza gufungura Andika amasaha yanjye). Kwandika ingendo bisaba ko Kurikirana ibirometero ifunguye kandi ufite nibura imodoka imwe. Kuri iPhone na iPad igihe kibarwa gihinduka ako kanya, naho igihe n'ingendo wongeraho ukoresheje Siri bibikwa ubutaha ufunguye WitnessWork, hanyuma bigera ku bindi bikoresho byawe bicishijwe muri iCloud Sync nk'inyandiko iyo ari yo yose. Ku isaha byoherezwa kuri iPhone yawe nk'izindi nyandiko zo ku isaha. Siri ishobora gukoresha gusa Ubwoko bwawe, amazina y'imodoka, igipimo cy'intera n'igihe kibarwa biva muri WitnessWork, kandi ntishobora gukoresha abantu, inyandiko cyangwa raporo. Siri na Shortcuts ntibiboneka kuri Android. Niba Siri itamenye interuro, fungura WitnessWork rimwe, utegereze umunota, hanyuma urebe ko Siri yemerewe WitnessWork muri Igenamiterere. Ku bindi bibazo, koresha ihuza ry'ubufasha bwihariye ryo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha kandi ntushyire inyandiko zawe muri raporo zishyirwa ahagaragara.
