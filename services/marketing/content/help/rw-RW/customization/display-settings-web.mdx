title: "Nakuza inyandiko cyangwa nkahindura uko bigaragara kuri murandasi nte?"
---

Koresha gukuza kwa mushakisha yawe kugira ngo ukuze cyangwa ugabanye ibiri kuri paji byose.

## Kuza inyandiko

- **Windows cyangwa Linux:** kanda Ctrl hamwe na Plus cyangwa Minus.
- **Mac:** kanda Command hamwe na Plus cyangwa Minus.
- **Telefoni cyangwa tablet:** koresha igenamiterere ry'ingano y'inyandiko cyangwa gukuza paji rya mushakisha yawe.

Gukuza guguma kuri app.witnesswork.app.

## Hitamo urumuri cyangwa umwijima

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Personalization → Itsinda ry'amabara**.
2. Hitamo **Igikoresho**, **Umwijima** cyangwa **Urumuri**.

**Igikoresho** gikurikiza igenamiterere rya mudasobwa yawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ibi bigenamiterere bihindura uko WitnessWork igaragara gusa, ntibigera bihindura amakuru yawe.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Inyandiko iracitse cyangwa biragoye kuyisoma:** bimenyeshe uvuye mu Kigo cy'ubufasha ufite ifoto ya ecran, mushakisha yawe n'urugero rwo gukuza. Banza upfuke amazina n'aderesi.
