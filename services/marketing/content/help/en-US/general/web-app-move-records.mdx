title: How do I bring my records into the WitnessWork web app?
category: general
---

Sign in to the web app with the same account you use in WitnessWork now. Your time, plans, contacts, and notes come over by themselves through Cloud Sync.

## Sign in with your recovery key

This works when the web app is on the same phone as your old WitnessWork, or on a new phone.

1. In the WitnessWork app you use now, open **Settings → Account**, tap **Security**, then **Show Recovery Key**. Copy the key. It starts with WW1-.
2. Open the web app and choose **Use a Recovery Key**.
3. Wait for the security check to pass, then paste or type your key.
4. If it asks to turn on Cloud Sync, choose **Turn On**.

Your records arrive in a minute or two. Keep the old app open and online until they do.

## Other ways to sign in

- **You have another phone with WitnessWork:** choose **Use my phone** and scan the code with that phone. See [sign in with your phone](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer).
- **You saved a passkey:** choose **Sign in with a passkey**.

## Use a backup file instead

If you'd rather not sign in, move a backup file.

1. In the WitnessWork app you use now, open **Settings → Backup & Restore**, tap **Create Backup**, and choose **Password**.
2. Save the file where the web app's phone can open it, such as your Downloads folder.
3. In the web app, choose **Create an Account**.
4. Open **Settings → Backup & Restore**, tap **Restore From Backup**, choose the file, and enter its password.

## Good to know

- A backup file leaves out photos and shared cars. Signing in brings everything.
- Keep your recovery key and backup files private. They can hold householder details.
- See [How do I back up and restore my data?](/help/backups/backup-restore) and [Why did my phone switch to Cloud Sync?](/help/backups/cloud-sync-from-web).
