title: Bushe WitnessWork iba pa Android?
---

Ee. WitnessWork ibomba pa Android, elyo ni ya bwamba. Kuti mwaibomfya na pa web browser.

## Ifyo ibomba pa Android

- Inshita ne malipoti, timer, abakulanshanya ne Abakukonkapo, ne mapange
- Mapu, mileage, ifibukisho, ne backups
- Buddies, ukubikapo na buddies abubomfya iPhone
- Scribe AI

Ifintu fimo fyafwaya ifibombelo fya Apple kanshi fishala pa iPhone na iPad: iCloud Sync, widgets, Live Activities, ne fikope fimbi fya app.

## Sungeni ifibombelo fyenu fyalingana

Cloud Sync ibomba pa Android, iPhone ne web. Google Drive Sync ibomba pa fibombelo fya Android. Moneni [Cloud Sync ni nshi?](/help/backups/cloud-sync).

## Ukuya ku foni ipya ya Android

1. Pa foni yenu ya kale, isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti** elyo mutinike **Pangeni Icibombelo Cimbi**.
2. Konkeni amashinte pa foni ipya. Na Cloud Sync, ifyo mwalemba fyaisa ukwabula ukubwekeshapo.
3. Nga mwaisula Backup mu **Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti**, foni ipya ikupeela ukuibwekeshapo.

Kuti mwabomfya na ifailo ya backup. Pa foni yenu ya kale, tinikeni **Ifyo Mwasala → Ukusunga & Ukubwekeshepo → Create Backup** elyo mukopolole ifailo ku foni ipya. Pa foni ipya, tinikeni **Naliibomfya kale WitnessWork** ilyo ukutendeka kwatendeka elyo musale **Ukufuma ku ifailo ya kusunga** pa cikope ca **Mwapokelelwe nakabili**. Pa pali foni sha Android, kuti mwasala **Ukufuma ku Google Drive** pa kuno.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Sungeni ifailo sha backup mu cisuma. Kuti shakwata ifyebo fya bantu ba kulanshanya.
- Ukubomfya WitnessWork mu browser, moneni [Bushe kuti nabomfya WitnessWork pa web browser?](/help/general/web-app).
