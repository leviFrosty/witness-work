title: Can I use Siri or Shortcuts with WitnessWork?
category: time
platforms:
  - ios
---

Yes, on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and on the Apple Watch app. Open WitnessWork once after updating, then say a phrase like "Add time in WitnessWork".

## Phrases to say

- "Add time in WitnessWork". You can also say log, enter, record, track or submit.
- "Log two hours in WitnessWork" or "Add 30 minutes to WitnessWork". Siri knows common amounts from 15 minutes to 12 hours. For any other amount, Siri asks how long.
- "Start my timer in WitnessWork", "Pause my timer in WitnessWork" or "Stop my timer in WitnessWork".
- "Log a trip in WitnessWork" or "Log a round trip in WitnessWork". Siri asks how far. For a round trip, say the one-way distance and it's doubled.

## Good to know

- Time and trips are added for today, as Standard time and with the car you used last. To pick a Type or car, add the action in the Shortcuts app. There you can also run it from the Action Button or a Home Screen shortcut.
- Stopping the timer adds its whole minutes as an entry for today. With less than a minute, nothing is added.
- Adding time and the timer need you to log hours. Turn on **Log my hours** first if you're a Kingdom Publisher.
- Logging trips needs Track Mileage on and at least one car.
- Siri listens in your Siri language, which can differ from the language you chose in WitnessWork.
- Siri uses only your Types, car names, distance unit and timer. It never sees your contacts, notes or reports.
- Siri and Shortcuts aren't available on Android.

## If something goes wrong

- **Siri doesn't recognize a phrase:** open WitnessWork once, wait a minute, and check that Siri is allowed for WitnessWork in your phone's Settings.
- **Still stuck:** use the Help Center's private support link, and keep your records out of public reports.
