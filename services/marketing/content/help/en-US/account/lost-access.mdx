---
id: lostAccess
title: What if I lose access to my WitnessWork account?
category: account
---

Try these, in this order:

1. **A new phone with your account key already on it.** On a new iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Keychain on, WitnessWork finds your account key by itself; on Android, setting up the new phone from your Google backup (with a screen lock) can bring it back.
2. **A passkey.** From version 1.47.0, choose to sign in with a passkey on the sign-in screen. If it can unlock your account, that's enough; otherwise WitnessWork asks for your phone or recovery key.
3. **Another signed-in device.** On a phone or a signed-in browser, show a Set Up Another Device code for a new phone, or, from version 1.47.0, approve a computer with Settings → Account → Sign In on a Computer. See [How do I move to a new phone](/help/account/account-handoff) and [How do I sign in on a computer with my phone?](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer).
4. **Your recovery key.** Enter it with Use a Recovery Key ([How do I use my recovery key?](/help/account/account-recovery-key-use)).

If none of these work and you have no recovery key, your account can't be recovered. WitnessWork keeps your account key and your synced records end-to-end encrypted, so no one at WitnessWork can read or reset them, and nothing in support can override that. You can still start a new account on a new device, but your old records can't be read from the server.

To stay safe, save your recovery key in a password manager or print it ([What is my recovery key?](/help/account/account-recovery-key)), and consider saving a passkey too ([What is a passkey?](/help/account/passkeys-overview)).
