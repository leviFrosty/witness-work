Yes. If you don't have the WitnessWork app on that device, the link's page offers **Continue on the web**. It opens WitnessWork at [app.witnesswork.app](https://app.witnesswork.app).

## Open a shared contact

1. Tap the link and choose **Continue on the web**.
2. Sign in if asked.
3. When WitnessWork asks **Import Contact?**, choose **OK** to add it, or **Cancel** to leave it out.

Nothing is saved until you choose.

## Open a Buddies invite

1. Tap the link and choose **Continue on the web**.
2. Sign in if asked.
3. Check who invited you, then accept.

## Good to know

- If WitnessWork is locked or open in another tab, unlock it or choose **Use Here** first. The contact or invite opens right after.
- WitnessWork keeps a link you haven't opened yet for up to an hour. After that, open the link again.
- Reloading the page or sharing a screenshot of it doesn't show the contact or invite.
- A Buddies invite works once and expires after seven days.

## If something goes wrong

- **The link isn't valid:** it was cut short or changed when it was copied. Ask the sender to share it again. See [problems with a Buddies invite](/help/buddies/buddies-invite-trouble).
