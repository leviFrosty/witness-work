title: How do I see a conversation and its follow-up, or bring a buddy along?
category: contacts
---

Tap a conversation in a contact's history, or a person on Home's conversations card. It opens in a sheet, so you don't lose your place.

## What the sheet shows

- **At the top:** who it was with. Tap them to open the contact, or use **Call**, **Text**, and **Navigate**.
- **The follow-up:** when it is, whether it's up next or missed, and the topic.
- **At the bottom:** the conversation itself, with your note. Tap the pencil to edit, or ••• for more, such as copying the note or deleting the conversation.

## Work with the follow-up

- **Log Visit** records the return visit. **Not at Home** logs a try right away, with **Undo**.
- **Edit Follow-Up** or **Reschedule** changes it.
- The X on a missed follow-up dismisses it. You can plan it again later.
- With no follow-up, the sheet offers **Plan Follow-Up** after the latest visit.

## Bring a buddy along

If you use Buddies, tap the dashed **+** on a follow-up that is still ahead to invite someone. This shares only its date and time, the householder's first name, the address, and the topic. To take someone off, edit the conversation. Invitations aren't available in data protection mode.

## Good to know

- Opening a conversation changes nothing. Only inviting a buddy shares anything.
- Deleting a conversation can't be undone.
