title: How do photos, exports and backups work on the web?
category: general
platforms: [web]
---

**Photos.** To add a profile or contact photo, or a photo in a note, choose a photo file. On a phone you can also take one with the camera. JPEG and PNG photos work everywhere; HEIC photos from an iPhone open in Safari only, so on other browsers choose a JPEG or PNG version. A photo can be up to 50 MB and 120 megapixels. WitnessWork shrinks it before saving, as the phone apps do, and a photo you choose is processed in your browser. Profile and contact photos are saved with your data. Photos in notes aren't saved yet on the web, so they disappear when you close or reload the tab.

**Downloads.** Create Backup, Export CSV for a mileage report, and other exports are downloaded as files to your browser's download folder. Your browser may ask where to save them, or show them in its downloads list. On a phone, sharing a contact or your pet's picture opens the phone's share sheet when your browser supports it, and downloads the file otherwise.

**Copying instead of sharing.** On a computer, Share Report and other text shares copy the text to your clipboard (you'll see "Copied!") so you can paste it where you need it. On a phone, they open the share sheet.

**Imports.** Restore From Backup, Import Contact and the MyTime import open your browser's file chooser. MyTime backups (.mytimedb) are read inside your browser: the file isn't uploaded, and one over 25 MB is refused. WitnessWork JSON backups are password-locked files; enter the password to restore them.

**If something doesn't work.** If a photo says your browser can't open it, choose a JPEG or PNG. If nothing downloads, check that your browser isn't blocking downloads for this site. A backup or other file you downloaded can contain householder details, so keep it private. For more help, see [Contact Support](/help).
