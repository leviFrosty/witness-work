---
id: cloudSyncPhotos
title: Do photos sync with Cloud Sync?
category: backups
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Yes. With Cloud Sync, your profile photo, contact photos and photos in notes sync to your other devices on Cloud Sync, including WitnessWork on the web. You don't need to turn anything on; Include images in sync is only for iCloud and Google Drive sync.

- **End-to-end encrypted.** Each photo is encrypted on your device before it's uploaded, with a key only your devices have. WitnessWork's server stores it without being able to see it, or tell which contact or note it belongs to.
- **Originals.** Photos are stored as you saved them.
- **On demand.** A device downloads only the photos its records use, after it syncs, and keeps them on the device.
- **Storage.** Your account has 2 GB for photos; see “How much photo storage does Cloud Sync have?”.
- **Older versions** of WitnessWork show initials or a placeholder instead of a photo that synced from another device. Update WitnessWork on every device.
- **Leaving Cloud Sync.** When none of your devices uses Cloud Sync any more, its photos are deleted from WitnessWork's server about a day later. They stay on your devices, and a device that comes back to Cloud Sync uploads them again.

If a photo doesn't appear on another device, open WitnessWork on the device that added it and tap Sync now in Settings → Device Sync.
