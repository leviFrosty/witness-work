title: Ibikoresho byo gukemurisha bikora iki?
---

Ibikoresho byo gukemurisha bifasha gukemura ibibazo hamwe n'ubufasha. Ubifungure muri Igenamiterere → Ibyisumbuye; bikoreshwa gusa ku gikoresho wabifunguyeho, kuri iOS na Android. Byongeraho urupapuro rw'Ibikoresho rufite ibikorwa byo gusuzuma, amakuru y'ikitegererezo no gukosora, bigaragaza amakuru ya tekiniki yiyongera kuri ecran zimwe, kandi bifungura kwandika amakuru arambuye yo gusuzuma. Ibikorwa bimwe by'Ibikoresho byongeraho amakuru y'ikitegererezo cyangwa bisiba amakuru burundu, bityo ubikoreshe gusa iyo ubufasha bubisabye, kandi banza ubike backup muri Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura. Gufungura cyangwa kuzimya akuma ntibihindura amakuru yawe; kazimye kugira ngo uhishe urupapuro rw'Ibikoresho n'amakuru yongeye.
