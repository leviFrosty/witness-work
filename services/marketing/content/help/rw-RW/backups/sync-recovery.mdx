title: "Ibyo nahisemo mu gutangira, abantu basibwe, n'inyibutsa bigenda bite mu guhuza?"
---

Gutangira bundi bushya bireba iki gikoresho gusa. Iyo igikoresho gisanzwe gifite inyandiko cyinjiye mu guhuza, WitnessWork ibaza icyo gukora na zo mbere y'uko hari igisimbuzwa.

## Ibyo ushobora kubona guhitamo

- **Huza byose:** ihuriza hamwe iki gikoresho n'amakuru ahuzwa, ibika verisiyo nshya ya buri kintu.
- **Garura muri iCloud** (cyangwa Google Drive, cyangwa Cloud Sync): isimbuza inyandiko z'iki gikoresho n'izihuzwa. WitnessWork ikubaza kwemeza.
- **Bika ay'iyi telefoni:** ibindi bikoresho byawe bihindukira ku nyandiko z'iki gikoresho ubutaha bihuza. Ibi bisimbuza impinduka byakorewemo mbere yaho.
- **Tangira bundi bushya:** guhuza bigumya guhagarara kugeza ubifunguye muri **Igenamiterere → Guhuza**. Hanyuma uhitemo **Huza byose** cyangwa kugarura.

## Byiza kumenya

- Amakuru y'abantu yasibwe ashobora kugarurwa mu minsi igera kuri 90.
- Nyuma yo guhuza cyangwa kugarura, inyibutsa zongera kubakwa kuri iki gikoresho n'igihe wahisemo kuri buri gusubirayo na gahunda. Hashyirwaho gusa inyibutsa z'igihe kizaza, kandi WitnessWork ikeneye uburenganzira bwo kohereza amenyesha.
- Kugarura ntibyohereza kwanga muri Buddies ku gahunda zibura muri backup.
- Kugira ngo wirinde, bika igikoresho gifite inyandiko zawe nshya kandi ubike dosiye ya backup mbere yo gusimbuza ikintu icyo ari cyo cyose. Niba ukeneye ubufasha, koresha uburyo bw'ubufasha bw'ibanga mu Kigo cy'Ubufasha.
