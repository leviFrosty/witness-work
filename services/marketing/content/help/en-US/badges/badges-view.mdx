title: How do I see a badge up close or share it?
category: badges
---

Tap any badge, on your profile, on the Badges screen, or on a buddy's page in Buddies, and it opens full screen: the badge, its level, what it counts, and when you earned it. “Found in your history” means older records reached it and the month isn't known. Under your own badges, a line like “Next: Pearl · 41 of 60 months” shows how far that badge is from its next level, or “All four levels collected” once you have Pearl; tap the info button beside it to see what helps it grow. A level you haven't reached yet shows “Not yet” and how to get there, and so does a level a buddy hasn't reached when you tap it under See all on their page. Tap the badge to spin it. To share one, take a screenshot: the screen shows your name, as in “Anna's badge,” and WitnessWork at the bottom, and nothing is sent anywhere on its own. Your name comes from your profile, in Settings → Profile; without a name, the screen leaves it off. Close the badge with the X, by swiping down, or with Back on Android. With Buddies, your buddies' reactions appear under your own badge, and a buddy's badge lets you send one; see the question about what buddies see.
