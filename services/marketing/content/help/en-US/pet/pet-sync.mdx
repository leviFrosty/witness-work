---
id: petSync
title: Does my pet sync, and what do my buddies see?
category: pet
---

Yes. Your pet syncs between your devices, and buddies see only its kind, coat, name and hat.

## What syncs

Your pet's name, hat, room, the hats and items you own, and keepsakes sync with the option you chose in **Settings → Sync**. They're also in your backups.

Hearts and coins aren't saved. Each device works them out from your records, so your devices agree. If two devices spend coins while offline, they may show 0 until you earn more. Nothing you bought is lost.

## What buddies see

Buddies see your pet's kind, coat, name and hat. Nothing else about your pet, and none of your records, is shared.

To stop, turn off **Pet** in Buddies Settings. The switch shows once you have a pet.
