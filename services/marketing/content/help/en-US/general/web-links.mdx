---
id: webLinks
title: Can I open a shared contact or Buddies invite on the web?
category: general
platforms: [web]
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Yes, from version 1.47.0. When someone shares a contact or sends you a Buddies invite link and you don't have the WitnessWork app on that device, the link's page offers **Continue on the web**. It opens WitnessWork on the web ([app.witnesswork.app](https://app.witnesswork.app)):

- A shared contact asks **Import Contact?** first. Choose OK to add it, or Cancel to leave it out. Nothing is saved until you choose.
- A Buddies invite opens the same screen as on your phone, showing who invited you before you accept.

If you aren't signed in yet, or WitnessWork is locked or open in another tab, sign in, unlock, or choose **Use Here** first; the contact or invite opens right after. WitnessWork keeps a link it hasn't opened yet for up to an hour, sealed in your browser's WitnessWork storage, so a reload or signing in doesn't lose it. After that, open the link again.

The contact or invite travels in the part of the link after the # sign, which your browser never sends to a server, and WitnessWork removes it from the address bar as soon as it's read, so reloading or sharing a screenshot of the page doesn't show it. Opening the same contact again asks again; it never imports by itself.

If WitnessWork says the link isn't valid, it was cut short or changed when it was copied. Ask the sender to share the contact or invite again. A Buddies invite works once and expires after seven days; see [problems with a Buddies invite](/help/buddies/buddies-invite-trouble).
