title: "Kuki umurongo w'umushyitsi cyangwa QR code bidafunguka, cyangwa kuki bivuga ko byarangiye?"
---

Urupapuro ruvuga ikibazo. Umurongo ukora kugeza igihe cyawo kirangiye cyangwa uwawusangije awurangije.

## Icyo urupapuro ruvuga

- **"Uyu murongo wararangiye":** igihe cy'umurongo cyaranzwe. Saba mushya niba ugikora.
- **"Uyu murongo warahagaritswe":** uwawusangije yawurangije, yagaruye agace, cyangwa yisubije umuhanda. Saba umurongo mushya.
- **"Uyu murongo ntukora":** hari igice cy'umurongo kibura. Porogaramu zo kohereza ubutumwa rimwe na rimwe zigabanya imirongo miremire. Koporora umurongo wose, cyangwa usuzume QR code.
- **"Nta murandasi ufite":** huza murandasi kugira ngo ufungure umurongo. Umaze kuwufungura, ibyo wandika nta murandasi bizoherezwa wasubiye kuri murandasi.
- **"Imirongo yahagaritswe by'agateganyo":** ongera ugerageze nyuma y'iminota mike.
- **"Nta muhanda w'uyu murongo ushobora kwandikwaho ubu":** imihanda yarashyikirijwe cyangwa irisubizwa. Baza uwasangije umurongo.
- **"Impinduka 1 ntiyabitswe. Uwo muhanda ntusangijwe uyu murongo."** cyangwa **"inyandiko ziva muri iyi murandasi ntizabitswe":** umurongo warangiye mbere y'uko telefoni yawe ibohereza. Menyesha uwasangije umurongo ibyo wanditse.

## Byiza kumenya

- Suzuma QR code ukoresheje kamera ya telefoni yawe hanyuma ukande umurongo igaragaza.
- Murandasi yawe ibika inyandiko z'umurongo kugeza urangiye. Kanda **Ibagirwa uyu murongo** kugira ngo uzisibe vuba. Inyandiko zamaze koherezwa zigumana n'itsinda.

## Niba ari wowe wasangije umurongo

Fungura agace urebe munsi ya **Basangijwe**. Umurongo warangiye cyangwa wahagaritswe werekana igihe. Kugira ngo ukore mushya, kanda **Sangiza**, hanyuma **Umurongo cyangwa QR code**. Reba [Nasangiza nte agace umuntu udafite porogaramu?](/help/territories/territories-guest-link)
