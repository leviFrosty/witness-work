title: "Nteganya nte mu mezi menshi cyangwa nkareba umwaka wanjye w'umurimo wose?"
---

Igice cya **Gahunda** gifite ubwoko bubiri. Koresha utubuto two hejuru kugira ngo uhindure hagati ya **Ukwezi** na **Umwaka**.

## Ubwoko bwa Ukwezi

Ibyumweru byawe bigaragara nka kalendari imwe ikomeza kunyuruka ku iherezo ry'ukwezi, ushobora rero guteganya uku kwezi n'ukurikira icyarimwe.

- Ukwezi urimo gusoma kugumya kurabagirana. Andi arahigana.
- Ikarita iri hejuru ya kalendari yerekana intego y'uko kwezi, hamwe n'igihe cyanditswe n'igiteganyijwe (imirongo), n'niba uri imbere cyangwa inyuma. Yikande kugira ngo urebe byinshi.
- Buri munsi werekana igihe wateganyije, cyangwa igihe wanditse iyo hari icyo wanditse.
- Kanda umunsi kugira ngo uwurebe cyangwa uwuhindure. Wukande ufate kugira ngo wongereho igihe cyangwa uwuteganye.

## Ubwoko bwa Umwaka

1. Kanda **Umwaka** kugira ngo ubone amezi cumi n'abiri y'umwaka w'umurimo, kuva muri Nzeri kugeza muri Kanama.
2. Kanda ukwezi, cyangwa ugure hanze n'intoki, kugira ngo uguzoome. Gura imbere kugira ngo usubire inyuma.
3. Koresha imyambi cyangwa utwike kugira ngo uhindure imyaka. Kanda **Uyu munsi** kugira ngo usubire.

Ikarita yo hejuru yerekana igihe cyawe cyanditswe n'igiteganyijwe ugereranyije n'intego yawe y'umwaka, niba uyifite. Umurongo ufite izina ugaragaza aho umwaka umwe w'umurimo urangirira n'ukurikira utangirira. Kanda kugira ngo urebe uwo mwaka.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ubwoko bwombi bwerekana gusa ibisanzwe biri mu nyandiko zawe. Guhindura ubwoko nta kintu bisangiza.
