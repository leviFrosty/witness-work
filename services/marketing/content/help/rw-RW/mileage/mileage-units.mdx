title: Nshobora gukoresha kilometero, litiro, cyangwa L/100 km?
---

Yego. Fungura Ibirometero → Igenamiterere (agashushanyo k'ibikoresho) → Ibipimo. Intera ishobora kuba mayili cyangwa kilometero, kandi ikoreshwa rya lisansi rishobora kuba mpg (US), mpg (UK), L/100 km, cyangwa km/L. Auto ikurikiza akarere k'igikoresho cyawe. Lisansi ihabwa igiciro kuri galoni na mpg (US) no kuri litiro n'ibindi byose. Ifaranga rikurikiza akarere k'igikoresho cyawe. Guhindura ibipimo bihindura gusa uko imibare yerekanwa; ingendo zawe ntizihindurwa.
