---
id: copyLastWeekPlans
title: Can I copy last week’s plans to this week?
category: plans
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Yes, from version 1.47.0. On the Schedule tab, touch and hold any day of the week you want to fill (on the web, right-click it), then choose Copy Last Week’s Plans. The same menu opens from a day in This Week on Home and from a day in the Progress tab's list of days. Each one-time plan from the week before is copied to the same weekday, with its start time, length, Type, title, place, note, and reminder. Weeks begin on your Start of Week, set in Settings → Preferences → Region & Formats.

Recurring plans already repeat, so they aren't copied, and neither are plans you joined through a buddy's invitation. Buddies you invited aren't invited to the copies; open a copy and tap Invite Buddies to invite them again.

Days that have already passed, your Off Days (Settings → Preferences → Plans → Availability), and days that already have a plan starting at the same time are skipped, so copying again doesn't make duplicates. If nothing is left to copy, WitnessWork tells you and changes nothing. Once every day of a week has passed, the action no longer shows for that week.

To take the copies back, tap Undo on the message that appears. After that, delete the ones you don't need like any other plan. Copies are ordinary plans: log your time as usual, and they sync and back up like your other plans.
