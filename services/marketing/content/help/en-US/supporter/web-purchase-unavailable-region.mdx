title: Why can't I buy on the web in my country?

For now, WitnessWork on the web doesn't sell Supporter subscriptions or Tips in the European Union, the United Kingdom, a number of other countries where tax applies from the first sale, or Canada (for now, because two provinces tax from the first sale). Selling there brings tax obligations we aren't set up for yet. The [Terms of Use](/terms) list every place.

If WitnessWork thinks you're in one of those places, **Settings → Become a supporter** shows a notice instead of web prices and buy buttons. The Supporter benefits are still listed. The notice points you to WitnessWork in the App Store and Google Play, where available. The stores sell Supporter subscriptions and Tips there and handle the tax.

- **A store purchase shows on the web too.** Sign in to the same WitnessWork account on the web, and your Supporter features appear there (WitnessWork 1.47.0 or later on the phone; open it once after updating).
- **Your country comes from your connection.** If you're traveling, or using a VPN or a work or school network that routes through one of these places, you may see the notice even though you live elsewhere. Turn the VPN off or reconnect, then reload WitnessWork.
- **Checkout checks your card too.** Payments are declined when the card or the billing address is from one of these places, even if you're browsing from somewhere else. The decline happens before anything is charged.

If you're somewhere the Terms don't list and still can't buy, email [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) with what you see.
