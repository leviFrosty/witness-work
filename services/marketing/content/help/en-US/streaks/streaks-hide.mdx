title: Can I turn off streaks?
category: streaks
---

Yes. Turn off **Show streak** in **Settings → Features → Streaks & Badges**.

## Turn off streaks

1. Open **Settings → Features → Streaks & Badges**.
2. Turn off **Show streak**.

To hide badges too, turn off **Show badges** on the same screen.

## Good to know

- Your streak no longer shows on Home or your profile, nothing celebrates it, and **Streak Reminders** stop.
- Your buddies' streaks are hidden too, and yours stops being shared with them.
- Nothing is lost. Turn **Show streak** back on and it shows again right away, unless it ended in the meantime.
- The setting follows you to your other devices when sync is on.
