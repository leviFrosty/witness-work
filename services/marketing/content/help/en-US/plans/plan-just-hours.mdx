---
id: planJustHours
title: Can I plan hours without picking a time?
category: plans
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Yes. On the Plan form, set how long with the − and + buttons or the quick amounts under the number; tap the number itself for an exact length. A new Plan has no set time: its Start time row reads Anytime. To give it a time, tap Start time and pick when you start and finish; the hours add up for you, and changing the hours afterward moves the finish time. New Plans open the way you planned last, so your usual way is ready next time. A Plan with no set time shows Anytime on your Schedule and counts toward your goals like any other Plan. With Notify Me on, it reminds you at 8:00 AM that day instead of before a start time. Buddies you invite see the day and how long, with no time. To take the time off a Plan later, edit it, tap Start time, and choose No set time. If another of your devices still runs an older version of WitnessWork, it shows a Plan with no set time at noon until it updates.
