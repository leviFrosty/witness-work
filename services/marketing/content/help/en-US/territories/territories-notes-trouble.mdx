Under the notes, the screen says which reader ran. "Read on this device" means Scribe AI didn't read them, and the next words say why:

- **Turn on Scribe AI in Settings to read notes in any language:** Scribe AI is off. Turn it on in Settings → Scribe AI.
- **Scribe AI has reached its limit for now:** you've used your Scribe AI allowance; see [How do Scribe AI limits work?](/help/scribe/scribe-usage)
- **Scribe AI needs an internet connection:** connect, then read the notes again.
- **These notes are too long for Scribe AI:** split them and add them in two or more goes, such as one street at a time.
- **Scribe AI isn't available right now** or **couldn't read these notes:** try again in a moment.

The on-device reader understands common English shorthand only: write each house as its number and what happened (NH or not home, spoke, DNC or do not call), with the street's name on its own line above its houses.

**A house is wrong or missing:** fix its house number or result, or remove it, before you tap Add Entries. Choose Street sets the street for entries without one, and an entry marked "(not in this territory)" names a street the territory doesn't have. Lines that couldn't be read are listed under Couldn't Read, where Add as Entry lets you fix one by hand. A house is read only when its number is written on its line, so a guess like "the blue house" isn't kept. If you added something by mistake, open the street, tap the house, and change or remove it.

**"Nothing here could be read as a house and what happened":** check that each line has a house number and a result.

If a problem continues, contact private support under Still need help?, using the support button in this screen's header. Leave people's names and addresses out of what you send.
