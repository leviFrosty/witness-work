title: Where do I find my app version and account ID?
category: general
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Open **Settings → About → App Info**. Your app version is at the top, and your account ID is under **Account**.

## Copy the details for support

1. Tap your profile picture, then **Settings**.
2. Open **About → App Info**.
3. Tap **Copy All Details**, or tap any row to copy just that value.
4. Paste it into a private email to support.

## What's there

- The WitnessWork version and build, your device and its system version, and the WitnessWork server's version.
- **Account ID** is your WitnessWork account. It says **No account yet** until this device signs in.
- **Purchases ID** is what your App Store or Google Play purchase is tied to.

## Good to know

- Copying doesn't send anything. You choose where to paste it.
- Share these IDs only in private email to support, never in a public bug report or screenshot.

## If something goes wrong

- **The server version says Unavailable:** this device couldn't reach WitnessWork. Check your connection and open **App Info** again.
