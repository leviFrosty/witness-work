---

Kuri ubu, WitnessWork kuri murandasi ntigurisha amafatabuguzi y'Umufasha cyangwa Tip mu Muryango w'Ubumwe bw'u Burayi, mu Bwongereza, mu bindi bihugu byinshi, cyangwa mu ntara za Canada za Manitoba na Saskatchewan. [Amabwiriza yo gukoresha](/terms) atondagura ahantu hose. Ahandi muri Canada hashobora kugura kuri murandasi.

## Icyo wakora

Niba WitnessWork ikeka ko uri muri kimwe muri ibi bihugu, **Igenamiterere → Ba umufasha** herekana "Not available where you are yet" aho kwerekana ibiciro. Koresha utubuto twa App Store cyangwa Google Play aho kugira ngo ugurire mu bubiko. Ibyaguzwe mu bubiko bigaragara no kuri murandasi: injira muri konti imwe ya WitnessWork kuri murandasi, ibiranga Umufasha bigaragare. Fungura porogaramu kuri telefoni yawe rimwe nyuma yo kuyivugurura.

## Niba ukeka ko uri ahantu hatari ho

- **Urimo gutembera, cyangwa ukoresha VPN, urusobe rw'akazi cyangwa rw'ishuri:** aho uri havuye ku muhuza wawe. Zimya VPN cyangwa wongere uhuze, hanyuma wongere upakire WitnessWork.
- **Muri Canada:** niba umuhuza wawe utagaragaza intara yawe, imenyesha rigaragara hose muri Canada. Gerageza urundi rusobe, hanyuma wongere upakire WitnessWork, cyangwa ugurire muri porogaramu.
- **Ikarita yawe ni iy'umwe muri ibi bihugu:** ubwishyu bwangwa iyo ikarita cyangwa aderesi y'ubwishyu ari iy'aho, nubwo wasuzumira ahandi. Nta kintu gikurwaho.

Niba uri ahantu Amabwiriza ataranditse ukaba utaragura, andikira [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) utubwire ibyo ubona.
