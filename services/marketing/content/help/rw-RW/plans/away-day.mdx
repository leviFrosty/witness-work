title: How do I mark a day I'm not going out without breaking my repeat plan?
---

Kanda gahunda iri kuri uwo munsi ku Ingengabihe kugira ngo uyifungure, kanda Ibindi (•••), hanyuma Siba → Siba iyi gahunda. Kora ibi kuri buri gahunda yo kuri uwo munsi. Hakurwaho gusa uwo munsi, kandi gahunda yisubiramo ikomeza guhera ku munsi ukurikira. Kugira ngo ujye kubwiriza mu kindi gihe kuri uwo munsi, kanda Hindura, uyihindure, hanyuma uhitemo Gusa kuri iyi tariki iyo ubitse.
