title: "Kuki amafoto y'abantu abura ku kindi gikoresho cyanjye?"
---

Igisubizo gikwiye giterwa n'uburyo bwo guhuza ukoresha. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Guhuza** urebe.

## Niba ukoresha Cloud Sync

Amafoto ahuzwa yonyine. Reba [Amafoto ahuzwa na Cloud Sync?](/help/backups/cloud-sync-photos).

1. Vugurura WitnessWork kuri buri gikoresho.
2. Ku gikoresho cyongeyeho ifoto, fungura **Igenamiterere → Guhuza** ukande **Huza ubu**.
3. Niba ifoto yerekana **Ntihuze: ububiko bwuzuye**, umwanya wawe wa GB 2 w'amafoto wuzuye. Kuraho amafoto udakeneye maze izajya hejuru yonyine.
4. Niba yerekana **Ntihuze: ifoto ni nini cyane**, isimbuze ifoto ntoya.

Murandasi ihuza amafoto ari mu nyandiko, ariko si ay'umwirondoro cyangwa abantu ubu. Abantu bagaragara aho n'inyuguti z'amazina.

## Niba ukoresha iCloud (Google Drive kuri Android)

Amafoto afite akabuto kayo. Kuri buri gikoresho, fungura **Igenamiterere → Guhuza** ufungure **Huza amafoto y'aderesi**. Amafoto abarwa mu mwanya wawe wa iCloud cyangwa Google. Vugurura WitnessWork kuri buri gikoresho.

Kugira ngo ukureho amafoto yamaze kujyayo, banza uhagarike guhuza amafoto ku bindi bikoresho byawe, hanyuma uhitemo **Kuraho amafoto ya iCloud**. Ubundi bishobora kongera kohereza kopi zabyo.

## Biracyabura?

Bika igikoresho gifite ifoto y'umwimerere, reba umwanya n'uburenganzira, hanyuma ukande **Huza ubu**. Niba bikinanirana, koresha uburyo bw'ubufasha bw'ibanga mu Kigo cy'Ubufasha. Ntukohereze amakuru y'abo usura mu bitekerezo by'abantu bose.
