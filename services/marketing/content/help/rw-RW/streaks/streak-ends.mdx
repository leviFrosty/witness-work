title: "Urukurikirane rwanjye rurangira ryari, kandi mbikomeza nte?"
---

Urukurikirane rwawe rurangira iyo umunsi wateganyijwe urangiye nta gihe cyanditswe. Ushobora gukomeza kwandika umunsi wateganyijwe kugeza ku iherezo ry'umunsi ukurikira.

## Rukomeze

- **Wagiye mu murimo ariko wibagiwe kwandika:** ongeraho igihe kuri uwo munsi maze urukurikirane rwawe rugaruke.
- **Udashoboye kujya mu murimo ku munsi wateganyijwe:** kuraho gahunda z'uwo munsi mbere y'igihe. Kuri **Gahunda**, kanda buri gahunda, kanda **Ibindi** (•••), hanyuma **Siba → Siba iyi gahunda**. Ku gahunda isubirwamo, itariki imwe gusa ni yo ikurwaho. Umunsi ukomeza kuba uwateganyijwe igihe hari gahunda isigaye kuri wo.

## Inyibutsa

- Iyo urukurikirane rwawe rugiye kurangira, ikibatsi Ahabanza cyerekana igihe gisigaye. **Inyibutsa y'urukurikirane** iza saa 6:00 z'umugoroba ku munsi wa nyuma.
- Ku Babwiriza, ukwezi gushobora kwandikwa kugeza ku iherezo ry'ukwezi gukurikira. Inyibutsa iza iminsi itatu mbere y'uko ukwezi kurangira.
- Kugira ngo uzimye inyibutsa, fungura **Igenamiterere → Amamenyesha** uzimye **Inyibutsa z'urukurikirane**. Zikeneye ko amamenyesha yemerewe WitnessWork.
