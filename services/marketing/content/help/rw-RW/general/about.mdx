Fungura **Igenamiterere → Ibyerekeye**. Harimo ihuza rya Politiki y'ibanga, Amabwiriza yo gukoresha n'inkomoko ya WitnessWork.

## Impushya z'inkomoko ifunguye

1. Kanda ifoto y'umwirondoro wawe, hanyuma **Igenamiterere**.
2. Manuka ugere kuri **Ibyerekeye**, ukande **Impushya z'inkomoko ifunguye**.
3. Shakisha ukoresheje izina rya paki, uruhushya cyangwa uwayikoze, hanyuma ukande paki kugira ngo ubone uruhushya rwayo rwose.

## Byiza kumenya

- Aho Scribe AI iboneka, **Ibyerekeye** kandi harimo ihuza rya politiki ivuga ibyo wohereza kuri Scribe AI.
- Fata ku ihuza iryo ariryo ryose kugira ngo ukopore cyangwa usangize.
