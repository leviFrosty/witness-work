Badges celebrate steady habits in your ministry, like sharing each month, sending your report, and going back to see people. There's nothing to set up. WitnessWork checks your records and adds a badge when you reach one.

## Find your badges

1. Tap your profile picture, then **Profile**.
2. Your badges are right under your profile card. Tap one to see it up close.
3. Tap **See all** to open **Badges**, where you can see every badge and what helps each one grow next.

Most badges grow through four levels: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Pearl. A few one-time badges, like First Bible Study, stay hidden until you have them.

## When you earn one

- When something you just did earns a badge, a small card slides in at the top of the screen. Tap it to celebrate full screen, or swipe it away. Several new badges stack, with the newest in front. Tap the ones behind to spread them out.
- A badge you don't tap, or one that arrives another way, doesn't interrupt you. It waits in the notification bell on Home. Tap **View** to celebrate it, or **X** to dismiss it. Either way it's saved, and stays marked new on your profile until you open your badges.
- If your records already reach badges the first time WitnessWork checks, a "Your badges are here" screen says how many you have. **Take a look** opens your profile, and **Later** closes it.

## Good to know

- Badges work on iPhone and Android. They update a moment after your records change, and whenever you return to WitnessWork.
- Badges aren't the red number on the app icon. That counts unread items in the notification bell.
- To learn what each one counts, see [What does each badge count, and why didn't a month count?](/help/badges/badges-collections)
