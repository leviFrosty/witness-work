title: "Kuki WitnessWork ivuga ko idashobora kwemeza iki gikoresho?"
---

WitnessWork isaba Apple cyangwa Google kwemeza ko telefoni yawe na porogaramu ari ukuri. Konti yawe, Buddies, Backup, Shyiraho ikindi gikoresho na Scribe AI bikora gusa muri porogaramu nyayo.

## Niba ubonye ubutumwa

- **Ntiremezwa:** Apple cyangwa Google ntibyabonetse. Tegereza iminota mike ukande **Ongera ugerageze**.
- **Ntishobora kwemeza iki gikoresho:** siba WitnessWork uyishyireho nanone uyikuye kuri App Store cyangwa Google Play. Vugurura sisitemu ya telefoni yawe, hanyuma ukande **Ongera ugerageze**.

Ubibona muri **Igenamiterere → Konti**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Igenzura rigarukira hafi buri minsi 90. Apple na Google babona igenzura gusa, ntibabona amakuru yawe cyangwa konti yawe.
- Ntirishobora kuzura kuri simulators, emulators, telefoni zafunguwe ku ngufu, sisitemu zihinduwe, cyangwa kopi zitemewe za porogaramu.
- Kuri Android, telefoni kandi ikenera Google Play Store na Google Play services bigezweho.
- Ibindi byose muri WitnessWork bikomeza gukora, kandi amakuru yawe aguma kuri telefoni yawe.

Biracyanze kwemeza telefoni nyayo? Vugana n'ubufasha ukoresheje Ikigo cy'ubufasha.
