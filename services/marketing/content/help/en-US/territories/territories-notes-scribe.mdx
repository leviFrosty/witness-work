title: Does Scribe AI read my territory notes, and what is sent?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Yes, when Scribe AI is on and available, it reads the notes you add with Add Results From Notes, and the screen says "Read by Scribe AI. Check each entry before adding." Nothing is added until you check the entries and tap Add Entries.

**What's sent:** the text of the notes, the names of the territory's streets and how they're numbered, and your app language. The territory's number, its do-not-calls, and its other records aren't sent. It goes over an encrypted connection to the same Zero Data Retention providers as the rest of Scribe AI, so it isn't stored or used for training, and WitnessWork's server doesn't keep the notes; it records only how much you used.

**What comes back:** only house numbers, streets, and results (Not at Home, Spoke, Do Not Call). Names, reasons, phone numbers, and any other words in the notes are dropped, so a do-not-call keeps only its address.

**When it reads on your device instead:** if Scribe AI is turned off, offline, has reached its limit, isn't available, couldn't read the notes, or the notes are too long (over about 8,000 characters or 300 lines), WitnessWork reads them on your device. The screen says why, for example "Scribe AI needs an internet connection." The on-device reader understands common English shorthand only, and nothing leaves your device.

**Usage:** reading notes counts toward your own Scribe AI limits, like any other Scribe AI request; a few lines use very little (see [How do Scribe AI limits work?](/help/scribe/scribe-usage)). The first time, Scribe AI asks you to agree to its terms, as it does everywhere else in the app.
