---
id: haptics
title: How do I turn off vibrations?
category: customization
---

Open Settings → Preferences → Audio & Haptics and turn off Haptics. WitnessWork then stops all of its vibration feedback, such as taps, chart scrubbing, and goal celebrations. The setting applies only to this device and isn't synced to your other devices. Some system controls, like menus and switches, may still vibrate based on your device's own settings; adjust those in your device's sound and haptics settings.
