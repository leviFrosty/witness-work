title: Nshani ukusunga no kubwesha ifyebo fyandi?
---

Sungeni ifailo ya backup mu **Ifyo Mwasala → Ukusunga & Ukubwekeshepo**, elyo muibweshe ukufuma apo. Ifailo ibomba pa iPhone, Android ne web, kanshi kuti mwaibomfya ne kusendela ifyo mwalemba ukufuma pa cimo ukuya pa cimbi.

Ku backup ya lwa mwine, moneni [WitnessWork Backup ni nshi?](/help/backups/account-backup).

## Ukupanga ifailo ya backup

1. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Ukusunga & Ukubwekeshepo**.
2. Tinikeni **Create Backup**.
3. Saleni ifyo mupinga ifailo. **Akaunti Yandi** yasula pa fibombelo fyenu ifyengile mu akaunti yenu, nelyo apo na pe ne cisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha. **Password** (ifimpendo 8 nelyo ukucilapo) yasula pa cibombelo conse ne password iyo, kanshi ibomfyeni ukusabankanya kopi.
4. Sungeni ifailo, iitwa witness-work-backup.json.

Pa web, ifailo ilesha mu folda ya downloads ya browser yenu.

## Ukubwesha ukufuma ku ifailo ya backup

1. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Ukusunga & Ukubwekeshepo**.
2. Tinikeni **Restore From Backup**.
3. Saleni ifailo ya backup. Lembeni password yaiko nga ikwata.

Pa foni ipya, kuti mwatinika **Naliibomfya kale WitnessWork** ilyo mulepanga, elyo mwasala **Ukufuma ku ifailo ya kusunga** pe pi **Inshila imbi sha kubwesha** pa screen ya **Mwapokelelwe nakabili**.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ukubwesha kupyana ifyo mwalemba pa cibombelo ici ifyo backup ikwata. Sungeni backup ya nomba pa ntanshi.
- Ifailo ikwata ifyo mwalemba fya mulimo, ubupange, abantu ba kulanshanya, ulutandalilo, ifipao, mileage, icinyama cenu, profile ne fyo mwasala ifisabankanishiwa. Ifikope, motoka shasabankanishiwa ne fyo mwasala pa cibombelo ici tafyabamo.
- Takuli uungasula ifailo iyapingwa ukwabula password yaiko nelyo akaunti yenu, elyo WitnessWork tingabwesha password iyalubwa.
- Backup iyapangwa ne WitnessWork ipya tayingabweshiwa ukufika ilyo mwakusha.
- Sungeni ifailo ne password yaiko mu fyasuma. Kuti yakwata ifyebo fya bantu ba mu ng'anda.

## Nga cabipa

- **Ilanda ukuti mwingile pa ntanshi:** ingileni mu akaunti yenu pa foni iyi ne **Ingila ne QR Code** nelyo icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha, elyo mueseko nakabili.
- **Ilanda ukuti ifailo ipingilwa ku akaunti imbi:** ibweshesheni pa cibombelo icingile mu akaunti iyo, nelyo mupange backup iyapingwa ne password uko.
- **Ifimbi tafyabelengwe:** ifishala fyabwelela, elyo WitnessWork ikweba ubwingi bwa fyashala.
- **Ichili bwino:** bomfyeni inshila ya support ya fyasuma mu Help Center.
