title: Kuki hari umubare ku ishusho ya porogaramu ya WitnessWork?
---

Kuri iOS, umubare uri ku ishusho ya porogaramu ni ibintu bingahe biri mu nzogera y'amamenyesha yo ku Ahabanza utarasoma. Amakuru aturuka kuri bagenzi bawe, nk'ikimenyetso gishya cya mugenzi wawe cyangwa igisubizo ku cyawe, ntabarwa na rimwe. Fungura inzogera uyimanure kugira ngo ushyire ibintu ku bisomwe; kubikuraho cyangwa kubisiba na byo bigabanya umubare. Uravugururwa WitnessWork ifunguye no iyo urwibutso rwa Gusubirayo, Gahunda cyangwa umuntu rugeze. Ibindi bintu, nka raporo y'ukwezi gushize cyangwa ubutumire bwa mugenzi, bibarwa ubutaha ufunguye WitnessWork, bityo umubare ushobora gutinda porogaramu ifunze. Buri gikoresho kigira umubare wacyo. Umubare ukenera uruhushya rw'amamenyesha; kugira ngo uwuhishe, zimya Badges kuri WitnessWork mu igenamiterere ry'amamenyesha ry'igikoresho cyawe. Iryo genamiterere rikora ku mubare gusa, si ku bimenyetso ukuza ku mwirondoro wawe. Kuri Android, ishusho ya porogaramu ntigaragaza umubare utarasomwa; launcher yawe ishobora kwerekana akadomo ku mamenyesha ari mu rwego rw'amamenyesha. Niba umubare ugaragara nabi, fungura WitnessWork, hanyuma uyivemo kugira ngo yivugurure. Niba ukomeje kudahura n'inzogera, vugana n'ubufasha uciye mu Kigo cy'ubufasha.
