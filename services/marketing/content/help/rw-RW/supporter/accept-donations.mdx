title: "Kuki mwemera impano?"
---

WitnessWork ni ubuntu kuri bose, ariko kuyubaka, kuyikoresha no kuyitaho bisaba amafaranga menshi n'igihe. Nk'umuvandimwe wo mu Nzu y'Ubwami, mbona ari amahirwe gukoresha ubumenyi bwanjye gufasha abandi mu itorero. Mbona gutanga impano nk'ukwishyurira undi lisansi nyuma yo kujyana na we mu murimo. Ni igikorwa cy'urukundo, kandi gishimwa cyane.

## Icyo impano zikoreshwa

- Telefoni na tablet nyazo zo kugerageza.
- Amafaranga ya serivisi z'ikarita n'aho umuntu ari.
- Amafaranga yo kubaka porogaramu no kuyishyira kuri App Store na Google Play.
- Amasaha menshi yakoreshejwe mu gutegura porogaramu.

Buri nkunga yose ifasha porogaramu gukomeza gukora.
