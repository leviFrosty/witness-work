On iPhone and iPad, yes. Android doesn't have WitnessWork home screen widgets.

## Add the widget

1. Touch and hold your home screen.
2. Tap the **+** button.
3. Search for WitnessWork and choose **Pet Companion**.

## Sizes

- Small shows your pet.
- Medium adds its name, hearts, your streak, and what its wall sign shows.
- Large shows its whole room with its name and hearts.

## Good to know

- The widget changes through the day on its own, and opening WitnessWork refreshes it.
- Until you adopt a pet, it asks you to choose one in the app.
