id: notesSync
title: Why is a photo missing from a note, or a note plain on another device?
category: notes
---

A note says **This photo isn't on this device** when the photo hasn't reached that device yet.

## Bring the photo over

1. Open WitnessWork on the device that added the photo.
2. Go to **Settings → Sync** and tap **Sync now**.
3. Wait for your other devices to sync.

## Good to know

- **Cloud Sync** sends photos on its own. If the first device says **Not synced: storage full**, your account's photo storage is full. See [Do photos sync with Cloud Sync?](/help/backups/cloud-sync-photos).
- **iCloud Sync** (Google Drive Sync on Android): turn on **Include images in sync** on both devices.
- Restoring a backup file doesn't bring photos back.

## If something goes wrong

- **A note looks plain on another device:** that device has an older WitnessWork and shows notes without formatting or photos. Editing the note there removes its formatting everywhere. Update WitnessWork on every device.
