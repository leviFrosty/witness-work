title: How do I delete my backup, and how long is it kept?
category: backups
---

Open **Settings → Account**. Tap a device's backup to delete just that one, or tap **Delete Backup** to delete the backups of all your devices. Deleted backups are gone right away, and backup turns off on this phone.

## Good to know

- Turning off **Back Up to WitnessWork** asks whether to delete this phone's backup too.
- Another device that still has backup on will back up again the next time you open WitnessWork on it.
- A device's backup that hasn't been updated for 400 days is deleted automatically.
- **Delete My Buddies Data** in Buddies settings also deletes your backup. If backup is on, your next backup goes to your new account.

## If something goes wrong

- **You were offline:** nothing was deleted. Try again when you're connected.
