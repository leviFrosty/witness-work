title: How do I log time or plan a specific day?
category: time
---

Tap the day to open it, then add time or a plan there.

## Open a day

Tap the day on the **Schedule** tab, in **This Week** on Home, or under **All Days** on the **Progress** tab. The day opens in the bottom half of the screen. Tap another day to switch to it.

## Log time

1. Open the day. Today and earlier days show the time form under **Time Reports**. For a later day, tap **Add Time** first.
2. Tap an amount such as 2h, tap − or + to change it by 15 minutes, or tap the time to type exact hours and minutes.
3. Change the Type or add a note if you like.
4. Tap **Add**.

## Plan a day

1. Open the day and tap **Plan Day** under **Plans**.
2. Add a title if you like.
3. Choose **One Time** or **Recurring**, and how long.
4. Tap **Add**.

## Good to know

- You can also choose a day while you add time. **Add Time** on Home shows the last seven days. Tap a day, or tap the date to pick any other day.
- If the day has a plan and no time yet, tap **Use** beside the planned time to fill in its length and Type.
- To log a plan you kept, touch and hold it and choose **Log as Time**.
- Tap an entry to change or delete it. See [How do I change or delete time I already logged?](/help/time/change-logged-time)
- To see more of the day, drag the bar at the top up or down. To close it, tap the X or drag it all the way down.
- Logging time shows up only if your status logs hours. Kingdom Publishers turn on **Log my hours** in **Settings → Profile** first.
