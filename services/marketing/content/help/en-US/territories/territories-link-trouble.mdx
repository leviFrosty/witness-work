The page says what's wrong. A link works until its time runs out or the person who shared it ends it.

## What the page says

- **"This Link Has Expired":** the link's time ran out. Ask for a new one if you're still working.
- **"This Link Has Ended":** whoever shared it ended it, returned the territory, or took the street back. Ask for a new link.
- **"This Link Doesn't Work":** part of the link is missing. Messaging apps sometimes cut long links. Copy the whole link, or scan the QR code instead.
- **"You're Offline":** connect to the internet to open the link. After it opens, what you record offline is sent when you're back online.
- **"Links Are Paused":** try again in a few minutes.
- **"None of this link's streets can be recorded right now":** the streets were handed off or taken back. Ask whoever shared the link.
- **"1 change wasn't saved. That street isn't shared with this link anymore."** or **"records from this browser weren't saved":** the link ended before your phone could send them. Tell whoever shared the link what you recorded.

## Good to know

- Scan the QR code with your phone's camera and tap the link it shows.
- Your browser keeps the link's records until it ends. Tap **Forget This Link** to delete them sooner. Records already sent stay with the group.

## If you shared the link

Open the territory and look under **Shared With**. A link that ended or expired shows when. To make a new one, tap **Share**, then **A Link or QR Code**. See [How do I share a territory with someone who doesn't have the app?](/help/territories/territories-guest-link)
