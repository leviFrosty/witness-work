---

Buddies igufasha guhuza n'abantu bagera kuri 50 mujyana mu murimo wo kubwiriza. Mubonana iminsi iteganyijwe, amasaha yo gutangira n'igihe cy'ibyumweru umunani biri imbere, n'ibimenyetso buri mugenzi asangiza. Ni ubuntu, kandi ikora kuri iPhone, iPad na Android.

## Fungura Buddies

1. Fungura **Gahunda**.
2. Kanda **Buddies** munsi y'umutwe wa **Gahunda**.
3. Ubwa mbere, rangiza intangiriro ukoresheje izina ry'umwirondoro wawe.

Akadomo kuri yo kerekana ko hari ikigutegereje, nk'ubusabe bwo kwemeza. Ushobora kandi kuyifungura mu **Mwirondoro** wawe. Kugira ngo wongereho umuntu, reba [Nongeraho nte mugenzi, kandi ni nde wemeza ubusabe?](/help/buddies/buddies-add).

## Kuri kalendari yawe

- Ifoto ya mugenzi cyangwa inyuguti ye ku munsi bivuga ko muzajyana mu murimo.
- Akadomo gato kerekana ko abandi bagenzi bateganya kujya. Mugenzi mushya nta tudomo yerekana kugeza ufunguye **Erekana kuri kalendari yanjye** kuri we munsi ya **Gusangiza n'imenyesha** kuri paji ye.
- Kanda umunsi uwo ariwo wose kugira ngo ubone bagenzi bose bazajya.
- Kuri iPhone na iPad, widget ya Kalendari yerekana ibimenyetso bimwe. Fungura porogaramu niba isa n'iyasigaye inyuma.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ushobora kongera igice cya **Hamwe** Ahabanza: kanda **Hindura Ekara y'ahabanza**, hanyuma **Ongeraho ibice**, hanyuma **Hamwe**. Gifite ubutumire bukitegereje n'ibyo wateguranye na bagenzi.
- Niba bagenzi bawe babura, vugurura WitnessWork kuri buri gikoresho cyawe.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Buddies ibura muri Gahunda:** vugurura WitnessWork.
- **Buddies isa n'itagaragara neza:** ntiboneka by'igihe gito. Bagenzi bawe wabitse bagumana kuri kalendari yawe. Ongera ugerageze nyuma.
- **Ivuga ko idashobora kwemeza iki gikoresho:** reba [Kuki WitnessWork ivuga ko idashobora kwemeza iki gikoresho?](/help/account/account-verification).
