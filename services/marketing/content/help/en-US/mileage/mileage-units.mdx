id: mileageUnits
title: Can I use kilometers, litres, or L/100 km?
category: mileage
---

Yes. Distance can be miles or kilometers, and fuel economy can be mpg (US), mpg (UK), L/100 km, or km/L.

## Change units

1. Open **Mileage**, then tap the gear for settings.
2. Tap **Units**.
3. Choose your distance and fuel economy units.

**Auto** follows your phone's region.

## Good to know

- Changing units only changes how numbers are shown. Your trips aren't altered.
- Fuel is priced per gallon with mpg (US), and per litre with every other choice.
- **Electric** (and any fuel you add as priced per kWh) is priced per kWh. Its fuel economy shows as mi/kWh, km/kWh, or kWh/100 km to match your choice.
- Currency follows your phone's region.
