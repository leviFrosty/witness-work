title: "Nasangiza ibintu bitandukanye na bagenzi batandukanye?"
---

Yego. **Igenamiterere rya Buddies** rireba bagenzi bawe bose, kandi ushobora guhindura buri gihinduka kuri mugenzi umwe gusa.

## Hindura igenamiterere kuri mugenzi umwe

1. Fungura **Buddies**, kanda akabuto ka menu, uhitemo **Igenamiterere rya Buddies**.
2. Kanda **Hitamo mugenzi** uhitemo mugenzi.
3. Hindura ibyo ushaka.

Cyangwa fungura paji ye, kanda ikaramu (**Edit Buddy**), hanyuma **Gusangiza n'imenyesha**.

Kuri mugenzi umwe ushobora guhitamo ifoto yawe, ubupayiniya, urukurikirane, ibimenyetso n'itungo, niba abona gahunda zawe, uko amasaha yo gutangira yerekanwa neza, niba gahunda ze zigaragara kuri kalendari yawe, n'**Imenyesha ryo Saba kwifatanya** n'**Imenyesha ry'ibimenyetso**.

## Subira ku igenamiterere risangiwe

Igenamiterere ry'uwo mugenzi gusa rigira igishushanyo cy'ibice kimuritse n'umwambi wo gusubiza inyuma iruhande rw'izina ryaryo. Kanda umwambi kugira ngo mugenzi akurikize **Bagenzi bose** nanone. Umwambi uri hejuru ubikora ku makuru ye yose.

## Byiza kumenya

- Guhindura igenamiterere muri **Bagenzi bose** bihindura kuri buri mugenzi udafite irye bwite.
- **Hagarika gusangiza** ireba bose, uko igenamiterere ryabo ryaba rimeze kose.
- Izina ryawe rijya kuri buri mugenzi buri gihe.
- Bagenzi ntibamenyeshwa ibyo uhisemo.
- Igenamiterere ry'ugusangiza rikora ku bikoresho byawe byose. Imenyesha rikora gusa kuri telefoni warishyizeho.
