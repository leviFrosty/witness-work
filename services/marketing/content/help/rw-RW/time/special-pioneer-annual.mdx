title: Ndi umupayiniya udasanzwe, kuki porogaramu inyereka intego y'umwaka?
---

Abapayiniya badasanzwe ntibagira intego y'umwaka. If yours appears, open Preferences → Publisher and turn off the annual goal. Your monthly goal remains in place and the annual bar on the Progress tab's Year sub-screen will be hidden.
