id: territoriesReturn
title: How do I return a territory, and what happens to its records?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

When you hand the card back, open the territory and tap Return Territory, then confirm. It moves to the returned territories, and WitnessWork deletes its house-by-house records (not-at-homes, Spoke results, and door pins). It keeps the streets, which streets were worked, and the do-not-calls with their address or pin and date.

If some streets aren't marked worked, the confirmation says how many, so you can mark them first. When every street you added is worked, the territory page asks "Is that the whole card?" and offers Return Territory, or Add Another Street.

**Pass the do-not-calls on:** open the returned territory and tap Copy List under Do Not Call, then paste the list wherever your congregation keeps them.

**If it was shared:** returning stops sharing for everyone, ends its guest links, and brings handed-off streets back to you. The people you shared it with keep a read-only copy until they remove it.

**Finding it again:** in the Territories tab, tap Show returned territories. Open one to see its streets and do-not-calls.

- **Move Back to Checked Out** reopens a territory you returned by mistake. Its house-by-house records don't come back.
- **Check Out Again** is for when you're given the territory again later. It starts fresh from today: worked dates and door counts are cleared, and the streets and do-not-calls are kept.

To remove a territory completely, open it, tap Edit, then Delete Territory. That deletes its streets, house-by-house records, and do-not-calls from your device.
