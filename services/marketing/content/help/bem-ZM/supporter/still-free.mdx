Ee, kabili cikalaba fyo inshita yonse. Ifibombelo fyonse ifikalamba fiyobweka kuli bonse: inshita na lipoti, imilimo mwaipangila, abantu no kubwelelamo, amapange, Buddies, na ma app ya Apple Watch na Wear OS.
