title: How do I share a territory with someone who doesn't have the app?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
platforms: [ios, android]
---

Make a guest link: open the territory, tap Share, choose Whole Territory or the streets to share, and tap A Link or QR Code. The link is made at once and lasts 1 day. To keep it longer, change Link Lasts on the link screen (up to 30 days from now); the same link and QR code keep working, and your choice is used for the next link you make. Send the link, or let them scan the QR code. It opens in their web browser, with no app and no sign-in.

One link is enough for a whole car group: everyone scans the same QR code or opens the same link on their own phone.

You need a connection to make a link. You don't need Buddies, and the person you send it to doesn't need the app or an account. The page shows them your profile name (your Buddies name, if you use Buddies) beside the territory; with no name set, it shows only the territory.

**What they see and do:** they type their name once, so the group sees who recorded each door, then pick the street they are on. Change Street switches to another one, and the page reopens on the street they last chose. Share shows the QR code on their own phone, so the next person in the car can scan it. They can record doors as Not at Home, Spoke, or Do Not Call, mark streets worked, and see everything on the link's streets, including do-not-calls and doors others recorded, until the link ends. They see only the link's streets, never your other streets or the card's photo. If they close the tab, opening the link again brings back their name, street and records. If they lose the connection, their browser keeps what they record and sends it when they're back online. With territory privacy mode on, the link lets them only mark streets worked and note do-not-calls.

**On your side:** the link screen shows the QR code, a short form of the link (tap it to see the whole link), Copy, Share, Link Lasts, who has joined with the link, and how long it has left. The link is also listed under Shared With on the territory's page; tap it to show the QR code again. Your other iPhones, iPads, and Android devices that sync with this one show the territory with the link and what guests record, and can show the QR code or end the link too.

**Ending a link:** it ends by itself when its time runs out; change Link Lasts before then to keep it going. To end it sooner, open it and tap End Link Now. Doors they already recorded stay. Returning the territory ends all its links, and taking back a handed-off street ends the links the buddy made for it.

A link covers up to 30 streets, and a territory can have 10 open links at a time. Handed-off streets aren't included in your links. The link holds a secret key that WitnessWork's server never sees, so share it only with people you want working the territory.
