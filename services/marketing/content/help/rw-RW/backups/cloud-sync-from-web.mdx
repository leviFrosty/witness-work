title: "Kuki telefoni yanjye yahinduye ikajya kuri Cloud Sync nyuma yo kwinjira kuri murandasi?"
---

Iyo winjiye kuri murandasi, igusaba gufungura [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync), kuko murandasi ikorana na Cloud Sync gusa. Niba uhisemo **Fungura**, telefoni yawe ihindukira kuri Cloud Sync ubutaha ufunguye WitnessWork kuri yo.

## Nta kintu gitakara

- Telefoni yawe ibika buri nyandiko na buri foto, ikabihuriza hamwe n'ibyari bisanzwe kuri Cloud Sync.
- Nta kintu wasibye kigaruka.
- Andi matelefoni yawe ari kuri konti imwe na yo arahinduka, buri imwe ubutaha ufunguye WitnessWork kuri yo.
- Inyandiko zawe ni ibanga. Ibikoresho byawe gusa ni byo bishobora kuzisoma.

## Subira inyuma

1. Kuri telefoni yawe, fungura **Igenamiterere → Guhuza**.
2. Hitamo iCloud (Google Drive kuri Android) cyangwa Zimya.

Telefoni yawe igumya ku buryo wahisemo. Murandasi ibaza bundi bushya ubutaha uyikoresheje.

## Niba itahindutse

- Reba ko telefoni yawe iri kuri murandasi kandi urangije gushyiraho WitnessWork, hanyuma wongere ufungure porogaramu.
- Niba murandasi ivuga **Vugurura WitnessWork kuri telefoni yawe**, vugurura porogaramu muri App Store cyangwa Google Play, hanyuma uyifungure.
- Ushobora buri gihe guhitamo Cloud Sync ubwawe muri **Igenamiterere → Guhuza**.
