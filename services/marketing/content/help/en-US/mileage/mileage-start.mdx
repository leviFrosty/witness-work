title: How do I turn mileage tracking on or off?

Mileage tracking is optional and available to every publisher. Turn it on, add a car, then log your trips.

## Turn on mileage

1. On Home, answer **Yes** on the **Track mileage?** card. Or open **Settings → Profile** and turn on **Track Mileage**.
2. Add your first car. Only a name is required.

## Log a trip

1. Tap **Log Trip** on Home's Mileage card, on the **Mileage** screen, or from the Add button.
2. Enter the distance. Turn on **Round Trip** to double it. Or switch to **Odometer** and enter the start and end readings. **Odometer Start** fills in from that car's last reading.
3. Save the trip.

Tap a trip to see its details. Touch and hold it to edit, log it again today, share, or delete it.

## Turn it off

Turn off **Track Mileage** in **Settings → Profile**. This hides the Home card, **Log Trip**, and the **Mileage** screen, but keeps your trips. Turn it back on and everything returns. If you chose **No Thanks** on the Home card, this switch turns it on anytime.

To hide only the Home card, use **Settings → Features → Home Screen**.

## Erase your mileage

Open **Mileage → Settings → Delete All Mileage Data…**. This deletes your own cars, fuels, and trips on every device that syncs with this one, and can't be undone. Shared cars are left, and you keep a copy of each in your cars.
