title: "Nakoresha nte umurongo wa menu kuri iPad?"
---

Kuri iPad, umurongo wa menu uri hejuru ya ecran ugufasha vuba kongeramo gusura, kongeramo umuntu, gushaka umuntu no kubona ubufasha.

## Erekana umurongo wa menu

1. Koresha Windowed Apps cyangwa Stage Manager kuri iPadOS 26 cyangwa irenga.
2. Manura uhereye ku nkombe yo hejuru ya ecran.

## Ibirimo

- **Dosiye:** Andika gusura, Shyiramo igihe (iyo wandika amasaha), Ongeraho aderesi, Gahunda nshya, na Kubika & Kugarura.
- **Hindura → Shaka umuntu:** ifungura Contacts aho gushakisha hateguye.
- **Ubufasha:** ifungura Ikigo cy'ubufasha, Ni ibihe bintu bishya?, porogaramu yawe y'imeri y'ubufasha bw'ibanga, na Reba ibishya.
- **Igenamiterere rya WitnessWork:** rifungura igenamiterere rya WitnessWork. Ikintu gisanzwe cya **Igenamiterere** gifungura igenamiterere rya iPad yawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ibi bintu bifungura ecran zimwe n'iza porogaramu. Gufungura ifishi nta kintu bibika.
- Umurongo wa menu nta kuri iPhone cyangwa Android.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Hari ibyabuze:** vugurura WitnessWork muri App Store uyifungure nanone. Ushobora kandi gukora ibyo bintu bimwe muri porogaramu.
- **Biracyanze:** koresha ihuza ry'imeri y'ibanga ryo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha, kandi ntushyire amakuru y'abantu na backup muri raporo rusange.
