title: How do I choose where my route starts and finishes, or save Home and my Kingdom Hall?
category: plans
---

Tap the place under **Start** at the top of the route, and the place under **Finish** at the bottom. You can also save Home and your Kingdom Hall so they are one tap away.

## Choose where you start

1. Tap the place under **Start**.
2. Choose your current location, the first stop, Home, your Kingdom Hall, one of the day's plans that has a place, or search for any place.
3. Tap the time to change when you leave.

If a plan with a place starts before your first follow-up, such as a meeting for field service, the route starts there at that time.

## Choose where you finish

1. Tap the place under **Finish**.
2. Choose the last stop (the default), where you started, Home, your Kingdom Hall, or search for a place.

The finish isn't a stop, so no time is spent there. The drive to it is added, and the finish time is when you'd get there.

## Save Home or your Kingdom Hall

1. In the **Start** or **Finish** menu, or in **Add Stop**, choose to set Home or your Kingdom Hall.
2. Search for the address, or drop a pin on the **Map** tab.

To change or remove one, touch and hold it in **Add Stop**.

## Good to know

- **Shortest Order** puts the stops in order from your start and keeps the finish last.
- Saved places stay on this phone and aren't synced.
