id: planJustHours
title: Nshobora guteganya amasaha nta gutoranya igihe?
---

Yego. Ku ifishi ya Gahunda, kanda buto Bimara igihe kingana iki? iruhande rw'itariki. Hitamo Amasaha gusa kugira ngo uteganye amasaha y'uwo munsi nta gihe, cyangwa Igihe cyuzuye kugira ngo uhitemo igihe utangirira n'igihe urangirira; amasaha arakusanywa kuri wowe. Gahunda nshya zifungukira muri ubwo buryo wakoresheje bwa nyuma, bityo uburyo busanzwe bwawe buba bwiteguye ubutaha. Gahunda ya Amasaha gusa igaragaza Igihe cyose ku Ingengabihe yawe kandi ibarwa ku ntego zawe nk'izindi Gahunda zose. Menyesha njyewe ifunguye, iguhumuriza saa 8:00 z'igitondo cy'uwo munsi aho kuba mbere y'igihe cyo gutangira. Bagenzi wawe utumira babona umunsi n'igihe imara, nta saha. Kugira ngo uyihe igihe nyuma, hindura Gahunda uhindukire kuri Igihe cyuzuye. Niba ikindi gikoresho cyawe kigikoresha verisiyo ishaje ya WitnessWork, kigaragaza Gahunda saa sita kugeza kivuguruwe.
