Ishyireho muri **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Spiritual Routine**. Ibi bikora kimwe kuri iPhone na Android.

## Shyiraho iminsi y'amateraniro yawe

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Spiritual Routine**.
2. Munsi ya **Meeting days**, hitamo umunsi w'iteraniro ryo mu cyumweru (kuva ku wa mbere kugeza ku wa gatanu).
3. Hitamo umunsi w'iteraniro ryo ku mpera z'icyumweru (ku wa gatandatu cyangwa ku cyumweru).

## Byiza kumenya

- **Midweek meeting prep** na **Weekend meeting prep** bigaragara ku ikarita ya Routine y'Ahabanza kuva ku minsi ibiri mbere ya buri teraniro kugeza ku munsi w'iteraniro. Nta minsi y'amateraniro, byombi bigaragara icyumweru cyose kugeza ubibaruyeho.
- Umufasha wa Gahunda akoresha iminsi imwe kandi agumya ibyo atanga bitaremereye kuri yo. Uyishyiraho rimwe gusa.
- Niba gahunda y'amateraniro yawe ihindutse, urugero mu gihe cy'uruzinduko rw'umugenzuzi w'akarere, ushobora kubarura imyiteguro yawe umunsi uwo ari wo wose w'icyo cyumweru ukoresheje ecran ya Spiritual Routine.
- Iminsi y'amateraniro yawe iri ku bikoresho byawe byose niba ukoresha guhuza.
