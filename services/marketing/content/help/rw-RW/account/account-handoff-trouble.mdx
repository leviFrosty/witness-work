title: "Bigenda bite iyo kwinjira ukoresheje QR code bidakora?"
---

Kora kode nshya kuri telefoni yawe ishaje ongere uyisikane. Buri kode ikora rimwe kandi imara iminota 10.

## Gerageza ibi

1. Kuri telefoni ishaje, kanda **Kora kode nshya**.
2. Reba ko telefoni zombi zifite murandasi.
3. Sikana kode nshya na telefoni nshya.
4. Reba ko telefoni zombi zerekana kode imwe y'imibare itandatu, hanyuma ukande **Emerera** kuri telefoni ishaje.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Kode ntizihura:** kanda **Ntiwemere** utangire bundi bushya. Hari undi muntu ushobora kuba yasikanye kode yawe.
- **Guhuza byacitse umaze gukanda YES:** komeza telefoni zombi kuri ecran. Telefoni nshya yerekana **Ongera ugerageze** kandi ikomeza ubwayo isubiye kuri murandasi.
- **Telefoni ishaje ivuga ko konti yawe itoherejwe:** reba murandasi yayo ukande **Emerera** nanone.
- **Telefoni nshya ntishobora gusikana:** mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe, emerera WitnessWork gukoresha kamera.
- **Telefoni nshya ntishobora kwiyemeza:** shyiraho WitnessWork uyikuye kuri App Store cyangwa Google Play. Reba [Kuki WitnessWork ivuga ko idashobora kwemeza iki gikoresho?](/help/account/account-verification).
- **Nta telefoni ishaje ugifite:** kuri telefoni nshya, jya kuri **Igenamiterere → Konti → Koresha urufunguzo rwo kugarura**. Reba [Nakora iki niba ntagishoboye kwinjira muri konti yanjye ya WitnessWork?](/help/account/lost-access).

Biracyanze? Vugana n'ubufasha ukoresheje Ikigo cy'ubufasha. Ntuzigere wohereza urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura ku muntu uwo ari we wese.
