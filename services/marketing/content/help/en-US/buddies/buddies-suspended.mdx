id: buddiesSuspended
title: Why does Buddies say it's been turned off for me?
category: buddies
---

WitnessWork can turn off Buddies for someone after a report of abuse, such as harassing a buddy. If you think it's a mistake, contact support and we'll turn it back on if the report was wrong.

## What happens while it's off

- Buddies can't send invites or share Plans.
- A Plan or Follow-up you invite buddies to isn't sent. The app tells you, and the Plan shows **Not sent** beside them. Change it once Buddies is back on to send it.
- You can still remove buddies and delete your Buddies data (**Buddies Settings → Delete My Buddies Data**).
- Your buddies aren't notified.

## Ask for help

1. Tap the support button in the header, under **Still need help?**.
2. Write to support and include your WitnessWork version.
