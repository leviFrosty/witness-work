---
id: buddiesMissingPlans
title: Why are a buddy's plans or invitations missing or out of date?
category: buddies
---

Check that the request has been confirmed and Show on my calendar is on for that buddy. Your buddy may have paused sharing, or stopped sharing plans with you; WitnessWork doesn't say when they do. A buddy who shares only the time of day or no start time shows Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Planned in place of a time. Calendar sharing covers only the next eight weeks. Both people need an internet connection to send and receive changes; open Buddies and pull down to refresh. Previously downloaded plans remain viewable offline, but new changes need a connection; Buddies says when you're offline, when it couldn't refresh, or when it can't verify this device. Seeing a buddy's calendar does not add their plans to yours. Turn on Buddies notifications in Buddies Settings and your device's notification settings, and check the Home notification bell for invitations even if no notification arrived. On Android, Buddies notifications need Google Play services; without them, changes still arrive when you open WitnessWork. The bell checks for new Buddies activity when you open it and when you return to WitnessWork; if it says it couldn't check, reconnect and tap Try Again.
