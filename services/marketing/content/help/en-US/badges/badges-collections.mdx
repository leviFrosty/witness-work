title: What does each badge count, and why didn't a month count?
category: badges
---

Sharing the Good News counts months where you logged time or marked that you shared. Year Round counts service years with at least 10 of those months. Reports Sent counts months whose report you sent from WitnessWork or marked as already sent. Ready to Go counts months with a Plan, once the Plan's day arrives. Kind Words counts months with a visit where you talked with someone; Not at Home visits don't count. Return Visits counts months you went back to someone you had visited before. Next Time counts months with a Follow-up that has a topic. Keeping in Touch counts the most different months you've talked with one person, in person, by phone, or by letter. Two by Two counts months a buddy went with you on a Plan or Follow-up, and shows only where Buddies is available. If a month didn't count: hours carried over by the rollover option don't count on their own, visits that came with a contact someone shared with you are theirs and don't count, a Plan counts only once its day arrives, and Two by Two needs a buddy who answered Going. One-time badges mark a first: your first Bible study, and your first buddy.
