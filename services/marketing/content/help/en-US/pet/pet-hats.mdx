You buy hats in the Shop with coins, which you earn by going out in the ministry. Your pet can wear one hat or none.

## Buy a hat

1. On **Home**, tap your pet's card, but not the pet itself.
2. Tap **Shop**.
3. Pick the **Hat** category.
4. Tap a hat to try it on, then tap **Buy**.

Your pet wears it right away. Hats cost from 100 coins for the Bow up to 3,200 for the Little fig tree. Read [How do I earn coins, and what can I buy in the Shop?](/help/pet/pet-shop) to learn how to earn coins.

## Change hats

1. Tap your pet's card.
2. Pick the **Hat** tab.
3. Tap a hat you own, or choose none.

A hat you own stays yours, even after **Change pet**. It says **New** until you pick it. Your pet wears it on Home, in its details, in shared pictures, in the Home Screen widget (iPhone and iPad), and when it visits your buddies. It takes the hat off while it rolls over or lies down.

## Rare hats

The five dearest hats have a blue frame. They move, change the room around your pet, and react when you pat your pet or earn coins.

- Fishbowl (1,200 coins): fish swim, and one leaps when you earn coins.
- Lighthouse (1,600): gulls circle by day, and a beam sweeps the room after dusk.
- Model railway (2,000): a little train loops the hill on your pet's head.
- Planetarium (2,600): stars fill the walls after dusk, with a shooting star now and then.
- Little fig tree (3,200): it grows from a sprout as you go out, over 50 days.

Pick a rare hat in the Shop to watch it react. The **Pat** and **New coins** buttons under its card play each reaction again. Other hats move too, and some have one small moving part. Update WitnessWork if you don't see these hats.

## Good to know

- To see night effects in the daytime, set the window to **Night** in your pet's details.
- With Reduce Motion on, moving hats stay still, and the **Pat** and **New coins** buttons don't show. They also don't move in shared pictures or the widget.
- Some hats are secrets. See [What are the secret hats and room items?](/help/pet/pet-secrets).

## If something goes wrong

- **A hat doesn't move:** check that Reduce Motion is off in your device's settings.
- **You bought a hat on a device that needs updating:** update WitnessWork. Until then, your pet shows without it.
- **Still wrong:** use **Still need help?** below this answer to contact support. Include your WitnessWork version, and leave out names and private notes.
