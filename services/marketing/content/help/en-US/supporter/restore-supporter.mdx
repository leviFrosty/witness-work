title: I already paid, but Supporter features or prices are missing. What should I do?
category: supporter
---

Your Supporter status belongs to your WitnessWork account. It shows on every device signed in to the same account, including WitnessWork on the web.

## Things to check

1. Check your internet connection. Prices and your status load by themselves once you're back online.
2. Open **Settings → Account** and check you're signed in to the account you bought with.
3. Check your phone's store is using the account you paid with.
4. Open **Settings → Become a supporter** and tap **Restore Purchases**. The web has no **Restore Purchases** button.

## Good to know

- A one-time Tip doesn't turn on Supporter features.
- If you bought while signed in to another WitnessWork account, sign in to the account you want, then tap **Restore Purchases**.
- Restoring purchases brings back Supporter access, not your service records.
- Syncing your devices doesn't depend on Supporter status.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Purchases Aren't Ready Yet":** check your connection and try again. It can show briefly after a device signs in to a different account.
- **No purchases found, prices don't load, or it still isn't recognized:** email support privately with your app version and what happened.
- If support asks for your account ID, open **Settings → About → App Info** and tap **Copy All Details**. Share it privately.
