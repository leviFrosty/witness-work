---
id: cloudSyncPhotoStorage
title: How much photo storage does Cloud Sync have?
category: backups
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Your account has 2 GB for Cloud Sync photos: your profile photo, contact photos and photos in notes. See how much you use in Settings → Account, under Photo Storage, on a device that uses Cloud Sync.

When it's full, Settings → Account says Storage full, and a new photo stays on the device that added it, marked “Not synced: storage full”. Photos that already synced aren't affected, and the rest of your data keeps syncing.

To make room:

1. Remove or replace photos you don't need: a contact's photo, your profile photo, or photos in notes. The space frees right away.
2. Open WitnessWork again. Photos that didn't sync upload on their own once there's room.

A deleted contact's photo keeps its space while the contact can still be restored. WitnessWork's server removes the files of deleted photos 30 days later.

One photo can be up to 25 MB. A larger one is marked “Not synced: photo too large” and stays on the device that added it; replace it with a smaller photo to sync it.

Photo storage applies to Cloud Sync only. iCloud and Google Drive photos use your own iCloud or Google storage.
