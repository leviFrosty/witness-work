id: backupRecovery
title: "Backup yanjye isa nk'itasomeka cyangwa inyandiko zanjye zagaruwe zirabura. Nakora iki?"
---

Dosiye ya backup ikorwa kugira ngo WitnessWork ibe ari yo iyisoma, bityo ntisa na raporo iyo uyifunguye. Bika dosiye y'umwimerere uko iri hanyuma uyigarure ukoresheje **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura → Garura ukoresheje backup**.

## Niba inyandiko zibura nyuma yo kugarura

1. Bika dosiye y'umwimerere ya backup. Ntuyihindure cyangwa ngo uyihindure izina.
2. Kora backup nshya y'ibiri kuri telefoni ubu: **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura → Kora Backup**.
3. Andikira ubufasha imeri ushyiremo verisiyo ya WitnessWork n'ibyabaye. Dosiye iyisangize mu ibanga.

## Byiza kumenya

- Dosiye za backup zishobora kubamo amakuru y'abantu n'inyandiko. Ntukazishyire mu bitekerezo by'abantu bose.
