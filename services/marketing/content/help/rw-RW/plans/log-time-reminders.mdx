title: "WitnessWork ishobora kunyibutsa kwandika igihe ku munsi nateganyije?"
---

Yego. Niba umunsi ufite gahunda ariko nta gihe cyanditswe, WitnessWork yohereza inyibutsa imwe nimugoroba.

## Ifungure

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Amamenyesha**.
2. Fungura **Unyibutse kwandika igihe**.
3. Hitamo igihe cya **Unyibutse saa**. Ni saa mbiri z'ijoro keretse uhindure.
4. Emera amamenyesha niba WitnessWork ibibajije.

Niba ubona iri genamiterere gusa nyuma yo gufungura **Andika amasaha yanjye**, ni ibisanzwe: rigaragara iyo ukurikirana amasaha.

## Byiza kumenya

- Inyibutsa iza nyuma y'uko gahunda z'umunsi zirangiye. Gahunda iri gukomeza ku gihe cy'inyibutsa yawe ikwibutsa umunsi ukurikiyeho.
- Gahunda nyinshi ku munsi umwe zitanga inyibutsa imwe.
- Kanda inyibutsa kugira ngo ufungure **Shyiramo igihe** itariki, igihe giteganyijwe n'ubwoko bisanzwe byujujwe.
- Kwandika igihe icyo ari cyo cyose kuri uwo munsi bihagarika inyibutsa yawo. Kimwe no gusiba gahunda cyangwa kuzimya igenamiterere.
- Iminsi wasibye mbere yo kubifungura, na gahunda zidafite igihe, ntizibona inyibutsa.
- Inyibutsa inagaragara mu nzogera ya Ahabanza mu cyumweru.
- Inyibutsa zikorerwa kuri telefoni yawe ziva muri gahunda zawe bwite. Nta kintu cyoherezwa ahandi.
- Fungura WitnessWork nibura buri byumweru bibiri kugira ngo inyibutsa zikomeze kuza.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta nyibutsa zigera:** reba ko amamenyesha yemerewe WitnessWork mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe, kandi ko Focus cyangwa Do Not Disturb bizimye. Kuri Android, reba kandi icyiciro cya Reminders.
- **Inyibutsa yaje umaze kwandika igihe ku kindi gikoresho:** fungura WitnessWork kuri iyi telefoni kugira ngo ishobore kuyihagarika.
- **Android itinda iminota mike:** sisitemu ishobora gutinza inyibutsa kugira ngo ibike umuriro.
