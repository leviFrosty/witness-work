title: "Gushyira imodoka mu bubiko no kuyisiba bitandukaniye he?"
---

Gushyira imodoka mu bubiko biyihisha ingendo nshya ariko bigakomeza ingendo zayo muri raporo zawe. Gusiba bikuraho imodoka n'ingendo zayo zose, kandi ntibisubizwa inyuma.

## Shyira imodoka mu bubiko cyangwa uyisibe

1. Fungura **Ibirometero**, hanyuma **Igenamiterere ry'ibirometero** (uruziga).
2. Kanda imodoka.
3. Hitamo **Shyira mu bubiko** cyangwa **Siba…**.

Kugira ngo ukoreshe imodoka yabitswe nanone, fungura **Imodoka zabitswe** uyikure mu bubiko.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gusiba imodoka kandi biyikuraho ku bikoresho byose bihuzwa n'iki.
- Imodoka isangiwe ntishobora gusibwa. Kuyishyira mu bubiko biyihisha ingendo nshya kuri buri wese uri ku modoka. Kugira ngo uhagarike kuyikoresha wowe, uyivemo. Reba [Bigenda bite iyo mvuye ku modoka isangiwe, ngakurwaho, cyangwa gusangiza bigahagarara?](/help/mileage/shared-cars-leave).
- Gusiba lisansi bikuraho amateka y'ibiciro byayo, bityo imodoka iyikoresha ihagarika kwerekana igiciro giteganyijwe.
