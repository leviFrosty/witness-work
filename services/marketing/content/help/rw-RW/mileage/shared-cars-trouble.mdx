title: "Kuki imodoka yanjye isangiwe itavugururwa, cyangwa kuki ntashobora gusangiza cyangwa kwifatanya?"
---

Impinduka ku modoka isangiwe zigera kuri bose mu masegonda porogaramu zabo zifunguye. Impinduka ukora nta murandasi ziguma kuri telefoni yawe kandi zisangizwa usubiye kuri murandasi.

## Reba impinduka nonaha

Manura kuri ecran ya **Ibirometero**.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

Ikimenyetso gito hejuru ya **Ibirometero** kivuga impamvu impinduka zidashobora gusangizwa. Impinduka zawe ziguma kuri telefoni yawe kugeza zishobora.

- **Nta murandasi ufite:** zisangizwa usubiye kuri murandasi.
- **Vugurura WitnessWork:** shyiraho ivugurura rivuye kuri App Store cyangwa Google Play.
- **Impinduka zawe ntizisangizwa ubu:** konti yawe yarabujijwe. Niba utekereza ko ari ikosa, vugana n'ubufasha.
- **Shared cars aren't available right now:** ntiziboneka by'igihe gito. Impinduka zawe zisangizwa zimaze kugaruka.
- **Check its date and time:** isaha ya telefoni yawe iri imbere. Shyira itariki n'isaha kuri byikora mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe.
- **Shorten long trip notes:** impinduka imwe ni nini cyane ku buryo idasangizwa. Gabanya inyandiko y'urugendo.
- **A shared car isn't ready on this device yet:** impinduka zawe zisangizwa iyo ibonetse.

## Ibibazo byo gusangiza cyangwa kwifatanya

- **Nta kabuto ka Share Car…, cyangwa nta bagenzi batumirwa:** ukeneye Buddies ishyizweho n'umugenzi umwe nibura. Niba utabona **People** mu igenamiterere ry'imodoka, vugurura WitnessWork.
- **"This car already has the most people it can.":** imodoka ifite abantu 10, harimo n'ubutumire bufunguye. Uwohereje ubutumire, cyangwa nyirayo, ashobora kububukuraho, kandi nyirayo ashobora gukuraho umuntu.
- **Ubutumire bwarangiye cyangwa bwabuze:** ubutumire bumara iminsi 7 bufunguye. Saba umuntu uri ku modoka ubundi bushya.
- **"This note is too long to share on this car.":** gabanya inyandiko kugira ngo ubike urugendo.
- **"Someone who joined" muri People:** kwifatanya k'uwo muntu ntikwarangiye. Nyirayo ashobora kumukuraho, kandi ashobora gutumirwa nanone.
- **Ntushobora kuva ku modoka, cyangwa Delete My Buddies Data ntirangira:** kuva bikeneye murandasi. Ongera ugerageze uhuye.

Niba ikibazo gikomeje, vugana n'ubufasha bw'ibanga munsi ya **Uracyakeneye ubufasha?** ukoresheje akabuto k'ubufasha kari mu mutwe w'iyi ecran, ushyiremo verisiyo ya porogaramu. Ntushyire amazina y'imodoka, inyandiko z'ingendo n'amazina y'abantu muri raporo rusange.
