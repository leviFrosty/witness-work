title: Is WitnessWork still free?
category: supporter
---

Yes, and it always will be. Every core feature stays free for everyone.

## What's free

- Time and reports, goals, contacts and return visits, and plans.
- Buddies, and the Apple Watch and Wear OS apps.
- Syncing your devices, with Cloud Sync, iCloud Sync or Google Drive Sync.

## What Supporter adds

Supporter is an optional subscription that helps fund development. As a thank-you, it adds Plan Today's Route, higher Scribe AI limits, and personal touches like accent colors, app icons on iPhone and iPad, and buddy colors.

Thank you to everyone who supports WitnessWork. Your support built Cloud Sync and WitnessWork on the web.
