---
id: copyLastWeekPlans
title: Can I copy last week’s plans to this week?
category: plans
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Yes. WitnessWork copies each one-time plan from last week to the same weekday this week.

## Copy last week's plans

1. On the **Schedule** tab, touch and hold any day of the week you want to fill. On the web, right-click it.
2. Tap **Copy Last Week’s Plans**.

You can also find this in a day under This Week on Home, or in the Progress tab's list of days. Update WitnessWork if you don't see it.

## Good to know

- Each copy keeps its start time, length, Type, title, place, note, and reminder.
- Recurring plans already repeat, so they aren't copied. Plans you joined through a buddy's invitation aren't copied either.
- Buddies you invited aren't invited to the copies. Open a copy and tap **Invite Buddies** to invite them again.
- Days that have passed, your Off Days, and days that already have a plan at the same time are skipped. Copying twice doesn't make duplicates.
- Weeks begin on your Start of Week, set in **Settings → Preferences → Region & Formats**.
- Copies are ordinary plans. Delete the ones you don't need.

## If something goes wrong

- **You didn't want the copies:** tap **Undo** on the message that appears.
- **Nothing was copied:** WitnessWork tells you when there is nothing left to copy.
