---
id: cloudSyncSwitch
title: Nshani ukucinja pa Cloud Sync na iCloud nelyo Google Drive?
---

Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Sync** elyo musale Cloud Sync, iCloud (Google Drive pa Android), nelyo Off. Fyonse fitatu fya bwamba.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ukucinja kusunga fyonse pa cibombelo ici elyo kulifilunganya ne fibombelo fyenu pa nshila ipya. Takuli cipyaniwa, elyo kunyina cintu mwafumyepo icibwela.
- Ukufuma pa Cloud Sync kufumyapo kopi ya cibombelo ici ku server ya WitnessWork. Ifibombelo fyenu fimbi fisunga ifyo fyasync kale.
- Ukufuma pa iCloud nelyo Google Drive kushiya kopi ya kulekelesha ya cibombelo ici uko. Ifibombelo fyenu fimbi fyatwalilila ukusync fye bene, lelo te na ici.
- Ukusync ifibombelo fyonse pamo, saleni inshila imo pa fyonse.
- Ifikope fyaya ne Cloud Sync lwa mwine. Na iCloud nelyo Google Drive, washeni **Ingiya ifikope mu sync**.
- WitnessWork pa web ibomfya fye Cloud Sync, kanshi ukuyibomfya kufwaya foni yenu pa Cloud Sync.
