The bell at the top of Home collects reminders and updates in one place, so they don't pile up on Home.

## What you'll find there

- Last month's Service Report and a pending time rollover
- Backup and data protection reminders
- Missed Follow-ups and reminders that already went off
- New badges and new items for your pet
- Buddies activity, where Buddies is available
- What's New
- A new sign-in, an approved browser, or a passkey added on another of your devices. Tap it to see your signed-in devices or passkeys. See [How do I see which devices use my account?](/help/account/account-activity).
- Occasional offers to turn on Cloud Sync or save a passkey

## Use the bell

- Tap an item to act on it.
- Tap **X** to dismiss an item, or **Clear All** to dismiss them all. A dismissed item doesn't come back.
- Invitations and requests waiting for your answer stay until you respond.

## Good to know

- The red number counts items you haven't seen yet.
- News from your buddies sits at the bottom under **From your buddies**. It never adds to the red number. A small dot on the bell means some of it is new.
- If you tap **Not Now** on an offer, it can come back at least 30 days later. After two times, it stops.
- Which items you've seen or dismissed is remembered on this device only.
