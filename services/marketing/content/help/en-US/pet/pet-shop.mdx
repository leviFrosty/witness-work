You earn gold coins by going out in the ministry, and spend them in the Shop on hats and room items. Coins never cost real money, and being a Supporter doesn't give you any.

## Open the Shop

1. On **Home**, tap your pet's card, but not the pet itself.
2. Tap **Shop** beside your coins.

You can also touch and hold the card's name and choose **Shop…**, or tap **More in Shop** at the end of a tab in the details.

## Buy something

1. Pick a category: **Hat**, **Wall**, **Floor**, **Bed**, **Shelf**, **Light**, or **Study**.
2. Tap an item to try it on in the room picture.
3. Tap **Buy**. The price is on the button.

The hat or item goes on right away. If you don't have enough coins, the button says how many more you need, like **Need 30 more**. Something you own says **Owned**. You put it on in your pet's details. Things open from the start are free and aren't in the Shop.

## How you earn coins

- 10 coins for each day you go out in the ministry since you adopted your pet. A day counts when you log time (even 0 minutes, if you mark that you shared) or log a visit. A day earns once, however many records you add.
- 10 more for each planned day you keep: a day you made a plan for and then logged time on, even less than you planned.
- 100 for each streak milestone, like 3, 5 or 10, while streaks are on in **Settings → Features → Streaks & Badges**.
- A month in which a Kingdom Publisher marks that they shared earns at least 80 coins.
- Spiritual Routine activities earn 4 to 5 coins each, and 500 for a finished reading plan.

Plans alone don't count. Time carried over by Automatic time rollover doesn't count. Neither do visits that came with a contact someone shared with you. Coins count from the day you adopted your pet.

## What things cost

- Hats and plain room items cost from 100 to 360 coins.
- Moving room items and the newer hats cost from 620 up to 3,200 coins. See [How do I get hats for my pet?](/help/pet/pet-hats) and [How do I decorate my pet's room?](/help/pet/pet-room).
- Secret items are never sold. See [What are the secret hats and room items?](/help/pet/pet-secrets).
- Prices can change in a later update. You never pay again for something you own.

## Good to know

- When you save time or a visit that earns coins, the amounts appear on screen and the total drops into your pet's coins. Tap anywhere to skip it.
- To turn off the animation or sounds, go to **Settings → Features → Rewards**, or tap the settings button at the top right of your pet's details.
- Once you buy an item, it's yours for good, even after **Change pet**.
- Coins are worked out from your records, so your devices agree and they come back after a restore. If you edit or delete old records, your coins are worked out again, and what you bought stays yours.

## If something goes wrong

- **Your coins didn't go up:** check that you logged time or a visit (not only a plan) on a day after you adopted your pet, that you hadn't already gone out that day, and that your devices finished syncing.
- **Still wrong, or Buy doesn't work:** use **Still need help?** below this answer. Include your WitnessWork version and what you expected, and leave out names and private notes.
