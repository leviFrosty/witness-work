title: How do I tell my passkeys apart, and how do I remove one?
category: account
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Each WitnessWork passkey is named "WitnessWork" with a 4-character code, such as WitnessWork 7K4Q. The same code shows in **Settings → Account → Passkeys**, so you can match them up.

## See your passkeys

Open **Settings → Account → Passkeys**. It lists each passkey with the device it was added on, when it was added and when it was last used. Each one is marked:

- **Can unlock your account:** signing in with it brings your account to a new browser or phone.
- **Sign-in only:** it signs you in, then asks you to finish with your other phone or your recovery key. After that, it may be able to unlock next time.

On a phone, tap a **Sign-in only** passkey and choose **Let It Unlock** to try again. Some password managers can only sign in.

## Remove a passkey

1. Open **Settings → Account → Passkeys**.
2. Tap the passkey.
3. Choose **Remove**.
4. Confirm with Face ID, your fingerprint or your passcode. On the web, confirm with one of your passkeys instead.

## Good to know

- Removing it here doesn't delete it from your password manager. Delete it there too.
- WitnessWork warns you before you remove the last passkey that can unlock your account.
- You can't rename a passkey.
- If the bell on Home says a passkey was added and it wasn't you, remove it and change your account key. See [What should I do if my phone or computer is lost or stolen?](/help/account/lost-device).
- Before you remove your only way in, check that you have your recovery key. See [What is my recovery key, and how do I keep it safe?](/help/account/account-recovery-key).
