id: scribeUsage
title: How do Scribe AI limits work?
category: scribe
---

Scribe AI usage depends on how much it reads and writes, not on how many messages you send. A quick update uses a little; long conversations and paper pages use more. To see how much of each limit you’ve used, as a percentage, and when it resets, tap the percentage at the top of Scribe AI or open Settings → Scribe AI → Usage. Without Supporter, you have a monthly limit that starts with your first use and resets 30 days later. Supporters get much higher limits: a session limit that resets 5 hours after it starts, and a weekly limit that resets 7 days after it starts. If your Supporter subscription ends, the monthly limit applies again, counting what you’ve already used in it. When you reach a limit, Scribe AI tells you when you can use it again, and anything it was already working on still finishes. You can always add time, contacts and plans yourself. Reading paper pages counts toward the same limits, and you can read a limited number of pages in any 24 hours.
