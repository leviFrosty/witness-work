title: Why won't a territory guest link or QR code open, or why does it say it ended or expired?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

A guest link works for 24 hours after it's made, until the person who shared it ends it. The page says what's wrong:

- **"This Link Has Expired":** links last 24 hours. Ask for a new one if you're still working.
- **"This Link Has Ended":** whoever shared it ended it, returned the territory, or took the street back. Ask them for a new link if you're still working.
- **"This Link Doesn't Work":** part of the link is missing. Messaging apps sometimes cut long links, so copy the whole link, or scan the QR code instead. If it still doesn't open, ask for a new one.
- **"You're Offline":** connect to the internet to open the link. Once it's open, what you record while offline is sent when you're back online.
- **"Links Are Paused":** recording with links is paused for a while. Try again in a few minutes.
- **"None of this link's streets can be recorded right now":** the streets were handed off or taken back. Ask whoever shared the link.
- **"1 change wasn't saved. That street isn't shared with this link anymore."** or **"records from this browser weren't saved":** the link or street ended before your browser could send them. Tell whoever shared the link what you recorded.

The link opens in a web browser, with no app or sign-in needed. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera and tap the link it shows. Your browser keeps the link's records only until it ends; tap Forget This Link to delete them sooner. Records already sent stay with the group.

**If you shared the link:** open the territory and look under Shared With. A link that ended or expired shows when it ended. Tap the territory's Share button and A Link or QR Code to make a new one (see [How do I share a territory with someone who doesn't have the app?](/help/territories/territories-guest-link)).
