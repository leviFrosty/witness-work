Open the **Territories** tab. **Insights** at the top shows the day and hour when people most often answered across all your territories.

## See your Insights

1. Open the **Territories** tab and tap the **Insights** card. Each territory also has its own **Insights** card on its page.
2. Look at the three charts. Each has a heading such as "Saturday is your best day".
   - **Time of Day:** how often someone answered at each hour.
   - **Day of the Week:** how often someone answered on each day.
   - **Day and Time:** each day and hour compared with your average. Greener squares mean more answers, orange means fewer. Gray means fewer than 3 doors.
3. Touch a bar or square, or drag across a chart, to see the answer rate and how many doors it's based on.

With doors in more than one territory, **By Territory** at the bottom lists each one's best day and time.

## Good to know

- Insights need about 40 recorded doors, for all territories together and for each territory alone. Until then the card says **Check back later** and shows how many more to record. You get a notification when there are enough.
- Every door recorded as **Spoke** or **Not at Home** counts, including doors others recorded on a territory you share. A door you remove or undo doesn't count. Results added from notes don't count.
- Recent months count the most, so Insights follow changes like the clocks going back.
- Each house in a street's list shows when it was last recorded, so you can try a not-at-home at a different time.
- After you return a territory, Insights keep only how many doors were answered at each day and hour. These counts stay on this phone and aren't backed up.
- With territory privacy mode on, no house records are kept, so nothing new is counted.
