title: How do Scribe AI limits work?
category: scribe
---

Scribe AI has a limit on how much you can use it. Reading and writing more uses more, so a quick update uses a little and a long conversation uses more. You can always add time, contacts and plans yourself.

## See how much you've used

Tap the percentage at the top of Scribe AI, or open **Settings → Scribe AI → Usage**. It shows how much of each limit you've used and when it resets.

## Your limits

- **Without Supporter:** a weekly limit that starts with your first use and resets 7 days later. It's enough for a few quick updates a week. Your first week has a higher limit so you can bring in your notes.
- **With Supporter:** much higher limits. A session limit resets 5 hours after it starts, and a weekly limit resets 7 days after it starts.
- If your Supporter subscription ends, the free weekly limit applies again, counting what you've already used that week.

## Good to know

- When you reach a limit, Scribe AI tells you when you can use it again. Anything it was already working on still finishes.
- On phones, reading paper pages counts toward the same limits. You can read a limited number of pages in any 24 hours.
- The limits are the same on the web and on your phones.
