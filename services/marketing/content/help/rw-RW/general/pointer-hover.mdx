title: Nakoresha nte trackpad, mouse cyangwa Apple Pencil kuri iPad?
---

Huza trackpad cyangwa mouse kuri iPad yawe, cyangwa wegereze Apple Pencil ishyigikiwe hejuru gato ya ecran. Utubuto, imirongo n'iminsi y'ikalendari bigaragara cyane mu gihe indanga ibinyuzeho. Iyo umurongo w'ibumoso wagabanyijwe ukaba ufite amashusho gusa, gushyira indanga ku shusho bigaragaza izina ryayo; utundi tubuto tudafite inyandiko, nko ku bikoresho by'ikarita no Amamenyesha, na two tugaragaza amazina yatwo. Shyira indanga ku rukuta rw'ibikorwa, ku gishushanyo cy'umuvuduko w'umwaka w'umurimo, cyangwa ku nkingi y'ibirometero kugira ngo ubone itariki n'agaciro byayo. Ku ikarita, shyira indanga ku kimenyetso kugira ngo ubone izina ry'umuntu, cyangwa ku muntu uri ku rutonde rw'uruhande rw'ikarita kugira ngo ubone ikimenyetso cye. Gukanda kwa kabiri (gukanda n'intoki ebyiri) bifungura menu n'igaragaza rimwe nko gukanda ugakomeza. Umurongo wo guhindura ubugari bw'umurongo w'ibumoso ugaragaza imyambi yerekeza aho ushobora kwimukira, kandi gukurura n'intoki ebyiri bimura ifoto y'umuntu mu gihe uyikata. Byose biracyashoboka ukoresheje gukoraho: kanda, kanda ukomeze, cyangwa kurura nk'ibisanzwe. Gushyira indanga nta cyo bihindura ku nyandiko zawe. Hapimwa gusa ko indanga cyangwa Pencil byakoreshejwe, inshuro imwe ku munsi nibura; nta myanya, amazina cyangwa agaciro byoherezwa. Kuri Android, mouse igaragaza imirongo, amazina n'agaciro ku bishushanyo, ariko ibimenyetso ku ikarita ntibigaragaza amazina iyo indanga ibiriho. iPhone nta ndanga ifite. Niba nta kintu gisubiza, reba ko trackpad, mouse cyangwa Pencil bihujwe, ushyire porogaramu ku gihe maze uyifungure bundi bushya. Ku bindi bibazo, koresha aho ubufasha bwa bwite buri mu Kigo cy'ubufasha kandi ntushyire amakuru bwite mu bitekerezo rusange.
