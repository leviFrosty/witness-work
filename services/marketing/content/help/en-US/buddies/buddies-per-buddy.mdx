title: Can I share different things with different buddies?
category: buddies
---

Yes. **Buddies Settings** applies to all your buddies, and you can change any setting for just one.

## Change a setting for one buddy

1. Open **Buddies**, tap the menu button, and choose **Buddies Settings**.
2. Tap **Choose a buddy** and pick the buddy.
3. Change what you want.

Or open their page, tap the pencil (**Edit Buddy**), then **Sharing & Alerts**.

For one buddy you can choose your photo, pioneer status, streak, badges and pet, whether they see your plans, how exactly start times show, whether their plans show on your calendar, and their **Ask to Join Alerts** and **Badge Alerts**.

## Go back to the shared setting

A setting that is theirs alone has a highlighted layers icon and an undo arrow beside its name. Tap the arrow to make that buddy follow **All Buddies** again. The arrow at the top does it for all of their settings.

## Good to know

- Changing a setting in **All Buddies** changes it for every buddy who doesn't have their own.
- **Pause sharing** covers everyone, whatever their own settings.
- Your name always goes to every buddy.
- Buddies aren't told about any of these choices.
- Sharing settings apply on all your devices. Alerts apply only to the phone you set them on.
