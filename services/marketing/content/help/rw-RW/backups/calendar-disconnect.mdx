title: "Bigenda bite iyo nzimije guhuza kalendari cyangwa nkakuramo WitnessWork?"
---

Fungura **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Guhuza kalendari** ukande **Zimya guhuza kalendari**. WitnessWork ibaza niba ubika ibyabaye bisanzwe muri kalendari yawe cyangwa ubikuraho.

## Hitamo ibigenda ku byabaye

- **Komeza ibikorwa:** ibyabaye bigumya muri kalendari yawe, ariko ntibikivugururwa.
- **Kuraho ibikorwa:** WitnessWork ikuraho ibyabaye byayo muri kalendari. Ibindi byabaye byawe na kalendari ubwayo bisigara.

Ushobora kandi gukanda **Zimya** ku butumwa "Kuvugurura kalendari byahagaze".

## Byiza kumenya

- Kuri iPhone na iPad, kuyizimya ku gikoresho kivugurura kalendari yawe bihagarika kuvugurura kuva ku bikoresho byawe byose. Ku bindi bikoresho byawe, bizima kuri icyo gikoresho gusa, kandi ibyabaye bigasigara.
- Gukuraho WitnessWork ntibikuraho ibyabaye muri kalendari. Ubikureho mbere niba utabishaka.
- Ushobora gusiba kalendari ya WitnessWork utagikoresha muri porogaramu yawe ya kalendari, umaze kureba ko nta kintu ushaka irimo.
- Kuyizimya ntibisiba na rimwe Abantu, Gusura cyangwa Gusubirayo byawe muri WitnessWork.
