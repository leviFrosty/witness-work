title: "Ngarura nte agace, kandi inyandiko zako zigenda zite?"
---

Iyo usubije ikarita, fungura agace, kanda **Garura agace**, hanyuma wemeze. Agace kajya mu duce wagaruye.

## Inyandiko zako zigenda zite

- Zisibwa: inyandiko z'inzu ku yindi (abatari mu rugo, ibyabaye bya **Twaganiriye**, n'udukadomo tw'inzu).
- Zibikwa: imihanda, imihanda yabwirijwe, na "Ntibasurwe" hamwe na aderesi cyangwa akadomo n'itariki.

Niba imihanda imwe itagaragajwe ko yabwirijwe, ubwemeza buvuga umubare, kugira ngo ubanze uyigaragaze. Iyo imihanda yose yabwirijwe, urupapuro rubaza ngo "Ese iyo ni ikarita yose?" rugatanga **Garura agace** cyangwa **Ongeraho undi muhanda**.

## Ohereza "Ntibasurwe"

1. Fungura agace wagaruye.
2. Kanda **Koporora urutonde** munsi ya **Ntibasurwe**.
3. Manika urutonde aho itorero ryanyu rubika.

## Ongera ukibone

Mu gice cya **Uduce**, kanda **Erekana uduce twagarutse** ufungure kimwe.

- **Subiza muri Ibyafashwe** gifungura agace wagaruye ku bw'impanuka. Inyandiko z'inzu ntizigaruka.
- **Ongera ufate** ni iyo bakongeye guha agace. Gitangira bundi bushya uhereye uyu munsi. Imihanda na "Ntibasurwe" bibikwa.

## Byiza kumenya

- Iyo cyari gisangijwe, kugarura bihagarika gusangiza kuri bose, birangiza imirongo yacyo y'abashyitsi, kandi bigarura imihanda yashyikirijwe. Abo wagisangije bagumana kopi yo gusoma gusa kugeza bayikuyeho.
- Kugira ngo ukureho agace burundu, ugafungure, ukande **Hindura**, hanyuma **Siba agace**. Ibi bisiba imihanda, inyandiko, na "Ntibasurwe" bya ko kuri telefoni yawe.
