title: Can I import data from MyTime, Ministry Assistant, or another app?
category: backups

MyTime backups are supported. In MyTime, go to More → Settings → Backup and email yourself a backup, then save the attached .mytimedb file on your device. On Android or the web, send it from MyTime on your iPhone and download the attachment on that device. On the web, WitnessWork reads the file inside your browser; it isn't uploaded, and a file over 25 MB is refused. Open Settings → Import Data → From MyTime, or choose From MyTime during setup, choose that file, and review the preview before importing. Other apps' backup formats are not automatically supported. If you can export readable text, paste it into Scribe AI and review what it adds. Review its proposed records and warnings before saving. WitnessWork JSON backups belong in Settings → Backup & Restore instead. To bring in data from Notes or paper later, use Settings → Import Data.
