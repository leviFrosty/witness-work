Install the full, free WitnessWork app on your Android phone straight from Chrome. It adds a WitnessWork icon to your Home screen and opens in its own window, just like any other app.

Use these steps if WitnessWork isn't in Google Play where you live, including the United States.

## Installation instructions

In the pictures, a red box shows what to tap.

1. Open **Chrome** on your Android phone and go to **app.witnesswork.app**. Wait for the **Sign In to WitnessWork** screen to load.
2. Tap Chrome's menu (the three dots at the top right), then tap **Add to Home screen**. Some phones show **Install app** instead.

![Chrome's menu with Add to Home screen](android-web-app-1.webp)

3. Tap **Install**. If your phone asks again, tap **Add to home screen**.

![The Install app box with the Install button](android-web-app-2.webp)

4. Close the Chrome tab, then open **WitnessWork** from your Home screen or app list.

![The WitnessWork icon on the Home screen](android-web-app-3.webp)

5. WitnessWork opens in its own window. Tap **Create an Account** if you're new, or sign in if you already use WitnessWork. See [bring your records into the web app](/help/general/web-app-move-records).

![WitnessWork's sign-in screen in its own window](android-web-app-4.webp)

You may also see an **Install App** banner at the top of the page in Chrome. Tapping **Install** there does the same thing as steps 2 and 3.

## Good to know

- Always open WitnessWork from its Home screen icon. Your records are saved on your phone, so it works offline and syncs when you're back online.
- Updates arrive automatically. When a new version is ready, tap **Reload**. See [getting the latest version](/help/general/web-updates).
- Reminders and phone notifications aren't available in the web app. See [what the web app doesn't have](/help/general/web-app).
- Don't clear Chrome's site data for app.witnesswork.app. That signs you out on this phone and removes any changes that haven't synced yet.
- Using an iPhone or iPad? See [using WitnessWork without the App Store](/help/general/iphone-web-app).

## If something goes wrong

- **It says WitnessWork Is Open in Another Tab:** tap **Use Here**, then close WitnessWork in Chrome. WitnessWork runs in one window at a time.
- **There's no Add to Home screen or Install app in the menu:** see [Why can't I install WitnessWork on my phone?](/help/general/web-install-trouble).
