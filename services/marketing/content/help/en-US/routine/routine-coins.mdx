category: routine

If you have a pet companion, the things you check off in Spiritual Routine earn it coins to spend in the Shop. Without a pet, no coins show, and you can still use Spiritual Routine.

## What each activity earns

- Bible reading: 4 coins for each day you check it.
- Personal study: 4 coins for each day you check it.
- Midweek meeting prep: 5 coins each week.
- Weekend meeting prep: 5 coins each week.
- Family worship (or personal worship evening): 5 coins each week.
- Finishing a reading plan: 500 coins.

## Good to know

- Spiritual Routine earns coins only. Your pet's hearts still follow your ministry.
- Coins count from the day you adopted your pet. A day you check off later, back to the start of last week, earns the same.
- When you check something off, what it earned shows on the screen, like **+4 Bible reading**. To turn that off, go to **Settings → Features → Rewards**.
- The Shop has a **Study** tab with pieces for a corner of your pet's room. You can buy them whether or not you use Spiritual Routine.
- If one of your devices isn't updated, it may show fewer coins. Update WitnessWork on it.
