Open the **Territories** tab, tap **Add Territory**, fill in the card's details, and tap **Save**. You add the streets next.

## Add a territory

1. Enter the territory's **Number** from the card. **Area or Name** is optional, such as "North Hill".
2. Check **Checked Out On**, the day you were given the card. **Return By** is set to four months later. Change it if you were given another date.
3. Under **Map (Optional)**, add whatever helps you find the territory, or skip it. See [Which map options can I add to a territory?](/help/territories/territories-map-options)
4. Tap **Save**. The territory opens, ready for its streets. See [How do I add streets to a territory?](/help/territories/territories-streets)

## Good to know

- After the **Return By** date, the territory shows as overdue.
- To change anything later, open the territory and tap **Edit**. Or touch and hold it in the list and tap **Edit Territory**. **Delete Territory** is in the same place.
