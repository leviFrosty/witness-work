Those are your buddies' pets, visiting for the day. A pet visits when its owner shares their pet with you and is part of something you're doing today, like a Plan or Follow-up you share. Each pet visits once and leaves when the day ends.

## Find out whose pet it is

- Tap your own pet. Each visitor says who it's visiting with.
- Touch and hold a visiting pet to see **View Plan** (or **View Follow-up**) and **View your buddy**.
- Tap your pet's card, then the **Visiting Today** row for the full list.

## Hide visiting pets

1. Tap your pet's card, then the settings button at the top right.
2. Turn off **Buddies' pets visit** to hide them all, or turn off one buddy.

You can also touch and hold a visiting pet and choose **Hide**, then tap **Undo** to bring it back. The same switch, **Their pets visit mine**, is in Buddies Settings, and under **Edit Buddy** then **Sharing & Alerts** for one buddy.

## Good to know

- Buddies aren't told, and they still see your pet if you share it.
- A visitor wears the hat its owner picked if their app is up to date. A visitor's hat never changes your room.
- Visitors sit behind your pet. If a pet is hidden in a crowd, use the **Visiting Today** list instead.
