title: Google Drive Sync isn't working on my Android device. What can I check?
category: backups
platforms:
  - android

Open **Settings → Sync** and read the status. It usually tells you what to do.

## Check these

1. Every device uses Google Drive and is connected to the same Google Account.
2. Google Play services is installed and up to date.
3. Your phone is online. Changes you made offline are sent when you're back online and WitnessWork is open.
4. Every device has the latest WitnessWork. Update any that don't.

## If something goes wrong

- **The status says to reconnect:** tap **Reconnect Google Drive** and allow access again. Google asks again if you removed access in your Google Account.
- **Your Google storage is full:** your changes stay on this phone. Free up space or get more storage, then tap **Sync now**.
- **It says Google Drive is busy:** Google is slowing requests for a while. Your changes stay on this phone, and sync tries again on its own.
- **It asks for Supporter access:** update WitnessWork. Google Drive Sync is free.
- **Still not syncing:** keep a saved backup file and the phone with your latest records, then use the private support option in the Help Center. Keep householder details out of public reports.
