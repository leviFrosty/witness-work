id: passkeysManage
title: How do I tell my passkeys apart, and how do I remove one?
category: account
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Every WitnessWork passkey is named "WitnessWork" with a 4-character code, such as WitnessWork 7K4Q. The same code is shown in Settings → Account → Passkeys, so people who share a password manager can tell their passkeys apart. WitnessWork sets the name when the passkey is created, and can't rename it afterward.

Settings → Account → Passkeys lists each passkey with its password manager (when WitnessWork recognizes it), the device it was added on, when it was added and last used, and marks it:

- **Can unlock your account:** signing in with it brings your account key to this device.
- **Sign-in only:** it signs you in but can't bring your account key. WitnessWork then asks you to use your phone or your recovery key. Once the key is on the device, WitnessWork saves it for that passkey so it can unlock next time. On a phone, you can also tap the passkey and choose Let It Unlock to try again; some password managers can only sign in.

**To remove one,** open Settings → Account → Passkeys, tap the passkey, and choose Remove. Once you confirm, WitnessWork asks for a fresh check: Face ID, fingerprint, or passcode on a phone, and a passkey tap on the web. Removing it from WitnessWork doesn't delete it from your password manager, so delete it there too. WitnessWork warns you before you remove the last passkey that can unlock, since signing in on a computer then needs your recovery key or another device. Removals show in Settings → Account → Account Activity.

Before you remove the only way you can sign in, check that you have your recovery key ([What is my recovery key?](/help/account/account-recovery-key)).
