title: How do I report a bug or request a feature?
---

Koresha buto ziri hasi kugira ngo umenyeshe ikibazo cya porogaramu cyangwa usabe ikintu gishya. Andika intambwe zo kukigaragaza, ibyo wari witeze, igikoresho cyawe na verisiyo ya sisitemu y'imikorere, hamwe na verisiyo ya WitnessWork igaragara munsi ya Uracyakeneye ubufasha?. Kuraho amakuru bwite y'abantu, inyandiko, nomero za konti, Kode za Buddies cyangwa amahuza y'ubutumire muri raporo n'amafoto ya ecran bishyirwa ahagaragara. Ku bibazo bya backup cyangwa ibindi byihariye, koresha ihuza rya imeri ryihariye riri muri Uracyakeneye ubufasha? aho. Kode y'inkomoko, Politiki y'ubuzima bwite, n'Amabwiriza yo gukoresha biri muri Igenamiterere → Ibyerekeye.
