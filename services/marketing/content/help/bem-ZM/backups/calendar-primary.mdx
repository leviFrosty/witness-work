title: Cinshi Calendar Sync ibomfya cibombelo ca ntanshi?
---

Icibombelo cimo fye citumina Ukukonkapo kwenu pa kuti tapabe ukupilibula. Icibombelo ica ntanshi ukutantika cabe ca ntanshi. Pa fibombelo fya iOS ifibomfya iCloud account imo, isuleni Settings → Ifyo Mwasala → Calendar Sync pa kulemba cila cibombelo, ukumona lyashi lya Icibombelo ca ntanshi, no kusala ica ntanshi. Calendar Sync yene tayifwaya Supporter. Ukukonkapo ukwacinjiwa pa cibombelo cimbi kufika ku ca ntanshi ukupitila mu kulunganikanya kwa fyebo fya app, uko kucili kufwaya Supporter na iCloud Sync iyawashiwa; isuleni WitnessWork pa ca ntanshi pa kutumina. Panuma ya kusenda, icibombelo ca ntanshi cipya cifwaya ukufika kuli akaunti imo iya kalenda no kusuminishiwa ukwa kalenda yonse. Nga ukusenda kulepembela, isuleni WitnessWork nakabili pa cibombelo ca ntanshi icapita pa kuti cipwishe icintu cakwe. Tapali ukupoka kwa bwangu nga icibombelo ico tacingabwelela nakabili; bomfyeni ukwafwa kwa cinkama pa Still need help? mu Pa Kwafwilisha. Ukufumyapo icibombelo icakale mu lyashi tekufumyapo ifyalembwa fya app nelyo ifiputulwa fya kalenda.
