id: webApp
title: Can I use WitnessWork in a web browser?
category: general
pinned: true
---

Yes. Open [app.witnesswork.app](https://app.witnesswork.app) in a current browser on a computer, phone, or tablet. It's the full WitnessWork app, and it uses the same account as your phones through Cloud Sync, so your time, plans, contacts, notes, and Buddies are there once you sign in. Scribe AI and the Map work too, and exports and backups download as files.

What the web app doesn't have:

- Device notifications and reminders. Plans and Follow-ups you set on the web remind you on your phones, and the Home bell works on the web; without the phone app, you won't get reminders.
- Widgets, Live Activities, Siri and Shortcuts, the Apple Watch and Wear OS apps, and alternate app icons.
- iCloud Sync and Google Drive Sync. The web uses Cloud Sync only. If your phones use iCloud Sync, Google Drive Sync, or no sync, the web asks you to turn on [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync) first.
- Calendar sync.

These are hidden on the web instead of showing as broken.

To get started, choose how to sign in: with a passkey, with your phone (from version 1.47.0; see [sign in on a computer with your phone](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer)), or with your recovery key. For now, you create your account in the phone app ([Can I create an account on the web?](/help/account/web-sign-up)). Your records are saved in your browser and sync through Cloud Sync, and the app keeps working offline ([offline on the web](/help/general/web-offline)). To use it like an app, see [installing it](/help/general/web-install).

Using WitnessWork on the web means trusting WitnessWork's website. Unlike the phone app, which you install once from a store, the web app is delivered by the website, and while it's open it runs in your browser with your decrypted records and account key. WitnessWork builds the page to run only its own code, and your synced records stay end-to-end encrypted on its server. If you'd rather not use a browser, WitnessWork works fully on your phone alone.
