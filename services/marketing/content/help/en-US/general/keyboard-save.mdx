---
id: keyboardSave
title: Can I save a form with a keyboard shortcut?
category: general
platforms:
  - ios
  - web
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Yes. From version 1.47.0, while you're typing in a form, press Command-Return on a Mac or iPad keyboard, or Ctrl+Enter on Windows, Linux, and ChromeOS, to do what the form's main button does: Save, Add, Done, Apply, Continue, or Send in Scribe AI.

- **It follows the button.** If the button is unavailable, for example because a required field is empty or a save is already running, the shortcut does nothing. If the form has a problem, you see the same message as when you tap the button.
- **Notes.** In the note editor, the shortcut closes the editor and keeps your text, like Done. It doesn't save the visit, time entry, plan, or trip underneath; press it again there. On the web in a wide browser window, where the note is typed right in the form, the shortcut saves the form straight away. Return alone still starts a new line.
- **Sheets and pop-ups.** The shortcut presses the button of the sheet you're typing in, not the screen behind it.
- **A field on its own.** On the web, in a field that saves as you go, such as a nickname or search, the shortcut finishes editing and leaves the field.
- **What it never does.** It never deletes, removes, signs out, restores, or approves a sign-in, and it never answers a confirmation.

On the web the shortcut works in every form. On iPad and iPhone it needs a hardware keyboard and works while the cursor is in a text field or the note editor. Nothing you type is measured or shared by the shortcut. If it does nothing, check that the cursor is in a field of the form and that the main button isn't dimmed; you can always tap the button instead. For other problems, use the Help Center's private support link and keep contact details out of public reports.
