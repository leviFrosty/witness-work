---
id: savedContactViews
title: Can I save a contact filter and switch to it quickly?
category: contacts
---

Yes. A saved view keeps your filters and sort under a name, so you can switch to them with one tap. Saved views are free.

## Save a view

1. In Contacts, tap **Sort & Filter** at the start of the chips under the search field.
2. Choose your filters and a sort.
3. Tap **Save as view** at the top.
4. Name it, using up to 30 characters, such as Bible studies.

## Use a view

Your views appear as buttons under the search field and at the top of the map. Tap one to show it. Tap it again to go back to your own filters. A view applies to the list and the map. Search text isn't saved.

## Change or delete a view

- Changes you make while a view is showing are marked **Edited**. Tap **Update view** to keep them, **Save as new view** to keep both, or **Reset** to discard them.
- To rename or delete a view, touch and hold it. Or tap ••• and choose **Edit Saved Views**.
- To reorder views, touch one, pause briefly, then drag it along the row.

## Good to know

- Deleting a view never changes your contacts.
- Views are included in backups and sync when sync is on. Which view is showing stays on each device.
- If you permanently delete a contact field, filters on it are ignored.

## If something goes wrong

- **A view shows unexpected contacts:** open **Sort & Filter** to check its filters, or tap **Reset** to undo unsaved changes.
