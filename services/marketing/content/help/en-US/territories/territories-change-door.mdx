Right after you record a door, tap **Undo** in the door recorder. Later, open the street and tap the house in the list.

## Change or remove a door

1. Open the street and tap the house.
2. Choose what to do:
   - **Change its result:** the recorder switches to that house. Tap **Not at Home**, **Spoke**, or **Do Not Call**.
   - **Remove:** deletes the house's record. **Undo** appears in the recorder in case you tapped it by mistake.
   - **Add Pin** or **Move Pin:** tap the map where the door is.
   - **Add as Contact** (for **Spoke**): starts a contact with the house's address.

## Good to know

- A house marked **Do Not Call** moves to **Do Not Call on This Street**. Tap it there to change it. See [How do I add or edit a do-not-call?](/help/territories/territories-do-not-calls)
- You can change doors only on streets you can record on. Doors on a returned territory, or on a street you handed off, can't be changed.
