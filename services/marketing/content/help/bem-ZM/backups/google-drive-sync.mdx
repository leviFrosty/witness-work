---
id: googleDriveSync
title: Bushe ningalunganikanya shani ifyebo fyandi pa fibombelo fya Android?
---

Google Drive Sync ni cibombelo ca Supporter pa Android, mu beta. Pa cila foni nelyo tablet iya Android, isuleni Settings → Google Drive Sync no kuwasha Lunganikanya na Google Drive, elyo musale Google Account imo mu cipao ca Google no kusuminisha WitnessWork ukusunga ifyebo fyayo muli Google Drive yenu. Ilunganikanya ifyalembwa fyenu ifya mulimo, ubupange, abantu ba kulanshanya, intandalilo, amacipande, amakilomita, profile, ne fyo mwasala ifya kusabankanya; ifikope fikwata cisonto cekala icitila Lundako ifikope mu kulunganikanya. Cila cibombelo cikwata cisonto cacene, kabili ifyo mwasala nga data protection mode, ukusuminisha analytics, na onboarding fisala pa cila cibombelo. Ilyo WitnessWork yaisuka, ifyo mwacinja pa fibombelo fyenu ifimbi fifika mu miniti iyalingana imo, kabili ifintu fimbi ilyo mwaisula nakabili; tinikeni Lunganikanya nomba pa kumona bwangu. Pa cibombelo cipya ica Android, salenipo Ukufuma ku Google Drive pa cipao ca kupanga ica “Tendekeni uko mwaleka”, pa kubwesha ifyebo fyenu, ico cileta na umusango wenu wa Supporter ilyo Google Account iyo yatantikwa. Nga icibombelo citantika Google Account imbi, ukulunganikanya kushimya uko ifyalembwa shishilabelengwa nelyo ukutumwa, kabili ifyalembwa fyenu fisala pa cibombelo. Ukulunganikanya pakati ka iPhone na cibombelo ca Android tekusuminishiwa; bomfyeni Settings → Ukusunga no Kubwesha pa kusendamo backup.
