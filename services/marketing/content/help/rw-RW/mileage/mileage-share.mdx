title: Nsangiza nte cyangwa nohereza nte raporo yanjye y'ibirometero?
---

Fungura Ibirometero, uhitemo Umunsi, Icyumweru, Ukwezi, cyangwa Umwaka (umwaka w'umurimo, Nzeri kugeza Kanama), hanyuma ukoreshe imyambi cyangwa unyuze urutoki kugira ngo uhitemo igihe. Kanda buto yo gusangiza hejuru kugira ngo Kopi raporo, uyisangize nk'inyandiko, cyangwa Sohora CSV ku buryo bwa spreadsheet. Inyandiko igaragaza ibiteranyo, ibiteranyo by'imodoka imwe imwe iyo ufite imodoka irenze imwe, na buri rugendo uretse mu Mwaka. CSV ifite umurongo umwe ku rugendo ufite imodoka, ibipimo bya odometer, intera, icyitonderwa, lisansi, ikoreshwa rya lisansi, igiciro n'igiciro giteganyijwe. Ibyitonderwa by'ingendo birimo, bityo ubisuzume mbere yo kohereza. Kugira ngo usangize urugendo rumwe, urufungure ukande buto yo gusangiza.
