---

Ibimenyetso byishimira imigenzo ihamye mu murimo wawe wo kubwiriza, nko kubwiriza buri kwezi, kohereza raporo yawe, no gusubira kureba abantu. Nta kintu cyo gushyiraho. WitnessWork isuzuma inyandiko zawe ikongeraho ikimenyetso iyo ugeze kuri kimwe.

## Shaka ibimenyetso byawe

1. Kanda ifoto yawe y'umwirondoro, hanyuma **Umwirondoro**.
2. Ibimenyetso byawe biri munsi gato y'ikarita y'umwirondoro wawe. Kanda kimwe kugira ngo ukirebe hafi.
3. Kanda **Reba byose** kugira ngo ufungure **Ibimenyetso**, aho ubona buri kimenyetso n'icyo gifasha buri kimwe gukura nyuma.

Ibimenyetso byinshi bikura mu nzego enye: Umuringa, Ifeza, Zahabu n'Isaro. Ibimenyetso bike by'incuri imwe, nka Icyigisho cya mbere cya Bibiliya, bigumya guhishwa kugeza ubifite.

## Iyo ukibonye

- Iyo ikintu wakoze kiguhesha ikimenyetso, ikarita ntoya iza hejuru y'ekara. Yikande kugira ngo wishimire ku ekara yose, cyangwa uyikure. Ibimenyetso bishya byinshi biraterana, icya vuba imbere. Kanda ibiri inyuma kugira ngo ubyerekane.
- Ikimenyetso utakanze, cyangwa kigeze ukundi, ntikiguhagarika. Gitegereza mu nzogera y'amenyesha kuri Ahabanza. Kanda **Reba** kugira ngo ucyishimire, cyangwa **X** kugira ngo ugikureho. Uko byagenda kose kirabikwa, kandi kigumya kiranga gishya ku mwirondoro wawe kugeza ufunguye ibimenyetso byawe.
- Niba inyandiko zawe zimaze kugera ku bimenyetso ubwa mbere WitnessWork isuzuma, ekara ya "Ibimenyetso byawe biri hano" ivuga umubare ufite. **Reba** ifungura umwirondoro wawe, na **Nyuma** iyifunga.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ibimenyetso bikora kuri iPhone na Android. Bivugururwa akanya nyuma y'uko inyandiko zawe zihinduka, no igihe cyose usubiye kuri WitnessWork.
- Ibimenyetso si umubare utukura ku kimenyetso cya porogaramu. Uwo ubara ibitarasomwe mu nzogera y'amenyesha.
- Kugira ngo umenye icyo buri kimwe kibara, reba [Buri kimenyetso kibara iki, kandi kuki ukwezi kutabaruwe?](/help/badges/badges-collections)
