For now, WitnessWork on the web doesn't sell Supporter subscriptions or Tips in the European Union, the United Kingdom, a number of other countries, or the Canadian provinces of Manitoba and Saskatchewan. The [Terms of Use](/terms) list every place. The rest of Canada can buy on the web.

## What you can do

If WitnessWork thinks you're in one of these places, **Settings → Become a supporter** shows "Not available where you are yet" instead of prices. Use the App Store or Google Play buttons there to buy in the store. A store purchase shows on the web too: sign in to the same WitnessWork account on the web, and your Supporter features appear. Open the app on your phone once after updating it.

## If you think you're in the wrong place

- **Traveling, or using a VPN, work or school network:** your location comes from your connection. Turn the VPN off or reconnect, then reload WitnessWork.
- **In Canada:** if your connection doesn't show your province, the notice shows anywhere in Canada. Try another network, then reload WitnessWork, or buy in the app.
- **Your card is from one of these countries:** payments are declined when the card or billing address is from there, even if you're browsing from somewhere else. Nothing is charged.

If you're somewhere the Terms don't list and still can't buy, email [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) and tell us what you see.
