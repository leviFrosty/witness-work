title: "\"Asabye kongera guhuza\" bivuga iki muri Buddies?"
---

"Anna yasabye kongera guhuza" bivuga ko mugenzi wawe yimuye Buddies ku muhuza mushya wizewe. Kanda **Reconnect** muzongera kuba bagenzi telefoni ye imaze kwemeza. Nta kindi ugomba gukora.

## Ongera uhuze

1. Fungura imenyesha cyangwa **Buddies**, ushake ubusabe ku murongo we.
2. Kanda **Reconnect**.

Ugumana izina ry'irihimbano, ibara n'igenamiterere ry'ugusangiza wari ufite kuri we.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gahunda n'ibyo gusubirayo musangiye, ubusabe bwo kwifatanya n'uko mwakiriye ibimenyetso bya mugenzi bitangira bushya. Amakuru yawe bwite, Aderesi na Gahunda biguma uko biri.
- Niba utongeye guhuza mu munsi umwe hafi, bavanwa muri **Buddies**. Ushobora gukomeza gukanda **Reconnect** iminsi 7. Nyuma yaho, mubaze ubutumire bushya.
- Gukuraho ubusabe birangiza umuhuza wawe ushaje n'uwo mugenzi.
- Niba wahinduye konti yawe bwite, bagenzi bawe bahabwa ubu busabe ubwabwo. Buri wese wongeye guhuza agaruka. Abandi bagaragara nka "Waiting for … to reconnect" kugeza babikoze cyangwa ubusabe burangiye.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **"This request can't be checked yet":** ongera ugerageze nyuma.
- **Reconnect ivuga ko ubutumire butaboneka:** bwakoreshejwe cyangwa bwarangiye. Mubaze kohereza ubundi bushya muri **Buddies**.
- **Ntubona ubusabe:** vugurura WitnessWork. Niba mugenzi yaragiye gusa, muhuze nanone mukoresheje ubutumire bushya.
