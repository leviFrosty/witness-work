title: How does WitnessWork work in Arabic and right to left?
category: general
---

Choose العربية under Settings → Language, or set your phone to Arabic and keep Device Default. WitnessWork restarts and reads right to left: screens, menus, swipe actions, progress bars, and charts are mirrored, and back arrows point the other way. Numbers show in Arabic-Indic digits (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩), and months use the names in Jehovah's Witnesses' Arabic publications, such as تشرين الأول (أكتوبر). When you enter hours, goals, or mileage, you can type Arabic-Indic or Latin digits. Names, phone numbers, addresses, and notes stay exactly as you typed them. Phone numbers and email addresses read left to right, and numbers you type into a field stay in the order you typed them. Date order, the clock, and the first day of the week follow your Format Region in Settings → Preferences → Region & Formats: choose Middle East (Arabic) there for weeks that start on Saturday, or leave it on Auto (Device) to follow your phone. Widgets and the Apple Watch and Wear OS apps follow WitnessWork's language and direction after the app has opened once. Searching contacts and help finds Arabic words with or without vowel marks. Your records are stored the same way in every language, so switching to another language, or back, changes only how they are shown.
