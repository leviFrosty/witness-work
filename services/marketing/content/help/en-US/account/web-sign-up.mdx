title: Can I create a WitnessWork account on the web without the phone app?

Yes. You need a browser that can save passkeys. You don't need an email address, phone number or password.

## Create an account

1. Open app.witnesswork.app and choose **Create an Account**.
2. Wait for the security check to pass, then choose **Create Account**.
3. Save the passkey your browser offers, named "WitnessWork" with a 4-character code. Confirm with Face ID, your fingerprint, or your screen lock. It may ask twice.
4. Save your recovery key with **Download**, **Print** or **Copy**. Keep it somewhere private.
5. Choose **I've Saved My Recovery Key**.

## Good to know

- Save the recovery key now. The web doesn't show it again later. If you lose your passkey, it's the only way back in, and no one at WitnessWork can reset it. See [What is my recovery key, and how do I keep it safe?](/help/account/account-recovery-key).
- Depending on your browser and password manager, your passkey may be sign-in only. Then on another computer you'll also need your recovery key or a signed-in phone. See [What is a passkey?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
- The security check is run by Cloudflare, which sees your internet address and browser details.
- Your records stay in this browser and sync through Cloud Sync.
- To use the account on a phone, enter your recovery key there. See [How do I use my recovery key on a new phone?](/help/account/account-recovery-key-use).
- You can't create an account in a browser that still has another account's data. Sign in to that account instead.

## If something goes wrong

- **You closed the passkey prompt:** no account was made. Choose **Create Account** again.
- **The security check didn't finish:** choose **Try Again**. If it keeps failing, turn off extensions that block content, or try another browser.
- **Couldn't reach WitnessWork, or you're offline:** check your connection.
- **An account is being created in another tab:** finish there, or close it and try again.
- **Sign-Up Is Paused:** try again later, or create your account in the phone app and sign in on the web.
- **Too many new accounts from this network:** wait for the time shown, then try again.
- **Finish Creating Your Account:** choose **Finish with Passkey** and pick the passkey it names. Your account may already exist, so this is the only way on in this browser.
- **You deleted the passkey before finishing:** choose **I Deleted That Passkey**. Save the recovery key it shows, then sign in with it.
- **This Browser Holds an Account Key:** save the key it shows, then sign in with it or with that account's passkey.
- **Your browser couldn't save your account:** leave private browsing, or allow this site to store data, then try again.

For anything else, use the support link in the Help Center.
