title: "Mpisha nte mugenzi, mpagarike gusangiza, cyangwa nsibe amakuru yanjye ya Buddies?"
---

Kugira ngo uhagarike kubona mugenzi, mukureho. Kugira ngo uhagarike kwerekana iminsi ye kuri kalendari yawe, zimya **Erekana kuri kalendari yanjye**. Kugira ngo usibe amakuru yawe yose ya Buddies, koresha **Siba amakuru yanjye ya Buddies**.

## Hisha mugenzi kuri kalendari yawe

1. Fungura paji ye ukande ikaramu (**Edit Buddy**).
2. Kanda **Gusangiza n'imenyesha**.
3. Zimya **Erekana kuri kalendari yanjye**.

Gahunda ze ziracyagaragara iyo ukanze umunsi.

## Kuraho mugenzi

1. Fungura paji ye ukande ikaramu (**Edit Buddy**).
2. Kanda **Kuraho mugenzi**.

Ibi birangiza umuhuza wanyu mwembi. Kugira ngo muhuze nanone ukeneye ubutumire bushya. Ntibamumenyesha, nubwo ashobora kubibona. Gukuraho mugenzi bivanaho ubutumire bwawe bufunguye kuri we, ariko ntibimukura ku modoka isangiwe.

## Siba amakuru yanjye ya Buddies

1. Fungura **Buddies → Igenamiterere rya Buddies**.
2. Kanda **Siba amakuru yanjye ya Buddies**.

Ibi biboneka gusa muri porogaramu ya telefoni, si muri mushakisha.

## Byiza kumenya

- Siba amakuru yanjye ya Buddies ikuraho imuhuza yose n'ubutumire. Inaguha konti nshya n'urufunguzo rwo kugarura, bityo isiba backup yawe ya WitnessWork na kopi ya Cloud Sync kuri seriveri yacu. Ntushobora kubisubiza inyuma.
- Ibanza kuva ku modoka zose zisangiwe. Ugumana kopi ya buri imwe mu modoka zawe. Niba imodoka itarashoboka kuvamo, nko igihe nta murandasi ufite, nta kintu gisibwa. Ongera ugerageze usubiye kuri murandasi.
- Amakuru yawe bwite y'umurimo na Aderesi biraguma.
- Niba gukuraho bitegereje nta murandasi ufite, bashobora gukomeza kubona gahunda zawe za nyuma zasangijwe kugeza WitnessWork ihuye.
