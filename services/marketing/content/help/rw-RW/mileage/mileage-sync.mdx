title: Ibirometero byanjye birahuzwa, kandi bikora kuri Android?
---

Ibirometero bikora kuri iOS na Android. Imodoka, lisansi, ibiciro n'ingendo bihuzwa hagati y'ibikoresho bya iOS na iCloud Sync, ikiranga cy'Umuterankunga, kandi bishyirwa muri backup zo muri Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura ku mbuga zombi. Kuri Android bihuzwa hagati y'ibikoresho bya Android na Google Drive Sync, na yo ikiranga cy'Umuterankunga. Hagati ya iPhone n'igikoresho cya Android, koresha backup. Ibirometero ntibisangizwa na Buddies na rimwe kandi ntibigaragara kuri kalendari yawe cyangwa Raporo y'umurimo.
