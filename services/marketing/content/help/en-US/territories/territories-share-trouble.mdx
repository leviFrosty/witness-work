title: Why can't my buddy see the territory I shared, or why can't I share it?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
platforms: [ios, android]
---

Your buddy needs Territories in their app and needs to accept the invitation. Open the territory: under Shared With, "Invited" beside their name means the invitation is still waiting for them.

**If your buddy doesn't see it:**

- **Their app doesn't have Territories yet.** They need WitnessWork 1.47.0 or later from the App Store or Google Play, with Territories turned on for them; it's rolling out gradually. Your invitation waits for them for up to 7 days.
- **They haven't accepted.** The invitation is in the notification bell on Home, with Join and Decline (Accept for a handed-off street). Once they join, it's in their Territories tab under Shared With Me (or Handed to Me).
- **The invitation expired** after 7 days, or they declined. Tap Stop beside their name, then share the territory with them again.
- **They're looking on another device.** Shared territories show on each of their devices with Buddies set up. Ask them to open WitnessWork there and wait a moment.

**If sharing doesn't work for you, the message says why:**

- **"Sharing territories needs Buddies on this device."** Set up Buddies on this device (see [What is WitnessWork Buddies?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview)). Sharing isn't available on the web.
- **"Add a Buddy First"**: you can share only with people you're paired with in Buddies. Invite them to Buddies first, or send a guest link.
- **"Couldn't reach WitnessWork"** or **"Couldn't start sharing. Check your connection"**: sharing needs a connection. Try again when you're online.
- **"Couldn't start sharing. Update WitnessWork, then try again."**: install the update from the App Store or Google Play.
- **"Sharing territories is paused right now."**: sharing is unavailable for a while. Your territory stays on your device; try again later.
- **"Only the territory's owner can do that."**: only the person who shared the territory can share it with others, change who has it, or return it (see [Why can't I edit or return a territory someone shared with me?](/help/territories/territories-shared-with-me)).
- **"This territory is too large to share."**: see [What are the limits on sharing a territory?](/help/territories/territories-limits)
- **"Pick a street that isn't handed off."**: handed-off streets can't be shared again until they're back with you.
- **"Some changes to (territory) weren't shared. That street was handed off meanwhile."**: the street was handed off or taken back while you were offline, so your changes to it couldn't be saved on the territory.

If a problem continues, contact private support under Still need help?, using the support button in this screen's header, and include your app version. Keep territory numbers, addresses, and people's names out of public bug reports.
