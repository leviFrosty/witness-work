title: How do I move to a new phone or switch between iPhone and Android?
category: account
---

Show a code on your old phone and scan it with the new one. This works between any two phones, including iPhone to Android and back. A browser can't set up another device.

## Move to a new phone

1. On the old phone, go to **Settings → Account → Set Up Another Device**. A QR code appears.
2. On the new phone, install WitnessWork.
3. During setup, tap **I already use WitnessWork**, then **From your other phone**. After setup, use **Sign In with QR Code** in **Settings → Account**.
4. Scan the QR code.
5. Check that both phones show the same six-digit code.
6. On the old phone, tap **YES**, then confirm with Face ID, your fingerprint, your passcode or your screen lock.

Your account and your buddies move to the new phone. Your old phone stays signed in.

## Bring your records too

Your service records, contacts and notes aren't part of this step. A new install starts with Cloud Sync on, so they arrive on their own once you're signed in. Or turn on **Backup** in **Settings → Account** on the old phone first, and the new phone offers to restore it. You can also use a file from **Settings → Backup & Restore**.

## Good to know

- Both phones need the internet, and the code lasts 10 minutes.
- If the new phone used Buddies with a different account, it asks before replacing it.
- A passkey can do this without your old phone. See [What is a passkey?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
- Code not working? See [What if signing in with a QR code doesn't work?](/help/account/account-handoff-trouble).
