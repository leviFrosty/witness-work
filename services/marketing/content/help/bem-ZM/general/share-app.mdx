title: Bushe ningatuma shani WitnessWork kuli munyinefwe?
---

Tinikeni icikope cenu ica profile, elyo Settings → Tuma WitnessWork. Saleni Android nelyo iPhone, kabili munyinefwe kuti asikena code ne kamera ya foni yakwe pa kwisula WitnessWork muli Google Play nelyo App Store. Tuma Link itumina ubutumwa bwa link sha stoo shibili, ico ibomba kuli bonse. WitnessWork ni ya bwebwe pa fibili. Ubutumwa bukwata fye ama link ya stoo; tapali icalanda pa imwe nelyo ifyalembwa fyenu. Nga code tayasikene, kuseni ubuuto bwa cilangililo nelyo mubomfye Tuma Link.
