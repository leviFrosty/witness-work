title: Bushe ningacinja shani ukufuma pali Kasabankanya ukuya kuli Painia wa Kwafwilisha pakati ka mweshi?
---

Isuleni Ifyo Mwasala → Umusango wa Kasabankanya no kusala ico calinga nomba: Kasabankanya, Painia wa Kwafwilisha, Painia wa Nshita Yonse, Painia Waibela, Kangalila wa Muputule, nelyo Custom. Ifyo mwapanga ukufikapo ne fyonse pamo fikacinja ukulingana na co. Bwelelapo ilyo mwapwisha. Imyeshi yapita ilasunga inshita iyo mwalembele, e ico ifyacitika fyenu fikaba fya cine.
