title: "Nifatanya nte cyangwa nanga imodoka isangiwe?"
---

Iyo mugenzi agusangije imodoka, ubona imenyesha. Ubutumire kandi butegereza mu nzogera y'Ahabanza, hamwe na **Join** na **Anga**.

## Ifatanye cyangwa wange

1. Ahabanza, kanda inzogera.
2. Shaka ubutumire, nka "Anna yagusangije Family Minivan."
3. Kanda **Join** cyangwa **Anga**.

**Join** yongera imodoka mu Birometero n'amateka yayo yose, harimo ingendo zabanje mbere y'uko wifatanya. Imodoka zawe bwite ziguma izawe. Uwagutumiye amenyeshwa ko wifatanyije.

## Byiza kumenya

- Buri wese uri ku modoka abona ingendo zayo zose, abazandika n'inyandiko zazo. Abatari bagenzi bawe bakubona mu izina n'ifoto usangiza.
- Ubutumire bumara iminsi 7 bufunguye.
- Ukeneye Buddies kugira ngo ubone ubutumire, kuko mugenzi gusa ni we ushobora kugutumira. Reba [WitnessWork Buddies ni iki, kandi kuki ntayibona?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview).

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ubutumire bwarangiye cyangwa bwabuze:** saba umuntu uri ku modoka kongera kugutumira.
- **"This car already has the most people it can.":** imodoka ifite abantu 10, harimo n'ubutumire bufunguye. Umuntu uri ku modoka agomba gukuraho ubutumire bufunguye, cyangwa nyirayo agakuraho umuntu.
