---

Yego, niba ukifungura. Amasaha ubuze ku ntego yawe ya buri kwezi ashobora kwimukira ku kwezi gukurikira.

## Fungura kwimura

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Umwirondoro → Iterambere**.
2. Fungura **Imura yikora**.

![Igenamiterere ry'umwirondoro ry'iterambere rifite akabuto ka Imura yikora](time-rollover.webp)

Kigaragara gusa niba wandika amasaha. Amasaha yimuwe agaragara ukwezi gushya gutangiye, bityo ntukeneye gukuramo cyangwa kongera kwandika ikintu.
