title: Cinshi WitnessWork ilanda ukuti tailingashinina icibombelo ici?
---

WitnessWork ipusha Apple nelyo Google ukushinina ukuti foni yenu ne app fya cine. Akaunti yenu, Buddies, Backup, Pangeni Icibombelo Cimbi ne Scribe AI fibomba fye mu app ya cine.

## Nga mwamona ubutumwa

- **Not verified yet:** Apple nelyo Google tafyafikilike. Lolelani amineti yantu elyo mutinike **Try Again**.
- **Can't verify this device:** fumyeni WitnessWork elyo mwibike nakabili ukufuma ku App Store nelyo Google Play. Cinjeni software ya foni yenu, elyo mutinike **Try Again**.

Mwasanga ifi mu **Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti**.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ukulangalila kubwela pa nshiku 90 shimo. Apple na Google balamona fye ukulangalila, te ifyo mwalemba nelyo akaunti yenu.
- Takwingapita pa simulators, emulators, amafoni yafumyapo ifipungo, software ya foni iyalungikwa, nelyo kopi sha app isha cine.
- Pa Android, foni ifwaya na Google Play Store ne Google Play services ifyapya.
- Ifintu fimbi fyonse mu WitnessWork fyashala fibomba, elyo ifyo mwalemba fyashala pa foni yenu.

Nacili tacipitila pa foni ya cine? Washeni support ukufuma mu Help Center.
