---
id: buddiesPause
title: Can I pause sharing or hide when I go out?
category: buddies
---

Yes. Open Buddies, tap the menu button, and choose Buddies Settings. Under Your Plans, Start times sets how precisely buddies see when you go out: Exact time, Time of day (Morning before noon, Afternoon until 5 PM, Evening after that), or Don't share. Buddies still see which days you plan and for how long. Pause sharing hides your plans, streak, badges, and pet from every buddy for a week, a month, or until you turn it back on; they still see your name and photo, and new badges aren't announced. While a pause is on, Buddies shows Sharing is paused with a Resume button. A timed pause ends by itself, and your plans return to buddies the next time WitnessWork refreshes Buddies on your device. To stop sharing plans with just one buddy, open their page, tap Sharing & Alerts, and turn off Share my plans; a pause still covers them too. Buddies aren't told about any of these choices, though they may notice your plans are gone. Plan and Follow-up invitations you send still go out while sharing is paused. Your choices apply on all your devices with Buddies. Your photo, pioneer status, streak, badges, and pet each have their own switch under How buddies see you.
