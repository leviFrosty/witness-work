Sign in to WitnessWork on the web, then choose a subscription or a Tip in settings. Your purchase belongs to your WitnessWork account.

## Become a Supporter

1. Sign in to WitnessWork on the web.
2. Open **Settings → Become a supporter**.
3. Choose **Monthly** or **Annual**, and pick a price.
4. Choose **Become a Supporter**.
5. On the payment page, enter your email (for your receipt), card details and billing address.

When payment goes through, you return to WitnessWork, which confirms your purchase.

## Leave a Tip instead

1. Open **Settings → Become a supporter**.
2. Choose **Send a tip**, pick an amount, and send it.

A Tip is a one-time thank-you. It doesn't turn on Supporter features.

## Good to know

- Prices are in US dollars, with tax added where required.
- Your email and billing address are used only for receipts, tax and refunds. WitnessWork doesn't keep them.
- Subscriptions renew each month or year until you cancel. See [How do I manage or cancel a subscription I bought on the web?](/help/supporter/web-manage-subscription).
- Your purchase also shows on your phones and tablets signed in to the same account. Open WitnessWork there while online. On the phone, open it once after updating.
- Web purchases aren't available in some places. See [Why can't I buy on the web in my country or province?](/help/supporter/web-purchase-unavailable-region).

## If something goes wrong

- **The price button waits, saying it's getting your account ready:** stay on the page while you're online. If it doesn't turn on after a minute, check your connection and reload the page. If it still waits, buy in the app on your phone instead, or email [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app).
- **Your purchase is still being confirmed:** wait a minute, then choose **Retry**. Don't buy again. If it still doesn't confirm, email support from the address you used at checkout.
- **It says your purchase is complete and to go back to WitnessWork:** checkout finished in a browser that isn't signed in to your account. Open WitnessWork where you're signed in.
- **It says it couldn't find a purchase here:** if you just checked out, open WitnessWork signed in to the account you used. If it isn't there after a few minutes, email support from the address you used at checkout.
- **It says the purchase is on the account that started checkout:** open WitnessWork signed in to that account.
- **No prices or buy buttons:** see [Why can't I buy on the web in my country or province?](/help/supporter/web-purchase-unavailable-region).
