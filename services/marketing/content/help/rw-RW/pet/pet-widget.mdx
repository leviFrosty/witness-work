Kuri iPhone na iPad, yego. Android nta widget za WitnessWork z'ekara y'ahabanza ifite.

## Ongeraho widget

1. Kanda ufate ekara yawe y'ahabanza.
2. Kanda akabuto ka **+**.
3. Shakisha WitnessWork uhitemo **Itungo ry'Inshuti**.

## Ubunini

- Ntoya yerekana itungo ryawe.
- Iringaniye yongeraho izina ryaryo, imitima, inkurikirane yawe, n'ibyo icyapa cyo ku rukuta cyaryo cyerekana.
- Nini yerekana icyumba cyaryo cyose gifite izina ryaryo n'imitima.

## Byiza kumenya

- Widget ihinduka umunsi wose yonyine, kandi gufungura WitnessWork birayivugurura.
- Kugeza ufashe itungo, ikubwira kurihitamo muri porogaramu.
