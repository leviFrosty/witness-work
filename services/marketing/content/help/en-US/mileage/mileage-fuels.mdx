WitnessWork adds three fuels for you, so a new car only needs a name: **Gasoline**, **Diesel**, and **Electric**.

## Choose a fuel for a car

1. Open **Mileage → Settings → Add Car**.
2. In the **Fuel** row, pick a fuel.
3. Enter the car's fuel economy to see an estimated cost.

## Change a fuel's price

1. Open **Mileage → Settings → Fuels**.
2. Tap the fuel.
3. Enter the price you pay.

Each fuel starts with an average 2026 price for your region, in your region's currency. It's a national average, not the price at your pump. If your region has no average, the fuel starts without a price. You can add one the same way.

## Good to know

- Gasoline and Diesel are priced per gallon or per litre, following your **Units** setting.
- **Electric** is priced per kWh. A car using it enters its fuel economy as mi/kWh, km/kWh, or kWh/100 km. To add another fuel priced per kWh, tap **Add Fuel** and set **Priced Per** to kWh.
- You can rename these fuels, change their prices, or delete them: tap the fuel, then **Delete…**. A fuel you delete stays deleted, including on your other devices.
- If you already had a fuel with the same name, WitnessWork keeps yours.
- A shared car keeps its own fuels, so these changes don't affect it.
