title: "Nakora iki niba telefoni cyangwa mudasobwa yanjye yatakaye cyangwa yibwe?"
---

Fata ingamba vuba, ukoresheje ikindi gikoresho. Bikore no igihe inzogera y'Ahabanza ikubwiye ko hari kwinjira cyangwa passkey itari wowe wakoze.

Niba nta kindi gikoresho cyinjiye ufite, banza winjire kuri mudasobwa cyangwa indi telefoni ukoresheje passkey cyangwa urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura.

## Rinda konti yawe

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Ibikoresho byinjiye** ku gikoresho kikiri cyinjiye. Kanda telefoni cyangwa mushakisha byatakaye ubisohore.
2. Niba ari igikoresho cya Apple, kikure kuri konti yawe ya Apple. Naho ubundi cyakira urufunguzo rwawe rushya.
3. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Change Account Key** ukurikize intambwe. Reba [Nahindura nte urufunguzo rwa konti yanjye?](/help/account/change-account-key).
4. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Ibikorwa kuri konti** urebe kwinjira utazi.
5. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Passkeys**. Ku passkey itari iyawe, yikande uhitemo **Kuraho**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gusohoka ntibihagije ubwabyo. Telefoni igifite konti yawe ishobora kongera kwinjira, bityo hindura urufunguzo rwawe niba undi muntu ashobora kuba aruzi.
- Mushakisha wasohoye isibwa ubutaha ihuye na murandasi. Ibyo yari itarahuza birabura.
- Sohora mushakisha mbere yo guhindura urufunguzo. Mushakisha ikiri yinjiye urufunguzo ruhindutse igumana amakuru yayo.
- Niba urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura rwari rubitswe ku gikoresho cyatakaye, guhindura urufunguzo rurasimbura.
- Ufite urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura wese afite konti yawe. Nta muntu ukorera WitnessWork ushobora kubihindura.
- Watakaje n'urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura? Reba [Nakora iki niba ntagishoboye kwinjira muri konti yanjye ya WitnessWork?](/help/account/lost-access).
