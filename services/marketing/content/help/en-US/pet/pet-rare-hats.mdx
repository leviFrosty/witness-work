title: What are rare hats, and what do they do?
category: pet
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Rare hats are the five dearest hats in the Shop, from 1,200 to 3,200 coins. They have a blue frame in the Shop and in your pet's details. Your pet shows them off in the room: they move, they change the room, and they react when you pat your pet and when you earn coins. Coins are never sold, so you can only save up for them by going out in the ministry and, if you use it, with Spiritual Routine.

- Fishbowl (1,200): fish swim and water light drifts over the wall. Pat your pet and the fish dart. New coins make one leap.
- Lighthouse (1,600): gulls circle by day, and a beam sweeps the room after dusk. Pat your pet to run the beam by day.
- Model railway (2,000): a little train loops the hill on your pet's head. Pat your pet for a burst of speed. New coins heap the last wagon with gold.
- Planetarium (2,600): stars fill the room's walls after dusk, with a shooting star now and then. Pat your pet to run the show by day.
- Little fig tree (3,200): it starts as a sprout and grows with your days out, in six stages, the last at 50 days out since you bought it. It follows the season in your window.

Hats that move don't read your hearts or your streak, so they work the same however your pet is doing. The lighthouse and the planetarium dim the room while they run, and your lamp goes back to how it was when they stop. "After dusk" follows your pet's window, so set the window to Night in your pet's details to see them any time.

Other hats move too. For example, the Mailbox's flag goes up on days you go out, the Cottage lights its windows after dusk, and the Cuckoo clock shows your real time and calls on the hour.

With Reduce Motion on, hats that move stay still. They don't move in shared pictures or in the Home Screen widget (iPhone and iPad), where a tall hat can be cut off in the smallest size.

If a rare hat doesn't move, check that Reduce Motion is off in your device's settings. If you bought one on a device that hasn't updated, update it; an older version keeps the hat you bought but shows your pet without it.
