id: googleDriveSyncTroubleshooting
title: Google Drive Sync ntikora ku gikoresho cyanjye cya Android. Ni iki nakwemeza?
---

Fungura Igenamiterere → Google Drive Sync usome imimerere. Reba ko buri gikoresho gihujwe na Konti imwe ya Google kandi ko serivisi za Google Play zashyizweho kandi zivuguruye. Niba imimerere ivuga ko ugomba kongera guhuza, kanda Ongera uhuze Google Drive wemere kugera nanone; Google irabaza nanone iyo uburenganzira bwakuweho mu igenamiterere rya Konti yawe ya Google. Niba ububiko bwawe bwa Google bwuzuye, impinduka zawe zikomeza kuba kuri iki gikoresho kugeza habonetse umwanya; vanaho ibintu cyangwa ugure ububiko bwisumbuyeho, hanyuma ukande Huza ubu. Guhuza bikenera interineti; imimerere igira iti Gutegereza interineti kugeza usubiye kuri interineti, kandi impinduka zakozwe nta interineti zoherezwa icyo gihe WitnessWork ifunguye. Niba ivuga ko Google Drive irahuze, Google iri kugabanya ibyifuzo mu gihe runaka; impinduka zawe zikomeza kuba kuri iki gikoresho kandi guhuza kongera kugerageza ubwako. Niba igikoresho gikoresha verisiyo nshya ya WitnessWork, vugurura ibikoresho byose. Google Drive Sync ikenera uburenganzira bw'Umuterankunga; niba burangiye, guhuza birahagarara hanyuma bigakomeza uburenganzira bugarutse. Bika backup yoherejwe n'igikoresho gifite amakuru yawe aheruka mbere yo kongera kubaka amakuru ya Google Drive. Vugana n'ubufasha bwihariye uciye mu Kigo cy'ubufasha niba guhuza bikomeje kunanirwa, kandi ntushyire amakuru ya ba nyir'inzu muri raporo zishyirwa ahagaragara.
