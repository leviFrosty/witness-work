title: What is my recovery key, and how do I keep it safe?
category: account
---

Your recovery key is a long code that starts with WW1-. It's the way back into your account, so keep it somewhere safe.

## Show your recovery key

1. Open **Settings → Account**.
2. Tap **Show Recovery Key**.
3. Confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, your passcode or your screen lock.
4. Copy it into a password manager, or print it.

On the web, WitnessWork asks you to confirm with a passkey first. A new web account also shows the key right after you create it, so save it then.

## Good to know

- Anyone who has your key has your account, including your Buddies. Don't send it or post it.
- Lost the key? View it again on any phone or browser that's still signed in.
- Think someone else has it? Change your account key. See [How do I change my account key?](/help/account/change-account-key).
- If you lose every signed-in device and your key, no one can recover the account, including us. See [What if I lose access to my WitnessWork account?](/help/account/lost-access).
- To use it on a new phone, see [How do I use my recovery key on a new phone?](/help/account/account-recovery-key-use).
