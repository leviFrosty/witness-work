title: "Nkoresha nte Scribe AI?"
---

Bwira Scribe AI ibyo wakoze cyangwa uteganya gukora, ihindura amagambo yawe amakarita ugomba kureba. Nta kintu cyongerwa kugeza ukanze **Ongera**.

## Tangira ukoresheje ijwi ryawe

1. Kuri **Ahabanza**, kanda mikoro irabagirana mu Ibikorwa byihuse. Cyangwa ukande **+** uhitemo **Vuga ibigezweho**.
2. Vuga ibyo wakoze cyangwa uteganya gukora, nka "Amasaha abiri uyu munsi mu gitondo, kandi naganiriye na John ku muhanda wa Oak 42, gusubirayo ku wa kabiri utaha."
3. Kanda **Byarangiye**.
4. Reba amakarita. Yerekana igihe, gahunda, n'abantu hamwe n'amasura yabo na gusubirayo.
5. Kuraho ikimenyetso ku kintu cyose udashaka. Niba ibaza "Maria uwuhe?", hitamo umuntu ukwiye.
6. Kanda **Ongera**.

Kugira ngo wandike ahubwo, kanda clavier iri hejuru y'ekara mu gihe yumva.

## Koresha inyandiko zawe

Kuri iPhone na iPad, ushobora gusuzuma inyandiko zanditse n'intoki. Kuri telefoni, ushobora kandi gusoma amafoto y'impapuro. Reba inyandiko mbere yo kuzohereza.

## Kuri murandasi

1. Kanda akabuto ko kuganira mu Ibikorwa byihuse, cyangwa ukande **+** uhitemo **Andika ibigezweho**.
2. Andika ibyo wakoze cyangwa uteganya gukora, ukande **Ohereza**.

Ijwi no gusuzuma ntibiri kuri murandasi. Reba [Nshobora gukoresha Scribe AI kuri murandasi?](/help/scribe/scribe-web).

## Subiramo

Ako kanya nyuma yo kongeraho, kanda **Subiramo** mu butumwa buri hasi y'ekara. Mu minsi 30, ushobora kandi gufungura Scribe AI, ugakanda **Amateka** ugakanda **Subiramo** ku kiganiro.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta mikoro mu Ibikorwa byihuse:** kanda ufate igice icyo ari cyo cyose cya Ahabanza, ukande **Hindura Ekara y'ahabanza**, wongere Ibikorwa byihuse.
