title: "Passkey ni iki, kandi nayibika nte kuri WitnessWork?"
---

Passkey igufasha kwinjira ukoresheje Face ID, igikumwe cyangwa gufunga ecran, nta jambobanga. Ni indi nzira yo kwinjira muri konti yawe imwe, kandi ntiyitegetswe.

Passkey ishobora kukwinjiza kuri app.witnesswork.app cyangwa kuri telefoni nshya. Imwe ishobora gufungura konti yawe kandi izana konti yawe kuri mushakisha cyangwa telefoni nshya nta kindi gikoresho cyangwa urufunguzo rwo kugarura.

## Bika passkey

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Passkeys**.
2. Kanda **Add a Passkey**.
3. Emeza ukoresheje Face ID, igikumwe cyangwa kode ya telefoni.

Mu gutegura, WitnessWork ishobora gutanga **Save a Passkey**. Kanda **Si ubu** kugira ngo ubirenge. Ushobora kugira zigeze kuri 10.

Kuri murandasi, ushobora kongeraho imwe kuri iyo ecran. Niba konti yawe nta passkey ifite, ongeraho iya mbere muri porogaramu ya telefoni, cyangwa mu minota mike umaze kwinjira kuri murandasi.

## Injira ukoresheje passkey

- **Kuri murandasi:** kuri app.witnesswork.app, hitamo **Sign in with a passkey**.
- **Kuri telefoni nshya:** mu gutegura, kanda **Wari usanzwe ukoresha? Subiza amakuru**, hanyuma **Sign in with a passkey**. Nyuma, koresha **Igenamiterere → Konti → Sign In with a Passkey**.

Niba passkey igutuma winjira ariko idashobora gufungura konti yawe, rangiza ukoresheje **Use Your Other Phone** cyangwa **Ahubwo koresha urufunguzo rwo kugarura**. Nyuma yaho, akenshi ishobora gufungura yonyine ubutaha.

## Byiza kumenya

- Niba telefoni isanzwe ifite indi konti, buri gihe ikubaza mbere yo kuyisimbuza.
- Passkey kuri telefoni zikenera iOS 16 cyangwa irenga, cyangwa Android ifite Credential Manager ya Google. Kuri iPhone, passkey ishobora gufungura konti yawe gusa kuva kuri iOS 18.
- Kuri murandasi, mbere yo kwerekana urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura cyangwa guhindura passkey, WitnessWork igusaba gukoresha imwe muri passkey zawe. Ako kanya umaze kwinjira, ntibibaza. Reba [Mushakisha imara igihe kingana iki yinjiye, kandi nakoresha zingahe?](/help/account/web-sessions).
- WitnessWork ishobora kukwibutsa kubika passkey mu nzogera y'Ahabanza. Niba ukanze **Si ubu** kabiri, inyibutsa zirahagarara.
- Kugira ngo umenye itandukaniro rya passkey cyangwa ukureho imwe, reba [Nzi nte itandukaniro rya passkey zanjye, kandi nakuraho nte imwe?](/help/account/passkeys-manage).
