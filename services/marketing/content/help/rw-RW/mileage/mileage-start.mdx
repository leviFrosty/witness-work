title: "Mfungura nte cyangwa nzimya gukurikirana ibirometero?"
---

Gukurikirana ibirometero ntibitegetswe kandi biboneka kuri buri mubwiriza. Bifungure, wongereho imodoka, hanyuma wandike ingendo zawe.

## Fungura ibirometero

1. Ahabanza, subiza **Yego** ku ikarita ya **Gukurikirana ibirometero?**. Cyangwa fungura **Igenamiterere → Umwirondoro** ufungure **Kurikirana ibirometero**.
2. Ongeraho imodoka yawe ya mbere. Izina gusa ni ryo ritegetswe.

## Andika urugendo

1. Kanda **Andika urugendo** ku ikarita y'Ibirometero y'Ahabanza, kuri ecran ya **Ibirometero**, cyangwa ukoresheje akabuto ka Ongera.
2. Andika intera. Fungura **Kugenda no kugaruka** kugira ngo uyikube kabiri. Cyangwa uhindukire kuri **Igipimo cy'ibirometero** wandike ibipimo by'itangiriro n'iby'iherezo. **Igipimo cyo gutangira** cyuzuzwa n'igipimo cya nyuma cy'iyo modoka.
3. Bika urugendo.

Kanda urugendo kugira ngo ubone ibisobanuro. Rufate kugira ngo urugarure, urwongere uyu munsi, urusangize, cyangwa urusibe.

## Bihagarike

Zimya **Kurikirana ibirometero** muri **Igenamiterere → Umwirondoro**. Ibi bihisha ikarita y'Ahabanza, **Andika urugendo** na ecran ya **Ibirometero**, ariko bigakomeza ingendo zawe. Ubifungure nanone byose bigaruka. Niba wahisemo **Oya, urakoze** ku ikarita y'Ahabanza, aka kabuto kabifungura igihe cyose.

Kugira ngo uhishe ikarita y'Ahabanza gusa, koresha **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Ekara y'ahabanza**.

## Siba ibirometero byawe

Fungura **Ibirometero → Igenamiterere → Siba amakuru yose y'ibirometero…**. Ibi bisiba imodoka zawe bwite, lisansi n'ingendo ku bikoresho byose bihuzwa n'iki, kandi ntibisubizwa inyuma. Imodoka zisangiwe ziravwa, kandi ugumana kopi ya buri imwe mu modoka zawe.
