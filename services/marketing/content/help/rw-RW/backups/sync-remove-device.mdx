title: Nakuraho nte igikoresho cya kera kuri iCloud Sync?
---

Buri gikoresho gikoresha iCloud Sync kibika kopi yacyo y'amakuru yawe muri iCloud yawe, kandi iyo kopi iguma nubwo wareka gukoresha icyo gikoresho, harimo na nyuma yo kongera gushyiraho porogaramu cyangwa kugura telefoni nshya. Gikureho igihe utagikoresha, urugero icyo wagurishije, wasimbuje cyangwa wasubije uko cyari kimeze. Ku gikoresho kigihuza, fungura Igenamiterere → iCloud Sync → Ibikoresho maze ukande Kuraho iruhande rw'igikoresho cya kera. Ibikoresho bitahuje mu minsi 90 cyangwa irenga biragaragazwa. Gukuraho bisiba gusa kopi y'icyo gikoresho muri iCloud, harimo n'amakuru yacyo y'abantu basibwe ahandi. Iki gikoresho kigumana ibyo cyamaze guhuza, kandi ibindi bikoresho byawe ntibihinduka. Niba igikoresho cyakuweho kigikoreshwa, cyongera kohereza amakuru yacyo ubutaha gihuza; banza uzimye iCloud Sync kuri cyo niba ushaka ko gihagarara. Mbere yo gukuraho igikoresho, iki gikoresho kigomba kurangiza guhuza amakuru yacyo; nibisabwa, garuka, ukande Huza ubu, ukosore ikibazo cyose uko gihagaze kigaragaza. Niba igikoresho gikoresha verisiyo nshya ya WitnessWork, banza uvugurure iki. Ibikoresho byasimbuwe na Ongera wubake amakuru ya iCloud bishobora gukurwaho igihe icyo ari cyo cyose. Amafoto ntabikwa kuri buri gikoresho, bityo gukuraho igikoresho ntibikuraho amafoto; koresha Kuraho amafoto ya iCloud kuri ibyo. Ibikoresho ni igice cya iCloud Sync kuri iOS na Google Drive Sync kuri Android (Igenamiterere → Google Drive Sync → Ibikoresho), byombi ni ibiranga by'abaterankunga; kuri Android kopi ziri muri Google Drive yawe. Niba ukeneye ubufasha, koresha uburyo bw'ubufasha bwa bwite bwo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha kandi ntushyire amakuru y'abo usura mu bitekerezo rusange.
