id: pauseSupport
title: Bushe kuti naleka ukwafwa mu cifulo ca kuleka bwino?
---

Ee. Isuleni Settings → Longolokeni ukulipila. Pa iPhone na iPad, salenipo ukuleka ukulipila umweshi 1, 3, nelyo 6. Ukuleka kutendeka ilyo inshita yenu ya nomba ipwa. Tamukalipishiwa ilyo mwaleka, kabili ifyo Supporter akwata fisala, pamo na iCloud Sync, umutundu wenu, ne cishibilo ca app. Ukulipila kutendeka nakabili bwangu na mutengo umo pa bushiku ubulangwa, kabili kuti mwaleka inshita iili yonse mu kulipila kwenu kwa App Store. Kuti mwaleka umuku umo pa myeshi 12; inshiku sha kuleka shisungwa pamo ne fyo mwasala ico ifibombelo fyenu ifimbi fishikonka. Pa Android, salenipo Leka muli Google Play pa kuleka ukulipila kwa cila mweshi umweshi 1 ukufika pa myeshi 3. Ifyo Supporter akwata nga icishibilo ne mutundu fileka nafyo, elyo fyabwelela bwangu ne fyo mwasala fisungwa, kabili kuti mwabwelela bwangu muli Google Play. Ifyo mwingasala pa kuleka tafimoneka ilyo muli mu kwesha kwa bwebwe, ubwafya bwa kulipila, ukulipila kwa Family Sharing, nelyo mu myeshi 12 iya kuleka kwa kulekelesha. Nga ukuleka kwabula, tapali icacinja; esheniko nakabili pa numa nelyo mulongoloke ukulipila kwenu mu stoo yenu. Pa fimbi, bomfyeni Still need help? pa kwebako abafwa mu cinkama.
