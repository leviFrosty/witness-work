Edit your Home screen to move sections around or hide the ones you don't use. Hiding a section never deletes your data.

## Rearrange or hide sections

1. On **Home**, press and hold a section, then choose **Edit Home Screen**. Or tap **Edit Home Screen** after the last section.
2. The sections start to jiggle. Hold a section and drag it to move it. On iPad, sections can move between the two columns.
3. To hide a section, tap the gray minus at its top corner. Tap **Undo** if you didn't mean to.
4. Tap **Done**. On Android, the back button works too.

## Show a hidden section again

1. Start editing as above.
2. Tap **Add Sections** at the top.
3. Choose the section. It comes back first on Home.

You can also show, hide and reorder sections in **Settings → Features → Home Screen**.

## Good to know

- A section with nothing to show right now, like Approaching Conversations with no follow-ups today, appears as an outline while you edit, so you can still move or hide it.
- Getting Started stays at the top until you finish or close it.
- Some sections depend on your role or device. Timer appears for roles that log time, Mileage when mileage tracking is on, and Service Year Summary on iPad with an annual goal.
- When Buddies is on, **Add Sections** also offers Together, which shows invitations from buddies and plans with buddies over the next two weeks. It isn't on Home until you add it.
- Your layout syncs with your other devices when sync is on, and is included in backups.
