title: "Amafoto, ibisohorwa na backup bikora bite kuri murandasi?"
---

Kuri murandasi, ibikururwa bijya mu bubiko bw'ibikururwa bwa mushakisha yawe. Amafoto mu nyandiko arakora; amafoto y'umwirondoro n'ay'abantu ntarakora.

## Amafoto

- **Amafoto mu nyandiko:** ongeraho rimwe ukoresheje akabuto k'ifoto k'umwanditsi w'inyandiko, ukoporora ifoto ukayihambira, cyangwa ukurura dosiye y'ifoto ukayishyira ku nyandiko. Ahuzwa na telefoni zawe binyuze kuri Cloud Sync. Amafoto y'inyandiko za telefoni zawe agaragara amaze gukururwa; mbere y'aho, inyandiko ivuga ngo "Iyi foto ntiri kuri iki gikoresho."
- Mushakisha yawe ibika kopi yayo ya buri foto, kandi gusohoka birayikuraho. Iyo mushakisha ibuze umwanya, WitnessWork irabikubwira.
- **Amafoto y'umwirondoro n'ay'abantu:** ntushobora kuyongeraho kuri murandasi, kandi ay'amatelefoni yawe ntagaragara aho. Aderesi zigaragaza inyuguti zibanza.
- Guhindura umuntu kuri murandasi ntibikoraho ifoto ye kuri telefoni zawe. Gukuraho cyangwa gusimbuza ifoto y'umuntu cyangwa y'umwirondoro wawe kuri murandasi bikuraho kuri telefoni zawe.

## Ibikururwa no gusangiza

- **Kora Backup**, **Sohora CSV** ya raporo y'ibirometero n'ibindi bisohorwa bikururwa nka dosiye. Mushakisha yawe ishobora kukubaza aho ubibika, cyangwa ikabishyira mu bikururwa byayo.
- Kuri mudasobwa, **Sangiza raporo** n'ibindi bisangizwa by'inyandiko bikoporora inyandiko. Ubona "Copied!" ukayihambira aho ushaka.
- Kuri telefoni, gusangiza bifungura urupapuro rwo gusangiza rwa telefoni. Niba mushakisha yawe itabishoboye, dosiye iraragururwa.

## Ibyinjizwa

**Garura ukoresheje backup**, **Import Contact** n'ibyinjizwa bya MyTime bifungura aho guhitamo dosiye muri mushakisha yawe. Dosiye za MyTime (.mytimedb) zigumana kuri mudasobwa yawe, kandi iri hejuru ya MB 25 irangwa. Backup za WitnessWork zifunzwe n'ijambobanga. Ryandike kugira ngo ugarure.

## Byiza kumenya

- Backup yakuwe ishobora kuba irimo amakuru y'abantu usura. Ibike mu ibanga.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta kintu gikururwa:** reba ko mushakisha yawe itabuza ibikururwa kuri uru rubuga. Ku bindi bifasha, reba [Vugana n'ubufasha](/help).
