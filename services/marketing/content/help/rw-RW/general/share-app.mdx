title: Nasangiza nte WitnessWork incuti yanjye?
---

Kanda ifoto y'umwirondoro wawe, hanyuma Igenamiterere → Sangiza WitnessWork. Hitamo Android cyangwa iPhone, maze incuti yawe ishobore gusikana kode ikoresheje kamera ya telefoni yayo kugira ngo ifungure WitnessWork muri Google Play cyangwa App Store. Sangiza ihuza yohereza ubutumwa burimo amahuza yombi y'amaduka, bityo bikora ku muntu wese. WitnessWork ni ubuntu kuri byombi. Ubutumwa burimo amahuza y'amaduka gusa — nta kintu kikuvuga cyangwa inyandiko zawe. Niba kode itasikanwa, ongera urumuri rwa ecran yawe cyangwa ukoreshe Sangiza ihuza.
