title: Ni iki gisangizwa iyo ntegura inzira?
---

Kugira ngo hamenyekane urukurikirane, WitnessWork yohereza imyanya y'ikarita y'aho utangirira na buri hantu uhagarara kuri seriveri ya WitnessWork, ihereza iyo myanya gusa kuri HERE, serivisi y'amakarita, kugira ngo ibare urugendo. Amazina, aderesi, inyandiko, ingingo n'amakuru ya gusura ntibyoherezwa na rimwe. Ibyo bisabwa binatwara indangamuntu y'ibanga ya konti ya porogaramu, ifitanye isano no kugura kwawe kw'Umuterankunga, kugira ngo seriveri yemeze imimerere y'Umuterankunga kandi ibare inzira zawe; ntoherezwa kuri HERE. Seriveri ntibika imyanya. Ibika gusa ibihe by'ibisabwa by'inzira zawe byo mu masaha 24 ashize kugira ngo ikoreshe imbibi za buri munsi. Iyo ufunguye inzira, porogaramu yawe y'inzira yakira aderesi ya buri hantu uhagarara, cyangwa imyanya yaho ku kamenyetso washyizeho, nk'uko bigenda iyo ugiye ku muntu umwe. Isesengura ry'imikoreshereze ribika imibare nk'ahantu inzira yari ifite hangana iki, ntirihe aho uri na rimwe.
