title: How do I see a conversation and its follow-up, or bring a buddy along?
category: contacts
---

Tap a conversation in a contact's history or a person on Home's conversations card, and it opens in a sheet without leaving where you are. Tapping a follow-up reminder opens it there too. At the top is who it was with: tap them to open the contact, or use Call, Text, and Navigate. Next is the follow-up: whether it's up next or missed, when, and the topic, with who's going and the reminder when there are any. While it's open, Log Visit records the return visit and Not at Home logs a try right away, with Undo. Edit Follow-Up or Reschedule changes it, and the X on a missed one dismisses it. One you kept links to the visit that kept it, and a dismissed one can be planned again. With no follow-up, the sheet links to your next visit with that person, or offers Plan Follow-Up after the latest one. At the bottom is the conversation itself: what happened, when, your note, and its fields. Tap the pencil to edit, or ••• to reschedule, copy the note or topic, dismiss the follow-up, or delete the conversation; deleting can't be undone. If you use Buddies, invited buddies and their answers show inside the follow-up. While it's still ahead, the dashed + invites someone right away and shares only its date and time, the householder's first name, the address, and the topic. To take someone off, edit the conversation. Follow-up invitations are unavailable in Data Protection Mode. Opening a conversation changes nothing, and only inviting a buddy shares anything. If something looks wrong, contact support from the Help Center and leave out names, addresses, and notes.
