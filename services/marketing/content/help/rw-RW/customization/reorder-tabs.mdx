title: Nshobora guhindura uko utubuto dukurikirana?
---

Yego. Fungura Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Uko utubuto dutondetse ukoreshe imyambi yo hejuru no hasi iri iruhande rwa buri gikoresho cy'ikibuto, cyangwa ukande igihe kirekire ku izina ry'ikibuto kugira ngo ukimure hejuru cyangwa hasi. Uko gukurikirana gushya gukoreshwa ako kanya ku murongo wo hasi ku matelefoni no ku murongo w'ibumoso kuri tablet, kuri iOS na Android. Utubuto dushobora gutondekwa ariko ntidushobora guhishwa. WitnessWork igifungukira ku Ahabanza, kandi Iterambere rigaragara gusa ku nshingano zandika amasaha; rigumana umwanya waryo mu gutondeka iyo uhinduye inshingano. Kuri iPad, ibimenyetso bya Command-nomero bya clavier na byo bikurikiza uko watondetse. Gutondeka ni ihitamo, bityo kubikwa muri backup kandi bihuzwa na iCloud iyo guhuza gufunguye; ntibihindura inyandiko zawe zose. Kugira ngo usubire uko byari bimeze, subiza utubuto kuri Ahabanza, Abantu, Iterambere, Ingengabihe. Niba gutondeka bidahinduka, ongera utangize porogaramu hanyuma ukoreshe Uracyakeneye ubufasha? hepfo.
