title: Inzira yanjye ifungukira ite muri Apple Maps, Google Maps cyangwa Waze?
---

Inzira yawe ifungukira muri Ikarita isanzwe yo gukoresha wahisemo muri Igenamiterere → Amerekezo. Google Maps ifungura inzira yose nk'urugendo rumwe hamwe n'aho uhagarara hose ukurikije urukurikirane. Apple Maps ikora kimwe kuri iOS 18.4 cyangwa irenzeho; ku mavugurura ya kera ifungura ahantu hamwe icyarimwe. Waze ihora ifungura ahantu hamwe icyarimwe kuko amahuza yayo yemera aho ujya hamwe gusa. Iyo ahantu hafungurwa hamwe hamwe, kanda Genda aho uhagarara hajya 1, hanyuma usubire muri WitnessWork ukande buto nanone ku hantu hakurikira, cyangwa ukande umwambi uri iruhande rw'aho uhagarara hose. Buri rugendo rutangirira aho uri, bityo aho wahisemo gutangirira ni ho hantu ha mbere gusa. Niba Google Maps itashyizweho, inzira ifungukira muri porogaramu yo kureba urubuga, ishobora kwerekana ahantu hake; shyiraho porogaramu cyangwa uhitemo indi porogaramu y'inzira. Kuri Android, hitamo Google Maps cyangwa Waze. Niba porogaramu y'ikarita idafunguka, reba ko yashyizweho hanyuma wongere ugerageze.
