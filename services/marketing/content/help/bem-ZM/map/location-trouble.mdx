---
id: locationTrouble
title: Cinshi mapu taingasanga apo ndi nomba?
---

Mapu ifwaya ukuti location yayaka pa WitnessWork. Moneni ifyo mwasala fya foni yenu, elyo mwisule mapu nakabili.

## Amashinte

1. Isuleni ifyo mwasala fya foni yenu elyo mwangalile ukuti location services yayaka.
2. Fwayeni WitnessWork elyo mulesheko location.
3. Isuleni mapu nakabili apo foni yenu yingapokelela signal.

## Nga cabipa

- **Nacili tacibombo:** kuti mwalemba adiresi, nelyo ukubika pin ya muntu wa kulanshanya na minwe, ukwabula apo muli nomba. Ukutuma ubwafya, ingishani foni yenu ne system version, elyo mushilike ifyebo fya bakulanshanya mu fikope.
