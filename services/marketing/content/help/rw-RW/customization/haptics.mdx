title: "Nazimya nte ibinyeganyega?"
---

Zimya Ibinyeganyega mu igenamiterere. WitnessWork ihita ireka ibinyeganyega byayo, nko gukoraho, gukurura ku gishushanyo, n'ibyishimo byo kugera ku ntego.

## Zimya ibinyeganyega

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Amajwi n'Ibinyeganyega**.
2. Zimya **Ibinyeganyega**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ibi bikora kuri iki gikoresho gusa. Ntibihuzwa ku bindi bikoresho byawe.
- Ibintu bimwe bya sisitemu, nka menu n'utubuto, bishobora gukomeza kunyeganyega. Ibihindure mu igenamiterere ry'amajwi n'ibinyeganyega bya telefoni yawe.
