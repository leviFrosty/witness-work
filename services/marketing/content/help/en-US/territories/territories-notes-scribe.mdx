title: Does Scribe AI read my territory notes, and what is sent?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"

Yes. When Scribe AI is on and available, it reads the notes you add with **Add Results From Notes**. Nothing is added until you check the entries and tap **Add Entries**.

## What is sent

- The text of your notes, the names of the territory's streets, and your app language.
- Not sent: the territory's number, its do-not-calls, and its other records.

Scribe AI doesn't store your notes or use them for training, and WitnessWork doesn't keep them. It records only how much you used.

## What comes back

Only house numbers, streets, and results (**Not at Home**, **Spoke**, **Do Not Call**). Names, reasons, phone numbers, and other words are dropped, so a do-not-call keeps only its address.

## When your phone reads the notes instead

If Scribe AI is off, offline, at its limit, unavailable, or the notes are too long, WitnessWork reads them on your phone. The screen says why. Nothing leaves your phone, but it understands common English shorthand only. See [Why didn't Scribe AI read my notes?](/help/territories/territories-notes-trouble)

## Good to know

- Reading notes counts toward your Scribe AI limits, but a few lines use very little. See [How do Scribe AI limits work?](/help/scribe/scribe-usage)
- The first time, Scribe AI asks you to agree to its terms, as it does everywhere in the app.
