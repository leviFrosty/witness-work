title: Nabona nte abantu bagomba gusurwa?
---

Munsi y'aho gushakisha muri Abantu, kanda akarango kugira ngo werekane gusa abantu bo muri iryo tsinda: Bamaze igihe, Uku kwezi, Iki cyumweru, cyangwa Ntiwigeze ubasura. Buri karango kagaragaza umubare w'abantu bakora kabamo, kandi ibara ryako rihura n'iry'akamenyetso k'umuntu ku ikarita. Nyuza uturango ku ruhande kugira ngo ubirebe byose. Kanda akarango nanone, cyangwa ukande Byose, kugira ngo werekane bose. Uturango tumwe tuba hejuru y'ikarita, aho tubara gusa abantu bafite aho baherereye kandi tunyungurura utumenyetso; dukubama iyo wimura ikarita, bityo kanda umutwe Abantu kugira ngo udusubize. Kugira ngo uhindure uko dukurikirana, kanda akarango, utegereze gato, hanyuma ugakurure ku murongo; Byose guhora ari ako mbere. Gutondeka ni ihitamo, bityo kubikwa muri backup kandi bihuzwa na iCloud Sync kuri iOS. Amatsinda yubakwa ku gusura kwawe kwa nyuma, kandi "Ntari mu rugo" bibarwa nk'gusura. Kugira ngo uhindure igihe umuntu ava mu itsinda ajya mu rindi, cyangwa amabara, kanda buto y'amakuru iri ku mpera y'uturango (ku ikarita, buto y'ibisobanuro) hanyuma ikaramu. Akarango kongeraho umuyunguruzi ushobora no kubona no gukuraho muri Tondeka & Shungura, kandi gakoreshwa ku rutonde no ku ikarita kimwe. Abantu warekeye ntibabarwa. Nta kintu gisangizwa; amatsinda abarwa ku gikoresho cyawe.
