Each tab on Progress (Month, Year and All-time) has its own cards under its summary, and you choose them separately. Removing a card never deletes your time or reports.

## Move, remove or resize cards

1. Press and hold any card, or tap **Edit Cards** after your cards. The cards start to jiggle.
2. Drag a card to move it.
3. To remove a card, tap the gray minus at its top corner. Tap **Undo** if you didn't mean to.
4. To make a card small or wide, drag the curved handle at its bottom corner, or tap it. Cards that come in only one size have no handle.
5. Tap **Done**, or tap the summary at the top. On Android, the back button works too.

## Bring back a card

1. Start editing as above.
2. Tap **Add Cards** at the top to see every card that tab offers.
3. Choose the card.

To start over on a tab, tap **Edit Cards** there, then **Reset Cards**.

## Cards on each tab

- **Month:** Daily Pace, Studies, Days Out, Visits, Credit, Weeks, Projected Month, Service Year, Year Pace, Streak, Lifetime and Days.
- **Year:** Service Year Pace, Needed a Month, Milestones, Months, Average, Best Month, Last Year, Days Out, Months Out, Studies and Visits.
- **All-time:** Best Year, Average, Best Month, Days Out, Months Out, Studies and Visits.

## Months and Days lists

Months on Year and Days on Month come in two lengths. Compact is a short list that scrolls on its own. Full Length shows every month or day at once. To switch, start editing and drag the card's handle down or up, or tap it.

To lift one of these lists, hold its title or headings rather than a row, since each row has its own menu.

On iPad, on an iPhone Duo's inner display, and in a wide browser window, Months has its own column beside the other cards. It shows every month, and it can be removed but not moved.

## Good to know

- The summary at the top of each tab always stays, and so does the list of years on All-time.
- Some cards only appear when they apply. Daily Pace and Projected Month need a monthly goal. Year Pace, Service Year Pace and Needed a Month need an annual goal. Milestones needs a role with an annual goal. Credit needs a role whose report counts hours. Streak needs streaks turned on, and Mileage needs mileage tracking.
- If your role changes, your arrangement is kept. Cards your new role doesn't have come back in the same place if you return to a role that has them.
- Your cards sync with your other devices when sync is on, and are included in backups.
