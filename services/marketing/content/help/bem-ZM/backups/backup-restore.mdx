---
id: backupRestore
title: Bushe kuti nasunga no kubwesha ifyebo fyandi shani?
---

Isuleni Settings → Ukusunga no Kubwesha → Panga Backup nelyo Bwesha ukufuma ku Backup pa kutuma nelyo ukubwesha backup ya JSON pa iOS nelyo Android. Pa cibombelo cipya, kuti mwasala na Ukufuma ku failo ya kusunga pa cipao ca kupanga ica “Tendekeni uko mwaleka”; ukubwesha kuko kupwisha ukupanga na profile yenu iyabweshiwa. Amabackup yakwata ifyalembwa fya mulimo, ubupange, abantu ba kulanshanya, intandalilo, amacipande, profile, ne fyo mwasala ifya kusabankanya. Ukubwesha kucinja ifyebo fya pa cibombelo ifyashilwamo; amabackup ya kale kuti yabula amacipande nelyo profile, ifyo fisala ifyo fine. Icishibilo ca cibombelo, ifyo mwasala fya iCloud nelyo Google Drive sync, data protection mode, ukusuminisha analytics, ne fyo mwapita mu onboarding fisala pa cibombelo ici. Ifikope tafikwatwa mu mabackup ya JSON. Pa fikope fya iCloud pa iOS, washeni ukulunganikanya ifikope mu nshila yekala. Sungeni backup ya nomba ilyo mushilabwesha kabili ibikeni pa ncende ya cinkama; ingakwata ifyebo fya bene ng'anda. Bomfyeni intambo ya Pa Kwafwilisha iya kwafwa mu cinkama pa kupokelela ukwafwa.
