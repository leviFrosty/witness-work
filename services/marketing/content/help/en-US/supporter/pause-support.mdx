title: Can I pause my support instead of cancelling?
category: supporter
platforms:
  - ios
  - android
---

Yes. When you start to cancel, WitnessWork offers a pause first.

## Pause on iPhone or iPad

1. Open **Settings → Manage subscription**.
2. Tap **Cancel support**.
3. Choose to pause payments for 1, 3 or 6 months.

The pause starts when your current period ends. You aren't charged during it, and Supporter features stay on. Payments then resume at the same amount on the date shown.

## Pause on Android

1. Open **Settings → Manage subscription**.
2. Tap **Cancel support**.
3. Choose **Pause** in Google Play, and pick 1 to 3 months. This works for monthly subscriptions.

Supporter features pause too, then return on their own with your settings kept. You can resume early in Google Play.

## Good to know

- You can pause once every 12 months.
- You can still cancel any time in your store's subscriptions.
- Pause isn't offered during a free trial, with a payment problem, with a Family Sharing subscription, or within 12 months of your last pause.

## If something goes wrong

- **The pause fails:** nothing changes. Try again later, or manage your subscription in your store.
- **Anything else:** use **Still need help?** to contact support privately.
