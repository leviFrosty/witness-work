title: "Ndi umupayiniya wa bwite, kuki porogaramu indangira intego y'umwaka?"
---

Abapayiniya ba bwite nta Ntego y'umwaka bagira mu buryo busanzwe. Kugira ngo uyizimye, fungura **Igenamiterere → Umwirondoro** ushyire **Intego y'umwaka** kuri **Oya**.

Intego yawe ya buri kwezi igumaho, kandi umurongo w'umwaka ku bwoko bwa **Umwaka** bw'igice cya **Iterambere** urahishwa.
