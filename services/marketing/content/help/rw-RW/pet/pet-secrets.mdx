title: "Ingofero n'ibintu by'icyumba by'ibanga ni ibihe?"
---

Ingofero n'ibintu by'icyumba by'ibanga ntibigurishwa na rimwe. Buri kimwe gifunguka cyonyine uko ukomeza gusohoka mu murimo wo kubwiriza no kwita ku itungo ryawe.

Kugeza ubonye kimwe, Iduka ryerekana igicucu cy'umukara gusa kitwa **???**. Icyo gifunguka ryari ni igice cy'igitangaza.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ibiceri ntibishobora kubigura, kandi nta na kimwe giterwa n'amasaha, mugenzi, Inkurikirane yawe y'umurimo, cyangwa gahunda zawe.
- Ntushobora kubura kimwe burundu. Ushobora kubona ikiri cyo cyose nyuma.
- Bimwe bifata igihe kirenze umwaka. Bibara amezi wasohokeyemo nibura umunsi umwe, si amezi akurikirana.
- Buri gice uretse **Idirishya** na **Icyapa** gifite iby'ibanga.
- Gishya kivuga **Gishya** mu makuru y'itungo ryawe. Gihitemo nk'ikindi kintu cyose.
- Iyo kimwe gifunguka, ikarita ntoya iza hejuru y'ekara. Yikande kugira ngo urebe ikintu, cyangwa uyikure. Niba uyibuze, inzogera y'amenyesha iri kuri Ahabanza irayibika kugeza ufunguye **Ingofero n'icyumba**.
- Ibanga wabonye bigumya kuba ibyawe kandi bihuzwa n'itungo ryawe.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ibanga wari witeze ntiribonetse:** ongera ufungure amakuru y'itungo ryawe nyuma y'uko ibikoresho byawe birangiza guhuza.
