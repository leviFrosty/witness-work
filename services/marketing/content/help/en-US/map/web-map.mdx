On the web, the Contacts map, pin pickers and Route Planning use Apple Maps. Pins, pin cards, long-press pins, place search and directions work as they do on your phone.

## Good to know

- **Open in Maps** opens Apple Maps on a Mac, iPhone or iPad, and Google Maps on other computers and phones.
- To show where you are, tap the location button (the arrow) and let your browser share your location. If you said no before, allow location for app.witnesswork.app in your browser's site settings, then reload the page.
- Contact names, addresses and notes stay in WitnessWork. Apple's map gets only the map's position, the places to draw, and your location if you allowed it. Like any map in a browser, Apple also sees your IP address.
- Searching for a place or getting directions goes through WitnessWork's server, without your name or account details.

## If something goes wrong

When the map says **Map unavailable**, the rest of WitnessWork keeps working, including your contacts list and route stops.

- **You're offline:** reconnect and tap **Try Again**.
- **The map is busy:** wait a minute, then tap **Try Again**.
- **The map can't load:** tap **Try Again**. If it keeps happening, reload the page, and check that a browser extension isn't blocking apple-mapkit.com or maps.witnesswork.app.
- **The map isn't in your browser yet:** it's still being turned on for the web. Everything else works.

If the map still won't load, report it through [Contact Support](/help) with your browser and its version. Leave contact details out of screenshots.
