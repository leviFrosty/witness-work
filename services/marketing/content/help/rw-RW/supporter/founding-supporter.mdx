title: Umuterankunga wa mbere ni nde?
---

Abaterankunga ba mbere bari basanzwe bateye inkunga WitnessWork mbere y'uko urwego rw'Abaterankunga rutangira muri Gicurasi 2026, igihe inkunga yazanaga nta nyungu na nke. Izina ni ukubashimira gusa. Si urwego rwihariye: Abaterankunga ba mbere bafite ibiranga bimwe n'abandi Baterankunga bose, kandi ntirishobora kugurwa cyangwa kugerwaho ubu. Mu gihe inkunga yawe ikora, izina Umuterankunga wa mbere rigaragara ku mwirondoro wawe no mu Igenamiterere. Riturutse ku itariki watangiyeho kuba Umuterankunga, bityo nta kindi kibikwa cyangwa gisangizwa. Niba inkunga yawe irangiye, izina rihishwa kugeza utanze inkunga nanone. Rigaruka niba konti yawe y'iduka igaragaza itariki yawe y'ibanze, ibyo bikunze kuba iyo wiyandikishije nanone ukoresheje konti imwe. Niba witezeho kurigaragara, fungura Igenamiterere → Ba umuterankunga ukoreshe Garura ibyigeze kugurwa.
