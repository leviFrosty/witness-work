title: "WitnessWork ibika ite aho buri nzu iri, kandi nkoresha nte ikarita y'agace?"
---

Iyo **Bika aho buri nzu iri** ifunguye, kandi isanzwe ifunguye, WitnessWork ibika aho uhagaze igihe cyose wandika inzu. Inzu ihita igaragara nk'akadomo ku ikarita y'agace.

## Fungura ikarita y'agace

1. Fungura agace.
2. Kanda **Ikarita** (cyangwa **Ikarita n'ifoto**).
3. Kanda akadomo urebe inzu, ibyayibayeho n'itariki, n'uwayanditse.

Ikarita inagaragaza inkombe y'agace na "Ntibasurwe". **Erekana aho ndi** yerekana aho uri. Mu rutonde rw'umuhanda, **Erekana ku ikarita** yibanda kuri uwo muhanda. **Erekana agace kose** isubiza ikarita mu bunini bwayo. **Imiyoboro** ku rupapuro rw'agace ifungura porogaramu yawe y'imiyoboro.

## Fungura cyangwa uzimye udukadomo

Koresha akabuto ka **Bika aho buri nzu iri** aho wandikira inzu, cyangwa muri **Hindura umuhanda**.

## Shyira cyangwa ukosore akadomo ukoresheje intoki

1. Fungura umuhanda ukande inzu.
2. Kanda **Shyiraho akadomo** cyangwa **Himura akadomo**.
3. Kanda ku ikarita aho inzu iri.

## Byiza kumenya

- Inzu zandikwa nta kadomo iyo WitnessWork idashobora gukoresha aho uri cyangwa uri kure y'umuhanda. Niba koko uri ku nzu, kanda igishushanyo cy'akadomo aho wandikira, hanyuma **Ndi ku nzu**.
- Uburyo bw'ibanga bw'ifasi ntibubika udukadomo tw'inzu. "Ntibasurwe" igumana akadomo kayo.
- Niba udukadomo tudabitswe, reba [Kuki WitnessWork itabika aho inzu ziri?](/help/territories/territories-location-off)
