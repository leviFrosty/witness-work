---
id: mileageStart
title: How do I track mileage?
category: mileage
---

Mileage Tracking is optional and available for every publisher type. Answer Yes on the "Track mileage?" card on Home, or turn on Track Mileage in Settings → Profile. Then add your first car; only a name is required. Log a trip with Log Trip on Home's Mileage card, from the Add button, or on the Mileage screen. Enter the distance (turn on Round Trip to double it) or switch to Odometer and enter start and end readings. Odometer Start fills in from that car's last reading. Tap a trip to see its details, or long-press it to edit, log it again today, share, or delete it.
