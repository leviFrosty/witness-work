title: Cinshi Plans nelyo ukwita kwa munyina fyabula nelyo fyakale?
---

Plans sha munyina kuti shabula pantu ico mwapempa tacasuminishiwa, ukusabankanya kwakwe kwazimya, nelyo foni yenu iba offline. Kokolenipo pa **Buddies** ukubwekeshapo.

## Moneni ifi

1. Moneni ukuti ico mwapempa casuminishiwa mu **Buddies → Ifyo Bapempa**.
2. Pa **Schedule**, tinikeni bushiku. Ilanga Plans sha banyina bonse sha bushiku ubo.
3. Isuleni **Buddies** elyo mukokolepo ukubwekeshapo. Imwe na munyina mufwaya intaneti.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Utwabalwa munshi ya nshiku tulanga fye banyina abakwata **Langa pa kalenda yandi** iyayaka. Itendeka yazimya pa munyina upya.
- Munyina wenu kuti aleka ukusabankanya nelyo aleka ukusabankanya Plans na imwe. WitnessWork tailamwebo.
- Munyina uusabankanya fye inshita imo alanga **Ilyo bwacibela**, **Pa Kasuba**, **Pa Mulalilo** nelyo **Yapangwa** pa mbali ya nshita.
- Mumona Plans sha mulungu cinelubali ulwakonkapo.
- Plans mwatulula kale shashala shaboneka ukwabula intaneti. Ifyapya fifwaya intaneti.
- Ukumona Plans sha munyina tekubika ku shenu.

## Nga cabipa

- **Takuli ifyakwishibisha fya kwita:** yakeni ifyakwishibisha fya Buddies mu WitnessWork na mu ifyo mwasala fya foni yenu. Kengele ya Home ilanga ukwita. Nga ilanda ukuti tayangalilile, bwelelenipo ku intaneti elyo mutinike **Try Again**.
- **Android:** ifyakwishibisha fya Buddies fyafwaya Google Play services. Ukwabula, ifyapya fyaisa ilyo mwaisula WitnessWork.
- **Buddies ilanda ukuti tailingashinina icibombelo ici:** moneni [Cinshi WitnessWork ilanda ukuti tailingashinina icibombelo ici?](/help/account/account-verification).
