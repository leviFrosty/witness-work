title: "Nzi nte itandukaniro rya passkey zanjye, kandi nakuraho nte imwe?"
---

Buri passkey ya WitnessWork yitwa "WitnessWork" ikurikiwe na kode y'inyuguti 4, nka WitnessWork 7K4Q. Iyo kode imwe igaragara muri **Igenamiterere → Konti → Passkeys**, bityo ushobora kuzihuza.

## Reba passkey zawe

Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Passkeys**. Herekana buri passkey n'igikoresho yongeweho, igihe yongeweho n'igihe yakoreshejwe ubushize. Buri imwe ifite akamenyetso:

- **Ishobora gufungura konti yawe:** kwinjira na yo bizana konti yawe kuri mushakisha cyangwa telefoni nshya.
- **Sign-in only:** igutuma winjira, hanyuma igasaba kurangiza ukoresheje indi telefoni yawe cyangwa urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura. Nyuma yaho, ishobora kuba ishobora gufungura ubutaha.

Kuri telefoni, kanda passkey ya **Sign-in only** uhitemo **Let It Unlock** kugira ngo ongere ugerageze. Ububiko bw'amajambobanga bumwe bushobora kwinjiza gusa.

## Kuraho passkey

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Passkeys**.
2. Kanda passkey.
3. Hitamo **Kuraho**.
4. Emeza ukoresheje Face ID, igikumwe cyangwa kode ya telefoni. Kuri murandasi, emeza ukoresheje imwe muri passkey zawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Kuyikuraho hano ntibiyisiba mu bubiko bw'amajambobanga bwawe. Iyisibe n'aho.
- WitnessWork iraburira mbere yo gukuraho passkey ya nyuma ishobora gufungura konti yawe.
- Ntushobora guhindura izina rya passkey.
- Niba inzogera y'Ahabanza ivuga ko passkey yongeweho kandi atari wowe, iyikureho uhindure urufunguzo rwa konti yawe. Reba [Nakora iki niba telefoni cyangwa mudasobwa yanjye yatakaye cyangwa yibwe?](/help/account/lost-device).
- Mbere yo gukuraho inzira yawe yonyine yo kwinjira, reba ko ufite urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura. Reba [Urufunguzo rwanjye rwo kugarura ni iki, kandi nza kurubika nte?](/help/account/account-recovery-key).
