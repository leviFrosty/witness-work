id: appleWatchUpNext
title: What does Up Next on my Apple Watch show?
category: time
platforms:
  - ios
---

Up Next shows whichever comes first: your next follow-up or your next plan. Plans appear only if you log hours. Add the Up Next complication to a watch face or your Smart Stack: touch and hold your watch face and tap Edit, or use the Watch app on your iPhone. It's also on the watch app's home screen. A follow-up or plan stays up for 15 minutes after it starts, so you can check the address or topic when you arrive, and then the next one takes its place. A plan without a start time shows for its whole day, after that day's timed items. Missed follow-ups don't appear; find them in WitnessWork on your iPhone. Names and topics are hidden while your wrist is down or your watch is locked, because others may see your watch face. Tap Up Next to open it, then tap Directions for directions in Maps on your watch, or Start Timer to start your iPhone's service timer for a plan; the timer needs your iPhone nearby. Up Next looks two weeks ahead and keeps moving along while your iPhone is away. Changes you make on your iPhone reach your watch the next time they connect. If it's out of date, open WitnessWork on your iPhone, then on your watch. For other problems, use the Help Center's private support link and keep householder details out of public reports.
