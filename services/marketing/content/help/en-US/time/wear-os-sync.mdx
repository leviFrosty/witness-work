Your watch shows **Syncing with phone** while time you added on the watch is still on its way to your phone. It finishes when your phone is nearby and you've opened WitnessWork on it.

## What to do

1. Check that your watch is connected to your phone in your watch's companion app.
2. Keep your phone nearby.
3. Open WitnessWork on your phone.
4. Open WitnessWork on your watch.

## Good to know

- The watch total includes the new time right away. Credit Time is added once your phone saves it, because your phone applies your monthly Credit limit.
- Each entry keeps the day you added it, and is saved only once.
- Starting, pausing and stopping the timer need your phone nearby. WitnessWork doesn't need to be open on it.

## If something goes wrong

- **The watch says the timer changed on your phone:** check the time, then try again.
- **The watch says your phone couldn't save an entry:** the time was not added. Try again.
- **The watch asks you to update WitnessWork on your phone:** update it, then try again.
- **The watch stays out of date, or its complications show a dash:** follow the four steps above.
- **Still stuck:** use the Help Center's private support link, and keep your records out of public reports.
