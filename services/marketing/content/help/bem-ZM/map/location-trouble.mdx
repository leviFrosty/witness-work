---
id: locationTrouble
title: Cinshi mapu tailamona incende yandi ya nomba?
---

Moneni ukuti location services yawashiwa kabili WitnessWork ikwete ukusuminishiwa kwa location mu fyo mwasala fya cibombelo. Isuleni mapu nakabili no kwesha uko icibombelo cingasanga incende. Kuti mwabika adiresi nelyo icishibilo ca muntu wa kulanshanya ku maboko ukwabula ukubomfya incende ya nomba. Nga ubwafya bucili bulefikilila, tumeni ubwafya na cibombelo cenu ne version ya operating system, mwatabika ifyebo fya bantu ba kulanshanya mu fikope.
