id: routinePrivacy
title: Who can see my Spiritual Routine?
category: routine
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

From version 1.47.0, what you check off in Spiritual Routine is private to you, on iOS and Android.

- It's kept on your device, and in your sync and backups, like the rest of your records, so it's on your other devices and comes back after you restore a backup.
- It's never shared with your buddies, never shown on your Buddy Card, and never included in your Service Report.
- It has no streaks and no rankings, and it isn't compared with anyone.

WitnessWork's analytics may count how often features are used, such as how many activities were checked on a day, but never what you read, your reading plan's portion, or your notes. You can turn analytics off in Settings.

If you turn Spiritual Routine off, nothing is deleted: your history stays on your devices and returns when you turn it on again.
