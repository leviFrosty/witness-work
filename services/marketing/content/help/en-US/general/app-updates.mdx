Most updates download by themselves while you use WitnessWork. When one is ready, the bell on Home shows **Update Ready**.

## Install a ready update

1. Tap the bell on Home.
2. Tap **Update Now**. WitnessWork restarts into the new version.

If you'd rather wait, leave it there. The update applies the next time you close WitnessWork completely and open it again. Your records, contacts, and notes are not touched.

## Check for an update yourself

1. Pull down on Home. Or tap your profile picture, then **Settings → More**.
2. Tap **Check for update**.

## Good to know

- Some updates come from the App Store, or the Google Play Store app on Android. Keep automatic updates on there too.

## If something goes wrong

- **An update won't download:** connect to the internet and tap **Check for update** again.
