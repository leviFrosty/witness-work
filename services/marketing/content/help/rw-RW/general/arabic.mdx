id: arabic
title: "WitnessWork ikora ite mu Cyarabu no kwandika uva iburyo ujya ibumoso?"
---

Hitamo العربية muri **Igenamiterere → Ururimi**, cyangwa ushyire telefoni yawe mu Cyarabu ukomeze **Igikoresho gisanzwe**. WitnessWork yongera gutangira ikasomwa uva iburyo ujya ibumoso.

## Ibihinduka

- Ecran, menu, ibikorwa byo gukurura, utubari tw'aho ugeze n'ibishushanyo birahindurwa uko biri, kandi imyambi yo gusubira inyuma irareba urundi ruhande.
- Imibare yerekanwa mu ndango z'Icyarabu (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩). Iyo wandika amasaha, intego cyangwa ibirometero, ushobora gukoresha indango z'Icyarabu cyangwa iz'Ikilatini.
- Amezi akoresha amazina yo mu bitabo by'Abahamya ba Yehova byo mu Cyarabu, nka تشرين الأول (أكتوبر).
- Gushakisha muri Aderesi no mu bufasha bibona amagambo y'Icyarabu yaba afite ibimenyetso by'amajwi cyangwa nta byo afite.
- Widgets n'amasaha akurikiza ururimi rwa WitnessWork porogaramu imaze gufungurwa rimwe.

## Ibitahinduka

- Amazina, nimero za telefoni, aderesi n'inyandiko bigumana uko wabyanditse.
- Nimero za telefoni n'aderesi z'imeri zisomwa uva ibumoso ujya iburyo.
- Amakuru yawe amera kimwe mu ndimi zose. Guhindura ururimi bihindura uko yerekanwa gusa.

## Uko ibyumweru n'amatariki byerekanwa

Uko amatariki akurikirana, isaha n'umunsi icyumweru gitangiriraho bikurikiza **Format Region** yawe. Kugira ngo ibyumweru bitangirire kuwa gatandatu, fungura **Igenamiterere → Akarere n’imiterere** uhitemo **Uburasirazuba bwo Hagati (Icyarabu)**. Reka kuri **Byikora (Igikoresho)** kugira ngo ukurikize telefoni yawe.
