title: "Nongeraho nte mugenzi, kandi ni nde wemeza ubusabe?"
---

Fungura **Gahunda**, kanda **Buddies**, hanyuma ukande **Tumira mugenzi**. Mugenzi wawe aremera, nawe ukemeza, mbere y'uko mubona gahunda z'undi.

## Ongeraho mugenzi

1. Fungura **Buddies** muri **Gahunda**.
2. Kanda **Tumira mugenzi** (cyangwa akabuto ka **+**) ushyireho ubutumire.
3. Mugenzi wawe arabufungura agakanda **Emera**.
4. Fungura **Buddies → Ibisabwa** ukande **Emeza**.

Muri kumwe imbonankubone? Kanda **Erekana kode ya Buddies** mugenzi wawe ayisikane muri WitnessWork. Niba hari uwakohereje ihuza, kanda **Andika ihuza ry'ubutumire** ukarihambire.

## Byiza kumenya

- Emeza gusa umuntu wari ugamije gutumira.
- Mubonana gahunda zanyu gusa nyuma y'uko wemeje.
- Kuri Gahunda nshya, kanda **Tumira bagenzi** niba nta mugenzi uragira.
