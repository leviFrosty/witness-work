title: "Nshobora kuzana amakuru ava muri MyTime, Ministry Assistant, cyangwa indi porogaramu?"
---

Ushobora kuzana backup ya MyTime. Dosiye z'izindi porogaramu ntizemewe, ariko ushobora kumanika inyandiko zisomeka muri Scribe AI.

## Zana ukoresheje MyTime

1. Muri MyTime, jya kuri **More → Settings → Backup** wiyoherereze backup kuri imeri.
2. Bika dosiye .mytimedb yometse ku gikoresho cyawe. Kuri Android cyangwa murandasi, yohereze uva muri MyTime kuri iPhone yawe hanyuma ukurure icyometse kuri icyo gikoresho.
3. Muri WitnessWork, fungura **Igenamiterere → Zana amakuru → Kuva kuri MyTime**. Mu gihe cyo gutangira, hitamo **Kuva kuri MyTime** ahubwo.
4. Hitamo dosiye.
5. Reba ibyo uzabona, hanyuma uzane.

## Byiza kumenya

- Kuri murandasi, dosiye igumya muri murandasi yawe kandi ntishyirwa kuri seriveri. Dosiye irenze MB 25 iranga.
- Ku yindi porogaramu, gerageza kohereza inyandiko zisomeka ubimanike muri Scribe AI. Reba ibyo itanga mbere yo kubika.
- Dosiye za backup za WitnessWork zijya muri **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura**, si hano.
- Kugira ngo uzane amakuru ava muri Notes cyangwa impapuro nyuma, koresha **Igenamiterere → Zana amakuru**.

## Niba ivuga ko dosiye atari database

Kuzana bikeneye dosiye y'umwimerere ya .mytimedb yakozwe na MyTime. PDF, inyandiko yoherejwe, backup ya WitnessWork, cyangwa dosiye yahinduwe izina ntibikora. Bika dosiye y'umwimerere uko iri. Niba backup yemewe ikinanirana, andikira ubufasha mu ibanga ushyiremo ikosa na verisiyo yawe ya WitnessWork. Ntukohereze backup mu ruhame.
