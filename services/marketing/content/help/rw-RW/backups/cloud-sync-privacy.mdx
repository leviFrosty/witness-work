title: "Cloud Sync iruta iCloud cyangwa Google Drive mu bwiza bw'ibanga?"
---

Cloud Sync ifite ibanga ryisumbuye. Inyandiko n'amafoto byawe bifungirwa ku gikoresho cyawe mbere yo koherezwa, bityo WitnessWork ntishobora kubisoma. Ibikoresho byawe gusa ni byo bishobora.

## Iyo ugereranyije na iCloud na Google Drive

- **iCloud:** kopi yawe ibikwa muri iCloud Drive yawe. Apple irayirinda, ikayifunga burundu niba wafunguye Advanced Data Protection. WitnessWork ntiyongeraho ifunga ryayo.
- **Google Drive:** kopi yawe ibikwa mu bubiko bwihishe muri Google Drive yawe. Google irayirinda. WitnessWork ntiyongeraho ifunga ryayo.

## Byiza kumenya

- Hamwe na Cloud Sync, seriveri imenya gusa ibintu nk'umubare w'ibikoresho bihuza n'ubunini bwa buri kopi. Ntibona ibiyirimo.
- Niba utakaje ibikoresho byose n'urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura, nta wushobora gusoma kopi zawe za Cloud Sync, harimo natwe.
