Save a backup file in **Settings → Backup & Restore**, and restore it from the same place. The file works on iPhone, Android and the web, so you can also use it to move your records from one to the other.

For an automatic backup instead, see [What is WitnessWork Backup?](/help/backups/account-backup).

## Create a backup file

1. Open **Settings → Backup & Restore**.
2. Tap **Create Backup**.
3. Choose how to lock the file. **My Account** opens on your devices signed in to your account, or anywhere with your recovery key. **Password** (at least 8 characters) opens on any device with that password, so use it to share a copy.
4. Save the file, named witness-work-backup.json.

On the web, the file downloads to your browser's download folder.

## Restore from a backup file

1. Open **Settings → Backup & Restore**.
2. Tap **Restore From Backup**.
3. Choose your backup file. Enter its password if it has one.

On a new phone, you can instead tap **I already use WitnessWork** when setup starts, then choose **From a backup file** under **Other ways to restore** on the **Welcome back** screen.

## Good to know

- A restore replaces the records on this device that the backup includes. Save a current backup first.
- The file includes service records, plans, contacts, visits, categories, mileage, your pet, profile and shared settings. It leaves out photos, shared cars, and this device's own settings.
- Nobody can open a locked file without its password or your account, and WitnessWork can't recover a forgotten password.
- A backup made with a newer WitnessWork can't be restored until you update.
- Keep the file and its password private. It can hold householder details.

## If something goes wrong

- **It says to sign in first:** sign in to your account on this phone with **Sign In with QR Code** or your recovery key, then try again.
- **It says the file is locked to another account:** restore it on a device signed in to that account, or create a password-locked backup there.
- **Some items couldn't be read:** the rest is restored, and WitnessWork tells you how many were left out.
- **Still stuck:** use the private support option in the Help Center.
