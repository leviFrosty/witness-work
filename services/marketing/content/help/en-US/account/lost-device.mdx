Act soon, from another device. Do the same if the bell on Home tells you about a sign-in or passkey that wasn't you.

If you have no other signed-in device, first sign in on a computer or another phone with a passkey or your recovery key.

## Protect your account

1. Open **Settings → Account → Signed-In Devices** on a device that's still signed in. Tap the lost phone or browser and sign it out.
2. If it's an Apple device, remove it from your Apple Account. Otherwise it receives your new key.
3. Open **Settings → Account → Change Account Key** and follow the steps. See [How do I change my account key?](/help/account/change-account-key).
4. Open **Settings → Account → Account Activity** and look for sign-ins you don't recognize.
5. Open **Settings → Account → Passkeys**. For a passkey that isn't yours, tap it and choose **Remove**.

## Good to know

- Signing out isn't enough on its own. A phone that still has your account can sign back in, so change your key if someone else may have it.
- A browser you sign out is erased the next time it connects. Anything it hadn't synced is lost.
- Sign the browser out before you change the key. A browser still signed in when the key changes keeps its records.
- If your recovery key was saved on the lost device, changing your key replaces it.
- Whoever has your recovery key has your account. No one at WitnessWork can override that.
- Lost your recovery key too? See [What if I lose access to my WitnessWork account?](/help/account/lost-access).
