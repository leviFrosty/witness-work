id: stopSupporting
title: What happens if I stop supporting?
category: supporter
---

To stop, open Settings → Manage subscription and tap Cancel support, or cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Before you go, you can tell us why in a short, optional survey. It's sent anonymously and never includes your records. Cancel where you subscribed: if this device can't manage your subscription, use the link in your receipt email. Supporter access continues until the end of the period you've paid for, and your existing data and local backups remain available. After that, your sync keeps working: Cloud Sync, iCloud Sync and Google Drive Sync don't depend on Supporter access, and neither do your photos on Cloud Sync. If your version of WitnessWork is older than 1.47.0, update it; older versions pause sync until Supporter access returns. What ends is the Supporter extras: your accent color and app icon return to the defaults (your choices are kept for when you support again), Shortest Order and Start Route in Plan Route need Supporter again, and Scribe AI goes back to the monthly limit. If you only need a break, Cancel support offers a pause first on iPhone, iPad and Android. Thank you for supporting WitnessWork: your support built Cloud Sync and WitnessWork on the web.
