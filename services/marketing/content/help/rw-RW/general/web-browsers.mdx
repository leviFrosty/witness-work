id: webBrowsers
title: "WitnessWork kuri murandasi ikora mu zihe mushakisha?"
---

WitnessWork kuri murandasi igeragezwa kuri Chrome, Safari na Firefox bigezweho, kuri mudasobwa, telefoni na tablet. Gumana mushakisha yawe ivuguruye. Izindi mushakisha zigezweho, nka Microsoft Edge, zishobora gukora ariko ntizigeragezwa.

## Byiza kumenya

- Kwinjira ukoresheje passkey bikenera mushakisha n'ububiko bw'amajwi y'ibanga cyangwa igikoresho bikorana na passkey. Reba [Passkey ni iki?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
- WitnessWork ibika amakuru yawe muri mushakisha. Iyo ayo makuru asibwe, wongera kwinjira, kandi impinduka zitari zahuzwa zirabura. Reba [WitnessWork ibika iki muri mushakisha yanjye?](/help/general/web-storage).

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Kwinjira na passkey ntibitangwa cyangwa biranga:** ahubwo hitamo **Use my phone** cyangwa **Koresha urufunguzo rwo kugarura** kuri ecran yo kwinjira.
