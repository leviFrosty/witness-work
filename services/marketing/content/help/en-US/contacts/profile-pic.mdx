Yes. Open a contact, tap the avatar, and pick a photo from your library or take a new one. On the web, you choose a photo file, or on a phone you can take one, and crop it the same way. This is especially handy when contacts share names or when a photo helps you spot the right door.

From version 1.47.0, with Cloud Sync, contact photos and your profile photo sync to your other devices on their own, end-to-end encrypted, using your account's 2 GB of photo storage (see “Do photos sync with Cloud Sync?”). Replacing or removing a photo frees its space right away. With iCloud (Google Drive on Android), turn on Include images in sync in Settings → Device Sync. A device with an older version of WitnessWork shows initials instead of a photo that synced from another device.
