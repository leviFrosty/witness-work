title: Kuki ibimenyetso bibara amezi aho kubara amasaha?
---

Kugira ngo buri wese akure ku muvuduko umwe, uko ibimeze kose. Buri kimenyetso kibara amezi y'ikalendari, ntikibara amasaha cyangwa intego. Ukwezi kubarwa rimwe, waba warabwirije iminota mike cyangwa umunsi wose, kandi waba wemeza ko wagize uruhare mu murimo cyangwa wandika amasaha nk'umupayiniya. Ibimenyetso ntibisubira kuri zeru cyangwa ngo bishingire ku mezi akurikirana, bityo kuruhuka kubera uburwayi, ingendo cyangwa igihe kigoye nta cyo bigukuraho; ukwezi gukurikira ugize uruhare mu murimo kurabarwa. Urukurikirane rwawe ni urundi kandi ntirwigera rugira ingaruka ku bimenyetso byawe. Umwaka wose ubara imyaka y'umurimo ifite nibura amezi 10 yo kugira uruhare mu murimo, bityo amezi abiri utagiyemo mu mwaka w'umurimo ntabarwa nabi. Kubera ko buri kwezi kubarwa rimwe gusa, urwego rwo hejuru, Imaragarita, rumara imyaka. Ni ko bigenewe.
