title: "Nshyikiriza nte umuhanda mugenzi wanjye, kandi nzawusubirana nte?"
---

Gushyikiriza bigira mugenzi wawe nyir'umuhanda umwe ngo awubwirize ku giti cye. Ukomeza kubona aho ageze, kandi ushobora kuwisubiza.

## Shyikiriza umuhanda

Ukeneye Buddies yashyizweho, murandasi, kandi ugomba kuba nyir'agace.

1. Kanda ufate umuhanda hanyuma ukande **Shyikiriza**. Cyangwa ufungure agace, ukande **Sangiza**, hanyuma **Shyikiriza umuhanda**.
2. Hitamo umuhanda munsi ya **Umuhanda uwuhe**, na mugenzi wawe munsi ya **Shyikiriza**.
3. Kanda **Shyikiriza** n'izina rya mugenzi wawe hanyuma wemeze.

Umuhanda ugumya ku rutonde rwawe, ugaragara nk'uwashyikirijwe, kugeza igihe mugenzi wawe awemereye. Abona amenyesha n'ubutumire mu nzogera iri kuri Ahabanza. Akanda **Emera**, maze umuhanda ukagaragara munsi ya **Nashyikirijwe** mu gice cye cya Uduce.

## Igihe wawushyikirije

- Umuhanda uba wo gusoma gusa kuri wowe n'abo wasangije agace. Imirongo yawe y'abashyitsi ntiwugeraho.
- Ubona inzu ze uko azandika. Iyo awugaragaje ko wabwirijwe, urutonde rwawe rwerekana "Wabwirijwe na" n'izina rye.
- Ashobora kuwusangiza bagenzi be, ariko ntashobora kongera kuwushyikiriza undi.

## Wisubirane

Fungura umuhanda ukande **Subirana**. Inzu yanditse zigumaho. Mugenzi wawe na we ashobora gukanda **Subiza**.

Iyo ntawe wemeye mu minsi 7, ubutumire burarangira kandi umuhanda ugumaho. Kugarura agace bigarura imihanda yose yashyikirijwe.
