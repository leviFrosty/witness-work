---
id: cloudSyncFromWeb
title: Mulandu nshi foni yandi yacinjile ku Cloud Sync pa numa ya kwingila pa web?
---

Ilyo mwingila pa web, ikwipusha ukuti muwashe [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync), pantu web ibomba fye na Cloud Sync. Nga mwasala **Washa**, foni yenu icinja ku Cloud Sync ilyo mwasula WitnessWork pa yo ilyo ikonkapo.

## Takuli cilubwa

- Foni yenu isunga ifyo mwalemba ne fikope fyonse elyo ilifilunganya ne fyaba kale pa Cloud Sync.
- Takuli cintu mwafumyepo icibwela.
- Amafoni yenu yambi pa akaunti imo nayo yacinja, cila yo ilyo mwasula WitnessWork pa yo ilyo ikonkapo.
- Ifyo mwalemba fya mu fyasuma. Ifibombelo fyenu fye e fingabelenga.

## Ukubwelela

1. Pa foni yenu, isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Sync**.
2. Saleni iCloud (Google Drive pa Android) nelyo Off.

Foni yenu yashala pa nshila mwasala. Web ipusha nakabili ilyo mwaibomfya ilyo ikonkapo.

## Nga tacinja

- Moneni ukuti foni yenu ili pa intaneti elyo mwapwisha ukupanga WitnessWork, elyo musule app nakabili.
- Nga web ilanda **Kushani WitnessWork pa foni yenu**, kushani app ukufuma ku App Store nelyo Google Play, elyo musule.
- Lyonse kuti mwasala Cloud Sync mwe mwine mu **Ifyo Mwasala → Sync**.
