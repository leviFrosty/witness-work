title: Cinshi motoka yandi iyasabankanywa taileluka, nelyo cinshi nshingasabankanya nelyo ukwingila imo?
---

Ifyo mwacinja pa motoka iyasabankanywa fyafika kuli bonse mu masekondi ilyo ama app yabo yaisulwa. Ifyo mwacinja ilyo muli offline fyashala pa foni yenu elyo fisabankanywa ilyo mwabwela pa intaneti.

## Moneni ifyacinja nomba

Kokolenipo pa cikope ca **Amakilomita**.

## Nga cabipa

Icakwishibisha cinono pa mutwe wa **Amakilomita** cilanda umulandu ifyo mwacinja tafingasabankanywa. Ifyo mwacinja fyashala pa foni yenu mpaka fyangasabankanywa.

- **Muli offline:** fisabankanywa ilyo mwapwatikana.
- **Update WitnessWork:** bikeni icapya ukufuma ku App Store nelyo Google Play.
- **Ifyo mwacinja tafisabankanywa nomba:** akaunti yenu yafintwilwa. Nga mulefwaya ukuti ni ukupusa, washeni support.
- **Shared cars aren't available right now:** tayabomba pa nshita. Ifyo mwacinja fisabankanywa ilyo yabwela.
- **Check its date and time:** wachi ya foni yenu yaya pa ntanshi. Bikeni bushiku ne nshita ku fyeka mu ifyo mwasala fya foni yenu.
- **Shorten long trip notes:** ico mwacinja cikalamba ukusabankanya. Fupisheni ilembo lya lwendo.
- **A shared car isn't ready on this device yet:** ifyo mwacinja fisabankanywa ilyo yaba ukuti yalungama.

## Ubwafya bwa ukusabankanya nelyo ukwingila

- **Takuli akatini ka Share Car…, nelyo takuli banyina bakwita:** mufwaya Buddies yapangwa na munyina umo pa kubala. Nga tamwamona **People** mu ifyo mwasala fya motoka, cinjeni WitnessWork.
- **"This car already has the most people it can.":** motoka ikwata abantu 10, ukubikako ukwita ukulolela. Uwatumine ukwita, nelyo nyina motoka, kuti afumyapo ukwita, elyo nyina motoka kuti afumyapo umuntu.
- **Ukwita kwapwa nelyo kwabula:** ukwita kushala kwaisulwa inshiku 7. Papeni uuli pa motoka ukwita kupya.
- **"This note is too long to share on this car.":** fupisheni ilembo ukusunga ulwendo.
- **"Someone who joined" mu People:** ukwingila kwa muntu uyo takwapwile. Nyina motoka kuti amufumyapo, elyo kuti bamwita nakabili.
- **Tamwingaleka motoka, nelyo Fumyapo Ifyebo Fyandi ifya Buddies tailapwa:** ukuleka kufwaya intaneti. Esheni nakabili ilyo mwapwatikana.

Nga ubwafya bwatwalilila, washeni support ya cisuma munshi ya **nomba line kabila afwilisha?** na akatini ka support pa mutwe wa cikope ici, elyo mwingishe version ya app yenu. Mushilike amashina ya ma motoka, amalembo ya fyalweendo, ne mashina ya bantu mu malipoti ya bantu bonse.
