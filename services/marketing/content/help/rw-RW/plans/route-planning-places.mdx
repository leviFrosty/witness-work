title: "Nahitamo nte aho inzira yanjye itangirira n'aho irangirira, cyangwa nkabika Mu rugo n'Inzu y'Ubwami yanjye?"
---

Kanda aho hari munsi ya **Itangira** hejuru y'inzira, n'aho hari munsi ya **Irangira** hasi. Ushobora kandi kubika Mu rugo n'Inzu y'Ubwami yawe kugira ngo biboneke ukanze rimwe.

## Hitamo aho utangirira

1. Kanda aho hari munsi ya **Itangira**.
2. Hitamo aho uri ubu, aho uhagarara bwa mbere, Mu rugo, Inzu y'Ubwami yawe, imwe muri gahunda z'umunsi ifite aho iri, cyangwa ushakishe ahandi hose.
3. Kanda igihe kugira ngo uhindure igihe ugenda.

Niba gahunda ifite aho iri itangira mbere y'Gusubirayo kwawe kwa mbere, nk'iteraniro ry'umurimo wo kubwiriza, inzira itangirira aho muri icyo gihe.

## Hitamo aho urangirira

1. Kanda aho hari munsi ya **Irangira**.
2. Hitamo aho uhagarara bwa nyuma (bisanzwe), aho watangiriye, Mu rugo, Inzu y'Ubwami yawe, cyangwa ushakishe ahandi.

Irangira si aho uhagarara, bityo nta gihe kihamara. Urugendo rujyayo rurongerwaho, kandi igihe cyo kurangira ni igihe wahagerera.

## Bika Mu rugo cyangwa Inzu y'Ubwami yawe

1. Muri menu ya **Itangira** cyangwa **Irangira**, cyangwa muri **Ongeraho aho uhagarara**, hitamo gushyiraho Mu rugo cyangwa Inzu y'Ubwami yawe.
2. Shakisha aderesi, cyangwa ushyireho akadomo ku gice cya **Ikarita**.

Kugira ngo uhindure cyangwa ukureho kimwe, kanda ugifate muri **Ongeraho aho uhagarara**.

## Byiza kumenya

- **Uburyo bugufi** bushyira aho uhagarara mu mpuzandengo uhereye aho utangirira kandi bugasiga Irangira nka nyuma.
- Ahantu hakubitswe hagumya kuri iyi telefoni kandi ntihahuzwa.
