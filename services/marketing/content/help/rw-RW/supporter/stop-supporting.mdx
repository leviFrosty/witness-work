id: stopSupporting
title: Bigenda bite mu gihe nahagaritse gushyigikirwa?
---

Kugira ngo uhagarike, fungura Igenamiterere → Gucunga ifatabuguzi uhitemo Gusesa cyangwa guhindura ifatabuguzi, cyangwa uhagarike mu mafatabuguzi yawe ya App Store cyangwa Google Play. Uburenganzira bw'Umuterankunga bukomeza kugeza ku iherezo ry'igihe wishyuriye, kandi amakuru yawe asanzweho na backup zo ku gikoresho bikomeza kuboneka. Nyuma yaho, iCloud Sync irahagarara mu gihe uburenganzira bw'Umuterankunga budahari. Niba guhuza byari bifunguye, bikomeza uburenganzira bugarutse; niba warahisemo kuzimya, bikomeza kuba bizimye. Guhuza amafoto bikurikiza igenamiterere ryako. Ibara ryawe nyamukuru n'ishusho ya porogaramu bisubira ku bisanzwe, ariko ibyo wahisemo bibikwa kugeza ukomeje gutera inkunga. Uburyo bw'abantu bubitswe buhishwa kandi ntibugikoreshwa, bityo Abantu hagaragaramo imyunguruzi yawe bwite; bugaruka uteye inkunga nanone. Reba Igenamiterere → iCloud Sync ku mimerere y'ubu. Niba ukeneye kuruhuka gusa, ushobora guhagarika by'agateganyo aho gusesa.
