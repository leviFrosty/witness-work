---
id: mileageSync
title: Does my mileage sync, and does it work on Android?
category: mileage
---

Mileage works on iOS and Android. Cars, fuels, prices, and trips sync between your devices with Device Sync, which is included for everyone from version 1.47.0 (earlier versions need Supporter access), using the option you choose in Settings → Device Sync: Cloud Sync, which also syncs between iPhone, Android, and the web, or iCloud (Google Drive on Android). They're included in WitnessWork Backup (Settings → Account) and in backups from Settings → Backup & Restore on both platforms. Mileage doesn't appear on your calendar or Service Report, and it isn't shared with Buddies, except a car you choose to share. From version 1.47.0, you can share a car with buddies to keep one history for a car you drive together (see “What are shared cars, and how do I share a car with my family?”). A shared car syncs on its own, end-to-end encrypted, and isn't part of Device Sync, WitnessWork Backup, or backup files (see “Do shared cars show on my other devices, my watch, and in backups?”).
