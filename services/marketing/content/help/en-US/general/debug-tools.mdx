id: debugTools
title: What does Debugging Tools do?
category: general
---

Debugging Tools helps troubleshoot problems with support. From version 1.47.0, open Settings → About → App Info and tap the version number under the app name 7 times; a check mark appears next to the version while it's on. Before version 1.47.0, turn it on in Settings → Advanced. It applies only to the device you turn it on for, on iOS and Android. It adds a Tools tab with diagnostic, test-data, and repair actions, shows extra technical details on some screens, and turns on more detailed diagnostic logging. Some Tools actions add sample data or permanently delete data, so only use them when support asks, and back up first under Settings → Backup & Restore. Turning it on or off doesn't change your data. To hide the Tools tab and details again, turn off the switch at the top of the Tools tab, or tap the version in App Info 7 times again.
