title: How do I see a badge up close or share it?
category: badges
---

Tap any badge and it opens full screen, with its level, what it counts and when you earned it.

## See a badge up close

1. Tap a badge on your profile, on the **Badges** screen, or on a buddy's page in Buddies.
2. Tap the badge to spin it.
3. To close it, tap the X, swipe down, or use Back on Android.

Under your own badge, a line like "Next: Pearl · 41 of 60 months" shows how far it is from the next level. Tap the info button beside it to see what helps it grow. A level not reached yet shows **Not yet** and how to get there.

## Share a badge

Take a screenshot. It shows your name, as in "Anna's badge", and WitnessWork at the bottom. Nothing is sent on its own. Your name comes from **Settings → Profile**. Without a name, the screen leaves it off.

## Good to know

- "Found in your history" means older records reached it, and the month isn't known.
- With Buddies, your buddies' reactions appear under your own badge, and a buddy's badge lets you send one. See [What do my buddies see of my badges, and how do reactions work?](/help/badges/badges-buddies)
