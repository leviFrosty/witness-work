title: WitnessWork ishyigikira indimi ki, kandi nsobanura nte ubusobanuzi bwakozwe nabi?
---

WitnessWork iboneka mu ndimi 18. Ikurikiza ururimi rw'igikoresho cyawe mu buryo busanzwe; kugira ngo uhitemo urundi, fungura Igenamiterere uhindure Ururimi. Niba ururimi rwawe rudashyigikiwe, porogaramu yerekana Icyongereza. Ubusobanuro bukorwa hakoreshejwe AI, bityo ijambo cyangwa interuro bishobora rimwe na rimwe kuba bidahuye, cyane cyane amagambo Abahamya ba Yehova bakoresha mu buryo bwihariye. Kugira ngo umenyeshe imwe, koresha ihuza rya imeri riri muri Uracyakeneye ubufasha? ushyiremo ururimi rwawe, ecran, inyandiko ubona, n'icyo yagombye kuvuga. Ifoto ya ecran irafasha; banza upfuke amazina, aderesi n'inyandiko. Ibikosorwa bigera mu ivugurura rya porogaramu rikurikira. Nta mushinga w'abakorerabushake w'ubusobanuro wo kwifatanyamo ukiriho.
