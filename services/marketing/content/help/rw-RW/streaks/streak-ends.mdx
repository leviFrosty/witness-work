title: Urukurikirane rwanjye rurangira ryari, kandi nabigenza nte ngo rukomeze?
---

Umunsi wari ufite gahunda ushobora kwandikwa kugeza ku iherezo ry'umunsi ukurikiraho. Iyo bwije nta gihe cyanditswe, urukurikirane rwawe rurangira. Iyo rwenda kurangira, urumuri ruri kuri Ahabanza rugaragaza igihe gisigaye, kandi Urwibutso rw'urukurikirane ruza saa kumi n'ebyiri z'umugoroba ku munsi wa nyuma. Ku babwiriza, ukwezi gushobora kwandikwa kugeza ku iherezo ry'ukwezi gukurikira, kandi urwibutso ruza iminsi itatu mbere y'uko ukwezi kurangira. Niba udashobora kujya kubwiriza ku munsi wari ufite gahunda, kuraho gahunda z'uwo munsi hakiri kare: kanda buri imwe kuri Ingengabihe, ukande Ibindi (•••), hanyuma Siba → Siba iyi gahunda. Ku gahunda yisubiramo, itariki imwe gusa ni yo ikurwaho. Umunsi ukomeza kugira gahunda igihe cyose hari izisigaye kuri wo. Niba wagiye kubwiriza ariko ukibagirwa kwandika, ongeraho igihe kuri uwo munsi maze urukurikirane rwawe ruragaruka. Zimya Inyibutsa z'urukurikirane muri Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Inkurikirane n'Ibimenyetso. Zisaba ko amamenyesha yemerewe WitnessWork.
