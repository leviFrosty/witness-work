title: "Kuki igisubizo cyanjye kivuga ngo Ntiharoherezwa, cyangwa ubusabe bwanjye bukavuga Gutegereza koherezwa?"
---

Igisubizo cyawe cyangwa ubusabe bwawe ntiburagera kuri mugenzi wawe, akenshi kubera ko telefoni yawe nta murandasi ifite. Nta mpamvu yo gusubiza cyangwa gusaba nanone. WitnessWork ibyohereza iyo uhuye.

## Ohereza vuba

1. Ihuze na murandasi.
2. Fungura Gahunda cyangwa gusubirayo ukande **Ongera ugerageze**. Cyangwa fungura **Buddies** umanure kugira ngo uvugurure.

## Byiza kumenya

- **Ndagenda** yoherezwa ako kanya.
- **Sinshobora** itegereza amasegonda 15 mu gihe wayikanze kubera impanuka. Kanda **Hindura** kugira ngo ukosore. Kuva muri porogaramu bihita biyohereza.
- **Ntiharoherezwa** cyangwa **Gutegereza koherezwa** bivuga ko byabitswe kuri telefoni yawe. WitnessWork inagerageza nanone nyuma y'akanya gato, mu gihe porogaramu ifunguye.
- Mugenzi wawe ntazabibona kugeza byoherejwe.
