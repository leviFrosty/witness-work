Archiving hides a contact and keeps everything. Deleting permanently erases the contact and every conversation with them. Dismissing hides a contact for a while, then brings them back.

## Archive or delete a contact

1. In Contacts or on the map, touch and hold the contact. Or open the contact and tap **More** (•••).
2. Choose **Archive** or **Delete Permanently…**.

You can also swipe a contact from the right to archive it.

## Archive

The contact and their conversations are kept. To bring them back, open **Settings → More → Recover Contacts** within 90 days. After that, the details are removed.

## Delete permanently

This erases the contact, their conversations, and their photos, on your phone and on every device that syncs with it. It can't be undone, so WitnessWork asks first and offers **Archive Instead**. To delete a contact you already archived, touch and hold it in **Recover Contacts** and choose **Delete Permanently…**.

## Hide a contact for a while

1. Open the contact and tap **Dismiss For**.
2. Choose how long: 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or 1 year.

The contact leaves your lists, then returns on its own. If notifications are on, you also get a reminder when they're back. To see or restore dismissed contacts, open **Settings → Dismissed Contacts**, or use the link at the bottom of your Contacts list.

## Good to know

- With data protection mode on there is no **Archive**. Removing a contact always deletes it.
- Backup files you saved earlier still contain deleted contacts.

## If something goes wrong

- **You deleted a contact by mistake:** the only way back is to restore a backup made before the delete. That replaces your current data, so create a fresh backup first.
