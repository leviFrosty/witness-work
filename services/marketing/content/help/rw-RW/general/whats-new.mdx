title: "Nabona he ibyahindutse mu ivugurura rya nyuma?"
---

Kanda ifoto y'umwirondoro wawe, hanyuma **Igenamiterere → Ni ibihe bintu bishya?** kugira ngo ubone incamake y'impinduka za vuba.

## Byiza kumenya

- Amavugurura manini kandi afungura urugendo rugufi ubwa mbere ufunguye porogaramu nyuma yo kuyivugurura.
- Niba uhisemo **Nyuma**, inzogera ibika inzira ngufi igana ku rugendo.
- **Ni ibihe bintu bishya?** ifite akabuto ko gukora urugendo nanone.
