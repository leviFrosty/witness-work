Calendar Sync adds your upcoming Follow-ups to a calendar on your Android phone. It's free.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings → Features → Calendar Sync**. During setup, or from the invitation in your notifications, tap **Add to Calendar** instead.
2. Tap **Add to Calendar** or **Choose a calendar**, then allow calendar access.
3. Create a WitnessWork calendar on this phone, or choose a calendar you use only for WitnessWork, such as one made in Google Calendar.

Your main calendar can't be chosen. A calendar made on the phone stays on this phone. A Google calendar also shows on your other devices and on the web.

## Good to know

- Every upcoming Follow-up is added as a 30-minute event, with the same alert as its **Notify Me** reminder. Past Follow-ups and plans aren't added.
- Updates happen when you open WitnessWork and after you save a change. Tap **Sync now** to update right away.
- Make changes in WitnessWork. If you delete an event in your calendar app, it comes back at the next update. Leave the link in each event's description as it is.
- Names and addresses stay out of events unless you turn on **Include names and addresses**.
- **Turn Off Calendar Sync** lets you keep or remove the events. It never deletes the calendar or your other events.
- Uninstalling WitnessWork leaves its events in your calendar. Remove them first if you don't want them.

## If something goes wrong

- **A "Calendar updates paused" message appears:** tap it to see the fix.
- **Calendar access was turned off:** tap **Open Settings**, then **Permissions → Calendar → Allow**. Return to WitnessWork.
- **Another error:** tap **Try Again**.
- **Still stuck:** use the private support option under Still need help? in the Help Center. Keep contact details and calendar screenshots out of public reports.
