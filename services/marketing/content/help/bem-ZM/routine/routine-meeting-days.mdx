---

Mubike mu **Ifyo Mwasala → Preferences → Spiritual Routine**. Ici cibomba cimo fye pa iPhone na Android.

## Bikeni inshiku shenu sha kulongana

1. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Preferences → Spiritual Routine**.
2. Munshi ya **Meeting days**, saleni bushiku bwa kulongana kwa pa mulungu (Monday ukufika Friday).
3. Saleni bushiku bwa kulongana kwa mpela ya mulungu (Saturday nelyo Sunday).

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- **Midweek meeting prep** na **Weekend meeting prep** shiboneka pa kadi lya Routine pa Home ukufuma pa nshiku shibili pa ntanshi ya kulongana ukufika pa bushiku bwa kulongana. Ukwabula inshiku sha kulongana, shonse shibili shiboneka pa mulungu onse mpaka mwashilemba.
- Plans assistant ibomfya inshiku shimo fye elyo ibika ifyo ipeela ifinono pa shiko. Mubika fye umuku umo.
- Nga ubupange bwa kulongana bwacinja, nga ilyo kwisa kamwangalila wa cilongano, kuti mwalemba ukwilungika kwenu bushiku bwili bonse pa mulungu uyo ukufuma pa cikope ca Spiritual Routine.
- Inshiku shenu sha kulongana shaba pa fibombelo fyenu fyonse nga mulebomfya sync.
