title: "Mu nzogera y'Ahabanza harimo iki?"
---

Inzogera iri hejuru y'Ahabanza ikusanya inyibutsa n'amakuru mu mwanya umwe, kugira ngo bitarundanira Ahabanza.

## Ibyo ubonamo

- Raporo y'umurimo y'ukwezi gushize n'igihe gisigaye kigomba kwimurirwa
- Inyibutsa za backup no kurinda amakuru
- Ibyo gusubirayo byasibwe n'inyibutsa zamaze kuvuza
- Ibimenyetso bishya n'ibintu bishya by'itungo ryawe
- Ibikorwa bya Buddies, aho Buddies iboneka
- Ni ibihe bintu bishya?
- Kwinjira gushya, mushakisha yemewe, cyangwa passkey yongeweho ku kindi gikoresho cyawe. Kanda kugira ngo ubone ibikoresho cyangwa passkey zawe zinjiye. Reba [Nabona nte ibikoresho bikoresha konti yanjye?](/help/account/account-activity).
- Rimwe na rimwe ubutumire bwo gufungura Cloud Sync cyangwa kubika passkey

## Koresha inzogera

- Kanda ikintu kugira ngo ugikoreho.
- Kanda **X** kugira ngo ukureho ikintu, cyangwa **Kuraho byose** kugira ngo ubikureho byose. Ikintu wakuyeho ntikigaruka.
- Ubutumire n'ibisabwa bitegereje igisubizo cyawe bigumaho kugeza usubije.

## Byiza kumenya

- Umubare utukura ubara ibintu utarabona.
- Amakuru ya bagenzi bawe aba hasi munsi ya **Ibivuye kuri bagenzi bawe**. Ntiyongera umubare utukura. Akadomo gato ku nzogera kerekana ko hari amashya.
- Niba ukanze **Si ubu** ku butumire, bushobora kugaruka nyuma y'iminsi 30 nibura. Nyuma y'inshuro ebyiri, buhagarara.
- Ibyo wabonye cyangwa wakuyeho bibikwa kuri iki gikoresho gusa.
