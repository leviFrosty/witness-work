title: "Uduce twanjye turahuzwa kandi tukabikwa?"
---

Yego. Uduce twawe, hamwe n'imihanda yatwo, ibyabaye ku nzu, "Ntibasurwe", n'uduce twagarutse, duhuzwa n'ibindi bikoresho byawe kandi tugashyirwa muri backup zawe, nk'uko bimeze ku bantu bawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Guhuza bikoresha uburyo telefoni yawe ikoresha: Cloud Sync, iCloud, cyangwa Google Drive kuri Android. Reba **Igenamiterere → Guhuza**.
- Impinduka no gusiba na byo birahuzwa. Agace usibye ku gikoresho kimwe gisibwa ku bindi byose.
- Ifoto y'ikarita igumya kuri telefoni wayishyizeho. Yongere ku kindi gikoresho niba ushaka ko iba yo.
- Uduce usangiza dugumya gusangizwa ku matelefoni yawe yose ahuza n'iyi, hamwe n'ibyo abandi bandika. Uduce wasangijwe cyangwa washyikirijwe tugaragara ku bikoresho byawe byose bifite Buddies.
- Iyo uburyo bw'ibanga bw'ifasi bukora, nta nyandiko z'inzu zibikwa, bityo nta zihuzwa cyangwa zibikwa muri backup. Kubufungura binazisiba ku bindi bikoresho byawe.
- Niba porogaramu ishaje itagaragaza Uduce, vugurura WitnessWork. Uduce twawe tugumya kuba mu mutekano muri icyo gihe.
