title: "Nshobora guteganya amasaha ntahisemo igihe?"
---

Yego. Gahunda nshya nta gihe cyashyizweho. Umurongo wayo wa **Igihe cyo gutangira** uvuga **Igihe icyo ari cyo cyose**, kandi uhitamo gusa uburebure.

## Teganya uburebure bw'igihe

1. Fungura urupapuro rwa Gahunda.
2. Shyiraho uburebure ukoresheje utubuto twa **−** na **+** cyangwa ingano zihuse ziri munsi y'umubare. Kanda umubare kugira ngo wandike uburebure nyakuri.

## Ongeraho igihe cyo gutangira

1. Kanda **Igihe cyo gutangira**.
2. Hitamo igihe utangirira n'igihe urangirira. Amasaha ariyongera yonyine.

Kugira ngo ukureho igihe, hindura Gahunda, ukande **Igihe cyo gutangira**, uhitemo **Nta gihe cyashyizweho**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gahunda idafite igihe cyashyizweho yerekana **Igihe icyo ari cyo cyose** kuri Gahunda yawe kandi ibarwa mu ntego zawe nk'izindi Gahunda.
- Iyo **Menyesha njyewe** ifunguye, ikwibutsa saa mbili za mu gitondo uwo munsi.
- Bagenzi utumiye babona umunsi n'uburebure, nta gihe.
- Gahunda nshya zifungukira nk'uko wateganyije ubushize, bityo uburyo busanzwe bwiteguye ubutaha.
