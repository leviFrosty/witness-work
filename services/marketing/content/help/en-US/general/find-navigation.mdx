The bottom bar has **Home**, **Schedule**, **Contacts**, and **Progress**, with **Add** beside them. Map, Buddies, and Settings are one tap further in.

## Where to find things

- **Map:** open **Contacts** and tap **Map** in the **List | Map** switch beside the search field. WitnessWork opens the view you used last.
- **Sort & Filter:** in **Contacts**, tap the **Sort & Filter** chip at the start of the chips under the search field.
- **Buddies:** where it's available, tap the **Buddies** row under the **Schedule** title. It's also on your Profile.
- **Service Report:** tap **View Report** at the top of **Progress**. This shows the month you're viewing.
- **Profile, Settings, Support, and Help Center:** tap your profile picture at the top left of any main screen.
- **Settings:** the gear next to the bell on **Home** also opens it.

## Good to know

- **Progress** and **Add Time** show when your role logs hours.
- On a wide tablet, Settings and Buddies are in the sidebar. If you hid the sidebar, tap **Show sidebar** in the page header.
- If you turned on **Hide Donate Heart**, Support is left out of the profile menu and stays in **Settings**.

## If something goes wrong

- **Something looks missing:** your contacts, plans, and records are safe. Update the app, then use **Still need help?** below.
