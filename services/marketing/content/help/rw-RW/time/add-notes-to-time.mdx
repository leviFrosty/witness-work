id: addNotesToTime
title: Ese nshobora kongeramo inyandiko ku gihe cyo kwiyandikisha?
---

Yego. Mu gihe wongeyeho cyangwa uhinduye inyandiko y'igihe, ushobora gushyiramo inyuguti (urugero: "Gutanga ubuhamya bw'ikarita" cyangwa "gukata ngufi"). You can also tag entries with categories so similar entries stay grouped in your time reports.
