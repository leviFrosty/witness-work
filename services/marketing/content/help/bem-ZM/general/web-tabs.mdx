---
id: webTabs
title: Cinshi WitnessWork ilanda ukuti yaisulwa mu tab imbi?
---

WitnessWork pa web ibomba mu browser tab imo pa nshita, ukuleka ama tab yabili ukushinjana. Tab ya bubili ilanda **WitnessWork Is Open in Another Tab**.

## Twalilileni ukubomba mu tab iyi

1. Saleni **Use Here**.
2. WitnessWork ipwisha ukusunga mu tab imbi, elyo iisula uku.

## Bwelelenipo ku tab imbi

Yeni ku tab imbi elyo musale **Use Here** uko.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ukupwisha tab mulebomfya kuleka. Tab imbi mwaisula nelyo mwabwekeshapo yaisulwa bwino.
- Ici cibomba pa browser imo fye. Ama browsers yambi ne foni yenu tayakumanwa. Yashala yalingana na Cloud Sync.
