---

Uduce tubika amakarita y'uduce wafashe kuri telefoni yawe, kugira ngo ubwirize umuhanda ku wundi. Andika buri nzu, subira ku batari mu rugo, ubike "Ntibasurwe", hanyuma ugarure agace igihe usubije ikarita. Ni ubuntu.

Vugurura WitnessWork niba utabona **Uduce**. Biri kugezwa ku bantu buhoro buhoro, bityo bishobora kutaragera kuri wowe.

## Aho kubisanga

- **Igice cya Uduce** gitondekanya uduce wafashe, utwo basangije cyangwa bakuhaye, n'utwo wagaruye munsi ya **Erekana uduce twagarutse**.
- **Ikarita ya Uduce kuri Ahabanza** igaragara umaze kugira agace. Kanda **Reba byose** kugira ngo ufungure igice. Kugira ngo uyimure cyangwa uyikureho, kanda ufate igice icyo ari cyo cyose cya Ahabanza ukande **Hindura Ekara y'ahabanza**.

Kugira ngo utangire, fungura igice cya **Uduce** ukande **Ongeraho agace**. Reba [Nakora iki ngo nongere agace nafashe?](/help/territories/territories-add)

## Shaka agace mu rutonde

- Kanda utuntu two hejuru kugira ngo ugabanye urutonde: **Byarengeje igihe**, **Abatari mu rugo**, cyangwa **Imihanda isigaye**. **Byose** biraziruka.
- Kanda **Tondeka & Shungura** kugira ngo utondeke ukurikije itariki yafashwe, itariki yo kugarura, nomero, cyangwa imihanda yabwirijwe. Ihitamo ryawe rigumya kuri iyi telefoni.
- Kanda ufate agace cyangwa umuhanda kugira ngo ubone inzira ngufi, nka **Fungura ikarita**, **Imiyoboro**, **Sangiza**, na **Garura agace**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Uduce dukora kuri iPhone, iPad, Android, na murandasi.
- Gusangiza, imirongo y'abashyitsi, no gushyikiriza umuhanda biri muri porogaramu za telefoni gusa. Gusangiza na bagenzi no gushyikiriza bikeneye Buddies. Umurongo w'umushyitsi ntubikeneye.
- Gusuzuma QR code y'ikarita, gufata ifoto y'ikarita, no gushaka imihanda mu nkombe na byo biri kuri telefoni gusa.
