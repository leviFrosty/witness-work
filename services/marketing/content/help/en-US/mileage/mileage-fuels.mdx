id: mileageFuels
title: Where did the Gasoline, Diesel, and Electric fuels come from, and can I change them?
category: mileage
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

WitnessWork adds three fuels for you, so a new car only needs a name: Gasoline, Diesel, and Electric. Pick one in the Fuel row when you add a car (Mileage → Settings → Add Car), then enter the car's fuel economy to see an estimated fuel cost.

Each fuel starts with an average 2026 price for your device's region, in your region's currency. It's a national average, not the price at your pump, so change it to what you pay: open Mileage → Settings → Fuels and tap the fuel. If your region has no average, or your device shows a different currency than your region uses, the fuel starts without a price and you can add one the same way.

Gasoline and Diesel are priced per gallon or per litre, following your Units setting. Electric is priced per kWh, and a car that uses it enters its fuel economy as mi/kWh, km/kWh, or kWh/100 km. To add another fuel priced per kWh, tap Add Fuel and set Priced Per to kWh.

They are ordinary fuels: rename them, change their price, or delete them (tap the fuel, then Delete…). A fuel you delete stays deleted, including on your other devices that sync; it isn't added again. If you already had a fuel with the same name, such as your own Gasoline, WitnessWork keeps yours and doesn't add a second one. A shared car keeps its own fuels, so these changes don't affect it.
