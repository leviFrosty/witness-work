title: "Scribe AI yibuka iki ku bwanjye?"
---

Scribe AI ibika inyandiko ngufi z'uko ukora, kugira ngo utisubiramo. Urugero, niba uyikosoye ("Oya, build ni cyo mpa izina LDC"), ibika inyandiko ngufi ikayikoresha mu kiganiro cyose gishya. Kugira ngo uyibagize ikintu, vuga "Ibagirwa ibyo navuze kuri build."

## Reba, uhindure cyangwa usibe inyandiko zawe

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Scribe AI → Kwibuka**.
2. Hindura inyandiko cyangwa wongereho izawe. Andika inyandiko imwe kuri buri murongo.
3. Kugira ngo usibe inyandiko zose, kanda **Siba ibyibutswe**.
4. Kugira ngo uhagarike Scribe AI gusoma cyangwa kubika inyandiko, zimya Kwibuka kuri iryo kera.

## Byiza kumenya

- Kwibuka bifata inyuguti zigera ku 1,500. Iyo byuzuye, gabanya cyangwa ukureho inyandiko kugira ngo ubone umwanya.
- Inyandiko zawe zigumya kuri iki gikoresho. Ntizihuzwa cyangwa ngo zibikwe muri backup.
- Scribe AI ntibika amakuru bwite y'abantu usura.
- Impinduka muri Igenamiterere zikora uhereye ku kiganiro cyawe gikurikira.
