id: webSignOut
title: "Nasohoka nte muri WitnessWork kuri murandasi?"
---

Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti** usohoke. WitnessWork ibanza kugerageza amasegonda make kohereza impinduka zose zitegereje kuri Cloud Sync. Hanyuma isiba konti yawe n'amakuru yose ya WitnessWork muri iyi mushakisha.

## Sohoka

1. Reba ko **Igenamiterere → Sync** herekana ko byose byahuzwe.
2. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti**.
3. Hitamo **Sohora**.

Impinduka zitari zahuzwe zirabura.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gusohoka ntibisiba konti yawe cyangwa amakuru yawe muri WitnessWork.
- Kopi ya Cloud Sync y'iyi mushakisha ibikwa iminsi 30, cyangwa kugeza mushakisha ikurikira winjiyemo ibaye yakiriye amakuru yawe.
- Kugira ngo ukoreshe iyi mushakisha nanone, injira ukoresheje passkey yawe, telefoni yawe, cyangwa urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura. Reba [Ninjira nte kuri mudasobwa nkoresheje telefoni yanjye?](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer). Amakuru yawe aragaruka.
- Ukoresha WitnessWork muri mushakisha gusa, nta telefoni iri kuri Cloud Sync? Ongera winjire mu minsi 30 kugira ngo ugumane amakuru yawe, cyangwa ukurure backup mbere yo gusohoka.
- Sohoka igihe cyose ukoresha mudasobwa itari iyawe cyangwa isangiwe n'abandi.
- Ushobora kandi gusohora mushakisha ukoresheje ikindi gikoresho. Reba [Mushakisha imara igihe kingana iki yinjiye, kandi nakoresha zingahe?](/help/account/web-sessions).
