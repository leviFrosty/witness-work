id: cloudSyncPrivacy
title: Is Cloud Sync more private than iCloud or Google Drive sync?
category: backups
---

Cloud Sync is more private. Your records and photos are locked on your device before they're uploaded, so WitnessWork can't read them. Only your own devices can.

## Compared with iCloud and Google Drive

- **iCloud:** your copy is stored in your iCloud Drive. Apple protects it, and locks it completely if you turned on Advanced Data Protection. WitnessWork doesn't add its own lock.
- **Google Drive:** your copy is stored in a hidden folder in your Google Drive. Google protects it. WitnessWork doesn't add its own lock.

## Good to know

- With Cloud Sync, the server only knows things like how many devices sync and how big each copy is. It never sees what's in it.
- If you lose every device and your recovery key, nobody can read your Cloud Sync copies, including us.
