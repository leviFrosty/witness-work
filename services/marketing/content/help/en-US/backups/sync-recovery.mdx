Restarting setup affects only this device. When a device that already has records joins sync, WitnessWork asks what to do with them before anything is replaced.

## Choices you may see

- **Merge both:** combines this device and the synced data, keeping the most recent version of each item.
- **Restore from iCloud** (or Google Drive, or Cloud Sync): replaces this device's records with the synced ones. WitnessWork asks you to confirm.
- **Keep this device's data:** your other devices switch to this device's records the next time they sync. This replaces changes made on them before then.
- **Set up fresh:** sync stays paused until you turn it on in **Settings → Sync**. Then choose **Merge both** or restore.

## Good to know

- Deleted contact details can be recovered for up to 90 days.
- After a merge or restore, reminders are rebuilt on this device with the time you chose for each follow-up and plan. Only future reminders are set, and WitnessWork needs notification permission.
- Restoring doesn't send Buddies declines for plans missing from the backup.
- To be safe, keep the device with your latest records and save a backup file before replacing anything. If you need help, use the private support option in the Help Center.
