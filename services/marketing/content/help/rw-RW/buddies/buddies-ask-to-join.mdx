title: Nsaba nte kwifatanya muri Gahunda ya mugenzi wanjye?
---

Kanda umunsi ku ngengabihe yawe ya Ingengabihe, hanyuma ukande Saba kwifatanya iruhande rwa mugenzi uteganya kujya kubwiriza uwo munsi. Ushobora kandi gukanda umwe mu minsi ye munsi ya Ibyumweru bibiri bikurikira ku rupapuro rw'amakuru ye. Icyifuzo cyawe gisangiza gusa ibyo usanzwe ubona: umunsi, igihe n'uburebure bwa Gahunda ye. Kigaragara nka Wasabye kugeza mugenzi wawe agutumiye, ukisubiza, cyangwa Gahunda itangiye; kanda Wasabye kugira ngo ucyisubize. Gusaba bifunga amasaha abiri mbere y'uko Gahunda itangira, kandi ushobora kugira ibyifuzo bigera ku bitatu bitegereje kuri buri mugenzi. Mugenzi wawe amenyeshwa gusa ubwa mbere usabye Gahunda, kandi ku byifuzo bitarenga bitatu ku munsi. Niba yimuye cyangwa akuyeho iyo Gahunda, icyifuzo cyawe kirisubizwa kuri wowe. Wasabye kigaragara gusa ku gikoresho wasabyeho. Mugenzi wawe nagutumira, subiza mu nzogera y'amamenyesha yo ku Ahabanza. Niba uhisemo Ndagenda ufite Gahunda yawe bwite icyo gihe, y'inshuro imwe cyangwa yisubiramo, ushobora kuyisimbuza kuri uwo munsi kugira ngo igihe cyawe kitabarwa kabiri. Gahunda yisubiramo ikomeza ku yindi minsi yawe.
