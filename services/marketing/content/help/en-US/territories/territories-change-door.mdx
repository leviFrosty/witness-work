title: How do I change or remove a door I recorded?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Right after you record a door, tap Undo in the door recorder. Later, open the street and tap the house in the list:

- **Change its result:** the recorder switches to that house and says "Now Not at Home · pick a new result". Tap Not at Home, Spoke, or Do Not Call.
- **Remove:** deletes the house's record. Undo appears in the recorder in case you tapped it by mistake.
- **Add Pin or Move Pin:** tap the map where the door is.
- **Add as Contact** (for Spoke): starts a contact with the house's address.

A house marked Do Not Call moves to Do Not Call on This Street. Tap it there to change its address, date, or pin, or tap Remove Do Not Call (see [How do I add or edit a do-not-call?](/help/territories/territories-do-not-calls)).

You can change or remove only doors on streets you can record on. Doors on a returned territory, or on a street you've handed off to someone, can't be changed.
