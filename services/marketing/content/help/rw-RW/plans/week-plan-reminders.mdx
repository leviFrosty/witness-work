title: "WitnessWork ishobora kunyibutsa guteganya icyumweru gikurikira?"
---

Yego. Niba icyumweru gikurikira nta gahunda gifite, WitnessWork yohereza inyibutsa imwe ku gihe wahisemo.

## Ifungure

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Amamenyesha**.
2. Fungura **Unyibutse guteganya icyumweru gikurikira**.
3. Hitamo **Unyibutse ku** na **Unyibutse saa**. Ni ku wa gatandatu saa kumi n'ebyiri z'umugoroba keretse uhindure.
4. Emera amamenyesha niba WitnessWork ibibajije.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ibyumweru bikurikiza igenamiterere ryawe rya Itangira ry'icyumweru.
- Gahunda iyo ari yo yose muri icyo cyumweru, harimo gahunda isubiramo, bivuze nta nyibutsa. Kongeraho imwe nyuma bikuraho inyibutsa wamaze kwakira.
- Niba buri munsi wagaragajwe nk'umunsi wo kuruhuka munsi ya Igihe mboneka, ntibakwibutsa.
- Kanda inyibutsa kugira ngo ufungure **Gahunda** kuri icyo cyumweru, aho ushobora gukanda umunsi kugira ngo uuteganye.
- Inyibutsa inagaragara mu nzogera ya Ahabanza kugeza icyo cyumweru kirangiye.
- Iraboneka ku bubwoko bwose bw'ababwiriza.
- Inyibutsa zikorerwa kuri telefoni yawe ziva muri gahunda zawe bwite. Nta kintu cyoherezwa ahandi.
- Fungura WitnessWork nibura buri byumweru bibiri kugira ngo inyibutsa zikomeze kuza.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta nyibutsa zigera:** reba ko amamenyesha yemerewe WitnessWork mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe, kandi ko Focus cyangwa Do Not Disturb bizimye. Kuri Android, reba kandi icyiciro cya Reminders.
- **Wateganyije ku kindi gikoresho:** fungura WitnessWork kuri iyi telefoni kugira ngo ishobore guhagarika inyibutsa.
- **Android itinda iminota mike:** sisitemu ishobora gutinza inyibutsa kugira ngo ibike umuriro.
