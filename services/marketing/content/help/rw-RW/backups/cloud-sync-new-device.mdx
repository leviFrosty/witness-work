title: "Nsubiza nte inyandiko zanjye kuri telefoni nshya?"
---

Kanda **Nari nsanzwe nkoresha WitnessWork** igihe gutangira kigeze. Ekara ya **Urakaza neza** igarura inyandiko zawe ziva kuri konti yawe ya WitnessWork ukoresheje Cloud Sync.

Niba ukanze **Tangira** ahubwo kandi WitnessWork ikabona inyandiko zawe, ikubuguriza **Urakaza neza** yonyine.

## Garura inyandiko zawe

- **Konti yawe isanzwe kuri iyi telefoni:** ibi biba iyo telefoni ikoresha Apple Account imwe na iPhone cyangwa iPad yawe indi, cyangwa Google Account imwe n'igikoresho cyawe cya Android. WitnessWork ibona inyandiko zawe, irazikurura ikavuga **Urakaza neza! Witeguye.** Amafoto yawe akururwa nyuma yaho.
- **Konti yawe ntiragera kuri iyi telefoni:** banza winjire. Kanda **Injira ukoresheje passkey**, **Uvuye ku yindi telefoni yawe** (suzuma QR code), cyangwa **Koresha urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura**. Cloud Sync ihita izana inyandiko zawe.
- **Wakoreshaga iCloud, Google Drive cyangwa dosiye ya backup:** yihitemo munsi ya **Ubundi buryo bwo kugarura**.
- **Uri mushya:** kanda **Ndi mushya hano**. Ushobora gufungura Cloud Sync nyuma muri **Igenamiterere → Guhuza**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Inyandiko zawe z'umurimo, abantu, gusura, gahunda n'ibyo ukunda bisangiwe biragaruka. Igenamiterere ry'igikoresho kimwe, nko guhuza kwacyo no kurinda amakuru, bigumya kuri buri gikoresho.
- Ibikoresho byawe gusa ni byo bishobora gusoma inyandiko zawe.
- Niba iyi telefoni isanzwe ifite inyandiko, WitnessWork irabanza ikabaza. Kanda **Garura & komeza guhuza**, hanyuma uhitemo **Huza** kugira ngo uzibike wongereho inyandiko zawe za Cloud Sync, cyangwa **Simbuza** kugira ngo ukoreshe inyandiko zawe za Cloud Sync gusa.
- Kugira ngo uzane inyandiko ziva muri MyTime, inyandiko cyangwa impapuro muri konti nshya, shyiraho nk'ushya ukoreshe intambwe ya **Zana inyandiko zawe**, cyangwa **Igenamiterere → Zana amakuru** nyuma.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ntiyashoboye kugera kuri Cloud Sync:** reba murandasi yawe ukande **Ongera ushake**.
- **Biracyakomeye:** koresha uburyo bw'ubufasha bw'ibanga mu Kigo cy'Ubufasha.
