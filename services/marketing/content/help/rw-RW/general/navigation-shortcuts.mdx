---

Yego. Iyo keyboard ihujwe na iPad iri kuri iPadOS 26 cyangwa irenga, Command hamwe n'umubare bifungura ecran nkuru.

## Koresha utwo tuyoboro

1. Huza keyboard na iPad yawe ujye mu rutonde nyamukuru rw'ahantu.
2. Fata **Command**. Utuzina tw'imibare duto tugaragara iruhande rw'utubuto two hasi cyangwa ibintu by'umurongo w'ibumoso.
3. Kanda umubare werekanwe ku ecran ushaka.

## Byiza kumenya

- Imibare ikurikiza uko ibintu bikurikirana ku ecran. Mu buryo busanzwe, **Ahabanza** ni Command 1 naho **Contacts** ni Command 2.
- Banza ufunge ifishi iyo ariyo yose ifunguye. Utu tuyoboro ntitubika cyangwa ngo dusige ibyo wakoze.
- Kugira ngo ubike ifishi urimo kwandikamo, reba [Nabika ifishi nkoresheje akayoboro ka keyboard?](/help/general/keyboard-save).
- Ibi nta kuri iPhone cyangwa Android.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta mazina agaragara:** reba ko keyboard ihujwe kandi uri kuri ecran nkuru. Vugurura porogaramu uyifungure nanone. Ushobora buri gihe gukanda ibintu.
- **Biracyanze:** koresha ihuza ry'ubufasha ry'ibanga ryo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha, kandi ntushyire amakuru y'abantu muri raporo rusange.
