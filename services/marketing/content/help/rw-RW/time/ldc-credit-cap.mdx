---
id: ldcCreditCap
title: "LDC n'andi masaha ya Credit abarwa mu biteranyo byanjye?"
---

Yego. Amasaha ya Credit, harimo LDC, abarwa mu giteranyo cyawe cya buri kwezi.

Ku giteranyo cy'umwaka wawe w'umurimo, abarwa amasaha ya Credit agera kuri 55 ku kwezi, nk'uko umupaka watangajwe n'ibiro by'ishami ubivuga. Ibirenga bigumya ku gihe cyanditswe ariko ntibyongerwa ku giteranyo cy'umwaka w'umurimo.
