Yes. Drag the pin to the right spot. A pin you move is used for directions and route planning instead of the address.

## Move a pin

1. Open the contact's address screen.
2. Drag the pin to the correct spot.

On the **Map**, you can also touch and hold a contact's pin, then drag it. A message with **Undo** appears. Tap **Undo** if you moved it by mistake.

## Good to know

- You can place a pin with no address at all. This helps where street numbers don't exist.
