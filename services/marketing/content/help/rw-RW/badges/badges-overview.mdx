title: Ibimenyetso bikora bite, kandi nabibona he?
---

Ibimenyetso byishimira imigenzo myiza mu murimo wawe, nko kugira uruhare mu murimo buri kwezi, kohereza raporo yawe, no gusubira gusura abantu. Nta cyo ugomba gutegura: WitnessWork igenzura inyandiko zawe kandi ikongeraho ikimenyetso iyo ugezeho. Kugira ngo ubone ibyawe, kanda ifoto yawe, hanyuma Umwirondoro. Ibimenyetso byawe biri munsi y'ikarita y'umwirondoro wawe; kanda kimwe kugira ngo ukirebe neza, cyangwa ukande Reba byose kugira ngo ufungure Ibimenyetso, aho ubona buri kimenyetso ufite n'icyo cyafasha buri kimwe gukura. Ibimenyetso byinshi bikura mu nzego enye: Umuringa, Ifeza, Zahabu, n'Imaragarita. Ibimenyetso bike by'inshuro imwe, nka Icyigisho cya mbere cya Bibiliya, bigumaho bihishe kugeza ubibonye. Iyo ikintu wakoze vuba aha gihesha ikimenyetso, nko kwandika igihe, kwemeza ko wagize uruhare mu murimo, kubika gusura cyangwa gahunda, kohereza raporo yawe, gusubiza Ndagenda, cyangwa kwemeza mugenzi, cyishimirwa ako kanya. Ikimenyetso kije muundi buryo, nko kugera kw'umunsi wa gahunda nijoro, igihe cyanditswe kuri watch yawe cyangwa ku kindi gikoresho, cyangwa igisubizo cya mugenzi, nticyakubangamira: ikarita y'Ikimenyetso gishya hejuru ya Ahabanza igitangaza. Kanda Reba kugira ngo ukirebe neza, cyangwa X kugira ngo ukureho ikarita; uko byagenda kose ikimenyetso kirabikwa kandi kigumaho kiranzwe gishya ku mwirondoro wawe kugeza ufunguye ibimenyetso byawe. Niba inyandiko imwe ihesha ikimenyetso kandi ikageza urukurikirane ku ntambwe, urukurikirane rurishimirwa naho ikimenyetso kigategereza kuri Ahabanza. Niba inyandiko zawe zimaze kugera ku bimenyetso ubwa mbere WitnessWork izigenzuye ku gikoresho, ecran y'inshuro imwe “Ibimenyetso byawe biri hano” ikubwira umubare ufite: Reba ifungura umwirondoro wawe, naho Nyuma ikayifunga. Itegereza igihe ufunguye WitnessWork nta rugendo rw'ivugurura. Ibimenyetso bigezweho n'inyandiko wongeraho cyangwa ugarura mu gihe ushyiraho WitnessWork byongerwa hatabayeho iyo ecran. Uko byagenda kose, ibimenyetso byawe byamaze kubikwa. Ibimenyetso bikora kuri iOS na Android kandi bivugururwa mu kanya gato nyuma y'uko inyandiko zawe zihindutse n'igihe cyose usubiye muri WitnessWork. Si nomero itukura uri ku kimenyetso cya porogaramu, ubara ibitarasomwa mu nzogera y'amamenyesha.
