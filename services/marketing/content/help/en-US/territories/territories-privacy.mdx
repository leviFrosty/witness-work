Yes. A territory you don't share stays on your phone and in your own sync and backups, which are private. See [Are my territories synced and backed up?](/help/territories/territories-sync)

## Shared territories

When you share a territory, only you and the people you share it with can read it. WitnessWork's server can't read the territory's number or name, street names, addresses, results, do-not-calls, or anyone's name. The photo of the card is never shared.

## Guest links

WitnessWork keeps nothing about a guest except the name they type, and that stays private to the territory. A guest's browser keeps the link's records until the link ends or they tap **Forget This Link**.

## What the people you share with see

Everything on the streets you share with them, including other people's doors with the name of who recorded each one, and the do-not-calls.

## Keep only what you need

- A do-not-call keeps only the address and the date. Don't add a name or a reason.
- Scribe AI returns only house numbers, streets, and results from your notes. See [Does Scribe AI read my territory notes?](/help/territories/territories-notes-scribe)
- For no house-by-house records at all, turn on territory privacy mode. See [What is territory privacy mode?](/help/territories/territories-privacy-mode)
