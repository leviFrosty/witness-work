title: Are LDC and other credit hours included in my totals?
category: time
related:

  - 9
  - 98
---

Yes. Credit hours, including LDC, count toward your monthly total.

For your service year total, up to 55 Credit hours a month count, following the branch's published limit. Anything above that stays on the entry but isn't added to the service year total.
