id: siri
title: Bushe kuti nabomfya Siri nelyo Shortcuts na WitnessWork?
---

Ee, pa iPhone na iPad iyakwata iOS 17 nelyo iyakonkelepo, na pa app ya Apple Watch. Isuleni WitnessWork umuku umo panuma ya kukusha, elyo mulande “Lundeni inshita muli WitnessWork” (nelyo “Lembeni inshita muli WitnessWork”), “Tendekeni icakupimako candi muli WitnessWork”, “Lekeni icakupimako candi muli WitnessWork”, “Pwisheni icakupimako candi muli WitnessWork”, “Lembeni ulwendo muli WitnessWork” nelyo “Lembeni ulwendo lwa kuya no kubwela muli WitnessWork”. Siri isheeka mu lulimi lwenu lwa Siri, ulwingalekana ne lulimi lwa mwasala muli WitnessWork. Siri ipusha inshita shinga nelyo ukuya kutali shinga. Ukuya kutali kubomfya cipimo cenu ca ndambo mu Mileage; pa lwendo lwa kuya no kubwela, landeni ndambo ya kuya fye kabili yalundwa kabili. Ukupwisha icakupimako inshita kulundako amineti yonse yaco nge cisanshi ca lelo no kucibika pa busana; nga ili panshi ya miniti imo, tapali icalundwako. Inshita ne ndwendo shilundwako ku lelo, nge nshita ya bulesa na motoka iyo mwabomfya pa kulekelesha. Pa kusala Umusango nelyo motoka, lundeni icintu mu app ya Shortcuts, uko kuti mwacitendeka na Action Button nelyo shortcut ya pa Home Screen. Ukulundako inshita ne cakupimako inshita fifwaya ukuti mulemba amaawala (Bakabilisha ba Bufumu bawasha Lemba amaawala yandi pa ntanshi). Ukulemba ndwendo kufwaya Track Mileage ukuwashiwa na motoka imo ukulubulwa. Pa iPhone na iPad icakupimako cinjila bwangu, kabili inshita ne ndwendo ishalundwako na Siri shisungwa ilyo mwaisula WitnessWork nakabili, elyo fyaya ku fibombelo fyenu ifimbi na iCloud Sync nge fisanshi fimbi. Pa wotchi fitumwa ku iPhone yenu nge fisanshi fimbi fya wotchi. Siri ibomfya fye Imisango yenu, amashina ya motoka, cipimo ca ndambo na cakupimako inshita ukufuma muli WitnessWork, tayibomfya abantu ba kulanshanya, amalembo nelyo amalipoti. Siri na Shortcuts tayaba pa Android. Nga Siri taileishiba ilyashi, isuleni WitnessWork umuku umo, pembeleni miniti, elyo mumone nga Siri yasuminishiwa pa WitnessWork mu Settings. Pa bwafya bumbi, bomfyeni intambo ya Pa Kwafwilisha iya kwafwa mu cinkama kabili ubeni ifyalembwa fyenu ukwabula mu malipoti ya bantu bonse.
