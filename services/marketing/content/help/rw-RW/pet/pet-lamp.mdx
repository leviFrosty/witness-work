title: "Itara riri mu cyumba risobanura iki?"
---

Itara ryerekana uko itungo ryawe rimeze. Rigurumana urwego rumwe kurushaho kuri buri mutima wose.

- Ufite umutima utageze ku umwe, ritaka. Itungo riryamye riba rifite igice cy'umutima.
- Ku mitima itanu, rigurumana cyane.
- Niba itungo ryawe ryapfuye, rirazima.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ntiritwa n'Inkurikirane yawe y'umurimo cyangwa urumuri wahisemo mu gice cya **Urumuri**.
- Reba [Imitima y'itungo ryanjye ikora ite?](/help/pet/pet-hearts).
