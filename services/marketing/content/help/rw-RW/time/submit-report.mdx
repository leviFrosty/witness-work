Oya. WitnessWork itegura raporo yawe, kandi uhitamo uko uyohereza.

## Ohereza raporo yawe

1. Kuri **Ahabanza**, kanda **Emeza** ku ikarita ya **Raporo y'umurimo**. Cyangwa ufungure **Raporo y'Umurimo wo mu Murimo** y'ukwezi.
2. Munsi ya raporo, kanda **Uburyo bwo kohereza** uhitemo uko uyohereza: uyikoporore, uyisangize, cyangwa uyifungure muri Hourglass cyangwa NW Publisher.
3. Kanda buto y'icyatsi ya **Emeza** iri munsi yayo.

![Raporo y'Umurimo wo mu Murimo ifite Uburyo bwo kohereza na buto ya Emeza](submit-report.webp)

Raporo ihita yerekana **Yoherejwe** n'itariki. Wayohereje ukundi? Kanda **Yandike ko yoherejwe** munsi ya buto.

## Byiza kumenya

- Hourglass na NW Publisher bifunguka bifite amasaha yawe, Credit n'ibyigisho bya Bibiliya byujujwe. Ubirebe maze wohereze uhereyo.
- Hourglass ibona Credit yawe mu gitekerezo cya raporo. Niba uhinduye igitekerezo, reba ko kikivuga ibyo ukeneye.
- NW Publisher yemera gusa raporo y'ukwezi gushize.
- WitnessWork yibuka ihitamo ryawe. Kugira ngo urihindure, fungura **Igenamiterere → Umwirondoro → Uburyo bwo kohereza**.
- Wari wamaze gutanga raporo ukundi? Kanda ufate umurongo wa **Emeza** kuri Ahabanza uhitemo **Byamaze gutangwa**.
- Vugurura WitnessWork niba utabona **Hourglass** cyangwa **NW Publisher** muri menu.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Wayanditseho ko yoherejwe ku bw'impanuka:** kanda **Subiramo** munsi ya buto ya **Emeza**. Ushobora kandi gukanda ufate ikarita y'ukwezi uhitemo **Yandike ko itoherejwe**.
- **Wayisangije kuri Android:** porogaramu ntishobora kumenya niba wayohereje. Kanda **Yandike ko yoherejwe** munsi ya buto ya **Emeza**.
- **Telefoni yawe yari nta murandasi:** usubiye kuri murandasi, WitnessWork ibaza niba raporo yagezeyo. Hitamo **Yandike ko yoherejwe** niba yagezeyo.
