title: Does my mileage sync, and does it work on Android?
category: mileage
---

Yes. Mileage works on iPhone and Android, and your cars, fuels, prices, and trips sync between your devices when sync is on.

## Good to know

- Sync is included for everyone. Choose it in **Settings → Sync**: Cloud Sync (which also works between iPhone, Android, and the web), or iCloud (Google Drive on Android).
- Mileage is included in WitnessWork Backup (**Settings → Account**) and in backups from **Settings → Backup & Restore**.
- Mileage doesn't appear on your calendar or Service Report.
- It isn't shared with Buddies, except a car you choose to share. See [What are shared cars, and how do I share a car with my family?](/help/mileage/shared-cars).
- A shared car updates on its own and isn't part of sync or backups. See [Do shared cars show on my other devices, my watch, and in backups?](/help/mileage/shared-cars-devices).
