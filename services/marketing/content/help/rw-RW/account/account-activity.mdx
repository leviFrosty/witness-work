title: "Nabona nte ibikoresho bikoresha konti yanjye?"
---

Jya muri **Igenamiterere → Konti → Ibikoresho byinjiye**. Hagaragara buri telefoni na mushakisha byinjiye muri konti yawe, hamwe n'igihe buri kimwe cyakoreshejwe ubushize. Icyawe cyanditseho **This device**.

## Sohora igikoresho

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Ibikoresho byinjiye**.
2. Kanda telefoni cyangwa mushakisha ushaka gukuraho.
3. Hitamo **Sohora**.

## Reba ibyabaye kuri konti yawe

Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Ibikorwa kuri konti**. Herekana iminsi 400 ya nyuma: kwinjira no gusohoka, passkey zongeweho cyangwa zikuweho, mushakisha zemewe ziturutse kuri telefoni, backup zakuwe cyangwa zasibwe, n'igihe urufunguzo rwa konti yawe rwahindurwaga.

Iyo ikindi gikoresho cyangwa mushakisha byinjiye, cyangwa passkey yongeweho, inzogera y'Ahabanza irakubwira. WitnessWork irabireba iyo ufunguye porogaramu n'iyo ufunguye **Igenamiterere → Konti**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Telefoni ivugurura uko yinjiye hafi buri mezi atatu. Ibyo bigaragara mu Bikorwa kuri konti nk'ivugurura, si nk'igikoresho gishya. Telefoni utafunguye igihe kinini ishobora kongera kwinjira ikagaragara nk'ishya, bityo banza urebe izina ry'igikoresho.
- WitnessWork ntibika aho wari uri cyangwa aderesi yawe ya murandasi.
- Ukeneye murandasi kugira ngo ubone izi ecran.

## Niba utazi igikoresho

1. Sohora icyo gikoresho muri **Ibikoresho byinjiye**, cyangwa ukureho passkey muri **Igenamiterere → Konti → Passkeys**.
2. Hindura urufunguzo rwa konti yawe. Reba [Nahindura nte urufunguzo rwa konti yanjye?](/help/account/change-account-key).

Telefoni igifite konti yawe ishobora kongera kwinjira, bityo guhindura urufunguzo ni ngombwa. Ku telefoni yatakaye, reba [Nakora iki niba telefoni cyangwa mudasobwa yanjye yatakaye cyangwa yibwe?](/help/account/lost-device).
