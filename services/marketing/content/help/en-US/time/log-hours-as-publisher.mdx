Kingdom Publishers report only whether they shared in the ministry, so Home shows a Shared the Good News checkbox instead of Add Time. To keep track of your hours for yourself, open Settings → Profile and turn on Log my hours. Add Time, the timer, and the Progress tab appear, and you can set an optional Monthly Goal. WitnessWork asks about this once, during setup or in the notifications on Home. If you said no, you can turn it on in Settings anytime, and turning it off again hides those without deleting the time you logged. Your report still says only whether you shared, and any time you log also counts as sharing. If you're auxiliary pioneering this month, set that up instead in Settings → Profile: you'll log hours toward that month's goal, and the next month goes back to the checkbox.
