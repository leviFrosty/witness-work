---
id: appleWatch
title: Hari porogaramu ya Apple Watch?
---

Yego, kuri iPhone ifite Apple Watch ikoresha watchOS 26 cyangwa irenzeho. Ishyirwaho ubwayo niba Automatic App Install ifunguye; naho ubundi fungura porogaramu ya Watch kuri iPhone yawe, shaka WitnessWork muri Available Apps, hanyuma ukande Install. Hanyuma fungura WitnessWork kuri iPhone yawe rimwe kugira ngo isaha yawe ibone iterambere ryawe. Ku isaha ushobora kongeraho igihe cy'uyu munsi hamwe n'Ubwoko (si ngombwa), gutangiza no guhagarika by'agateganyo igihe kibarwa cya iPhone yawe, no kubika igihe cyacyo nk'inyandiko. Niba utandika amasaha nk'Umubwiriza w'Ubwami, ushobora kwerekana ko wasangije abandi ubutumwa bwiza muri uku kwezi. Complication ebyiri zishobora gushyirwa ku isura y'isaha cyangwa muri Smart Stack: Iterambere ry'ukwezi rigaragaza amasaha yawe ugereranyije n'intego y'uku kwezi, rifite akamenyetso ku hantu gahunda zawe zikugeza uyu munsi; mu mwanya uzengurutse ushobora guhitamo uruziga cyangwa igipimo, intego yawe iri ku mpera yarwo cyangwa isaha iri munsi, kandi nta ntego, rigaragaza amasaha yawe gusa. Ibikurikira bigaragaza gusubirayo kwawe gukurikira cyangwa gahunda yawe ikurikira. Ushobora kandi gukoresha Siri ku isaha yawe kugira ngo wongereho igihe, utangize, uhagarike cyangwa uhagarike burundu igihe kibarwa, wandike urugendo, ukoresheje amagambo amwe nko kuri iPhone, nka "Ongeraho igihe muri WitnessWork" cyangwa "Andika urugendo muri WitnessWork". iPhone yawe ni yo ibika inyandiko zawe. Isaha yakira iterambere ryawe ry'ukwezi, imimerere ya raporo, igihe kibarwa, Ubwoko, amazina y'imodoka n'igipimo cy'intera, naho kuri Ibikurikira yakira izina, aderesi n'ingingo bya gusubirayo kwawe, n'umutwe n'ahantu bya gahunda zawe z'ibyumweru bibiri biri imbere. Ntiyakira inyandiko cyangwa andi makuru y'abantu, kandi ntishobora guhindura cyangwa gusiba inyandiko. Porogaramu y'isaha ikenera iPhone yawe, bityo ntiboneka kuri Apple Watch yashyizweho hakoreshejwe Family Setup. Ukoresha telefoni ya Android, koresha porogaramu ya Wear OS aho.
