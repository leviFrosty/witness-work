id: mileageCars
title: Itandukaniro riri hagati yo kubika mu bubiko no gusiba imodoka ni irihe?
---

Fungura Ibirometero → Igenamiterere, ukande imodoka, uhitemo Ububiko cyangwa Siba…. Kubika mu bubiko bihisha imodoka ku ngendo nshya ariko bigakomeza ingendo zayo muri raporo zawe; uyigarure mu bubiko igihe icyo ari cyo cyose uciye mu Imodoka zabitswe. Gusiba bikuraho imodoka n'ingendo zayo zose ku bikoresho byose bihuza n'iki, kandi ntibishobora gusubirwamo. Gusiba lisansi bikuraho amateka y'ibiciro byayo, bityo imodoka zikoresha iyo lisansi zireka kwerekana igiciro giteganyijwe.
