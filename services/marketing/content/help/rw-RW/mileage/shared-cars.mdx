---

Imodoka isangiwe ibika amateka y'ibirometero amwe y'imodoka mutwara muri benshi, nk'uwo mwashakanye cyangwa umuryango. Buri wese uri ku modoka ashobora kwandika, guhindura no gusiba ingendo zayo, kandi ibiteranyo bibara ingendo za bose. Imodoka zisangiwe ni ubuntu kandi zikora kuri iPhone, iPad na Android.

Ukeneye Buddies ishyizweho n'umugenzi umwe nibura. Reba [WitnessWork Buddies ni iki, kandi kuki ntayibona?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview).

## Sangiza imodoka

1. Fungura **Ibirometero**, hanyuma ukande uruziga kugira ngo ufungure **Igenamiterere ry'ibirometero**.
2. Kanda imodoka.
3. Munsi ya **People**, kanda **Share Car…**. Yerekana **Sharing…** mu gihe yimura imodoka n'amateka yayo.
4. Ku rupapuro rukurikira, kanda **Tumira** iruhande rwa buri mugenzi ushaka ku modoka.

Buri mugenzi abona ubutumire mu nzogera y'Ahabanza, hamwe n'imenyesha, kandi afite iminsi 7 yo kwifatanya. Umenyeshwa iyo umuntu yifatanyije.

## Byiza kumenya

- Usangiza imodoka imwe icyarimwe. Izindi modoka zawe ziguma izawe.
- Imodoka ifite abantu bagera ku 10, harimo n'ubutumire bufunguye. Buri wese uyiriho ashobora gutumira bagenzi be bwite.
- Kugira ngo urebe icyo buri muntu ashobora gukora, reba [Ni nde ushobora gukora iki ku modoka isangiwe?](/help/mileage/shared-cars-people).

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta bagenzi ufite ubu:** urupapuro rurabivuga. Banza utumire umuntu muri Buddies, hanyuma usangize imodoka.
