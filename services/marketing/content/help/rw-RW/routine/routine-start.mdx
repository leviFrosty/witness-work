---

Spiritual Routine igufasha gukomeza ibikorwa byawe bwite by'umwuka hamwe n'umurimo wawe wo kubwiriza. Ubibaruraho ku ikarita ya Routine Ahabanza. Ni ubuntu, ntitegetswe, kandi ikora kuri iPhone na Android.

## Yishyireho

1. Kanda **Set up** ku ikarita ya **Spiritual Routine** Ahabanza. Cyangwa ufungure **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Spiritual Routine**.
2. Hitamo ibikorwa wifuza gushyiramo.
3. Hitamo uko usoma Bibiliya, iminsi y'amateraniro yawe, na nimugoroba w'ugusenga kw'umuryango wawe.

Inyibutsa ya buri munsi irahari ariko izimije keretse uyifunguye. Niba ukanze **Si ubu** ku ikarita y'Ahabanza, iragenda. Ushobora kuyishyiraho nyuma mu Igenamiterere.

## Barura ibintu

Kanda uruziga ruri ku iherezo ry'ikintu ku ikarita ya Routine. Iyo byose by'uyu munsi birangiye, ikarita igabanuka igahinduka **All done today**.

Ibyo ushobora kubaruraho:

- **Bible reading:** rimwe ku munsi. Ushobora kandi gukurikira gahunda yo gusoma. Reba [Gahunda zo gusoma Bibiliya zikora zite muri Spiritual Routine?](/help/routine/routine-reading-plans)
- **Personal study:** umunsi uwo ari wo wose wize. Mu buryo busanzwe ikarita irabisaba kugeza wize rimwe muri icyo cyumweru. Ushobora guhindura intego ikaba inshuro ku cyumweru, cyangwa iminsi wahisemo.
- **Midweek meeting prep** na **Weekend meeting prep:** rimwe buri cyumweru. Buri kimwe kigaragara kuva ku minsi ibiri mbere y'iteraniro kugeza ku munsi w'iteraniro.
- **Family worship:** rimwe ku cyumweru, ku mugoroba wahisemo. Niba utari kumwe n'umuryango, ushobora kukita Personal worship evening.

## Reba byinshi

- Kanda ikintu ubwacyo, si uruziga rwacyo, kugira ngo ufungure ecran yacyo. Yerekana ikarita y'ubushyuhe y'iminsi wabaruyeho, na **Recent days**, aho ushobora kubarura umunsi wibagiwe, kugeza ku itangiriro ry'icyumweru gishize. Kanda umunsi wabaruwe kugira ngo ugusubize inyuma.
- Kanda umutwe w'ikarita ya Routine kugira ngo ubone ibikorwa byose wafunguye n'inshuro wabibaruyeho muri uku kwezi.

## Byiza kumenya

- Nta nkurikirane zihari. Kubura umunsi ntibigera bikubarwaho nabi.
- Kugira ngo uhishe ikarita, hindura ecran yawe y'Ahabanza ukureho Spiritual Routine. Cyangwa uzimye icyo kiranga muri **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Spiritual Routine**. Nta cyo wabaruyeho gitakara.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Igikorwa nticyigaragara Ahabanza:** ibikorwa by'icyumweru bigaragara gusa ku minsi yabyo, kandi personal study ihishwa umaze kugera ku ntego y'icyumweru. Reba **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Spiritual Routine**.
- **Biracyanze:** koresha **Uracyakeneye ubufasha?** munsi y'iki gisubizo kugira ngo wandikire ubufasha.
