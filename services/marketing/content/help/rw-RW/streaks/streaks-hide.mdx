title: Nshobora kuzimya inkurikirane?
---

Yego. Fungura Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Inkurikirane n'Ibimenyetso maze uzimye Erekana urukurikirane. Urukurikirane rwawe ntirukigaragara kuri Ahabanza cyangwa ku mwirondoro wawe, nta cyishimira, Inyibutsa z'urukurikirane zirahagarara, kandi indamukanyo ya Ahabanza ntikibara iminsi ikurikiranye. Inkurikirane za bagenzi bawe nazo zirahishwa, kandi urwawe ntirukibasangizwa. Nta cyatakaye: urukurikirane rwawe ruva muri gahunda zawe n'igihe wandika, bityo iyo wongeye gufungura Erekana urukurikirane ruhita rugaragara, keretse rwarangiye hagati aho. Iri hitamo rikurikira ku bindi bikoresho byawe iyo guhuza byafunguwe. Kugira ngo uhishe n'ibimenyetso, zimya Erekana ibimenyetso kuri iyo ecran.
