---
id: darkMode
title: Bushe kuti nasala dark mode nelyo light mode?
---

Ee. WitnessWork kuti yakonka ifyo foni yenu yasala, nelyo ukubomfya lyonse dark nelyo light.

## Saleni ifyo mufwaya

1. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Ukuipangila → Imisango ya Malangi**.
2. Saleni **Icintu**, **Icafita** nelyo **Ulubuuto**.

**Icintu** ikonka ifyo foni yenu yasala.
