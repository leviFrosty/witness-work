id: googleDriveSyncPrivacy
title: "Google Drive Sync ibika iki, kandi ni nde ubibona?"
---

Amakuru yawe ya WitnessWork abikwa mu bubiko bwihishe muri Google Drive yawe bwite. WitnessWork yonyine ni yo ishobora gufungura ubwo bubiko, kandi ntishobora kubona ikindi kintu muri Drive yawe. Amakuru yawe ajya hagati ya telefoni yawe na Google mu buryo butaziguye, ntaca kuri seriveri za WitnessWork.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ububiko ntibugaragara muri porogaramu ya Drive cyangwa urubuga, kandi izindi porogaramu ntizibusoma.
- Google ibubika nk'izindi dosiye zawe za Drive. Bubarirwa mu bubiko bwawe bwa Google.
- WitnessWork ntibika aderesi yawe ya imeri.
- Dosiye zawe za backup zo muri **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura** zitandukanye. Zigumya aho wazibitse.

## Reka gukoresha Google Drive Sync

- **Kuri telefoni imwe:** fungura **Igenamiterere → Guhuza** uvaneho Google Drive. Ibindi bikoresho byawe bikomeza gukoresha ubwo bubiko.
- **Ku bikoresho byose:** zimya guhuza kuri buri kimwe. Hanyuma, muri Google Drive kuri murandasi, fungura **Settings → Manage apps → WitnessWork → Options → Delete hidden app data**.
- **Kuraho uburenganzira burundu:** kuraho WitnessWork munsi ya Third-party apps & services muri Google Account yawe.
