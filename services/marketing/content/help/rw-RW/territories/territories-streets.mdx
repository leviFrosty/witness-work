title: "Nakora iki ngo nongere imihanda ku gace?"
---

Fungura agace ukande **Ongeraho umuhanda**. Tangirana n'umuhanda wa mbere uri ku ikarita, hanyuma wongereho indi nk'uko uzayibwiriza.

## Ongeraho umuhanda

1. Andika izina rya **Umuhanda**.
2. Munsi ya **Nomero z'amazu**, hitamo **Impande zombi** (1, 2, 3), **Uruhande rw'inyamibwa** (1, 3, 5), **Uruhande rw'imibare ibiri** (2, 4, 6), cyangwa **Nta nomero z'amazu**. Nta nomero, inzu zibarwa: Urugi 1, Urugi 2, Urugi 3.
3. Andika **Nomero ya mbere**, aho utangirira.
4. Fungura **Nomero zigabanuka** niba nomero zigenda ziba nto uko ugenda.
5. Reka **Bika aho buri nzu iri** ifunguye niba ushaka ko inzu zigaragara ku ikarita. Isanzwe ifunguye.

Kugira ngo uhindure umuhanda nyuma, uwufungure ukande **Hindura umuhanda**. Cyangwa ukande ufate umuhanda ukande **Hindura umuhanda**. **Siba umuhanda** na yo irahari.

## Shaka imihanda mu nkombe

Ibi biri muri porogaramu za telefoni gusa. Niba warashushanyije inkombe y'agace:

1. Kanda **Shaka imihanda mu nkombe**. Ni buto ya mbere munsi ya **Imihanda** ku gace kataragira imihanda.
2. Kuraho ikimenyetso ku muhanda utari ku ikarita. Ibi ni ibyo ikarita itekereza.
3. Kanda **Ongeraho imihanda**.

Niba nta yabonetse, ongeraho imihanda ukoresheje intoki. Kuri Android, ibaza gukoresha aho uri kugira ngo ishake amazina y'imihanda.
