title: How do I rearrange or hide the sections on Home?
category: customization
related:

  - 259
---

Press and hold a section, then choose Edit Home Screen, or press and hold an empty space on Home. From version 1.46.0, you can also tap Edit Home Screen after the last section. The sections start to jiggle. Hold a section and drag it to move it; on iPad, sections can move between the two columns. Tap the gray minus at a section's top corner to hide it (tap Undo if you didn't mean to). To show a hidden section again, tap Add Sections at the top; it comes back first on Home. Tap Done, or tap an empty space, when you're finished; on Android, the back button works too. A section with nothing to show right now, like Approaching Conversations when you have no follow-ups today, appears as an outline while you edit, so you can still move or hide it. Getting Started stays at the top until you finish or close it. Some sections depend on your role or device: Timer appears for roles that log time, Mileage when mileage tracking is on, and Service Year Summary on iPad with an annual goal. From version 1.46.0, when Buddies is on, Add Sections also offers Together (invitations from buddies to answer, then Plans and Follow-ups with buddies over the next two weeks); it isn't on Home until you add it. With Reduce Motion on, sections stay still while you edit. You can also show, hide, and reorder sections in Settings → Preferences → Home Screen. Hiding a section never deletes your data. Your layout is a preference, so it's included in backups and syncs with your other devices when sync is on.
