title: "Ibikurikira ku Apple Watch yanjye byerekana iki?"
---

**Ibikurikira** byerekana icyo kiza mbere: Gusubirayo kwawe gukurikira cyangwa gahunda yawe ikurikira. Gahunda zigaragara gusa niba wandika amasaha.

## Yongere ku isaha yawe

1. Kanda ufate isura y'isaha yawe ukande **Hindura**. Cyangwa ukoreshe porogaramu ya Watch kuri iPhone yawe.
2. Ongeraho complication ya **Ibikurikira** ku isura y'isaha yawe cyangwa Smart Stack.

Ushobora kandi kuyifungura ukoresheje ekara y'ahabanza ya porogaramu y'isaha.

## Yikoreshe

1. Kanda **Ibikurikira**.
2. Kanda **Imiyoboro** kugira ngo ubone imiyoboro muri Maps ku isaha yawe.
3. Kuri gahunda, kanda **Tangiza igihe kibarwa** kugira ngo utangize igihe cy'umurimo cya iPhone yawe. iPhone yawe igomba kuba hafi.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gusubirayo cyangwa gahunda bigumaho iminota 15 nyuma yo gutangira, kugira ngo urebe aderesi cyangwa insanganyamatsiko. Hanyuma ikurikira ihafata umwanya.
- Gahunda idafite igihe cyo gutangira yerekana umunsi wose, nyuma y'ibifite igihe by'uwo munsi.
- Ibikurikira bireba ibyumweru bibiri imbere.
- Amazina n'insanganyamatsiko bihishwa mu gihe ukuboko kwawe kuri hasi cyangwa isaha yawe ifunze, kuko abandi bashobora kubona isura y'isaha yawe.
- Gusubirayo kwasibye ntikugaragara. Kubone muri WitnessWork kuri iPhone yawe.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ntikijyanye n'igihe:** fungura WitnessWork kuri iPhone yawe, hanyuma ku isaha yawe.
- **Biracyakomeye:** koresha umurongo w'ubufasha bw'ibanga w'Ikigo cy'Ubufasha, kandi ntukohereze amakuru y'abo usura mu bitekerezo by'abantu bose.
