A buddy's page shows their upcoming days and your shared Plans. To open it, tap **Buddies**, then tap the buddy. Tap their photo to see it larger.

## What's on the page

- **Needs your answer:** invitations from them that you haven't answered. Choose **Going** or **Can't Make It**.
- **Together:** upcoming Plans and Follow-ups you share, with each answer. Tap a Plan to open it.
- **Next two weeks:** the days they plan to go out, next to yours. Tap one to plan the same time or ask to join.
- **Invite to a Plan:** starts a new Plan with this buddy already invited.
- **View on Calendar:** opens **Schedule**.
- Their pet, if they share it and your pet section is on Home. See [Can I see my buddies' pets and their rooms?](/help/pet/pet-buddy-rooms).

## Rename a buddy or change their color

1. Tap the pencil at the top of their page (**Edit Buddy**).
2. Change the nickname or color.

A nickname is private. Your buddy isn't told, and clearing it brings back their own name. Supporters can pick any color; **Default** brings back the original. Your nicknames and colors come back if you move to a new phone.

**Sharing & Alerts** is also under **Edit Buddy**.
