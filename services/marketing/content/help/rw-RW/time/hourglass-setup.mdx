title: Nohereza nte raporo yanjye kuri Hourglass cyangwa NW Publisher?
---

Fungura Raporo y'umurimo y'ukwezi, uhitemo Hourglass cyangwa NW Publisher mu rutonde rumanuka ruri hejuru ya Emeza, hanyuma ukande Emeza. Suzuma kandi wohereze raporo muri porogaramu wohereje. Gukoporora no Gusangiza na byo birahari. NW Publisher yemera gusa raporo y'ukwezi gushize. Hourglass yakira igihe cyo kubwiriza ukwacyo, Igihe cy'inyongera cyashyizwe mu bisobanuro bya raporo bisanzwe; niba usimbuye ibisobanuro, reba ko biracyavuga ibyo ukeneye. Vugurura WitnessWork niba uburyo bwo kohereza bubura.
