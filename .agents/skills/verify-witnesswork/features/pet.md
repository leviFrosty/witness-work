# Pet Companion

An opt-in Home section with a small pixel-art pet (lion cub, pup, kitten, donkey or lamb) that lives in a room and stays happy while the User keeps going out in the ministry. Until there's a pet, the pet's Home slot offers one ("Meet your companion", with the five animals idling): "Choose a pet" opens the picker, "Not now" hides the offer (Settings → Preferences → Home Screen → "Pet" brings it back). The picker shows the five animals in a grid, the chosen one's coats as round swatches (the pup and the kitten), and, when adopting, an optional name; "Save" closes it; "Adopt" turns the sheet into a three-step intro ("Go out, fill its hearts", "Earn coins" with a room that has every item and the top hat, "Make it yours"; "Next", then "Done"), and the pet plays its species' entrance on Home once it closes. The card shows the name (or the species name) and five pixel hearts above the room. Tapping the pet itself pats it (a heart floats up and it makes its pat sound in a small bubble), dragging sideways across it rubs it, and 15 quick pats play its trick. Tapping anywhere else on the card (the name row, the room away from the pet, the card's padding) opens the pet's details. Half a heart means it's asleep ("<Name> is asleep. Tap it to wake it."); 60 quiet days and it died (greyed, lying down, "<Name> died" and a green "Tap to wake <Name>" button). Waking it brings it to one heart. Hearts are worked out from the User's records, never stored (ADR 0030). Hidden moments (a rainbow, the lion and lamb in the window, a shooting star, a gift on the rug, a visiting buddy's pet, gold hearts) are listed in `docs/pet-companion.md` and never in the app.

## Sub-features

- `pet-prompt`: the Home card "Meet your companion" with "A little friend who grows with you in your ministry.", the five animals (buttons "Lion cub", "Pup", "Kitten", "Donkey", "Lamb"; each opens the picker with that animal chosen, `PetPicker` `{ mode: 'adopt', species }`), "Not now" and "Choose a pet". It holds the pet's place in the Home order.
- `pet-picker`: route `PetPicker` (`{ mode: 'adopt' | 'change' }`), a sheet sized to its content on iOS and full screen on Android. Title "Choose a pet" or "Change pet"; the animals are radio buttons labelled "Lion cub", "Pup", "Kitten", "Donkey", "Lamb"; coats are radio buttons labelled by coat ("Gold", "Cream", "Liver", "Black"; "Orange", "Grey", "Black", "White", "Tuxedo", "Calico", "Siamese", "Tortoiseshell"); "Name (optional)" with the species name as placeholder, adopting only, up to 20 characters. "Adopt" or "Save" (disabled while nothing changed). No Cancel: pull the sheet down. After "Adopt", the intro (`pet_intro_closed`): its room preview pats like the card's.
- `pet-card`: the Home card. Header (a button named by the pet's name, with a chevron; it opens the details, `pet_details_opened` `from: header`): the name, the hearts ("N of 5 hearts" to VoiceOver), a gold note "A whole year of full hearts." on the day the hearts turn gold, and the asleep or died line. The room: "<Name>'s room" with the hint "Tap to pat. Drag across to rub." (or "Tap to wake."); only a tap on the pet (and its hat) pats, a drag rubs only when it starts on the pet, and a tap anywhere else in the room or the card's padding opens the details (`from: room`). Long-press the header for "Rename…", "Change Pet…", "Hat & Room…", "Shop…" (`pet_shop_opened` `from: menu`), "Share…" and "Edit Home Screen" (the shared Home section menu). In Home's edit mode the card (or the offer) jiggles, moves and hides like any section; Add Sections lists Pet while it's hidden or the offer was answered "Not now", and adding it brings the offer back. The room's frame clock stops while the card is scrolled fully off screen (checked every 0.5 s with `measureInWindow`).
- `pet-motion`: entrances (adopting, Change Pet), the trick (15 pats in 5 s), the wake of a pet that died (colour washes back up, hearts burst), and the greeting bubble with the pet's mood sound each time Home shows.
- `pet-room`: route `PetRoom`, the pet's details ("Hat & Room…" in the menu), its locker: only what's owned, never anything locked or for sale. A large sheet on iPhone (full screen on iPad, iPhone Duo open and Android, the room and header beside the tabs and cards from 760 pt) on washi paper, like every pet screen past the Home card. Header: the pet's name and its hearts (a button, VoiceOver "5 of 5 hearts"; tap: a "Hearts" popover saying how they fill), square pixel "Share" and "Settings" buttons top right (a "Close" after them where it's full screen), then the wallet pill (a gold coin and the coins left; VoiceOver "12 coins"; shown even at 0; tap: "Coins", how they're earned) and the dark "Shop" button, then a Visiting Today row when buddies' pets visit. The room in a hinoki frame (tap the pet to pat it) and pixel tabs "Hat" (first, selected on open), "Wall", "Floor", "Bed", "Shelf", "Light", "Sign", "Window" stay pinned; a tab holding a "New" item has a red dot (VoiceOver "<Tab>, new"). Under the tabs, a line names what's in use: "Wearing: Cap", "No hat on", "In room: Stone", "Color: Sage" (Wall and Window name each part). Then the owned items as cards two to four to a row (radio buttons labelled by option, e.g. "Top hat", "Clay tile", "Hours this month"): the one in use ringed with a check and "Wearing"/"In room"; owned items not picked yet on this install say "New". A part the Shop still sells more of ends with a "More in Shop" card ("9 to buy"; VoiceOver "More in Shop, 9 to buy") that opens the Shop at the first of them (`pet_shop_opened` `from: more`). The Window tab has a "Live window" switch row (info: follows your clock and the season); off, it shows "Time of day" and "Season" cards. A tap puts an item on at once (`pet_room_changed` `from: room`); no Save, no Cancel. The cog opens `pet-settings`.
- `pet-shop`: route `PetShop` (`{ focus?: 'part:option', from?: 'room' | 'more' | 'menu' }`, `pet_shop_opened`), from "Shop" in the details, a "More in Shop" card, or "Shop…" on the card's menu. Only for buying. A washi-paper shop in sumi ink (light and dark): back (44 pt), the "Shop" noren sign, and the wallet pill (tap: how coins are earned). Under 760 pt: a strip of the room trying on the picked item, the category row "Hat", "Wall", "Floor", "Bed", "Shelf", "Light" (the last tab in view cut about in half, so it reads as scrollable) pinned on top, two to four item cards to a row by width, starters left out (picture, name, and the price, or a check and "Owned" once bought or found; locked secrets as a silhouette named `???`), and a bottom bar with the picked item's name and its button: "Buy" with the price ("Buy Cap for 12 coins" to VoiceOver), "Need N more" (disabled), or "Owned" (disabled; under the name "Wearing it now", "In the room now" or "Yours to use in <Name>'s room"). It opens on the focused item, else the first one still for sale. A full-height sheet on compact iOS, a modal on Android, and full screen on a narrower regular-width window such as iPad mini in portrait (744 pt), still in this layout. From 760 pt (iPad, iPhone Duo open): full screen, the counter (header, room, item panel with a stacked button) on the leading side and the categories and three or four cards to a row on the trailing side, split at the fold on a half-open Duo. Buying stores a purchase (`pet_item_bought` with `item`, `part`, `price`), puts it on at once (`pet_room_changed` `from: shop`), floats "−12" over the wallet as it counts down, and shows a toast: "Cap bought. Biscuit is wearing it." (a room item: "Stone bought. It's in Biscuit's room.").
- `pet-settings`: route `PetSettings`, from the cog in the details: a large sheet on iOS (full screen on Android) with back and a "Pet Settings" plaque. "Your Pet": "Name" (value: the name or the species; opens `pet-rename`) and "Change pet" (value "Pup · Gold"; opens the picker in change mode). "Show on Home" switch. "Rewards": "Reward animations" switch (info: what the stack does), "Sounds" with "Punch", "Retro", "Soft", "Off" in a row (radio buttons; each previews), "Play a sample"; a note when Audio & Haptics has sounds off. With Buddies, "Buddies' Pets": "Buddies' pets visit" (`sharing.petVisits`) and a switch per buddy who shares a pet. Switches are pixel toggles with `accessibilityRole` switch.
- `pet-rename`: route `PetRename`, from "Rename…" on the card's menu or "Name" in Pet Settings: a content-sized paper sheet with a "Rename pet" plaque, "Name (optional)" (the species name as placeholder, 20 characters, focused, its frame dark while typing) and "Save" (disabled until the name changes). Saving an empty name removes it.
- `pet-share`: route `PetShare` (`pet_share_opened` with `from: menu | details`), from "Share…" in the card menu or the share button in the details: a sheet sized to its content on iOS (full screen on Android) titled "Share <Name>", the share card (the room still, whole device pixels per scene pixel; the name; the hearts; up to three facts, each an icon, a number and a label: "<N> planned days in a row" or "<N> months in a row" while the Service Streak shows (3 or more, Show streak on), "<N> days together", "<N> hats" once one is open; the app icon and "WitnessWork"), a "Show sign" switch (off on open; hidden while the room has no sign) that draws the wall sign and its records into the picture, and a green "Share". Share snapshots the card (`makeImageFromView`) to `<Name>-YYYY-MM-DD.png` in the cache folder and opens the share sheet (`pet_shared` with `sign` after it closes).
- `pet-rewards`: the reward stack. What a save earns the pet plays where the User is: each source stomps in as a line just above the confirming toast ("+1 Time added", "+1 Visit added", "+1 Planned day kept", "+8 Month shared", "+10 Streak milestone · 5"), the total slams with a shake, then flies into the toast's coin tally, which counts up with rising ticks. A lone small line (+1) just rises with a tick. A milestone line waits for the streak celebration and plays inside it; the celebration lifts as the total flies back to the toast. What was earned elsewhere (on another device, or days ago) plays over the pet's Home card the next time it's fully on screen, behind a scrim, and lands in the card's coin count. A tap anywhere skips the whole run (`reward_stack_skipped`). Settings → Preferences → "Rewards" (listed only with a pet): "Reward animations" (off: the toast shows a plain "+N" chip), "Sounds" ("Punch", the default, "Retro", "Soft", "Off"; each previews when picked; the footer says when Audio & Haptics has sounds off), and "Play a sample". No stack with VoiceOver or Reduce Motion on.
- `pet-setting`: Settings → Preferences → Home Screen lists "Pet". With a companion it shows or hides the card; before one, it's on while the offer shows, and turning it on brings back an offer answered with "Not now" (`isHomeSectionShown` / `setHomeSectionShown` in `src/lib/homeSections.ts`, shared with Add Sections).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Home → "Choose a pet" → the picker → an animal → a coat → a name → "Adopt" → the intro → "Next", "Next", "Done" → Home plays the entrance.
- Home → tap the pet card's name, or the room away from the pet → the details → "Hat" → a hat.
- Home → long-press the pet card's header → "Change Pet…" → the picker → "Save".
- Home → long-press the pet card's header → "Hat & Room…" → a tab → a picture.
- Home → long-press the pet card's header → "Rename…" → type → "Save".
- Home → long-press the pet card's header → "Share…" → the share sheet.
- Home → tap the pet (pat), drag across it (rub), tap the gift on the rug (keep it), or "Tap to wake <Name>".
- Account menu → "Settings" → "Preferences" → "Home Screen" → "Pet".

## Driving it with ww-verify

Preconditions:

- `wwv seed pioneer` (no pet yet: the prompt shows) or any onboarded seed. A seed doesn't release a pet made earlier on the device; run `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.pet.release()'` and `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.pet.resetPrompt()'` for the prompt.
- `__WW_DEV__.pet` is for setup, recording and read-back, never the proof of a user path:
  - `adopt(species, coat?, name?)` replaces any pet with a new one adopted today and plays its entrance when Home shows the card; `setAppearance(species, coat?)` is Change Pet; `rename(name?)`; `setRoom({ floor: 'stone', lamp: 'lantern', window: { time: 'night', season: 'winter' } })`; `release()`; `resetPrompt()`.
  - `seen({ coins, hearts })` sets what this device last showed of the hearts and coins, so the next time the Home card's header is fully on screen (or the pet's details or the Shop is in front) it plays the change to now; `seen()` reads it, `seen(null)` forgets it.
  - `override({ hearts, dead, gold, goldStartedToday, rainbow, lampLevel, wallet, secrets, window: { time, season }, paradise, star, gift, sill, visitors })` fakes a life state or hidden moment on top of the real one (merged with earlier overrides); `clearOverride()` drops it. `wallet` (a whole number of coins from 0) is what's been earned, as if from records; what's bought still comes off it, and a purchase made with it is real and stored. `secrets: true` opens every secret hat and room item (shown, never stored); without it a locked secret shows as a silhouette with a `?` badge and the name `???`. Waking a faked asleep or died pet updates the override, so the whole wake can be recorded. Keeping a faked gift moves it to the sill.
  - `play('entrance' | 'trick' | 'twirl' | 'wake' | 'cheer')`, `pat(count)` (120 ms apart; 15 plays the trick) and `scrub(ms)` (rub steps 100 ms apart in one drag, through the real toucher; 2 s of steps twirls it) act on the mounted Home card.
  - `state()` reads back `pet`, `room`, `promptDismissed`, `override`, `pendingEntrance`, `unlocks` (`stored`: found secrets and items earned before the Shop, stored on the pet; `purchases`: Shop purchases `{ item, price, on }`; `current`: `{ wallet, unlocked }` as the Home card last worked them out, null while it isn't mounted) and `card` (what the Home card last drew: `life`, `window`, `input`, `sign`, `shown`, `roll`, `unlocks`; null while the card isn't mounted).
  - `reward('soft' | 'save' | 'milestone')` plays the reward stack as a save would, earning nothing: `soft` is a lone +1, `save` is +1 Time added and +1 Planned day kept, `milestone` adds +10 and opens the streak celebration it plays in. `awayFor(days = 3)` makes the Home card play "back after a few days" (that many fewer days out and planned days kept, and a milestone fewer when there is one) the next time it's fully on screen; `seenNow()` marks everything shown, so nothing plays. Read a run back with `__WW_DEV__.stores.rewardStack.getState().run` (`slot`, `phase`, `lines`, `played`, `counts`; null when none plays).
  - `keepsakes(on)` fills the shelf with 20 keepsakes (every kind in turn) or empties it; `items(on)` owns every hat and room item, secrets too, or locks all but the starters (badge-earned secrets come straight back); `refund()` takes back every purchase. All synced, like the real thing.
  - `forgetSecrets()` lets `pet_secret_found` send again and makes owned items say "New" again.
- Developer Tools → Pet collects all of the above: Companion (prompt, release, wipe), Adopt, Collection (Keepsakes and Every item owned switches, Secrets shown, Refund purchases), the Wallet and what earned it, Fake wallet, Life (hearts, asleep, gold), Rewards (the animations switch, play +1 / a planned day kept / a streak milestone, Back after 3 days, forget what this device showed, the sound sets) and Moments.
- Developer Tools → Streak (`devStreakOverride` preference, local only) sets the Service Streak count the app shows: the Home chip, Progress, the profile and the widget's streak label. It no longer changes the lamp (the hearts do) or what's owned (the Shop does). Presets 0, 3, 5, 9, 30, 120 or any number; "off" goes back to the recorded streak. From the harness: `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.stores.preferences.getState().set({ devStreakOverride: 9 })'`.

Steps:

- **Prompt.** After `wwv seed pioneer`, the card "Meet your companion" is on Home (scroll down past the Service Report: `wwv ad scroll down --settle`). Capture with `wwv shot pet-prompt`. Press "Not now": the card is gone and `__WW_DEV__.stores.preferences.getState().petPromptDismissed` is `true`. Bring it back with `__WW_DEV__.pet.resetPrompt()`.
- **Adopt.** Press "Choose a pet". The route is `PetPicker` (`wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.state().route'`). Press "Pup", then "Liver", type a name in "Name (optional)", and capture `wwv shot pet-picker`. Press "Adopt": the sheet shows the intro ("Go out, fill its hearts"); press "Next" twice (the second step, "Earn coins", has a room with every item and the top hat) and "Done". Back on Home, the pup runs in and says "Woof!". Read back `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.pet.state().pet'`: `species: 'dog'`, `coat: 'liver'`, the name, `adoptedOn` today. Presses inside the sheet may miss on iOS; use `wwv ad snapshot --raw` and press the element's center by coordinates.
- **Details.** Tap the card's name row: the route is `PetRoom`. Back on Home, tap the room's window (well away from the pet): `PetRoom` again. Tap the pet itself: a heart and the pat sound, and the route stays `Dashboard`. On the Hat tab, pick an owned hat; `pet.state().room.hat` is its id, the line over the cards says "Wearing: <Hat>", and the pet wears it on Home.
- **Locker.** Tap the card anywhere but on the pet (or long-press the card header and press "Hat & Room…"). The route is `PetRoom`. Press a tab, then a card; read back `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.pet.state().room'`. A new pet's Floor tab shows only "Wood" and a "More in Shop" card ("3 to buy"); pressing that opens the Shop on Stone (`ITEM_PRICES` in `src/features/pet/lib/prices.ts`: stone 14, brick 20, tile 30; hats bow 10, cap 12, beanie 14 … cowboy 30, safari 32, top hat 36). `pet.items(true)` owns everything: no "More in Shop" cards. Capture each tab with `wwv shot pet-room-<tab>`.
- **Reveal.** Hearts and the wallet show as this device last showed them until on screen, then play the change once (ADR 0035). Seed `pioneer`, adopt, then `pet.override({ hearts: 5, wallet: 62 })` and `pet.seen({ coins: 42, hearts: 3.5 })`. Scroll Home so the pet card is below the fold: it shows nothing new yet. Scroll it fully into view: within about half a second the hearts fill one half at a time (the pet cheers), a "+20" rises over the coin count as five coins fly from the pet into it and it counts to 62. Read back `pet.seen()`: `{ coins: 62, hearts: 5 }`; scrolling away and back plays nothing. For a save, see **Reward stack** below. A loss: `pet.seen({ coins: 80 })`, then open the pet's details: the count goes down to now under a grey "−18", no haptic. With Reduce Motion on (`wwv ad settings reduce-motion on` where supported) the numbers change at once.
- **Shop.** From the details press "Shop" (route `PetShop`). A new pet has an empty wallet, so every price reads "Need N more"; `override({ wallet: 40 })` gives it something to spend. Pick "Cap": the room strip wears it, the bar says "Buy" with 12 coins and "Leaves you 28 coins". Press "Buy": `pet.state().unlocks.purchases` lists `hat:cap` at 12, `pet.state().room.hat` is `cap`, the card says "Owned", the button "Owned" (disabled), a toast "Cap bought. Biscuit is wearing it.", and the wallet counts down to 28. "No hat" and the other starters aren't in the Shop. Go back: the details' Hat tab lists Cap, ringed, under "Wearing: Cap". `wwv flow e2e/maestro/pet-shop.yaml` drives this. Switch categories; on a phone the row's last tab is cut in half. Check dark mode (`wwv ad settings appearance dark`), `wwv up --ipad` (two panes, full screen), `--ipad-mini` (narrow layout, full screen, below the status bar), `--se` (with a 3-digit wallet the sign moves left of center instead of overlapping it), and `wwv up --duo` closed (phone layout beside the vertical bar), open and half-open (`wwv ad fold …`: the panes meet at the fold only when half-open). The presentation is picked when the Shop opens: unfolding with it open keeps the sheet, so go back and press "Shop" again before capturing open or half-open. Real earnings: each day out since adoption (a Time Entry or a logged Visit) earns 1 coin, a Planned day kept 1 more, and each Service Streak milestone 10 while streaks show; a month at the monthly pace with a Time Entry earns at least 8. A pet adopted today on a seed earns only today's, so check the math in `currency.test.ts`, and the breakdown in Developer Tools → Pet → Wallet.
- **Reward stack.** Seed `pioneer` (it has Plans), adopt, then add time for today on Home ("Quick Action" → "Add Time", or `wwv link 'witnesswork://add-time'`) and save. Just above the "Added" toast, "+1 Time added" stomps in with a hit, then "+1 Planned day kept" in orange; "+2" slams and flies into the toast's tally, which counts up. When the save reaches a streak milestone, the celebration opens and "+10 Streak milestone · N" plays inside it before the celebration lifts. Read back `__WW_DEV__.pet.state().unlocks.current.wallet` (up by the total) and `stores.rewardStack.getState().run` (null once it's done). Tap mid-run: it ends at once with the wallet at its new count. For the card: `pet.awayFor(3)`, scroll Home until the card is fully on screen: a scrim, then "+3 Days out", "+3 Planned days kept", the milestone, and the total lands in the card's coin count. Settings → Preferences → Rewards: pick each sound set (it previews), turn "Reward animations" off and save again (the toast shows "+N" beside a coin, nothing stomps). Record (`wwv ad record start` … `stop`) and check frames: a still can't show the stomp or the count. With the celebration open, on Android check that the stack sits above the bottom edge (the Modal reports no insets).
- **Secret items.** In the Shop, every category ends with secret items not found yet: a dark silhouette over grey named `???`, whose panel says only "Found along the way, never sold". The details never show one until it's found. To open one for real, earn its badge (`hat:duck` and `bed:donut` follow Months Shared Bronze, so any onboarded seed with a shared month opens them once badges are evaluated) and read `pet.state().unlocks.stored`; the details' Hat and Bed tabs then list them with "New" (and a red dot on the tab) until one is picked (`pet.forgetSecrets()` to see it again), and the Shop marks them "Owned". `override({ secrets: true })` shows every secret opened for screenshots and stores nothing.
- **Rename.** Long-press the card header, press "Rename…", type, "Save"; `pet.state().pet.name` changes. Or from the details: the cog, then "Name". Clear the field and save: the name is gone and the card shows the species name.
- **Share.** Long-press, "Share…". The PNG is in the app's `Library/Caches` (iOS: `xcrun simctl get_app_container <udid> <bundle id> data`); read it back to check it.
- **Change Pet.** Long-press the card header, press "Change Pet…" (or the cog in the details, then "Change pet"), pick "Kitten" and "Calico", "Save". The kitten drops in (from Settings, into the details' room once Settings closes). `pet.state().pet` keeps the name and `adoptedOn`.
- **Pat and trick.** Tap the pet: a heart floats up, the bubble shows the pat sound ("Prrrrr…" for the kitten). `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.pet.pat(15)'` plays the trick (kitten zoomies). Rubs don't count toward it.
- **Twirl (hidden).** Keep one finger down and scrub back and forth across the pet for 2 s or so: a success haptic, then it crouches, hops with three quick turns and lands with a burst of hearts and its happy sound; once per drag. `pet.scrub(3000)` drives the same rub steps; `pet.play('twirl')` plays the motion alone. The first one sends `pet_secret_found` `twirl` (`pet.forgetSecrets()` to see it again).
- **Cheer.** Hearts that went up since Home last showed them (log time, press the "shared" checkbox, save a visit) make the pet cheer a second after Home shows: joy face, a burst of hearts, its happy sound. `pet.play('cheer')` plays it alone.
- **Monthly hearts.** `wwv seed publisher` (a Kingdom Publisher): an active day fills all five hearts, and they hold through the next month (ADR 0031); the intro's first step reads "Each month you log service activity fills all its hearts. Skip a month, and they slowly drain." Read back `pet.state().card.life`; check the fading against `life.test.ts` rather than by waiting.
- **States.** `override({ hearts: 0.5 })` (asleep, Zzz), `override({ dead: true })` (greyed, "Tap to wake <Name>"), `override({ hearts: 5, gold: true, goldStartedToday: true })` (gold hearts, sparkles and the gold note). Capture each with `wwv shot pet-asleep`, `pet-died`, `pet-gold`. Pressing "Tap to wake <Name>" plays the wake and records it: `pet.state().pet.wakes` ends with today.
- **Moments.** `override({ window: { time: 'day', season: 'spring' }, rainbow: true })`, `override({ paradise: true })`, `override({ window: { time: 'night', season: 'summer' }, star: true })`, `override({ gift: 'lily' })` (tap the gift on the rug; it moves to the sill), `override({ visitors: [{ species: 'lamb', name: 'Snow', buddy: 'Ana' }] })` (up to 20; each `{ species, coat?, name?, buddy?, activity? }`, with made-up buddies and Plan; `null` for the real ones).
- **Visiting pets.** Real ones come from Buddies: `wwv flag buddies on`, then seed made-up buddies with pets (`__WW_DEV__.stores.buddies.setState({ buddies: [{ inboxId: 'verify-sam', name: 'Sam Rivera', status: 'active', pairedAt: 1, pet: { species: 'cat', coat: 'grey', name: 'Biscuit' } }] })`), and something today with them: a Day Plan in `stores.serviceReports` with `buddies: ['verify-sam']` (date noon UTC today), a Visit in `stores.conversations` whose `followUp` is today with `buddies`, or an `incomingShares` entry `{ type: 'followUp', status: 'going', details: { d: <today>, s: <minutes> } }`. Each buddy visits once, earliest first, seated by `VISITOR_SPOTS` (the first beside the pet, then either side, then rows behind). Tap your own pet: once its hearts are gone, each visitor says "Visiting with <buddy>!" in turn. Long-press a visitor: after 0.2 s it's outlined (light, then dark) and drawn in front of the other visitors, still behind your pet and the lamp (and the stool a small lamp stands on), and stays so while its menu is open (on iOS the room blurs and a card with the pet, its name and whose it is lifts above the menu). The menu has View Plan (or View Follow-up), View <buddy>, and Hide <buddy>'s Pet (an Undo toast; the buddy's `overrides.petVisits` turns `false`). A plain tap on a visitor opens the details, which show one Visiting Today row (up to 4 stacked sprites, "<N> Pets Visiting Today", "With <buddy> and N others"); it opens `PetVisits`, a scrolling sheet with a row per visitor (a tap opens the Plan or Follow-up; a long press has the same menu). It closes once the last one is hidden. `__WW_DEV__.pet.override({ visitors: [...] })` with 20+ entries checks the scroll. The details' settings cog (top right, beside Share) opens Pet Settings (`PetSettings`, see `pet-settings`), whose "Buddies' Pets" holds "Buddies' pets visit" (`sharing.petVisits`) and a switch per buddy who shares a pet. A buddy's Buddies settings show the same switch under Your Pet once the User has a pet.
- **Record.** `wwv ad record start`, then `pet.play('entrance')`, `pet.play('trick')` or `pet.override({ dead: true })` plus the wake button, then `wwv ad record stop`.
- **Dark mode.** `wwv ad settings appearance dark`, then capture the card and the picker again.
- **Android.** Run the same recipes with `--platform android`; the picker opens full screen there.
- **Proof.** Capture `wwv shot` at each surface you drove, then run `wwv errors`.
- **Scripted.** Run `wwv flow e2e/maestro/pet.yaml`: it adopts a kitten from the Home offer, opens its room from the card and renames it from Pet Settings. `wwv flow e2e/maestro/pet-shop.yaml` buys a cap and finds it worn in the details.

## Gotchas

- The room and the idling sprites redraw ten times a second, so `--settle` never settles while the prompt, the card or the picker is on screen. Drop `--settle` and wait with `wwv ad wait text` instead, or press by coordinates. Scene reads (`pet.state().card`) update just after the render, so read them on the next command.
- Motions only play on the mounted Home card, while Home is focused and the app is in the foreground. `play`/`pat` with Home in the background do nothing.
- With Reduce Motion on, entrances and tricks are skipped (the pet just makes its sound), there's no idle bob, and the frame clock stops two seconds after the last touch.
- The paradise walkers and the shooting star are rolled once per Home visit (1 in 20 and 1 in 15, Live window only); use the override to see them.
- The rainbow never shows in a night sky, even when overridden.
- Overrides live in memory only: a relaunch (`wwv up`) clears them; store changes (`adopt`, `setRoom`, wakes, found secrets, purchases) persist.
- A purchase made under `override({ wallet })` is stored like a real one, and clearing the override leaves the item owned with the real wallet short by its price (it reads 0 until earnings catch up). Release and adopt again to start clean.
- Hearts come from the seed's records: a seeded pioneer adopting today starts at 3 hearts and gains one for each later active day.
- Fast Refresh keeps the mounted card's scene player (it lives in component state), so after editing `art/`, `motion/` or `render/` the card can keep drawing with the old code. Relaunch the app (`xcrun simctl terminate`/`launch` with the dev bundle id) before recording or judging art.
- `override({ window })` must use real values (`dawn | day | dusk | night`, `spring | summer | autumn | winter`); the harness throws on anything else.
- If `wwv ad record` hangs on the machine, record with `xcrun simctl io <udid> recordVideo --codec h264 --force <file>.mov` (stop it with SIGINT) and check frames with an ffmpeg tile sheet (`-vf "fps=4,crop=…,scale=200:-2,tile=8x3"`).
