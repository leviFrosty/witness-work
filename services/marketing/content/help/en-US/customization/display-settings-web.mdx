category: customization

Use your browser's zoom to make everything on the page larger or smaller.

## Make text larger

- **Windows or Linux:** press Ctrl and Plus or Minus.
- **Mac:** press Command and Plus or Minus.
- **Phone or tablet:** use your browser's text size or page zoom setting.

Zoom stays on for app.witnesswork.app.

## Choose light or dark

1. Open **Settings → Personalization → Color Scheme**.
2. Choose **Device**, **Dark** or **Light**.

**Device** follows your computer's setting.

## Good to know

- These settings change only how WitnessWork looks, never your records.

## If something goes wrong

- **Text is cut off or hard to read:** report it from the Help Center with a screenshot, your browser and your zoom level. Cover names and addresses first.
