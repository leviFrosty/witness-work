Change it if you lost a device or think someone else has your recovery key. Your account, Supporter status, Cloud Sync data and backups all stay. Only the key changes, and the old one stops working everywhere.

## Before you start

- If you lost an iPhone, iPad or Mac, remove it from your Apple Account first. Otherwise it receives the new key too.
- Sign the lost device out in **Settings → Account → Signed-In Devices**. WitnessWork then offers to change your key.

## Change your key on your phone

1. Open **Settings → Account → Change Account Key**. You need the internet.
2. Read what will happen, then tap **Change Key**. Confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, your passcode or your screen lock.
3. Copy the new recovery key into a password manager, or print it.
4. Turn on **I've saved my new recovery key**, then tap **Change Key**.
5. Keep WitnessWork open until it says **Account key changed**.

## Change it in a browser

- The browser must have been signed in for at least a day. If not, WitnessWork says when you can, or you can change it on your phone.
- Your account needs a passkey. The browser asks you to confirm with it. If you have none, add one on your phone, or change the key there.

## What happens next

- **Your other devices are signed out.** Their records stay on them. Sign each one back in with the new recovery key (**Settings → Account → Use a Recovery Key**) or a QR code from a signed-in phone (**Sign In with QR Code**).
- **Apple devices** on the same Apple Account get the new key and sign back in by themselves.
- **Older versions of WitnessWork** can't sign back in until they're updated. WitnessWork lists any it finds before you start. Update them first.
- **Your buddies** get an invite to reconnect.
- **Passkeys** keep working for signing in on the web.
- **Account Activity** shows **Account key changed**.

## If something goes wrong

- **It stopped partway:** open WitnessWork again. **Settings → Account** shows **Account key change not finished**. Tap **Continue** to finish, or **Discard New Key** to keep your current key.
- **It says the key was changed on another device:** sign in with that device's new recovery key.
- **An old recovery key doesn't work:** that's expected after a change.

## Good to know

- No one can undo a change or bring back an old key, including WitnessWork support.
- Whoever has your recovery key has your account. Keep the new one somewhere only you can reach.
- Lost every device and your key? See [What if I lose access to my WitnessWork account?](/help/account/lost-access).
