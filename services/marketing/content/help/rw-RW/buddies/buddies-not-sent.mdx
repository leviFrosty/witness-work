title: Kuki igisubizo cyanjye kigira kiti Kohereza nyuma ya cyangwa Ntiharoherezwa, cyangwa icyifuzo cyanjye kigira kiti Gutegereza koherezwa?
---

Ndagenda yoherezwa kuri mugenzi wawe ako kanya. Sinshobora itegereza amasegonda 15 mbere, mu gihe wayikanze kubw'impanuka: kanda Hindura kugira ngo uyikosore mbere y'uko yoherezwa. Kuva muri porogaramu bihita byohereza. Ntiharoherezwa cyangwa Gutegereza koherezwa bivuze ko igisubizo cyawe cyangwa icyifuzo cyawe cyo Saba kwifatanya kitaragera kuri mugenzi wawe, akenshi kuko udafite interineti cyangwa Buddies itaboneka by'agateganyo. Ntukeneye gusubiza cyangwa gusaba nanone: kibitswe kuri iki gikoresho kandi cyoherezwa ubwacyo ubutaha Buddies ivugurura, nko iyo ufunguye Buddies cyangwa usubiye muri WitnessWork ufite interineti. Kugira ngo cyoherezwe vuba, ongera uhuze, ufungure Buddies, umanure kugira ngo uvugurure. Mugenzi wawe ntazabona igisubizo cyangwa icyifuzo kugeza cyoherejwe.
