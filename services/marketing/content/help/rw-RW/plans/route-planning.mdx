title: "Nteganya nte inzira y'umunsi wanjye w'umurimo?"
---

Gutegura inzira bishyira aho uhagarara ku munsi ku ikarita mu buryo bwiza bwo gutwara. Ni ikintu cy'Abafasha kuri iPhone, iPad, na Android. Umuntu wese ashobora kuyifungura kugira ngo arebe umunsi ku ikarita, ariko **Uburyo bugufi** na **Tangira inzira** bikeneye kuba Umufasha.

## Fungura inzira y'umunsi

1. Kanda umunsi kuva uyu munsi, kuri Ahabanza cyangwa muri **Gahunda**.
2. Kanda **Teganya inzira**.

Ushobora kandi gukanda ufate umunsi ugahitamo **Teganya inzira**, cyangwa ugahitamo **Teganya inzira** muri menu ya gahunda. Umunsi udafite gusubirayo cyangwa gahunda ushobora gutegurwa uhereye ku busa.

Ikarita yerekana ikarita kuri buri hantu uhagarara ifite igihe wahagerera. Agasanduku ko hejuru kerekana igihe umunsi utangirira, igihe urangirira, n'uburebure bwacyo. Koresha imyambi iri iruhande rw'itariki kugira ngo uteganye undi munsi.

## Ongeraho, himura, kandi ukureho aho uhagarara

- Aho uhagarara hatangira nka Gusubirayo gufunguye n'ibya gahunda by'umunsi bifite aderesi cyangwa akadomo.
- Kanda **Ongeraho aho uhagarara** kugira ngo wongereho Mu rugo, Inzu y'Ubwami yawe, ahantu hava mu gushakisha, umwe mu bantu bawe, cyangwa akadomo washyize ku gice cya **Ikarita**.
- Kugira ngo uhindure uko bikurikirana, kurura aho uhagarara ukoresheje ikiganza cyaho, cyangwa ukoreshe **Zamura** na **Manura** muri menu yaho.
- Kanda **Uburyo bugufi** kugira ngo ushyire aho uhagarara mu buryo bwihuse bwo gutwara. Bikeneye murandasi.
- Kuraho aho uhagarara muri menu yaho. Ahakuweho hatondekanywa hasi kugira ngo ubashe kuhagarura.
- Shyiraho igihe uzamara aho uhagarara ukoresheje akarango k'igihe cyaho.

Inzira ishobora kugira aho uhagarara hagera ku 10, kandi ushobora gukoresha **Uburyo bugufi** inshuro 10 mu masaha 24 yose. Kugira ngo uhitemo aho utangirira n'aho urangirira, reba [Nahitamo nte aho inzira yanjye itangirira n'aho irangirira?](/help/plans/route-planning-places)

## Tangira inzira yawe

1. Kanda **Tangira inzira**.
2. Inzira yawe ifungukira muri porogaramu yahisemo munsi ya **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Imiyoboro**, uko bikurikirana byerekanwa.

Google Maps ifungura inzira yose nk'urugendo rumwe. Apple Maps na yo ibikora kuri iOS 18.4 cyangwa irenga. Waze ifungura aho uhagarara hamwe icyarimwe. Iyo aho uhagarara hafungutse hamwe icyarimwe, subira kuri WitnessWork ukande **Jya aho uhagarara 2** (n'ibindi) ku gukurikira. Kugira ngo ujye aho uhagarara hamwe, fungura menu yaho ukande **Jya hano**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Igihe cy'ingendo gikurikiza imihanda kandi kirimo umuvundo igihe uteganya. Nta murandasi ni ibigereranyo. Ubigereranye n'ibiteganyijwe bidahinduka.
- Aho uhagarara herekana igihe wakererwa ku gihe cyaho cyashyizweho. Iyo **Tangira gusura ku masaha yashyizweho** ifunguye, kugera kare bitegereza igihe cyashyizweho.
- Muri **Ibisobanuro**, huza inzira na imwe muri gahunda z'umunsi, utumire bagenzi, kandi wongereho inyandiko. Reba [Nteganya nte inzira na bagenzi?](/help/plans/route-planning-buddies)
- Inzira yawe ibikwa kuri iyi telefoni kuri uwo munsi. Ntihuzwa.
- Kanda **Tangira bundi bushya** kugira ngo uyubake bundi bushya uva kuri gusubirayo na gahunda by'umunsi. Gutegura inzira ntibihindura abantu bawe, gusubirayo, cyangwa gahunda.
- Ibanga: reba [Ni iki gisangizwa iyo nteganya inzira?](/help/plans/route-planning-privacy)

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Porogaramu y'ikarita ntifunguka:** reba ko yashyizweho ukongere ugerageze. Niba Google Maps itashyizweho, inzira ifungukira muri murandasi ikaba ishobora kwerekana aho uhagarara hake. Kuri Android, hitamo Google Maps cyangwa Waze.
