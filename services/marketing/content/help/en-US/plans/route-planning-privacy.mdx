id: routePlanningPrivacy
title: What's shared when I plan a route?
category: plans
---

Only map points are shared, and only to work out your route. Names, addresses, notes, and visit details are never sent.

## What leaves your phone

- **To work out the order and drive times:** the map points of your start and each stop go through WitnessWork to a mapping service. WitnessWork doesn't keep them. On iPhone and iPad, driving directions come from Apple Maps, the same as when you ask Maps for directions.
- **On the web:** the map is Apple's. It receives only map points, pin colors, and map settings such as the layer and language.
- **When you tap Start Route:** your navigation app gets each stop's address, just as when you navigate to one contact.
- **Counts only:** WitnessWork's usage counts record things like how many stops a route had, never places.

## What stays on your phone

- The route you build, with its notes, added stops, start, and finish, is kept only on this phone. It isn't synced.
- The addresses you save as Home and Kingdom Hall stay here too.

## Buddies

Buddies you invite get the route's plan: its date, time, title, place, and note. They don't get the route, its stops, or its notes.
