title: "Nshobora gukoresha Siri cyangwa Shortcuts na WitnessWork?"
---

Yego, kuri iPhone na iPad zifite iOS 17 cyangwa irenga, no kuri porogaramu ya Apple Watch. Fungura WitnessWork rimwe nyuma yo kuvugurura, hanyuma uvuge interuro nka "Add time in WitnessWork".

## Interuro zo kuvuga

- "Add time in WitnessWork". Ushobora kandi kuvuga log, enter, record, track cyangwa submit.
- "Log two hours in WitnessWork" cyangwa "Add 30 minutes to WitnessWork". Siri izi ingano zisanzwe kuva ku minota 15 kugeza ku masaha 12. Ku yindi ngano, Siri ibaza uburebure.
- "Start my timer in WitnessWork", "Pause my timer in WitnessWork" cyangwa "Stop my timer in WitnessWork".
- "Log a trip in WitnessWork" cyangwa "Log a round trip in WitnessWork". Siri ibaza intera. Ku rugendo rw'urugendo-rugaruka, vuga intera y'urugendo rumwe ikikuba kabiri.

## Byiza kumenya

- Igihe n'ingendo byongerwaho kuri uyu munsi, nk'igihe Gisanzwe kandi n'imodoka wakoresheje ubushize. Kugira ngo uhitemo Ubwoko cyangwa imodoka, ongeraho igikorwa muri porogaramu ya Shortcuts. Aho kandi ushobora kugikoresha ukoresheje Action Button cyangwa shortcut ya Home Screen.
- Guhagarika igihe kibarwa byongeraho iminota yacyo yose nk'igihe cyanditswe cy'uyu munsi. Iyo kitageze ku munota, nta kintu cyongerwaho.
- Kongeraho igihe n'igihe kibarwa bikeneye ko wandika amasaha. Banza ufungure **Andika amasaha yanjye** niba uri Umubwiriza w'Ubwami.
- Kwandika ingendo bikeneye **Kurikirana ibirometero** bifunguye n'imodoka nibura imwe.
- Siri yumva mu rurimi rwawe rwa Siri, rushobora gutandukana n'urwo wahisemo muri WitnessWork.
- Siri ikoresha gusa Ubwoko bwawe, amazina y'imodoka, igipimo cy'intera n'igihe kibarwa. Ntibona abantu bawe, inyandiko cyangwa raporo.
- Siri na Shortcuts ntibiboneka kuri Android.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Siri ntimenya interuro:** fungura WitnessWork rimwe, utegereze umunota, ureba ko Siri yemerewe WitnessWork muri Igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe.
- **Biracyakomeye:** koresha umurongo w'ubufasha bw'ibanga w'Ikigo cy'Ubufasha, kandi ntukohereze inyandiko zawe mu bitekerezo by'abantu bose.
