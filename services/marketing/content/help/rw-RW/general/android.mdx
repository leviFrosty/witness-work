title: "WitnessWork iboneka kuri Android?"
---

Yego. WitnessWork ikora kuri Android kandi ni ubuntu. Ushobora no kuyikoresha muri mushakisha.

## Ibikora kuri Android

- Igihe na raporo, igikoresha igihe, Aderesi n'ibyo gusubirayo, na gahunda
- Ikarita, ibirometero, inyibutsa na backup
- Buddies, harimo na bagenzi bakoresha iPhone
- Scribe AI

Ibintu bike bikenera serivisi za Apple bikaguma kuri iPhone na iPad: iCloud Sync, widgets, Live Activities n'ibishushanyo bisimburana bya porogaramu.

## Gumya ibikoresho byawe bihuje

Cloud Sync ikora hagati ya Android, iPhone na murandasi. Google Drive Sync ikora hagati y'ibikoresho bya Android. Reba [Cloud Sync ni iki?](/help/backups/cloud-sync).

## Kwimukira kuri telefoni nshya ya Android

1. Kuri telefoni yawe ishaje, fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti** ukande **Shyiraho ikindi gikoresho**.
2. Kurikiza intambwe ziri kuri telefoni nshya. Ukoresheje Cloud Sync, amakuru yawe aza nta kuyagarura.
3. Niba warafunguye Backup muri **Igenamiterere → Konti**, telefoni nshya iragusaba kuyigarura.

Ushobora no gukoresha dosiye ya backup. Kuri telefoni ishaje, kanda **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura → Kora Backup** ukopore dosiye kuri telefoni nshya. Kuri telefoni nshya, kanda **Wari usanzwe ukoresha? Subiza amakuru** igihe iyinjizwa ritangiye, uhitemo **Kuri dosiye y'ububiko** kuri ecran ya **Muraho!**. Hagati ya telefoni za Android ushobora guhitamo **Kuri Google Drive** aho.

## Byiza kumenya

- Bika dosiye za backup mu ibanga. Zishobora kuba zirimo amakuru y'abantu usura.
- Kugira ngo ukoreshe WitnessWork muri mushakisha, reba [Nakoresha WitnessWork muri mushakisha?](/help/general/web-app).
