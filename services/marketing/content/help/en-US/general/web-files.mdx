title: How do photos, exports and backups work on the web?
category: general

---

On the web, downloads go to your browser's download folder. Photos in notes work; profile and contact photos don't yet.

## Photos

- **Photos in notes:** add one with the note editor's photo button, by pasting an image you copied, or by dragging an image file onto the note. They sync with your phones through Cloud Sync. Your phones' note photos appear once they've downloaded; until then the note says "This photo isn't on this device."
- Your browser keeps its own copy of each photo, and signing out removes it. If the browser runs out of space for them, WitnessWork tells you.
- **Profile and contact photos:** you can't add them on the web yet, and your phones' don't show there. Contacts show their initials.
- Editing a contact on the web leaves its photo on your phones alone. Removing or replacing a contact's or your profile photo on the web does remove it from your phones.

## Downloads and sharing

- **Create Backup**, **Export CSV** for a mileage report, and other exports download as files. Your browser may ask where to save them, or list them in its downloads.
- On a computer, **Share Report** and other text shares copy the text. You see "Copied!" and can paste it where you need it.
- On a phone, sharing opens the phone's share sheet. If your browser can't do that, the file downloads.

## Imports

**Restore From Backup**, **Import Contact**, and the MyTime import open your browser's file chooser. MyTime files (.mytimedb) stay on your computer, and one over 25 MB is refused. WitnessWork backups are locked with a password. Enter it to restore.

## Good to know

- A downloaded backup can contain householder details. Keep it private.

## If something goes wrong

- **Nothing downloads:** check that your browser isn't blocking downloads for this site. For more help, see [Contact Support](/help).
