title: How do I use my recovery key on a new phone?
category: account
---

Install WitnessWork on the new phone and enter your recovery key. It works between iPhone and Android.

If you still have your old phone, it's easier to use **Set Up Another Device**. See [How do I move to a new phone or switch between iPhone and Android?](/help/account/account-handoff).

## Use your recovery key on a phone

1. Install WitnessWork on the new phone.
2. Open **Settings → Account → Use a Recovery Key**.
3. Type your recovery key. Capital letters, spaces and dashes don't matter. WitnessWork tells you if a character is wrong.

## Use it in a web browser

1. Open app.witnesswork.app.
2. Choose **Use a Recovery Key** on the sign-in screen.
3. Pass the security check, then enter your key.

A browser signs in to an account that already exists. It can't make one from a key. If you have a passkey, that's usually easier.

## Good to know

- If the phone used Buddies with a different account, WitnessWork asks first. That account's Buddies data is removed from this phone.
- The key brings back your account and Buddies. If your account has a backup, WitnessWork then offers to restore your records. Otherwise Cloud Sync brings them over (a new install starts with it on), or use **Settings → Backup & Restore**.
- An account made on the web works the same way, with the key you saved then.
- On iPhone and iPad, your other Apple devices on the same iCloud Keychain switch to this account too.
