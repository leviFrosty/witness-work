category: general
platforms: [web]
---

WitnessWork on the web is tested in current Chrome, Safari, and Firefox, on computers, phones, and tablets. Keep your browser up to date. Other current browsers, such as Microsoft Edge, may work but aren't tested.

## Good to know

- Signing in with a passkey needs a browser and a password manager or device that support passkeys. See [What is a passkey?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
- WitnessWork keeps your data in the browser. If that data is erased, you sign in again, and changes that hadn't synced are lost. See [What does WitnessWork keep in my browser?](/help/general/web-storage).

## If something goes wrong

- **Passkey sign-in isn't offered or fails:** choose **Use my phone** or **Use a Recovery Key** on the sign-in screen instead.
