title: Bushe WitnessWork ingabelenga backup yandi?
---

Awe. Backup yenu ipingwa pa foni yenu ilyo shilafuma, elyo ifibombelo fyenu fye e fingaipingula. WitnessWork tailamona ishina lyenu, imeili, ishina lya cibombelo nelyo ifyo mwalemba.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Pa mulandu uyu, takuli uungabwesha backup yenu. Nga mwaluba amafoni yonse aya pa akaunti yenu ne cisokoshi ca kubwesha, takuli ungaisula, ne ifwe.
- Sungeni icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha apabula ubwafya. Ukucimona, isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti → Langa Icisokoshi ca Kubwesha**.
