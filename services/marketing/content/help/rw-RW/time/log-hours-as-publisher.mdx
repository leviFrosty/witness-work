title: "Ndi Umubwiriza w'Ubwami. Nandika nte amasaha yanjye?"
---

Fungura **Andika amasaha yanjye** muri **Igenamiterere → Umwirondoro**. Hanyuma ushobora kwandika igihe no kureba aho ugeze, ku bwawe gusa.

Ababwiriza b'Ubwami batanga raporo y'uko babwirije gusa, bityo Ahabanza herekana akazu ko gushyiraho ikimenyetso mu mwanya wa **Shyiramo igihe**.

## Fungura kwandika amasaha

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Umwirondoro**.
2. Fungura **Andika amasaha yanjye**.
3. Niba ubishaka, shyiraho **Intego ya buri kwezi**.

**Shyiramo igihe**, igihe kibarwa n'igice cya **Iterambere** bihita bigaragara.

## Byiza kumenya

- Raporo yawe ikomeza kuvuga gusa niba wabwirije. Igihe cyose wandika kibarwa nk'ukubwiriza.
- WitnessWork ibibaza rimwe, mu gihe cyo gutangira cyangwa mu menyesha ari kuri Ahabanza. Niba wavuze ko oya, ushobora kubifungura hano igihe cyose.
- Kubizimya bibihisha ntibisiba igihe wanditse.
- Uri umupayiniya wungirije muri uku kwezi? Ibyo ubishyireho muri **Igenamiterere → Umwirondoro** ahubwo. Uzandika amasaha agana ku ntego y'uko kwezi, kandi ukwezi gukurikira kugasubira ku kazu.
