Google Drive Sync keeps your records the same on your Android phones and tablets. It's free and still in beta.

## Turn it on

Do this on each Android device:

1. Open **Settings → Sync**.
2. Choose **Google Drive**.
3. Turn on **Sync with Google Drive**.
4. In Google's window, choose the same Google Account on every device, and allow WitnessWork to keep its data in your Google Drive.

## Good to know

- It syncs your service records, plans, contacts, visits, categories, mileage, profile and shared settings. Photos have their own **Include images in sync** switch. Shared cars sync on their own.
- Settings such as data protection mode and analytics stay on each device.
- While WitnessWork is open, changes from your other devices arrive within about a minute. Otherwise they arrive the next time you open it. Tap **Sync now** to check right away.
- If a device connects a different Google Account, sync turns off there and your records stay on the device.
- It syncs only with your other Android devices. To sync an iPhone and an Android phone, use [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync).

## Set up a new Android phone

When setup starts, tap **I already use WitnessWork**, then choose **From Google Drive** on the **Welcome back** screen. Your records come over, and so does your Supporter status once that Google Account is connected.

For problems, see [Google Drive Sync isn't working](/help/backups/google-drive-sync-troubleshooting).
