Ni wowe wenyine. Ibyo ubaruyeho muri Spiritual Routine ni ibanga, kuri iPhone na Android.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ntibisangizwa na bagenzi bawe, ntibigaragara ku ikarita yawe ya Buddy, kandi ntibijya muri Raporo yawe y'umurimo.
- Nta nkurikirane cyangwa gutondekanya bigira, kandi ntibigereranywa n'umuntu uwo ari we wese.
- Bibikwa hamwe n'andi makuru yawe, bityo biri ku bindi bikoresho byawe kandi bigaruka iyo ugaruye backup.
- Imibare y'ikoreshwa ya WitnessWork ishobora kwandika inshuro ibiranga bikoreshwa, ariko ntiyigera yandika ibyo usoma cyangwa inyandiko zawe. Ushobora kuyizimya mu Igenamiterere.
- Niba uzimije Spiritual Routine, nta kintu gisibwa. Amateka yawe agaruka iyo uyifunguye nanone.
