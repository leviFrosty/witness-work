---

Guhuza kalendari byongera Gusubirayo kwawe kuri kalendari yo kuri iPhone cyangwa iPad yawe. Ni ubuntu. Kuri Android, reba [Guhuza kalendari bikora bite kuri Android?](/help/backups/calendar-android).

## Bifungure

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Guhuza kalendari**. Mu gihe cyo gutangira, cyangwa ukoresheje ubutumire buri mu menyesha ryawe, kanda **Ongeraho kuri kalendari** ahubwo.
2. Emera kugera kuri kalendari yose.
3. Kora kalendari ya WitnessWork, cyangwa uhitemo kalendari ukoresha kuri WitnessWork gusa.

Kalendari yawe isanzwe na kalendari ziri kuri iki gikoresho gusa ntizishobora guhitamo.

Kugira ngo ukoreshe Google cyangwa Outlook, banza ukore kalendari kuri uwo mutanga. Hanyuma wongere konti muri **Igenamiterere → Porogaramu → Kalendari → Konti za kalendari** za iPhone ufungure kalendari zayo.

## Byiza kumenya

- Buri Gusubirayo kuzaza kongerwaho nk'ibyabaye by'iminota 30, harimo ibyo wabitse mbere. Gusubirayo kwahise na gahunda ntibyongerwaho.
- Buri cyabaye gihabwa imenyesha rimwe n'irya **Menyesha njyewe** rya Gusubirayo kwacyo. Niba **Menyesha njyewe** izimye, ibyabaye nta menyesha.
- Google ishobora kongeraho amenyesha yayo. Ukureho muri Event notifications mu igenamiterere rya kalendari muri Google Calendar.
- Kuvugurura bikeneye murandasi no kwinjira muri iCloud, ndetse no kuri kalendari za Google cyangwa Outlook.
- Hindurira muri WitnessWork. Impinduka ukora muri porogaramu yawe ya kalendari zishobora gusimbuzwa.
- Kugira ngo uhagarike kongeraho Gusubirayo, kanda **Zimya guhuza kalendari**.
