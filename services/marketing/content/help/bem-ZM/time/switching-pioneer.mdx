title: Nshani ukucinja pa kasabankanya na Painiya wa Kwafwilisha pa kati ka mweshi?
---

Cinjeni umusango wenu mu **Ifyo Mwasala → Profile → Umusango**. Ico mwapanga ne bwingi fyalingana.

## Ukucinja umusango wenu

1. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Profile → Umusango**.
2. Saleni ico cilingana nomba: Kabilisha wa Bufumu, Regular Auxiliary, Painiya wa Cilatemwa, Painiya wa Pa Lwakwe, Circuit Overseer, nelyo Custom.

![Ifyo mwasala fya Profile ne menu ya Umusango](switching-pioneer.webp)

Bwelelani ilyo mwapwisha. Imyeshi iyapita isunga inshita mwalembele, kanshi ifyo mwacita fyashala bwino.
