---
id: streakBuddies
title: Can my buddies see my streak?
category: streaks
---

Yes, from its first day, if you share it. (Before version 1.45.1, a streak showed once it reached 3, and buddies on those versions still see it only from 3.) Your buddies see the number on your card in their Buddies list and, from version 1.46.0, under their own streak when they tap it on Home. They never see your time or reports. It's end-to-end encrypted like the rest of what you share. To stop sharing it, open Buddies Settings and turn off Streak. A streak that ended stops showing on your buddies' phones even if your app hasn't opened since.
