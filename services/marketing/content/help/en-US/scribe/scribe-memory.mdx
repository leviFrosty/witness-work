title: What does Scribe AI remember about me?
category: scribe
minAppVersion: "1.45.0"
---

Scribe AI keeps short notes about how you work, so you don't have to repeat yourself. For example, if you correct it ("No, the build is what I call LDC"), it saves a short note and uses it in every new conversation. To make it forget one, say "Forget what I said about the build."

## See, change or clear your notes

1. Open **Settings → Scribe AI → Memory**.
2. Edit the notes or add your own. Write one note per line.
3. To erase every note, tap **Clear memory**.
4. To stop Scribe AI from reading or saving notes, turn off Memory on the same screen.

## Good to know

- Memory holds up to 1,500 characters. When it's full, shorten or remove a note to make room.
- Your notes stay on this device. They aren't synced or backed up.
- Scribe AI doesn't save personal details about the people you visit.
- A change in Settings applies from your next conversation.
