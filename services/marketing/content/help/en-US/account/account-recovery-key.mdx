---
id: accountRecoveryKey
title: What is my recovery key, and how do I keep it safe?
category: account
---

Your recovery key is your account key written out as WW1- and eleven groups of five letters and numbers. To see it, go to Settings → Account → Show Recovery Key; your phone asks for Face ID, Touch ID, your passcode, or your screen lock first when one is set. On the web, WitnessWork asks you to confirm it's you with one of your passkeys first, which needs an internet connection; if your account has no passkey, it shows the key for a few minutes after you sign in, and after that you can view it in the phone app. From version 1.47.0, WitnessWork on the web also shows the key right after you create an account there, so save it then. Copy it into a password manager, or share it to a private note or print it, and keep it somewhere only you can reach. Anyone who has it has your account, including your Buddies, so don't send it to other people or post it. If you lose it, view it again on any phone or browser that still uses your account. If you think someone else has it, delete your Buddies data (Buddies Settings → Delete My Buddies Data): that removes your buddies and gives you a new account and recovery key. If you lose every signed-in device, every passkey that can unlock, and the key, no one can recover the account, including us (see "What if I lose access to my WitnessWork account?").
