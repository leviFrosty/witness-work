---
id: buddiesWhosGoing
title: Who can see that I'm going to a buddy's Plan?
category: buddies
minAppVersion: "1.46.0"
---

When you answer Going to a buddy's Plan, everyone invited to it sees your name under Who's Going on the Plan's page, even if they aren't your buddies, and so does anyone invited later. That way, everyone invited can see who'll be there before they answer. Your photo or emoji shows there too when Photo is on for All Buddies in Buddies Settings. Nothing else about you is shared with them: not your streak, badges, pet, or other Plans.

To see who's going, open the Plan from the day in Schedule or from the notification bell on Home. Who's Going shows everyone's photos and how many are going, whether or not you've answered; tap it for the full list: the organizer, you with your answer, and the others who answered Going. Your own buddies show there as you know them, and you can tap them to open their page. To answer or change your answer, tap the check (Going) or the X (Can't Make It) beside You, under the photos. Only people who answered Going are listed: who hasn't answered yet, or can't make it, is seen only by the organizer.

**Choosing Can't Make It** takes your name off the list for everyone, and you still see who's going. Changing back to Going puts your name back.

**Someone on the list isn't your buddy?** You can't add them from the Plan. Tap their name, then Show My Buddies Code, and have them scan it when you're together. They then show as your buddy on every Plan you share.

**Someone shows only as "1 more going"?** Their app is older than version 1.46.0, or they answered before updating, so the organizer's app doesn't share their name. The list also needs the organizer to be on version 1.46.0 or later; until then you see only the organizer.

The list comes from the organizer's phone and is end-to-end encrypted like the rest of Buddies, so WitnessWork's server can't read it. It's available on iPhone, iPad, and Android.
