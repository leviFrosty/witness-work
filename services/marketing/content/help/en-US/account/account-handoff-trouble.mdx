id: accountHandoffTrouble
title: What if signing in with a QR code doesn't work?
category: account
---

Make a new code on your old phone and scan it again. Each code works once and lasts 10 minutes.

## Try these

1. On the old phone, tap **Make a New Code**.
2. Check that both phones are online.
3. Scan the new code with the new phone.
4. Check that both phones show the same six-digit code, then tap **YES** on the old phone.

## If something goes wrong

- **The codes don't match:** tap **NO** and start over. Someone else may have scanned your code.
- **The connection dropped after you tapped YES:** keep both phones on the screen. The new phone shows **Try Again** and continues by itself when it's back online.
- **The old phone says your account wasn't sent:** check its connection and tap **YES** again.
- **The new phone can't scan:** in your phone's settings, allow WitnessWork to use the camera.
- **The new phone can't verify itself:** install WitnessWork from the App Store or Google Play. See [Why does WitnessWork say it can't verify this device?](/help/account/account-verification).
- **You no longer have your old phone:** on the new phone, go to **Settings → Account → Use a Recovery Key**. See [What if I lose access to my WitnessWork account?](/help/account/lost-access).

Still stuck? Contact support from the Help Center. Never send your recovery key to anyone.
