title: Nshobora gutakaza ibimenyetso byanjye, kubimurira ku gikoresho gishya, cyangwa kubihisha?
---

Ibimenyetso ni urwibutso: iyo ufite kimwe, kiguma, nubwo wasiba nyuma igihe, gusura cyangwa gahunda biri inyuma yacyo, cyangwa mugenzi akagenda. Iterambere rijya ku rwego rukurikira naryo rirabikwa iyo uburyo bwo kurinda amakuru buvuze gusiba amakuru ya kera. Ibimenyetso byawe bihuzwa hagati y'ibikoresho byawe na iCloud Sync kandi bishyirwa muri dosiye z'ububiko, bityo kugarura ububiko kuri telefoni nshya biragarura. Iyo amateka yinjijwe cyangwa yagaruwe agera ku bimenyetso bishya, byongerwa bucece hamwe n'incamake imwe aho kwishimirwa kimwe kimwe, cyangwa nta ncamake na nke iyo winjije cyangwa ugarura mu gihe ushyiraho WitnessWork. Kugira ngo uhishe ibimenyetso, fungura Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Inkurikirane n'Ibimenyetso maze uzimye Erekana ibimenyetso. Ibyo bibihisha hose, harimo no kuri bagenzi bawe no mu byishimo, nta kubyibagirwa; ubifunguye bwongera bikagaruka.
