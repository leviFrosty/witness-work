title: What is a Founding Supporter?
category: supporter
---

Founding Supporters were already supporting WitnessWork before the Supporter tier launched in May 2026, when support came with no perks at all. The label is simply our thank-you, and your support built Cloud Sync and WitnessWork on the web. It isn't a separate tier: Founding Supporters get the same features as every other Supporter, and it can't be purchased or earned now. While your support is active, Founding Supporter appears on your profile and in Settings. It comes from your Supporter start date, so nothing extra is stored or shared. If your support lapses, the label is hidden until you support again. It returns if your store account still reports your original start date, which is usually the case when you resubscribe with the same account. If you expected to see it, open Settings → Become a supporter and use Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't appear, use the support button in this screen's header to open Still need help? and contact support privately.
