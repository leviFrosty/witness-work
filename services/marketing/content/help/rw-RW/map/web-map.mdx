title: "Ikarita ikora ite kuri murandasi?"
---

Kuri murandasi, ikarita ya Contacts, ihitamo ry'ibimenyetso na Gutegura inzira bikoresha Apple Maps. Ibimenyetso, amakarita y'ibimenyetso, ibimenyetso bifatwa, gushakisha ahantu n'inzira bikora nk'uko bikora kuri telefoni yawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- **Fungura muri Maps** ifungura Apple Maps kuri Mac, iPhone cyangwa iPad, na Google Maps kuri izindi mudasobwa na telefoni.
- Kugira ngo werekane aho uri, kanda akabuto k'aho uri (umwambi) ureke mushakisha yawe isangize aho uri. Niba warabyanze mbere, emerera app.witnesswork.app aho uri mu igenamiterere ry'urubuga rwa mushakisha yawe, hanyuma wongere upakire paji.
- Amazina y'abantu, aderesi n'inyandiko biguma muri WitnessWork. Ikarita ya Apple ibona gusa aho ikarita iri, ahantu ho gushushanya, n'aho uri niba warabyemeye. Nk'ikarita iyo ariyo yose muri mushakisha, Apple ibona kandi aderesi yawe ya IP.
- Gushakisha ahantu cyangwa kubona inzira binyura kuri seriveri ya WitnessWork, nta izina ryawe cyangwa amakuru ya konti.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

Iyo ikarita ivuze **Map unavailable**, ibindi bya WitnessWork bikomeza gukora, harimo urutonde rw'abantu bawe n'aho guhagarara ku nzira.

- **Nta murandasi ufite:** subira kuri murandasi ukande **Ongera ugerageze**.
- **Ikarita irahuze:** tegereza umunota, hanyuma ukande **Ongera ugerageze**.
- **Ikarita ntishobora gukururwa:** kanda **Ongera ugerageze**. Niba bikomeza, ongera upakire paji, urebe ko nta kintu cyongerwa kuri mushakisha kibuza apple-mapkit.com cyangwa maps.witnesswork.app.
- **Ikarita ntiragera muri mushakisha yawe:** iracyafungurwa kuri murandasi. Ibindi byose bikora.

Niba ikarita ikinanirwa gukururwa, bimenyeshe ukoresheje [Vugana n'ubufasha](/help) ushyiremo mushakisha yawe na verisiyo yayo. Ntushyire amakuru y'abantu mu mafoto ya ecran.
