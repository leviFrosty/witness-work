---
id: cloudSyncSwitch
title: How do I switch between Cloud Sync and iCloud or Google Drive?
category: backups
platforms:
  - ios
  - android
---

Open Settings → Device Sync and choose Cloud Sync, iCloud (Google Drive on Android), or Off. Switching keeps everything on this device and combines it with your devices on the new option; nothing is replaced, and nothing deleted on your other devices comes back. Leaving Cloud Sync removes this device's copy from WitnessWork's server; your other devices on Cloud Sync keep what they've already synced from it. Leaving iCloud or Google Drive leaves this device's last copy there, and your other devices keep syncing with each other but not with this one. A device that already used iCloud or Google Drive before Cloud Sync keeps it, and Settings offers the switch once. From version 1.47.0, Cloud Sync, iCloud and Google Drive sync are all included for everyone, so you can switch whenever you like. From version 1.47.0, photos travel with Cloud Sync on their own; with iCloud or Google Drive they need Include images in sync. WitnessWork on the web uses only Cloud Sync, so using it needs your phone on Cloud Sync. To sync all your devices together, choose the same option on each.
