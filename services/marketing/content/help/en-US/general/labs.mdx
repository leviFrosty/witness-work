title: What is Labs?
category: general
---

Labs lets you try new features before they're finished. Each one has its own switch, and you can turn it on or off at any time.

## Turn a feature on or off

1. Tap your profile picture, then **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Labs** and tap it. The number beside it shows how many features you have turned on, out of how many you can try.
3. Turn on the switch next to the feature you want to try. Tap the **i** next to its name to read what it does.

## Good to know

- Features in Labs may change, or have problems, while we finish them. If one gets in your way, turn it off.
- Your choice applies only to the phone, tablet, or browser where you make it.
- Turning a feature off hides it, but keeps anything you saved with it. Turn it on again to see it.
- When a feature is ready, it leaves Labs and is on for everyone.
- If you don't see **Labs** in Settings, there are no features to try right now.
