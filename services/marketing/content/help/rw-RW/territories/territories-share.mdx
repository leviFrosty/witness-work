title: "Nasangiza nte agace na bagenzi banjye?"
---

Fungura agace, kanda **Sangiza**, hitamo icyo usangiza n'abo usangiza, hanyuma ukande **Sangiza na**. Bagenzi bawe bashobora kwandika inzu muri ko, kandi buri nzu yerekana uwayanditse.

Ukeneye Buddies yashyizweho ifite nibura mugenzi umwe. Reba [WitnessWork Buddies ni iki?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview) Nta yo, **Na bagenzi** yerekana **Shyiraho Buddies**.

## Sangiza na mugenzi

1. Fungura agace ukande **Sangiza**. Cyangwa ukande ufate agace ku rutonde ukande **Sangiza**.
2. Munsi ya **Icyo usangiza**, hitamo **Agace kose**, cyangwa uhitemo imihanda.
3. Munsi ya **Na bagenzi**, kanda buri mugenzi ushaka.
4. Kanda **Sangiza na** n'izina rya mugenzi. Ukeneye murandasi.

Buri mugenzi abona amenyesha n'ubutumire mu nzogera iri kuri Ahabanza. Akanda **Injira**, maze bigaragara mu gice cye cya Uduce munsi ya **Nasangijwe**. Ubutumire bumara iminsi 7.

## Icyo bashobora gukora

- Kwandika inzu, kugaragaza ko imihanda yabwirijwe, no kongeraho "Ntibasurwe" kuri ibyo wasangije.
- Kubona ibiri byose, harimo inzu z'abandi na "Ntibasurwe".
- Uwahawe agace kose abona kandi imihanda wongeraho nyuma.

Ntibashobora guhindura agace, kongeraho cyangwa gusiba imihanda, kugasangiza abandi, cyangwa kugagarura. Ni wowe wenyine ubishobora. Ibyo banditse bigumya ku gace, n'izina ryabo, nubwo bavuyemo.

## Hagarika gusangiza

Urupapuro rw'agace rutondekanya buri wese munsi ya **Basangijwe**. Kanda **Hagarika** iruhande rw'izina. Bagumana kopi yo gusoma gusa, nta nyandiko z'inzu ku yindi, kugeza bayikuyeho.

## Byiza kumenya

- Agace gafata abantu bagera ku 10. Reba [Ni ibihe bipimo byo gusangiza agace?](/help/territories/territories-limits)
- Ku muntu udafite WitnessWork, koresha [umurongo w'umushyitsi](/help/territories/territories-guest-link).
- Kugira ngo uhe mugenzi umuhanda umwe ngo awukore wenyine, [wushyikirize](/help/territories/territories-hand-off).
