Your Bible reading can be a simple daily check, or a reading plan that shows you each day's portion. Plans work the same on iPhone and Android.

## Choose a plan

1. Open **Settings → Features → Spiritual Routine** and pick how you read the Bible. Or tap **Bible reading** on the Routine card, then **Start a reading plan**.
2. Choose one:
   - **Read today:** one check for each day you read, whatever you read. This is the default.
   - **Bible in a year:** all 66 books in order, in 365 daily portions of about three chapters.
   - **Bible in a year, in order of events:** the same chapters, arranged roughly in the order things happened.
   - **My own plan:** pick the book to start with and how many chapters to read a day (1 to 10).

## Read with a plan

The Routine card on Home shows today's portion, like Genesis 1–3. Checking it marks that portion read and counts as your Bible reading for the day.

To see where you are, tap **Bible reading** on the card. The screen shows your progress, today's reading, and the next few portions. You can read ahead by checking a later one. Portions you've read move into **Completed**. Tap a portion there to uncheck it.

## Set dates

Tap the dates line under the progress bar to open **Plan dates**.

- **Started:** change it if you began before you set up the plan in the app.
- **Set a finish date:** turn it on to pick the day you want to finish by. The screen says whether one reading a day gets you there, or how many extra readings it takes.

## Good to know

- Plans never fall behind. If you're away, the plan waits and shows the next portion you haven't read.
- You can switch plans any time. The new plan starts at Day 1, and the old one keeps its progress.
- Finishing a plan plays a short celebration. If you have a pet, it earns 500 coins. See [How does Spiritual Routine earn coins for my pet?](/help/routine/routine-coins)
- These are WitnessWork's own chapter lists. They don't follow the meeting workbook's weekly reading.
- Your plan and progress are on your other devices and in your backups.
