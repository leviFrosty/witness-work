Yes. WitnessWork can remind you on the 1st of each month that last month's report is ready. It never sends your report for you.

## Turn on the reminder

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Turn on **Remind Me to Submit My Report**.
3. If asked, allow notifications.

You can also tap **Remind Me** when the report screen offers it after you submit.

## Good to know

- The reminder comes at your **Remind At** time. It's 9:00 AM unless you change it.
- Tap the reminder to open that month's report, then send it the way you usually do.
- There's no reminder for a month with nothing logged, or for a report you've already sent.
- Each device sets its own reminder. If you submit on another device, open WitnessWork on this one so it knows.
- Until you submit, last month's report also shows on Home's **Service Report** card and in the Home bell, even with reminders off.
- If you sent it another way, tap ⋯ on the card and choose **Mark as Sent**.

## If something goes wrong

- **No reminder came:** check that notifications are allowed for WitnessWork in your phone's settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them. On Android, also check its Reminders category.
- **On Android it came a few minutes late:** your phone may delay reminders to save battery.
- **Still stuck:** report the problem from the Help Center, without names or other private details.
