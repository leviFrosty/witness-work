title: "WitnessWork Backup ni iki?"
---

WitnessWork Backup ibika kopi y'ibanga y'inyandiko zawe kuri murandasi, kugira ngo uzigarure kuri telefoni nshya, ndetse uve kuri iPhone ujye kuri Android. Ni ubuntu kandi izimye kugeza uyifunguye. Ni wowe wenyine ushobora kuyisoma.

## Bifungure

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti**.
2. Fungura **Kora backup kuri WitnessWork**.

WitnessWork ihita ikora backup iyo inyandiko zawe zihindutse, iyo uvuye muri porogaramu, nibura rimwe ku munsi. Kanda **Kora backup ubu** kugira ngo uyikore ako kanya. **Backup ya nyuma** yerekana igihe yaherukaga gukorwa.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ikubiyemo inyandiko zawe z'umurimo, igihe, gahunda, abantu, gusura, inyandiko, ibyiciro, ibirometero, umwirondoro n'igenamiterere risangiwe.
- Amafoto n'imodoka zisangiwe ntibirimo. Amafoto agenda na [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync). Imodoka zisangiwe zigaruka iyo ushyizeho telefoni nshya ukoresheje urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura.
- Igenamiterere bwite ry'iyi telefoni, nko kurinda amakuru na analytics, ntiririmo.
- Backup ishobora kugera kuri MB 20, kandi konti ishobora kubika backup z'ibikoresho 8.
- Kugira ngo wimukire kuri telefoni nshya, reba [Nsubiza nte backup yanjye kuri telefoni nshya?](/help/backups/account-backup-restore).

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

Niba **Backup ya nyuma** yerekana ikibazo mu mwanya w'itariki, soma inyandiko iri munsi yayo:

- **Utegereje murandasi:** ikomeza yonyine umaze kubona murandasi.
- **Ivugurura rirakenewe:** vugurura WitnessWork.
- **Nini cyane:** inyandiko zawe zirenga MB 20. Ahubwo bika dosiye ya backup muri **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura**.
- **Ibikoresho byinshi cyane:** banza usibe backup y'ikindi gikoresho. Reba [Nasiba nte backup yanjye?](/help/backups/account-backup-delete)
