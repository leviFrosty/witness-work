id: shareApp
title: How do I share WitnessWork with a friend?
category: general
---

Tap your profile picture, then Settings → Share WitnessWork. Pick Android or iPhone, and your friend can scan the code with their phone's camera to open WitnessWork in Google Play or the App Store. Share Link sends a message with both store links, so it works for anyone. WitnessWork is free on both. The message only contains the store links — nothing about you or your records. If a code won't scan, turn up your screen's brightness or use Share Link instead.
