title: What can Scribe AI do?
category: scribe
---

Scribe AI can log your service time and keep track of plans, contacts and visits. It shows everything it would change as cards for you to review first.

## What it can do

- Log time, including in a category such as LDC or a new one you name. Change or delete time you logged.
- Create Day Plans and change their date, start time or length.
- Add contacts and update their name, phone, email or address.
- Log visits with follow-ups.
- Answer questions about your time this month or service year, your plans, your contacts and visits, and plans your buddies share with you.

## What it can't do

Apart from time you logged, it can't delete anything. It also can't change your settings, create or change Recurring Plans, invite buddies, or send anything to anyone.

## Good to know

- When you plan with a buddy, the plan's card reminds you to invite them once it's added.
- If a visit note reads like instructions to an AI, Scribe AI may skip it and say it couldn't read it.
