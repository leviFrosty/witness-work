title: How does WitnessWork save where each door is, and how do I use the territory map?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

When **Save Where Each Door Is** is on, which it is by default, WitnessWork saves where you're standing each time you record a door. The door then shows as a pin on the territory map.

## Open the territory map

1. Open the territory.
2. Tap **Map** (or **Map & Card**).
3. Tap a pin to see the house, its result and date, and who recorded it.

The map also shows the territory's outline and the do-not-calls. **Show My Location** shows where you are. From a street's menu, **Show on Map** focuses on that street. **Show the Whole Territory** zooms back out. **Directions** on the territory's page opens your navigation app.

## Turn pins on or off

Use the **Save Where Each Door Is** switch in the door recorder, or in **Edit Street**.

## Place or fix a pin by hand

1. Open the street and tap the house.
2. Tap **Add Pin** or **Move Pin**.
3. Tap the map where the door is.

## Good to know

- Doors are recorded without a pin when WitnessWork can't use your location or you're away from the street. If you really are at the doors, tap the pin icon in the recorder, then **I'm at the Doors**.
- Territory privacy mode keeps no door pins. A do-not-call keeps its pin.
- If pins aren't being saved, see [Why isn't WitnessWork saving where the doors are?](/help/territories/territories-location-off)
