title: "Nafunga nte WitnessWork nkoresheje mu maso, igikumwe, kode cyangwa passkey?"
---

Ushobora gutuma WitnessWork igusaba mu maso, igikumwe cyangwa kode ya telefoni buri gihe ifunguwe.

## Fungura gufunga

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Ibanga**.
2. Fungura **Saba Face ID**. Kuri izindi telefoni handitse **Saba Touch ID**, **Saba kode y'ibanga** cyangwa, kuri Android, **Saba gufungura hakoreshejwe ibimenyetso by'umubiri**.
3. Emeza ko ari wowe.
4. Munsi ya **Saba**, hitamo igihe WitnessWork igusaba nanone nyuma yo kuyivamo: ako kanya, cyangwa nyuma y'iminota 1, 5 cyangwa 15.

## Bihagarike

Fungura **Igenamiterere → Ibanga**, uzimye akabuto, wemeze ko ari wowe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Niba mu maso cyangwa igikumwe bitamenyekanye, koresha kode, PIN, igishushanyo cyangwa ijambobanga bya telefoni yawe.
- Gufunga bikora kuri telefoni wabifunguyeho gusa. Bifungure kuri buri gikoresho ukwacyo.
- Telefoni yawe ikeneye kode cyangwa gufunga ecran. Niba nta yo ifite, kanda **Fungura Igenamiterere** WitnessWork iyigusabye, uyishyireho, hanyuma wongere ufungure gufunga.
- Gufunga bikora, uburyo bwo guhindura porogaramu bwerekana ikarita ityaye. Kuri Android, ibi bisaba Android 13 cyangwa irenga.
- Gufunga bireba porogaramu gusa. Widgets, amamenyesha, Siri n'isaha yawe byerekana ibisanzwe.
- Niba ukanze inyibutsa cyangwa ihuza WitnessWork ifunze, bifunguka umaze kuyifungura.

## Kuri murandasi

Gufunga kuri murandasi bikoresha passkey, bityo konti yawe ibanza kuyigira. Reba [Passkey ni iki?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Ibanga**.
2. Fungura **Require a Passkey** wemeze ukoresheje passkey yawe. Ugomba kuba ufite murandasi.
3. Munsi ya **Saba**, hitamo iminota 1, 5 cyangwa 15. Kuri murandasi nta nzira y'ako kanya.

Kugira ngo ufungure, kanda **Unlock with Passkey**. Kugira ngo uzimye gufunga, zimya akabuto ukoreshe passkey yawe nanone.

- Gufunga bireba iyo mushakisha gusa. Gusohoka birabihagarika.
- Bihisha WitnessWork kugeza ufunguye, ariko ntibigusohora. Kuri mudasobwa dusangiye, sohoka urangije.
- Niba ukuye kuri konti yawe passkey zose ikoresha, gufunga birihagarika ubwabyo.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Gufungura bikomeza kunanirwa kuri telefoni yawe:** reba ko kode yawe ikora kuri ecran yo gufunga ya telefoni, hanyuma uhagarike WitnessWork burundu uyifungure nanone.
- **Nta passkey yawe iboneka kuri murandasi:** hitamo **Sohora** kuri ecran yo gufunga wongere winjire. Ibyo iyo mushakisha itari yahuje bisibwa.
- **Biracyanze:** koresha ihuza ry'ubufasha ry'ibanga ryo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha, kandi ntushyire amakuru bwite muri raporo rusange.
