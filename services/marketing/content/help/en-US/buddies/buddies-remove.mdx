title: How do I hide a buddy, stop sharing, or delete my Buddies data?
category: buddies
---

Open a buddy's page, tap Sharing & Alerts, and turn off Show on my calendar to hide their plans on your calendar on all your devices with Buddies; sharing continues, and your own Plans with them still show. Remove Buddy ends the connection for both people, and reconnecting takes a new invite. They are not notified, though they may notice. If removal is pending while offline, they may still see your last shared plans until WitnessWork reconnects and completes it. Buddies Settings → Delete My Buddies Data removes all connections and invites and erases your encrypted Buddies data and encryption identity. Because it gives you a new account and recovery key, it also deletes your WitnessWork backup and your Cloud Sync copy on our server. It's only in the phone app; a browser can't do it. It cannot be undone; reconnect and retry if deletion is pending. Your personal service records and contacts remain.
