title: "Nshobora kongera inyandiko ku gihe nanditse?"
---

Yego. Iyo wongeye cyangwa uhinduye igihe wanditse, ushobora kukiyandikaho inyandiko, nka "kubwiriza ku igare" cyangwa "byahagaritswe".

Ushobora kandi guha igihe wanditse Ubwoko, kugira ngo ibisa bigumye hamwe muri raporo zawe z'amasaha.
