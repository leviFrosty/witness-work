Turn on **Log my hours** in **Settings → Profile**. Then you can log time and see your progress, just for yourself.

Kingdom Publishers report only whether they shared in the ministry, so Home shows a checkbox for that instead of **Add Time**.

## Turn on hour logging

1. Open **Settings → Profile**.
2. Turn on **Log my hours**.
3. If you like, set a **Monthly Goal**.

**Add Time**, the timer and the **Progress** tab now appear.

## Good to know

- Your report still says only whether you shared. Any time you log also counts as sharing.
- WitnessWork asks about this once, during setup or in the notifications on Home. If you said no, you can turn it on here at any time.
- Turning it off hides these without deleting the time you logged.
- Auxiliary pioneering this month? Set that in **Settings → Profile** instead. You'll log hours toward that month's goal, and the next month goes back to the checkbox.
