title: "Amafoto ahuzwa na Cloud Sync?"
---

Yego. Hamwe na Cloud Sync, ifoto yawe y'umwirondoro, amafoto y'abantu n'amafoto ari mu nyandiko bihuzwa hagati y'amatelefoni yawe. Nta kintu ukeneye gufungura. Konti yawe ifite GB 2 z'umwanya wo kuyabika.

## Byiza kumenya

- Amafoto ni ibanga. Ibikoresho byawe gusa ni byo bishobora kuyabona.
- Telefoni ikurura amafoto inyandiko zayo zikoresha nyuma yo guhuza.
- Murandasi ihuza amafoto ari mu nyandiko, mu byerekezo byombi. Amafoto y'umwirondoro n'ay'abantu ntagaragara aho ubu: ubona inyuguti z'amazina, kandi guhindura umuntu kuri murandasi bisiga ifoto ye uko iri.
- Vugurura WitnessWork kuri buri gikoresho. Verisiyo ishaje yerekana inyuguti z'amazina cyangwa ikimenyetso mu mwanya w'ifoto ihuzwa.
- Niba nta gikoresho cyawe gikoresha Cloud Sync bikiriho, kopi yacyo y'amafoto yawe isibwa kuri seriveri ya WitnessWork nyuma y'umunsi umwe. Agumya ku bikoresho byawe.
- Umwanya w'amafoto ureba Cloud Sync gusa. Amafoto ya iCloud na Google Drive akoresha umwanya wawe bwite wa iCloud cyangwa Google.

## Reba umwanya umaze gukoresha

Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti** urebe munsi ya **Ububiko bw'amafoto**, ku gikoresho gikoresha Cloud Sync.

## Niba ifoto itagaragara ku kindi gikoresho

1. Ku gikoresho cyongeyeho ifoto, fungura WitnessWork.
2. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Guhuza** ukande **Huza ubu**.

## Niba umwanya wuzuye

**Igenamiterere → Konti** ivuga **Ububiko bwuzuye**, kandi amafoto mashya yerekana **Ntihuze: ububiko bwuzuye** akagumya kuri telefoni yayongeyeho. Amafoto yamaze guhuzwa ntiyangirika.

1. Kuraho cyangwa usimbuze amafoto udakeneye: ifoto y'umuntu, ifoto yawe y'umwirondoro, cyangwa amafoto ari mu nyandiko.
2. Ongera ufungure WitnessWork. Amafoto atahuzwe yihita ajya hejuru.

Ifoto y'umuntu wasibye igumana umwanya wayo mu gihe uwo muntu ashobora kugarurwa.

## Niba ifoto ari nini cyane

Ifoto imwe ishobora kugera kuri MB 25. Iyirenze yerekana **Ntihuze: ifoto ni nini cyane**. Isimbuze ifoto ntoya.
