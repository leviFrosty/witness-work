title: "Imipaka ya Scribe AI ikora ite?"
---

Scribe AI ifite umupaka w'ingano ushobora kuyikoresha. Gusoma no kwandika byinshi bikoresha byinshi, bityo ibigezweho bigufi bikoresha bike n'ikiganiro kirekire gikoresha byinshi. Ushobora buri gihe kongeraho igihe, abantu na gahunda ubwawe.

## Reba ibyo umaze gukoresha

Kanda ijanisha riri hejuru ya Scribe AI, cyangwa ufungure **Igenamiterere → Scribe AI → Imikoreshereze**. Yerekana ingano y'umupaka umaze gukoresha n'igihe usubizwa.

## Imipaka yawe

- **Utari Umufasha:** umupaka wa buri cyumweru utangira ku ikoreshwa ryawe rya mbere ugasubizwa nyuma y'iminsi 7. Uhagije ku bigezweho bike ku cyumweru. Icyumweru cyawe cya mbere gifite umupaka urenze kugira ngo uzane inyandiko zawe.
- **Uri Umufasha:** imipaka iruta cyane. Umupaka w'isomo usubizwa nyuma y'amasaha 5 utangiye, n'umupaka wa buri cyumweru usubizwa nyuma y'iminsi 7 utangiye.
- Niba ifatabuguzi ryawe ry'Umufasha rirangiye, umupaka w'ubuntu wa buri cyumweru wongera gukurikizwa, ubara ibyo wamaze gukoresha muri icyo cyumweru.

## Byiza kumenya

- Iyo ugeze ku mupaka, Scribe AI ikubwira igihe ushobora kongera kuyikoresha. Ikintu cyose yari irimo gukora kiracyarangira.
- Kuri telefoni, gusoma impapuro bibarwa mu mipaka imwe. Ushobora gusoma umubare ntarengwa w'impapuro mu masaha 24 yose.
- Imipaka ni imwe kuri murandasi no ku matelefoni yawe.
