Open the territory, tap **Share**, pick what to share and who with, and tap **Share With**. Your buddies can then record doors on it, and every door shows who recorded it.

You need Buddies set up with at least one buddy. See [What is WitnessWork Buddies?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview) Without it, **With Buddies** shows **Set Up Buddies**.

## Share with a buddy

1. Open the territory and tap **Share**. Or touch and hold it in the list and tap **Share**.
2. Under **What to Share**, choose **Whole Territory**, or pick the streets.
3. Under **With Buddies**, tap each buddy you want.
4. Tap **Share With** and the buddy's name. You need an internet connection.

Each buddy gets a notification and an invitation in the bell on Home. They tap **Join**, and it appears in their Territories tab under **Shared With Me**. An invitation stays open for 7 days.

## What they can do

- Record doors, mark streets worked, and add do-not-calls on what you shared.
- See everything on it, including other people's doors and the do-not-calls.
- Someone given the whole territory also gets streets you add later.

They can't edit the territory, add or delete streets, share it onward, or return it. Only you can. What they recorded stays on the territory, with their name, even after they leave.

## Stop sharing

The territory's page lists everyone under **Shared With**. Tap **Stop** beside a name. They keep a read-only copy, without house-by-house records, until they remove it.

## Good to know

- A territory holds up to 10 people. See [What are the limits on sharing a territory?](/help/territories/territories-limits)
- For someone without WitnessWork, use a [guest link](/help/territories/territories-guest-link).
- To give one street to a buddy to work alone, [hand it off](/help/territories/territories-hand-off).
