id: webFiles
title: How do photos, exports and backups work on the web?
category: general
platforms: [web]
---

**Photos.** You can't add a profile, contact or note photo on the web yet, because photos don't sync between the web and your phones yet. Contacts show their initials there, and a photo in a note shows "This photo isn't on this device." Editing a contact or note on the web leaves the photos on your phones as they are. Removing or replacing a contact's or your profile photo on the web does remove it from your phones.

**Downloads.** Create Backup, Export CSV for a mileage report, and other exports are downloaded as files to your browser's download folder. Your browser may ask where to save them, or show them in its downloads list. On a phone, sharing a contact or your pet's picture opens the phone's share sheet when your browser supports it, and downloads the file otherwise.

**Copying instead of sharing.** On a computer, Share Report and other text shares copy the text to your clipboard (you'll see "Copied!") so you can paste it where you need it. On a phone, they open the share sheet.

**Imports.** Restore From Backup, Import Contact and the MyTime import open your browser's file chooser. MyTime backups (.mytimedb) are read inside your browser: the file isn't uploaded, and one over 25 MB is refused. WitnessWork JSON backups are password-locked files; enter the password to restore them.

**If something doesn't work.** If nothing downloads, check that your browser isn't blocking downloads for this site. A backup or other file you downloaded can contain householder details, so keep it private. For more help, see [Contact Support](/help).
