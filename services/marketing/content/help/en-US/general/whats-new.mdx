title: Where can I see what changed in the latest update?
category: general
related:
  - 33
---

Tap your profile picture, then Settings → What's New, for a summary of recent changes. Big updates also open a short tour the first time you launch after updating. If you choose Later, the notification bell keeps a shortcut to it, and What's New has a button to take the tour again.
