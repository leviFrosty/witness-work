id: territoriesSharedWithMe
title: Why can't I edit or return a territory someone shared with me?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
platforms: [ios, android]
---

Only the territory's owner, the person who added it, can edit it, add or delete streets, or return it. A territory shared or handed to you shows "Shared with you by" or "Handed to you by" and the owner's name, and lets you work it.

## If a buddy shared it with you

- You can record doors, mark streets worked, add do-not-calls, and add results from notes on what they shared. You can't share it onward.
- To take it off your list, open it and tap **Leave**. The doors you recorded stay with the owner.

## If a buddy handed you a street

- It's yours to work. You can share it with your own buddies or make a guest link for it, but you can't hand it off again.
- When you're done, tap **Give Back**. The doors you recorded stay on it.

## If it says "No longer shared with you"

The owner stopped sharing it, took the street back, or returned the territory. It stays on your list read-only, with the worked streets and do-not-calls, until you tap **Remove From My List**.

## Good to know

- If the territory needs a change, such as a missing street, ask its owner.
- To work a territory you were given on paper on your own, add it yourself from the **Territories** tab.
