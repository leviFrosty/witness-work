id: arabic
title: How does WitnessWork work in Arabic and right to left?
category: general
---

Choose العربية under **Settings → Language**, or set your phone to Arabic and keep **Device Default**. WitnessWork restarts and reads right to left.

## What changes

- Screens, menus, swipe actions, progress bars, and charts are mirrored, and back arrows point the other way.
- Numbers show in Arabic-Indic digits (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩). When you enter hours, goals, or mileage, you can type Arabic-Indic or Latin digits.
- Months use the names from Jehovah's Witnesses' Arabic publications, such as تشرين الأول (أكتوبر).
- Searching contacts and help finds Arabic words with or without vowel marks.
- Widgets and the watch apps follow WitnessWork's language after the app has opened once.

## What stays the same

- Names, phone numbers, addresses, and notes stay exactly as you typed them.
- Phone numbers and email addresses read left to right.
- Your records are the same in every language. Changing the language only changes how they are shown.

## Week and date style

Date order, the clock, and the first day of the week follow your **Format Region**. To start weeks on Saturday, open **Settings → Region & Formats** and choose **Middle East (Arabic)**. Leave it on **Auto (Device)** to follow your phone.
