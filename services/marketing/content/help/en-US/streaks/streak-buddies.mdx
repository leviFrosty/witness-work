---
id: streakBuddies
title: Can my buddies see my streak?
category: streaks
---

Yes, if you share it. Your buddies see your streak number on your card in their Buddies list, and under their own streak when they tap it on Home. They never see your time or reports.

## Stop sharing your streak

1. Open **Buddies Settings**.
2. Turn off **Streak**.

## Good to know

- Only you and your buddies can see what you share.
- A streak that has ended stops showing on your buddies' phones, even if you haven't opened the app since.
