title: Mulandu nshi munyina tamona territory njasabankanyeko, nelyo mulandu nshi nshingasabankanya?
---

Munyina afwaya Territories mu app yakwe elyo ukusumina ukwita. Isuleni territory. Pe pi **Abo Mwasabankanya**, "Invited" pa mbali ya shina lyakwe ilola ukuti tasumina kale.

## Nga munyina tamona

- **App yakwe tailakwata Territories.** Afwaya ukukusha WitnessWork ukufuma ku App Store nelyo Google Play. Territories ilesa panono panono. Ukwita kwenu kulolela ukufika pa nshiku 7.
- **Tasumina.** Ukwita kuli mu cibelu pa Home, na **Ingila** na **Kana** (**Sumina** pa musebo uwapelwa). Ilyo aingila, kuli mu tab yakwe ya Territories pe pi **Abo Mwasabankanya Nabo** (nelyo **Yapelwa Ine**).
- **Ukwita kwapwa nelyo akaana.** Tinikeni **Imya** pa mbali ya shina lyakwe, elyo musabankanye territory nakabili.
- **Alolesha pa cibombelo cimbi.** Territory shasabankanishiwa shimoneka pa fibombelo fyakwe fyonse ifikwata Buddies. Mulomba ukuti asule WitnessWork uko elyo alolele panono.

## Nga ukusabankanya tekubombela imwe

Icebo cilanda umulandu:

- **"Set Up Buddies":** ukusabankanya na banyina no kupela kufwaya Buddies pa foni iyi. Tinikeni ukuti mupange. Moneni [WitnessWork Buddies ni nshi?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview) Nelyo mutume link ya mwenyi, iyashifwaya Buddies. Ukusabankanya takwabako pa web.
- **"Add a Buddy First":** kuti mwasabankanya fye na bantu mwalingana na bo mu Buddies. Baiteni pa ntanshi, nelyo mutume link ya mwenyi.
- **"Couldn't reach WitnessWork"** nelyo **"Couldn't start sharing. Check your connection":** tantikeni ku intaneti elyo mueseko nakabili.
- **"Couldn't start sharing. Update WitnessWork, then try again.":** ikeni ukukusha ukufuma ku App Store nelyo Google Play.
- **"Sharing territories is paused right now.":** eseni pa numa. Territory yenu yashala pa foni yenu.
- **"Only the territory's owner can do that.":** moneni [Mulandu nshi nshingalungika nelyo ukubwesha territory umbi asabankanyeko?](/help/territories/territories-shared-with-me)
- **"This territory is too large to share.":** moneni [Imipaka ya kusabankanya territory ni nshi?](/help/territories/territories-limits)
- **"Pick a street that isn't handed off.":** umusebo uwapelwa tawingasabankanishiwa nakabili ukufika wabwelela kuli imwe.
- **"Some changes to (territory) weren't shared. That street was handed off meanwhile.":** umusebo wapelwa nelyo watolwa ilyo mwabula intaneti, kanshi ifyo mwacinja fyashasungwa.

Nga icabipa citwalilila, bomfyeni akatini ka support pamulu pa screen iyi, mulole pe pi **nomba line kabila afwilisha?**, elyo mubike inshita ya app yenu. Mwisabika nambala sha territory, adiresi, na mashina mu fyebo fya bwafya fya bantu bonse.
