title: Why did my phone switch to Cloud Sync after I signed in on the web?
category: backups
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

WitnessWork on the web works only with [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync). When you sign in on the web and your phone uses iCloud Sync (Google Drive Sync on Android) or has sync off, the web asks you to turn on Cloud Sync. If you choose **Turn On**, the next time you open WitnessWork on your phone, it switches to Cloud Sync by itself and shows "Cloud Sync is on". The web continues once your phone's records have reached Cloud Sync.

## Nothing is lost

Your phone keeps every record and photo and combines them with anything already on Cloud Sync. Nothing is replaced, and nothing you deleted comes back. Its last copy on iCloud or Google Drive stays where it was, so your devices still using iCloud Sync or Google Drive Sync aren't affected. Your other phones on the same account switch too, each the next time you open WitnessWork on it. Like the rest of Cloud Sync, your records are end-to-end encrypted, so only your own devices can read them.

## Switching back

Open **Settings → Device Sync** on your phone and choose iCloud (Google Drive on Android) or Off. Your phone switches only once each time you turn on Cloud Sync from the web, so it stays on the option you choose. WitnessWork on the web needs a phone on Cloud Sync to get your records, so it asks again the next time you use it.

## If it doesn't switch

- Make sure your phone is online and you've finished setting up WitnessWork on it, then open the app again.
- If the web says "Update WitnessWork on your phone", update the app from the App Store or Google Play, then open it.
- You can always choose Cloud Sync yourself in **Settings → Device Sync**.
