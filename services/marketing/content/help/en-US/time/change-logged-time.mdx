id: changeLoggedTime
title: How do I change or delete time I already logged?
category: time
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Open Add Time from Home or from the + button and tap the day in the row of days at the top, or tap the date above them to pick any other day from a calendar. The time already logged on that day is listed under the days, each entry with its length, Type and the first line of its note.

- **Change an entry:** tap it. It opens in the same form, which now reads Update Time. Change the time, Type or note, or tap another day to move the entry there, and tap Save. Add Time stays open on a new entry, so you can fix one entry and still log more.
- **Leave an entry without saving:** tap it again, or tap its day. If you changed something, WitnessWork asks before discarding it.
- **Delete an entry:** touch and hold it (on the web, right-click it) and choose Delete, or open it and tap Delete…. Deleting can't be undone.

A day with more than three entries shows the first two; tap Show more to see the rest.

To log time on more than one day in one go, add the first entry, then tap Add Another on the message that confirms it. Add Time opens again on the same day, and you can tap a different day there.

You can also change an entry from its day: tap the day on the Schedule tab, in This Week on Home, or under All Days on the Progress tab, then tap the entry. See [How do I log time or plan a specific day?](/help/time/log-time-for-day)

Logging time appears only if your status logs hours; Kingdom Publishers turn on Log my hours in Settings → Profile first.
