id: wearOs
title: Is there a Wear OS app?
category: time
platforms:
  - android
---

Yes, for an Android phone with a watch on Wear OS 3 or later. Install WitnessWork from the Play Store on your watch, or from WitnessWork's Play Store page on your phone, then open WitnessWork on your phone once so your watch gets your progress. The watch shows this month's hours and whether you're ahead of or behind your plans, counted the same way as on your phone (without plans, how much a day it takes to reach your goal). On the watch you can add time for today with an optional Type, start and pause your phone's timer, and save the timer's time as an entry. If you don't log hours as a Kingdom Publisher, you can mark that you shared the good news this month. Add the Monthly Progress and Up Next complications to a watch face, and the Monthly Progress tile to start or pause your timer or add time with a swipe. While your timer runs, it shows on your watch face with Pause and Stop; Stop adds the timer's whole minutes as an entry for today. Allow notifications when your watch asks, so the running timer can show there. Your phone keeps your records. The watch receives your monthly progress, report status, timer, Types, car names and distance unit, and for Up Next the name, address and topic of your follow-ups and the title and place of your plans for the next two weeks. It never receives notes or other contact details, and it can't edit or delete entries. Because others may see your watch, Up Next shows "Follow Up" instead of a name on watch faces, and in the app while your wrist is down; open it to see who it is. Wear OS can't add time or log trips by voice, so use the tile or the app instead. The watch app needs your phone with WitnessWork installed.
