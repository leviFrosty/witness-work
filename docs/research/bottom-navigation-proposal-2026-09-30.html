Protect the Map workflow

Map appears in 5.2% of observed sessions. Yet 37.5% of Map sessions have no Contacts screen event. Keep List / Map visible, remember the selection, preserve deep links, and test the extra step for existing Map users.

Add Time reached 84% of observed IDs.

Keep a direct Home action and logging from a selected day. Retain the global Add menu until its entry points are measured.

This is a layout proposal, not a shipped redesign. Source review covers both platforms; live app inspection was blocked by an occupied Android device and an iOS simulator discovery error.

The research window has substantial data only from September 19 onward. Buddies demand and a full monthly reporting cycle remain unmeasured.
