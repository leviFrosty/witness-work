title: Mafuta ya Gasoline, Diesel, na Electric yafumine kwi, elyo kuti nayacinja?
---

WitnessWork imwongelako mafuta yatatu, kanshi motoka ipya ifwaya fye ishina: **Gasoline**, **Diesel**, na **Electric**.

## Saleni mafuta ya motoka

1. Isuleni **Amakilomita → Ifyo Mwasala → Lundapo Motoka**.
2. Pa mulongo wa **Mafuta**, saleni mafuta.
3. Lembeni ifyo motoka ipita pa mafuta ukumona umutengo wa kupima.

## Cinjeni mutengo wa mafuta

1. Isuleni **Amakilomita → Ifyo Mwasala → Mafuta**.
2. Tinikeni mafuta.
3. Lembeni umutengo mulipila.

Mafuta yonse yatendeka na mutengo wa pakati wa 2026 pa icalo cenu, mu mashuma ya icalo cenu. Ni mutengo wa pakati wa calo, te uwa pa pump yenu. Nga icalo cenu tacikwata mutengo wa pakati, mafuta yatendeka ukwabula mutengo. Kuti mwaongelako umo mu nshila imo fye.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Gasoline na Diesel shitengwa pa gallon nelyo pa litre, ukukonka ifyo mwasala mu **Ifipimo**.
- **Electric** itengwa pa kWh. Motoka iyabomfya yo ilemba ifyo ipita nga mi/kWh, km/kWh, nelyo kWh/100 km. Ukwongelako mafuta yambi ayatengwa pa kWh, tinikeni **Lundapo Mafuta** elyo mubike **Priced Per** ku kWh.
- Kuti mwacinja amashina ya mafuta ayo, ukucinja imitengo yayo, nelyo ukuyafumyapo: tinikeni mafuta, elyo **Fumyapo…**. Mafuta mwafumyapo yashala yafumishiwapo, na pa fibombelo fimbi fyenu.
- Nga mwakwata kale mafuta ya shina limo, WitnessWork ishala na ya kwenu.
- Motoka iyasabankanywa ishala na mafuta yaiko, kanshi ukucinja uku tacimukwata.
