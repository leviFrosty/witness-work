title: Why is there a number on the WitnessWork app icon?
category: general
platforms:
  - ios
  - android

On iPhone, the number on the app icon is how many items in the bell on Home you haven't read yet. News from your buddies, like a buddy's new badge or a reaction to yours, is never counted.

## Make the number go down

1. Open WitnessWork and tap the bell on Home.
2. Scroll through the items, or dismiss or clear them.

## Hide the number

Open your phone's notification settings for WitnessWork and turn off **Badges**. This only hides the number. It doesn't affect the badges you earn on your profile.

## Good to know

- The number needs notification permission.
- It updates while WitnessWork is open and when a Follow-up, Plan, or contact reminder arrives. Other items, like last month's report, are counted the next time you open WitnessWork.
- Each device keeps its own count.
- On Android, the app icon doesn't show a number. Your launcher may show a dot instead.

## If something goes wrong

- **The number looks wrong:** open WitnessWork and leave it for a moment to refresh. If it still doesn't match the bell, contact support from the Help Center.
