Oya. Ibimenyetso ni ibyibutso. Iyo ufite kimwe, kigumaho, nubwo nyuma wasiba igihe, amasura cyangwa gahunda biri inyuma yacyo, cyangwa mugenzi akagenda.

## Jyana ibimenyetso kuri telefoni nshya

Garura backup yawe kuri telefoni nshya maze ibimenyetso byawe bigaruke. Niba guhuza bifunguye, bigaragara no ku bindi bikoresho byawe.

## Hisha ibimenyetso

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Inkurikirane n'Ibimenyetso**.
2. Zimya **Erekana ibimenyetso**.

Ibi biyihisha hose, harimo kuri bagenzi bawe no mu byishimo. Ongera ubifungure bigaruke.

## Byiza kumenya

- Aho ugeze ku rwego rukurikira na ho hakabikwa iyo uburyo bwo kurinda amakuru butanga inama yo gusiba amakuru ya kera.
- Iyo amateka yazanywe cyangwa yagaruwe agera ku bimenyetso bishya, byongerwaho mu bucece hamwe n'incamake imwe, nta byishimo kuri buri kimwe. Mu gihe ushyiraho WitnessWork, byongerwaho nta ncamake na gato.
