title: How do I decorate my pet’s room?
category: pet
---

Tap your pet's card on Home anywhere except on your pet (tapping your pet pats it), or long-press the card's name and choose Hat & Room…. This opens your pet's details: its name and hearts, your coins beside the Shop, and its room. Pick a tab (Hat, Wall, Floor, Bed, Shelf, Light, Sign, or Window) and tap a picture of something you own; the room at the top changes right away and the change is saved. From version 1.46.0, a line above the pictures says what's in use, like Wearing: Cap. On an iPad or an unfolded iPhone Duo, the details fill the screen with the room beside your items; tap Close to leave. Live window follows your clock and the season where you are (from your region; seasons are reversed south of the equator); turn it off to choose a time of day and a season, each of which can also stay Live. The sign on the wall can show your hours this month, your hours this service year, the months you shared in the ministry, your next plan, or this month's conversations and return visits, or nothing.

Some items are open from the start. From version 1.46.0, you buy the others in the Shop with coins, which you earn by going out in the ministry (see the question about coins and the Shop). The details show only what you own, like a game's locker; everything else is in the Shop. Tap Shop beside your coins, or the More in Shop card at the end of a tab, which opens the Shop at the next item there to buy. Buying an item puts it in the room right away. An item you own but haven't picked yet on this device says New, and its tab has a red dot. Once you own an item, it's yours for good. Some items are secrets, which are never sold; see the question about secret items. Hats are bought the same way (see the question about hats).

Before version 1.46.0, each of the others opened up the first time your streak reached the number on its flame: from 1 to 120 kept planned days for pioneers and other roles that log hours, or from 1 to 12 months in a row for publishers. If you turned off Show streak in Settings → Preferences → Streaks & Badges, or you logged hours but hadn't made any plans, one more item opened up instead for each month you were active (any day with logged time, a visit, or a saved plan) since you adopted your pet. Items that opened up that way stay yours after you update, and your streak no longer opens anything.

When a secret item or hat opens up from something you just did, a small card with it slides in at the top of the screen (from version 1.45.0; before 1.46.0 this happened for any item that opened up): tap it to see the item in your pet's room, then Try it on or Decorate Room to come here, or swipe the card away. A secret that opens up while WitnessWork isn't open, or whose card you don't tap, waits in the notification bell on Home; opening Hat & Room clears it.
