---

Gusoma Bibiliya kwawe bishobora kuba ukubarura kworoshye buri munsi, cyangwa gahunda yo gusoma ikwereka igice cya buri munsi. Gahunda zikora kimwe kuri iPhone na Android.

## Hitamo gahunda

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Spiritual Routine** uhitemo uko usoma Bibiliya. Cyangwa ukande **Bible reading** ku ikarita ya Routine, hanyuma **Start a reading plan**.
2. Hitamo imwe:
   - **Soma uyu munsi:** ikimenyetso kimwe kuri buri munsi usomye, icyo wasomye cyose. Ni yo isanzwe.
   - **Bibiliya mu mwaka umwe:** ibitabo byose 66 uko bikurikirana, mu bice 365 bya buri munsi by'ibice bitatu hafi.
   - **Bibiliya mu mwaka umwe, ukurikije uko ibintu byabaye:** ibice bimwe, bitondetse hafi uko ibintu byabaye.
   - **Gahunda yanjye bwite:** hitamo igitabo utangiriraho n'umubare w'ibice usoma ku munsi (1 kugeza 10).

## Soma ukurikije gahunda

Ikarita ya Routine Ahabanza yerekana igice cy'uyu munsi, nka Itangiriro 1–3. Kukibaruraho bikigira igisomwe kandi bibarwa nk'isoma ryawe rya Bibiliya ry'uwo munsi.

Kugira ngo ubone aho ugeze, kanda **Bible reading** ku ikarita. Ecran yerekana aho ugeze, isomwa ry'uyu munsi n'ibice bike bikurikira. Ushobora gusoma imbere ubaruraho igikurikira. Ibice wasomye bijya muri **Ibyakozwe**. Kanda igice aho kugira ngo ugikureho ikimenyetso.

## Shyiraho amatariki

Kanda umurongo w'amatariki munsi y'akabari k'aho ugeze kugira ngo ufungure **Plan dates**.

- **Started:** hindura niba warabitangiye mbere yo gushyiraho gahunda muri porogaramu.
- **Shyiraho itariki yo kurangiriraho:** ifungure kugira ngo uhitemo umunsi ushaka kurangirizaho. Ecran ivuga niba gusoma rimwe ku munsi bikugeza aho, cyangwa ni ingahe z'inyongera zikenewe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gahunda ntizigera zisigara inyuma. Niba uri kure, gahunda iratereza ikerekana igice gikurikira utarasoma.
- Ushobora guhindura gahunda igihe icyo ari cyo cyose. Gahunda nshya itangira ku Munsi wa 1, kandi ishaje igumana aho yari igeze.
- Kurangiza gahunda bivuza ishimwe rigufi. Niba ufite itungo, ribona amacoin 500. Reba [Spiritual Routine ibonera itungo ryanjye amacoin ite?](/help/routine/routine-coins)
- Izi ni urutonde rw'ibice bwite bwa WitnessWork. Ntizikurikiza gusoma kwa buri cyumweru kwo mu gitabo cy'amateraniro.
- Gahunda yawe n'aho ugeze biri ku bindi bikoresho byawe no muri backup zawe.
