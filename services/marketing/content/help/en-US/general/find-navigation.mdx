title: Where are Map, Buddies, and Settings?
category: general
pinned: true
---

The bottom bar shows Home, Schedule, Contacts, and Progress, with Add beside them for logging time, creating a Plan, or adding a contact. Progress and Add Time appear when your role logs hours. Map is inside Contacts, and WitnessWork opens the view you used last. From version 1.46.0, tap Map in the List | Map switch beside the search field; on the map, the switch stays under the Contacts title. Before 1.46.0, tap the map button beside the Contacts title. Sort & Filter opens from Filters at the start of the chips under the search field, or from ••• at the top of Contacts (before 1.46.0, the sliders button at the top). Buddies, when available, opens from Schedule: from 1.46.0, tap the Buddies row under the Schedule title (before, the Buddies button at the top). From 1.46.0, the Buddies row on your Profile opens it too, as does the Together section if you add it to Home, and on a wide tablet layout Buddies has its own place in the sidebar. View Report at the top of Progress opens the Service Report for the month you're viewing. Tap your profile picture at the top left of any main screen for Profile (your role, time served, and stats), Settings, Support, and Help Center. On a wide tablet layout, Settings is also in the sidebar; from 1.46.0, it's at the bottom of the sidebar, under your picture and name, which leave the page headers while the sidebar shows. Also from 1.46.0, Home has a Settings button (the gear) beside the notification bell, except where the sidebar or iPhone Duo's side rail already shows Settings at its bottom. If you hid the sidebar, tap Show sidebar in the page header to bring it back. If you turned on Hide Donate Heart, Support is left out of that menu and stays in Settings. Your contacts, plans, and records are unchanged; only where you find them moved. If something still seems missing, update the app, then use Still need help? below.
