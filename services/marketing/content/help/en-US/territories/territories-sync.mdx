title: Are my territories synced and backed up?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Yes. Your territories, with their streets, door results, do-not-calls, and returned territories, sync to your other devices and are included in your backups, the same way your contacts are.

## Good to know

- Syncing uses the option your phone uses: Cloud Sync, iCloud, or Google Drive on Android. See **Settings → Sync**.
- Changes and deletions sync too. A territory you delete on one device is deleted on all of them.
- The photo of the card stays on the phone where you added it. Add it again on another device if you want it there.
- Territories you share stay shared on each of your phones that sync with this one, with what others record. Territories shared or handed to you appear on each of your devices that have Buddies set up.
- With territory privacy mode on, no house-by-house records are kept, so none are synced or backed up. Turning it on also clears them from your other devices.
- If an older app doesn't show Territories, update WitnessWork. Your territories stay safe in the meantime.
