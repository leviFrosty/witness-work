Bomfyeni ifibatani ifyapanshi pa kutuma ubwafya bwa app nelyo ukupempa icipya. Lundeni ifyo mwacita pa kuleta ubwafya, ifyo mwasubile, icibombelo cenu ne version ya operating system, ne version ya WitnessWork iyo mulemona pa Still need help?. Fumyeni ifyebo fya bantu ba kulanshanya, amalembo, ama ID ya akaunti, na Buddies Codes nelyo ama link ya kwita mu malipoti ya bantu bonse na fikope. Pa bwafya bwa backup nelyo ubwafya bumbi ubwa cinkama, bomfyeni link ya email ya cinkama mu Still need help?. Source code, Ifunde lya Cinkama, na Fya Kubomfya fyaba mu Settings → Pa Mulandu.
