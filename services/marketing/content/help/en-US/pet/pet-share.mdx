id: petShare
title: How do I share a picture of my pet?
category: pet
---

Tap your pet's card on Home (anywhere except on your pet) and tap Share (the share button at the top right), or long-press the card's name and choose Share…. Its share card shows your pet in its room with its name and hearts, a few facts (your Service Streak while it's above 0, unless you turned off Show streak in Settings → Preferences → Streaks & Badges; how many days you've been together; and how many hats it has), and the WitnessWork name at the bottom. Tap Share to send the card as a picture with any app in the share sheet. Nothing is sent until you choose where it goes, and the picture holds nothing else about you or your records. If sharing fails, a message says so; try again, and if it keeps failing, close and reopen WitnessWork.
