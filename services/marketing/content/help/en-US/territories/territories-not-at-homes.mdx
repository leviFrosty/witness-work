title: How do I go back to the houses where nobody was home?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Open the territory and look under **Not at Home**. It lists each street with houses to try again.

## Go back to them

1. Open the territory and find **Not at Home**.
2. Tap a street to see only its not-at-homes.
3. Record each house again with the door recorder.

## Good to know

- Recording a house again on another day adds a try. The list shows "Tried 2 times".
- Each house shows when it was last recorded, so you can try again on a different day or hour. To see when people are most often home, see [How do I see the best days and times to find people home?](/help/territories/territories-insights)
- On a street with several houses recorded, switch between **All** and **Not at Home** above the list.
- A house you record as **Spoke** or **Do Not Call** leaves the list.
- With territory privacy mode on, no house records are kept, so there's no list. See [What is territory privacy mode?](/help/territories/territories-privacy-mode)
