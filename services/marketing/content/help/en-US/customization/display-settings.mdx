title: Does WitnessWork support Bold Text, larger text, and other display settings?

Yes. WitnessWork follows your phone's display settings, so there's nothing to turn on in the app.

## Settings it follows

- **Text size:** Larger Text on iPhone and iPad, Font size on Android. WitnessWork wraps rows so words stay whole. On a tablet, very large text swaps the sidebar for the bottom bar.
- **Bold text:** Bold Text on iPhone and iPad, Bold text on Android 12 or later. On Android, the change shows when you come back to WitnessWork.
- **Reduce Transparency (iPhone and iPad):** makes blurred headers and bars solid.
- **Increase Contrast (iPhone and iPad):** makes gray text and borders stand out more. On Android, the system's High contrast text applies.
- **Reduce Motion (Remove animations on Android):** keeps celebrations still, with no confetti.

To choose light or dark, open **Settings → Personalization → Color Scheme**.

## Good to know

- These settings change only how WitnessWork looks, never your records.

## If something goes wrong

- **Text is cut off or hard to read:** report it from the Help Center with a screenshot, your phone and your text size. Cover names and addresses first.
