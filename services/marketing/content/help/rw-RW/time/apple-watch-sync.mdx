title: Kuki Apple Watch yanjye igira iti Guhuza na iPhone?
---

Igihe n'ingendo wongeraho ku isaha yawe byoherezwa kuri iPhone yawe ako kanya iyo iri hafi, cyangwa ubutaha zihuye, kandi bikabikwa WitnessWork ikora kuri iPhone yawe. Kugeza icyo gihe isaha yawe igaragaza Guhuza na iPhone. Igiteranyo cyayo na complication zayo birimo icyo gihe ako kanya, uretse Igihe cy'inyongera, cyongerwaho iPhone yawe imaze kukibika kandi gishyirwaho imbibi z'ukwezi z'inyongera. Buri nyandiko igumana umunsi wayongeyeho ku isaha, ibikwa rimwe gusa n'iyo yoherejwe kabiri, kandi ntigarurwa niba wari warayisibye kuri iPhone yawe. Inyandiko zibitswe zigera ku bindi bikoresho byawe zicishijwe muri iCloud Sync nk'izindi nyandiko zose. Gutangiza, guhagarika by'agateganyo, guhagarika no kubika igihe kibarwa bikenera iPhone yawe iri hafi. Niba isaha ivuga ko igihe kibarwa cyahindutse kuri iPhone yawe, reba igihe hanyuma ugerageze nanone. Niba isaha yawe ikomeza kutavugururwa, komeza iPhone yawe hafi kandi idafunze, fungura WitnessWork kuri iPhone yawe, hanyuma uyifungure ku isaha yawe. Nyuma yo gutangiza iPhone yawe bundi bushya, yifungure rimwe mbere yo guhuza. Ku bindi bibazo, koresha ihuza ry'ubufasha bwihariye ryo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha kandi ntushyire inyandiko zawe muri raporo zishyirwa ahagaragara.
