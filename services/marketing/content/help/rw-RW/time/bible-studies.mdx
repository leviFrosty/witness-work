id: bibleStudies
title: Ni mu buhe buryo inyigisho za Bibiliya zibarwa?
---

Ubushakashatsi bukorwa buri munsi, ntabwo bukorwa buri munsi. To count a study, open a contact, log a conversation, and mark it as "Conducted Bible Study." That contact will count as 1 study for the current month, even if you have multiple sessions with them. Kugira ngo wongere umubare w'abashakashatsi, andika ikiganiro cyo kwiga hamwe n'undi muntu ukora.
