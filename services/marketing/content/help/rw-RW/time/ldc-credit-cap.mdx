id: ldcCreditCap
title: Are LDC and other credit hours included in my totals?
---

Yego. Amasaha y'inguzanyo abarwa ku isaha yawe ya buri kwezi. Mu mwaka w'umurimo, amasaha 55 y'inguzanyo buri kwezi arabarwa, hakurikijwe ibipimo byatangajwe n'ishami. Buri kintu cyose kiri hejuru y'igipimo kiracyashyirwa ku rutonde rw'ibicuruzwa, ariko ntikijya mu gipimo cy'umwaka.
