---
id: stopSupporting
title: What happens if I stop supporting?
category: supporter
---

To stop, open Settings → Manage subscription and choose Cancel or change subscription, or cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Supporter access continues until the end of the period you've paid for, and your existing data and local backups remain available. After that, sync pauses while Supporter access is unavailable. If sync was on, it resumes when access returns; an explicit choice to turn it off stays off. Photo sync follows its own setting. Your accent color and app icon return to the defaults, but your choices are kept for when you support again. Check Settings → Device Sync for the current status. If you only need a break, you can pause instead.
