Yes. With a keyboard connected to an iPad on iPadOS 26 or later, Command plus a number opens a main screen.

## Use the shortcuts

1. Connect a keyboard to your iPad and go to the main navigation.
2. Hold **Command**. Small number labels appear beside the bottom tabs or sidebar items.
3. Press the number shown for the screen you want.

## Good to know

- Numbers follow the order of what you see. By default, **Home** is Command 1 and **Contacts** is Command 2.
- Close any open form first. These shortcuts don't save or discard anything.
- To save the form you're typing in, see [Can I save a form with a keyboard shortcut?](/help/general/keyboard-save).
- This isn't on iPhone or Android.

## If something goes wrong

- **No labels appear:** check that the keyboard is connected and you're on a main screen. Update and reopen the app. You can always tap the items instead.
- **Still stuck:** use the Help Center's private support link, and keep contact details out of public reports.
