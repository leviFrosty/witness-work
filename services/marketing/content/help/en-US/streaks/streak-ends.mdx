title: When does my streak end, and how do I keep it?
category: streaks
---

Your streak ends when a planned day passes with no time logged. You can still log a planned day until the end of the next day.

## Keep it going

- **You went out but forgot to log it:** add the time on that day and your streak comes back.
- **You can't go out on a planned day:** remove that day's plans ahead of time. On **Schedule**, tap each plan, tap **More** (•••), then **Delete → Delete This Plan**. For a recurring plan, only that date is removed. The day stays planned while any plan is left on it.

## Reminders

- When your streak is about to end, the flame on Home shows the time left. A **Streak Reminder** comes at 6:00 PM on the last day.
- For Publishers, a month can be recorded until the end of the next month. The reminder comes three days before that month is over.
- To turn reminders off, open **Settings → Notifications** and turn off **Streak Reminders**. They need notifications allowed for WitnessWork.
