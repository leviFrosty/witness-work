Territories keeps the territory cards you've checked out on your phone, so you can work them street by street. Record each door, go back to the not-at-homes, keep the do-not-calls, and return the territory when you hand the card back. It's free.

Update WitnessWork if you don't see **Territories**. It's rolling out gradually, so it may not have reached you yet.

## Where to find it

- **The Territories tab** lists the territories you have checked out, the ones shared or handed to you, and the ones you've returned under **Show returned territories**.
- **A Territories card on Home** appears once you have a territory. Tap **See All** to open the tab. To move or remove the card, hold any Home section and tap **Edit Home Screen**.

To start, open the **Territories** tab and tap **Add Territory**. See [How do I add a territory I've checked out?](/help/territories/territories-add)

## Find a territory in the list

- Tap the chips at the top to narrow the list: **Overdue**, **Not at Homes**, or **Streets Left**. **All** clears them.
- Tap **Sort & Filter** to sort by date checked out, return date, number, or streets worked. Your choice stays on this phone.
- Touch and hold a territory or street for shortcuts, such as **Open Map**, **Directions**, **Share**, and **Return Territory**.

## Good to know

- Territories works on iPhone, iPad, Android, and the web.
- Sharing, guest links, and handing off a street are only in the phone apps. Sharing with buddies and handing off need Buddies. A guest link doesn't.
- Scanning the card's QR code, photographing the card, and finding streets in an outline are also phone-only.
