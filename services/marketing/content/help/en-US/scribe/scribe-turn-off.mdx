id: scribeTurnOff
title: Can I turn off Scribe AI?
category: scribe
minAppVersion: "1.46.0"
---

Yes. Turn it off and Scribe AI disappears from the app, and nothing is sent to it.

## Turn it off

1. Open **Settings → Scribe AI**.
2. Turn off **Use Scribe AI**.

To turn it back on, use the same switch.

## Good to know

- The microphone in Quick Actions and **Speak an update** in the **+** menu are hidden. On the web, the chat button and **Type an update** are hidden.
- Turning it off doesn't delete your conversations or Scribe AI's memory. To clear the memory first, use **Settings → Scribe AI → Memory**.
- The switch follows you to your other devices when sync is on.

## The first time you use it

Scribe AI explains what it does and asks you to agree to WitnessWork's [Terms of Use](/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy). If you're not ready, swipe the sheet away. Scribe AI stays available and asks again next time. To read about how it handles your data later, open **Settings → Scribe AI → Zero Data Retention** and tap **Learn more**.
