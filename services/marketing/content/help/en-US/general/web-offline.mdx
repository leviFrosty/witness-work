---
id: webOffline
title: Does WitnessWork work offline on the web?
category: general
platforms: [web]
---

Yes, the way a document editor does. WitnessWork on the web saves the app in your browser, so it can open without a connection. Edits are saved in your browser first and sync through Cloud Sync when the connection comes back.

When you're offline, a banner says: "You're offline. Reconnect to sync your changes. They're saved on this device until then." Keep working. Syncing, Scribe AI, and map search and directions need a connection and wait until you're back online.

Changes that haven't synced exist only in this browser. They're lost if the browser's data is erased, for example when you sign out, clear site data, or Safari erases a site's storage. See [What does WitnessWork keep in my browser?](/help/general/web-storage), including Safari's 7-day rule. If you work offline for long, reconnect to let your changes sync before you sign out or switch computers.
