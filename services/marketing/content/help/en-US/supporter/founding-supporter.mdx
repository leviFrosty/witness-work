category: supporter
---

Founding Supporters were already supporting WitnessWork before the Supporter tier launched in May 2026, when support came with no extras. The label is our thank-you. It can't be bought or earned now.

## Good to know

- Founding Supporters get the same features as every other Supporter.
- While your support is active, **Founding Supporter** appears on your profile and in Settings.
- If your support lapses, the label is hidden. It comes back if you support again with the same store account.

## If you expected to see it and don't

1. Open **Settings → Become a supporter**.
2. Tap **Restore Purchases**.

If it still doesn't appear, use the support button at the top of the screen to open **Still need help?** and contact support privately.
