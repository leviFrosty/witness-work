---
id: appInfo
title: Where do I find my app version and account ID?
category: general
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Tap your profile picture, then Settings, and open About → App Info. The top shows the WitnessWork version and build. Below it are the commit, the installed update and its channel, your device and operating system, and the version of the WitnessWork server. On the web there is no update row.

Under Account, Account ID is your WitnessWork account, and Purchases ID is the ID that the App Store or Google Play purchase is tied to. Account ID shows No account yet until this device has signed in to its WitnessWork account. Tap any row to copy its value, or tap Copy All Details to copy everything at once, for example to paste into an email to support. Copying doesn't send anything; you choose where to paste it. Share these IDs only in private email to support, never in a public bug report or screenshot.

If the server version says Unavailable, the device couldn't reach the WitnessWork server; check your connection and open App Info again.
