title: What's on a Plan's page, and how do I change a Plan?
category: plans
---

A Plan's page shows its date, time, length, Type, how often it repeats, the place, and the note. To change it, tap **Edit** (the pencil).

## Open a Plan

1. Tap a day on the **Schedule** tab, then tap one of its Plans. Or tap a Plan on Home.

A Plan with no set time shows **Anytime**. If it runs past midnight, the page shows the next day's date too. Tap the place to open it in Maps.

## Change a Plan

1. Open the Plan and tap **Edit**.
2. Make your changes and save.
3. For a recurring Plan, choose whether the change is for only this date, this and future dates, or all dates.

## Use the menu

Tap **More (•••)** to see **Duplicate**, **Log as Time**, **Open in Maps**, **Copy Address**, **Copy Note**, and **Delete**. Duplicate opens a new Plan with the same details. A one-time Plan's copy starts on the same day next week, and you can change the date before you save.

## Good to know

- If you log hours, today's Plan has a **Timer** row. Tap **Start timer**, then **Pause timer** or **Resume timer** as you need. Save or reset the time from the Timer on Home.
- With Buddies, the page lists who you invited and how each answered. Tap **Invite Buddies** to invite someone while the Plan is still ahead. They get the date, time, title, place, and note. To take someone off, use **Edit**.
- For a Plan you joined through a buddy's invitation, the page shows who organized it. You can switch between **Going** and **Can't Make It** until the Plan ends. **Can't Make It** takes it off your schedule, and **Going** puts it back.
- After a Plan ends, you can still open it for a month, with its note and photos, but you can't change your answer.
