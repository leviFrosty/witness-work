---
id: timezoneShift
title: Will my entries, plans, or reminders move if I travel to another time zone?
category: time
related:
  - 309
  - 261
---

Entries and plans stay on their day. As of the April 2026 update, every time entry and plan is anchored to its calendar date independent of time zone, so traveling or crossing a daylight-saving boundary won't move them to a different day. A Plan's time stays on the clock too: a 10:00 AM Plan reads 10:00 AM on the day clocks change. Older entries created before that update are migrated automatically the first time you open the new version. When you open WitnessWork after changing time zones, Home, Schedule, and Progress switch to the new zone's today, and reminders set for a time of day, such as a Plan's reminder or the reminders to log time, plan next week, or submit your report, move to that same time where you are. Follow-ups keep their exact moment, so they show at the matching local time. If you ever spot an entry on the wrong day, please send a bug report and we'll take a look.
