title: Nshani ukubwelela ku ng'anda apo takwali muntu?
---

Isuleni territory elyo mutale pe pi **Not at Home**. Ilemba umusebo uliwonse ukwakwata ing'anda sha kwesha nakabili.

## Ukubwelela kuli ena

1. Isuleni territory elyo musange **Not at Home**.
2. Tinikeni umusebo ukuti mumone fye Not at Home yaiko.
3. Lembeni ing'anda iliyonse nakabili ne cilembelo ca miseko.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ukulemba ing'anda nakabili pa bushiku bumbi kulundako ukwesha. Umulongo ulanga "Tried 2 times".
- Ing'anda iliyonse ilanga inshita yalembwa ukulekelesha, kanshi kuti mwesha pa bushiku nelyo inshita imbi. Ukumona inshita abantu baikala sana, moneni [Nshani ukumona inshiku ne nshita isawama ukusanga abantu mu ng'anda?](/help/territories/territories-insights)
- Pa musebo uwalembwamo ing'anda shingi, cinjeni pa **Fyonse** na **Not at Home** pamulu pa mulongo.
- Ing'anda mwalembele nga **Nalanshanya** nelyo **Tamutandalile** ifuma mu mulongo.
- Ilyo territory privacy mode yaishiba, takuli ifyalembwa fya ng'anda ifyasungwa, kanshi takuli mulongo. Moneni [Territory privacy mode ni nshi?](/help/territories/territories-privacy-mode)
