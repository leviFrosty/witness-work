id: buddiesReconnect
title: What does "asked to reconnect" mean in Buddies?
category: buddies
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

A buddy who changes their account key moves Buddies to a new, secure connection, and your notifications show "Anna asked to reconnect". So does Buddies, on Anna's row and screen. Tap Reconnect, and you're buddies again once their phone confirms, with the nickname, color, and sharing settings you had for them. Nothing else to do.

What carries over and what doesn't: shared Plans and Follow-ups, requests to join, and badge reactions with that buddy start fresh. Your own service records, contacts, and Plans stay as they are.

If you don't reconnect within about a day, the old connection ends and they no longer appear in Buddies. It can end sooner, but the request stays in your notifications. You can still tap Reconnect while the invitation lasts (7 days); after that, ask them for a new invite. If Reconnect says the invite isn't available, it was already used or expired: ask them to send a new one from Buddies.

A request comes only from a current buddy, through your existing encrypted connection. Before you reconnect, WitnessWork checks with its server that the new connection is the one their account moved to. If it can't check yet, you'll see "This request can't be checked yet": try again later. If more than one request arrives, Reconnect uses the one the server confirms. Dismissing the request ends your old connection with that buddy.

On an older version of WitnessWork, you won't see the request; the buddy simply leaves, and you can pair again with a new invite.

If you changed your own account key, your buddies get this invitation automatically. Each one who reconnects shows up in Buddies again; the others show as "Waiting for … to reconnect" until they do or the invitation expires. Now and then an invitation can't be delivered. That buddy then leaves Buddies without one, and you can pair again with a new invite.
