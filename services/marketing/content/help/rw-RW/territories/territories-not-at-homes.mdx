title: "Nasubira nte ku mazu itari mu rugo?"
---

Fungura agace urebe munsi ya **Abatari mu rugo**. Hatondekanya buri muhanda ufite amazu yo kongera kugerageza.

## Subirayo

1. Fungura agace ushake **Abatari mu rugo**.
2. Kanda umuhanda urebe gusa abatari mu rugo bo kuri wo.
3. Ongera wandike buri nzu ukoresheje aho wandikira inzu.

## Byiza kumenya

- Kongera kwandika inzu ku munsi wundi byongeraho inshuro. Urutonde rwerekana "Wagerageje inshuro 2".
- Buri nzu igaragaza igihe yaherukaga kwandikwa, ushobora rero kugerageza ku munsi cyangwa isaha bitandukanye. Kugira ngo umenye igihe abantu bakunze kuba mu rugo, reba [Nabona nte iminsi n'amasaha meza yo gusanga abantu mu rugo?](/help/territories/territories-insights)
- Ku muhanda ufite amazu menshi yanditswe, hindura hagati ya **Byose** na **Abatari mu rugo** hejuru y'urutonde.
- Inzu wanditse nka **Twaganiriye** cyangwa **Ntibasurwe** ive ku rutonde.
- Iyo uburyo bw'ibanga bw'ifasi buri bukora, nta nyandiko z'inzu zibikwa, bityo nta rutonde. Reba [Uburyo bw'ibanga bw'ifasi ni iki?](/help/territories/territories-privacy-mode)
