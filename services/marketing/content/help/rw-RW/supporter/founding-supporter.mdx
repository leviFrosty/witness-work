title: "Founding Supporter ni iki?"
---

Abafasha b'ibanze bari basanzwe bafasha WitnessWork mbere y'uko urwego rw'Umufasha rutangira muri Gicurasi 2026, igihe inkunga nta bindi yazanaga. Iri zina ni ishimwe ryacu. Ntiryagurwa cyangwa ngo ribonwe ubu.

## Byiza kumenya

- Founding Supporters babona ibiranga bimwe n'ibya buri Mufasha wese.
- Igihe inkunga yawe ikora, **Founding Supporter** igaragara mu mwirondoro wawe no mu Igenamiterere.
- Niba inkunga yawe irangiye, iri zina rirahishwa. Rigaruka niba wongeye gufasha ukoresheje konti imwe y'ububiko.

## Niba wari witeze kuribona ntuliboneho

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Ba umufasha**.
2. Kanda **Garura ibyigeze kugurwa**.

Niba bikibura, koresha akabuto k'ubufasha kari hejuru ya ecran kugira ngo ufungure **Uracyakeneye ubufasha?** uvugane n'ubufasha mu ibanga.
