id: territoriesHandOff
title: How do I hand off a street to a buddy, and get it back?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
platforms: [ios, android]
---

Touch and hold the street and tap Hand Off, or open the territory, tap Share, then Hand Off a Street. Pick the street and the buddy, and tap Hand Off. The buddy works that street on their own, and you see their progress.

You need Buddies set up, a connection, and to be the territory's owner. Then:

1. Pick the street under Which Street, and the buddy under Hand Off To.
2. Tap Hand Off to (their name) and confirm.
3. The street stays in your list, marked as handed off, and its page says "Waiting for (name) to accept" until they do. The buddy gets it in the notification bell on Home, along with a notification. If they aren't on the territory already, they tap Accept. It then shows in their Territories tab under Handed to Me.

**While it's handed off:** the street is read-only for you and anyone you shared the territory with, and your guest links don't reach it. You see the buddy's doors as they record them, and when they mark it worked, your list shows "Worked by (name)". The buddy can share their street with their own buddies or make a guest link for it, but can't hand it off again.

**Getting it back:** open the street and tap Take Back. It leaves the buddy's list and comes back to yours, and the doors they recorded stay on it. The buddy can also tap Give Back on their side. An invitation nobody accepts expires after 7 days, and the street comes back to you. Returning the territory brings every handed-off street back.
