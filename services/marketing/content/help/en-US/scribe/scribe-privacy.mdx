---
id: scribePrivacy
title: What does Scribe AI send, and what’s kept?
category: scribe
---

Your voice is turned into text on your device; recordings are never saved or uploaded. When you send a message, Scribe AI sends the conversation’s text and only the details it needs to understand it, such as today’s date, your goal, your upcoming plans and your contacts’ names, plus anything it looks up while answering. It goes over an encrypted connection through OpenRouter to a model host under a Zero Data Retention arrangement, so it isn’t stored or used for training. It carries a code made from your account that can’t be traced back to you, so the host’s safety checks apply only to you. WitnessWork’s server doesn’t keep your messages; it records only how much you used. Your conversations stay on this device and aren’t synced or backed up: it keeps your 50 most recent conversations, each for 90 days after you last used it. The records you add sync like any others. Photos of paper pages are resized on your device, with their location and other details removed, and read the same way; the app deletes its copy of each page once it’s read or you skip it, or when you close Scribe AI or start a new conversation. Your original photos stay in your library. With data protection mode on, Scribe AI doesn’t send your contact list or visit notes. Find the OpenRouter ZDR policy in Settings → About.
