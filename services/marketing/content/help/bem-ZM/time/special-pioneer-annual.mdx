---
id: specialPioneerAnnual
title: Ndi painiya wa pa lwakwe, mulandu nshi app ilanga Annual Goal?
---

Ba Painiya ba pa Lwakwe tabakwata Annual Goal ukufuma ku ntanshi. Ukuishimya, isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Profile** elyo mubike **Annual Goal** pa **No**.

Ico mwapanga mu mweshi cishala, elyo akakwena ka mwaka mu **Year** pa tab ya **Ukukula** kafisama.
