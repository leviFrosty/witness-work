---
id: territoriesSync
title: Are my territories synced and backed up?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Yes. Your territories, with their streets, door results, not-at-homes, do-not-calls, and returned territories, sync through the sync option your device uses (Cloud Sync, iCloud, or Google Drive on Android; see Settings → Sync). They're also included in WitnessWork Backup and in backup files, so they arrive on a new phone the same way your contacts do.

The photo of the card stays on the device where you added it; on your other devices, add the photo again if you want it there.

Changes and deletions sync too: a territory you delete on one device is deleted on all of them. A device still on a version before 1.47.0 doesn't show territories, but it keeps them safe for your other devices.

Territories you share, with buddies or with a guest link, stay shared on each of your iPhones, iPads, and Android devices that sync with this one, with what others record on them. Territories shared or handed to you appear on each of your devices that have Buddies set up.

With territory privacy mode on, no house-by-house records are kept, so none are synced or backed up either. Turning it on also clears those records from your other devices as they sync.
