id: streaks
title: Urukurikirane rukora rute?
---

Urukurikirane rwawe rugaragaza uko ukomeza gukurikiza gahunda ushyiraho. Ku bapayiniya no ku bandi banditse amasaha, buri munsi wari ufite gahunda wandikaho igihe icyo ari cyo cyose wongeraho umwe, nubwo wakwandika igihe kiri munsi y'icyo wateganyije. Iminsi idafite gahunda ntiyongera ku rukurikirane kandi ntiyarurangiza, bityo tegura iminsi ushaka kujya kubwiriza. Ku babwiriza, buri kwezi ugira uruhare mu murimo wongeraho kimwe, waba ugushyize ku Ahabanza cyangwa wandika igihe. Urukurikirane rwawe rugaragara ku mutwe wa Ahabanza no ku Mwirondoro wawe iyo rugeze kuri 3. Iyo kwandika umunsi bigejeje urukurikirane ku ntambwe nka 3, 5 cyangwa 10, hakinwa ibyishimo bigufi; indi minsi, no kwiyongera kuza mu bundi buryo, nko mu gihe cyanditswe kuri watch yawe cyangwa ku kindi gikoresho, bituma urumuri ruri kuri Ahabanza rwaka cyane. Iyo rwenda kurangira, rukande kuri Ahabanza kugira ngo wandike umunsi usigaye. Kugira ngo uhishe urukurikirane rwawe, zimya Erekana urukurikirane muri Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Inkurikirane n'Ibimenyetso.
