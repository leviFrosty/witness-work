Yes, by typing. Scribe AI on the web shows what it understood as cards, and nothing is added until you tap **Add**.

## Use Scribe AI on the web

1. Sign in to WitnessWork on the web.
2. On **Home**, tap the chat button in Quick Actions. Or tap **+** and choose **Type an update**.
3. Type what you did or plan to do, like "Two hours this morning, and I talked with John at 42 Oak St, follow up next Tuesday."
4. Tap **Send**.
5. Uncheck anything you don't want, then tap **Add**.

Undo works the same as on your phone.

## Good to know

- Scribe AI does the same things as on your phone ([What can Scribe AI do?](/help/scribe/scribe-what-it-does)) with the same limits ([How do Scribe AI limits work?](/help/scribe/scribe-usage)).
- Speaking, scanning notes and reading photos of paper pages aren't on the web. Use the app on your phone for those.
- The first time, Scribe AI asks you to agree to WitnessWork's [Terms of Use](/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
- Your conversations and Scribe AI's memory are saved only in that browser. They aren't synced or backed up, and signing out erases them. See [What does Scribe AI send, and what's kept?](/help/scribe/scribe-privacy).

## If something goes wrong

- **You're offline:** Scribe AI says so. You can still add time, contacts and plans yourself. See [Does Scribe AI work offline?](/help/scribe/scribe-offline).
- **It says to update WitnessWork:** reload the page.
- **You've reached your limit:** it tells you when you can use it again.
- **Still stuck:** email support from **Still need help?** in the Help Center. Describe what happened without sharing personal details.
