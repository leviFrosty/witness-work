title: Where are the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and open-source licenses?
category: general
---

Tap your profile picture, then Settings, and scroll to About. It links to the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and WitnessWork's source code. Where Scribe AI is available, it also links to the OpenRouter ZDR policy that covers what you send to Scribe AI. Open Source Licenses lists every open-source package WitnessWork is built with, along with its version, license, and author; tap a package to open its page, which shows the full license and source. Search by package name, license, or author. Long-press any link to copy or share it.
