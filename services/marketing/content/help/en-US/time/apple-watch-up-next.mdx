**Up Next** shows whichever comes first: your next follow-up or your next plan. Plans appear only if you log hours.

## Add it to your watch

1. Touch and hold your watch face and tap **Edit**. Or use the Watch app on your iPhone.
2. Add the **Up Next** complication to your watch face or Smart Stack.

You can also open it from the watch app's home screen.

## Use it

1. Tap **Up Next**.
2. Tap **Directions** to get directions in Maps on your watch.
3. For a plan, tap **Start Timer** to start your iPhone's service timer. Your iPhone needs to be nearby.

## Good to know

- A follow-up or plan stays up for 15 minutes after it starts, so you can check the address or topic. Then the next one takes its place.
- A plan with no start time shows all day, after that day's timed items.
- Up Next looks two weeks ahead.
- Names and topics are hidden while your wrist is down or your watch is locked, because others may see your watch face.
- Missed follow-ups don't appear. Find them in WitnessWork on your iPhone.

## If something goes wrong

- **It's out of date:** open WitnessWork on your iPhone, then on your watch.
- **Still stuck:** use the Help Center's private support link, and keep householder details out of public reports.
