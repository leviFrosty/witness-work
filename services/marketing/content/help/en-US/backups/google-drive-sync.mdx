Google Drive Sync is in beta on Android. From version 1.47.0 it's included for everyone; earlier versions need Supporter access. On each Android phone or tablet, open Settings → Device Sync, choose Google Drive, and turn on Sync with Google Drive, then choose the same Google Account in Google's window and allow WitnessWork to keep its data in your Google Drive. It syncs your service records, plans, contacts, visits, categories, mileage, profile, and shared preferences; photos have a separate Include images in sync switch. Each device has its own switch, and settings like data protection mode, analytics consent, and onboarding stay on each device. While WitnessWork is open, changes from your other devices arrive within about a minute, and otherwise the next time you open it; tap Sync now to check right away. On a new Android device, choose From Google Drive on the “Pick up where you left off” setup screen to restore your data, which also brings your Supporter status over once that Google Account is connected. If a device connects a different Google Account, sync turns off there before anything is read or sent, and your records stay on the device. Google Drive Sync syncs only with your other Android devices that use it. To sync between an iPhone and an Android device, use Cloud Sync in Settings → Device Sync instead (see “What is Cloud Sync?”). To move your records to another phone, use Cloud Sync, Backup in Settings → Account, or a backup file from Settings → Backup & Restore.
