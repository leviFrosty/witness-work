title: "Nakoresha nte urufunguzo rwanjye rwo kugarura kuri telefoni nshya?"
---

Shyiraho WitnessWork kuri telefoni nshya wandike urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura. Rukora hagati ya iPhone na Android.

Niba ugifite telefoni ishaje, biroroshye gukoresha **Shyiraho ikindi gikoresho**. Reba [Nimukira nte kuri telefoni nshya cyangwa nkahindura hagati ya iPhone na Android?](/help/account/account-handoff).

## Koresha urufunguzo rwawe kuri telefoni

1. Shyiraho WitnessWork kuri telefoni nshya.
2. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Koresha urufunguzo rwo kugarura**.
3. Andika urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura. Inyuguti nkuru, ahantu hasa n'imirongo ntibibuza. WitnessWork ikubwira niba inyuguti imwe itari yo.

## Rukoreshe muri mushakisha

1. Fungura app.witnesswork.app.
2. Hitamo **Koresha urufunguzo rwo kugarura** kuri ecran yo kwinjira.
3. Tsinda igenzura ry'umutekano, hanyuma wandike urufunguzo rwawe.

Mushakisha yinjira muri konti isanzweho. Ntishobora gukora indi ikoresheje urufunguzo. Niba ufite passkey, akenshi biroroshye.

## Byiza kumenya

- Niba telefoni yarakoresheje Buddies ifite indi konti, WitnessWork irabanza ikakubaza. Amakuru ya Buddies y'iyo konti akurwa kuri iyi telefoni.
- Urufunguzo rugarura konti yawe na Buddies. Niba konti yawe ifite backup, WitnessWork ihita igusaba kugarura amakuru yawe. Naho ubundi Cloud Sync iyazana (telefoni nshya itangirana na yo ifunguye), cyangwa ukoreshe **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura**.
- Konti yakorewe kuri murandasi ikora ityo, ikoresheje urufunguzo wabitse icyo gihe.
- Kuri iPhone na iPad, ibindi bikoresho bya Apple biri kuri iCloud Keychain imwe na byo bihindukira kuri iyi konti.
