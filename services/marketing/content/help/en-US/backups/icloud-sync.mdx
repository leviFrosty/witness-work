Yes. On iPhone and iPad, iCloud Sync keeps your records the same on your devices that use the same iCloud account. It's free. [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync) is another option that also works on Android and the web.

## Turn it on

Do this on each iPhone or iPad:

1. Open **Settings → Sync**.
2. Choose iCloud.
3. Use the same iCloud account on every device.

It syncs your service records, contacts, visits and preferences. Photos have their own **Include images in sync** switch.

## Good to know

- A device syncs with one option at a time, and only with your devices on the same option. Choose the same one on each. See [How do I switch between Cloud Sync and iCloud or Google Drive?](/help/backups/cloud-sync-switch).
- Without an internet connection, the status says **Waiting for connection**. Your changes are saved and sync when you're back online.
- If a device signs in to a different Apple Account, iCloud Sync turns off there and your records stay on the device. Turn sync back on to choose how to combine them with that account's data.
- On Android, use [Google Drive Sync](/help/backups/google-drive-sync) between Android devices.
- Calendar Sync is separate. It adds your Follow-ups to a calendar and doesn't sync your records.
- To move to a new phone, see [How do I move to a new phone or switch between iPhone and Android?](/help/account/account-handoff).

If syncing stops, see [My devices are not syncing](/help/backups/sync-troubleshooting).
