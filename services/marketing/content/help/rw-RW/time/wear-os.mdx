id: wearOs
title: "Hari porogaramu ya Wear OS?"
---

Yego. WitnessWork ifite porogaramu ya Wear OS ku telefoni ya Android ihujwe n'isaha ifite Wear OS 3 cyangwa irenga.

## Yishyireho

1. Shyira WitnessWork kuri isaha yawe ukoresheje Play Store. Cyangwa ufungure urupapuro rwa WitnessWork muri Play Store kuri telefoni yawe uyishyire ku isaha uhereyo.
2. Fungura WitnessWork kuri telefoni yawe rimwe, kugira ngo isaha yawe ibone aho ugeze.
3. Emera amamenyesha isaha yawe igihe ibibajije, kugira ngo igihe kibarwa gikora kigaragare aho.

## Icyo ushobora gukora

- Reba amasaha y'uku kwezi n'niba uri imbere cyangwa inyuma ya gahunda zawe.
- Ongeraho igihe cy'uyu munsi, ufite Ubwoko ushobora kongeraho.
- Tangiza kandi uhagarike by'agateganyo igihe kibarwa cya telefoni yawe. Mu gihe gikora, kigaragara ku isura y'isaha yawe gifite **Hagarika by'agateganyo** na **Hagarika**. **Hagarika** yongeraho iminota yose y'igihe kibarwa nk'igihe cyanditswe cy'uyu munsi.
- Andika ko wabwirije muri uku kwezi, niba utandika amasaha nk'Umubwiriza.
- Ongeraho complications za **Iterambere ry'ukwezi** na **Ibikurikira** ku isura y'isaha, na tile ya Iterambere ry'ukwezi kugira ngo utangize cyangwa uhagarike igihe kibarwa cyangwa wongereho igihe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Telefoni yawe ibika inyandiko zawe. Isaha ntishobora guhindura cyangwa gusiba ibyanditswe.
- Isaha ntiyakira inyandiko zawe cyangwa amakuru y'abantu. Kuri Ibikurikira yakira amazina, aderesi n'insanganyamatsiko bya Gusubirayo kwawe n'imitwe n'aho gahunda zawe ziri mu byumweru bibiri biri imbere.
- Kubera ko abandi bashobora kubona isaha yawe, Ibikurikira yerekana "Gusubirayo" mu mwanya w'izina ku isura z'isaha. Kiyifungure kugira ngo ubone uwo ari we.
- Wear OS ntishobora kongeraho igihe cyangwa kwandika ingendo mu ijwi. Koresha tile cyangwa porogaramu ahubwo.
- Porogaramu ikeneye telefoni yawe ifite WitnessWork yashyizweho.
- Niba isaha ivuga **Guhuza na telefoni**, reba [Kuki isaha yanjye ya Wear OS ivuga Guhuza na telefoni?](/help/time/wear-os-sync)
