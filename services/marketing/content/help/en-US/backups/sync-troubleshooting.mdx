Start by opening **Settings → Sync**. Devices sync only with your devices on the same option (Cloud Sync, iCloud or Google Drive), so choose the same one on each. Then tap **Sync now**.

## Check these

1. Your internet connection, on both devices.
2. Every device has the latest WitnessWork. An older version or an unreadable file shows in the status.
3. For iCloud on iPhone or iPad: use the same Apple Account, turn on iCloud Drive, and turn on WitnessWork under iCloud on each device.
4. For Cloud Sync: keep the app open on both devices.
5. On Android, see [Google Drive Sync isn't working](/help/backups/google-drive-sync-troubleshooting).

## If something goes wrong

- **It says Cloud Sync is paused:** nothing is wrong on your side. Your changes wait on this device and sync when it's back.
- **The status stays on Uploading to iCloud:** it changes once Apple confirms the upload. **Last synced** counts only confirmed uploads.
- **Your iCloud storage is full:** your changes stay on this device. Free up space or upgrade in **iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud**, and uploading resumes.
- **It asks for Supporter access:** update WitnessWork. Syncing is free.
- **A photo is missing:** see [Why are contact photos missing on my other device?](/help/backups/sync-photos).
- **Still incomplete:** keep a saved backup file and the device with your latest records. **Rebuild iCloud data** replaces the iCloud data with this device's records. Your other devices switch to them the next time they sync, replacing changes made on them before then. Update WitnessWork on every device first. Or use the private support option in the Help Center, and keep householder details out of public reports.
