title: What does Google Drive Sync store, and who can see it?
category: backups
platforms:
  - android
---

Your WitnessWork data is kept in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. Only WitnessWork can open that folder, and it can't see anything else in your Drive. Your data goes straight between your phone and Google, never through WitnessWork's servers.

## Good to know

- The folder doesn't show in the Drive app or website, and other apps can't read it.
- Google stores it like your other Drive files. It counts against your Google storage.
- WitnessWork doesn't keep your email address.
- Your backup files from **Settings → Backup & Restore** are separate. They stay wherever you saved them.

## Stop using Google Drive Sync

- **On one phone:** open **Settings → Sync** and disconnect Google Drive. Your other devices keep using the folder.
- **On every device:** turn off sync on each one. Then, in Google Drive on the web, open **Settings → Manage apps → WitnessWork → Options → Delete hidden app data**.
- **Remove access completely:** remove WitnessWork under Third-party apps & services in your Google Account.
