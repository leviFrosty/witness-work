---
id: webTabs
title: Why does WitnessWork say it's open in another tab?
category: general
platforms: [web]
---

WitnessWork on the web works in one browser tab at a time, so two tabs can't overwrite each other. A second tab says **WitnessWork Is Open in Another Tab**.

## Keep working in this tab

1. Choose **Use Here**.
2. WitnessWork finishes saving in the other tab, then opens here.

## Go back to the other tab

Switch to the other tab and choose **Use Here** there.

## Good to know

- Closing the tab you're using frees it. The next tab you open or reload opens normally.
- This only applies to one browser. Other browsers and your phone aren't affected. They stay in sync through Cloud Sync.
