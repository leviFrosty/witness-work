Turn off Play sounds in settings. This quiets celebration chimes and reminders while WitnessWork is open.

## Turn off sounds

1. Open **Settings → Features → Audio & Haptics**.
2. Turn off **Play sounds**.

## Good to know

- Reminders still appear, just without sound. On Android, they show as a short message in the app.
- Reminders that arrive while the app is closed follow your phone's notification settings.
- This also quiets the sounds your pet's rewards make. To change only those, use **Settings → Features → Rewards**.
