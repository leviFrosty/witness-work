Tap your profile picture, then **Settings → What's New** for a summary of recent changes.

## Good to know

- Big updates also open a short tour the first time you open the app after updating.
- If you choose **Later**, the bell keeps a shortcut to the tour.
- **What's New** has a button to take the tour again.
