title: Nshi nshila ya kwishiba passkeys shandi, elyo ukufumyapo imo?
---

Passkey imo imo ya WitnessWork ishitwa "WitnessWork" na code ya mashiwi 4, nga WitnessWork 7K4Q. Code imo fye iboneka mu **Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti → Passkeys**, kanshi kuti mwashilingantika.

## Moneni passkeys shenu

Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti → Passkeys**. Ilanga passkey imo imo na cibombelo cayongelwapo, inshita yayongelwa, ne nshita yabomfiwa umuku wa kulekelesha. Imo imo ilangwa:

- **Can unlock your account:** ukwingila na yo kuleta akaunti yenu pa browser nelyo foni ipya.
- **Sign-in only:** ilamwingisha, elyo ikumwipusha ukupwisha na foni yenu imbi nelyo icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha. Pa numa, kuti yacita ukwisula umuku wakonkapo.

Pa foni, tinikeni passkey ya **Sign-in only** elyo musale **Let It Unlock** ukwesha nakabili. Ama password managers yamo yengacita fye ukwingila.

## Fumyeni passkey

1. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti → Passkeys**.
2. Tinikeni passkey.
3. Saleni **Fumya**.
4. Tinikisheni na Face ID, ukunwe nelyo passcode. Pa web, tinikisheni na imo ya passkeys shenu.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ukuifumyapo kuno takuifumyapo mu password manager yenu. Ifumyeni uko nakabili.
- WitnessWork ilamwebo pa ntanshi ya kufumyapo passkey ya kulekelesha iingaisula akaunti yenu.
- Tamwingacinja ishina lya passkey.
- Nga kengele pa Home ilanda ukuti passkey yaongelwa elyo tamwacita, ifumyeni elyo mucinje akaunti key yenu. Moneni [Nshi nshingacita nga foni nelyo kompyuta yandi yaloba nelyo yaibwa?](/help/account/lost-device).
- Pa ntanshi ya kufumyapo inshila yenu imo fye, moneni ukuti mukwata icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha. Moneni [Icisokoshi candi ca kubwesha ni nshi, elyo nshi nshila ya kucisunga bwino?](/help/account/account-recovery-key).
