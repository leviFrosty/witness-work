Yes. WitnessWork has a Wear OS app for an Android phone paired with a watch on Wear OS 3 or later.

## Install it

1. Install WitnessWork from the Play Store on your watch. Or open WitnessWork's Play Store page on your phone and install it on the watch from there.
2. Open WitnessWork on your phone once, so your watch gets your progress.
3. Allow notifications when your watch asks, so a running timer can show there.

## What you can do

- See this month's hours and whether you're ahead of or behind your plans.
- Add time for today, with an optional Type.
- Start and pause your phone's timer. While it runs, it shows on your watch face with **Pause** and **Stop**. **Stop** adds the timer's whole minutes as an entry for today.
- Mark that you shared in the ministry this month, if you don't log hours as a Publisher.
- Add the **Monthly Progress** and **Up Next** complications to a watch face, and the Monthly Progress tile to start or pause your timer or add time.

## Good to know

- Your phone keeps your records. The watch can't edit or delete entries.
- The watch never receives your notes or contact details. For Up Next it gets the names, addresses and topics of your follow-ups and the titles and places of your plans for the next two weeks.
- Because others may see your watch, Up Next shows "Follow Up" instead of a name on watch faces. Open it to see who it is.
- Wear OS can't add time or log trips by voice. Use the tile or the app instead.
- The app needs your phone with WitnessWork installed.
- If the watch says **Syncing with phone**, see [Why does my Wear OS watch say Syncing with phone?](/help/time/wear-os-sync)
