title: "Ku paji ya mugenzi hariho iki, kandi nahindura izina cyangwa ibara rye?"
---

Paji ya mugenzi yerekana iminsi ye iri imbere na Gahunda musangiye. Kugira ngo uyifungure, kanda **Buddies**, hanyuma ukande mugenzi. Kanda ifoto ye kugira ngo uyibone nini.

## Ibiri ku paji

- **Bikeneye igisubizo cyawe:** ubutumire bwe utarasubiza. Hitamo **Ndagenda** cyangwa **Sinshobora**.
- **Hamwe:** Gahunda n'ibyo gusubirayo biri imbere musangiye, hamwe na buri gisubizo. Kanda Gahunda kugira ngo uyifungure.
- **Ibyumweru bibiri bikurikira:** iminsi ateganya kujya mu murimo, iruhande y'iyawe. Kanda umwe kugira ngo ushyireho igihe kimwe cyangwa usabe kwifatanya.
- **Tumira muri Gahunda:** itangiza Gahunda nshya mugenzi uyu yamaze gutumirwa.
- **View on Calendar:** ifungura **Gahunda**.
- Itungo rye, niba arisangiza kandi igice cy'itungo ryawe kiri Ahabanza. Reba [Nabona amatungo ya bagenzi banjye n'ibyumba byayo?](/help/pet/pet-buddy-rooms).

## Hindura izina rya mugenzi cyangwa ibara rye

1. Kanda ikaramu hejuru ya paji ye (**Edit Buddy**).
2. Hindura izina ry'irihimbano cyangwa ibara.

Izina ry'irihimbano ni iry'ibanga. Mugenzi wawe ntabimenyeshwa, kandi kurikuraho bigarura izina rye bwite. Abafasha bashobora guhitamo ibara iryo ariryo ryose; **Default** igarura iryari risanzwe. Amazina n'amabara wahisemo agaruka niba wimukiye kuri telefoni nshya.

**Gusangiza n'imenyesha** nabyo biri munsi ya **Edit Buddy**.
