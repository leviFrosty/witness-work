id: wearOs
title: Hari porogaramu ya Wear OS?
---

Yego, kuri telefoni ya Android ifite isaha ikoresha Wear OS 3 cyangwa irenzeho. Shyira WitnessWork kuva muri Play Store ku isaha yawe, cyangwa kuva ku rupapuro rwa WitnessWork muri Play Store kuri telefoni yawe, hanyuma ufungure WitnessWork kuri telefoni yawe rimwe kugira ngo isaha yawe ibone iterambere ryawe. Ku isaha ushobora kongeraho igihe cy'uyu munsi hamwe n'Ubwoko (si ngombwa), gutangiza no guhagarika by'agateganyo igihe kibarwa cya telefoni yawe, no kubika igihe cyacyo nk'inyandiko. Niba utandika amasaha nk'Umubwiriza w'Ubwami, ushobora kwerekana ko wasangije abandi ubutumwa bwiza muri uku kwezi. Ongeraho complication Iterambere ry'ukwezi na Ibikurikira ku isura y'isaha, na tile ya Iterambere ry'ukwezi kugira ngo utangize cyangwa uhagarike igihe kibarwa cyangwa wongereho igihe ukoresheje gukurura urutoki. Igihe kibarwa gikora, kigaragara ku isura y'isaha gifite Hagarika by'agateganyo na Hagarika; Hagarika yongeraho iminota yose y'igihe kibarwa nk'inyandiko y'uyu munsi. Emera amamenyesha isaha yawe iyabajije, kugira ngo igihe kibarwa gikora gishobore kuhagaragara. Telefoni yawe ni yo ibika inyandiko zawe. Isaha yakira iterambere ryawe ry'ukwezi, imimerere ya raporo, igihe kibarwa, Ubwoko, amazina y'imodoka n'igipimo cy'intera, naho kuri Ibikurikira yakira izina, aderesi n'ingingo bya gusubirayo kwawe, n'umutwe n'ahantu bya gahunda zawe z'ibyumweru bibiri biri imbere. Ntiyakira inyandiko cyangwa andi makuru y'abantu, kandi ntishobora guhindura cyangwa gusiba inyandiko. Kubera ko abandi bashobora kubona isaha yawe, Ibikurikira bigaragaza "Gusubirayo" aho kuba izina ku misura y'isaha, no muri porogaramu iyo ukuboko kwawe kuri hasi; fungura kugira ngo urebe uwo ari we. Wear OS ntishobora kongeraho igihe cyangwa kwandika ingendo mu majwi, bityo koresha tile cyangwa porogaramu aho. Porogaramu y'isaha ikenera telefoni yawe ifite WitnessWork yashyizweho.
