title: Bushe kuti nalundapo ifyebo ku calembwa ca nshita?
---

ee. ilyo imwe lundapo nelyo lungika icalembwa ca nshita, kuti mwa attach akashiwi (e.g. "cart witnessing" nelyo "cut cipi"). kuti mwa na kabili tag ifyalembwa na amalupange efyo similar ifyalembwa stay grouped mu inshita yenu malipoti.
