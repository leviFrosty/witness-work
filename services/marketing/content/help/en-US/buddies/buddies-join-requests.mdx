title: How do I answer or turn off requests to join my Plans?
category: buddies
---

When a buddy asks to join one of your Plans, their request appears in the notification bell on Home. Tap Invite to add them to your Plan and send the invitation right away; the button turns into a check. Tap the request itself to open the Plan, where Invite works the same way. Tap Not Now (or dismiss it) to clear it quietly: your buddy is never told, and the request lapses when the Plan starts. If the Plan came from someone else's invitation, only its organizer can invite. Inviting someone to one date of a recurring plan turns that date into its own one-time Plan, and the recurring plan stays the same on your other days. If your Plans that day changed since they asked, open the Plan you're keeping to invite them. To stop alerts for every request, turn off Ask to Join Alerts in Buddies Settings. To stop alerts from one buddy, open their page, tap Sharing & Alerts, and turn off Ask to Join Alerts, or long-press their request and choose Turn Off Alerts. With alerts off for everyone, you can turn them on there for one buddy. Requests still appear in the bell either way. Not Now and these settings apply to this device only, so a request you cleared on one device can still show on another.
