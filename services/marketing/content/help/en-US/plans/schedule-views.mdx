id: scheduleViews
title: How do I plan across months or see my whole service year?
category: plans
---

The **Schedule** tab has two views. Use the buttons at the top to switch between **Month** and **Year**.

## Month view

Your weeks appear as one calendar that keeps scrolling past the end of a month, so you can plan this month and next side by side.

- The month you're reading stays bright. The others fade back.
- The card above the calendar shows that month's goal, with logged and planned time (striped), and whether you're ahead or behind. Tap it for more.
- Each day shows the time you planned, or the time you logged once you log some.
- Tap a day to see or change it. Touch and hold it to add time or plan it.

## Year view

1. Tap **Year** to see the twelve months of the service year, September through August.
2. Tap a month, or pinch outward, to zoom into it. Pinch inward to zoom back out.
3. Use the arrows or swipe to change years. Tap **Today** to come back.

The card above shows your logged and planned time against your yearly goal, if you have one. A labeled line marks where one service year ends and the next begins. Tap it to see that year.

## Good to know

- Both views only show what's already in your records. Changing views shares nothing.
