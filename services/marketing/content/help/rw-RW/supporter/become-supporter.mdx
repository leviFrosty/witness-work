---

Ifatabuguzi ry'Umufasha rikora rigufasha kubona ibintu bike by'inyongera nk'ishimwe. Ibyingenzi byose biguma ari ubuntu.

## Ibyo Abafasha babona

- Ikimenyetso cy'Umufasha.
- Amabara yihariye.
- Gutegura Inzira y'uyu munsi, ishyira ibyo gusubirayo na gahunda by'umunsi mu rutonde rw'inzira ngufi yo gutwara.
- Ibishushanyo bisimburana bya porogaramu kuri iPhone na iPad.
- Guhitamo ibara rya buri mugenzi.
- Imipaka ihanitse cyane ya Scribe AI. Reba [Imipaka ya Scribe AI ikora ite?](/help/scribe/scribe-usage).

## Byiza kumenya

- Kuyungurura Aderesi, kuzitondeka, kubika uburyo bwo kureba na Buddies biguma ari ubuntu kuri bose.
- Impano imwe (Tip) itanga gushimirwa nk'utanga impano, ariko ntifungura ibiranga Umufasha.
- Murakoze kuri bose bafasha WitnessWork. Inkunga yanyu yubatse Cloud Sync na WitnessWork kuri murandasi.
