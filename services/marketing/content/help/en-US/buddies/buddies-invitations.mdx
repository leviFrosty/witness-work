title: How do I invite a buddy to a Plan or Follow-up, and answer an invitation?
category: buddies
---

Invite a buddy when you add or edit a Plan or Follow-up, or tap **Invite to a Plan** on their page. Your buddy answers **Going** or **Can't Make It**.

## Invite a buddy

1. Open the Plan or scheduled Follow-up, and tap **Invite Buddies**.
2. Choose your confirmed buddies.
3. Save.

On an existing Plan or Follow-up, **Invite Buddies** sends the invitation right away.

## Answer an invitation

1. Open the notification bell on Home, or the buddy's page.
2. Tap the invitation.
3. Choose **Going** or **Can't Make It**.

You can change your answer later. An invitation stays in the bell until you answer it, it is canceled, or the visit has happened.

## Good to know

- A Plan invitation shares its date, time, length, title, place and note, with any photos in the note. Check these before you invite.
- A Follow-up invitation shares the date, time, the householder's first name, the address or pin, and the topic. These disappear a day after the visit. Your buddy doesn't get the contact or visit history.
- Follow-up invitations aren't available in Data Protection Mode.
- **Going** adds a linked Plan to your schedule. It follows the organizer's changes. See [Why can't I change the date or time of a Plan a buddy invited me to?](/help/buddies/buddies-linked-plan-edit).
- Under **Who's Going**, everyone invited sees who is going.
- **Can't Make It** takes it off your schedule. Choose **Going** on the Plan's page to put it back.

## If something goes wrong

- **Not sent** beside a buddy: they stopped being your buddy before the invitation arrived. It stays until the Plan has happened.
