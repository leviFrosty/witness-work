title: "Nakoresha WitnessWork muri mushakisha?"
---

Yego. Fungura [app.witnesswork.app](https://app.witnesswork.app) muri mushakisha igezweho kuri mudasobwa, telefoni cyangwa tablet. Ni WitnessWork yuzuye, ikoresha konti imwe na telefoni zawe, bityo igihe cyawe, gahunda, Aderesi, inyandiko na Buddies biba biriho umaze kwinjira.

## Injira

Hitamo kimwe muri ibi kuri ecran yo kwinjira:

1. Passkey.
2. Telefoni yawe. Reba [kwinjira kuri mudasobwa ukoresheje telefoni yawe](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer).
3. Urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura.

Kugira ngo utangire bundi bushya, hitamo **Create an Account**. Reba [Nakora konti kuri murandasi?](/help/account/web-sign-up).

Umaze kwinjira, WitnessWork ibanza kuzana amakuru yawe. Akazi kawe kabikwa muri mushakisha yawe, kagahuzwa binyuze kuri Cloud Sync, kandi gakomeza gukora nta murandasi. Reba [gukora nta murandasi kuri murandasi](/help/general/web-offline). Kugira ngo uyifungure nka porogaramu, reba [kuyishyiraho](/help/general/web-install).

## Ibyo porogaramu ya murandasi idafite

- Amamenyesha n'inyibutsa by'igikoresho. Gahunda n'ibyo gusubirayo washyizeho kuri murandasi bikwibutsa kuri telefoni zawe. Inzogera y'Ahabanza ikora kuri murandasi.
- Widgets, Live Activities, Siri na Shortcuts, porogaramu z'amasaha n'ibishushanyo bisimburana bya porogaramu.
- iCloud Sync na Google Drive Sync. Murandasi ikoresha Cloud Sync gusa. Niba telefoni zawe zikoresha ikindi, murandasi igusaba kubanza gufungura [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync).
- Guhuza kalendari.
- Gutanga amajwi n'amafoto muri Scribe AI. Uyandikira.
- Amafoto y'umwirondoro n'ay'abantu. Aderesi zigaragaza inyuguti zibanza. Amafoto mu nyandiko arakora. Reba [dosiye n'amafoto kuri murandasi](/help/general/web-files).

## Byiza kumenya

- Ikarita irakora, kandi ibyo usohora na backup bikururwa nka dosiye.
- Porogaramu ya murandasi iva ku rubuga rwa WitnessWork buri gihe uyifunguye. Niba udashaka gukoresha mushakisha, WitnessWork ikora neza kuri telefoni yawe yonyine.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ntiyashoboye gutegura konti yawe:** reba murandasi yawe uhitemo **Ongera ugerageze**, cyangwa usohoke.
- **Handitse ko Cloud Sync yahagaze, cyangwa ikindi gikoresho cyabitse amakuru mushakisha itarasoma:** amakuru yawe ntacyo yabaye. Ongera upakire paji nyuma, hanyuma uhitemo **Ongera ugerageze**.
