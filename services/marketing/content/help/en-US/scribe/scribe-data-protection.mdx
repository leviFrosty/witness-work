---
id: scribeDataProtection
title: Can Scribe AI add contacts in data protection mode?
category: scribe
minAppVersion: "1.46.1"
---

Yes. Scribe AI can add a new contact when data protection mode is on, but the householder must agree to you keeping their details first.

## Add a contact

1. Make sure data protection mode is on in **Settings → Privacy**.
2. Tell Scribe AI about the person as usual, such as "I talked with Daniel at 14 Birch Lane, he wants me to come back Thursday."
3. On the contact's card, ask the householder if you may keep their details. The info button beside the switch shows what to say.
4. Once they agree, turn on the switch that says they agreed.
5. Tap **Add**.

**Add** stays off until the switch is on. If they would rather you didn't keep their details, uncheck that card and add the rest.

## Good to know

- The contact is saved with the date and time they agreed. To see or withdraw that agreement, edit the contact.
- If the name matches someone you already have, the card asks which person you mean.
- In this mode, Scribe AI doesn't use your contact list or visit notes, and it doesn't log Not at Home visits, since nobody was there to agree.
