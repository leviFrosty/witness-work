title: How do I manage or cancel a subscription I bought on the web?
category: supporter
platforms:
  - web
---

On WitnessWork on the web, open **Settings → Manage subscription** and choose **Manage**. Your subscription page opens in a new tab. There you can cancel, change your payment method, and see your receipts.

Every receipt email also links to the same page, so you can manage a subscription from your email without signing in to WitnessWork.

- **Cancelling stops the next renewal.** Your Supporter access continues until the end of the period you've paid for.
- **A Tip is one-time.** There's nothing to cancel, and you won't be billed again.
- **A subscription from the App Store or Google Play.** Manage opens that store's subscriptions page instead, where you cancel it (or pause it, on Google Play).
- **Pausing isn't available for web subscriptions.** Pause is a Google Play feature. You can cancel and subscribe again whenever you like.
- **The phone app can't manage a web subscription.** Use the web, or the link in your receipt email.
- **If you move to a country where the web doesn't sell,** renewals and card changes with a card or billing address from there are declined. Subscribe in the App Store or Google Play instead.
- **Manage subscription isn't in Settings.** Your Supporter access isn't a subscription you can change here, for example a complimentary grant.

To ask for money back, see [Can I get a refund for a web purchase?](/help/supporter/web-purchase-refund). If you can't find your subscription, email [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) from the address you used at checkout.
