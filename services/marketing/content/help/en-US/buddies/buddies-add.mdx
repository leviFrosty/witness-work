title: How do I add a buddy, and who confirms the request?
category: buddies
---

Open Buddies from Schedule, or tap Invite Buddies on a new Plan before you have a buddy, and tap Invite a Buddy or the + button to share a Buddies Invite. To pair in person, use the code button to show your Buddies Code and have the other person scan it inside WitnessWork. You can also paste a link through Enter Invite Link. The recipient taps Accept; the person who created the invite then opens Buddies → Requests and taps Confirm. You start seeing each other's plans only after that confirmation. Confirm only the person you intended to invite.
