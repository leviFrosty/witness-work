title: "Bigenda bite iyo mvuye ku modoka isangiwe, ngakurwaho, cyangwa gusangiza bigahagarara?"
---

Ushobora kuva ku modoka isangiwe igihe icyo ari cyo cyose. Ugumana kopi y'imodoka n'ingendo zayo mu modoka zawe, nk'uko yari igihe uheruka guhuza.

## Va ku modoka

1. Fungura **Ibirometero**, hanyuma **Igenamiterere ry'ibirometero** (uruziga).
2. Kanda imodoka.
3. Munsi ya **People**, kanda **Leave Car…**.

Kuva bikeneye murandasi. Niba nta murandasi ufite, ongera ugerageze uhuye.

## Wakuweho, cyangwa gusangiza biragahagarara

Nyirayo gusa, uwasangije imodoka, ashobora gukoresha **Remove from Car…** cyangwa **Stop Sharing…**. Muri ibyo byombi, no iyo uvuyeho, ugumana kopi y'imodoka n'ingendo abandi banditse. WitnessWork iragukubwira, kandi ntiwongera kubona impinduka nshya ku modoka isangiwe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Iyo nyirayo avuyeho, uwifatanyije bwa mbere aba nyirayo.
- Iyo abandi bose bavuyeho, imodoka iba imodoka yawe bwite nanone.
- **Siba amakuru yose y'ibirometero…** isiba imodoka zawe bwite, lisansi n'ingendo, ikava ku modoka zose zisangiwe, ikagumana kopi ya buri imwe. Niba itarashoboye kuva ku modoka, urugero igihe nta murandasi ufite, ibindi birasibwa kandi irabikubwira. Ongera ugerageze uhuye.
- **Siba amakuru yanjye ya Buddies** mu igenamiterere rya Buddies nayo ibanza kuva ku modoka zose zisangiwe, kandi ntirangira igihe imodoka itarashoboye kuvwamo.
- Gukuraho mugenzi ntibimukura ku modoka, ariko bivanaho ubutumire bwawe bufunguye kuri we.
