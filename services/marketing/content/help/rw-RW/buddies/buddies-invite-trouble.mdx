title: "Kuki ubutumire bwanjye bwa Buddies buri gutegereza, bwarangiye cyangwa buvuga ko imyanya yose iri gukoreshwa?"
---

Ubutumire butegereza kugeza ubwemeje, bukora rimwe, kandi burarangira nyuma y'iminsi irindwi. Niba ubwawe budakora, kora ubundi bushya.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Butegereje:** fungura **Buddies → Ibisabwa** ukande **Emeza**.
- **Bwakoreshejwe, bwakuweho cyangwa bwarangiye:** saba ihuza ry'ubutumire rishya.
- **Imyanya yawe yose iri gukoreshwa:** ushobora kugira bagenzi 50, ubutumire butegereje n'ibisabwa hamwe. Kuraho ubutumire butakoreshejwe cyangwa ukureho mugenzi kugira ngo ubone umwanya.
- **Gukora ubutumire byananiranye:** kanda **Ongera ugerageze**. Niba ubwa mbere bwagiyeho, WitnessWork burakwereka aho gukora ubundi. Buri ku rutonde rw'ubutumire bwawe butegereje, aho ushobora kububusangiza cyangwa kububukuraho.
- **Ihuza rifungura paji ya murandasi:** shyiraho WitnessWork uyikuye kuri App Store cyangwa Google Play, hanyuma ukande ihuza nanone. Cyangwa kopora ihuza, ufungure **Buddies**, ukande **Andika ihuza ry'ubutumire** ukarihambire.
- **Ntushobora gusikana kode:** emerera WitnessWork gukoresha kamera mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe, cyangwa uhambire ihuza ry'ubutumire.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ushobora kugira amahuza y'ubutumire agera kuri atanu adakoreshejwe afunguye icyarimwe.
