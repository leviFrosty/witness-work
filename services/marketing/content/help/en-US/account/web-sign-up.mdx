---
id: webSignUp
title: Can I create a WitnessWork account on the web without the phone app?
category: account
platforms: [web]
---

Not yet. For now, you create your account in the WitnessWork app on your phone, then sign in on the web.

1. Install WitnessWork on your iPhone or Android phone.
2. Turn on [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync) there (Settings → Device Sync), so your records reach the web. New installs start with it on.
3. Open [app.witnesswork.app](https://app.witnesswork.app) and sign in with your phone ([How do I sign in on a computer with my phone?](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer)), a passkey, or your recovery key.

You need no email address, phone number, or password. Keep your recovery key private: whoever has it can sign in to your account, and no one at WitnessWork can reset it for you ([What is my recovery key?](/help/account/account-recovery-key)).
