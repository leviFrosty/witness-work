Buddies always see your name and the days you plan to go out over the next eight weeks. Only you and the buddies you choose can read what you share. You choose the rest.

## What buddies can see

- **Always:** your name, and the days you plan to go out with how long.
- **Unless you turn them off:** your photo, pioneer status, streak, badges, pet (including its room), and start times. Badges go without progress, counts or dates.
- **Never:** your recorded service time, reports, goals, contact list, visit history or notes.

## Change what they see

1. Open **Buddies**, tap the menu button, and choose **Buddies Settings**.
2. Change the switches, or tap **Pause sharing**.

To change it for one buddy, see [Can I share different things with different buddies?](/help/buddies/buddies-per-buddy). Buddies aren't told when you change anything.

## Good to know

- Plan and Follow-up invitations share more, such as a Plan's note and photos or a Follow-up's address. Check before you invite.
- When you answer **Going** to a buddy's Plan, everyone invited to it sees your name, and your photo if **Photo** is on, even people who aren't your buddies.
- A car you share in Mileage shares its trips, notes and fuel with everyone on it. See [Are shared cars private, and what do the people on a car see?](/help/mileage/shared-cars-privacy).
- WitnessWork knows you only by an anonymous number, never your name, email or phone number.
- The Privacy Policy is under **Still need help?**, using the support button in the header.
