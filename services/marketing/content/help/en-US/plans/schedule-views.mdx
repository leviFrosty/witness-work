id: scheduleViews
title: How do I plan across months or see my whole service year?
category: plans
---

The Schedule tab has two views; switch between them with the buttons at the top. Month shows your weeks as one calendar you scroll through without stopping at the end of a month, so you can plan this month and next side by side. The month you're reading stays bright and the others fade back, and the card above the calendar shows that month's goal: logged and planned time (striped) against your goal, the time you've logged, and whether you're ahead of or behind your plans. Tap it for goal coverage, schedule pace, and the Assistant. Each day shows the time you planned, or the time you logged once you log some. Tap a day to see or change it, or touch and hold it to add time or plan it. Year shows the twelve months of the service year (September through August), each day colored like the calendar, under the same card for the whole service year: logged and planned time (striped) against your annual goal if you have one, the time you've logged, and whether you're ahead of or behind your plans. Tap a month, or pinch outward, to zoom into it; pinch inward in Month to zoom back out. A labeled line marks where one service year ends and the next begins; tap it to see that year. Use the arrows or swipe to change years, and tap Today to come back. Both views only show what's already in your records; changing views doesn't share anything.
