title: "Mbona nte verisiyo nshya ya WitnessWork kuri murandasi?"
---

Nta kintu ugomba gukurura. Iyo verisiyo nshya iteguye, WitnessWork kuri murandasi yerekana **Update available** hamwe n'akabuto ka **Reload**.

## Bona verisiyo nshya

1. Hitamo **Reload**.
2. Niba warashyizeho porogaramu ya murandasi, iyifunge uyifungure nanone.

## Byiza kumenya

- WitnessWork ntiyongera gupakira ubwayo mu gihe urimo gukora. Niba utegereje, ubutumwa buragaruka usubiye kuri tab.
- Tab imaze igihe kirekire ifunguye ishobora gupakira rimwe ubwayo.
- Gupakira ntibisiba amakuru yawe. Ibitari byahuzwa bihuzwa nyuma.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Handitse Update Required:** ongera upakire paji. Niba bidafashije, funga tab, ufungure app.witnesswork.app nanone, kandi urebe murandasi yawe.
