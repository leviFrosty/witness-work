---
id: iCloudSync
title: Bushe kuti nasync ifyebo fyandi pa fibombelo?
---

Ee. Pa iPhone na iPad, iCloud Sync ilenga ifyo mwalemba ukulingana pa fibombelo fyenu ifibomfya iCloud account imo. Ni ya bwamba. [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync) ni nshila imbi iyabomba na pa Android ne web.

## Ukuwasha

Citeni ici pa iPhone nelyo iPad iliyonse:

1. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Sync**.
2. Saleni iCloud.
3. Bomfyeni iCloud account imo pa fibombelo fyonse.

Isync ifyo mwalemba fya mulimo, abantu ba kulanshanya, ulutandalilo ne fyo mwasala. Ifikope fikwata switch yafyo ya **Ingiya ifikope mu sync**.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Cibombelo cisync na nshila imo pa nshita, elyo fye na fibombelo fyenu ifili pa nshila imo. Saleni imo pa fyonse. Moneni [Nshani ukucinja pa Cloud Sync na iCloud nelyo Google Drive?](/help/backups/cloud-sync-switch).
- Ukwabula intaneti, status ilanda **Cilepembela ukutantika**. Ifyo mwacinja fyasungwa elyo fisync ilyo mwabwela pa intaneti.
- Nga cibombelo cingila mu Apple Account iyimbi, iCloud Sync yashimiwa uko elyo ifyo mwalemba fyashala pa cibombelo. Washeni sync nakabili ukusala ifyo mulunganya ne fyebo fya akaunti iyo.
- Pa Android, bomfyeni [Google Drive Sync](/help/backups/google-drive-sync) pa pali ifibombelo fya Android.
- Calendar Sync ya kulekanya. Ilundako Ukukonkapo kwenu ku kalenda elyo tisync ifyo mwalemba.
- Ukuya ku foni ipya, moneni [Nshani ukuya ku foni ipya nelyo ukucinja pa iPhone na Android?](/help/account/account-handoff).

Nga sync yaleka, moneni [Ifibombelo fyandi tafilesync](/help/backups/sync-troubleshooting).
