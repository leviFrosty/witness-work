title: What's in the bell on Home?
category: general
---

The bell at the top of Home collects reminders and updates, so they wait there instead of piling up on Home when you open the app. You'll find things like last month's Service Report, a pending time rollover, backup and data protection reminders, missed Follow-ups, reminders that already went off, new badges and new items for your pet that you haven't looked at yet, Buddies activity (where Buddies is available), and What's New. From version 1.47.0, a phone's bell can also hold a one-time offer to turn on Cloud Sync, for an install from before sync was free, and a reminder to save a passkey. If you tap Not Now or dismiss it, it can come back the next time you turn on Cloud Sync or Backup or set up another device, at least 30 days later; after two Not Nows it stops. From version 1.47.0, the bell on a phone or in a browser also tells you about a new sign-in, a browser approved, or a passkey added on your account's other devices (a phone renewing its sign-in about every three months isn't one, but a phone on a version before 1.47.0, or one not opened in the month before its sign-in runs out, signs in again and shows up, so check the device's name before you change your account key); tap it to open Signed-In Devices or Passkeys. A new one joins an item of the same kind only while that item is still in the bell, which then shows a count; if there are too many to list, one item opens Account Activity instead ([How do I see which devices use my account?](/help/account/account-activity)). The red badge counts items you haven't seen yet. News from your buddies, like a buddy's new badge or a reaction to yours, sits at the bottom under From your buddies and never adds to that number; a small dot on the bell means some of it is new. Tap an item to act on it, tap X to dismiss it, or tap Clear All. Invitations and requests still waiting for your answer stay until you respond. A dismissed item doesn't come back. Which items you've seen or dismissed is remembered on this device only.
