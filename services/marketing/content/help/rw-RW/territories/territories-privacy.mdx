Yego. Agace utasangije kagumya kuri telefoni yawe no mu guhuza no muri backup byawe, ari byo by'ibanga. Reba [Uduce twanjye turahuzwa kandi tukabikwa?](/help/territories/territories-sync)

## Uduce dusangijwe

Iyo usangije agace, ni wowe n'abo musangiye gusa mushobora kugasoma. Seriveri ya WitnessWork ntishobora gusoma nomero cyangwa izina ry'agace, amazina y'imihanda, aderesi, ibyabaye, "Ntibasurwe", cyangwa izina rya buri wese. Ifoto y'ikarita ntisangizwa na rimwe.

## Imirongo y'abashyitsi

WitnessWork ntibika ikintu na kimwe ku mushyitsi uretse izina yandika, rikaba ibanga ry'agace. Murandasi y'umushyitsi ibika inyandiko z'umurongo kugeza umurongo urangiye cyangwa akanda **Ibagirwa uyu murongo**.

## Ibyo abo musangiye babona

Ibiri byose ku mihanda wabasangije, harimo inzu z'abandi n'izina ry'uwanditse buri imwe, na "Ntibasurwe".

## Bika gusa ibikenewe

- "Ntibasurwe" ibika aderesi n'itariki gusa. Ntukandike izina cyangwa impamvu.
- Scribe AI igarura gusa nomero z'amazu, imihanda, n'ibyabaye biva mu nyandiko zawe. Reba [Scribe AI isoma inyandiko zanjye z'agace?](/help/territories/territories-notes-scribe)
- Ku nyandiko z'inzu zitabaho rwose, fungura uburyo bw'ibanga bw'ifasi. Reba [Uburyo bw'ibanga bw'ifasi ni iki?](/help/territories/territories-privacy-mode)

Kugira ngo ubone Politiki y'ibanga, koresha buto y'ubufasha iri hejuru y'iri kera urebe munsi ya **Ukeneye ubufasha bundi?**
