title: "Ibyabaye muri kalendari birabura cyangwa kuvugurura byahagaze. Nakora iki?"
---

Niba Guhuza kalendari bihagaze, ubutumwa bwa **Kuvugurura kalendari byahagaze** bugaragara mu menyesha ryawe, inzogera iri kuri Ahabanza. Bukande urebe igisubizo. Ushobora kandi gufungura **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Guhuza kalendari** ugasoma ikosa.

## Bikosore

1. Niba WitnessWork ikeneye kugera kuri kalendari, kanda **Fungura Igenamiterere rya iPhone**, ukande **Kalendari**, uhitemo **Kugera kose**. **Ongeraho ibyabaye gusa** ntihagije.
2. Subira kuri WitnessWork. Kuvugurura bikomeza byonyine.
3. Ku bibazo bya murandasi, reba murandasi yawe, kwinjira muri iCloud na konti ya kalendari, hanyuma ukande **Ongera ugerageze**.
4. Niba gutangira bivuga ko nta konti ya kalendari ishobora kubika kalendari ya WitnessWork, fungura iCloud Calendars muri Igenamiterere rya iPhone, hanyuma ukande **Ongera ugerageze**.

Niba utagishaka Gusubirayo muri kalendari yawe, kanda **Zimya** ku butumwa.

## Niba ibyabaye bibura

1. Tegereza konti yawe ya kalendari irangize guhuza.
2. Reba ko wahisemo kalendari imwe ukoresha kuri WitnessWork gusa. Niba yahinduwe izina cyangwa ikavanwaho, uyihitemo bundi bushya.
3. Garura ibyabaye bya WitnessWork wimuriye kuri indi kalendari.
4. Kanda **Garura ibikorwa byabuze**. Niba kopi zigikururwa, ushobora kubona ibisubiramo, bityo banza utegereze.

Gusubirayo kwahise ntikwongerwaho.

## Niba ukoresha iPhone cyangwa iPad irenze imwe

Igikoresho kimwe gusa ni cyo kivugurura kalendari yawe. Icya mbere washyizeho ni cyo kibikora. Kugira ngo uhindure, fungura **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Guhuza kalendari** uhitemo ikindi gikoresho munsi ya **Igikoresho nyamukuru**. Hanyuma ufungure WitnessWork ku gikoresho gishya ugihe uburenganzira bwo kugera kuri kalendari yose.

Niba guhindura bitegereje, fungura WitnessWork ku gikoresho cya mbere kugira ngo kirangize. Gusubirayo uhindurira ku kindi gikoresho bigera kuri kalendari yawe umaze gufungura WitnessWork ku gikuru. Ibi bikeneye guhuza gufunguye muri **Igenamiterere → Guhuza**.

## Niba bikidakora

Koresha uburyo bw'ubufasha bw'ibanga mu Kigo cy'Ubufasha. Shyiramo verisiyo yawe ya WitnessWork, ubwoko bwa telefoni n'ikosa ryagaragaye. Ntukohereze amakuru y'abantu, amafoto y'ekara ya kalendari, inyandiko cyangwa backup mu bitekerezo by'abantu bose.
