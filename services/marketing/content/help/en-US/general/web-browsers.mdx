---
id: webBrowsers
title: Which browsers does WitnessWork on the web work in?
category: general
platforms: [web]
---

WitnessWork on the web is tested in current versions of Chrome, Safari, and Firefox, on computers and on phones and tablets. Keep your browser up to date. Other current browsers, such as Microsoft Edge, may work but aren't part of that testing.

Passkeys, which are the main way to sign in, need a browser and a password manager or device that support them. If sign-in with a passkey isn't offered or fails, choose Use my phone or Use a recovery key on the sign-in screen instead. See [What is a passkey?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).

WitnessWork saves your data in the browser and needs the browser to keep site data. If that data is erased, you sign in again and changes that hadn't synced are lost. See [What does WitnessWork keep in my browser?](/help/general/web-storage).
