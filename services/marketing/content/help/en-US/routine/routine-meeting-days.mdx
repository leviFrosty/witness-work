Set them in **Settings → Features → Spiritual Routine**. This works the same on iPhone and Android.

## Set your meeting days

1. Open **Settings → Features → Spiritual Routine**.
2. Under **Meeting days**, choose your midweek meeting day (Monday to Friday).
3. Choose your weekend meeting day (Saturday or Sunday).

## Good to know

- **Midweek meeting prep** and **Weekend meeting prep** show on Home's Routine card from two days before each meeting through the meeting day. Without meeting days, both show all week until you check them.
- The Plans assistant uses the same days and keeps its suggestions lighter on them. You only set them once.
- If your meeting schedule changes, for example during a circuit overseer's visit, you can check off your prep any day that week from the Spiritual Routine screen.
- Your meeting days are on all your devices if you use sync.
