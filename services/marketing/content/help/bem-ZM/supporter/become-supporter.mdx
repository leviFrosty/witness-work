title: Nshi nshipokelela nga naba Supporter?
---

Ukulipila kwa Supporter ukwabomba kumupeela ifintu fimo ifya kumwebo natotela. Ifintu fyonse fya ntanshi fyashala fya bwamba.

## Ico Supporters bapokelela

- Baji ya Supporter.
- Amabala ya kwisalila.
- Pangeni Inshila ya Lelo, iyabika Ukukonkapo na mapange ya bushiku mu mulongo wa kuendela uwapalamina.
- Ifikope fimbi fya app pa iPhone na iPad.
- Ukusala ibala lya munyina onse.
- Imipaka iingi iya Scribe AI. Moneni [Imipaka ya Scribe AI ibomba shani?](/help/scribe/scribe-usage).

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ifya kusalapo abakulanshanya, ukupangulula, ama views yasungwa na Buddies fyashala fya bwamba kuli bonse.
- Tip imo fye ipeela ukutotelwa ukwa Donor, lelo tayingayaka ifya Supporter.
- Twatotela bonse abafwa WitnessWork. Ukwafwa kwenu kwapanga Cloud Sync na WitnessWork pa web.
