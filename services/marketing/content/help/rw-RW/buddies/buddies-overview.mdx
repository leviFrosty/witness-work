title: WitnessWork Buddies ni iki, kandi kuki ntayibona?
---

Buddies igufasha guhuza n'abantu bagera kuri batanu mujyana kubwiriza no kubonana gahunda zanyu z'iminsi, ibihe byo gutangira, n'igihe bimara mu byumweru umunani biri imbere. Unabona ibimenyetso buri mugenzi asangiza. Ikora kuri iPhone, iPad na Android, kandi bagenzi bari ku mbuga zitandukanye bashobora guhuza. Ku ngengabihe ya Ingengabihe, ifoto cyangwa inyuguti ya mbere ya mugenzi ku munsi bivuze ko muzajyana kubwiriza, naho akadomo gato munsi y'umunsi kavuga ko abandi bagenzi bateganya kujya kubwiriza. Kanda umunsi ubone abo ari bo. Kuri iPhone na iPad, widget ya Kalendari igaragaza ibimenyetso bimwe kandi ivugurura iyo WitnessWork ivuguruye Buddies, bityo fungura porogaramu niba igaragara ko itinze. Ni ubuntu aho iboneka. Fungura Ingengabihe, ukande Buddies hejuru, urangize intangiriro ukoresheje izina ry'umwirondoro wawe. Akadomo kuri iyo buto kavuga ko hari icyifuzo gitegereje ko wemeza. Buddies ishobora kutaboneka mu gihe cyo gutangizwa buhoro buhoro. Vugurura porogaramu washyizeho niba buto ya Buddies ibura. Niba buto yijimye, Buddies ntiboneka by'agateganyo; bagenzi wabitse bagumaho ku ngengabihe yawe, bityo ongera ugerageze nyuma.
