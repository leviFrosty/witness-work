Yes. You can add your own fields, such as **Publication**, to the conversation form.

## Add a field

1. Open **Settings → Features → Conversation Fields**. Or tap **Add Custom Fields** on the conversation form.
2. Add a field and give it a name of up to 14 characters.

The field now appears on the form for conversations and **Not at Home** visits. Leave it empty when it doesn't apply. What you enter shows on that visit in the contact's history.

## Good to know

- When you tap a field, things you've entered before appear as suggestions. Tap one to fill it in.
- Under each field, **Earlier visits** lists what you entered on that contact's past visits, so you can choose something new.
- You can rename and reorder fields any time without losing what you entered.
- Archiving a field hides it but keeps its values. Deleting it from the Archived list removes the field and its values for good.
- Fields stay on your phone, are included in backups, and sync when sync is on. They aren't included when you share a contact.
- With data protection mode on, don't record religion, health, family situation, language, or ethnicity.

## If something goes wrong

- **A field is missing from the form:** it may have been archived. Check the Archived list.
