title: "Menyesha nte ikosa cyangwa nsabe ikintu gishya?"
---

Koresha utubuto turi hasi kugira ngo umenyeshe ikosa cyangwa usabe ikintu gishya.

## Ibyo gushyiramo

- Intambwe zigeza ku kibazo
- Ibyo wari witeze ko biba
- Telefoni yawe na verisiyo ya sisitemu yayo
- Verisiyo ya WitnessWork, igaragara muri **Igenamiterere → Ibyerekeye → App Info**

## Byiza kumenya

- Kuraho amakuru y'ibanga y'abantu, inyandiko, indangamuntu za konti, Buddies Codes n'amahuza y'ubutumire muri raporo rusange n'amafoto ya ecran.
- Ku bibazo bya backup cyangwa ibindi by'ibanga, koresha ihuza ry'imeri y'ibanga riri munsi ya **Uracyakeneye ubufasha?**
- Politiki y'ibanga, Amabwiriza yo gukoresha n'inkomoko biri muri **Igenamiterere → Ibyerekeye**.
