---

Fungura paji y'ifatabuguzi ryawe uvuye muri WitnessWork kuri murandasi cyangwa mu imeri y'inyemezabwishyu. Aho ushobora guhagarika, guhindura uburyo bwo kwishyura no kubona inyemezabwishyu zawe.

## Fungura paji y'ifatabuguzi ryawe

1. Muri WitnessWork kuri murandasi, fungura **Igenamiterere → Gucunga ifatabuguzi**.
2. Hitamo **Manage**. Paji y'ifatabuguzi ryawe ifunguka mu tab nshya.
3. Niba ibaza imeri yawe, andika iyo wakoresheje igihe wishyuraga.
4. Fungura ihuza ryo kwinjira ibohereza.

Buri imeri y'inyemezabwishyu kandi ifite ihuza rigana kuri iyi paji, bityo ushobora kuyikoresha utinjiye muri WitnessWork.

## Byiza kumenya

- Guhagarika bihagarika kongera ifatabuguzi gukurikira. Uburenganzira bwawe bw'Umufasha bukomeza kugeza ku iherezo ry'igihe wishyuye.
- Tip ni rimwe. Nta kintu cyo guhagarika, kandi ntuzongera kwishyuzwa.
- Guhagarika by'igihe ntibiboneka ku mafatabuguzi yo kuri murandasi. Ushobora guhagarika ukongera kwiyandikisha igihe ushatse.
- Porogaramu ya telefoni ntishobora gucunga ifatabuguzi ryo kuri murandasi. Koresha murandasi, cyangwa ihuza riri mu imeri y'inyemezabwishyu.
- Niba waraguze kuri App Store cyangwa Google Play, **Manage** ifungura paji y'amafatabuguzi y'ubwo bubiko.
- Niba wimukiye mu gihugu aho murandasi itagurisha, kuvugurura no guhindura ikarita bikoresheje ikarita cyangwa aderesi y'ubwishyu ivuye aho birangwa. Ahubwo iyandikishe kuri App Store cyangwa Google Play.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta huza ryo kwinjira:** reba ko wanditse imeri wakoresheje igihe wishyuraga, urebe no mu bubiko bw'ubutumwa budakenewe.
- **Nta Gucunga ifatabuguzi mu Igenamiterere:** uburenganzira bwawe bw'Umufasha si ifatabuguzi ushobora guhindurira aho, urugero impano yatanzwe ku buntu.
- **Ntushobora kubona ifatabuguzi ryawe:** andikira [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) uvuye kuri aderesi wakoresheje igihe wishyuraga.

Kugira ngo usabe gusubizwa amafaranga, reba [Nshobora gusubizwa amafaranga y'ibyo naguze kuri murandasi?](/help/supporter/web-purchase-refund).
