Handing off gives one street to a buddy to work on their own. You can still see their progress, and you can take the street back.

## Hand off a street

You need Buddies set up, an internet connection, and to be the territory's owner.

1. Touch and hold the street and tap **Hand Off**. Or open the territory, tap **Share**, then **Hand Off a Street**.
2. Pick the street under **Which Street**, and your buddy under **Hand Off To**.
3. Tap **Hand Off to** your buddy's name and confirm.

The street stays in your list, marked as handed off, until your buddy accepts. They get a notification and an invitation in the bell on Home. They tap **Accept**, and the street appears under **Handed to Me** in their Territories tab.

## While it's handed off

- The street is read-only for you and anyone you shared the territory with. Your guest links don't reach it.
- You see their doors as they record them. When they mark it worked, your list shows "Worked by" and their name.
- They can share the street with their own buddies, but can't hand it off again.

## Get it back

Open the street and tap **Take Back**. The doors they recorded stay on it. Your buddy can also tap **Give Back**.

If nobody accepts within 7 days, the invitation ends and the street stays with you. Returning the territory brings every handed-off street back.
