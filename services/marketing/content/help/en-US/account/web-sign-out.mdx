id: webSignOut
title: How do I sign out of WitnessWork on the web?
category: account
platforms: [web]
---

Open **Settings → Account** and sign out. WitnessWork first tries for a few seconds to send any waiting changes to Cloud Sync. Then it erases your account and all WitnessWork data from this browser.

## Sign out

1. Check that **Settings → Sync** shows everything synced.
2. Open **Settings → Account**.
3. Choose **Sign Out**.

Changes that hadn't synced are lost.

## Good to know

- Signing out doesn't delete your account or your records from WitnessWork.
- This browser's Cloud Sync copy is kept for 30 days, or until the next browser you sign in to has taken in its records.
- To use this browser again, sign in with your passkey, your phone, or your recovery key. See [How do I sign in on a computer with my phone?](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer). Your records come back.
- Use WitnessWork only in browsers, with no phone on Cloud Sync? Sign in again within 30 days to keep your records, or download a backup before you sign out.
- Sign out whenever you use a computer that isn't yours or that others share.
- You can also sign a browser out from another device. See [How long does a browser stay signed in, and how many can I use?](/help/account/web-sessions).
