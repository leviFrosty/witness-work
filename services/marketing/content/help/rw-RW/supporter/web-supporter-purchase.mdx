title: "Mba nte Umufasha cyangwa nsiga Tip kuri murandasi?"
---

Injira muri WitnessWork kuri murandasi, hanyuma uhitemo ifatabuguzi cyangwa Tip mu igenamiterere. Ibyo waguze byanditse kuri konti yawe ya WitnessWork.

## Ba Umufasha

1. Injira muri WitnessWork kuri murandasi.
2. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Ba umufasha**.
3. Hitamo **Buri kwezi** cyangwa **Buri mwaka**, uhitemo igiciro.
4. Hitamo **Ba Umufasha**.
5. Ku rupapuro rwo kwishyura, andika imeri yawe (y'inyemezabwishyu), amakuru y'ikarita n'aderesi y'ubwishyu.

Iyo ubwishyu bwemewe, usubira muri WitnessWork, yemeza ibyo waguze.

## Subiza Tip

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Ba umufasha**.
2. Hitamo **Ohereza inkunga**, uhitemo umubare, ukohereze.

Tip ni ishimwe rimwe. Ntifungura ibiranga Umufasha.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ibiciro biri mu madolari y'Amerika, hiyongereyeho umusoro aho ugomba.
- Imeri yawe n'aderesi y'ubwishyu bikoreshwa gusa ku nyemezabwishyu, umusoro no gusubizwa amafaranga. WitnessWork ntibibika.
- Amafatabuguzi yongerwa buri kwezi cyangwa buri mwaka kugeza uyahagaritse. Reba [Nacunga nte cyangwa nahagarika ifatabuguzi naguriye kuri murandasi?](/help/supporter/web-manage-subscription).
- Ibyo waguze kandi bigaragara kuri telefoni na tablet zawe zinjiye muri konti imwe. Fungura WitnessWork aho ufite murandasi. Kuri telefoni, ifungure rimwe nyuma yo kuyivugurura.
- Ibyaguzwe kuri murandasi ntibiboneka ahantu hamwe. Reba [Kuki ntashobora kugura kuri murandasi mu gihugu cyangwa intara yanjye?](/help/supporter/web-purchase-unavailable-region).

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Akabuto k'igiciro karategereje, kavuga ko gategura konti yawe:** guma kuri paji ufite murandasi. Niba kadafunguka nyuma y'umunota, reba murandasi yawe wongere upakire paji. Niba rigitegereje, gurira muri porogaramu kuri telefoni yawe, cyangwa wandikire [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app).
- **Ibyo waguze biracyemezwa:** tegereza umunota, hanyuma uhitemo **Retry**. Ntiwongere kugura. Niba bikinanirwa kwemezwa, andikira ubufasha uvuye kuri aderesi wakoresheje igihe wishyuraga.
- **Ivuga ko ibyo waguze byuzuye kandi ko usubira muri WitnessWork:** kwishyura byarangiriye muri mushakisha itinjiye muri konti yawe. Fungura WitnessWork aho winjiye.
- **Ivuga ko itabonye ibyaguzwe hano:** niba umaze kwishyura, fungura WitnessWork winjiye muri konti wakoresheje. Niba ntihagaragara nyuma y'iminota mike, andikira ubufasha uvuye kuri aderesi wakoresheje igihe wishyuraga.
- **Ivuga ko ibyaguzwe biri kuri konti yatangije kwishyura:** fungura WitnessWork winjiye muri iyo konti.
- **Nta biciro cyangwa utubuto two kugura:** reba [Kuki ntashobora kugura kuri murandasi mu gihugu cyangwa intara yanjye?](/help/supporter/web-purchase-unavailable-region).
