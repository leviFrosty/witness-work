Open your pet's details and tap a picture of something you own. The room changes right away and is saved.

## Decorate the room

1. On **Home**, tap your pet's card, but not the pet itself. Tapping the pet pats it.
2. Pick a tab: **Hat**, **Wall**, **Floor**, **Bed**, **Shelf**, **Light**, **Sign**, or **Window**.
3. Tap something you own.

You can also touch and hold the card's name and choose **Hat & Room…**. A line above the pictures says what's in use, like **Wearing: Cap**. On an iPad, tap **Close** to leave.

## Get more items

Some items are open from the start. Buy the others in the Shop with coins.

1. Tap **Shop** beside your coins, or the **More in Shop** card at the end of a tab.
2. Tap an item, then **Buy**.

The item goes in the room right away and stays yours for good. Something you own but haven't picked yet says **New**. Some items are secrets that are never sold. See [What are the secret hats and room items?](/help/pet/pet-secrets) and [How do I earn coins, and what can I buy in the Shop?](/help/pet/pet-shop).

## Window and sign

- **Live window** follows your clock and the season where you are. Turn it off to choose a time of day and a season yourself.
- The sign can show your hours this month, your hours this service year, the months you shared, your next plan, this month's conversations and return visits, or nothing.

## Items that move

Each Shop tab (except **Hat**) also has four items that move: two with a green frame, and two rare ones with a blue frame. They come after the plain items and cost from 620 to 2,800 coins.

- Green items have one moving part, like the Hourglass, Clouds or Tall grass.
- Rare items change the room around them and react when you pat your pet or earn coins. Examples are the Gramophone, Volcano model, Koi pond, Northern lights and Campfire.
- Pick a rare item in the Shop to see it react. **Pat** and **New coins** buttons under its card replay the reaction.
- A wall pattern keeps the wall color you choose.

## Good to know

- To see night effects in the daytime, set the window to **Night** in your pet's details.
- Lights go out when your pet has no hearts. Other moving items don't depend on hearts.
- With Reduce Motion on, they stay still and the **Pat** and **New coins** buttons don't show. They also don't move in shared pictures or the widget. Buddies see your pet and its hat, not your room.

## If something goes wrong

- **An item doesn't move:** check that Reduce Motion is off in your device's settings.
- **You see a different item than you bought:** update WitnessWork on that device.
- **Still wrong:** use **Still need help?** below this answer. Include your WitnessWork version, and leave out names and private notes.
