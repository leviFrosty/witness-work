category: pet
---

Secret hats and room items are never sold. Each one opens up on its own as you keep going out in the ministry and caring for your pet.

Until you find one, the Shop shows only a dark shadow named **???**. Which one opens up when is part of the surprise.

## Good to know

- Coins can't buy them, and none depend on hours, a buddy, your Service Streak, or your plans.
- You can't miss one for good. You can find any of them later.
- A few take well over a year. They count the months you went out at least one day, not months in a row.
- Every tab except **Window** and **Sign** has secrets.
- A new one says **New** in your pet's details. Pick it like any other item.
- When one opens up, a small card slides in at the top of the screen. Tap it to see the item, or swipe it away. If you miss it, the notification bell on Home holds it until you open **Hat & Room**.
- Secrets you've found stay yours and sync with your pet.

## If something goes wrong

- **A secret you expected hasn't shown up:** open your pet's details again after your devices finish syncing.
