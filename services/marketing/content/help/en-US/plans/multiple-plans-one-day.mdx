Yes. A day can have as many plans as you like, one-time or recurring. For example, field service every Friday morning and a Bible study every Friday evening.

## Add another plan

1. Open the **Schedule** tab and tap the day.
2. Tap **+** next to **Plans**.

## Good to know

- Every plan on a day counts toward that day's planned time, the time left to plan, and your projected total.
- To skip a recurring plan for one day, or change it for one day, see [Can I edit or delete a single day of a recurring plan?](/help/plans/edit-recurring-plan)
- Plans stay on your phone and sync to your other devices if you use sync. If you use Buddies, they only see a plan's day, time, and length.

## If something goes wrong

- **A day shows more time than you expect:** tap the day and delete the plan you don't need.
