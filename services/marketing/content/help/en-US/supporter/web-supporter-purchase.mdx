title: How do I become a Supporter or leave a Tip on the web?
category: supporter
platforms:
  - web
---

1. Sign in to WitnessWork on the web with your WitnessWork account. Buying on the web needs it, so your purchase belongs to your account.
2. Open **Settings → Become a supporter**.
3. Choose **Monthly** or **Annual**, pick a price, then choose **Become a Supporter**. To give once instead, choose **Send a tip**, pick an amount, then send it. A Tip is a one-time thank-you and doesn't activate Supporter features.

From version 1.47.0, right after you sign in, WitnessWork syncs your account's data first, so the price button waits until your account is ready to buy. That usually takes a few seconds.

Checkout opens on a secure payment page from RevenueCat, with payments processed by Stripe. It asks for:

- an email address, where your receipt is sent;
- your card details;
- your billing address.

Prices are in US dollars, with taxes included or added where required. Checkout and your receipts show **WitnessWork Supporter (Monthly)**, **WitnessWork Supporter (Annual)** or **WitnessWork Tip**. Your email and billing address are kept in our Stripe and RevenueCat accounts for receipts, tax and refunds. They're never stored on WitnessWork's servers or in the app.

When payment goes through, you return to WitnessWork, which confirms your purchase and thanks you.

Your purchase belongs to your WitnessWork account, so it shows on your phones and tablets signed in to the same account (WitnessWork 1.47.0 or later on the phone; open it once after updating). Open WitnessWork there while it's online, and your Supporter features appear shortly. Subscriptions renew automatically each month or year until you cancel (see [How do I manage or cancel a subscription I bought on the web?](/help/supporter/web-manage-subscription)).

If something doesn't look right:

- **It says it's getting your account ready to buy.** WitnessWork is still syncing your account's data. Stay on the page while you're online, and the button turns on by itself. If it doesn't after a minute:
  1. Check that you're connected to the internet.
  2. Reload the page.
  3. If it still waits, become a Supporter or leave a Tip in the WitnessWork app on your iPhone, iPad or Android phone instead, or email [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app). A purchase in the app shows here too, since it's the same account.
- **It says your purchase is still being confirmed.** Wait a minute, then choose **Retry**. Don't buy again. If it still doesn't confirm, email [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) from the address you used at checkout, and we'll check.
- **It says your purchase is complete and to go back to WitnessWork.** Checkout finished in a browser that isn't signed in to your account. Open WitnessWork where you're signed in to see it.
- **It says it couldn't find a purchase here.** The page couldn't tell what you bought, and your account shows no new purchase. If you just checked out, open WitnessWork signed in to the account you used. If it isn't there after a few minutes, email [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) from the address you used at checkout.
- **It says the purchase is on the account that started checkout.** You started checkout in another browser or signed in to another account. Open WitnessWork signed in to that account to see it.
- **You don't see prices or buy buttons at all.** See [Why can't I buy on the web in my country or province?](/help/supporter/web-purchase-unavailable-region)

Web purchases aren't available in some places, including the EU, the UK, and Manitoba and Saskatchewan in Canada; see [Why can't I buy on the web in my country or province?](/help/supporter/web-purchase-unavailable-region).
