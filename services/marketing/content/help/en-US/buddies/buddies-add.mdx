Open **Schedule**, tap **Buddies**, then tap **Invite a Buddy**. Your buddy accepts, and you confirm, before you see each other's plans.

## Add a buddy

1. Open **Buddies** from **Schedule**.
2. Tap **Invite a Buddy** (or the **+** button) and share the invite.
3. Your buddy opens it and taps **Accept**.
4. Open **Buddies → Requests** and tap **Confirm**.

Together in person? Tap **Show Buddies Code** and have your buddy scan it in WitnessWork. If someone sent you a link, tap **Enter Invite Link** and paste it.

## Good to know

- Confirm only the person you meant to invite.
- You see each other's plans only after you confirm.
- On a new Plan, tap **Invite Buddies** if you don't have a buddy yet.
