Show a QR code in the browser and approve it from your phone. You need WitnessWork on an iPhone or Android phone that's signed in to your account, and the internet on both devices.

## Sign in on a computer

1. On the computer, open app.witnesswork.app and choose **Use my phone**. A QR code appears.
2. On your phone, open **Settings → Account → Sign In on a Computer**.
3. Scan the code inside WitnessWork. Your camera app won't open it.
4. Check that the six-digit code on your phone matches the one in the browser.
5. Tap **YES**, then confirm with Face ID, your fingerprint or your passcode.

Your phone shows **Approved**. The browser signs in and offers to add a passkey so next time you can skip the phone.

## Good to know

- Only approve a computer that's in front of you. If the phone warns the browser seems far away, continue only if it's your own computer.
- If you didn't start this, tap **NO**.
- Only a phone can approve a browser. Without your phone, use a passkey or your recovery key. See [What if I lose access to my WitnessWork account?](/help/account/lost-access).
- A browser showing it was approved by a phone that isn't yours: choose **That Wasn't Me**. See [What should I do if my phone or computer is lost or stolen?](/help/account/lost-device).

## If something goes wrong

- **The phone can't scan:** in your phone's settings, allow WitnessWork to use the camera.
- **The code isn't from WitnessWork, or is for another phone:** on the computer, open app.witnesswork.app and choose **Use my phone** again.
- **The code expired:** codes change about every minute and work once. Choose **Show a New Code** in the browser.
- **The phone says it can't verify this device:** it can't approve a computer. Use a passkey or your recovery key instead.
