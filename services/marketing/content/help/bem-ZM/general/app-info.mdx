title: Bushe ndafwaya kwi ukumona app version ne akaunti ID yandi?
---

Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Pa Mulandu → App Info**. App version yenu yaba pa nshi, elyo akaunti ID yenu yaba munshi ya **Akaunti**.

## Kopololeni ifyo support ifwaya

1. Tinikeni icikope cenu, elyo **Ifyo Mwasala**.
2. Isuleni **Pa Mulandu → App Info**.
3. Tinikeni **Copy All Details**, nelyo tinikeni umulongo uuli onse ukukopolola fye ico.
4. Bikeni mu email ya cisuma kuli support.

## Ifyo ciba kuno

- Version ne build ya WitnessWork, icibombelo cenu ne system version yaco, ne version ya server ya WitnessWork.
- **Account ID** ni akaunti yenu ya WitnessWork. Ilanda **No account yet** mpaka icibombelo ici cingile.
- **Purchases ID** ni ico ukusumina kwenu kwa App Store nelyo Google Play kwaikatilwa.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ukukopolola takutuma icili conse. Imwe e musala apo mwabika.
- Sabankanyeni ama ID aya fye mu email ya cisuma kuli support, te mu lipoti ya bantu bonse nelyo icikope.

## Nga cabipa

- **Version ya server ilanda Unavailable:** icibombelo ici tacafikile kuli WitnessWork. Moneni intaneti yenu elyo mwisule **App Info** nakabili.
