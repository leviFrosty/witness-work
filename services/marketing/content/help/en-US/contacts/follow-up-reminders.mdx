title: Can I keep visit notes without scheduling a return visit or reminder?
category: contacts
related:
  - 480
---

Yes. When you add or edit a visit, turn off **Follow Up** and save. The visit and its notes stay, and nothing is scheduled or shown as overdue.

## Stop a follow-up

1. Open the visit and edit it.
2. Turn off **Follow Up**.
3. Save.

## Good to know

- You can turn **Follow Up** on later and choose a date and time.
- For more about reminders, see [How do follow-up, plan, and contact reminders work?](/help/contacts/reminders).
