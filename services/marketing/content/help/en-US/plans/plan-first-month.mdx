---
id: planFirstMonth
title: Can WitnessWork plan my month for me?
category: plans
---

Yes. During setup, if you log hours and have a monthly goal, you can have WitnessWork spread your goal across the days you usually go out.

## Plan your month during setup

1. Pick the days you usually go out.
2. Look over the plans it suggests. Tap any day to change it with the slider, or plan that day yourself.
3. Tap **Add to my schedule** to save them. Or tap **I'll plan later** to continue without plans.

## Good to know

- It plans this month, or next month if fewer than seven days are left.
- This month's plan starts on the 1st, so days that already passed get plans too. Log the time you already spent on them. Days where you've already logged time are left alone.
- The weekdays you didn't pick become off days, so no plans are suggested on them. Change this in **Settings → Features → Plans → Availability**.
- Plans don't count toward your report. Log the time you actually spend.
- After setup, tap any day on the **Schedule** tab to plan it. The Assistant there suggests plans whenever you're short of your goal.
- Kingdom Publishers who don't log hours, and anyone without a monthly goal, skip this step. They can still add plans from the **Schedule** tab.
