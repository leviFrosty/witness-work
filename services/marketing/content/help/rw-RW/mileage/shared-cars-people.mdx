id: sharedCarsPeople
title: "Ni nde ushobora gukora iki ku modoka isangiwe?"
---

Buri wese uri ku modoka isangiwe ashobora kwandika no guhindura ingendo. Nyirayo gusa, uwasangije imodoka, ashobora gukuraho abantu cyangwa guhagarika gusangiza.

## Reba abari ku modoka

1. Fungura **Ibirometero**, hanyuma **Igenamiterere ry'ibirometero** (uruziga).
2. Kanda imodoka.
3. Reba munsi ya **People**. Herekana buri wese uri ku modoka n'ubutumire bufunguye.

Imodoka ifite abantu bagera ku 10, harimo n'ubutumire bufunguye.

## Buri wese uri ku modoka ashobora

- Kongeraho, guhindura no gusiba ingendo, ibikoresho bya lisansi, lisansi n'ibiciro.
- Guhindura izina ry'imodoka, cyangwa kuyishyira mu bubiko, bikayihisha ingendo nshya kuri bose kandi bigakomeza ingendo zayo muri raporo.
- Gutumira bagenzi be bwite akoresheje **Invite Buddy…**. Abantu bari ku modoka ntibakeneye kuba bagenzi.
- Gukuraho ubutumire yohereje akoresheje **Cancel Invitation**.
- Kuva ku modoka akoresheje **Leave Car…**.

## Nyirayo gusa ashobora

- Gukura umuntu ku modoka akoresheje **Remove from Car…**.
- Kurangiza gusangiza kuri bose akoresheje **Stop Sharing…**.
- Gukuraho ubutumire bw'uwo ari we wese.

Iyo nyirayo avuyeho, uwifatanyije bwa mbere aba nyirayo.

## Byiza kumenya

- Imodoka isangiwe ntishobora gusibwa. Ishyire mu bubiko kuri bose, cyangwa uyive wowe ubwawe. Reba [Bigenda bite iyo mvuye ku modoka isangiwe, ngakurwaho, cyangwa gusangiza bigahagarara?](/help/mileage/shared-cars-leave).
- Imodoka isangiwe ifite lisansi zayo, zakoporowe kuri nyirayo igihe yasangizwaga. Guhindura igiciro cya lisansi yawe ntibihindura lisansi y'imodoka isangiwe, bityo vugurura byombi niba ukoresha byombi.
