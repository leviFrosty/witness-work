If Calendar Sync stops, a **Calendar updates paused** message appears in your notifications, the bell on Home. Tap it to see the fix. You can also open **Settings → Features → Calendar Sync** to read the error.

## Fix it

1. If WitnessWork needs calendar access, tap **Open iPhone Settings**, tap **Calendars**, and choose **Full Access**. **Add Events Only** isn't enough.
2. Return to WitnessWork. Updates continue on their own.
3. For connection problems, check your internet connection, iCloud sign-in and calendar account, then tap **Try Again**.
4. If setup says no calendar account can hold a WitnessWork calendar, turn on iCloud Calendars in iPhone Settings, then tap **Try Again**.

If you no longer want Follow-ups in your calendar, tap **Turn Off** on the message.

## If events are missing

1. Wait for your calendar account to finish syncing.
2. Make sure you chose the same calendar you use only for WitnessWork. If it was renamed or removed, choose it again.
3. Move back any WitnessWork events you moved to another calendar.
4. Tap **Restore missing events**. If copies are still downloading, you may get duplicates, so wait first.

Past Follow-ups aren't added.

## If you use more than one iPhone or iPad

Only one device updates your calendar. The first one you set up does it. To change it, open **Settings → Features → Calendar Sync** and choose another device under **Primary device**. Then open WitnessWork on the new device and give it full calendar access.

If a switch is pending, open WitnessWork on the previous device so it can finish. Follow-ups you edit on another device reach your calendar after you open WitnessWork on the main one. This needs sync turned on in **Settings → Sync**.

## If it still doesn't work

Use the private support option in the Help Center. Include your WitnessWork version, phone model and the error shown. Keep contact details, calendar screenshots, notes and backups out of public reports.
