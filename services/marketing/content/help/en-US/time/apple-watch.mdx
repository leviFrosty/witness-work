Yes. WitnessWork has an Apple Watch app for an iPhone paired with an Apple Watch on watchOS 26 or later.

## Install it

1. If Automatic App Install is on, the app installs by itself. Otherwise, open the Watch app on your iPhone.
2. Find WitnessWork under **Available Apps** and tap **Install**.
3. Open WitnessWork on your iPhone once, so your watch gets your progress.

## What you can do

- See this month's hours and whether you're ahead of or behind your plans.
- Add time for today, with an optional Type.
- Start and pause your iPhone's timer, and save its time as an entry.
- Mark that you shared in the ministry this month, if you don't log hours as a Publisher.
- Add the **Monthly Progress** complication to a watch face to see your hours toward this month's goal, and **Up Next** to see your next follow-up or plan. See [What does Up Next on my Apple Watch show?](/help/time/apple-watch-up-next)
- Use Siri on your watch. See [Can I use Siri or Shortcuts with WitnessWork?](/help/time/siri)

## Good to know

- Your iPhone keeps your records. The watch can't edit or delete entries.
- The watch never receives your notes or contact details. For Up Next it gets the names, addresses and topics of your follow-ups and the titles and places of your plans for the next two weeks.
- The app needs your iPhone, so it isn't available on an Apple Watch set up with Family Setup.
- With an Android phone, use the Wear OS app instead.
- If the watch says **Syncing with iPhone**, see [Why does my Apple Watch say Syncing with iPhone?](/help/time/apple-watch-sync)
