id: cloudSyncDefault
title: Is Cloud Sync on by default?
category: backups
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Yes. A new install of WitnessWork starts with Cloud Sync on, and your records and photos sync between your devices. Only you can read them.

## Keep your records on one device

1. During setup, find the Cloud Sync step.
2. Turn the **Cloud Sync** switch off.

Nothing syncs, and you can turn it on later in **Settings → Sync**.

## If you already used WitnessWork

Your setting doesn't change. A message in your notifications, the bell on Home, may offer **Turn On** or **Turn On Cloud Sync**. Tap it to switch, or dismiss it. It won't come back.

- If you sync with iCloud (Google Drive on Android), that stays until you change it.
- If you turned sync off, it stays off.

## Good to know

- To change it on any device, open **Settings → Sync** and choose Cloud Sync, iCloud (Google Drive on Android), or Off.
- Your devices sync only with devices on the same option, so choose the same one on each.
- WitnessWork on the web uses only Cloud Sync.
- Switching never replaces records, and nothing you deleted comes back.
