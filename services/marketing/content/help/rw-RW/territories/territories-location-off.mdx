title: "Kuki WitnessWork itabika aho inzu ziri?"
---

WitnessWork ibika aho inzu iri gusa iyo ishobora gukoresha aho uri, **Bika aho buri nzu iri** ikaba ifunguye ku muhanda, kandi uri kuri uwo muhanda. Iyo aho uri hatabonetse, ibindi byose bikomeza gukora.

## Bikosore

Shaka kimwe muri ibi bimenyesha aho wandikira inzu cyangwa ku ikarita y'agace:

- **"Aho uri hazimye kuri WitnessWork."** Kanda **Fungura Igenamiterere** wemerere WitnessWork gukoresha aho uri. Kuri Android buto ishobora kuvuga **Emera aho ndi**. Kuri murandasi, emerera uru rubuga gukoresha aho uri mu igenamiterere rya murandasi yawe.
- **"WitnessWork ifite aho uri hagereranyijwe gusa."** Kanda **Fungura Igenamiterere** ufungure aho uri nyakuri.
- **"Aho uri hazimye kuri iki gikoresho."** Kanda buto iri mu menyesha kugira ngo ufungure aho uri kuri telefoni yawe.
- **"Uri kure y'uyu muhanda, bityo nta dukadomo ubu."** Niba koko uri ku nzu, kanda igishushanyo cy'akadomo aho wandikira inzu, hanyuma **Ndi ku nzu**.

## Izindi mpamvu

- **Bika aho buri nzu iri** izimye kuri uyu muhanda. Fungura ako kabuto aho wandikira inzu, cyangwa muri **Hindura umuhanda**.
- Uburyo bw'ibanga bw'ifasi buri bukora. Ntibubika udukadomo tw'inzu. Reba [Uburyo bw'ibanga bw'ifasi ni iki?](/help/territories/territories-privacy-mode)
- Niba nta menyesha rigaragara, telefoni yawe ishobora kuba itabona umurongo aho uri, nko mu nzu.

## Shyiraho akadomo ukoresheje intoki

1. Fungura umuhanda ukande inzu.
2. Kanda **Shyiraho akadomo**.
3. Kanda ku ikarita aho inzu iri.

Ibi bikora nta kumenya aho uri.
