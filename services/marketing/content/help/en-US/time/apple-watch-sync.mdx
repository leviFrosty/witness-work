title: Why does my Apple Watch say Syncing with iPhone?

Your watch shows **Syncing with iPhone** while time you added on the watch is still on its way to your iPhone. It finishes when your iPhone is nearby and you've opened WitnessWork on it.

## What to do

1. Keep your iPhone nearby and unlocked.
2. Open WitnessWork on your iPhone.
3. Open WitnessWork on your watch.

## Good to know

- The watch total includes the new time right away. Credit Time is added once your iPhone saves it, because your iPhone applies your monthly Credit limit.
- Each entry keeps the day you added it, and is saved only once.
- Starting, pausing and stopping the timer need your iPhone nearby.
- After your iPhone restarts, unlock it once before syncing.

## If something goes wrong

- **The watch says the timer changed on your iPhone:** check the time, then try again.
- **The watch says your iPhone couldn't save an entry:** the time was not added. Try again.
- **The watch asks you to update WitnessWork on your iPhone:** update it, then try again.
- **The watch stays out of date, or its complications show a dash:** follow the three steps above.
- **Still stuck:** use the Help Center's private support link, and keep your records out of public reports.
