Yes. A shared car appears on any of your devices that use the same Buddies identity. That's also how it comes back on a new phone.

## Good to know

- Shared cars update on their own for everyone on the car. They aren't part of sync (Cloud Sync, iCloud, or Google Drive), WitnessWork Backup, or backups from **Backup & Restore**.
- Set up another device with your recovery key or **Set Up Another Device**, and it shows your shared cars.
- **Log Trip** with Siri on iPhone and Apple Watch includes shared cars. Siri isn't available on Android, and Wear OS can't log trips.

## If something goes wrong

- **A notice says "A shared car isn't ready on this device yet":** your changes stay on this device and are shared once it is ready.
