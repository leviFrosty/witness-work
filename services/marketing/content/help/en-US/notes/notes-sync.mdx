id: notesSync
title: Why is a photo missing from a note, or a note plain on another device?
category: notes
---

A note says “This photo isn't on this device” when the photo hasn't reached this device yet. Open WitnessWork on the device that added the photo and use Sync now in Settings → Device Sync; the photo appears on your other devices after the next sync.

- **Cloud Sync.** From version 1.47.0, photos sync on their own. If the device that added the photo says “Not synced: storage full”, your account's photo storage is full; see “How much photo storage does Cloud Sync have?”. With an older version, a photo stays on the device that added it.
- **iCloud Sync (Google Drive Sync on Android).** Turn on Include images in sync on both devices.

Restoring a backup file doesn't bring photos back. A device with an older version of WitnessWork shows notes as plain text, without formatting or photos, and editing a note there replaces its formatting everywhere. Update WitnessWork on every device to keep it.
