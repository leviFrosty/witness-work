title: Politiki y'ubuzima bwite, Amabwiriza yo gukoresha, n'impushya z'inkomoko ifunguye biri he?
---

Kanda ifoto y'umwirondoro wawe, hanyuma Igenamiterere, maze umanuke ugere ku Ibyerekeye. Rifite amahuza ya Politiki y'ubuzima bwite, Amabwiriza yo gukoresha, na kode y'inkomoko ya WitnessWork. Ku bikoresho aho Kwinjiza inyandiko biboneka, rifite kandi ihuza rya politiki ya OpenRouter ZDR igenga inyandiko wohereza kuri Scribe AI. Impushya z'inkomoko ifunguye zirondora buri paki y'inkomoko ifunguye WitnessWork yubakishijwe, hamwe na verisiyo yayo, uruhushya n'uwayikoze; kanda paki kugira ngo ufungure urupapuro rwayo, rugaragaza uruhushya rwuzuye n'inkomoko. Shakisha ukoresheje izina rya paki, uruhushya cyangwa uwayikoze. Kanda igihe kirekire ihuza iryo ari ryo ryose kugira ngo ukopore cyangwa risangize.
