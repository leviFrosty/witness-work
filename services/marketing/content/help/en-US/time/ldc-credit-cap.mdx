title: Are LDC and other credit hours included in my totals?
category: time
related:
  - 124
  - 9
  - 98
---

Yes. Credit hours count toward your monthly hour total. For the service-year total, up to 55 credit hours per month are counted, in line with the branch's published cap. Anything above the cap is still kept on the entry but doesn't roll into the annual total.
