---
id: googleDriveSyncTroubleshooting
title: "Google Drive Sync ntikora ku gikoresho cyanjye cya Android. Nareba iki?"
---

Fungura **Igenamiterere → Guhuza** usome uko bihagaze. Akenshi ikubwira icyo gukora.

## Reba ibi

1. Buri gikoresho gikoresha Google Drive kandi gihujwe na Google Account imwe.
2. Google Play services irimo kandi ivuguruye.
3. Telefoni yawe iri kuri murandasi. Impinduka wakoze nta murandasi zoherezwa usubiye kuri murandasi kandi WitnessWork ifunguye.
4. Buri gikoresho gifite WitnessWork nshya cyane. Vugurura ibitayifite.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Uko bihagaze bivuga ko wongera guhuza:** kanda **Ongera uhuze Google Drive** wemere kugera kuri yo bundi bushya. Google irongera ibaze niba wakuyeho uburenganzira muri Google Account yawe.
- **Ububiko bwawe bwa Google bwuzuye:** impinduka zawe zigumya kuri iyi telefoni. Kuraho ibintu cyangwa ugure umwanya, hanyuma ukande **Huza ubu**.
- **Ivuga ko Google Drive ifite akazi kenshi:** Google iri kugabanya ibyifuzo mu gihe runaka. Impinduka zawe zigumya kuri iyi telefoni, kandi guhuza kongera kugerageza kwonyine.
- **Isaba kuba Umufasha:** vugurura WitnessWork. Google Drive Sync ni ubuntu.
- **Biracyadahuza:** bika dosiye ya backup n'telefoni ifite inyandiko zawe nshya, hanyuma ukoreshe uburyo bw'ubufasha bw'ibanga mu Kigo cy'Ubufasha. Ntukohereze amakuru y'abo usura mu bitekerezo by'abantu bose.
