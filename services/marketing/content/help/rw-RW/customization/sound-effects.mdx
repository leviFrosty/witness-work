title: "Nazimya nte amajwi y'ibyishimo n'ay'inyibutsa?"
---

Zimya Vuza amajwi mu igenamiterere. Ibi bicecekesha amajwi y'ibyishimo n'inyibutsa mu gihe WitnessWork ifunguye.

## Zimya amajwi

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Amajwi n'Ibinyeganyega**.
2. Zimya **Vuza amajwi**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Inyibutsa zikomeza kugaragara, nta jwi gusa. Kuri Android, zigaragara nk'ubutumwa bugufi muri porogaramu.
- Inyibutsa ziza porogaramu ifunze zikurikiza igenamiterere ry'amamenyesha rya telefoni yawe.
- Ibi kandi bicecekesha amajwi y'ibihembo by'itungo ryawe. Kugira ngo uhindure ayo gusa, koresha **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Ibihembo**.
