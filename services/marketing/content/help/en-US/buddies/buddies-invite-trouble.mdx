title: Why is my Buddies Invite waiting, expired, or saying all spots are in use?
category: buddies
---

An accepted request waits until the inviter confirms it in Buddies → Requests. Each Buddies Invite works once and expires after seven days; ask for a new link if it was used, cancelled, or expired. Your five spots include buddies and pending invites or requests. Cancel an unused invite or remove a buddy to make room. If making an invite fails because the connection dropped, tap Try Again: if the first invite was made after all, WitnessWork shows that one instead of making another, and it's listed with your pending invites so you can share or cancel it. Only a limited number of unused invite links can be open at once. If tapping an invite link opens a web page instead of WitnessWork, install WitnessWork from the App Store or Google Play and tap the link again, or copy the link and paste it in Buddies → Enter Invite Link. If scanning is unavailable, allow camera access in your device's settings or share and paste the invite link instead.
