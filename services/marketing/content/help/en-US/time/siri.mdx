---
id: siri
title: Can I use Siri or Shortcuts with WitnessWork?
category: time
platforms:
  - ios
---

Yes, on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and on the Apple Watch app. Open WitnessWork once after updating, then say "Add time in WitnessWork", "Start my timer in WitnessWork", "Pause my timer in WitnessWork", "Stop my timer in WitnessWork", "Log a trip in WitnessWork" or "Log a round trip in WitnessWork". To add time you can also say log, enter, record, track or submit, and you can say how long, like "Log two hours in WitnessWork" or "Add 30 minutes to WitnessWork". Siri knows common amounts from 15 minutes to 12 hours; for any other amount, say "Add time in WitnessWork" and Siri asks how long. Siri listens in your Siri language, which can differ from the language you chose in WitnessWork. For trips, Siri asks how far. Distances use your Mileage distance unit; for a round trip, say the one-way distance and it's doubled. Stopping the timer adds its whole minutes as an entry for today and resets it; with less than a minute, nothing is added. Time and trips are added for today, as Standard time and with the car you used last. To pick a Type or car, add the action in the Shortcuts app, where you can also run it from the Action Button or a Home Screen shortcut. Adding time and the timer need you to log hours (Regular Publishers turn on Log my hours first). Logging trips needs Track Mileage on and at least one car. On iPhone and iPad the timer changes right away, and time and trips you add with Siri are saved the next time you open WitnessWork, then reach your other devices with sync like any entry. On the watch they're sent to your iPhone like other watch entries. Siri can only use your Types, car names, distance unit and timer from WitnessWork, never contacts, notes or reports. Siri and Shortcuts aren't available on Android. If Siri doesn't recognize a phrase, open WitnessWork once, wait a minute, and check that Siri is allowed for WitnessWork in Settings. For other problems, use the Help Center's private support link and keep your records out of public reports.
