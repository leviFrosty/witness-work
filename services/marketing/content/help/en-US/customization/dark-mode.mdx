category: customization
related:

Yes. WitnessWork can follow your phone's setting, or always use dark or light.

## Choose a look

1. Open **Settings → Personalization → Color Scheme**.
2. Choose **Device**, **Dark** or **Light**.

**Device** follows your phone's setting.
