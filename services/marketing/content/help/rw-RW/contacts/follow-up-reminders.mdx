title: "Nshobora kubika inyandiko z'isura ntateganyije gusubira gusura cyangwa inyibutsa?"
---

Yego. Iyo wongeyeho cyangwa uhinduye isura, zimya **Gusubirayo** ubike. Isura n'inyandiko zayo bigumaho, kandi nta kintu giteganywa cyangwa kigaragara nk'ikirengeje igihe.

## Hagarika gusubirayo

1. Fungura isura uyihindure.
2. Zimya **Gusubirayo**.
3. Bika.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ushobora gufungura **Gusubirayo** nyuma ugahitamo itariki n'igihe.
- Ku bindi bijyanye n'inyibutsa, reba [Inyibutsa za gusubirayo, gahunda, n'umuntu zikora zite?](/help/contacts/reminders).
