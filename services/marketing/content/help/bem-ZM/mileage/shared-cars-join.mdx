title: Nshi nshila ya kwingila nelyo ukukana motoka iyasabankanywa?
---

Ilyo munyina asabankanya motoka na imwe, mupokelela icakwishibisha. Ukwita nakwo kulolela mu kengele pa Home, na **Join** na **Kana**.

## Ingileni nelyo mukane

1. Pa Home, tinikeni kengele.
2. Fwayeni ukwita, nga "Anna shared Family Minivan with you."
3. Tinikeni **Join** nelyo **Kana**.

**Join** yongelako motoka ku Amakilomita na lyashi lyayo lyonse, ukubikako ifyalweendo fya pa ntanshi ya kwingila. Ama motoka yenu yashala yenu. Uwamwitile alebebwa ukuti mwaingila.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Bonse abali pa motoka balamona ifyalweendo fyonse, uwafilembele, ne malembo yabo. Abatali banyina benu balamona imwe ku shina ne cikope mwasabankanya.
- Ukwita kushala kwaisulwa inshiku 7.
- Mufwaya Buddies ukupokelela ukwita, pantu munyina fye e wingamwita. Moneni [WitnessWork Buddies ni nshi, elyo cinshi nshimona?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview).

## Nga cabipa

- **Ukwita kwapwa nelyo kwabula:** papeni uuli pa motoka ukuti amwite nakabili.
- **"This car already has the most people it can.":** motoka ikwata abantu 10, ukubikako ukwita ukulolela. Umbi uuli pa motoka afwile ukufumyapo ukwita ukwaisulwa, nelyo nyina motoka afumyepo umbi.
