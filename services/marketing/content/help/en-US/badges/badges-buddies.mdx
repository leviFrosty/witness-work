Your buddies see the badges you have, each at its highest level. You and your buddies can react to each other's badges.

## What your buddies see

- A row of your badges on your page in Buddies. **See all** shows each collection with the levels you've reached and the ones still ahead, plus your one-time badges.
- They never see your progress, counts or dates.
- In data protection mode, First Bible Study isn't shared.

To stop sharing badges, turn off **Badges** in **Buddies Settings**.

## Encourage a buddy

1. Open their page in Buddies.
2. Tap one of their badges.
3. Pick a reaction: 🎉, 🎊, 🔥, 👏, 👍 or 🙌.

You can react to each badge once. Picking another reaction replaces the first. Only that buddy sees who reacted, and nothing else is sent. If you're offline, your reaction is sent when you're back online.

## Where buddy news shows up

- In the notification bell on Home, under **From your buddies**. A buddy's new badge shows with the six reactions, so you can tap one right away. Afterwards the row shows what you sent, and **Change** lets you pick another.
- Tap the row or its badge to see the badge full screen, marked New if you hadn't seen it. Several badges from one buddy within a day share one row.
- When a buddy reacts to one of your badges, tap it to open your badge with every buddy's reaction.
- Buddy news doesn't add to the red number on the bell or app icon. A small dot on the bell means something new.
- Dismiss a row with **X** or **Clear All**, or let it clear after 30 days.

## Alerts

- When a buddy gets a new badge, you get an alert like "Levi earned a badge". You get at most one alert per buddy a day. Later badges that day arrive quietly in the bell.
- When a buddy reacts to your badge, you get an alert like "Anna reacted 🔥 to your badge", also at most once per buddy a day.
- Badges found in imported or restored history, and one-time badges, aren't announced.
- To stop alerts on this device, turn off **Badge Alerts** in **Settings → Notifications**. The news still appears in the bell.
- When you earn a badge and share badges, the New badge card says who will see it, like "Ben and Grace will see it".
- With **Show badges** off, you can't react, and reactions to your badges aren't shown.
