Yes. Only people on a shared car can read it. WitnessWork's server can't see the car's name, trips, notes, or amounts, or who is on the car.

## What people on the car see

- Its whole history, including trips from before they joined.
- Who logged and edited each trip.
- The car's fuels and prices, and trip notes.
- You, by the name and photo you share, if they aren't your buddies.

## Good to know

- A trip's note is shared with everyone on the car, and the note screen says so. A note too long to share can't be saved until you shorten it.
- Photos aren't shared. A shared car's trip form has no photo button. A photo a trip already had stays on the device that has it.
- Your other cars and their trips stay private. Only the car you share is shared.
- When someone leaves or is removed, they can't read new changes.
- Nobody can read a car without an invitation from someone on it.

For the Privacy Policy, tap the support button in this screen's header and look under **Still need help?**.
