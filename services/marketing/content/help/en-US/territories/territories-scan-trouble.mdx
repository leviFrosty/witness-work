**Scanning the card's QR code** (iPhone, iPad, and Android):

- **"WitnessWork needs the camera to scan a QR code."** Tap Open Settings and allow the camera for WitnessWork, then try again.
- **"That QR code has no web link."** The code holds something other than a web address, so there's no map to open. Use Pin on a Map or Outline the Area instead, or take a photo of the card.
- **Nothing happens:** hold the card flat and steady in good light, with the whole code in view. Tap Stop Scanning to give up and enter the link instead.

Scan the Card's QR Code isn't on the web; paste the link with Enter Map Link instead.

**A map link:** Enter Map Link saves any link you paste, and you can open it from the territory's Map button. WitnessWork also tries to find the spot so it can pin it for Directions, and that works for links to a place in Google Maps, Apple Maps, or OpenStreetMap. A short link (such as maps.app.goo.gl) needs a connection while "Finding the location…" shows. If no pin appears, the link still opens; tap Pin on a Map and tap where the territory is. A pin you placed yourself isn't moved by a link.

**Scanning notes** (iPhone and iPad): if you see "No writing was found in the scan", scan again in better light with the page flat, or type the notes. On Android, copy the text from a photo with Google Lens, then paste it.
