title: What does WitnessWork keep in my browser?
category: general
platforms: [web]
---

WitnessWork on the web keeps your records and sign-in in your browser, so it opens straight to your data and works offline. They stay there until you sign out.

## Good to know

- Signing out erases your records and sign-in from that browser. So does clearing site data. Your account and synced records are not lost, as long as you have a way to sign in again. See [What if I lose access?](/help/account/lost-access).
- There's no automatic lock. Anyone who can use that browser can open WitnessWork in it. On a shared computer, sign out when you're done. See [Signing out on the web](/help/account/web-sign-out).
- WitnessWork works in one tab at a time. See [Why does WitnessWork say it's open in another tab?](/help/general/web-tabs).

## Safari

Safari erases a site's data after 7 days without a visit. A web app on an iPhone Home Screen is exempt. If it happens, sign in again, for example with your passkey, and your synced records come back. Changes that hadn't synced are lost, so keep Cloud Sync on and let it finish.

## If something goes wrong

- **Your browser can't save:** WitnessWork keeps working and tells you once. If you're out of space, free up space on your device and keep the tab open. WitnessWork saves your changes as soon as it can.
- **You're in a private window:** some don't let sites save anything. Changes are lost when you close the tab, so use a regular window.
