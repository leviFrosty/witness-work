Calendar Sync is free on iPhone, iPad, and Android. This answer covers iPhone and iPad, and How does Calendar Sync work on Android? covers Android. During first-time setup, or from the invitation in the notifications tray after updating, tap Add to Calendar to create a WitnessWork calendar (in iCloud when available). You can also set it up anytime in Settings → Preferences → Calendar Sync: allow full calendar access, then create a WitnessWork calendar or choose a calendar used only for WitnessWork. Updates need iCloud sign-in and an internet connection, even for Google or Outlook calendars. To use Google or Outlook, create a calendar with that provider, add the account in iPhone Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts, and turn on its calendars. Your default calendar and calendars stored only on this device can't be selected. Once a calendar is selected, every upcoming Follow-up is added automatically, including ones you already saved, as a 30-minute event. Calendar Sync adds all of your upcoming Follow-ups or none; to stop adding them, turn it off in Settings → Preferences → Calendar Sync. Past Follow-ups, service Plans, and recurring Plans aren't added. Each event gets the same alert as its Follow-up's Notify Me reminder, and no alert when Notify Me is off. Your calendar provider may add its own default alerts; for Google, remove them under that calendar's Event notifications in Google Calendar settings. Make changes in WitnessWork, because calendar edits, including alerts, may be replaced on the next update.
