---
id: googleDriveSyncPrivacy
title: Bushe Google Drive Sync isunga finshi, kabili ni nani wingaimona?
---

Cila cibombelo ca Android cisunga icikope cimo ica fyebo fya WitnessWork, pamo ne fikope (nga mwafisala), mu folda iyafisama ya app muli Google Drive ya akaunti iyo mwatantika. WitnessWork fye ingaisula folda iyo: tamoneka mu app nelyo pa webusayiti ya Drive, ama app yambi tayingaibelenga, kabili WitnessWork tayingamona ifintu fimbi muli Drive yenu. Ifyebo fyenu fiya fye pakati ka cibombelo cenu na Google kabili tafipita mu maseva ya WitnessWork. Google ifisunga mu Google Account yenu nge failo shimbi sha Drive; tafyacingililwa ukufuma ku ntendekelo ukufika ku mpela. WitnessWork isunga fye code iyakatemwa iya akaunti iyatantikwa pa cibombelo, te email yenu. Folda ipendwa mu cifulo cenu ica Google. Pa kuifumyapo, shimyeni ukulunganikanya pa fibombelo fyonse, elyo mu Google Drive pa web isuleni Settings → Manage apps → WitnessWork → Options → Delete hidden app data. Ukufumya Google Drive mu Settings → Google Drive Sync kuleka ukufika ukufuma pa cibombelo ico kabili kusha folda ku fibombelo fyenu ifimbi. Pa kufumyapo ukufika kwa WitnessWork pa fibombelo fyonse, ifumyeni mu Third-party apps & services mu Google Account yenu. Amabackup yenu aya JSON aya mu Settings → Ukusunga no Kubwesha yalekana kabili yasala uko mwayasunga.
