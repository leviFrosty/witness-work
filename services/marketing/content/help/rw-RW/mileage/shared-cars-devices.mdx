title: "Imodoka zisangiwe zigaragara ku bindi bikoresho byanjye, ku isaha yanjye no muri backup?"
---

Yego. Imodoka isangiwe igaragara ku gikoresho cyawe icyo ari cyo cyose gikoresha indangamuntu imwe ya Buddies. Ni nako igaruka kuri telefoni nshya.

## Byiza kumenya

- Imodoka zisangiwe zivugururwa ubwazo kuri buri wese uziriho. Ntizirimo muri guhuza (Cloud Sync, iCloud cyangwa Google Drive), Backup ya WitnessWork, cyangwa backup ziva muri **Kubika & Kugarura**.
- Shyiraho ikindi gikoresho ukoresheje urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura cyangwa **Shyiraho ikindi gikoresho**, kandi kizerekana imodoka zawe zisangiwe.
- **Andika urugendo** ukoresheje Siri kuri iPhone na Apple Watch irimo imodoka zisangiwe. Siri ntiboneka kuri Android, kandi Wear OS ntishobora kwandika ingendo.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Imenyesha rivuga "A shared car isn't ready on this device yet":** impinduka zawe ziguma kuri iki gikoresho kandi zisangizwa iyo gihagurutse.
