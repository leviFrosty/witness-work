title: What does becoming a Supporter unlock?
---

Ifatabuguzi rikora ry'Umuterankunga rigufasha kubona ikimenyetso cy'Umuterankunga, amabara nyamukuru wihitiramo, uburyo bw'abantu bubitswe, na Tegura inzira y'uyu munsi, ishyira gusubirayo na gahunda by'umunsi mu murongo w'inzira ngufi, hamwe na iCloud Sync n'amashusho asimburwa ya porogaramu kuri iOS, na Google Drive Sync kuri Android. Abaterankunga kandi bahabwa umubare munini wemewe wa Scribe AI mu Kwinjiza inyandiko kuri iOS na Android aho iboneka; imibare yemewe y'ubuntu n'iy'Abaterankunga ubu igaragara mu Bufasha no ku rupapuro rw'inkunga. Ibiranga by'ingenzi bikomeza kuba ubuntu, harimo kuyungurura no gutondeka abantu, na Buddies iyo iboneka; Abaterankunga kandi bashobora guhitamo ibara rya buri mugenzi. Impano imwe (Tip) iguha izina ry'Uwatanze impano ariko ntifungura ibiranga by'Umuterankunga.
