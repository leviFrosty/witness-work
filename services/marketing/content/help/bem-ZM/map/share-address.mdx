isula uwa kutandalila na tinikeni yabo adiresi. muka ba offered Apple Maps, Google Maps, nelyo Waze depending pa what's installed. kuti mwa na kabili icishibilo ca pa mapu contact's uko muli ukwabula adiresi, ico ni useful pa lwa areas uko street numbers don't exist.
