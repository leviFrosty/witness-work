---
id: territoriesNotAtHomes
title: How do I go back to the houses where nobody was home?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Open the territory and look under Not at Home: it lists each street with houses to try again and how many. Tap a street to see only its not-at-homes, then record each one again with the door recorder. Recording a house again on another day adds a try, so the list shows "Tried 2 times". Each house also shows the day and time it was last recorded, such as "Sat, Nov 8 at 10:30 AM", so you can try again on a different day or at a different hour. To see when people in your territories are most often home, see [How do I see the best days and times to find people home?](/help/territories/territories-insights)

On a street with several houses recorded, you can also switch between All and Not at Home above the list.

A house you record as Spoke or Do Not Call leaves the not-at-home list. With territory privacy mode on, no house-by-house records are kept, so there's no not-at-home list (see [What is territory privacy mode?](/help/territories/territories-privacy-mode)).
