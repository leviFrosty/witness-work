---
id: scribeMemory
title: What does Scribe AI remember about me?
category: scribe
minAppVersion: "1.45.0"
---

Scribe AI keeps short notes about how you work, so you don’t have to repeat yourself. When you correct it (“No, the build is what I call LDC”), use your own word for something, mention a lasting preference, or say “Remember…”, it quietly saves a short note, and every new conversation starts with these notes. To forget one, say “Forget what I said about the build.”

To see, edit, or add notes yourself, go to Settings → Scribe AI → Memory. Write one note per line. Memory holds up to 1,500 characters; when it’s full, a note has to be shortened or removed before another can be saved, and you can make room yourself. Turn off Memory on the same screen to stop Scribe AI from reading or saving notes, or tap Clear memory to erase them all.

Your notes stay on this device. They aren’t synced or backed up. They’re sent along with each conversation, the same way as the rest of your messages, so they’re never stored or used for training. Scribe AI doesn’t save personal details about the people you visit, or anything it reads in your visit notes. A change you make in Settings applies from your next conversation.
