id: petHats
title: How do I get hats for my pet?
category: pet
---

Your pet can wear a hat, or none. From version 1.46.0, you buy hats in the Shop with coins, which you earn by going out in the ministry (see the question about coins and the Shop). There are 39 to buy from version 1.47.0 (12 before it), and secret hats you find along the way (see the question about secret items). They cost from 100 coins for the Bow to 3,200 for the Little fig tree (the first 12 cost 10 to 36 before version 1.47.0, when every coin amount was multiplied by 10). Many of the newer hats move, and the five dearest are rare (see the question about rare hats).

To buy one, tap your pet's card on Home anywhere except on your pet, tap Shop, pick the Hat category, tap a hat to try it on, and tap Buy. Your pet wears it right away. You can also tap More in Shop at the end of the Hat tab in the pet's details. To change hats later, pick the Hat tab in the details and tap one you own; the details list only your hats, and the Shop only sells them. It's saved right away, and your pet wears it on Home, in its details, in shared pictures, and in the Home Screen widget (iPhone and iPad). A hat you own stays yours, also after Change pet, and it says New until you pick it. Your pet takes its hat off while it rolls over or lies down. From version 1.47.0, your buddies see your pet's hat when it visits them; on older versions they see it without one.

Before version 1.46.0, the 12 hats opened up with your streak on their own numbers: the first three came quickly, at 3, 5, and 9 kept planned days (or in your first month for publishers), and the rest took longer, up to 120 days (or 12 months). If you turned off Show streak, or you logged hours but hadn't made any plans, the first three opened up in your first active month and the rest one month at a time. Hats that opened up that way stay yours after you update, and your streak no longer opens hats.
