id: webInstall
title: Nshi nshila ya kubika WitnessWork pa home screen nelyo desktop yandi?
---

Kuti mwabomfya WitnessWork mu browser tab, nelyo ukuibika ukuti iisule mu windo yaiko nga app. Ni app imo fye.

## Ibikeni

- **Chrome nelyo Edge pa kompyuta:** isuleni app.witnesswork.app elyo mubomfye icikope ca install mu address bar, nelyo icintu ca **Install** mu menu.
- **Safari pa Mac:** isuleni menu ya **Ifailo** elyo musale **Add to Dock**.
- **iPhone nelyo iPad:** mu Safari, tinikeni **Share**, elyo **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** mu Chrome, tinikeni **Install** pa banner ya **Install App** pa pamulu, elyo mutinikishe. Nelyo mubomfye menu ya browser elyo musale **Install app** nelyo **Add to Home Screen**.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Web app pa iPhone Home Screen isunga ifyo mwalemba na nga tamwaisula inshita. Moneni [Cinshi WitnessWork isunga mu browser yandi?](/help/general/web-storage).
- Pa iPhone na iPad, Safari ilanga banner ya Apple iya app ya App Store.
- Pwisheni banner ya **Install App** na X yayo. Ishala ipwishiwa mu browser iyo mpaka data ya site yafumyapo nelyo mufume.
- Takuli banner pa kompyuta, nelyo ilyo WitnessWork yabikwa.
