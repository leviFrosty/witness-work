---
id: cloudSyncDefault
title: Is Cloud Sync on by default?
category: backups
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

From version 1.47.0, Cloud Sync is included for everyone, and a new install of WitnessWork starts with it on. Settings → Device Sync shows Cloud Sync once you've finished setting up the app, and your records and photos sync to your other devices on Cloud Sync, end-to-end encrypted.

If you already used WitnessWork before 1.47.0, version 1.47.0 shows one message in the notifications tray (the bell on Home) offering Cloud Sync, on any device that isn't on it yet. Tap its button to switch, or dismiss it; it doesn't come back.

- **Your setting isn't changed.** From version 1.47.0, a device that didn't sync stays that way until you choose. (Version 1.46.0 may turn Cloud Sync on by itself on a device that never chose a sync option; you can turn it off in Settings → Device Sync.)
- **iCloud and Google Drive stay.** A device that syncs with iCloud (Google Drive on Android) keeps it. Switch any time in Settings → Device Sync; nothing is replaced, and nothing deleted on your other devices comes back.
- **Off stays off.** If you turned sync off, it stays off. You can turn it on again in Settings → Device Sync.

To change it on any device, open Settings → Device Sync and choose Cloud Sync, iCloud (Google Drive on Android), or Off. Your devices sync only with devices on the same option, so choose the same one on each. WitnessWork on the web uses only Cloud Sync.
