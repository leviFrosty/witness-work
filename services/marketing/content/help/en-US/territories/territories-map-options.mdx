When you add or edit a territory, **Map (Optional)** offers five ways to add a map. Use whichever matches your card, or none.

## The options

- **Scan the Card's QR Code** (phone apps only): point the camera at the code on the card. It saves the card's web link so you can open its map. It doesn't add an outline or streets.
- **Enter Map Link:** paste the address of the territory's map, such as a link a congregation sent you. For a Google Maps, Apple Maps, or OpenStreetMap link, WitnessWork also tries to pin the spot so **Directions** works.
- **Pin on a Map:** tap the map where the territory is. This adds **Directions**.
- **Outline the Area** (phone apps only): tap each corner of the territory, at least three. Tap a corner to move or delete it, or use **Undo Last Corner**. WitnessWork can then suggest the streets inside it.
- **Photo of the Card:** take a photo of the paper card, or choose one. On the web, you choose a picture file.

## Use the map later

The territory's page shows **Map** (or **Map & Card** with a photo) and **Directions**. **Map** opens the outline, pins, and doors. The card photo can be pinched to zoom and turned. If the territory has only a link, **Map** opens the link.

## Good to know

- The photo of the card stays on your phone. It isn't shared with the people you share the territory with.
- If a QR code won't scan or a link won't open, see [Why can't I scan the card's QR code?](/help/territories/territories-scan-trouble)
