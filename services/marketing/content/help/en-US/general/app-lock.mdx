---
id: appLock
title: How do I lock WitnessWork with my face, fingerprint, passcode, or a passkey?
category: general
---

You can make WitnessWork ask for your face, fingerprint, or passcode every time it opens.

## Turn on the lock

1. Open **Settings → Privacy**.
2. Turn on **Require Face ID**. On other phones it says **Require Touch ID**, **Require Passcode**, or, on Android, **Require Biometric Unlock**.
3. Confirm it's you.
4. Under **Require**, choose how soon WitnessWork asks again after you leave it: right away, or after 1, 5, or 15 minutes.

## Turn it off

Open **Settings → Privacy**, turn the switch off, and confirm it's you.

## Good to know

- If your face or fingerprint isn't recognized, use your phone's passcode, PIN, pattern, or password.
- The lock is only on the phone where you turn it on. Turn it on separately on each device.
- Your phone needs a passcode or screen lock. If it has none, tap **Open Settings** when WitnessWork asks, set one up, then turn the lock on again.
- While the lock is on, the app switcher shows a blank card. On Android, this needs Android 13 or later.
- The lock covers the app only. Widgets, notifications, Siri, and your watch still show what they normally do.
- If you tap a reminder or link while WitnessWork is locked, it opens after you unlock.

## On the web

The web lock uses a passkey, so your account needs one first. See [What is a passkey?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).

1. Open **Settings → Privacy**.
2. Turn on **Require a Passkey** and confirm with your passkey. You need to be online.
3. Under **Require**, choose 1, 5, or 15 minutes. The web has no right-away option.

To unlock, click **Unlock with Passkey**. To turn the lock off, turn the switch off and use your passkey again.

- The lock is for that browser only. Signing out turns it off.
- It hides WitnessWork until you unlock, but it doesn't sign you out. On a shared computer, sign out when you're done.
- If you remove every passkey it uses from your account, the lock turns off by itself.

## If something goes wrong

- **Unlocking keeps failing on your phone:** check that your passcode works on the phone's lock screen, then close WitnessWork completely and open it again.
- **None of your passkeys is available on the web:** choose **Sign Out** on the lock screen and sign in again. Anything that browser hadn't synced is erased.
- **Still stuck:** use the Help Center's private support link, and keep personal details out of public reports.
