id: becomeSupporter
title: What do I get as a supporter?
category: supporter
---

An active Supporter subscription gives you the Supporter badge, custom accent colors and Plan Today's Route, which puts the day's follow-ups and plans in the shortest driving order, plus Device Sync, and alternate app icons on iOS. Device Sync is Cloud Sync, the only option that syncs live, even between iPhone and Android, or iCloud Sync on iOS and Google Drive Sync on Android. Supporters also get generous Scribe AI limits on iOS and Android; without Supporter, Scribe AI has a smaller monthly limit (see How do Scribe AI limits work?). Core features remain free, including contact filters, sorting and saved views, and Buddies when available; Supporters can also choose each buddy's color. A one-time Tip gives Donor recognition but does not activate Supporter features.
