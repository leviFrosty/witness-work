Tap **+** and choose **Log Visit**, then pick the person.

## Log a visit

1. Tap **+**, then **Log Visit**.
2. Pick the person. Type to search. If they aren't there, tap **Add as Contact** to create them first.
3. Tap **Had a Conversation** to add notes and a follow-up. Or tap **Not at Home** to log it right away. **Undo** shows for a few seconds.

On a contact's page, **Not at Home** logs it the same way. To open a contact instead, touch and hold the person.

## Good to know

- The list starts with **Nearby** (within about 250 m, closest first), then **Follow-ups Due**, **Recent**, and all contacts A to Z.
- **Nearby** appears only if you allow location. Your location is used only to sort the list, and it isn't saved or sent anywhere.
- With data protection mode on, **Not at Home** isn't offered and the visit form opens directly.
- On iPad, **Log Visit…** is also in the **File** menu.

## If something goes wrong

- **Nearby is missing:** check that location is allowed for WitnessWork in your phone's settings, and that the contact has a location on the map.
