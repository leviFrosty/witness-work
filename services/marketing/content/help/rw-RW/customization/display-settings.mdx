title: "WitnessWork ikorana na Bold Text, inyandiko nini n'andi magenamiterere yo kwerekana?"
---

Yego. WitnessWork ikurikiza igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe ryo kwerekana, bityo nta kintu ugomba gufungura muri porogaramu.

## Igenamiterere ikurikiza

- **Ingano y'inyandiko:** Larger Text kuri iPhone na iPad, Font size kuri Android. WitnessWork ihina imirongo kugira ngo amagambo adacikamo. Kuri tablet, inyandiko nini cyane isimbuza umurongo w'ibumoso umurongo wo hasi.
- **Inyandiko y'umubyimba:** Bold Text kuri iPhone na iPad, Bold text kuri Android 12 cyangwa irenga. Kuri Android, impinduka igaragara usubiye muri WitnessWork.
- **Reduce Transparency (iPhone na iPad):** ituma imitwe n'imirongo isa n'ibyijimye ikomera.
- **Increase Contrast (iPhone na iPad):** ituma inyandiko z'ibara ry'ivu n'imbibi bigaragara cyane. Kuri Android, High contrast text ya sisitemu irakurikizwa.
- **Reduce Motion (Remove animations kuri Android):** ituma ibyishimo bigumya gutuza, nta confetti.

Kugira ngo uhitemo urumuri cyangwa umwijima, fungura **Igenamiterere → Personalization → Itsinda ry'amabara**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ibi bigenamiterere bihindura uko WitnessWork igaragara gusa, ntibigera bihindura amakuru yawe.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Inyandiko iracitse cyangwa biragoye kuyisoma:** bimenyeshe uvuye mu Kigo cy'ubufasha ufite ifoto ya ecran, telefoni yawe n'ingano y'inyandiko yawe. Banza upfuke amazina n'aderesi.
