title: What is WitnessWork Buddies, and why don't I see it?

Buddies lets you connect with up to five people you go out in service with and see each other's planned days, start times, and durations for the next eight weeks. You also see the badges each buddy shares. It works on iPhone, iPad, and Android, and buddies on different platforms can connect with each other. On the Schedule calendar, a buddy's photo or initial on a day means you're going out together, and a small dot under a day means other buddies plan to go out. Tap the day to see who. On iPhone and iPad, the Calendar widget shows the same marks and updates when WitnessWork refreshes Buddies, so open the app if it looks behind. It is free when available, and needs WitnessWork from the App Store or Google Play on a device that can be verified (see "Why does WitnessWork say it can't verify this device?"). Open Schedule, tap Buddies at the top (from version 1.46.0, the Buddies row under the Schedule title), and complete the introduction with your profile name. A dot on it means something is waiting for you, like a request to confirm. From 1.46.0, Buddies also opens from the Buddies row on your Profile and, on a wide tablet layout, from the sidebar. You can also add a Together section to Home, which lists invitations waiting for your answer, then what you've planned with buddies over the next two weeks, and opens Buddies too: tap Edit Home Screen after the last section on Home, then Add Sections at the top, then Together. Buddies may be unavailable during rollout. Update the installed app if Buddies is missing from Schedule. If it's dimmed, Buddies is temporarily unavailable; your saved buddies stay on your calendar, so try again later.
