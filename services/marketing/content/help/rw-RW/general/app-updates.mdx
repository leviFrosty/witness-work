title: "Nabona nte ivugurura rishya rya WitnessWork?"
---

Ivugurura ryinshi ryikururira ubwaryo igihe ukoresha WitnessWork. Iyo rimwe riteguye, inzogera y'Ahabanza yerekana **Ibishya biteguye**.

## Shyiraho ivugurura riteguye

1. Kanda inzogera iri Ahabanza.
2. Kanda **Vugurura nonaha**. WitnessWork yongera gutangira ifite verisiyo nshya.

Niba ushaka gutegereza, ribe riri aho. Rizakora ubutaha uzahagarika WitnessWork burundu ukongera kuyifungura. Amakuru yawe, Aderesi n'inyandiko ntibikozweho.

## Ishakire ivugurura

1. Manura Ahabanza. Cyangwa kanda ifoto y'umwirondoro, hanyuma **Igenamiterere → Ibindi**.
2. Kanda **Reba ibishya**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Amavugurura amwe aturuka kuri App Store, cyangwa kuri Google Play Store kuri Android. Ngaho nabwo komeza kureka amavugurura yikora afunguye.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ivugurura ntirikururwa:** ihuze na murandasi wongere ukande **Reba ibishya**.
