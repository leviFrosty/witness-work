# Badges

Badges celebrate steady habits by counting calendar months, never hours. Nine collections (Sharing the Good News, Year Round, Reports Sent, Ready to Go, Kind Words, Return Visits, Next Time, Keeping in Touch, and Two by Two where Buddies is available) each grow through Bronze, Silver, Gold and Pearl. Two one-time badges, First Bible Study and First Buddy, stay hidden until earned. The app checks the user's records a moment after they change and whenever it comes back to the foreground. A level reached this month or last month by something the User just did (Add Time, the checkbox, saving a visit or Plan, sending a report, answering Going, confirming a buddy) plays a full-screen "New badge" celebration; one that arrives any other way (another device, the Watch, a buddy's reply, a Plan's day arriving overnight) shows a dismissible "New badge" card at the top of Home instead (ADR 0021). Badges found in older records arrive quietly: the first check on a device shows a one-time "Your badges are here" screen, and a later import or restore shows one "Your records already include N badges" summary. Earned badges show on the profile and on the Badges screen, which also shows progress toward each next level. Tapping any medallion opens that badge full screen. Buddies see them on the user's buddy page, and can react to them. A badge is never taken away.

## Sub-features

- `badges-profile`: the profile overlay's badges section, directly under the profile card and above the stat tiles. Its header reads "N badges" (or "Badges" before any), with the newest medallion per collection in a row and "See all" (or "See badges"). On Settings → "Profile" ("Edit your profile"), the profile card has a badge row that opens Badges ("Opens your badges"), and a "Your badges" row ("N badges") does the same.
- `badges-screen`: the Badges screen, titled "Badges". The hero shows "N badges", "Newest: <badge>" and an "About badges" info button. Each collection has a card with its levels, progress ("Next: Silver" plus how to grow it, or "All four levels collected"), and the one-time badges sit under "Moments". Leaving the screen marks every badge seen.
- `badges-view`: tapping a medallion opens the badge full screen (route `BadgeView`, a transparent modal). The coin flips out of the tapped medallion (a crossfade under Reduce Motion) and settles large over the level ("BRONZE"), the name, its description and "Earned <month>" or "Found in your history". The header reads "<Name>, <Level>" (for example "Year Round, Bronze") to VoiceOver. Your own earned badges add an owner chip ("<profile name>'s badge", left off without a profile name) and a status line, "Next: <level> · <n> of <m> months" over a thin bar with an info button, or "All four levels collected". A level not reached yet (from the Badges screen) shows the outline coin, "Not yet", progress toward it and how to grow it, with no owner. A buddy's badge shows "<Buddy>'s badge" and no date. "WitnessWork" with the app icon sits at the bottom. Tapping the coin spins it. Close with "Close" (the X), a swipe down, or Android's back; it flies back into a medallion that's still on screen and fades otherwise (from the profile overlay).
- `badges-reactions` (Buddies flag, Show badges on): on your own earned badge, "From your buddies" lists each reacting buddy's avatar with their emoji, newest first, up to 8 and then "+N". On a buddy's badge, "Encourage <Buddy>" offers 🎉 🎊 🔥 👏 👍 🙌; the one you sent is highlighted (selected), and picking another replaces it.
- `badges-welcome`: on a device's first check that finds history, a one-time full-screen "Your badges are here" with the fanned top coins, "You've already earned N badges", "Take a look" (opens the profile overlay, or Badges without a profile) and "Later". It's a takeover with the lowest priority, and never on a launch that showed the update reveal: it waits for the next cold launch without one. `preferences.badgesWelcome` goes `pending` → `done` (not synced).
- `badges-celebration`: "New badge" names the leading badge, then "Added to your collection" and "+N more", with "Done" and "See all badges". It plays only within 15 s of the User's own action, as a takeover. With Buddies set up, Badges sharing on and active buddies, a quiet line under it says who will see it, beside up to three stacked avatars: "<Name> will see it", "<Name> and <Name> will see it", or "Your buddies will see it" for three or more. It's absent with no buddies, sharing off, or First Bible Study in data protection mode. The history summary reads "Your records already include N badges" and offers "See badges" and "Done".
- `badges-home-card`: a badge that arrived without a celebration shows a card at the top of Home: "NEW BADGE", the medallion, its title ("Ready to Go, Bronze") and description (or "and N more"), "View" (labelled "View <title>"; opens the badge full screen) and an X ("Dismiss"). Either closes it for good (`preferences.badgeCardDismissed`, not synced). The badge stays "new" on the profile; opening Badges (which marks badges seen) retires the card too. Only badges from the last month; never a buddy's.
- `badges-new`: badges earned since Badges was last opened read "<title>, new" and carry a dot.
- `badges-buddies` (Buddies flag): the buddy page has a "BADGES" grid right under their profile card, with every badge they share (each collection's highest level and their One-time Badges), earned only, with no progress. A coin opens it full screen. Buddies Settings has a "Badges" sharing switch and "Badge Alerts". A buddy's new badge adds "<Name> has a new badge" to the Home bell (`badges-bell-news`), and its push reads "A buddy has a new badge".
- `badges-bell-news` (Buddies flag, Show badges on): buddies' news sits at the bottom of the Home bell under "From your buddies", below everything else, and never counts toward the bell's red number or the app icon (`notificationsTray.unread`). While only news is unread, the bell shows a small accent dot and reads "Notifications. New from your buddies." A buddy's new badge reads "<Name> has a new badge" (or "<Name> has N new badges" once news from one buddy within 20 hours folds into one row), the badge line ("Year Round, Gold + 1 more"), and Today, This week, or Earlier, never a clock time. Its six reaction chips send at once and highlight the one sent; reopened later, the row shows "You sent 👏" with "Change". A reaction to your badge reads "<Name> reacted 👏 to <badge>". Tapping a row or its coin opens the badge full screen (theirs with "New" while the row was unread; yours for a reaction). Nothing shows with no buddies or Show badges off.
- `badges-setting`: the "Show badges" switch on Settings → "Profile" hides badges everywhere, stops sharing them and stops celebrations. Earned badges are kept. Turning it off also hides the "Your badges" row.
- `badges-onboarding`: the onboarding step "Grow your badges", just before the notifications step.
- `badges-reveal`: the update reveal page "Badges to grow". Its caption differs without Buddies.
- `badges-help`: the Help Center category "Badges" with seven FAQs, including "How do I see a badge up close or share it?" and "Where do my buddies' new badges and reactions show up?".

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Account menu ("Account and settings") → "Profile" → the badges section → a medallion → the badge full screen. "See all" → Badges → any medallion (earned or not yet), the hero's newest badge, or a Moments row → the badge full screen.
- Account menu → "Settings" → "Profile" → the profile card's badge row, or "Your badges" → Badges.
- "See all badges" or "See badges" on a celebration card.
- Schedule → "Buddies" → a buddy → the "BADGES" grid → a coin → that buddy's badge full screen ("<Name>'s badge") with the reaction bar.
- Home bell → "From your buddies" → "<Name> reacted 🎉 to <badge>" → your badge full screen with their reaction.
- The first check on a device with history (an upgrade) → "Your badges are here" → "Take a look" → the profile overlay.
- Account menu → "Settings" → "Profile" → "Show badges".
- Schedule → "Buddies" → Buddies Settings → "Badges" (sharing) and "Badge Alerts".
- Home bell → "From your buddies" → "<Name> has a new badge" → a reaction chip (Encourage), or the row → that badge full screen ("<Name>'s badge", "New").
- A fresh install's onboarding → "Grow your badges".
- The update reveal, or Tools → Special updates → "Play now" to replay it → "Badges to grow".
- Help Center → "Badges".

## Driving it with ww-verify

Preconditions:

- `wwv seed badges` gives a pioneer with a year and a bit of history. It earns exactly 14 badges: Silver in Sharing the Good News, Reports Sent, Kind Words, Return Visits and Keeping in Touch; Bronze in Year Round, Ready to Go and Next Time; and First Bible Study.
- `wwv seed pioneer` is for earning a badge live, because nothing in it reaches Ready to Go yet (its plans are all in the future). `wwv seed onboarded` gives the empty state.
- Every onboarded seed files what its records reach as history that's already seen, so a seed never shows a card and only badges earned afterwards read as new. `wwv seed fresh` leaves that first check until onboarding is done, like a real install.
- `__WW_DEV__.badges` is for setup and read-back only, never the proof: `state()` (now with `card`, the badges the Home card names, `cardDismissed`, `takeover` and `celebrationExpiresInMs`), `evaluate(quiet?)`, `celebrate(key)`, `history(n)`, `earnAll()`, `earnRandom()`, `reset()`, `simulateBuddyBadge()` and `simulateBuddyReaction(badgeKey?, emoji?)`.
- The action rule (ADR 0021): `evaluate()` and `celebrate(key)` celebrate full screen only within 15 s of `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.noteUserAction()'` (or a real save in the UI). Without one they take the quiet path: `celebrate(key)` records an unearned badge as earned just now, and the Home card names it. `__WW_DEV__.takeover.state()` shows the takeover on screen, the queue (with each claim's `expiresInMs`), the holds (`screen`, `profile`, `tray`, `popover:…`, `sheet:…`, `background`, `inactive`, `navigation`) and what has `shown` this launch.

Steps:

- **Profile to Badges.** After `wwv seed badges` and `wwv ad snapshot -i`, run `wwv ad press 'label="Account and settings"' --settle`, then `wwv ad press 'label="Profile"' --settle`. The overlay shows "14 badges" and "See all". Run `wwv ad press 'label="See all"' --settle`. The route is `Badges` (`wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.state().route'`) and the hero reads "14 badges". Capture with `wwv shot badges-screen`.
- **Badge view.** Run `wwv ad press 'label="Year Round, Bronze"' --settle`. The route is `BadgeView` (params `{ badgeKey: 'yearRound.1', owner: 'me', origin }`) and the screen shows the "Year Round, Bronze" header, "Shared in at least 10 months of a service year", "Earned <month>" (the seed's history records carry the month that reached them; "Found in your history" appears only when that month is unknown), the owner chip, and "Next: Silver · 1 of 3 service years". Capture with `wwv shot badge-view`, then `wwv ad settings appearance dark` and capture again. Record the flip with `wwv ad record start` before the press and `wwv ad record stop` after it settles. Press "Close"; the route is `Badges` again. Press a not-yet level such as "Year Round, Pearl, not yet earned" for the locked view ("Not yet", progress, how to grow). `e2e/maestro/badges.yaml` drives open → view → close.
- **From the overlay.** In the profile overlay, press a medallion (for example "Year Round, Bronze"): the overlay fades and the badge opens full screen. Closing it lands on Home.
- **Earn one live (the User's action).** After `wwv seed pioneer`, run `wwv ad press 'label="Quick Action"' --settle`, then `wwv ad press 'label="Create Plan"' --settle` (or `wwv link 'witnesswork://day'`). Keep today's date and save (schedule-plans.md). Saving marks the action. Back on the tabs, run `wwv ad wait text "See all badges"` (only the celebration has it; the Home card also reads "New badge"). The celebration names "Ready to Go, Bronze". Capture with `wwv shot badge-celebration`, then press "Done". Read back with `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.badges.state()'`: `earned` includes `prepared.1`, `celebrations` is empty, `cardDismissed` includes `prepared.1` and `card` is empty (celebrated, so no Home card). `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.stores.preferences.getState().earnedBadges["prepared.1"]'` has no `history`. Scripted: `wwv flow e2e/maestro/badge-celebration.yaml`.
- **Arrive quietly (no action).** After `wwv seed pioneer`, add today's Plan without the UI, as a sync would: `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.stores.serviceReports.getState().addDayPlan({ id: "verify-quiet", date: new Date(), minutes: 60 })'`. About 2 s later nothing takes over (`__WW_DEV__.takeover.state().active` stays null), and Home's top card reads "NEW BADGE" and "Ready to Go, Bronze" (`__WW_DEV__.badges.state().card` is `["prepared.1"]`). Capture with `wwv shot badge-home-card`. Press "View Ready to Go, Bronze": the route is `BadgeView` with `badgeKey: "prepared.1"`. Close it: the card is gone, and stays gone after relaunching (`wwv up`); `cardDismissed` includes `prepared.1`. Repeat with a fresh seed and press "Dismiss" instead. The badge still reads "Ready to Go, Bronze, new" in the profile overlay.
- **One at a time.** With the profile overlay open (Account and settings → Profile), run `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.badges.celebrate("yearRound.2")'` after `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.noteUserAction()'`: nothing mounts while the overlay is open (`takeover.state().holds` includes `profile`), and the celebration plays about 0.7 s after closing it, if that's within 15 s of the action; later, it expires and the Home card names it. The bell's tray holds the same way.
- **New dot.** After earning live, open the profile overlay. The Ready to Go medallion reads "Ready to Go, Bronze, new". Open "See all", go back, and reopen the overlay: the label is "Ready to Go, Bronze" again.
- **Kept after delete.** Delete the plan you just made. `prepared.1` is still in `__WW_DEV__.badges.state().earned`.
- **Welcome.** This is the upgrade path. Run `wwv seed badges`, then `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.badges.reset()'` (it also clears `badgesWelcome`) and `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.badges.evaluate()'`, a first check that isn't quiet. Then run `wwv ad wait text "Your badges are here"` and `wwv shot badges-welcome`; it reads "You've already earned 14 badges". On a launch that played the update reveal (Tools → Special updates → "Play now" counts too), it doesn't come: `badgesWelcome` stays `pending` and `takeover.state().shown` includes `update-reveal`; relaunch with `wwv up` and it shows. Press "Take a look": the profile overlay opens with the badges section at the top. Read back `__WW_DEV__.stores.preferences.getState().badgesWelcome`, which is `done`. Run the evaluate again: nothing shows.
- **History summary.** This is the import or restore path, on a device that was already welcomed. Run `wwv seed pioneer`, then `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.badges.reset()'`, `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.stores.preferences.getState().set({ badgesWelcome: "done" })'` and `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.badges.evaluate()'`. Then run `wwv ad wait text "Your records already include"` and `wwv shot badge-history`. The trigger is only setup. Report the card itself as what was verified, and the import and restore paths as unverified unless you drove them.
- **Show badges.** Run `wwv ad press 'label="Account and settings"' --settle`, then `wwv ad press 'label="Settings"' --settle` and `wwv ad press 'label="Profile"' --settle`. Turn off "Show badges"; the "Your badges" row disappears. Reopen the profile overlay: the badges section is gone. Read back with `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.stores.preferences.getState().showBadges'`, which is `false`, and with `__WW_DEV__.badges.state().earnedCount`, which hasn't changed. Turn it on again and the section returns.
- **Buddies.** Pair two devices first (buddies.md, `--api local`, `wwv flag buddies on`). On each one, the other buddy's page shows the "BADGES" grid under their card. Press a coin: the badge opens full screen with "<Name>'s badge" and "Encourage <Name>". Press 🎉: it's selected at once (`__WW_DEV__.stores.buddies.getState().sentBadgeReactions`), and the other device's bell gets the reaction. Turn off "Badges" in Buddies Settings on one device; after a sync, the grid is gone on the other. To see the bell entry without a second badge being earned, run `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.badges.simulateBuddyBadge()'` (or Tools → Badges → "Simulate a buddy's new badge"). The bell shows "<Name> has a new badge" under "From your buddies", and pressing it opens that badge full screen. The bell lists Buddies entries only once Buddies is set up on that device. To see a buddy's reaction to one of your badges, run `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.badges.simulateBuddyReaction()'` (or Tools → Badges → "Simulate a reaction to my badge"): it adds a random reaction from the first active buddy to your newest badge (or pass a key such as `yearRound.2` and an id such as `party`). The bell shows "<Name> reacted 🎉 to <badge>", pressing it opens that badge full screen, and the view lists the reaction. With no active buddy, neither the bell nor the view lists it.
- **Bell news without pairing.** Seed made-up buddies (the bell only reads Buddies state): `wwv flag buddies on`, then `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.stores.buddies.setState({ onboardingComplete: true, registeredInboxId: "verify-inbox-me", lastSyncAt: Date.now() + 315e9, buddies: ["Tomás Rivera", "Grace Kim"].map((name, i) => ({ inboxId: "verify-buddy-" + i, name, colorIndex: i, status: "active", pairedAt: Date.now(), showOnCalendar: true, dhPub: "x", inviteSecret: "x", badges: [] })) })'`. The future `lastSyncAt` keeps the bell's "Couldn't check" banner away, since that inbox doesn't exist on any relay. Pairing earns First Buddy, which waits on the Home "New badge" card since no action was behind it. Then run `__WW_DEV__.badges.simulateBuddyBadge()` twice (one buddy, so the second folds into the first row) and `__WW_DEV__.badges.simulateBuddyReaction()`. Read back `__WW_DEV__.stores.notificationsTray.getState().unread`: it leaves the news out, and the bell's label is "Notifications. New from your buddies." while nothing else is unread. Open the bell and scroll (`wwv ad swipe 200 550 200 150` inside the popover): "From your buddies" sits below everything else. Press a chip, then read `__WW_DEV__.stores.buddies.getState().sentBadgeReactions` (`{ <inboxId>: { <badgeKey>: { e } } }`). Press a row: the route is `BadgeView` with `isNew: true` and the "New" pill shows. Close it: `notificationsTray.seen` holds the row ids and every folded queue entry is `read`. Turn Show badges off, or set `buddies: []`: the group and the dot are gone. `__WW_DEV__.noteUserAction()` then `__WW_DEV__.badges.celebrate('yearRound.2')` shows the "will see it" line on the celebration; set `sharing: { ...sharing, badges: false }` and it's gone.
- **Onboarding.** Run `wwv seed fresh` and follow onboarding.md. "Grow your badges" appears before the notifications step. After finishing, `__WW_DEV__.badges.state().backfilledAt` is set.
- **Reveal.** Tools → Special updates → "Play now". Page through to "Badges to grow". While it's on screen, nothing else can take over: `noteUserAction()` plus `badges.celebrate(…)` and a streak replay (streaks.md) only queue (`takeover.state().queue`), and play one at a time after it closes. A push tap or link that comes in meanwhile waits for it too (`holds` includes `navigation`). On Android, Back closes the reveal only while it's on screen with nothing pushed over it.
- **Android.** Run the same recipes with `--platform android`. Badges, the screen, the badge view (Back closes it), the welcome and celebrations all work there. Buddies runs on Android too (ADR 0020), so the buddy grid, reactions, the Buddies Settings switches and badge alerts work there once Buddies is set up.
- **Art.** Tools → Badges → "Show gallery" renders every medallion at every level and locked. "Celebrate …" shows any badge's card on demand. Use these for screenshots, not as proof of earning.
- **Proof.** Capture `wwv shot` at each surface you drove, then run `wwv errors`.

## Gotchas

- Only a level reached this month or last month celebrates. Backdated records two or more months old are filed as history, maybe with a summary. Expect a card only for recent activity.
- Every badge moment is a takeover (ADR 0021): it waits until nothing else is on screen and nothing holds the tabs (a sheet, popover, pushed screen, the profile overlay, the bell, the app in the background), then 0.7 s more. `__WW_DEV__.takeover.state()` shows why it's waiting. "Show badges" must be on.
- A celebration plays only within 15 s of the User's action. If it can't get its turn by then, or the app goes to the background, it expires and the Home card names the badge instead. A setup that writes stores directly (seeds, `addDayPlan` from eval) is not an action.
- One celebration per action: if the same save reaches a streak milestone and a badge, the streak plays and the badge goes to the Home card. A streak milestone that comes after its action's badge celebration started flares the chip instead.
- The check runs about 1.5 s after a change, and again when the app returns to the foreground. Use `wwv ad wait text` rather than an immediate snapshot.
- Closing a celebration clears the summary queued behind it, on purpose.
- Badges are never removed. Reseed or run `__WW_DEV__.badges.reset()` between recipes. After a reset, the next check is a first check again, so it shows the welcome (or, with `badgesWelcome` already `done`, the summary) rather than a celebration.
- A celebration goes before the welcome or summary; closing the welcome drops a queued summary.
- The badge view's coin is drawn over the content, and the wordmark is hidden from accessibility; assert on the description or the header label, not on "WitnessWork".
- Two by Two shows only with Buddies, unless it's already earned. One-time badges are hidden until earned. The `badges` seed has neither.
- Seeds are relative to today. Assert badge keys (`prepared.1`) rather than months. The `badges` seed always lands on exactly 14 badges.
- Refs from `wwv ad snapshot -i` go stale by the next `wwv` command, and chips repeat labels ("👏" on every badge row). Press by coordinates from `wwv ad snapshot --raw` (points; the iPhone is 375 wide, not the screenshot's pixels).
- Push delivery of "A buddy has a new badge" can't be verified here (Limits). Pushes are skipped on the local relay. Verify the bell entry and the Badge Alerts switch state instead.
- Medallion art, the flip and the celebration's motion are visual calls. Capture them, and report the taste as unverified.
