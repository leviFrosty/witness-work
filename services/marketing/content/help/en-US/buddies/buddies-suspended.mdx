id: buddiesSuspended
title: Why does Buddies say it's been turned off for me?
category: buddies
---

WitnessWork can turn off Buddies for someone after a report of abuse, such as harassing a buddy. While it's off, Buddies can't sync, send invites, or share Plans. A Plan or Follow-up you invite buddies to then isn't sent, now or later: the app tells you, and the Plan shows Not sent beside them (change it once Buddies is back on to send it). You can still remove buddies and delete your Buddies data (Buddies Settings → Delete My Buddies Data). Your buddies aren't notified. WitnessWork knows you only by an anonymous account number your device made, with no name, email, or phone number, and can't read anything you share with buddies. If you think this is a mistake, contact support under Still need help?, using the support button in this screen's header, and include your app version. If the report was wrong, we turn Buddies back on.
