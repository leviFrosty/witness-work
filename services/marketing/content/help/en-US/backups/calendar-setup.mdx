Calendar Sync adds your upcoming Follow-ups to a calendar on your iPhone or iPad. It's free. On Android, see [How does Calendar Sync work on Android?](/help/backups/calendar-android).

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings → Features → Calendar Sync**. During setup, or from the invitation in your notifications, tap **Add to Calendar** instead.
2. Allow full calendar access.
3. Create a WitnessWork calendar, or choose a calendar you use only for WitnessWork.

Your default calendar and calendars that live only on this device can't be chosen.

To use Google or Outlook, first create a calendar with that provider. Then add the account in iPhone **Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts** and turn on its calendars.

## Good to know

- Every upcoming Follow-up is added as a 30-minute event, including ones you saved earlier. Past Follow-ups and plans aren't added.
- Each event gets the same alert as its Follow-up's **Notify Me** reminder. If **Notify Me** is off, the event has no alert.
- Google may add its own alerts. Remove them under that calendar's Event notifications in Google Calendar settings.
- Updates need an internet connection and an iCloud sign-in, even for Google or Outlook calendars.
- Make changes in WitnessWork. Changes you make in your calendar app may be replaced.
- To stop adding Follow-ups, tap **Turn Off Calendar Sync**.
