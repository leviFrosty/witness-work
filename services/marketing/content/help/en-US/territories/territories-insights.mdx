Open the Territories tab. **Insights** at the top shows the day and the hour when people most often answered across all your territories. Each territory also has its own **Insights** card on its page, because an apartment area and a rural one can behave very differently. Tap a card to see three charts, each headed by what it suggests, such as "Saturday is your best day", with the time when the fewest people answered underneath:

- **Time of Day**: how often someone answered at each hour.
- **Day of the Week**: how often someone answered on each day.
- **Day and Time**: each day and hour compared with your average answer rate. The greener the square, the more often people answered then; the more orange, the less. Gray squares have fewer than 3 doors.

Touch a bar or a square, or drag across a chart, to see its answer rate and how many doors it's based on (and, for a square, how many percentage points above or below your average it is). With a mouse or trackpad, point at it instead.

When you have doors in more than one territory, **By Territory** at the bottom of Insights lists each one with its best day and time.

## Getting started

Insights need about 40 recorded doors, for all territories together and for each territory on its own. Until then the card says **Check back later** and shows how many more doors to record. When there are first enough, you'll get a notification in the notifications tray.

Every door recorded as Spoke or Not at Home counts. In a territory you share, that includes the doors everyone recorded, so you all see the same pattern. Recording a not-at-home again on another day counts both visits. A door you remove or undo isn't counted. Results you add from notes aren't counted, because notes don't say what time each door was visited.

Each house in a street's list also shows the day and time it was last recorded, so you can plan to try a not-at-home again at a different time.

## Keeping it current

Recent months count the most, so Insights follow changes such as the clocks going back. Older visits still count, so nothing you recorded is lost, and visits from the same time of year in earlier years count extra, since daylight and weather were alike. When most of the doors behind Insights are more than 3 months old, the summary says so.

## What's kept

Each house keeps the times of its recent visits with its record, so a territory's insights travel with it to your other devices and to the people you share it with. After you return a territory, Insights keep only how many doors were answered on each day and hour, never which house or street. Those counts stay on this device and aren't synced or included in backups. When territory privacy mode is on, no house records are kept, so nothing new is counted.
