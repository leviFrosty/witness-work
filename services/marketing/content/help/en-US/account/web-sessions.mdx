title: How long does a browser stay signed in, and how many can I use?
category: account
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

A browser stays signed in to your account while you use it, so WitnessWork on the web opens straight to your data. Its sign-in ends after 180 days without use, or when you sign out ([Signing out on the web](/help/account/web-sign-out)).

You can sign in up to 3 browsers at once. Browsers have their own limit, separate from your phones. If you sign in a fourth, the browser you used least recently is signed out and shows the sign-in screen; sign in again there to carry on.

To see and sign out your browsers and devices, go to Settings → Account → Signed-In Devices, on a phone or on the web. Browsers are listed by name and system, such as "Chrome on macOS". Tap one to sign it out. A browser you sign out from another device is erased the next time it connects to WitnessWork. Anything it hasn't synced is lost.

Each new sign-in, on a phone or in a browser, also shows in the bell on Home on your other devices and in Settings → Account → Account Activity, along with passkeys added and browsers approved from a phone. WitnessWork checks for them when you open it or click back into its tab (at most every 15 minutes) and when you open Settings → Account. Your own phones sign in again about every three months, so you'll see them here too. If a sign-in isn't yours, tap it in the bell to open Signed-In Devices and sign that browser or device out, then change your account key: see [What if my phone or computer is lost or stolen?](/help/account/lost-device).

Signing in counts as confirming it's you for a few minutes, and so does using your passkey. Right after you sign in, you can add or remove a passkey or view your recovery key without using one, even if none of your passkeys is on that computer. After that, WitnessWork on the web asks for one of your passkeys before sensitive actions ([What is a passkey?](/help/account/passkeys-overview)). If your account has no passkey yet, or none of yours is on that computer, choose Sign In Again and sign in with your recovery key or your phone. Sign in as the same account: another account's sign-in is refused, and nothing in the browser changes. You stay signed in while you do, your data stays in the browser, and you then have a few minutes again. Signing in again counts as a new sign-in, so if 3 browsers are already signed in, the one you used least recently may be signed out. To hide WitnessWork when you step away from a browser that stays signed in, turn on App Lock ([How do I lock WitnessWork?](/help/general/app-lock)).
