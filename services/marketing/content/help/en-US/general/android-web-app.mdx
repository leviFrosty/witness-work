Install WitnessWork from Chrome instead. It's the full app, free like everywhere else. It gets its own icon and opens in its own window, like any other app. WitnessWork isn't in Google Play in some countries right now, including the United States.

## Install it from Chrome

In the pictures, a red box shows what to tap.

1. On your Android phone, open **Chrome** and go to **app.witnesswork.app**. Wait for the **Sign In to WitnessWork** screen.
2. Tap Chrome's menu, the three dots at the top right. Then tap **Add to Home screen**. Some phones say **Install app** instead.

![Chrome's menu with Add to Home screen](android-web-app-1.webp)

3. Tap **Install**. If your phone asks again, tap **Add to home screen**.

![The Install app box with the Install button](android-web-app-2.webp)

4. Close the Chrome tab. Then find **WitnessWork** on your Home screen or in your list of apps, and tap it.

![The WitnessWork icon on the Home screen](android-web-app-3.webp)

5. WitnessWork opens in its own window. If you're new, tap **Create an Account**. If you already use WitnessWork, sign in. See [bring your records into the web app](/help/general/web-app-move-records).

![WitnessWork's sign-in screen in its own window](android-web-app-4.webp)

You may also see an **Install App** banner at the top of WitnessWork in Chrome. Tapping its **Install** button does the same as steps 2 and 3.

## Good to know

- Always open WitnessWork from its icon. Your records are kept on your phone, so it works without a connection, and they sync when you're back online.
- It updates by itself. When a new version is ready, tap **Reload**. See [getting the latest version](/help/general/web-updates).
- It can't send reminders or notifications on your phone. See [what the web app doesn't have](/help/general/web-app).
- Don't clear Chrome's data for app.witnesswork.app. That signs you out of WitnessWork on this phone. Changes that hadn't synced yet are lost.
- On iPhone or iPad? See [using WitnessWork without the App Store](/help/general/iphone-web-app).

## If something goes wrong

- **It says WitnessWork Is Open in Another Tab:** tap **Use Here**, then close WitnessWork in Chrome. It works in one place at a time.
- **There's no Add to Home screen or Install app in the menu:** see [Why can't I install WitnessWork on my phone?](/help/general/web-install-trouble).
