title: How do I add photos to a note?
category: notes
---

In the note editor, tap the photo button, then Take Photo or Choose Photos. A note holds up to 10 photos. To make one smaller or bigger, tap it while editing and drag a corner; it keeps that share of the note's width on every device. Photos are resized and saved on this device; tap one in a note to see it full screen or share it. With data protection mode on, visit notes can't hold photos, because a photo of someone's home is personal information about them. Photos reach your other devices only when Include photos is on in iCloud Sync (Google Drive Sync on Android); Cloud Sync doesn't carry photos yet. Backup files and contact shares leave them out. A Plan you invite buddies to shares its note's photos too, end-to-end encrypted; a buddy sees a placeholder until a photo finishes downloading, or while photo sharing is paused. Shared photos are deleted from WitnessWork's servers a month after the Plan, or right away when you remove them, delete the Plan, or stop sharing it.
