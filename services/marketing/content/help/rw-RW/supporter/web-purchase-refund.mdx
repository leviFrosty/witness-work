title: "Nshobora gusubizwa amafaranga y'ibyo naguze kuri murandasi?"
---

Ushobora kubisaba. Andikira [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) uvuye kuri aderesi wakoresheje igihe wishyuraga, utubwire hafi igihe wishyuriye.

## Byiza kumenya

- Dusuzuma buri busabe. Amafaranga yemewe asubira ku ikarita wishyuriyeho, kandi banki yawe ishobora gutwara iminsi myinshi kugira ngo ayagaragaze.
- Ntushobora kwisubiza amafaranga kuri paji y'ifatabuguzi.
- Gusubizwa amafaranga bihita bihagarika ibyo yafunguye. Gusubiza ubwishyu bwa nyuma bw'ifatabuguzi bihagarika uburenganzira bw'Umufasha. Gusubiza Tip bikuraho gushimirwa nk'utanga impano.
- Guhagarika ifatabuguzi si ugusubizwa amafaranga. Kugira ngo uhagarike ubwishyu buzaza, reba [Nacunga nte cyangwa nahagarika ifatabuguzi naguriye kuri murandasi?](/help/supporter/web-manage-subscription).
- Ibyaguzwe kuri App Store bisubizwa na Apple. Saba ukoresheje konti yawe ya App Store.
- Ku byaguzwe kuri Google Play, saba muri Google Play. Niba Google ikohereje kuri twe, andikira ubufasha.
