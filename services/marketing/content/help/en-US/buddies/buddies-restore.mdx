id: buddiesRestore
title: Can I recover my buddies on another device or from a backup?
category: buddies
---

Often yes. On iPhone and iPad your buddies follow you to other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. On Android they can return after a reinstall or on a new phone. Regular WitnessWork backup files don't include them.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Make sure iCloud Keychain is on (**Passwords** in your iCloud settings).
2. Open WitnessWork on the other device.
3. Open **Buddies** and pull down to refresh.

With sync on, your buddies, your answers, the nicknames and colors you chose, and what you've read in the notification bell appear on each device. Turn on sync so what your buddies see stays the same.

## Android

Your buddies come back when you reinstall WitnessWork on the same phone while Google backup is on (**Settings → Google → Backup**). With a screen lock set, they can also come back when you set up a new phone from that backup. Without a screen lock, they stay on that phone.

## Good to know

- Each Android phone, and an iPhone and an Android phone, have their own Buddies connections.
- On the web, Buddies comes through Cloud Sync after you sign in.
- Buddies see one name for you: the one you last changed in **Profile**.
- Keep your personal records backed up separately.

## If something goes wrong

- **Your buddies didn't come back:** reconnect with new invites. This also applies after **Delete My Buddies Data**.
