title: Can I plan more than one activity on the same day?
category: plans
related:
  - 547
---

Yes. A day can hold as many plans as you like, one-time, recurring, or both, like field service every Friday morning and a Bible study every Friday evening. Every plan on a day adds up: the day's planned time on the Schedule calendar, the time left to plan, and your projected total all count each one. To add another plan, tap the day on the Schedule tab, then tap + next to Plans. To skip a recurring plan on one day, open it, tap More (•••), then Delete → Delete This Plan; your other days stay the same. To change it for one day only, tap Edit and choose Only this date when you save. One-time plans used to replace a day's recurring plans. When you updated, days where that happened were set to skip the recurring plan, so your totals didn't change. If a day shows more time than you expect, tap it and delete the plan you don't need. Plans stay on your device, sync between your devices if you turned sync on, and only their day, time, and length are shared with your buddies if you use Buddies.
