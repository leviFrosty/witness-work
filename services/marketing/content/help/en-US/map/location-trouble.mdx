---
id: locationTrouble
title: Why can't the map find my current location?
category: map
---

Check that location services are on and WitnessWork has location permission in your device's settings. Reopen the map and try again where your device can obtain a location. You can still enter an address or place a contact's pin manually without using your current location. If the problem continues, report the error with your device and operating-system version, leaving private contact details out of screenshots.
