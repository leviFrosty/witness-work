title: What do I get as a supporter?
category: supporter
---

An active Supporter subscription gives you the Supporter badge, custom accent colors and Plan Today's Route, which puts the day's follow-ups and plans in the shortest driving order, and alternate app icons on iOS. Supporters also get generous Scribe AI limits on iOS and Android; without Supporter, Scribe AI has a smaller monthly limit (see How do Scribe AI limits work?). Core features remain free, including contact filters, sorting and saved views, and Buddies when available; Supporters can also choose each buddy's color. A one-time Tip gives Donor recognition but does not activate Supporter features. Thank you to everyone who supports WitnessWork: your support built Cloud Sync and WitnessWork on the web.
