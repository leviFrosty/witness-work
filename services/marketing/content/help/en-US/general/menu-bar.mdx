On an iPad, the menu bar at the top of the screen gives you quick ways to add a visit, add a contact, find a contact, and get help.

## Show the menu bar

1. Use Windowed Apps or Stage Manager on iPadOS 26 or later.
2. Swipe down from the top edge of the screen.

## What's in it

- **File:** Log Visit, Add Time (when you log hours), Add Contact, New Plan, and Backup & Restore.
- **Edit → Find Contact:** opens Contacts with the search field ready.
- **Help:** opens the Help Center, What's New, your email app for private support, and Check for Update.
- **WitnessWork Preferences:** opens WitnessWork's settings. The standard **Settings** item opens your iPad's settings.

## Good to know

- These items open the same screens as the app. Opening a form doesn't save anything.
- The menu bar isn't on iPhone or Android.

## If something goes wrong

- **Items are missing:** update WitnessWork from the App Store and open it again. You can still do the same things inside the app.
- **Still stuck:** use the Help Center's private email link, and keep contact details and backups out of public reports.
