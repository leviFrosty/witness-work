Buddies lets you connect with up to 50 people you go out in service with. You see each other's planned days, start times and lengths for the next eight weeks, and the badges each buddy shares. It's free, and works on iPhone, iPad and Android.

## Open Buddies

1. Open **Schedule**.
2. Tap **Buddies** under the **Schedule** title.
3. The first time, finish the introduction with your profile name.

A dot on it means something is waiting for you, such as a request to confirm. You can also open it from your **Profile**. To add someone, see [How do I add a buddy, and who confirms the request?](/help/buddies/buddies-add).

## On your calendar

- A buddy's photo or initial on a day means you're going out together.
- A small dot means other buddies plan to go out. A new buddy shows no dots until you turn on **Show on my calendar** for them under **Sharing & Alerts** on their page.
- Tap any day to see every buddy going out.
- On iPhone and iPad, the Calendar widget shows the same marks. Open the app if it looks behind.

## Good to know

- You can add a **Together** section to Home: tap **Edit Home Screen**, then **Add Sections**, then **Together**. It lists invitations waiting for you and what you've planned with buddies.
- If your buddies are missing, update WitnessWork on each of your devices.

## If something goes wrong

- **Buddies is missing from Schedule:** update WitnessWork.
- **Buddies is dimmed:** it's temporarily unavailable. Your saved buddies stay on your calendar. Try again later.
- **It says it can't verify this device:** see [Why does WitnessWork say it can't verify this device?](/help/account/account-verification).
