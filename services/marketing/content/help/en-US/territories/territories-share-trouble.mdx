Your buddy needs Territories in their app and needs to accept the invitation. Open the territory. Under **Shared With**, "Invited" beside their name means they haven't accepted yet.

## If your buddy doesn't see it

- **Their app doesn't have Territories yet.** They need to update WitnessWork from the App Store or Google Play. Territories is rolling out gradually. Your invitation waits for up to 7 days.
- **They haven't accepted.** The invitation is in the bell on Home, with **Join** and **Decline** (**Accept** for a handed-off street). Once they join, it's in their Territories tab under **Shared With Me** (or **Handed to Me**).
- **The invitation expired or they declined.** Tap **Stop** beside their name, then share the territory again.
- **They're looking on another device.** Shared territories show on each of their devices that have Buddies set up. Ask them to open WitnessWork there and wait a moment.

## If sharing doesn't work for you

The message says why:

- **"Set Up Buddies":** sharing with buddies and handing off need Buddies on this phone. Tap it to set up. See [What is WitnessWork Buddies?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview) Or send a guest link, which doesn't need Buddies. Sharing isn't on the web.
- **"Add a Buddy First":** you can share only with people you're paired with in Buddies. Invite them first, or send a guest link.
- **"Couldn't reach WitnessWork"** or **"Couldn't start sharing. Check your connection":** connect to the internet and try again.
- **"Couldn't start sharing. Update WitnessWork, then try again.":** install the update from the App Store or Google Play.
- **"Sharing territories is paused right now.":** try again later. Your territory stays on your phone.
- **"Only the territory's owner can do that.":** see [Why can't I edit or return a territory someone shared with me?](/help/territories/territories-shared-with-me)
- **"This territory is too large to share.":** see [What are the limits on sharing a territory?](/help/territories/territories-limits)
- **"Pick a street that isn't handed off.":** a handed-off street can't be shared again until it's back with you.
- **"Some changes to (territory) weren't shared. That street was handed off meanwhile.":** the street was handed off or taken back while you were offline, so your changes to it couldn't be saved.

If a problem continues, use the support button in this screen's header, look under **Still need help?**, and include your app version. Keep territory numbers, addresses, and names out of public bug reports.
