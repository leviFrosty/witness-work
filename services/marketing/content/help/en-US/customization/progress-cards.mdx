Each tab on Progress (Month, Year, and All-time) has its own cards under its summary, and you choose them separately. To change a tab's cards, press and hold any card; it lifts and the cards start to jiggle, and you can drag it straight away. You can also tap Edit Cards after your cards (before version 1.46.0, the grid button at the top did the same). While the cards jiggle, drag a card to move it, tap the gray minus at its top corner to remove it (tap Undo if you didn't mean to), and drag the curved handle at its bottom corner to make it small or wide; tapping the handle switches sizes too. Cards that come in only one size have no handle. Tap Add Cards at the top to see every card that tab offers. Month has Daily Pace, Studies, Days Out, Visits, Credit, Weeks, Projected Month, Service Year, Year Pace, Streak, Lifetime, and Days. Year has Service Year Pace, Needed a Month, Average, Best Month, Last Year, Days Out, Months Out, Studies, and Visits for the service year shown. All-time has Best Year, Average, Best Month, Days Out, Months Out, Studies, and Visits across all your records. The Days list on Month can show every day of the month or, when compact, only the latest few in a short list that scrolls on its own; drag its handle down or up to switch. To lift the Days list, hold its title rather than a day, since each day has its own menu. Tap Done, or tap the summary at the top, when you're finished. Each tab's summary (the month's report, the service year's total, or your lifetime hours) and the lists of months and years always stay. Some cards only appear when they apply: Daily Pace and Projected Month need a monthly goal, Year Pace, Service Year Pace, and Needed a Month need an annual goal, Credit needs a role whose report counts hours, hour cards on Year need a role that logs hours, Streak needs streaks turned on, and Mileage needs mileage tracking. With Reduce Motion on, the cards stay still while you edit. Removing a card never deletes your time or reports. Your cards are a preference, so they're included in backups and sync with your other devices when sync is on. To start over on a tab, tap Edit Cards there, then Reset Cards.
