id: territoriesPrivacy
title: Are my territories private, and what can WitnessWork's server see?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Yes. A territory you don't share stays on your device and in your own sync and backups, which are private (see [Are my territories synced and backed up?](/help/territories/territories-sync)).

**Shared territories are end-to-end encrypted.** WitnessWork's server stores only encrypted records, so it can't see the territory's number or name, street names, addresses, results, do-not-calls, or anyone's name, or who is on the territory. It sees only how many records there are, their sizes, and when they change. The photo of the card is never shared.

**Guest links:** the key that opens a guest link is part of the link itself and never reaches WitnessWork's server. WitnessWork keeps nothing about a guest except the name they type, which is encrypted with the territory's records. A guest's browser keeps the link's records until the link ends or they tap Forget This Link.

**What the people you share with see:** everything on the streets you share with them, including other people's doors, with the name of whoever recorded each one, and the do-not-calls.

**Keep only what you need.** A do-not-call keeps only the address and the date of the request; don't add a name or a reason. When you add results from notes, Scribe AI returns only house numbers, streets, and results (see [Does Scribe AI read my territory notes?](/help/territories/territories-notes-scribe)). For no house-by-house records at all, turn on territory privacy mode (see [What is territory privacy mode?](/help/territories/territories-privacy-mode)).
