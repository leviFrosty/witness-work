---
id: webSignUp
title: Can I create a WitnessWork account on the web without the phone app?
category: account
platforms: [web]
---

Yes. At [app.witnesswork.app](https://app.witnesswork.app), choose to create an account. You need no email address, phone number, or password.

1. Pass a short security check. WitnessWork uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated sign-ups, and Cloudflare sees your IP address and browser details for it.
2. Your browser makes your secret account key and asks you to create a passkey. Approve it with your device's lock (Face ID, fingerprint, Windows Hello, or PIN) or your password manager.
3. WitnessWork shows your recovery key. Save it now: download it, print it, or copy it into a password manager. You can't continue until you tap "I've saved my recovery key". If you reload before that, the page reopens on the recovery key.

Keep the recovery key private. Whoever has it can sign in to your account, and no one at WitnessWork can reset it for you. If your passkey can't unlock your account on its own, the recovery key screen says so, and the recovery key matters even more.

A web account has every WitnessWork feature, at the same limits as any other account. Sign-up limits how many accounts one connection can create, and it can be paused; the page says so when it is.

To move to a phone later, install WitnessWork on the phone, tap "I already use WitnessWork", and sign in with your passkey, with the Set Up Another Device code from a signed-in browser, or with your recovery key. See [How do I move to a new phone](/help/account/account-handoff). Your records follow through Cloud Sync.
