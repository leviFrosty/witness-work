title: How do I resize or hide the sidebar on my tablet?
category: customization
---

On a wide tablet window, you can drag the sidebar to make it narrower or wider, or hide it.

## Resize the sidebar

1. Find the small bar halfway down the sidebar's right edge.
2. Drag it left or right.

A narrow sidebar shows only icons. Widen it to see the names again.

## Hide the sidebar

1. Tap **Hide sidebar** beside WitnessWork at the top of the sidebar. If it's wide, narrow it first.
2. To bring it back, tap **Show sidebar** in the page header.

## Good to know

- WitnessWork remembers your width and whether it's hidden on this device. They aren't synced or backed up, and resizing doesn't change your records.
- The sidebar needs a window at least 1,000 points wide. In a narrower window, or with very large text, the bottom bar returns. Widen the window or turn your tablet sideways to use the sidebar again.
- At the bottom of the sidebar, tap your picture and name for Profile, Settings and Help Center.
- With a screen reader, adjust the **Resize sidebar** control to make it wider or narrower.

## If something goes wrong

- **The navigation seems missing:** restart the app. If it's still missing, use **Still need help?** in the Help Center, and keep contact details out of public reports.
