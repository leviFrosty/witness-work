title: Why can’t I speak to Scribe AI?
category: scribe
---

Voice needs your phone to understand speech in your app's language, and it needs microphone access. If voice isn't available, Scribe AI opens with the keyboard so you can type.

## Things to check

1. Open your phone's **Settings** and make sure **Microphone** is on for WitnessWork.
2. If another app is using the microphone, close it and try again.
3. The first time you speak in a language, wait a moment. Your phone may be downloading that language.

## Good to know

- On Android, voice needs Android 12 or later with speech recognition on your phone for your language.
- The web has no voice input, so Scribe AI always opens with the keyboard there.
- If a call or switching apps stops a recording, what Scribe AI heard waits in the message box. Finish it and tap **Send**.
