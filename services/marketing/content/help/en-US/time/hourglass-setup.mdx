title: How do I send my report to Hourglass or NW Publisher?
category: time
related:
  - 392
  - 399
  - 429
---

Open the month's Service Report, choose Hourglass or NW Publisher in the dropdown above Submit, and tap Submit. Review and send the report in the destination app. Copy and Share are also available. NW Publisher accepts only the previous month's report. Hourglass receives preaching time separately, with Credit Time included in the default report comment; if you replace the comment, check that it still says what you need. Update WitnessWork if a submission option is missing.
