title: Bushe WitnessWork yaba pa Android?
---

Ee. WitnessWork nabwelela pa Google Play, kabili ni ya bwebwe. Inshita ne malipoti, icakupimako inshita, abantu ba kulanshanya ne Kukonkapo, ubupange, mapu (na Google Maps), amakilomita, ukukumbusha, na mabackup fyonse fibomba pa Android. Buddies ibomba na pa Android, pamo na banyina abali pa iPhone. Aba Supporter kuti basunga ifibombelo fyabo ifya Android fililunganikanya na Google Drive Sync. Ifintu fimo fifwaya ifya Apple kabili fisala pa iPhone na iPad: iCloud Sync, widgets, Live Activities, ne fishibilo fya app. Ukuleta Amalembo na Scribe AI kulesendwa pa Android. Pa kuya pa foni ipya pa cibombelo conse, pangeni backup (Settings → Ukusunga no Kubwesha → Panga Backup), kopololeni failo ku foni ipya, elyo musale Ukufuma ku failo ya kusunga pa cipao ca kupanga ica “Tendekeni uko mwaleka”. Pakati ka fibombelo fya Android kuti mwasala na Ukufuma ku Google Drive uko. Sungeni amafailo ya backup inkama; yangakwata ifyebo fya bene ng'anda.
