id: territoriesDoNotCalls
title: How do I add or edit a do-not-call?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

The quickest way is at the door: tap Do Not Call in the door recorder and confirm. The house is added to the territory's do-not-calls with today's date.

To add one by hand, open the territory and tap Add Do Not Call under Do Not Call. Pick the street (or Another place), enter the house number or address, and tap Save Do Not Call. Each street's page has the same list for that street, under Do Not Call on This Street.

**Keep only the address and the date of the request.** Don't add a name or a reason. On streets without house numbers, a description of the place, such as "Blue gate at the corner", or a pin on the map stands in for the address.

**To edit one,** tap it in the list to open Edit Do Not Call. You can change the address, the Date of Request (the day the person asked not to be called, which elders use to decide when an old do-not-call should be checked again), or the pin, or tap Remove Do Not Call.

**Do-not-calls stay with the territory.** Returning the territory keeps them, with their address or pin and date, and so does territory privacy mode. On a returned territory, tap Copy List to pass them on with the card. People you share the territory with see the do-not-calls on the streets you share with them.
