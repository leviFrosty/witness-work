id: timerRounding
title: Mulandu nshi icakupimako inshita candi cakumene ku mineti 5 iyapepi?
---

Icakupimako inshita tacilakumana nakabili. Ilyo mwacipwisha, WitnessWork isunga inshita ine iyabela pa stopwatch.

Nga mucili mumona ukukumana kwa mineti 5, kushani WitnessWork ku nshita ya kulekelesha.
