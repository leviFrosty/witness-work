title: Mulandu nshi Apple Watch yandi ilanda Cilelunganikanya na iPhone?
---

Wachi yenu ilanga **Cilelunganikanya na iPhone…** ilyo inshita mwalundako pa wachi icili mu nshila ukuya ku iPhone yenu. Ipwa ilyo iPhone yenu yaba pepi elyo mwasula WitnessWork pa yo.

## Ico mwingacita

1. Sungeni iPhone yenu pepi elyo yaisulwa.
2. Isuleni WitnessWork pa iPhone yenu.
3. Isuleni WitnessWork pa wachi yenu.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ubwingi bwa pa wachi bwakwata inshita ipya pa nshita imo. Credit Time ilundwa ilyo iPhone yenu yaisunga, pantu iPhone yenu e ibika umupaka wenu wa Credit wa mweshi.
- Icintu conse cisunga ubushiku mwacilundeko, elyo cisungwa umuku umo fye.
- Ukutendeka, ukuleka no kupwisha icakupimako inshita fifwaya iPhone yenu pepi.
- Pa numa ya kutendeka nakabili kwa iPhone yenu, isuleni umuku umo pa ntanshi ya kusync.

## Nga cabipa

- **Wachi ilanda ukuti icakupimako inshita cacinja pa iPhone yenu:** moneni inshita, elyo mueseko nakabili.
- **Wachi ilanda ukuti iPhone yenu tayasungile icintu:** inshita tayalundwa. Eseni nakabili.
- **Wachi ilanda ukuti mukushe WitnessWork pa iPhone yenu:** ikushani, elyo mueseko nakabili.
- **Wachi yashala iyakale, nelyo ama complication yalanga akalongo:** konkeni amafunde atatu ya pamulu.
- **Ichili bwino:** bomfyeni link ya support ya fyasuma ya Help Center, elyo mwisabika ifyo mwalemba mu fyebo fya bwafya fya bantu bonse.
