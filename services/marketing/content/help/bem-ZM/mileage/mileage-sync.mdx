title: Bushe amakilomita yandi yasync, elyo yabomba pa Android?
---

Ee. Amakilomita yabomba pa iPhone na Android, elyo ama motoka yenu, mafuta, imitengo ne fyalweendo fisync pa fibombelo fyenu ilyo sync yayaka.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Sync yaba kuli bonse. Isaleni mu **Ifyo Mwasala → Sync**: Cloud Sync (ibomba na pa iPhone, Android, ne web), nelyo iCloud (Google Drive pa Android).
- Amakilomita yaba mu WitnessWork Backup (**Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti**) na mu backups ukufuma ku **Ifyo Mwasala → Ukusunga & Ukubwekeshepo**.
- Amakilomita taboneka pa kalenda yenu nelyo Service Report.
- Tayasabankanywa na Buddies, ukwabula motoka mwasala ukusabankanya. Moneni [Ama motoka yasabankanywa ni nshi, elyo nshi nshila ya kusabankanya motoka na bakwe?](/help/mileage/shared-cars).
- Motoka iyasabankanywa yaluka pa lwayo elyo tayaba mu sync nelyo backups. Moneni [Bushe ama motoka yasabankanywa yaboneka pa fibombelo fyandi fimbi, wachi, na mu backups?](/help/mileage/shared-cars-devices).
