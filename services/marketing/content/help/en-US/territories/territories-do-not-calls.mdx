The quickest way is at the door: tap **Do Not Call** in the door recorder and confirm. The house is added with today's date.

## Add one by hand

1. Open the territory and tap **Add Do Not Call** under **Do Not Call**.
2. Pick the street, or **Another place**.
3. Enter the house number or address.
4. Tap **Save Do Not Call**.

Each street's page lists its own under **Do Not Call on This Street**.

## Change or remove one

1. Tap it in the list to open **Edit Do Not Call**.
2. Change the address, the **Date of Request**, or the pin. Or tap **Remove Do Not Call**.

The **Date of Request** is the day the person asked not to be called. Elders use it to decide when to check an old do-not-call again.

## Good to know

- Keep only the address and the date. Don't add a name or a reason.
- On streets without house numbers, describe the place instead, such as "Blue gate at the corner", or use a pin.
- Do-not-calls stay with the territory when you return it. On a returned territory, tap **Copy List** to pass them on with the card.
- People you share the territory with see the do-not-calls on the streets you share with them.
