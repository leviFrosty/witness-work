id: reportBug
title: How do I report a bug or request a feature?
category: general
---

Use the buttons below to report a bug or request a feature. Include the steps to reproduce it, what you expected, your device and operating-system version, and the WitnessWork version shown under Still need help?. Remove private contact details, notes, account IDs, and Buddies Codes or invite links from public reports and screenshots. For backup or other sensitive problems, use the private email link in Still need help? instead. Source code, the Privacy Policy, and Terms of Use are under Settings → About.
