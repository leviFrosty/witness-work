title: Bushe Cloud Sync iba mu fyasuma ukucila iCloud nelyo Google Drive sync?
---

Cloud Sync iba mu fyasuma sana. Ifyo mwalemba ne fikope fipingwa pa cibombelo cenu pa ntanshi ya kutumwa, kanshi WitnessWork tingabelenga. Ifibombelo fyenu fye e fingabelenga.

## Ukulingana na iCloud na Google Drive

- **iCloud:** kopi yenu isungwa mu iCloud Drive yenu. Apple iyisunga, elyo iipinga bwino nga mwawasha Advanced Data Protection. WitnessWork tailundako cipingo cayo.
- **Google Drive:** kopi yenu isungwa mu folda ifisiwa mu Google Drive yenu. Google iyisunga. WitnessWork tailundako cipingo cayo.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Na Cloud Sync, server imfwa fye ifintu nga ifibombelo ifisync ne bukulu bwa kopi. Tailamona ico ikwata.
- Nga mwaluba ifibombelo fyonse ne cisokoshi ca kubwesha, takuli uungabelenga kopi sha Cloud Sync, ne ifwe.
