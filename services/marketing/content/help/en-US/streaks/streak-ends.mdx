---
id: streakEnds
title: When does my streak end, and how do I keep it?
category: streaks
---

A planned day can still be logged until the end of the next day. If that passes with no time logged for it, your streak ends. When yours is about to end, the flame on Home shows the time left, and a Streak Reminder comes at 6:00 PM on the last day. For publishers, a month can be recorded until the end of the next month, and the reminder comes three days before that month is over. If you can't go out on a planned day, remove that day's plans ahead of time: tap each one on Schedule, tap More (•••), then Delete → Delete This Plan. For a recurring plan, only that date is removed. The day stays planned while any plan is left on it. If you went out but forgot to log it, add the time on that day and your streak comes back. Turn Streak Reminders off in Settings → Preferences → Streaks & Badges. They need notifications allowed for WitnessWork.
