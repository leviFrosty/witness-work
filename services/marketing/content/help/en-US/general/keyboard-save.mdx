title: Can I save a form with a keyboard shortcut?
category: general
platforms:
  - ios
  - web
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Yes. While you're typing in a form, press Command-Return on a Mac or iPad keyboard, or Ctrl+Enter on Windows, Linux, and ChromeOS. It does what the form's main button does: **Save**, **Add**, **Done**, **Apply**, **Continue**, or **Send** in Scribe AI.

## Good to know

- If the button is dimmed, for example because a required field is empty, the shortcut does nothing.
- In the note editor, the shortcut closes the editor and keeps your text, like **Done**. It doesn't save the visit, time entry, plan, or trip underneath. Press it again there.
- Return alone still starts a new line.
- In a sheet or pop-up, the shortcut presses that sheet's button, not the screen behind it.
- It never deletes, removes, signs out, or restores anything, and it never answers a confirmation.
- On iPhone and iPad it needs a hardware keyboard, with the cursor in a text field. On the web it works in every form.

## If something goes wrong

- **Nothing happens:** check that the cursor is in a field of the form and that the main button isn't dimmed. You can always tap the button instead.
- **Still stuck:** use the Help Center's private support link, and keep contact details out of public reports.
