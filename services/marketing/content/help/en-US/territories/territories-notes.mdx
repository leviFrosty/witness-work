---
id: territoriesNotes
title: How do I add results from notes my partner wrote or texted?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Open the territory (or one of its streets) and tap Add Results From Notes. Type or paste the notes, tap Read With Scribe AI (or Read Notes), check what was read, and tap Add Entries.

1. **Add the notes.** Type them, or paste what a partner wrote down or texted you. Put a street's name on its own line above its houses, then one house and what happened per line, like "12 NH" or "14 spoke". A range like 12-20 covers each house in between. On iPhone and iPad, tap Scan Notes to copy handwritten notes from the camera. On Android, copy the text from a photo with Google Lens, then paste it.
2. **Read them.** With Scribe AI on, it reads notes in your own words and shorthand, in any language (see [Does Scribe AI read my territory notes?](/help/territories/territories-notes-scribe)). Otherwise WitnessWork reads them on your device, which understands common English shorthand only: NH or not home, spoke, DNC or do not call.
3. **Check each entry.** Entries are grouped by street. You can fix a house number, change a result, or remove an entry, and tap Choose Street for entries that don't have one. Lines that couldn't be read are listed under Couldn't Read: tap Add as Entry to fix one by hand, or dismiss it. If a house was already recorded, the entry says whether saving replaces that result or adds another try.
4. **Say when and who.** Set When Were These Worked?, and under Who Worked These? choose Me or Someone Else and type their name. A name you type is kept only with this territory.
5. Tap Add Entries (for example, Add 6 Entries). Nothing is saved until you do. Do-not-calls in the notes are added to the territory's list.

Add Results From Notes is hidden on returned territories and while territory privacy mode is on, because no house-by-house records are kept then.
