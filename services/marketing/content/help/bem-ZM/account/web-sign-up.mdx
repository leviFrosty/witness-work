title: Bushe kuti napanga akaunti ya WitnessWork pa web ukwabula app ya pa foni?
---

Ee. Mufwaya browser iingasunga passkeys. Tamufwaya adiresi ya email, inambala ya foni nelyo password.

## Pangeni akaunti

1. Isuleni app.witnesswork.app elyo musale **Create an Account**.
2. Lolelani security check ipite, elyo musale **Create Account**.
3. Sungeni passkey browser yenu ipeela, iyitwa "WitnessWork" na code ya mashiwi 4. Tinikisheni na Face ID, ukunwe, nelyo icipungo ca cikope. Kuti yamwipusha imiku ibili.
4. Sungeni icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha na **Download**, **Print** nelyo **Copy**. Cisungeni pa cifulo ca cisuma.
5. Saleni **I've Saved My Recovery Key**.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Sungeni icisokoshi ca kubwesha nomba. Web tayicilanga nakabili pa numa. Nga mwaloba passkey yenu, e nshila yeka ya kwingila nakabili, elyo takuli uwa ku WitnessWork uwingacipanga nakabili. Moneni [Icisokoshi candi ca kubwesha ni nshi, elyo nshi nshila ya kucisunga bwino?](/help/account/account-recovery-key).
- Ukulingana na browser yenu na password manager, passkey yenu kuti yaba ya sign-in only. Kanshi pa kompyuta imbi mukafwaya na icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha nelyo foni iyaingila. Moneni [Passkey ni nshi?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
- Security check ibomfiwa na Cloudflare, iyamona adiresi yenu ya intaneti ne fyo browser yenu ilanda.
- Ifyo mwalemba fyashala mu browser iyi elyo fisync na Cloud Sync.
- Ukubomfya akaunti pa foni, lembeni icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha uko. Moneni [Nshi nshila ya kubomfya icisokoshi candi ca kubwesha pa foni ipya?](/help/account/account-recovery-key-use).
- Tamwingapanga akaunti mu browser iyacili ne data ya akaunti imbi. Ingileni mu akaunti iyo.

## Nga cabipa

- **Mwapwisha passkey prompt:** takuli akaunti iyapangwa. Saleni **Create Account** nakabili.
- **Security check tayapwile:** saleni **Try Again**. Nga yacili yabipa, zimyeni extensions ishifintwila ifintu, nelyo mwesheko browser imbi.
- **Tayafikilike kuli WitnessWork, nelyo muli offline:** moneni intaneti yenu.
- **Akaunti ilepangwa mu tab imbi:** ipwisheni uko, nelyo mwiipwishe elyo mwesheko nakabili.
- **Sign-Up Is Paused:** esheni pa numa, nelyo mupange akaunti mu app ya pa foni elyo mwingile pa web.
- **Too many new accounts from this network:** lolelani inshita ilangwa, elyo mwesheko nakabili.
- **Finish Creating Your Account:** saleni **Finish with Passkey** elyo musale passkey iyo ilanda. Akaunti yenu kuti yaliko kale, kanshi e nshila yeka ya kwingila mu browser iyi.
- **Mwafumyapo passkey ilyo shamwapwisha:** saleni **I Deleted That Passkey**. Sungeni icisokoshi ca kubwesha ico ilanga, elyo mwingile na co.
- **This Browser Holds an Account Key:** sungeni key iyo ilanga, elyo mwingile na yo nelyo na passkey ya akaunti iyo.
- **Browser yenu tayasunga akaunti yenu:** fumeni mu private browsing, nelyo muleshe site iyi ukusunga data, elyo mwesheko nakabili.

Ku fintu fimbi, bomfyeni link ya support mu Help Center.
