WitnessWork iboneka mu ndimi 19. Ikurikiza ururimi rwa telefoni yawe keretse uhisemo urundi.

## Indimi

Icyarabu, Igibemba, Igishinwa (cyoroheje n'ikinyakera), Igiholandi, Icyongereza, Igifaransa, Ikidage, Igitaliyani, Ikiyapani, Ikinyarwanda, Igikoreya, Igiporutugali (Burezili na Porutugali), Ikirusiya, Icyesipanyoli, Igiswahili, Ikinyaukereniya n'Ikivietinamu. Niba ururimi rwawe ruri hanze y'uru rutonde, WitnessWork yerekana Icyongereza.

## Hindura ururimi

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere**.
2. Kanda **Ururimi** hejuru uhitemo rumwe.

WitnessWork yongera gutangira muri urwo rurimi. **Igikoresho gisanzwe** gikurikiza telefoni yawe. Ururimi uhisemo muri WitnessWork ruruta igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe.

Ushobora no gushyira ururimi rwa WitnessWork yonyine mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe. Kuri iPhone na iPad, fungura **Igenamiterere → Porogaramu → WitnessWork → Ururimi**. Rigaragara iyo telefoni yawe ifite indimi zirenze imwe ukunda. Kuri Android 13 cyangwa irenga, fungura **Igenamiterere → Porogaramu → WitnessWork → Ururimi**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Inyandiko zimwe ziva kuri telefoni yawe, bityo zikurikiza ururimi rwayo: utubuto tw'imenyesha, ibyo guhitamo itariki, ibyo gusangiza n'urutonde rwa widgets.
- Kuri iPhone na iPad, ibisobanuro biri mu masaba y'uruhushya, nk'ahantu cyangwa kamera, buri gihe biba biri mu Cyongereza.
- Ubuhinduzi bukorwa na AI, bityo ijambo cyangwa interuro bishobora kudahuza, cyane cyane amagambo Abahamya ba Yehova bakoresha mu buryo bwihariye.

## Menyesha ikosa mu guhindura

Koresha ihuza ry'imeri riri munsi ya **Uracyakeneye ubufasha?** ushyiremo ururimi rwawe, ecran, inyandiko ubona n'ibyo yagombye kuvuga. Ifoto ya ecran irafasha. Banza upfuke amazina, aderesi n'inyandiko. Ibikosorwa bizaza mu ivugurura rizakurikira.
