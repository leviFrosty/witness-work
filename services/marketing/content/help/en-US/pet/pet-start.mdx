title: How do I get a pet, and how do I turn it off?
category: pet

The Pet Companion is optional. You adopt one from **Home**, and you can hide it at any time.

## Adopt a pet

1. On **Home**, find the **Meet your companion** card.
2. Tap **Choose a pet**.
3. Pick a lion cub, pup, kitten, donkey, or lamb.
4. Type a name if you like.
5. Tap **Adopt**.

A short intro then shows how hearts fill, how going out earns coins for hats and room items, and how to decorate. Tap **Next**, or close it to skip. You have one companion at a time.

## Bring back the offer

If you chose **Not now**, touch and hold an empty space on **Home**, tap **Add Sections**, then **Pet**. Or turn on **Pet** in **Settings → Features → Home Screen**.

## Hide your pet

- Touch and hold the pet's card, choose **Edit Home Screen**, and tap the gray minus on it.
- Or turn off **Show on Home** in your pet's settings (the settings button at the top right of its details).
- Or turn off **Pet** in **Settings → Features → Home Screen**.

Hiding keeps your pet, its room and its keepsakes. Turn **Pet** back on and everything returns.
