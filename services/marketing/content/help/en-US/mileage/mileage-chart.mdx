id: mileageChart
title: What does the chart on the Mileage screen show?
category: mileage
---

The chart shows how far you drove each day, or each month of the service year (September through August).

## See the chart

1. Open **Mileage**.
2. Choose **Week**, **Month**, or **Year**.
3. Tap a bar to see that day's or month's trips.

## Good to know

- With more than one car, each bar is split by car, using the colors next to each car's total.
- The **Day** view and periods without trips show no chart.
- Bars show distance, not estimated cost.
