title: How do I add photos to a note?
category: notes
---

In the note editor, tap the photo button, then **Take Photo** or **Choose Photos**. A note holds up to 10 photos.

On the web, you can also paste an image you copied or drag an image file onto the note.

## Change a photo's size

1. While editing the note, tap the photo.
2. Drag a corner to make it smaller or bigger.

## View or share a photo

Tap a photo in a note to see it full screen or share it.

## Good to know

- Photos are resized and saved on your device.
- On the web, text you paste from a page keeps its words but not its pictures. Copy the picture itself to add it. If the browser can't open a file, use a JPEG or PNG.
- A trip on a shared car has no photo button. Its note is shared with everyone on the car, but photos aren't.
- With data protection mode on, visit notes can't hold photos, because a photo of someone's home is personal information.
- To see photos on your other devices, including the web, use Cloud Sync, which sends them on its own, or turn on **Include images in sync** for iCloud (Google Drive on Android). See [Why is a photo missing from a note?](/help/notes/notes-sync).
- Backup files and contact shares leave photos out.
- A Plan you invite buddies to shares its note's photos too. A buddy sees a placeholder until a photo finishes downloading. Shared photos are deleted a month after the Plan, or right away when you remove them, delete the Plan, or stop sharing it.
