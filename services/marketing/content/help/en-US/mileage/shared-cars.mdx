A shared car keeps one mileage history for a car you drive with others, such as your spouse or family. Everyone on the car can log, edit, and delete its trips, and totals count everyone's trips. Shared cars are free and work on iPhone, iPad, and Android.

You need Buddies set up with at least one buddy. See [What is WitnessWork Buddies, and why don't I see it?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview).

## Share a car

1. Open **Mileage**, then tap the gear to open **Mileage Settings**.
2. Tap the car.
3. Under **People**, tap **Share Car…**. It shows **Sharing…** while it moves the car and its history.
4. On the next sheet, tap **Invite** beside each buddy you want on the car.

Each buddy gets the invitation in the bell on Home, with a notification, and has 7 days to join. You're told when someone joins.

## Good to know

- You share one car at a time. Your other cars stay your own.
- A car holds up to 10 people, counting open invitations. Anyone on it can invite their own buddies.
- To see what each person can do, see [Who can do what on a shared car?](/help/mileage/shared-cars-people).

## If something goes wrong

- **You have no buddies yet:** the sheet says so. Invite someone to Buddies first, then share the car.
