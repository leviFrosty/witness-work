title: "Nasangiza nte WitnessWork incuti yanjye?"
---

Fungura **Igenamiterere → Sangiza WitnessWork** werekane incuti yawe kode cyangwa umwoherereze ihuza. WitnessWork ni ubuntu kuri iPhone na Android.

## Sangiza ukoresheje kode

1. Kanda ifoto y'umwirondoro wawe, hanyuma **Igenamiterere → Sangiza WitnessWork**.
2. Hitamo **Android** cyangwa **iPhone**.
3. Incuti yawe isikana kode na kamera ya telefoni yayo kugira ngo ifungure WitnessWork muri Google Play cyangwa App Store.

## Sangiza ukoresheje ihuza

Kanda **Sangiza ihuza**. Ubutumwa burimo amahuza yombi y'amaduka, bityo bukora kuri buri wese.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ubutumwa burimo amahuza y'amaduka gusa. Nta kintu bivuga kuri wowe cyangwa amakuru yawe.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Kode ntisomwa:** zamura umucyo wa ecran yawe, cyangwa ukoreshe **Sangiza ihuza** aho.
