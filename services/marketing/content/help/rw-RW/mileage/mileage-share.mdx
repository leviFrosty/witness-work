title: "Nsangiza nte cyangwa nsohora nte raporo yanjye y'ibirometero?"
---

Fungura **Ibirometero**, hitamo igihe, hanyuma ukande akabuto ko gusangiza kari hejuru. Ushobora gukopora raporo, kuyisangiza nk'inyandiko, cyangwa kuyisohora ku rupapuro rw'ibarura.

## Sangiza raporo

1. Fungura **Ibirometero**.
2. Hitamo **Umunsi**, **Icyumweru**, **Ukwezi** cyangwa **Umwaka**, ukoreshe imyambi cyangwa gukurura kugira ngo uhitemo igihe.
3. Kanda akabuto ko gusangiza kari hejuru.
4. Hitamo **Kopi**, uyisangize nk'inyandiko, cyangwa uhitemo **Sohora CSV**.

Kugira ngo usangize urugendo rumwe, rufungure ukande akabuto karwo ko gusangiza.

## Byiza kumenya

- Inyandiko ifite ibiteranyo, ibiteranyo bya buri modoka iyo ufite imodoka zirenze imwe, na buri rugendo (keretse mu buryo bwa **Umwaka**).
- Dosiye y'urupapuro rw'ibarura ifite umurongo umwe kuri buri rugendo, hamwe n'imodoka, ibipimo by'ibirometero, intera, inyandiko, lisansi, ingano ya lisansi, igiciro n'igiciro giteganyijwe.
- Inyandiko z'ingendo zirimo, bityo zirebe mbere yo kohereza.
- Umwaka ni umwaka w'umurimo, kuva Nzeri kugeza Kanama.
