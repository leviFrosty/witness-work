title: How do I share a territory with someone who doesn't have the app?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
platforms: [ios, android]
---

Make a guest link: open the territory, tap Share, choose Whole Territory or the streets to share, and tap A Link or QR Code. Send the link, or let them scan the QR code. It opens in their web browser, with no app and no sign-in, and works for 24 hours.

You need a connection to make a link. You don't need Buddies, and the person you send it to doesn't need the app or an account. The page shows them your profile name (your Buddies name, if you use Buddies) beside the territory; with no name set, it shows only the territory.

**What they see and do:** they type their name once, so the group sees who recorded each door, then tap Start Recording. They can record doors as Not at Home, Spoke, or Do Not Call, mark streets worked, and see everything on the link's streets, including do-not-calls and doors others recorded, until the link ends. They see only the link's streets, never your other streets or the card's photo. If they lose the connection, their browser keeps what they record and sends it when they're back online. With territory privacy mode on, the link lets them only mark streets worked and note do-not-calls.

**On your side:** the link screen shows the QR code, Copy, Share, who has joined with the link, and how long it has left. Preview What Guests See shows the page as a guest sees it, and nothing you do there is saved. The link is also listed under Shared With on the territory's page; tap it to show the QR code again. Your other iPhones, iPads, and Android devices that sync with this one show the territory with the link and what guests record, and can show the QR code or end the link too.

**Ending a link:** it ends by itself after 24 hours and can't be extended; make a new one if they're still working. To end it sooner, open it and tap End Link Now. Doors they already recorded stay. Returning the territory ends all its links, and taking back a handed-off street ends the links the buddy made for it.

A link covers up to 30 streets, and a territory can have 10 open links at a time. Handed-off streets aren't included in your links. The link holds a secret key that WitnessWork's server never sees, so share it only with people you want working the territory.
