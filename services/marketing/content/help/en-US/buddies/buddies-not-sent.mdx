---
id: buddiesNotSent
title: Why does my answer say Sending in or Not sent yet, or my request say Waiting to Send?
category: buddies
---

Your answer or request hasn't reached your buddy yet, usually because your phone is offline. You don't need to answer or ask again. WitnessWork sends it when you're connected.

## Send it sooner

1. Connect to the internet.
2. Open the Plan or Follow-up and tap **Try Again**. Or open **Buddies** and pull down to refresh.

## Good to know

- **Going** is sent right away.
- **Can't Make It** waits 15 seconds in case you tapped it by mistake. Tap **Change** to fix it. Leaving the app sends it right away.
- **Not sent yet** or **Waiting to Send** means it's saved on your phone. WitnessWork also tries again shortly after, while the app is open.
- Your buddy won't see it until it's sent.
