title: Ese igihe cyanjye cy'ukwezi gukurikira?
---

Yego. Igice cy'amasaha ari munsi y'intego yawe ya buri kwezi gishobora kwiyongera kugeza mu kwezi gutaha. Fungura Preferences → Publisher hanyuma uhindure amahitamo ya rollover. Amasaha yatwawe agaragara mu buryo bwikora iyo ukwezi gutangiye, bityo ntukeneye gukuramo intoki cyangwa kongera kwinjiza ikintu icyo ari cyo cyose.
