title: "Inkurikirane zikora zite?"
---

Urukurikirane rwawe rubara uko ukomeza neza gahunda utegura. Rugaragara Ahabanza no mu Mwirondoro wawe.

## Uko rukura

- **Niba wandika amasaha:** buri munsi wateganyijwe wandikaho igihe icyo ari cyo cyose wongeraho rimwe, nubwo wandika igihe gito kurusha icyo wari uteganyije.
- **Iminsi nta gahunda** ntiyongera urukurikirane cyangwa ngo irurangize, bityo tegura iminsi uteganya kujya mu murimo.
- **Ku Babwiriza:** buri kwezi wagize uruhare mu murimo wongeraho rimwe, waba ukuranga Ahabanza cyangwa wandika igihe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Iyo kwandika umunsi bigeze ku ntambwe nka 3, 5 cyangwa 10, hacurangwa ishimwe rigufi. Indi minsi, n'igihe cyanditswe ku isaha yawe cyangwa ku kindi gikoresho, bituma ikibatsi Ahabanza kirabagirana.
- Kanda ikibatsi Ahabanza kugira ngo ubone igihe urukurikirane rwawe rwatangiriye, urukurikirane rwawe rwiza kurusha izindi, n'inkurikirane bagenzi bawe bagusangiza.
- Iyo rugiye kurangira, rukande kugira ngo wandike umunsi ugezweho. Reba [Urukurikirane rwanjye rurangira ryari, kandi mbikomeza nte?](/help/streaks/streak-ends)
- Buri ntambwe urukurikirane rwawe rugezeho ruha itungo ryawe amacoin 100 yo gukoresha mu Iduka (reba [igisubizo cy'iduka ry'itungo](/help/pet/pet-shop)), mu gihe **Erekana urukurikirane** ifunguye. Intambwe zagezweho mbere y'uko wakira itungo ntizibarwa.
- Kugira ngo uhishe urukurikirane rwawe, reba [Nazimya inkurikirane?](/help/streaks/streaks-hide)
