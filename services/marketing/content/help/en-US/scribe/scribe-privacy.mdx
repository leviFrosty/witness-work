title: What does Scribe AI send, and what’s kept?
category: scribe
---

Scribe AI sends only what it needs to understand your message, and WitnessWork doesn't keep your messages. Nothing you send is stored or used to train AI.

## What it sends

- The text of your conversation. Your voice is turned into text on your phone, and recordings are never saved or uploaded.
- A few details to help it understand you: today's date, your goal, your upcoming plans and your contacts' names.
- On phones, photos of paper pages, shrunk on your phone with their location removed. The app deletes its copy of each page once it's read or skipped, or when you close Scribe AI or start a new conversation. Your original photos stay in your library.

With data protection mode on, Scribe AI doesn't send your contact list or visit notes.

## What's kept

- Your conversations stay on this device. They aren't synced or backed up. Scribe AI keeps your 50 most recent conversations, each for 90 days after you last used it.
- The records you add sync like any others.
- WitnessWork only records how much you used.

## On the web

You type; there's no voice or photo input. Conversations are saved only in that browser and are erased when you sign out of it.

Your messages reach the AI through OpenRouter, under an agreement that they aren't stored. Its policy is in **Settings → About**.
