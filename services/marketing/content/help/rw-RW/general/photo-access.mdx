id: photoAccess
title: "Kuki mbona amwe mu mafoto yanjye gusa?"
---

Igihe iPhone cyangwa iPad yawe yabazaga ku mafoto, wahisemo **Select Photos**, bityo WitnessWork ibona gusa amafoto wayisangije.

## Sangiza andi mafoto

1. Muri WitnessWork, fungura ihitamo ry'amafoto, urugero uvuye ku ifoto y'umuntu cyangwa ku nyandiko.
2. Kanda **Manage** hejuru y'amafoto.
3. Hitamo amafoto ushaka, cyangwa ukureho ayo wifuza kugumana mu ibanga.
4. Emeza. Ahita agaragara.

## Reka WitnessWork ibone amafoto yawe yose

1. Fungura porogaramu ya **Igenamiterere** ya iPhone yawe.
2. Jya kuri **Porogaramu → WitnessWork → Amafoto**.
3. Hitamo **Full Access**.

## Byiza kumenya

- WitnessWork ikopora gusa amafoto wahisemo. Nta kindi isoma mu bubiko bw'amafoto yawe.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ifoto ntifunguka:** ahari iracyakururwa muri iCloud. Reba murandasi yawe wongere ugerageze.
