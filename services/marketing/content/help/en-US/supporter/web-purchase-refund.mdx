You can ask for one. Email [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) from the address you used at checkout, and tell us roughly when you paid.

## Good to know

- We review each request. An approved refund goes back to the card you paid with, and your bank may take several days to show it.
- You can't refund yourself from the subscription page.
- A refund ends what it unlocked right away. Refunding a subscription's latest payment ends that Supporter access. Refunding a Tip removes its Donor recognition.
- Cancelling a subscription isn't a refund. To stop future payments, see [How do I manage or cancel a subscription I bought on the web?](/help/supporter/web-manage-subscription).
- App Store purchases are refunded by Apple. Ask through your App Store account.
- For Google Play purchases, ask in Google Play. If Google sends you to us, email support.
