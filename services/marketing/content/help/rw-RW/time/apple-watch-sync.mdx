title: "Kuki Apple Watch yanjye ivuga Guhuza na iPhone?"
---

Isaha yawe yerekana **Guhuza na iPhone** mu gihe igihe wongeyeho ku isaha kikiri mu nzira ijya kuri iPhone yawe. Birangira iyo iPhone yawe iri hafi kandi wafunguye WitnessWork kuri yo.

## Icyo gukora

1. Gumana iPhone yawe hafi kandi idafunze.
2. Fungura WitnessWork kuri iPhone yawe.
3. Fungura WitnessWork ku isaha yawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Igiteranyo cy'isaha gihita kirimo igihe gishya. Igihe cya Credit cyongerwaho iPhone yawe imaze kukibika, kuko iPhone yawe ari yo ishyira ku mupaka wawe wa buri kwezi wa Credit.
- Buri gihe cyanditswe kigumana umunsi wacyongeyeho, kandi kibikwa rimwe gusa.
- Gutangiza, guhagarika by'agateganyo no guhagarika igihe kibarwa bikeneye iPhone yawe hafi.
- Nyuma yo gutangira bundi bushya kwa iPhone yawe, kuyifungura rimwe mbere yo guhuza.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Isaha ivuga ko igihe kibarwa cyahindutse kuri iPhone yawe:** reba igihe, hanyuma wongere ugerageze.
- **Isaha ivuga ko iPhone yawe itashoboye kubika igihe:** igihe ntikiongeweho. Ongera ugerageze.
- **Isaha igusaba kuvugurura WitnessWork kuri iPhone yawe:** yivugurure, hanyuma wongere ugerageze.
- **Isaha igumya idakurikije igihe, cyangwa complications zayo zerekana akarongo:** kurikiza intambwe eshatu zo hejuru.
- **Biracyakomeye:** koresha umurongo w'ubufasha bw'ibanga w'Ikigo cy'Ubufasha, kandi ntukohereze inyandiko zawe mu bitekerezo by'abantu bose.
