id: changeAccountKey
title: "Nahindura nte urufunguzo rwa konti yanjye?"
---

Rihindure niba watakaje igikoresho cyangwa ukeka ko hari undi ufite urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura. Konti yawe, ubufasha bwawe nk'Umufasha, amakuru ya Cloud Sync na backup byose biguma. Urufunguzo ni rwo rwonyine ruhinduka, kandi urushaje rurahagarara hose.

## Mbere yo gutangira

- Niba watakaje iPhone, iPad cyangwa Mac, banza uyikure kuri konti yawe ya Apple. Naho ubundi nayo yakira urufunguzo rushya.
- Sohora igikoresho cyatakaye muri **Igenamiterere → Konti → Ibikoresho byinjiye**. WitnessWork ihita igusaba guhindura urufunguzo rwawe.

## Hindura urufunguzo kuri telefoni yawe

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Change Account Key**. Ukeneye murandasi.
2. Soma ibigiye kuba, hanyuma ukande **Change Key**. Emeza ukoresheje Face ID, Touch ID, kode ya telefoni cyangwa gufunga ecran.
3. Kopora urufunguzo rushya rwo kugarura mu bubiko bw'amajambobanga, cyangwa urucape.
4. Fungura **I've saved my new recovery key**, hanyuma ukande **Change Key**.
5. Komeza WitnessWork ifunguye kugeza ivuze **Account key changed**.

## Rihindure muri mushakisha

- Mushakisha igomba kuba yarinjiye nibura umunsi umwe. Niba bitabaye bityo, WitnessWork ikubwira igihe ushobora kurihindura, cyangwa ushobora kurihindurira kuri telefoni yawe.
- Konti yawe igomba kugira passkey. Mushakisha igusaba kwemeza ukoresheje yo. Niba nta yo ufite, ongeraho imwe kuri telefoni yawe, cyangwa uhindurire urufunguzo aho.

## Ibikurikiraho

- **Ibindi bikoresho byawe birasohoka.** Amakuru yabyo agumaho. Ongera winjize buri kimwe ukoresheje urufunguzo rushya rwo kugarura (**Igenamiterere → Konti → Koresha urufunguzo rwo kugarura**) cyangwa QR code ivuye kuri telefoni yinjiye (**Injira ukoresheje QR code**).
- **Ibikoresho bya Apple** biri kuri konti imwe ya Apple byakira urufunguzo rushya kandi byongera kwinjira ubwabyo.
- **Verisiyo zishaje za WitnessWork** ntizishobora kongera kwinjira kugeza zivuguruwe. WitnessWork ikwereka izo ibonye mbere y'uko utangira. Zivugurure mbere.
- **Bagenzi bawe** bahabwa ubutumire bwo guhuza nanone.
- **Passkey** zikomeza gukora mu kwinjira kuri murandasi.
- **Ibikorwa kuri konti** herekana **Account key changed**.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Byahagaze hagati:** fungura WitnessWork nanone. **Igenamiterere → Konti** herekana **Account key change not finished**. Kanda **Komeza** kugira ngo urangize, cyangwa **Discard New Key** kugira ngo ugumane urufunguzo rwawe rw'ubu.
- **Handitse ko urufunguzo rwahinduwe ku kindi gikoresho:** injira ukoresheje urufunguzo rushya rwo kugarura rw'icyo gikoresho.
- **Urufunguzo rushaje rwo kugarura ntirukora:** ibyo birateganyijwe nyuma yo guhindura.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ntawe ushobora gusubiza impinduka cyangwa kugarura urufunguzo rushaje, harimo n'ubufasha bwa WitnessWork.
- Ufite urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura wese afite konti yawe. Bika urushya ahantu wowe wenyine ushobora kugera.
- Watakaje ibikoresho byose n'urufunguzo rwawe? Reba [Nakora iki niba ntagishoboye kwinjira muri konti yanjye ya WitnessWork?](/help/account/lost-access).
