---
id: syncTroubleshooting
title: Ibikoresho byanjye ntibiri guhuza. Nakwemeza iki?
---

Kuri iOS, koresha Apple Account imwe, fungura iCloud Drive, hanyuma wemerere WitnessWork muri iCloud kuri buri gikoresho. Kuri Android, reba “Google Drive Sync ntikora ku gikoresho cyanjye cya Android. Ni iki nakwemeza?” iCloud Sync ikenera uburenganzira bw'Umuterankunga. Fungura Igenamiterere → iCloud Sync ukoreshe Huza ubu. Impinduka zitegereje koherezwa zongera kugeragezwa; dosiye idasomeka cyangwa verisiyo nshya ya porogaramu bigaragara mu mimerere, bityo vugurura ibikoresho byose hanyuma wongere ugerageze. Imimerere igaragaza Kohereza kuri iCloud kugeza Apple yemeje koherezwa, kandi Guhuza kwa nyuma kubara gusa ibyoherejwe byemejwe. Niba ububiko bwawe bwa iCloud bwuzuye, impinduka zikomeza kuba kuri iki gikoresho; vanaho ibintu cyangwa wongere ububiko muri Igenamiterere → \[Izina ryawe\] → iCloud maze koherezwa gukomeza ubwako. Bika backup yoherejwe n'igikoresho gifite amakuru yawe aheruka. Ongera wubake amakuru ya iCloud isimbuza amakuru ya iCloud n'amakuru y'iki gikoresho kandi ikuraho amafoto ya iCloud atarimo gukoreshwa. Ibindi bikoresho byawe bihindukirira ayo makuru ubutaha bizahuza, bigasimbura impinduka zakozwe kuri byo mbere yaho, kandi inzogera yo ku Ahabanza irabigaragaza. Banza uvugurure WitnessWork ku bikoresho byose: verisiyo ishaje ikomeza amakuru yayo kugeza ivuguruwe. Vugana n'ubufasha bwihariye uciye mu Kigo cy'ubufasha niba guhuza bikomeje kudashira. Ntushyire amakuru ya ba nyir'inzu muri raporo zishyirwa ahagaragara.
