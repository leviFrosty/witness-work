title: Bushe Ifunde lya Cinkama, Fya Kubomfya, na ma license ya open-source fili kwi?
---

Tinikeni icikope cenu ica profile, elyo Settings, no kukoka ukufika ku Pa Mulandu. Pali ama link ya Ifunde lya Cinkama, Fya Kubomfya, na source code ya WitnessWork. Pa fibombelo uko Ukuleta Amalembo kwaba, kuli na link ku funde lya OpenRouter ZDR ilicingilila amalembo ayo mutuma kuli Scribe AI. Open Source Licenses ilanga ama package yonse aya open-source ayo WitnessWork yapangilwa nayo, pamo na version, license, na uwayapanga; tinikeni package pa kwisula ipepala lyayo, ilanga license yonse ne source. Fwayeni ukulingana ne shina lya package, license, nelyo uwayapanga. Tinikeni no kukakatila pa link iili yonse pa kuikopolola nelyo ukuitumina.
