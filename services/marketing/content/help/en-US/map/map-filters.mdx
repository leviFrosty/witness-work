title: Why are some contacts missing from the map?
category: map
---

The map shows your active contacts that have an address or a pin, and it follows the same filters as your Contacts list. A contact hidden from the list is hidden from the map too.

## Show everyone

1. Look at the chips at the top of the map to see what's on.
2. Tap **All**, or tap the selected Saved View again.
3. Or tap the **Sort & Filter** chip and tap **Reset**.

If the chips are tucked away after you move the map, tap the **Contacts** title to bring them back.

## Good to know

- The map's own search only narrows the pins. It doesn't change the list.
- A contact you create by dropping a pin stays on the map until you switch to the list, even if your filters would hide it.
- Dismissed contacts don't appear. Find them under ••• → **Dismissed Contacts**.

## If something goes wrong

- **A contact has no address or pin:** open the contact and add an address, or place the pin.
- **A contact has an address but no pin yet,** such as one from an import: tap the bar at the top that says to find contacts on the map. It looks up the addresses, and X hides it. The lookup sends those addresses to WitnessWork's server, which uses a mapping service called HERE and doesn't keep them.
- **An address can't be found:** it isn't offered again until you edit it. Place that contact's pin by hand.
- **Data protection mode is on:** addresses aren't looked up. Place pins by hand.
- **Saved without internet:** the contact appears once you're back online with WitnessWork open. If it doesn't, edit its address or place its pin.
- **Still missing:** check its address, then contact support from the Help Center. Leave out names and addresses.
