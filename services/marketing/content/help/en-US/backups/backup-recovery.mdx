A backup file is made for WitnessWork to read, so it won't look like a report if you open it. Keep the original file as it is and restore it from **Settings → Backup & Restore → Restore From Backup**.

## If records are missing after a restore

1. Keep the original backup file. Don't change or rename it.
2. Make a new backup of what's on the phone now: **Settings → Backup & Restore → Create Backup**.
3. Email support with your WitnessWork version and what happened. Share any file privately.

## Good to know

- Backup files can hold contact details and notes. Keep them out of public reports.
