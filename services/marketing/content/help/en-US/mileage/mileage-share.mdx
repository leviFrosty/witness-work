category: mileage
---

Open **Mileage**, pick a period, and tap the share button at the top. You can copy the report, share it as text, or export it for a spreadsheet.

## Share a report

1. Open **Mileage**.
2. Choose **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, or **Year**, and use the arrows or swipe to pick the period.
3. Tap the share button at the top.
4. Choose **Copy**, share it as text, or choose **Export CSV**.

To share one trip, open it and tap its share button.

## Good to know

- The text lists totals, per-car totals when you have more than one car, and each trip (except in the **Year** view).
- The spreadsheet file has one row per trip, with the car, odometer readings, distance, note, fuel, fuel economy, price, and estimated cost.
- Trip notes are included, so check them before you send.
- The year is the service year, September to August.
