title: Does the app submit my service report for me?
---

WitnessWork ntiyohereza raporo yawe ahandi yonyine. Uko wohereza n'uwo woherereza biterwa nawe na komite y'umurimo y'itorero ryanyu. Fungura Raporo y'umurimo wo kubwiriza y'ukwezi, uhitemo uburyo mu rutonde rumanuka ruri hejuru ya buto Emeza, hanyuma ukande Emeza. Ushobora gukoporora incamake y'inyandiko isanzwe, kuyisangiza, cyangwa kuyiha Hourglass cyangwa NW Publisher ako kanya, zifunguka amasaha yawe, inyongera n'ibyigisho bya Bibiliya byamaze kuzuzwa kugira ngo ubisuzume ubyohereze. NW Publisher yemera gusa raporo y'ukwezi gushize. WitnessWork yibuka ibyo wahisemo; ushobora kandi kubihindura mu Mahitamo → Uburyo bwo kohereza.
