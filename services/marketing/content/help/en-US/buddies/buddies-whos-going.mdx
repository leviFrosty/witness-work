title: Who can see that I'm going to a buddy's Plan?

Everyone invited to a Plan sees your name under **Who's Going** once you answer **Going**, even if they aren't your buddies. Nothing else about you is shared with them.

## See who's going

1. Open the Plan from the day in **Schedule**, or from the notification bell on Home.
2. Look under **Who's Going**. It shows photos and how many are going.
3. Tap it for the full list.

To answer or change your answer, tap the check (**Going**) or the X (**Can't Make It**) beside **You**.

## Good to know

- Your photo shows too, when **Photo** is on for **All Buddies** in **Buddies Settings**.
- Only people who answered **Going** are listed. Who hasn't answered, or can't make it, is seen only by the organizer.
- **Can't Make It** takes your name off the list for everyone. Choosing **Going** again puts it back.
- Anyone invited later sees the list too.

## If something goes wrong

- **Someone isn't your buddy:** tap their name, then **Show My Buddies Code**, and have them scan it when you're together.
- **Someone shows only as "1 more going":** their WitnessWork is out of date. Ask them to update.
- **You see only the organizer:** the organizer's WitnessWork needs an update.
