Yego. Fungura contact, kanda kuri avatar, hanyuma utore ifoto mu isomero ryawe cyangwa ufate indi nshya. Ibi bifasha cyane cyane mu gihe abakoresha imbuga nkoranyambaga basangiye amazina cyangwa iyo ifoto igufasha kumenya urugi rwiza.
