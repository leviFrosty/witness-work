---
id: webUpdates
title: How do I get the latest version of WitnessWork on the web?
category: general
platforms: [web]
---

There's nothing to download. WitnessWork on the web updates when WitnessWork releases a new version, and you get it by reloading the page. When a new version is ready, WitnessWork shows an Update available message with a Reload button. Choose Reload to get it. WitnessWork never reloads on its own for this while you're working; if you wait, the message comes back the next time you return to the tab. An installed web app does the same: close it and open it again.

A tab you've left open for a long time can reload once by itself when it needs a newer file. If WitnessWork says Update Required, reload the page. If that doesn't clear it, close the tab, open app.witnesswork.app again, and check your connection.

Reloading doesn't erase your records: they're saved in your browser, and anything that hadn't synced yet syncs afterward.
