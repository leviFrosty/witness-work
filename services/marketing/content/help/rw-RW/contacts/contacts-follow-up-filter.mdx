title: "Nshaka nte abantu nta gusubirayo giteganyijwe?"
---

Fungura **Tondeka & Shungura** muri Aderesi, kanda **Ongeraho ishungura**, uhitemo **Gusubirayo**. Hanyuma ushobora guhitamo abantu bafite gusubira gusura giteganyijwe, cyangwa nta na kimwe.

## Shaka abantu ukurikije gusubirayo

1. Fungura **Aderesi**.
2. Kanda **Tondeka & Shungura** ku itangiriro ry'utuntu munsi y'aho gushakira.
3. Kanda **Ongeraho ishungura**, hanyuma uhitemo **Gusubirayo**.
4. Hitamo agaciro ukande **Shyira mu bikorwa**.

Uduciro dusobanura:

- **Kuza:** gusubirayo kukiri imbere, nta sura kuva icyo gihe.
- **Nta kuza:** nta gusubira gusura giteganyijwe.
- **Kwasibye:** gusubirayo kwarenze hashize amasaha arenga 4, mu minsi 30 ishize, nta sura kuva icyo gihe. Ni abantu bamwe n'inyibutsa zawe za gusubirayo kwasibye.

## Reba uzakurikira

Muri **Tondeka & Shungura**, hitamo **Gusubirayo gukurikira** munsi ya **Tondeka**. Buri muntu hanyuma yerekana igihe gusubirayo kwe kuzabera. Ukwegereje kuza mbere ukoresheje **Kumanuka**, ukwa nyuma ukoresheje **Kuzamuka**. Abantu badafite gusubirayo bajya nyuma.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gusubirayo kwavanyweho ntikubarwa.
- Ishungura rikora ku rutonde no ku ikarita, kandi rishobora kuba igice cy'uburyo bwabitswe.
- Nta kintu gisangizwa.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Umuntu agaragara aho utari witeze:** fungura isura ye ya vuba urebe itariki yo gusubirayo, cyangwa niba isura yakurikiyeho yamaze kuyisubiza.
