---
id: backupRecovery
title: Backup yanjye isa nk'idasomeka cyangwa inyandiko nagaruye zirabura. Nkore iki?
---

Backup ya WitnessWork ni JSON itunganyije igenewe porogaramu gusoma, bityo ntisa na Raporo y'umurimo ishobora gucapwa. Komeza dosiye y'umwimerere uko iri kandi uyigarure ukoresheje Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura → Garura ukoresheje backup. Niba inyandiko zibuze, bika backup y'umwimerere, kandi ukore backup itandukanye y'amakuru y'ubu y'igikoresho mbere yo kugerageza kugarura nanone. Ohereza imeri ku bufasha ushyiremo verisiyo ya porogaramu n'ibyabaye; tegura mu ibanga uko dosiye zisangizwa. Dosiye za backup zishobora kuba zirimo amakuru y'abantu n'inyandiko, bityo uzirinde muri raporo z'ibibazo zishyirwa ahagaragara.
