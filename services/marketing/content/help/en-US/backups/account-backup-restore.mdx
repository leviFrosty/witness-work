title: How do I restore my backup on a new phone?
category: backups
---

Install WitnessWork on the new phone, sign in to your account, then restore your backup when the app offers it.

## Sign in on the new phone

If you still have your old phone:

1. On the old phone, open **Settings → Account → Set Up Another Device**.
2. On the new phone, during setup, tap **I already use WitnessWork**, then **From your other phone**.
3. Scan the code shown on the old phone.

If you don't have your old phone, open **Settings → Account → Use a Recovery Key** on the new phone and enter your key.

## Restore your backup

1. When the new phone offers your backup, tap **Restore**.
2. If the phone already has records, choose **Combine** to keep them and add the backup's, or **Replace** to use only the backup.
3. Turn on **Back Up to WitnessWork** on the new phone too.

You can also restore later from **Settings → Account → Restore from Backup**.

## Good to know

- If your other devices use [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync), your records arrive that way instead.
- If you have backups from several devices, restoring combines them.
- A backup made with a newer WitnessWork is left out until you update. The app tells you when this happens.
