title: Ibikurikira kuri Apple Watch yanjye bigaragaza iki?
---

Ibikurikira bigaragaza icyo kibanjirije ikindi: gusubirayo kwawe gukurikira cyangwa gahunda yawe ikurikira. Gahunda zigaragara gusa niba wandika amasaha. Ongeraho complication ya Ibikurikira ku isura y'isaha cyangwa muri Smart Stack: kanda ufate ku isura y'isaha ukande Edit, cyangwa ukoreshe porogaramu ya Watch kuri iPhone yawe. Kiba kandi ku rupapuro rw'ibanze rwa porogaramu y'isaha. Gusubirayo cyangwa gahunda bikomeza kugaragara iminota 15 nyuma yo gutangira, kugira ngo urebe aderesi cyangwa ingingo ugeze aho, hanyuma ikurikira ikabisimbura. Gahunda idafite igihe cyo gutangira yerekanwa umunsi wose, nyuma y'ibifite igihe by'uwo munsi. Gusubirayo byatinze ntibigaragara; ubibone muri WitnessWork kuri iPhone yawe. Amazina n'ingingo bihishwa iyo ukuboko kwawe kuri hasi cyangwa isaha yawe ifunze, kuko abandi bashobora kubona isura y'isaha yawe. Kanda Ibikurikira kugira ngo ubifungure, hanyuma ukande Inzira kugira ngo ubone inzira muri Maps ku isaha yawe, cyangwa Tangiza igihe kibarwa kugira ngo utangize igihe kibarwa cy'umurimo cya iPhone yawe kuri gahunda; igihe kibarwa gikenera iPhone yawe iri hafi. Ibikurikira bireba ibyumweru bibiri biri imbere kandi bikomeza kugenda iPhone yawe itari hafi. Impinduka ukora kuri iPhone yawe zigera ku isaha yawe ubutaha zihuye. Niba itaravugururwa, fungura WitnessWork kuri iPhone yawe, hanyuma ku isaha yawe. Ku bindi bibazo, koresha ihuza ry'ubufasha bwihariye ryo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha kandi ntushyire amakuru ya ba nyir'inzu muri raporo zishyirwa ahagaragara.
