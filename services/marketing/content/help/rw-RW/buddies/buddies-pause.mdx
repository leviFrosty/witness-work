---

Yego. Ushobora guhagarika gusangiza kuri bose, cyangwa guhindura ibyo buri mugenzi abona.

## Hagarika gusangiza

1. Fungura **Buddies**, kanda akabuto ka menu, uhitemo **Igenamiterere rya Buddies**.
2. Fungura **Hagarika gusangiza**.
3. Hitamo icyumweru, ukwezi, cyangwa kugeza ubyongeye.

**Buddies** ihita yerekana **Gusangiza byahagaritswe** hamwe n'akabuto ka **Komeza**. Guhagarika kw'igihe kurangira ubwako.

Mu gihe byahagaritswe, bagenzi ntibabona gahunda zawe, urukurikirane, ibimenyetso cyangwa itungo. Baracyabona izina ryawe n'ifoto yawe. Ubutumire wohereje bukomeza kugenda.

## Hisha igihe utangira

Munsi ya **Gahunda zawe**, kanda **Amasaha yo gutangira** uhitemo **Isaha nyirizina**, **Igihe cy'umunsi** (**Mu gitondo**, **Nyuma ya saa sita** cyangwa **Nimugoroba**), cyangwa **Ntusangize**. Bagenzi baracyabona iminsi uteganya n'igihe uzamara.

## Hagarika gusangiza gahunda na mugenzi umwe

1. Fungura paji ye ukande ikaramu (**Edit Buddy**).
2. Kanda **Gusangiza n'imenyesha**.
3. Zimya **Sangiza gahunda zanjye**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Bagenzi bawe ntibamenyeshwa ibyo uhisemo, nubwo bashobora kubona ko gahunda zawe zabuze.
- Ibyo uhisemo bikora ku bikoresho byawe byose bifite Buddies.
- Ifoto yawe, ubupayiniya, urukurikirane, ibimenyetso n'itungo buri kimwe gifite akabuto kacyo munsi ya **Uko bagenzi bawe bakubona**.
