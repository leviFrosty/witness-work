title: "Spiritual Routine ibonera itungo ryanjye amacoin ite?"
---

Niba ufite itungo, ibyo ukuraho muri Spiritual Routine bibonera amacoin yo gukoresha mu Iduka. Nta tungo, nta macoin agaragara, kandi ushobora gukomeza gukoresha Spiritual Routine.

## Icyo buri gikorwa kibona

- Bible reading: amacoin 4 kuri buri munsi ukibaruyeho.
- Personal study: amacoin 4 kuri buri munsi ukibaruyeho.
- Midweek meeting prep: amacoin 5 buri cyumweru.
- Weekend meeting prep: amacoin 5 buri cyumweru.
- Family worship (cyangwa nimugoroba w'ugusenga bwite): amacoin 5 buri cyumweru.
- Kurangiza gahunda yo gusoma: amacoin 500.

## Byiza kumenya

- Spiritual Routine ibonera amacoin gusa. Imitima y'itungo ryawe ikomeza gukurikira umurimo wawe wo kubwiriza.
- Amacoin abarwa kuva ku munsi wakiriye itungo. Umunsi ubaruyeho nyuma, kugeza ku itangiriro ry'icyumweru gishize, ubona angana.
- Iyo ubaruyeho ikintu, ibyo cyabonye bigaragara kuri ecran, nka **+4 Bible reading**. Kugira ngo ubizimye, jya kuri **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Ibihembo**.
- Iduka rifite tab ya **Ikigisho** ifite ibice by'inguni y'icyumba cy'itungo ryawe. Ushobora kubigura waba ukoresha Spiritual Routine cyangwa utayikoresha.
- Niba kimwe mu bikoresho byawe kitavuguruwe, gishobora kwerekana amacoin make. Vugurura WitnessWork kuri cyo.
