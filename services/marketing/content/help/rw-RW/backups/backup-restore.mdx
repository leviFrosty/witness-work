---
id: backupRestore
title: Ni gute nabika no kubika amakuru yanjye?
---

Fungura Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura → Kora Backup cyangwa Garura ukoresheje backup kugira ngo wohereze cyangwa ugarure backup ya JSON kuri iOS cyangwa Android. Ku gikoresho gishya, ushobora kandi guhitamo Kuri dosiye y'ububiko ku rupapuro rwo gutangira rwitwa “Ngaho nimukomeze mujye aho mwasize”; kugarura aho birangiza gutegura hamwe n'umwirondoro wawe wagaruwe. Backup zirimo inyandiko z'umurimo, gahunda, abantu, gusura, ibyiciro, umwirondoro n'amahitamo asangiwe. Kugarura bisimbuza amakuru yo kuri iki gikoresho arimo; backup za kera zishobora kutagira ibyiciro cyangwa umwirondoro, bikomeza uko biri. Indangamuntu y'igikoresho, igenamiterere ryo guhuza rya iCloud cyangwa Google Drive, uburyo bwo kurinda amakuru, uruhushya rw'isesengura n'intambwe zo gutangira bikomeza kuba kuri iki gikoresho. Amafoto ntashyirwa muri backup za JSON. Ku mafoto ya iCloud kuri iOS, fungura guhuza amafoto ukwako. Bika backup ya none mbere yo kugarura kandi uyibike ahantu hiherereye; ishobora kuba irimo amakuru ya ba nyir'inzu. Koresha uburyo bw'ubufasha bwihariye bwo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha ku bufasha mu kugarura.
