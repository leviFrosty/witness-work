---
id: locationTrouble
title: Why can't the map find my current location?
category: map
---

The map needs location turned on for WitnessWork. Check your phone's settings, then reopen the map.

## Steps

1. Open your phone's settings and make sure location services are on.
2. Find WitnessWork and allow location.
3. Reopen the map in a place where your phone can get a signal.

## If something goes wrong

- **Still not working:** you can enter an address, or place a contact's pin by hand, without your current location. To report the problem, include your phone and system version, and leave private contact details out of screenshots.
