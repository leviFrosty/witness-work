title: Can I use WitnessWork on iPhone or iPad without the App Store?
category: general
---

Yes. Add the WitnessWork web app to your Home Screen from Safari. It's the full app, and it opens in its own window like any other app. Use this if WitnessWork isn't in the App Store where you live, or if you'd rather not install it from there.

## Add it to your Home Screen

In the pictures, a red box shows what to tap.

1. Open **Safari** and go to **app.witnesswork.app**. Wait for the **Sign In to WitnessWork** screen.
2. Tap the menu button at the left of the address bar, the three lines. Then tap **Share**. On older iPhones, tap **Share** at the bottom of the screen.

![Safari's menu with Share](iphone-web-app-1.webp)

3. Tap **View More**.

![Safari's share options with View More](iphone-web-app-2.webp)

4. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.

![More share options with Add to Home Screen](iphone-web-app-3.webp)

5. Leave **Open as Web App** on, and tap **Add**.

![The Add to Home Screen screen with the Add button](iphone-web-app-4.webp)

6. Open **WitnessWork** from your Home Screen. If you're new, tap **Create an Account**. If you already use WitnessWork, sign in.

![The WitnessWork icon on the Home Screen](iphone-web-app-5.webp)

## Good to know

- Always open WitnessWork from its Home Screen icon. Safari erases a website's data after 7 days without a visit, but a web app on your Home Screen keeps it.
- It can't send reminders or notifications. See [what the web app doesn't have](/help/general/web-app).
- To move your records over, see [bring your records into the web app](/help/general/web-app-move-records).
- On Android? See [getting WitnessWork on Android without Google Play](/help/general/android-web-app).
