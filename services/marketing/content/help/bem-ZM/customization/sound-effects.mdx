id: soundEffects
title: Bushe ningashimya shani amaswi ya kusekelela ne ya kukumbusha?
---

Isuleni Settings → Ifyo Mwasala → Ifyunda no Kutenkana no kushimya Lisheni ifyunda. Ici cilola amaswi ya kusekelela ne ya kukumbusha ilyo WitnessWork yaisuka; ukukumbusha kucili kwisa, lelo ukwabula iswi (pa Android, nge butumwa bunono mu app). Ukukumbusha ukupokelelwa ilyo app yafungwa kukonka ifyo mwasala fya fyakwishibisha fya cibombelo; cinjeni ifyo mu nshila yekala nga cilefwaikwa.
