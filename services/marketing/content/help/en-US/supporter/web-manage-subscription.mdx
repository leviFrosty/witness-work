Open your subscription page from WitnessWork on the web or from a receipt email. There you can cancel, change your payment method, and see your receipts.

## Open your subscription page

1. On WitnessWork on the web, open **Settings → Manage subscription**.
2. Choose **Manage**. Your subscription page opens in a new tab.
3. If it asks for your email, enter the one you used at checkout.
4. Open the sign-in link it sends you.

Every receipt email also links to this page, so you can use it without signing in to WitnessWork.

## Good to know

- Cancelling stops the next renewal. Your Supporter access continues until the end of the period you've paid for.
- A Tip is one-time. There's nothing to cancel, and you won't be billed again.
- Pausing isn't available for web subscriptions. You can cancel and subscribe again whenever you like.
- The phone app can't manage a web subscription. Use the web, or the link in your receipt email.
- If you bought in the App Store or Google Play, **Manage** opens that store's subscriptions page instead.
- If you move to a country where the web doesn't sell, renewals and card changes with a card or billing address from there are declined. Subscribe in the App Store or Google Play instead.

## If something goes wrong

- **No sign-in link:** check you entered the email you used at checkout, and look in your spam folder.
- **No Manage subscription in Settings:** your Supporter access isn't a subscription you can change there, for example a complimentary grant.
- **Can't find your subscription:** email [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) from the address you used at checkout.

To ask for money back, see [Can I get a refund for a web purchase?](/help/supporter/web-purchase-refund).
