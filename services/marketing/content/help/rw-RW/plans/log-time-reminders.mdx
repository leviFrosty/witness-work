title: WitnessWork ishobora kunyibutsa kwandika igihe cy'umunsi nateganyije?
---

Yego. Jya kuri Amahitamo → Gahunda, ufungure Unyibutse kwandika igihe, maze uhitemo isaha ya Unyibutse saa (saa mbiri z'ijoro keretse uyihinduye). Niba umunsi ufite gahunda ariko nta gihe cyanditse, uhabwa urwibutso rumwe kuri iyo saa nyuma y'uko gahunda z'uwo munsi zirangira: gahunda ya mu gitondo ikwibutsa nimugoroba, naho gahunda igikomeje ku isaha wahisemo ikwibutsa bukeye. Gahunda nyinshi zo ku munsi umwe zitanga urwibutso rumwe, kandi iyo inyibutsa z'iminsi ibiri zigomba kuza icyarimwe, zihuzwa. Kwandika igihe icyo ari cyo cyose cy'uwo munsi bihagarika urwibutso rwawo kandi bikuraho urwo wari wamaze guhabwa; gukuraho gahunda z'uwo munsi cyangwa kuzimya iri genamiterere nabyo birabikora. Umunsi ufite gahunda idafite igihe ntukwibutsa, kandi iminsi wasibye mbere y'uko ufungura iri genamiterere ntirimo. Gukanda ku rwibutso bifungura Ongeraho igihe, itariki, igihe cyateganyijwe n'ubwoko byamaze kuzuzwa. Urwibutso kandi rugaragara mu nzogera ya Ahabanza mu cyumweru, nubwo amamenyesha ya sisitemu yaba azimye. Iri genamiterere rigaragara iyo wandika amasaha; Ababwiriza basanzwe babanza gufungura Andika amasaha yanjye. Kuyifungura bisaba uruhushya rw'amamenyesha, cyangwa bigufungurira igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe niba wari warabyanze. Inyibutsa zibarwa kuri telefoni yawe ukurikije gahunda zawe n'igihe cyawe, kandi ntizoherezwa ahandi. Buri gikoresho gitegura inyibutsa zacyo kugeza ku byumweru bibiri imbere, bityo fungura WitnessWork nibura buri byumweru bibiri kugira ngo zikomeze kuza. Niba wanditse igihe ku kindi gikoresho, fungura WitnessWork kuri iki kugira ngo gihagarike urwibutso rw'uwo munsi. Kuri Android, sisitemu ishobora gutinza urwibutso iminota mike kugira ngo ibike bateri. Niba inyibutsa zitaza, reba ko amamenyesha yemerewe WitnessWork mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe kandi ko Focus cyangwa Ntugire uwo unsuhuza (Do Not Disturb) bitazihagarika. Niba zikomeje kutaza, menyesha ikibazo ukoresheje Ikigo cy'ubufasha utavuze amazina cyangwa andi makuru bwite.
