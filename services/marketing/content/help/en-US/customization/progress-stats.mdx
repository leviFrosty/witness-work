id: progressStats
title: What do the Year and All-time cards count?
category: customization
---

On Year, time counts as each month's report counts it: credit time counts up to that month's limit. Average on Year runs from your first month with time to the last month that's ended; the month under way joins only while it's the only one. Last Year compares this service year so far with last service year at the same point, or the whole of both once the year is over. On All-time, time counts as you logged it, before monthly credit limits, the same as your lifetime hours and Year by Year. Average on All-time covers the service years that have ended, or the one under way if it's your first. Months Out counts the months you shared in the ministry, with or without hours, starting from your first record; the month under way counts once you've shared in it, and it's never shown as missed. On All-time it also shows your longest run of months in a row. Studies counts each contact you marked a Bible study with once, and shows the most any one month had, which is that month's figure for your report. Contacts you've deleted aren't counted. Visits counts visits where you talked with someone; not-at-homes are counted separately. Visits that came with a contact someone shared with you are theirs, so Studies and Visits leave them out. Everything is worked out on this device from your own records, and nothing new is stored or shared. If a figure looks wrong, check the month's report and the visits on the contact; you can contact support from the Help Center, leaving out names and notes.
