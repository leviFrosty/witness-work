title: How do I report a bug or request a feature?
category: general
---

Use the buttons below to report a bug or request a feature.

## What to include

- The steps that lead to the problem
- What you expected to happen
- Your phone and its system version
- The WitnessWork version, shown in **Settings → About → App Info**

## Good to know

- Remove private contact details, notes, account IDs, and Buddies Codes or invite links from public reports and screenshots.
- For backup or other private problems, use the private email link under **Still need help?** instead.
- The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and source code are under **Settings → About**.
