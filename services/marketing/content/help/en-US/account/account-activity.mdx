---
id: accountActivity
title: How do I see which devices use my account?
category: account
---

Go to Settings → Account → Signed-In Devices. It lists every device signed in to your WitnessWork account and when each was last active, with "This device" marked. From version 1.47.0, it lists browsers too, such as "Chrome on macOS". Tap another device to sign it out, for example a phone you sold or lost. Signing out ends that device's connection. A browser you sign out is erased the next time it connects, and anything it hasn't synced is lost, but if WitnessWork on a phone still has your account, it signs back in the next time it's used. See "What should I do if my phone or computer is lost or stolen?". If you think someone else has your account, change your account key (Settings → Account → Change Account Key, from version 1.47.0): the old key stops working and your account stays the same. See [How do I change my account key?](/help/account/change-account-key). Settings → Account → Account Activity shows your account's history for the last 400 days: sign-ins and sign-outs, and, from version 1.47.0, passkeys added or removed, browsers approved from a phone, devices signed out from another device, your account sent to a new phone, backups deleted or downloaded, Buddies set up or its data deleted, your account key changed, and suspensions. "Signed out … to protect your account" means a device's sign-in was used again after it had been replaced, which can mean someone copied it; check Signed-In Devices for anything you don't recognize. From version 1.47.0, a new sign-in on another device or browser, a browser approved from a phone, or a passkey added also shows in the bell on Home on your other devices. WitnessWork checks when you open the app or, on the web, click back into its tab (at most every 15 minutes), and when you open Settings → Account. New ones join an item that's still in the bell, which then shows a count; if there are too many to list, one item opens Account Activity. Sign-ins and passkeys from the device you're using don't show there. About every three months each of your phones renews its sign-in; from version 1.47.0 that shows in Account Activity as "Sign-in renewed", not in the bell. But a phone on an older version, or one you don't open in the month before its sign-in runs out, signs in again instead and shows up as a new sign-in, so check the device's name before you change your account key. If you don't recognize one, tap it to open Signed-In Devices (or Passkeys), sign that device out or remove the passkey, and change your account key: see "What should I do if my phone or computer is lost or stolen?". Device names are encrypted so only your devices can read them, and WitnessWork's server doesn't keep locations, IP addresses, or anything you share. Both need the internet, and they appear once your account is set up.
