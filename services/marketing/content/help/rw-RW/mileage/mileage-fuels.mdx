title: "Lisansi za Gasoline, Diesel na Electric zaturutse he, kandi nazihindura?"
---

WitnessWork igushyiriraho lisansi eshatu, bityo imodoka nshya ikeneye izina gusa: **Gasoline**, **Diesel** na **Electric**.

## Hitamo lisansi y'imodoka

1. Fungura **Ibirometero → Igenamiterere → Ongeraho imodoka**.
2. Mu murongo wa **Lisansi**, hitamo lisansi.
3. Andika ingano ya lisansi imodoka ikoresha kugira ngo ubone igiciro giteganyijwe.

## Hindura igiciro cya lisansi

1. Fungura **Ibirometero → Igenamiterere → Lisansi**.
2. Kanda lisansi.
3. Andika igiciro wishyura.

Buri lisansi itangirana n'igiciro rusange cya 2026 cy'akarere kawe, mu mafaranga y'akarere kawe. Ni impuzandengo y'igihugu, si igiciro cyo ku iposita yawe. Niba akarere kawe nta mpuzandengo gafite, lisansi itangira nta giciro. Ushobora kukongeraho mu buryo bumwe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gasoline na Diesel bigurishwa ku gaganda cyangwa ku kilitiro, bikurikije igenamiterere rya **Ibipimo**.
- **Electric** igurishwa kuri kWh. Imodoka iyikoresha yandika ingano ikoresha nka mi/kWh, km/kWh, cyangwa kWh/100 km. Kugira ngo wongereho indi lisansi igurishwa kuri kWh, kanda **Ongeraho lisansi** ushyire **Priced Per** kuri kWh.
- Ushobora guhindura amazina y'izi lisansi, guhindura ibiciro byazo, cyangwa kuzisiba: kanda lisansi, hanyuma **Siba…**. Lisansi wasibye iguma isibwe, no ku bindi bikoresho byawe.
- Niba wari usanzwe ufite lisansi ifite izina rimwe, WitnessWork igumana iyawe.
- Imodoka isangiwe igumana lisansi zayo, bityo izi mpinduka ntizayigiraho ingaruka.
