id: mytimeTrouble
title: Ukuleta kwa MyTime kulanda ukuti failo te database. Nshi ningabomfya?
---

Ukuleta kwa MyTime kufwaya backup ya ntanshi iya .mytimedb iyapangwa na MyTime. PDF, amalembo yatumwa, backup ya JSON ya WitnessWork, nelyo failo iyacinjiwa ishina ku .mytimedb tafibomba. Sungeni icikope cipwa ica backup ya MyTime pa cibombelo cenu no kuisala ukupitila mu Settings → Ukuleta kwa MyTime. Sungeni failo ya ntanshi ukwabula ukucinja. Nga backup iyasuma icili ilesha, lembeleni abafwa email mu cinkama na bwafya na version ya app; mwatabika backup mu lyashi lya bantu bonse.
