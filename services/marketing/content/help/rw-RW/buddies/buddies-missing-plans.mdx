title: Kuki gahunda cyangwa ubutumire bwa mugenzi bibura cyangwa ntibigezweho?
---

Reba ko icyifuzo cyemejwe kandi Erekana kuri kalendari yanjye ifunguye kuri uwo mugenzi. Gusangira kalendari bikubiyemo ibyumweru umunani biri imbere gusa. Abantu bombi bakeneye interineti kugira ngo bohereze kandi bakire impinduka; fungura Buddies umanure kugira ngo uvugurure. Gahunda zakuruwe mbere zikomeza kugaragara nta interineti, ariko impinduka nshya zikeneye interineti; Buddies ivuga iyo udafite interineti cyangwa itashoboye kuvugurura. Kubona kalendari ya mugenzi ntibyongera gahunda ze ku zawe. Fungura Amamenyesha ya Buddies muri Igenamiterere rya Buddies no mu igenamiterere ry'amamenyesha ry'igikoresho cyawe, kandi urebe inzogera y'amamenyesha yo ku Ahabanza ku butumire n'iyo nta menyesha ryaje. Kuri Android, amamenyesha ya Buddies akenera serivisi za Google Play; nta zo, impinduka zigeraho iyo ufunguye WitnessWork. Inzogera igenzura ibikorwa bishya bya Buddies iyo uyifunguye n'iyo usubiye muri WitnessWork; niba ivuga ko itashoboye kugenzura, ongera uhuze kandi ukande Ongera ugerageze.
