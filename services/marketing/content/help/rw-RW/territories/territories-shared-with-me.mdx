title: "Kuki ntashobora guhindura cyangwa kugarura agace wansangije?"
---

Ni nyiri agace, uwakongeyeho, wenyine ushobora kugahindura, kongeraho cyangwa gusiba imihanda, cyangwa kugagarura. Agace wasangijwe cyangwa washyikirijwe kaba kariho "Wagasangijwe na" cyangwa "Waragashyikirijwe na" n'izina rya nyiri ko, kandi kemerera kukabwiriza.

## Niba mugenzi yagusangije

- Ushobora kwandika inzu, kugaragaza ko imihanda yabwirijwe, kongeraho "Ntibasurwe", no kongeraho ibyavuye mu nyandiko kuri ibyo bagusangije. Ntushobora kubisangiza abandi.
- Kugira ngo ukivane ku rutonde rwawe, ugifungure ukande **Va muri ko**. Inzu wanditse zigumana na nyiri ko.

## Niba mugenzi yakushyikirije umuhanda

- Ni uwawe ngo uwubwirize. Ushobora kuwusangiza bagenzi bawe cyangwa gukora umurongo w'umushyitsi wawo, ariko ntushobora kongera kuwushyikiriza undi.
- Iyo urangije, kanda **Subiza**. Inzu wanditse zigumaho.

## Niba ivuga "Ntikigusangijwe"

Nyiri ko yahagaritse gusangiza, yisubije umuhanda, cyangwa yagaruye agace. Kigumya ku rutonde rwawe ari icyo gusoma gusa, gifite imihanda yabwirijwe na "Ntibasurwe", kugeza ukanze **Kuraho ku rutonde rwanjye**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Niba agace gakeneye impinduka, nk'umuhanda ubura, saba nyiri ko.
- Kugira ngo ubwirize agace wahawe ku mpapuro wenyine, ukongere ubwawe mu gice cya **Uduce**.
