id: widgets
title: Bushe kuli widgets sha pa home nelyo lock screen?
---

Ee, pa iPhone. Kakilileni pa home nelyo lock screen yenu, tinikeni **+**, mufwaye WitnessWork, elyo musale widget.

## Widgets sha nshi

- **Service Report** (home nelyo lock screen): amawala ya mweshi uno ya kufika ku cipekanyo cenu ne ifyo cifwaya pa bushiku ukufika. Nga tamulemba amawala, ilanga ifya kulanshanya ne masambililo ya Baibolo aya mweshi uno.
- **Kalenda**, **Inkumana**, **Abakulanshanya**, na **Pet**: home screen.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Widgets shiya ku bushiku bupya pa mpela ya bushiku na nga tamwaisula WitnessWork.

## Nga cabipa

- **Widget ilemoneka ukuti yakale:** isuleni WitnessWork.
