title: Nshani ukusync ifyebo fyandi pa fibombelo fya Android?
---

Google Drive Sync ilenga ifyo mwalemba ukulingana pa mafoni ne ma tablet yenu aya Android. Ni ya bwamba elyo icili beta.

## Ukuwasha

Citeni ici pa cibombelo conse ica Android:

1. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Sync**.
2. Saleni **Google Drive**.
3. Washeni **Lunganikanya na Google Drive**.
4. Mu cipao ca Google, saleni Google Account imo pa fibombelo fyonse, elyo musuminishe WitnessWork ukusunga ifyebo fyayo mu Google Drive yenu.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Isync ifyo mwalemba fya mulimo, ubupange, abantu ba kulanshanya, ulutandalilo, ifipao, mileage, profile ne fyo mwasala ifisabankanishiwa. Ifikope fikwata switch yafyo ya **Ingiya ifikope mu sync**. Motoka shasabankanishiwa shisync lwa mwine.
- Ifyo mwasala nga data protection mode na analytics fishala pa cibombelo conse.
- Ilyo WitnessWork yaisulwa, ukucinja ukufuma pa fibombelo fyenu fimbi kwingila pa mineti imo. Nga tayaisulwa, kwingila ilyo mwasula ilyo ikonkapo. Tinikeni **Lingana nomba** ukuti mulole pa nshita imo.
- Nga cibombelo catantika Google Account iyimbi, sync yashimiwa uko elyo ifyo mwalemba fyashala pa cibombelo.
- Isync fye na fibombelo fyenu fimbi fya Android. Ukusync iPhone na foni ya Android, bomfyeni [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync).

## Ukupanga foni ipya ya Android

Ilyo ukupanga kutendeka, tinikeni **Naliibomfya kale WitnessWork**, elyo musale **Ukufuma ku Google Drive** pa screen ya **Mwapokelelwe nakabili**. Ifyo mwalemba fyaisa, ne mimonekele yenu ya Supporter ilyo Google Account iyo yatantika.

Pa bwafya, moneni [Google Drive Sync tailabomba](/help/backups/google-drive-sync-troubleshooting).
