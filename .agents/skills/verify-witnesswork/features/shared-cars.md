# Shared Cars

Shared Cars lets up to 10 people keep one car's mileage together, end to end encrypted. Its owner shares one of their cars from its form and invites buddies, who join from the Home bell. Everyone on the car sees and edits its whole history, and each trip says who logged it. Mileage can show everyone's trips, only the User's, or one person's, and its report and CSV follow. Anyone can leave, and the owner can remove people or stop sharing; whoever's no longer on the car keeps a copy of its trips in their own cars. It sits behind the `shared-cars` remote flag and needs Buddies. It runs on iOS and Android, and an iOS and an Android device share one car the same way.

## Sub-features

- `shared-cars-share` is Share Car… in a car's form (Mileage Settings → the car → People). It moves the car's history to the car's space ("Sharing…" while it runs), then opens the Invite to <car> sheet. With no buddies yet, the sheet says so and offers Go to Buddies, and nothing is shared.
- `shared-cars-invite` is the Invite to <car> sheet: each buddy with Invite, "On this car", or "Invited · Expires <date>". A car is full at 10 people, counting open invitations; then every row is dimmed and Invite Buddy… says "This car already has the most people it can." Inviting shows "Inviting Ana…", then "Invited Ana" or why it couldn't, in one toast.
- `shared-cars-join` is the invitation in the invitee's Home bell ("Anna shared Mazda CX-5 with you", Join / Decline). Joining turns Mileage on, and the car shows in Mileage right away. The owner's bell then shows "Ana joined Mazda CX-5", which opens that car's People.
- `shared-cars-people` is the car form's People section: each person's photo and name (a buddy by the User's nickname, the User as "You", the owner marked "Owner"), anyone whose join never finished as "Someone who joined", open invitations as "Invited · Expires <date>", and an ellipsis menu per row: Leave Car… and Stop Sharing… for the User (Stop Sharing only for the owner), Remove from Car… on others and on "Someone who joined" (owner only), Cancel Invitation (whoever sent it, or the owner). Every one of them confirms first. A screen reader reads each person as one stop ("Ana Pereira, Owner") and their menu as the next. A Shared Car's form offers Archive… (in the accent color, since it takes nothing from anyone) and never Delete.
- `shared-cars-person-filter` is the Whose Trips menu on Mileage, above the summary (only once the User is on a Shared Car). It reads Everyone, Me, or a person's name, and offers Everyone, Me, then each person on the User's cars. The summary, chart, per-car totals, trip list, Share Report text and CSV follow it, and a report filtered to Me or one person says whose trips it holds ("Logged by: Levi"). Someone who isn't the User's buddy is a different person on each car, so two people with one name read "Ben · Family Minivan" and "Ben · Mazda CX-5".
- `shared-cars-logged-by` covers who logged a trip: trip rows name someone else's trip, Trip Details shows Logged By (and Edited By when someone else changed it), a trip's Share text says "Logged by: Ana", and the CSV gains a Logged By column after Car when any exported trip is on a Shared Car, naming every one of the User's trips the same way (their Buddy Card name, else the name on their cars, else "Me"). A trip moved from one Shared Car to another keeps who logged it.
- `shared-cars-notice` is the slim notice at the top of Mileage, and on a Shared Car's form for that car alone, when changes can't be shared now: Update Required, the account suspended, sharing switched off (the server's switch, or the `shared-cars` flag off on this device), offline, changes held back because this device's clock is far ahead, or a change too large to share. Changes stay on the device and go out once a sync works.
- `shared-cars-refresh` is pull to refresh on Mileage, which syncs every Shared Car.
- `shared-cars-endings` covers being removed or the owner stopping sharing ("You're no longer on Mazda CX-5. A copy of its trips is in your cars.", a top toast, once) and everyone else leaving ("Everyone else left Mazda CX-5. It's one of your own cars again."). The User's own Leave and Stop Sharing toast at the bottom instead.
- `shared-cars-settings` covers Mileage Settings: each Shared Car row shows a people icon and count, a Shared Car's fuels read "Gasoline · Family Minivan" (and its fuel form says "Changes apply to everyone on Family Minivan."), and Delete All Mileage Data deletes the User's own mileage and leaves every Shared Car, keeping a copy of each (its alert says so). Its toast says "Deleting mileage data…" while it leaves, then "Deleted", or, when the server hasn't heard a leave yet, "Couldn't leave Mazda CX-5 yet" with "The rest of your mileage data was deleted. Try again when you're connected."
- `shared-cars-trip-form` covers the trip form on a Shared Car: the car picker marks Shared Cars ("Family Minivan · 3 people"), the note says "Shared with 2 other people on this car.", and it has no photo button. Photos already in a note stay, with "Its photos aren't shared."; other devices show them as a photo that isn't on that device. A note too long to share ("This note is too long to share on this car…") blocks Save; nothing is cut. On any car, odometer readings that overlap another trip on it get a gentle warning ("These readings overlap a trip on <date>.") that doesn't block Save. A car that went while the form was open (deleted, or a Shared Car that ended) has to be picked again, and editing a trip whose car ended closes the form.
- `shared-cars-watch` covers Log Trip with Siri, on the iPhone or the Apple Watch: Shared Cars are in the car list, the default car is the one the User last logged a trip on, and the trip is logged by the User. Wear OS can't log trips (`shortcut.log-trip` is a gap in `docs/watch/features.json`), so there's nothing to drive there.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Home → the Mileage card (or Progress, or Settings → Preferences → Publisher → Mileage) → Mileage. Mileage Settings (the header's gear) → a car → People.
- Home → the bell → a Shared Car invitation → Join. A "joined" entry opens that car's People.
- Mileage → a trip → Trip Details. Mileage → Log Trip → Car and Note.
- Mileage Settings → Delete All Mileage Data….

## Driving it with ww-verify

Preconditions:

- Shared Spaces on the local worker. Until the ww-api half is on `main`, run `up` with `WW_API_DIR=<ww-api worktree with Shared Spaces>` on both devices (same checkout). `spaces:enabled` defaults on in dev; `node <ww-api checkout>/scripts/verify/dev.mjs kv put spaces:enabled false` turns it off (every op but leave, remove and delete answers `disabled`).
- Flags per device: `wwv flag buddies on` and `wwv flag shared-cars on` (`--platform ios|android`). Re-run them after any reload.
- `wwv doctor` shows `api` as `local … 200`.

One device, no backend (People, the filter, Logged By, Edited By, the notice, no Delete):

- `e2e/maestro/shared-cars.yaml` proves it: `wwv flow e2e/maestro/shared-cars.yaml` (`--platform android` too). It seeds `pioneer`, turns both flags on, and adds a made-up car with `__WW_DEV__.sharedCars.addLocalCar({ name, me, people, owner, trips: [{ by, distanceMiles, date, note }] })` (`by` is `'me'` or a name in `people`, whose member ids are `person-0`, `person-1`, …; it returns the `spaceId`). The car lives on this device only, so leave Buddies unstarted (`stores.buddies.getState().registeredInboxId === null`): the Shared Cars runtime runs only while Buddies does, and its first sync would end the made-up car. The flow saves Ana's trip from its form (Edited By) and goes offline twice with `__WW_DEV__.network.offline(true)` for the notice, on Mileage and on the car's form.
- For screenshots, seed the same way with `wwv eval`, then `wwv nav Mileage` or `wwv nav MileageVehicleForm '{"vehicleId":"<the car's vehicles[0].id>"}'`.
- States the flow doesn't reach, seeded the same way: "Someone who joined" (`stores.sharedCars.setState` with a `spaceMembers` entry that has no `memberRecords` entry, and the same id in `unnamedMembers`); held and too-large changes (`useSharedCarsSession` isn't on `__WW_DEV__`, so prove these in the unit tests, `SharedCarsNotice.test.tsx`); an invitation (`invites` and `spaceInvites` entries with the same `inviteId`); a full car (ten people, counting invitations).

Two devices (iOS and Android, paired as buddies):

- **Pair.** Follow [buddies.md](./buddies.md) (or its `__WW_DEV__.buddiesEngine` shortcut). Both rosters list the other.
- **Share.** On iOS, add a car and a trip (Mileage → Add Car, then Log Trip), open Mileage Settings → the car → Share Car…. The sheet "Invite to <car>" lists the Android buddy; tap Invite. Read back with `wwv eval 'JSON.stringify(__WW_DEV__.sharedCars.state())'`: one car, `isOwner: true`, one invite.
- **Join.** On Android, open the Home bell (its label starts "Notifications": `wwv ad find Notifications click --first`) and tap Join on the invitation. Read back the same state there: the same `spaceId`, two `members`, the trips. `stores.preferences.getState().mileageTrackingEnabled` is `true`.
- **Log and see it.** On Android, log a trip on the shared car. On iOS, pull Mileage down to refresh; the trip row names the Android person, and Trip Details shows Logged By. Whose Trips → their name keeps only theirs.
- **Endings.** On iOS, the Android row's menu → Remove from Car… → Remove. On Android, after a sync (`wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.sharedCars.sync()'`), the top toast says "You're no longer on <car>", and `stores.mileage.getState().vehicles` holds the copy. Leave Car… and Stop Sharing… run the same way.
- **Engine calls.** `__WW_DEV__.sharedCars` is the app's engine: `state()`, `shareCar(vehicleId)`, `createInvite(spaceId, { name, inboxId })` (the other device's `stores.buddies.getState().registeredInboxId`), `joinCar(payload)`, `cancelInvite`, `declineInvite`, `sync(spaceId?)`, `leaveCar`, `removeMember(spaceId, memberId)`, `stopSharing`, `live()`. Each call returns a promise (eval doesn't await); read the result with `last()`.
- **A third person.** `src/app/sharedCars/testing/headlessMember.ts` runs the engine headless. Write a throwaway vitest file modeled on `src/app/sharedCars/sharedCars.interop.test.ts` (its `clientFor`, with `SHARED_SPACES_URL` and `ACCOUNTS_DEV_BYPASS` from `wwv up`'s `api` output), and have it `joinCar` a payload from `__WW_DEV__.sharedCars.createInvite(spaceId, { name: 'Cal', tag })` (`memberTag(spaceId, member.inboxId)`), log a trip with `member.store.getState().saveTrip(spaceId, trip)` and `engine.syncCar(spaceId)`. Its store is in memory, so do one person's whole part in one run; to come back to the car in a later run, keep its seed in a temp JSON and pick the car up again with `engine.addCarFromRoster(spaceId, Date.now())`. Use it for a full car, someone else's invitation (which only the owner or its sender can cancel), and the owner handing off. Delete the file afterwards.
- **Proof.** `wwv shot shared-cars-ios --platform ios`, `wwv shot shared-cars-android --platform android`, then `wwv errors` on both.

## Gotchas

- `addLocalCar` cars never sync. Starting Buddies (inviting someone, or the onboarding step) starts the Shared Cars runtime, which ends them with a "You're no longer on…" toast and a copy in the User's cars. Re-seed instead.
- With the `shared-cars` flag off (or Buddies unavailable) and a Shared Car still on the device, the car is still the User's data, so everything that describes it stays: it lists with its people count, its trips say who logged and edited them (rows, Trip Details, share text, report, CSV), the person filter works, and the trip form keeps its note rules. Nothing new is shared: no Share Car…, Invite Buddy…, Remove, Cancel Invitation, or pull to refresh, and nothing syncs. The way off stays open: the car's People lists everyone, with Leave Car… on the User's row (and Stop Sharing… for the owner), and Delete All Mileage Data still leaves every car; the server takes `leave` and `delete` even while its own switch is off. Mileage and the car's form show the "Shared cars aren't available right now" notice meanwhile. One of the User's own cars shows no People section.
- `__WW_DEV__.network.offline(true)` lasts until `offline(false)` or a relaunch, so a flow that stops while offline leaves the next flows offline: the shared-cars flow sets it back in its setup, and `wwv eval '__WW_DEV__.network.offline(false)'` does it by hand.
- The flag overrides don't survive a reload or `wwv up`. The flow clears them at its end; if it fails partway, `wwv flag buddies clear` and `wwv flag shared-cars clear`.
- Local alerts: APNs can't reach a simulator from `wrangler dev`, so the invitation reaches iOS on its next Buddies sync (reopen the bell, or `__WW_DEV__.buddiesEngine.sync()`). Android gets FCM with the key ([buddies.md](./buddies.md)).
- Leases hold one iOS device per worktree. For two people on iOS, use the headless member.
- The relay and the Spaces worker rate-limit per IP; avoid tight sync loops.
