WitnessWork asks Apple or Google to confirm that your phone and the app are genuine. Your account, Buddies, Backup, Set Up Another Device and Scribe AI only work in the real app.

## If you see a message

- **Not verified yet:** Apple or Google couldn't be reached. Wait a few minutes and tap **Try Again**.
- **Can't verify this device:** delete WitnessWork and install it again from the App Store or Google Play. Update your phone's system software, then tap **Try Again**.

You'll find these in **Settings → Account**.

## Good to know

- The check repeats about every 90 days. Apple and Google only see the check, never your records or your account.
- It can't pass on simulators, emulators, rooted phones, custom system software, or unofficial copies of the app.
- On Android, the phone also needs an up-to-date Google Play Store and Google Play services.
- Everything else in WitnessWork keeps working, and your data stays on your phone.

Still can't verify a genuine phone? Contact support from the Help Center.
