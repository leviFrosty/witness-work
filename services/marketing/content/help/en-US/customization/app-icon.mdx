Yes, on iPhone and iPad, if you're a Supporter. Choose from six groups of icons: Classic, Colors, Pets, Scenes, Seasons and Playful.

## Change the icon

1. Open **Settings → Personalization → App Icon**.
2. Tap an icon. Your Home Screen shows it right away.

To go back to the usual green icon, tap **Default**.

## Let the icon follow the seasons

Tap **Seasonal** in the Seasons group. The icon changes with the time of year where you live: spring, summer, fall and winter.

Alternate icons aren't available on Android.
