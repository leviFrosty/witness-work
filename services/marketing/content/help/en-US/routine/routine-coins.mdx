id: routineCoins
title: How does Spiritual Routine earn coins for my pet, and why are coin numbers bigger?
category: routine
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

From version 1.47.0, if you have a pet companion, the things you check off in Spiritual Routine also earn it coins to spend in the Shop. You don't need a pet to use Spiritual Routine; without one, nothing about coins shows.

What each activity earns:

- Bible reading: 4 coins for each day you check it.
- Personal study: 4 coins for each day you check it.
- Midweek meeting prep: 5 coins each week.
- Weekend meeting prep: 5 coins each week.
- Family worship (or personal worship evening): 5 coins each week.
- Finishing a reading plan: 500 coins.

A day in the ministry still earns more than any one of these. Spiritual Routine earns coins only; your pet's hearts still follow your ministry. Like the rest of your coins, Spiritual Routine coins count from the day you adopted your pet, and a day you check off later (back to the start of last week) earns the same.

Why the numbers got bigger: in version 1.47.0, every coin amount in the app was multiplied by 10 at the same moment, so the smaller Spiritual Routine amounts could be whole coins. A day out now earns 10 instead of 1, a planned day kept 10 more, and a streak milestone 100. Shop prices went up by the same amount, from 100 to 360 coins. Your balance was multiplied too, so nobody lost anything: what you could buy before, you can still buy.

The Shop also has a new Study tab for a corner of your pet's room, with pieces to buy. That corner starts empty, and you can buy its pieces whether or not you use Spiritual Routine.

If you use WitnessWork on more than one device and one of them hasn't updated to 1.47.0 yet, that device shows coins in the old, smaller numbers and doesn't count what Spiritual Routine earned, so it can show fewer coins, or 0 if you spent them on the updated device. Update that device and it matches again.

When you check something off on the Routine card, what it earned lands on the screen, like +4 Bible reading, and drops into your pet's coins. To turn that animation off, go to Settings → Preferences → Rewards.
