id: webInstall
title: "Nashyira nte WitnessWork ku ecran y'ahabanza cyangwa kuri mudasobwa?"
---

Ushobora gukoresha WitnessWork kuri tab ya mushakisha, cyangwa ukayishyiraho ngo ifunguke mu idirishya ryayo nka porogaramu. Ni porogaramu imwe uko byaba bimeze kose.

## Yishyireho

- **Chrome cyangwa Edge kuri mudasobwa:** fungura app.witnesswork.app ukoreshe igishushanyo cyo kwishyiraho kiri mu murongo wa aderesi, cyangwa ikintu cya **Install** muri menu.
- **Safari kuri Mac:** fungura menu ya **File** uhitemo **Add to Dock**.
- **iPhone cyangwa iPad:** muri Safari, kanda **Share**, hanyuma **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** muri Chrome, kanda **Install** ku kimenyetso cya **Install App** kiri hejuru, hanyuma wemeze. Cyangwa ukoreshe menu ya mushakisha uhitemo **Install app** cyangwa **Add to Home Screen**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Porogaramu ya murandasi iri ku Home Screen ya iPhone ibika amakuru yayo nubwo utayifungura igihe kirekire. Reba [WitnessWork ibika iki muri mushakisha yanjye?](/help/general/web-storage).
- Kuri iPhone na iPad, Safari yerekana ikimenyetso cya Apple cya porogaramu ya App Store.
- Funga ikimenyetso cya **Install App** ukoresheje X yacyo. Kigumaho gifunze muri iyo mushakisha kugeza amakuru y'urubuga asibwe cyangwa usohotse.
- Nta kimenyetso kiba kuri mudasobwa, cyangwa WitnessWork imaze kwishyirwaho.
