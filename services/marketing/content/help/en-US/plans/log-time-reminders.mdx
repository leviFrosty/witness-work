Yes. If a day has a plan but no time logged, WitnessWork sends one reminder in the evening.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Turn on **Remind Me to Log Time**.
3. Choose a **Remind At** time. It's 8:00 PM unless you change it.
4. Allow notifications if WitnessWork asks.

If you only see this setting after turning on **Log my hours**, that's expected: it appears when you track hours.

## Good to know

- The reminder comes after the day's plans end. A plan still going at your reminder time reminds you the next day.
- Several plans on one day give one reminder.
- Tap the reminder to open **Add Time** with the date, planned time, and type already filled in.
- Logging any time for that day cancels its reminder. So does deleting the plans or turning the setting off.
- Days you missed before turning this on, and plans with no set time, don't get reminders.
- The reminder also shows in the bell on Home for a week.
- Reminders are made on your phone from your own plans. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Open WitnessWork at least every two weeks to keep reminders coming.

## If something goes wrong

- **No reminders arrive:** make sure notifications are allowed for WitnessWork in your phone's settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is off. On Android, also check the Reminders category.
- **A reminder came after you logged time on another device:** open WitnessWork on this phone so it can cancel it.
- **Android is a few minutes late:** the system may delay reminders to save battery.
