id: buddiesPause
title: Can I pause sharing or hide when I go out?
category: buddies
---

Yes. You can pause sharing for everyone, or change what each buddy sees.

## Pause sharing

1. Open **Buddies**, tap the menu button, and choose **Buddies Settings**.
2. Turn on **Pause sharing**.
3. Choose a week, a month, or until you turn it back on.

**Buddies** then shows **Sharing is paused** with a **Resume** button. A timed pause ends by itself.

While paused, buddies can't see your plans, streak, badges or pet. They still see your name and photo. Invitations you send still go out.

## Hide when you start

Under **Your Plans**, tap **Start times** and choose **Exact time**, **Time of day** (**Morning**, **Afternoon** or **Evening**), or **Don't share**. Buddies still see which days you plan and for how long.

## Stop sharing plans with one buddy

1. Open their page and tap the pencil (**Edit Buddy**).
2. Tap **Sharing & Alerts**.
3. Turn off **Share my plans**.

## Good to know

- Your buddies aren't told about any of these choices, though they may notice your plans are gone.
- Your choices apply on all your devices with Buddies.
- Your photo, pioneer status, streak, badges and pet each have their own switch under **How buddies see you**.
