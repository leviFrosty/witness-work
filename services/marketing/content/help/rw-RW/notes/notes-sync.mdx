title: "Kuki ifoto ibura mu nyandiko, cyangwa inyandiko ikaba yoroshye ku kindi gikoresho?"
---

Inyandiko ivuga **Iyi foto ntiri kuri iki gikoresho** iyo ifoto itaragera kuri icyo gikoresho.

## Zana ifoto

1. Fungura WitnessWork ku gikoresho cyongeyeho ifoto.
2. Jya kuri **Igenamiterere → Guhuza** ukande **Huza ubu**.
3. Tegereza ibindi bikoresho byawe bihuze.

## Byiza kumenya

- **Cloud Sync** yohereza amafoto yonyine. Niba igikoresho cya mbere kivuga **Ntihuze: ububiko bwuzuye**, ububiko bw'amafoto bwa konti yawe bwuzuye. Reba [Amafoto ahuzwa na Cloud Sync?](/help/backups/cloud-sync-photos).
- **iCloud Sync** (Google Drive Sync kuri Android): fungura **Huza amafoto y'aderesi** ku bikoresho byombi.
- Kugarura dosiye ya backup ntibigarura amafoto.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Inyandiko isa nk'iyoroshye ku kindi gikoresho:** icyo gikoresho gifite WitnessWork ishaje kandi cyerekana inyandiko nta miterere cyangwa amafoto. Guhindura inyandiko aho bikuraho imiterere yayo hose. Vugurura WitnessWork ku bikoresho byose.
