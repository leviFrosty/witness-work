id: accountOverview
title: Akaunti yandi ya WitnessWork ni nshi?
---

Ni akaunti ya cisuma iileka Buddies, Scribe AI, Backup, ne kwingila pa fibombelo fimbi ukubomba. Takuli email, inambala ya foni nelyo password.

Foni yenu iipanga umuku wa kubalilapo ilyo mwabomfya cimo pa fintu ifi, nelyo ilyo mwalanga icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha.

## Apo ingasangwa

Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti**. Ifyo ilanga ilanda ifyo ibomba:

- **Offline:** icibombelo ici tacikwata intaneti. Ifintu fyonse fyacili fibomba, elyo ifyo ilanga fyaluka ilyo mwabwela pa intaneti.
- **Not responding:** WitnessWork tayafikilike. Lolelani amineti yantu, elyo mutinike **Try Again**.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ifyo mwalemba ifya mulimo, abakulanshanya ne malembo fyashala pa cibombelo cenu. Nga mwaiyaka **Backup**, kopi isungwa iyo imwe fye mwingabelenga.
- Buddies tayamona shina lyenu nelyo ico mushasabankanya.
- Kuti mwingila na passkey, nelyo ukubomfya akaunti yenu mu web browser pa app.witnesswork.app ilyo yapangwa pa foni yenu. Moneni [Passkey ni nshi?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
- Icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha e nshila yakwingila nakabili mu akaunti yenu. Moneni [Icisokoshi candi ca kubwesha ni nshi, elyo nshi nshila ya kucisunga bwino?](/help/account/account-recovery-key).
- Nga icibombelo tacingashinina ukuti ni app ya cine, moneni [Cinshi WitnessWork ilanda ukuti tailingashinina icibombelo ici?](/help/account/account-verification).
