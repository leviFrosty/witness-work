id: scribeOffline
title: Does Scribe AI work offline?
category: scribe
---

No. Scribe AI needs an internet connection. While you're offline, its button shows a no-internet badge, and tapping it explains why. You can still add time, contacts and plans yourself, and Scribe AI works again once you're back online.

## If your connection drops

- **While Scribe AI is answering:** it picks up again by itself when you're back online. If you typed or sent something in between, tap **Try again**.
- **While it's reading photos of paper pages:** Scribe AI keeps the pages it hasn't read. When you're back online, tap **Try again** in the message box to read just those pages, or tap **Skip unread pages** to keep only what was read.

## Good to know

- On the web, an offline banner shows at the top of the page. What you add yourself is saved in your browser and syncs when you reconnect.
