title: Scribe AI got something wrong. What do I do?
category: scribe
---

Fix it before you tap **Add**, or undo it afterward. Nothing is added until you tap **Add**.

## Fix it before adding

1. Uncheck any card you don't want.
2. To change something, tap **Say a change**, or type it (on the web, type). For example, "Make that Saturday at ten."
3. Scribe AI replaces the cards with new ones. Tap **Add** when they look right.

## Undo after adding

1. Tap **Undo** in the message that appears at the bottom of the screen.
2. Or, within 30 days, open Scribe AI, tap **History**, open the conversation, and tap **Undo**.

Undo removes what was added and puts back what was changed or deleted. It leaves alone anything you have edited since, or a contact or type that something else now uses. Scribe AI tells you how many stayed.

## Good to know

- You can always edit any record yourself.
- If Scribe AI keeps getting something wrong, email support from **Still need help?** in the Help Center. Describe what happened without sharing personal details.
