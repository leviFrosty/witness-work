title: "Cloud Sync ifunguye mu buryo busanzwe?"
---

Yego. WitnessWork nshya itangirana na Cloud Sync ifunguye, kandi inyandiko n'amafoto byawe bihuzwa hagati y'ibikoresho byawe. Ni wowe wenyine ubisoma.

## Bika inyandiko zawe ku gikoresho kimwe

1. Mu gihe cyo gutangira, shaka intambwe ya Cloud Sync.
2. Zimya akabuto ka **Cloud Sync**.

Nta kintu gihuzwa, kandi ushobora kuyifungura nyuma muri **Igenamiterere → Guhuza**.

## Niba wari usanzwe ukoresha WitnessWork

Igenamiterere ryawe ntirihinduka. Ubutumwa buri mu menyesha ryawe, inzogera iri kuri Ahabanza, bushobora gutanga **Fungura** cyangwa **Fungura Cloud Sync**. Bukande kugira ngo uhindure, cyangwa ubuvane. Ntibuzagaruka.

- Niba uhuza na iCloud (Google Drive kuri Android), bigumaho kugeza uhinduye.
- Niba warazimije guhuza, bigumya bizimye.

## Byiza kumenya

- Kugira ngo uhindure ku gikoresho icyo ari cyo cyose, fungura **Igenamiterere → Guhuza** uhitemo Cloud Sync, iCloud (Google Drive kuri Android), cyangwa Zimya.
- Ibikoresho byawe bihuza gusa n'ibikoresho biri ku buryo bumwe, bityo hitamo bumwe kuri buri kimwe.
- WitnessWork kuri murandasi ikoresha Cloud Sync gusa.
- Guhindura ntibisimbuza inyandiko, kandi nta kintu wasibye kigaruka.
