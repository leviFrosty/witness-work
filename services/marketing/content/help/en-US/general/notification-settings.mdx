Open **Settings → Notifications**. Every reminder and alert WitnessWork can send is on that one page.

## What you can change

- **Allow Notifications:** shows whether your phone lets WitnessWork send anything. If it's off, tap it to turn it on. If it's on, tap it to open your phone's settings for sounds, banners, and lock screen previews.
- **Plans & Follow-ups:** whether new Plans and Follow-ups start with **Notify Me** on, and how long before them the reminder arrives. You can still change both on each one.
- **Service Reminders:** reminders to plan next week, log your time, and submit your report.
- **Streaks & Routine:** your streak and daily routine reminders. These show only while those features are on.
- **Buddies:** alerts about your buddies, where Buddies is available. For one buddy, open their page, tap the pencil (**Edit Buddy**), then **Sharing & Alerts**.
- **Backups:** a reminder when it's been a while since your last backup.

## Good to know

- Turning off a reminder doesn't remove what's already in the bell on Home.

## If something goes wrong

- **A reminder doesn't arrive:** check that **Allow Notifications** is on and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing it.
- **Still missing:** contact support from the Help Center and say which reminder. Don't include names or addresses of the people you visit.
