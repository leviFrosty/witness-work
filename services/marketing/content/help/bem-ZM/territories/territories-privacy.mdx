---

Ee. Territory ishamwasabankanya shishala pa foni yenu na mu sync ne backup yenu, ifyasuma. Moneni [Bushe territory shandi shisync no kusungwa mu backup?](/help/territories/territories-sync)

## Territory shasabankanishiwa

Ilyo mwasabankanya territory, imwe fye na bo mwasabankanya na bo e bangabelenga. Server ya WitnessWork tingabelenga nambala nelyo ishina lya territory, amashina ya misebo, adiresi, ifyo mwafikapo, Tamutandalile, nelyo ishina lya muntu uuli onse. Icikope ca kadi tacisabankanishiwa lyonse.

## Ama link ya mwenyi

WitnessWork tasunga cimbi pa mwenyi ukucila ishina alemba, elyo lishala mu fyasuma fya territory. Browser ya mwenyi isunga ifyalembwa fya link ukufika link ilepwa nelyo atinika **Lekeni Ukwibukila Link Iyi**.

## Ico abo mwasabankanya na bo bamona

Fyonse pa misebo mwasabankanya na bo, pamo na miseko ya bambi ne shina lya wakalembele, na Tamutandalile.

## Sungeni fye ico mufwaya

- Tamutandalile isunga fye adiresi ne bushiku. Mwilundako ishina nelyo umulandu.
- Scribe AI ibwesha fye nambala sha ng'anda, imisebo ne fyo mwafikapo ukufuma ku malembo yenu. Moneni [Bushe Scribe AI ibelenga amalembo yandi aya territory?](/help/territories/territories-notes-scribe)
- Ukwabula ifyalembwa fya ng'anda ing'anda, washeni territory privacy mode. Moneni [Territory privacy mode ni nshi?](/help/territories/territories-privacy-mode)

Ukusanga Privacy Policy, bomfyeni akatini ka support pamulu pa screen iyi elyo mulole pe pi **nomba line kabila afwilisha?**
