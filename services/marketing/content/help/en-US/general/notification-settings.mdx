Open Settings → Notifications. Every reminder and alert WitnessWork can send is on that one page, on iPhone, iPad, and Android.

- **Allow Notifications** shows whether your device lets WitnessWork send anything. When it's off, tap it to turn it on. When it's on, tap it to open your device's settings for sounds, banners, and lock screen previews.
- **Plans & Follow-ups**: whether new Plans and Follow-ups start with Notify Me on, and how long before them the reminder arrives. You can still change both on each Plan or Follow-up.
- **Service Reminders**: reminders to plan next week, log your time, and submit your report.
- **Streaks & Routine**: Streak Reminders and your Daily Routine Reminder. These show only while Show streak or Spiritual Routine is on.
- **Buddies**: Buddies Notifications, Ask to Join Alerts, and Badge Alerts, wherever Buddies is available. To change alerts for one buddy, open their page and tap Sharing & Alerts (from version 1.47.0, tap Edit Buddy, the pencil at the top, first).
- **Backups**: a reminder when it's been a while since your last backup.

Turning off a reminder here doesn't remove what's already in the notification bell on Home. If a reminder doesn't arrive, check that Allow Notifications is On and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing it. If it still doesn't arrive, contact support from the Help Center and describe which reminder is missing; don't include names or addresses of the people you visit.
