title: What is territory privacy mode, and what does data protection mode change?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"

Territory privacy mode keeps no house-by-house records. WitnessWork keeps only which streets you've worked and the do-not-calls, with their address or pin and date.

## Turn it on or off

1. Open **Settings → Privacy**.
2. Turn **Territory privacy mode** on or off.

Turning it on deletes the house-by-house records already on your phone, after you confirm, and on your other devices as they sync. Do-not-calls are kept. Turning it off doesn't bring the records back.

## What changes

- No not-at-homes, **Spoke** results, or door pins are kept.
- **Add Results From Notes** is hidden.
- A territory's page says "Territory privacy mode is on · no house-by-house records". **Privacy Settings** opens the switch.

## Data protection mode

Territory privacy mode follows data protection mode until you change it yourself. Turning on data protection mode in **Settings → Privacy** also turns on territory privacy mode. To keep house-by-house records, turn territory privacy mode off.

## Shared territories

- **If you own the territory,** your setting applies to everyone on it, guests included. Nobody records doors. People can only mark streets worked and add do-not-calls.
- **If someone shared it with you,** your setting applies only to your phone. You don't keep or see other people's doors, but you can still mark streets worked and add do-not-calls.
