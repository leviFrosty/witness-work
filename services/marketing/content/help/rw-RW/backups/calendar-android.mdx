title: Guhuza kalendari bikora bite kuri Android?
---

Guhuza kalendari ni ubuntu kuri Android. Mu gihe cyo gutangira bwa mbere, mu butumire buri mu rwego rw'amamenyesha, cyangwa muri Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Guhuza kalendari, kanda Ongeraho kuri kalendari cyangwa Hitamo kalendari, hanyuma wemere kugera kuri kalendari. Kora kalendari ya WitnessWork kuri iki gikoresho, cyangwa uhitemo kalendari isanzweho ikoreshwa kuri WitnessWork gusa, nk'iyo wakoreye muri Google Calendar ugashyiraho ko ihuzwa n'iyi telefoni. Kalendari yakozwe ku gikoresho ikomeza kuba kuri iki gikoresho; kalendari ya Google igaragara no ku bindi bikoresho byawe no kuri murandasi. Kalendari nkuru ntishobora guhitwamo. Buri Gusubirayo kuzaza noneho kongerwaho nk'igikorwa cy'iminota 30 gifite imenyesha rimwe n'urwibutso rwa Menyesha njyewe; Guhuza kalendari byongeraho Gusubirayo byose bizaza cyangwa nta na kimwe. Gusubirayo byashize na Gahunda ntibyongerwaho. Kuvugurura bikorwa iyo ufunguye WitnessWork no nyuma y'uko ubitse impinduka, kandi Huza ubu bivugurura ako kanya. Ibisobanuro bya buri gikorwa bifite ihuza rya WitnessWork rigaragaza Gusubirayo kwacyo, bityo ivugurura rihindura igikorwa gisanzweho aho kongeraho ikindi, n'iyo ikindi gikoresho cya Android kivugurura kalendari imwe ya Google. Ntuhindure iryo huza. Igikorwa usibye muri porogaramu ya kalendari kigaruka ku vugurura rikurikira. Android nta gikoresho nyamukuru igira kandi ntikoresha iCloud, kandi Shyiramo amazina na aderesi bikoreshwa kuri iki gikoresho gusa. Niba kuvugurura bihagaze, urugero kuko uburenganzira bwo kugera kuri kalendari bwazimijwe, amenyesha Ivugurura rya kalendari ryahagaritswe kigaragara mu rwego rw'amamenyesha; kanda ugire icyo ukemura, cyangwa ufungure Guhuza kalendari usome ikosa, hanyuma ukoreshe Fungura Igenamiterere cyangwa Huza ubu. Zimya guhuza kalendari ubika cyangwa ukuraho ibikorwa bya WitnessWork; ntibisiba kalendari cyangwa ibindi bikorwa byawe. Kuvanaho WitnessWork bisiga ibikorwa byayo, bityo ubikureho mbere niba utabishaka. Niba ikibazo gikomeje, koresha uburyo bw'ubufasha bwihariye buri munsi ya Uracyakeneye ubufasha? mu Kigo cy'ubufasha, kandi ntushyire amakuru y'abantu n'amafoto ya kalendari muri raporo zishyirwa ahagaragara.
