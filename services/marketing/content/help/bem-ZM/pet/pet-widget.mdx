id: petWidget
title: Bushe kuti namona icinyama candi pa home screen?
---

Pa iPhone na iPad, ee. Android tayakwata ama widget ya WitnessWork pa home screen.

## Ukulundako widget

1. Tinikisheni pa home screen yenu.
2. Tinikeni akatini ka **+**.
3. Fwayeni WitnessWork elyo musale **Pet Companion**.

## Ubukulu

- Icinono cilanga icinyama cenu.
- Icapakati cilundako ishina lyaco, imitima, ukukonkana kwenu, ne fyo icishibilo ca lubi cilanga.
- Icikulu cilanga ing'anda yaco yonse, ne shina ne mitima.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Widget ilacinja pa bushiku bonse lwa mwine, elyo ukusula WitnessWork ilibwekeshapo.
- Ukufika ilyo mwasala icinyama, ilemipo ukuti musale mu app.
