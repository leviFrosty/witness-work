Territories keeps the territory card you've checked out on your phone, so you can work it street by street: record each door as Not at Home, Spoke, or Do Not Call, go back to the not-at-homes, keep the territory's do-not-calls, and return it when you hand the card back. It's free.

Territories is rolling out gradually, starting with version 1.47.0, so it may not be in your app yet. Update WitnessWork from the App Store or Google Play; if Territories still isn't there, it hasn't reached you yet.

When it's on, you'll find it in two places:

- **The Territories tab** lists the territories you have checked out, the ones buddies shared or handed to you, and, under Show returned territories, the ones you've returned.
- **A Territories card on Home** appears once you have a territory checked out. It shows up to three of them; tap See All to open the tab. To move or remove the card, hold any Home section and tap Edit Home Screen.

To start, open the Territories tab and tap Add Territory (see [How do I add a territory I've checked out?](/help/territories/territories-add)).

Touch and hold a territory or a street for shortcuts, such as Open Map, Directions, Share, Add Results From Notes, Mark Street Worked, and Return Territory.

Territories works on iPhone, iPad, Android, and the web. Sharing a territory with buddies, guest links, and handing off a street use Buddies, so they're only in the iPhone, iPad, and Android apps. Scanning the card's QR code, taking a photo of the card, and finding streets in an outline are only in the phone apps too.
