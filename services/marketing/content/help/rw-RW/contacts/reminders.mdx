title: Ibyibutso bya gusubirayo, gahunda n'abantu bikora bite?
---

Fungura Menyesha njyewe iyo wongeraho cyangwa uhindura Gusubirayo cyangwa Gahunda y'inshuro imwe, hanyuma uhitemo igihe mbere y'uko itangira urwibutso rugomba kugera. Kureka umuntu na byo bikwibutsa igihe yongeye kuboneka. Niba Menyesha njyewe izimye kuko amamenyesha atemewe, kuyifungura bigusaba uruhushya; niba warabyanze mbere, bifungura igenamiterere ry'igikoresho cyawe. Niba igihe cy'urwibutso cyamaze gushira, ifishi irabivuga kandi nta rwibutso rwoherezwa. Guhindura igihe bigumana igihe cy'urwibutso wahisemo. Iyo urwibutso rugeze, runashyirwa ku nzogera yo ku Ahabanza, n'iyo amamenyesha ya sisitemu yazimye, kandi gukanda urwo ari rwo rwose bifungura gusubirayo, gahunda cyangwa umuntu. Buri gikoresho kiteganya ibyibutso byacyo gikoresheje inyandiko zawe zibitswe, bityo nyuma ya iCloud Sync cyangwa kugarura backup, fungura WitnessWork rimwe kuri icyo gikoresho kugira ngo ibyibutso bitegurwe. Gusiba gusura, gahunda cyangwa umuntu bikuraho ibyibutso byabyo, harimo ibyamaze kugaragara. Iyo uburyo bwo kurinda amakuru bufunguye, ibyibutso ntibigaragaza amazina n'ingingo. Kuri Android, sisitemu ishobora gutanga urwibutso rutinze iminota mike kugira ngo ibike bateri. Niba ibyibutso bitagera, reba ko amamenyesha yemerewe WitnessWork mu Mahitamo no mu igenamiterere ry'igikoresho cyawe, kandi ko Focus cyangwa Do Not Disturb bitabihagaritse. Niba bikomeje kutagera, menyesha ikibazo uciye mu Kigo cy'ubufasha utashyizemo amazina cyangwa andi makuru bwite.
