title: Which map options can I add to a territory, and which QR codes and links work?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

When you add or edit a territory, Map (Optional) offers five options. Use whichever matches your card; each one is optional.

- **Scan the Card's QR Code** (iPhone, iPad, and Android): point the camera at the QR code printed on the card. Any QR code that holds a web link works. It saves the link so you can open the card's map; it doesn't add an outline or streets. If the code holds no web link, you'll see "That QR code has no web link."
- **Enter Map Link**: paste the address of the territory's map, such as a link a congregation sent you. When it's a Google Maps, Apple Maps, or OpenStreetMap link to a place, WitnessWork tries to pin that spot too, so Directions works.
- **Pin on a Map**: tap the map where the territory is. This adds Directions to the territory. If a link already found the spot, tap the map to move the pin.
- **Outline the Area**: tap the map at each corner of the territory, at least three. Tap a corner to move or delete it, or use Undo Last Corner. An outline shows the territory's edges on its map and lets WitnessWork suggest the streets inside it (iPhone, iPad, and Android).
- **Photo of the Card**: take a photo of the paper card, or choose one, to keep the card's map on your phone. On the web, you choose a picture file.

Once a territory has a map, its page shows Map (or Map & Card, when there's a photo) and Directions. Map opens the territory's outline, pins and doors, and Card shows your photo, which you can pinch to zoom and turn. If the territory has only a link, Map opens the link.

The photo of the card stays on your device and isn't shared with anyone you share the territory with.
