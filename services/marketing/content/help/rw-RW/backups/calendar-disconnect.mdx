title: Bigenda bite iyo nzimije Guhuza kalendari cyangwa nkavanaho WitnessWork?
---

Muri Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Guhuza kalendari, hitamo Zimya guhuza kalendari, cyangwa uhitemo Zimya ku kimenyetso Ivugurura rya kalendari ryahagaritswe. Ku gikoresho nyamukuru, kivugurura kalendari yawe, ushobora kubika ibikorwa bimaze kongerwaho cyangwa ukabikuraho. Kuraho ibikorwa bikuraho ibikorwa bifite ikimenyetso muri kalendari wahisemo, harimo amateka yoherejwe mbere; ibikorwa bitari ibya WitnessWork na kalendari ubwayo bisigara. Kuzimya ku gikoresho nyamukuru bihagarika kuvugurura kuri buri gikoresho. Ku kindi gikoresho cyose, Guhuza kalendari kuzima kuri icyo gikoresho gusa kandi ibikorwa bigasigara. Ibikorwa wabitse, cyangwa bisigaye nyuma yo kuvanaho porogaramu, ntibigivugururwa kugeza wongeye gutegura Guhuza kalendari. Kuvanaho WitnessWork ntibikuraho ibikorwa bya kalendari. Ushobora gukuraho kalendari yihariye idakoreshwa muri porogaramu yawe ya kalendari umaze kwemeza ko nta kintu ushaka kubika irimo. Kuzimya Guhuza kalendari ntibisiba Abantu, Gusura cyangwa Gusubirayo byawe muri WitnessWork.
