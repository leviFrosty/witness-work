id: pauseSupport
title: Can I pause my support instead of cancelling?
category: supporter
platforms:
  - ios
  - android
---

Yes. Open Settings → Manage subscription. On iPhone and iPad, choose to pause payments for 1, 3, or 6 months. The pause starts when your current period ends. You aren't charged during it, and Supporter features stay on, including sync, your accent color, and app icon. Payments then resume automatically at the same amount on the date shown, and you can still cancel anytime in your App Store subscriptions. You can pause once every 12 months; the pause dates are saved with your settings so your other devices respect them. On Android, choose Pause in Google Play to pause a monthly subscription for 1 to 3 months. Supporter features such as the badge and accent color pause too, then return automatically with your settings kept, and you can resume early in Google Play. Pause options don't appear during a free trial, a payment problem, a Family Sharing subscription, or within 12 months of your last pause. If a pause fails, nothing changes; try again later or manage your subscription in your store. For anything else, use Still need help? to contact support privately.
