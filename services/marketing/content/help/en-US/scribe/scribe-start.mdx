Tell Scribe AI what you did or plan to do, and it turns your words into cards for you to review. Nothing is added until you tap **Add**.

## Start with your voice

1. On **Home**, tap the glowing microphone in Quick Actions. Or tap **+** and choose **Speak an update**.
2. Say what you did or plan to do, like "Two hours this morning, and I talked with John at 42 Oak St, follow up next Tuesday."
3. Tap **Done**.
4. Check the cards. They show time, plans, and contacts with their visits and follow-ups.
5. Uncheck anything you don't want. If it asks "Which Maria?", pick the right person.
6. Tap **Add**.

To type instead, tap the keyboard at the top of the screen while it listens.

## Use your notes

On iPhone and iPad, you can scan handwritten notes. On phones, you can also read photos of paper pages. Check the text before you send it.

## On the web

1. Tap the chat button in Quick Actions, or tap **+** and choose **Type an update**.
2. Type what you did or plan to do, and tap **Send**.

Voice and scanning aren't on the web. See [Can I use Scribe AI on the web?](/help/scribe/scribe-web).

## Undo

Right after adding, tap **Undo** in the message at the bottom of the screen. For 30 days, you can also open Scribe AI, tap **History** and tap **Undo** on the conversation.

## If something goes wrong

- **No microphone in Quick Actions:** press and hold any Home section, tap **Edit Home Screen**, and add Quick Actions back.
