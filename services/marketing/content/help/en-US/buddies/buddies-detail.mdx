title: What's on a buddy's page, and can I rename them or change their color?
category: buddies
---

Open Buddies and tap a buddy; tap their photo to see it full size. From version 1.47.0, if they share their pet and your pet section is on Home, their pet shows in its room right under their card (see "Can I see my buddies' pets and their rooms?"). Needs your answer lists their invitations you haven't answered; choose Going or Can't Make It there or in the Home notification bell. Together lists upcoming Plans and Follow-ups between you two, whoever invited whom, with each answer; tap a Plan to open its page, or use the menu on a buddy's invitation to change your answer. Next two weeks shows the days your buddy plans to go out next to your own; tap one of their days to plan the same time or ask to join. Invite to a Plan opens a new Plan with that buddy already invited, and View on Calendar opens Schedule. Their days for the next eight weeks are marked on your calendar while Show on my calendar is on, and tapping a day on your Schedule lists their plans either way.

From version 1.47.0, tap Edit Buddy (the pencil at the top of their page) for their nickname, color, Sharing & Alerts, and Remove Buddy. Before 1.47.0, these are on their page itself. A nickname is a private name only you see; your buddy isn't told, and clearing it brings back their own name. Supporters can choose a buddy's color from the same swatches as the accent color, or any color with the eyedropper; Default brings back the color given when you paired, which everyone else keeps. Nicknames and colors are kept with your encrypted Buddies data, so they return when Buddies restores on another device. Sharing & Alerts opens their settings: what they see, whether their plans show on your calendar, and alerts.
