A passkey lets you sign in with Face ID, your fingerprint, or your screen lock, with no password. It's another way into your same account, and you don't need one.

A passkey can sign you in at app.witnesswork.app or on a new phone. One that can unlock your account also brings your account to a new browser or phone with no other device and no recovery key.

## Save a passkey

1. Open **Settings → Account → Passkeys**.
2. Tap **Add a Passkey**.
3. Confirm with Face ID, your fingerprint or your passcode.

During setup, WitnessWork may offer **Save a Passkey**. Tap **Not Now** to skip it. You can have up to 10.

On the web, you can add one from the same screen. If your account has no passkey yet, add the first one in the phone app, or within a few minutes of signing in on the web.

## Sign in with a passkey

- **On the web:** at app.witnesswork.app, choose **Sign in with a passkey**.
- **On a new phone:** in setup, tap **I already use WitnessWork**, then **Sign in with a passkey**. Later, use **Settings → Account → Sign In with a Passkey**.

If the passkey signs you in but can't unlock your account, finish with **Use Your Other Phone** or **Use a Recovery Key Instead**. After that it can usually unlock by itself next time.

## Good to know

- If the phone already has a different account, it always asks before replacing it.
- Passkeys on phones need iOS 16 or later, or Android with Google's Credential Manager. On iPhone, a passkey can unlock your account only from iOS 18.
- On the web, before showing your recovery key or changing passkeys, WitnessWork asks you to use one of your passkeys. Right after you sign in, it doesn't ask. See [How long does a browser stay signed in, and how many can I use?](/help/account/web-sessions).
- WitnessWork may remind you to save a passkey in the bell on Home. If you tap **Not Now** twice, the reminders stop.
- To tell passkeys apart or remove one, see [How do I tell my passkeys apart, and how do I remove one?](/help/account/passkeys-manage).
