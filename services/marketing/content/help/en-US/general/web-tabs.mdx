---
id: webTabs
title: Why does WitnessWork say it's open in another tab?
category: general
platforms: [web]
---

WitnessWork on the web works in one browser tab at a time, so your changes stay in one place and two tabs can't overwrite each other. If you open it in a second tab, that tab says "WitnessWork Is Open in Another Tab".

- To keep working where you are, select **Use Here**. WitnessWork finishes saving your changes in the other tab, then opens here. The other tab then shows the same message.
- To go back, switch to the other tab and select **Use Here** there.
- Closing the tab you're using frees it: the next tab you open, or reload, opens WitnessWork normally.

This is per browser. WitnessWork open in two different browsers, or on your phone, isn't affected; those keep in step through Cloud Sync.
