title: Nshani ukusabankanya territory na banyina?
---

Isuleni territory, tinikeni **Tumina**, saleni ico mwasabankanya na bo mwasabankanya na bo, elyo mutinike **Abo Mwasabankanya**. Banyina pa numa kuti balemba imiseko, elyo umuseko onse ulanga uwalembele.

Mufwaya Buddies yapangwa na munyina umo nelyo ukucilapo. Moneni [WitnessWork Buddies ni nshi?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview) Ukwabula ici, **Na Banyina** ilanga **Set Up Buddies**.

## Ukusabankanya na munyina

1. Isuleni territory elyo mutinike **Tumina**. Nelyo tinikisheni pa yo mu mulongo elyo mutinike **Tumina**.
2. Pe pi **Ico Mwasabankanya**, saleni **Territory Yonse**, nelyo musale imisebo.
3. Pe pi **Na Banyina**, tinikeni munyina onse mulefwaya.
4. Tinikeni **Abo Mwasabankanya** na shina lya munyina. Mufwaya intaneti.

Munyina onse apokelela icakwishibisha no kwita mu cibelu pa Home. Atinika **Ingila**, elyo imoneka mu tab yakwe ya Territories pe pi **Abo Mwasabankanya Nabo**. Ukwita kubomba nshiku 7.

## Ico bengacita

- Ukulemba imiseko, ukulemba imisebo iyabombelwa, no kulundako Tamutandalile pa fyo mwasabankanya.
- Ukumona fyonse, pamo na miseko ya bambi na Tamutandalile.
- Uwapeelwa territory yonse apokelela na misebo imwalundako pa numa.

Tabengalungika territory, ukulundako nelyo ukufumyapo imisebo, ukuisabankanya, nelyo ukuibwesha. Imwe fye. Ico balembele chishala pa territory, ne shina lyabo, na pa numa ya kufuma.

## Ukuleka ukusabankanya

Peji ya territory ilemba bonse pe pi **Abo Mwasabankanya**. Tinikeni **Imya** pa mbali ya shina. Bashala na kopi iyabelengwa fye, ukwabula ifyalembwa fya ng'anda ing'anda, ukufika ilyo bayafumyapo.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Territory ikwata abantu ukufika pa 10. Moneni [Imipaka ya kusabankanya territory ni nshi?](/help/territories/territories-limits)
- Kuli umuntu ushakwata WitnessWork, bomfyeni [link ya mwenyi](/help/territories/territories-guest-link).
- Ukupeela munyina umusebo umo ukuti abombe lwa mwine, [mupele](/help/territories/territories-hand-off).
