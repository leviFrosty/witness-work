---
id: stillFree
title: Is WitnessWork still free?
category: supporter
---

Yes, and it always will be. Every core feature stays free for everyone, including time and reports, goals, contacts and return visits, plans, Buddies, and the Apple Watch and Wear OS apps. Supporter is an optional subscription that helps fund development. As a thank-you, it adds Device Sync between your devices, Plan Today's Route, higher Scribe AI limits, and personal touches like accent colors.
