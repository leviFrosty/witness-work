---
id: iCloudSync
title: Nshobora guhuza amakuru yanjye hagati y'ibikoresho?
---

iCloud Sync ni ikiranga cy'Umuterankunga kuri iOS. Fungura Igenamiterere → iCloud Sync kuri buri gikoresho gishyigikiwe, ukoresheje konti imwe ya iCloud, kugira ngo uhuze inyandiko z'umurimo, abantu, gusura n'amahitamo yawe. Amafoto afite akuma gatandukanye kitwa Shyiramo amafoto mu guhuza. Iyo nta interineti ihari, imimerere igira iti Gutegereza interineti; impinduka zawe zibikwa ku gikoresho kandi zigahuzwa uzasubira kuri interineti. Niba igikoresho cyinjiye muri Apple Account itandukanye, iCloud Sync iracika kuri cyo mbere y’uko inyandiko iyo ari yo yose isomwa cyangwa yoherezwa, kandi zikomeza kuba ku gikoresho; fungura guhuza nanone kugira ngo uhitemo uko kizahuzwa n’amakuru ya iCloud y’iyo konti. Icyo gikoresho kandi kireka gusangira imimerere y'Umuterankunga yo kuri konti yawe ya mbere; niba yaraguzwe ukoresheje konti ya App Store y'iki gikoresho, koresha Garura ibyigeze kugurwa. Igikoresho cyashyizweho hakoreshejwe backup y'ikindi gikoresho gihuza ubwacyo nk'igikoresho gishya. Guhuza kalendari ni ikiranga gitandukanye kandi cy'ubuntu gishyira Gusubirayo byawe bizaza muri kalendari yihariye; ntigihuza inyandiko za porogaramu hagati y'ibikoresho. Kuri Android, Abaterankunga bahuza telefoni na tablet bya Android bakoresheje Google Drive Sync (reba “Nahuza nte amakuru yanjye hagati y'ibikoresho bya Android?”). Guhuza hagati ya iPhone n'igikoresho cya Android ntibishoboka; koresha Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura kugira ngo wimure backup hagati yabyo.
