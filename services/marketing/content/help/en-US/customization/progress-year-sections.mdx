title: How do I rearrange the Year tab on Progress, or see every month?
category: customization
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

On Progress → Year, everything under the service year's total is a card you can move, resize, or remove, including Milestones and Months. The total at the top (your hours for the service year) always stays first.

Press and hold a card, or tap Edit Cards after your cards. The cards start to jiggle. Hold one and drag it to move it, tap the gray minus at its top corner to remove it (tap Undo if you didn't mean to), and tap Done when you're finished; on Android, the back button works too. Milestones lifts from anywhere on the card. To lift Months, hold its Month and Hours headings rather than a month, since each month has its own menu.

**Months comes in two lengths.** Compact, the default, is a short list that scrolls on its own: it opens with the current month second from the top, and the last row is cut off part-way to show there is more to scroll to. Full Length shows all twelve months of the service year at once. To switch, start editing, then drag the curved handle at the bottom corner of Months down or up, or tap it.

On iPad, on an iPhone Duo's inner display, and in a wide browser window, Months has its own column beside the other cards. It shows every month there, because that column already scrolls on its own, and it can be removed but not moved.

Milestones appears for roles with an annual goal. If your role changes, your arrangement is kept: cards your new role doesn't have are left out, and they come back in the same place if you return to a role that has them.

To bring back a card you removed, tap Add Cards while editing. Removing a card never deletes your time or reports. To start over, tap Edit Cards, then Reset Cards. Your arrangement is a preference, so it's included in backups and syncs with your other devices when sync is on.
