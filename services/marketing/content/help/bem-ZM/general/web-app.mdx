id: webApp
title: Bushe kuti nabomfya WitnessWork pa web browser?
---

Ee. Isuleni [app.witnesswork.app](https://app.witnesswork.app) mu browser iipya pa kompyuta, foni, nelyo tablet. Ni app yonse iya WitnessWork pa akaunti imo fye na foni shenu, kanshi inshita, mapange, abakulanshanya, amalembo, na Buddies yenu fyaba uko ilyo mwingila.

## Ingilileni

Saleni cimo pa cikope ca kwingila:

1. Passkey.
2. Foni yenu. Moneni [ukwingila pa kompyuta na foni yenu](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer).
3. Icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha.

Ukutendeka ukupya, saleni **Create an Account**. Moneni [Bushe kuti napanga akaunti pa web?](/help/account/web-sign-up).

Ilyo mwingila, WitnessWork ileta ifyo mwalemba pa ntanshi. Umulimo wenu usungwa mu browser yenu, usync na Cloud Sync, elyo wabomba na ilyo mushili pa intaneti. Moneni [ukubomba ukwabula intaneti pa web](/help/general/web-offline). Ukuisula nga app, moneni [ukuibika](/help/general/web-install).

## Ifyo web app tayakwata

- Ifyakwishibisha ne fibukisho pa cibombelo. Ubupange ne Abakukonkapo mwabika pa web fyalamubukisha pa foni shenu. Kengele pa Home ibomba pa web.
- Widgets, Live Activities, Siri na Shortcuts, apps sha wachi, ne fikope fimbi fya app.
- iCloud Sync na Google Drive Sync. Web ibomfya Cloud Sync fye. Nga foni shenu shibomfya cimbi, web ilemupusha ukuyaka [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync) pa ntanshi.
- Calendar sync.
- Ukulanda na ifikope mu Scribe AI. Mulalemba fye.
- Ifikope fya profile ne fya bakulanshanya. Abakulanshanya balanga fye amalembo ya pa ntanshi ya shina. Ifikope mu malembo fyabomba. Moneni [ifailo ne fikope pa web](/help/general/web-files).

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Mapu yabomba, elyo exports na backups fyatululwa nga ifailo.
- Web app iisa ukufuma ku website ya WitnessWork cila ilyo mwaisula. Nga mwafwaya ukubula ukubomfya browser, WitnessWork ibomba bwino pa foni yenu fye.

## Nga cabipa

- **Tayaleshibwa ukupanga akaunti yenu:** moneni intaneti yenu elyo musale **Try Again**, nelyo mufume.
- **Ilanda ukuti Cloud Sync yaima, nelyo icibombelo cisunga data iyo browser itailabelenga:** ifyo mwalemba fili bwino. Bwekeshapo icikope pa numa, elyo musale **Try Again**.
