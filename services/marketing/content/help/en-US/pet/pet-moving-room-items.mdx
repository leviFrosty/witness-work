title: Which room items move, and what do the rare ones do?
category: pet

From version 1.47.0, every part of your pet's room that the Shop sells (Wall, Floor, Bed, Shelf, Light, and Study) has four items that move: two with a green frame and two rare ones with a blue frame. They are in the Shop after the plain items of each tab, on iOS and Android, and you buy them with coins like anything else. Coins are never sold, so you save up for them by going out in the ministry and, if you use it, with Spiritual Routine.

Items with a green frame cost from 620 to 1,000 coins and have one part that moves:

- Shelf: Hourglass (620), whose sand runs out and turns over, and Tea set (660), which steams.
- Study: Rocking chair (700) and Typewriter (760).
- Wall pattern: Clouds (720), which drift across the wall, and Falling leaves (840).
- Floor: Tall grass (780), which sways, and Seashore (920), where a wave washes in and out.
- Bed: Leaf pile (820) and Kiddie pool (960), with a toy boat.
- Light: Wood stove (880) and Street lamp (1,000), with a moth around it.

Rare items cost from 1,300 to 2,800 coins. They move, they change the room around them, and they react when you pat your pet and when you earn coins:

- Gramophone (Shelf, 1,300): notes drift along the wall. Pat your pet for a flurry of notes. New coins play gold ones.
- Lamplit desk (Study, 1,400): its lamp lights the corner after dusk. Pat your pet to brighten it. New coins glint on the desk.
- Volcano model (Shelf, 1,600): it smokes. Pat your pet and it rumbles. New coins make it erupt with gold sparks.
- Mountain view (Wall pattern, 1,700): the sun, moon and stars follow the time in your window. Pat your pet and birds take off. New coins set the ridge sparkling.
- Hot spring (Bed, 1,800): steam rises up the wall. Pat your pet for a splash. New coins send up gold bubbles.
- Blanket fort (Study, 1,900): it glows from inside after dusk. Pat your pet to lift the flap. New coins sparkle at the opening.
- Shadow lantern (Light, 2,000): it throws bird shadows across the walls. Pat your pet and they race by. New coins sweep the wall with gold.
- Koi pond (Floor, 2,200): koi swim and water light plays on the wall. Pat your pet and the koi dart. New coins make a golden one leap.
- Flower bed (Bed, 2,400): butterflies fly around the room. Pat your pet and they scatter. New coins make the buds bloom.
- Northern lights (Wall pattern, 2,500): ribbons of light glow after dusk and dim the room. Pat your pet to run them by day. New coins send gold through them.
- Forest floor (Floor, 2,600): fireflies fill the room after dusk. Pat your pet to see them by day. New coins make them flash gold.
- Campfire (Light, 2,800): sparks rise through the room. Pat your pet and the fire flares. New coins send up a shower of gold sparks.

To see a rare item react before you buy it, pick it in the Shop: it plays its pat reaction in the room picture, and Pat and New coins buttons appear under its card. The same buttons are in your pet's details once you own it. A wall pattern keeps the wall color you choose.

"After dusk" follows your pet's window, so set the window to Night in your pet's details to see the night effects any time. The Northern lights and the Forest floor dim the room while they glow, and your light goes back to how it was when they stop. The four lights follow your pet's hearts like every light: with no hearts they go out, and the Shadow lantern's birds and the Campfire's sparks stop with them. The other items don't read your hearts or your streak.

With Reduce Motion on, these items stay still, and the Pat and New coins buttons aren't shown. They don't move in shared pictures or in the Home Screen widget (iPhone and iPad). Buddies don't see your room, only your pet and its hat.

If an item doesn't move, check that Reduce Motion is off in your device's settings. If you bought one on a device that hasn't updated, update it; an older version keeps the item you bought but shows the room's first item of that kind instead.
