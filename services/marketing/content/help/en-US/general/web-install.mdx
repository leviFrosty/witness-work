category: general

You can use WitnessWork in a browser tab, or install it so it opens in its own window like an app. It's the same app either way.

## Install it

- **Chrome or Edge on a computer:** open app.witnesswork.app and use the install icon in the address bar, or the menu's **Install** entry.
- **Safari on a Mac:** open the **File** menu and choose **Add to Dock**.
- **iPhone or iPad:** in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** in Chrome, tap **Install** on the **Install App** banner at the top, then confirm. Or use the browser's menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**.

## Good to know

- A web app on an iPhone Home Screen keeps its data even if you don't open it for a while. See [What does WitnessWork keep in my browser?](/help/general/web-storage).
- On iPhone and iPad, Safari shows Apple's banner for the App Store app instead.
- Close the **Install App** banner with its X. It stays closed in that browser until its site data is erased or you sign out.
- There's no banner on a computer, or once WitnessWork is installed.
