---

Fungura agace (cyangwa umwe mu mihanda yako) ukande **Ongeraho ibyavuye mu nyandiko**. Manika cyangwa wandike inyandiko, uzisome, urebe ibyabonetse, hanyuma ukande **Ongeraho ibyanditswe**.

## Ongeraho ibyavuye mu nyandiko

1. Andika cyangwa umanike inyandiko. Shyira izina ry'umuhanda ku murongo wacyo, hanyuma inzu imwe n'ibyabaye ku murongo, nka "12 NH" cyangwa "14 spoke". Intera nka 12-20 ikubiyemo buri nzu iri hagati.
2. Kanda **Soma na Scribe AI**, cyangwa **Soma inyandiko** niba Scribe AI izimye. Scribe AI isobanukirwa amagambo yawe mu rurimi urwo ari rwo rwose. Naho ubundi telefoni yawe isoma ibimenyetso by'Icyongereza bisanzwe: NH cyangwa not home, spoke, DNC cyangwa do not call. Reba [Scribe AI isoma inyandiko zanjye z'agace?](/help/territories/territories-notes-scribe)
3. Reba buri nyandiko. Kosora nomero y'inzu, uhindure ibyabaye, cyangwa ukureho inyandiko. Kanda **Hitamo umuhanda** ku nyandiko zitawufite.
4. Shyiraho **Byabwirijwe ryari?** Munsi ya **Byabwirijwe na nde?**, hitamo **Njyewe** cyangwa **Undi muntu** wandike izina rye.
5. Kanda **Ongeraho ibyanditswe**. Nta kintu kibikwa kugeza ubikoze.

## Byiza kumenya

- **Inyandiko zanditse n'intoki:** kuri iPhone na iPad, kanda **Suzuma inyandiko** kugira ngo uzikoporore ukoresheje kamera. Kuri Android, koporora inyandiko ziri ku ifoto ukoresheje Google Lens, hanyuma uzimanike.
- Imirongo itashoboye gusomwa iri munsi ya **Ntibyasomwe**. Kanda **Ongeraho nk'inyandiko** kugira ngo uyikosore ukoresheje intoki.
- Niba inzu yari yaranditswe, inyandiko yayo ivuga niba kubika bisimbuza ibyabaye cyangwa byongeraho inshuro.
- "Ntibasurwe" ziri mu nyandiko zongerwa ku rutonde rw'agace.
- Izina wandika rigumana n'aka gace gusa.
- Ibi bihishwa ku duce twagarutse no mu gihe uburyo bw'ibanga bw'ifasi bukora.
