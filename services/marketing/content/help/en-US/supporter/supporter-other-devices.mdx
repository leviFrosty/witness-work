title: Why am I a Supporter on one device but not another?
category: supporter
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Your Supporter status belongs to your WitnessWork account, so every device signed in to the same account shares it, whichever device you bought on. That includes WitnessWork on the web. It usually shows within a minute of syncing.

## If one device doesn't show you as a Supporter

1. Open WitnessWork on it while it's connected to the internet.
2. Open **Settings → Account** and check it's signed in to the same account.
3. Open **Settings → Sync** and check sync is on.

If it still doesn't show after a day, tap **Restore Purchases** on the phone or tablet you bought on (the web doesn't have it), or contact support.

## Good to know

- A device signed in to a different WitnessWork account shows that account's status instead. Signing back in to your own account brings yours back.
