---
id: petChange
title: Can I change or rename my pet?
category: pet
---

Yes. Tap your pet's card on Home (anywhere except on your pet) to open its details, tap the settings button at the top right, and choose Name or Change pet (before version 1.46.0, tap Rename or Change pet in the details); or long-press the card's name for Rename… and Change Pet…. Rename lets you type a name of up to 20 characters; clear it and save to use the animal's name instead. Change pet picks another animal or coat for the same companion: it keeps its name, hearts, hat, room, keepsakes, and everything you bought in the Shop. Names are yours and are never translated.
