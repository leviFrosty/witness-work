title: How do I hide a buddy, stop sharing, or delete my Buddies data?
category: buddies
---

To stop seeing a buddy, remove them. To stop showing their days on your calendar, turn off **Show on my calendar**. To erase all your Buddies data, use **Delete My Buddies Data**.

## Hide a buddy from your calendar

1. Open their page and tap the pencil (**Edit Buddy**).
2. Tap **Sharing & Alerts**.
3. Turn off **Show on my calendar**.

Their plans still list when you tap a day.

## Remove a buddy

1. Open their page and tap the pencil (**Edit Buddy**).
2. Tap **Remove Buddy**.

This ends the connection for both of you. To connect again you need a new invite. They aren't told, though they may notice. Removing a buddy cancels your open invitations to them, but doesn't take them off a shared car.

## Delete my Buddies data

1. Open **Buddies → Buddies Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete My Buddies Data**.

This is only in the phone app, not in a browser.

## Good to know

- Delete My Buddies Data removes all connections and invites. It also gives you a new account and recovery key, so it deletes your WitnessWork backup and Cloud Sync copy on our server. You can't undo it.
- It first leaves every shared car. You keep a copy of each in your cars. If a car can't be left yet, such as when you're offline, nothing is deleted. Try again when you're connected.
- Your personal service records and contacts stay.
- If a removal is waiting while you're offline, they may still see your last shared plans until WitnessWork reconnects.
