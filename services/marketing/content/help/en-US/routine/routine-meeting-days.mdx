---
id: routineMeetingDays
title: How do I set my meeting days?
category: routine
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

From version 1.47.0, you set your meeting days in Settings → Spiritual Routine, under Meeting days. Choose the day of your midweek meeting (Monday to Friday) and the day of your weekend meeting (Saturday or Sunday). This works the same on iOS and Android.

Your meeting days are used in two places:

- **Spiritual Routine**: Midweek meeting prep and Weekend meeting prep show on Home's Routine card from two days before each meeting through the meeting day. Without meeting days, both show all week until you check them.
- **Plans**: the schedule assistant still reads the same meeting days and keeps its suggestions lighter on those days. Its settings link to Settings → Spiritual Routine, so you only set them once.

If your congregation changes its meeting schedule, for example during a circuit overseer's visit week, you can still check off your prep any day that week from the Spiritual Routine screen. Meeting days sync between your devices.
