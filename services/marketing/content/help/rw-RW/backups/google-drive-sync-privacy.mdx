id: googleDriveSyncPrivacy
title: Google Drive Sync ibika iki, kandi ni nde ushobora kukibona?
---

Buri gikoresho cya Android kibika kopi imwe y'amakuru yawe ya WitnessWork, hamwe n'amafoto (niba uyahisemo), mu bubiko buhishwe bwa porogaramu muri Google Drive ya konti wahujije. WitnessWork yonyine ni yo ishobora gufungura ubwo bubiko: ntibugaragara muri porogaramu ya Drive cyangwa kuri urubuga rwayo, izindi porogaramu ntizishobora kubusoma, kandi WitnessWork ntishobora kubona ikindi kintu cyose muri Drive yawe. Amakuru yawe anyura mu buryo butaziguye hagati y'igikoresho cyawe na Google kandi ntanyura kuri seriveri za WitnessWork. Google iyabika kuri Konti yawe ya Google nk'izindi dosiye zawe za Drive; ntahishwe impande zombi (end-to-end). WitnessWork ibika ku gikoresho gusa kode imwe y'iyo konti ihujwe, itari imeri yawe. Ubwo bubiko bubarwa mu bubiko bwawe bwa Google. Kugira ngo ubukureho, zimya guhuza kuri buri gikoresho, hanyuma muri Google Drive kuri murandasi ufungure Igenamiterere → Gucunga porogaramu → WitnessWork → Amahitamo → Siba amakuru ahishwe ya porogaramu. Gutandukanya Google Drive muri Igenamiterere → Google Drive Sync bihagarika uburyo bwo kugera kuri Drive buturutse kuri icyo gikoresho kandi bisiga ubwo bubiko ku bindi bikoresho byawe. Kugira ngo ukureho uburenganzira bwa WitnessWork ku bikoresho byose, yikureho muri Porogaramu na serivisi z'abandi muri Konti yawe ya Google. Backup zawe za JSON zo muri Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura zirihariye kandi zikomeza kuba aho wazibitse.
