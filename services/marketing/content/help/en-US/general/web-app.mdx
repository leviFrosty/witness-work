title: Can I use WitnessWork in a web browser?
category: general
pinned: true

Yes. Open [app.witnesswork.app](https://app.witnesswork.app) in a current browser on a computer, phone, or tablet. It's the full WitnessWork app on the same account as your phones, so your time, plans, contacts, notes, and Buddies are there once you sign in.

## Sign in

Choose one of these on the sign-in screen:

1. A passkey.
2. Your phone. See [sign in on a computer with your phone](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer).
3. Your recovery key.

To start fresh, choose **Create an Account**. See [Can I create an account on the web?](/help/account/web-sign-up).

After you sign in, WitnessWork brings in your records first. Your work is saved in your browser, syncs through Cloud Sync, and keeps working offline. See [offline on the web](/help/general/web-offline). To open it like an app, see [installing it](/help/general/web-install).

## What the web app doesn't have

- Device notifications and reminders. Plans and Follow-ups you set on the web remind you on your phones. The bell on Home works on the web.
- Widgets, Live Activities, Siri and Shortcuts, the watch apps, and alternate app icons.
- iCloud Sync and Google Drive Sync. The web uses Cloud Sync only. If your phones use something else, the web asks you to turn on [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync) first.
- Calendar sync.
- Voice and photo input in Scribe AI. You type to it instead.
- Profile and contact photos. Contacts show initials. Photos in notes do work. See [files and photos on the web](/help/general/web-files).

## Good to know

- The Map works, and exports and backups download as files.
- The web app comes from WitnessWork's website each time you open it. If you'd rather not use a browser, WitnessWork works fully on your phone alone.

## If something goes wrong

- **It couldn't get your account ready:** check your connection and choose **Try Again**, or sign out.
- **It says Cloud Sync is paused, or a device saved data the browser can't read yet:** your data is safe. Reload the page later, then choose **Try Again**.
