title: Buri kimenyetso kibara iki, kandi kuki ukwezi kutabaruwe?
---

Gutangaza Ubutumwa Bwiza kibara amezi wandikiyemo igihe cyangwa wemeje ko wagize uruhare mu murimo. Umwaka wose kibara imyaka y'umurimo ifite nibura amezi 10 nk'ayo. Raporo zoherejwe kibara amezi raporo yayo wayohereje uvuye muri WitnessWork cyangwa wemeje ko yamaze koherezwa. Witeguye kibara amezi afite Gahunda, umunsi wa Gahunda ugeze. Amagambo meza kibara amezi afite gusura aho waganiriye n'umuntu; gusura Ntari mu rugo ntibibarwa. Gusubira gusura kibara amezi wasubiyemo gusura umuntu wari warasuye mbere. Ubutaha kibara amezi afite Gusubirayo gufite ingingo. Kuguma mu itumanaho kibara amezi menshi atandukanye waganiriyemo n'umuntu umwe, imbonankubone, kuri telefoni cyangwa mu rwandiko. Babiri babiri kibara amezi mugenzi yagiye nawe muri Gahunda cyangwa Gusubirayo, kandi kigaragara gusa aho Buddies iboneka. Niba ukwezi kutabaruwe: amasaha yimuwe n'uburyo bwo kwimura ntabarwa wenyine, Gahunda ibarwa gusa umunsi wayo ugeze, kandi Babiri babiri bisaba mugenzi wasubije Ndagenda. Ibimenyetso by'inshuro imwe bigaragaza ibya mbere: icyigisho cyawe cya mbere cya Bibiliya, na mugenzi wawe wa mbere.
