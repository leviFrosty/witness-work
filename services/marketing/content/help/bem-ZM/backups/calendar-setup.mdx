id: calendarSetup
title: Bushe ningalanga shani Ukukonkapo mu kalenda yandi?
---

Calendar Sync ni ya bwebwe pa iPhone, iPad, na Android. Ubu bwasuko bwaba pa iPhone na iPad, kabili Bushe Calendar Sync ibomba shani pa Android? bwaba pa Android. Ilyo mulepanga pa ntanshi, nelyo ukufuma ku kwita mu cipao ca fyakwishibisha panuma ya kukusha, tinikeni Lundapo ku Kalenda pa kupanga kalenda ya WitnessWork (muli iCloud nga yaliko). Kuti mwapanga inshita iili yonse mu Settings → Ifyo Mwasala → Calendar Sync: suminisheni ukufika kwa kalenda yonse, elyo mupange kalenda ya WitnessWork nelyo musale kalenda ibomfiwa fye pa WitnessWork. Ukukusha kufwaya ukwingila muli iCloud na intaneti, nangu pa makalenda ya Google nelyo Outlook. Pa kubomfya Google nelyo Outlook, pangeni kalenda ku mupeele wa ico, lundeni akaunti mu iPhone Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts, no kuwasha amakalenda yaco. Kalenda yenu ya bulesa na makalenda ya sungwa fye pa cibombelo ici tayingasalwa. Ilyo kalenda yasalwa, cila Ukukonkapo ukwisa kulundwako bwangu, pamo na uko mwasunga kale, nge ciputulwa ca miniti 30. Calendar Sync ilundako Ukukonkapo konse ukwisa nelyo ukwabula; pa kuleka ukulundako, ishimyeni mu Settings → Ifyo Mwasala → Calendar Sync. Ukukonkapo kwapita, Ubupange bwa mulimo, ne Bupange ubucitwa nakabili tafilundwako. Cila ciputulwa cipokelela ukukumbusha kumo ne kwa Nkumbushe pa Kukonkapo kwaco, kabili tapali ukukumbusha nga Nkumbushe yashimiwa. Umupeele wa kalenda yenu kuti alundako ukukumbusha kwakwe; pa Google, ukufumyapo mu Event notifications ya kalenda iyo mu fyo mwasala fya Google Calendar. Cinjeni mu WitnessWork, pantu ifyo mwacinja mu kalenda, pamo na ukukumbusha, fingacinjiwa pa kukusha ukukonkapo.
