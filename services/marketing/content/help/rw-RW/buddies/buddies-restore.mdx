title: "Nagarura bagenzi banjye ku kindi gikoresho cyangwa mvuye kuri backup?"
---

Akenshi yego. Kuri iPhone na iPad, bagenzi bawe bakurikira ku bindi bikoresho byinjiye muri konti imwe ya Apple. Kuri Android bashobora kugaruka nyuma yo kongera gushyiraho cyangwa kuri telefoni nshya. Dosiye za backup zisanzwe za WitnessWork ntizibafite.

## iPhone na iPad

1. Reba ko iCloud Keychain ifunguye (**Amajambobanga** mu igenamiterere ryawe rya iCloud).
2. Fungura WitnessWork ku kindi gikoresho.
3. Fungura **Buddies** umanure kugira ngo uvugurure.

Guhuza bifunguye, bagenzi bawe, ibisubizo byawe, amazina y'irihimbano n'amabara wahisemo, n'ibyo wasomye mu nzogera y'amamenyesha bigaragara kuri buri gikoresho. Fungura guhuza kugira ngo ibyo bagenzi bawe babona bigume bimeze kimwe.

## Android

Bagenzi bawe bagaruka iyo wongeye gushyiraho WitnessWork kuri telefoni imwe backup ya Google ifunguye (**Igenamiterere → Google → Backup**). Gufunga ecran bishyizweho, bashobora kandi kugaruka iyo utegura telefoni nshya uvuye kuri iyo backup. Nta gufunga ecran, baguma kuri iyo telefoni.

## Byiza kumenya

- Buri telefoni ya Android, na iPhone na telefoni ya Android, bifite imuhuza yabyo ya Buddies.
- Kuri murandasi, Buddies iza binyuze kuri Cloud Sync umaze kwinjira.
- Bagenzi babona izina rimwe ryawe: iryo uherutse guhindura muri **Umwirondoro**.
- Bika amakuru yawe bwite muri backup ukwayo.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Bagenzi bawe ntibagarutse:** muhuze nanone mukoresheje ubutumire bushya. Ibi bireba kandi nyuma ya **Siba amakuru yanjye ya Buddies**.
