title: Is WitnessWork on Android?
category: general
---

Yes. WitnessWork works on Android, and it's free. You can also use it in a web browser.

## What works on Android

- Time and reports, the timer, contacts and follow-ups, and plans
- The map, mileage, reminders, and backups
- Buddies, including with buddies who use iPhone
- Scribe AI

A few features need Apple services and stay on iPhone and iPad: iCloud Sync, widgets, Live Activities, and alternate app icons.

## Keep your devices in sync

Cloud Sync works between Android, iPhone, and the web. Google Drive Sync works between Android devices. See [What is Cloud Sync?](/help/backups/cloud-sync).

## Move to a new Android phone

1. On your old phone, open **Settings → Account** and tap **Set Up Another Device**.
2. Follow the steps on the new phone. With Cloud Sync, your records arrive without a restore step.
3. If you turned on Backup in **Settings → Account**, the new phone offers to restore it.

You can also use a backup file. On your old phone, tap **Settings → Backup & Restore → Create Backup** and copy the file to the new phone. On the new phone, tap **I already use WitnessWork** when setup starts and choose **From a backup file** on the **Welcome back** screen. Between Android phones you can choose **From Google Drive** there instead.

## Good to know

- Keep backup files private. They can contain householder details.
- To use WitnessWork in a browser, see [Can I use WitnessWork in a web browser?](/help/general/web-app).
