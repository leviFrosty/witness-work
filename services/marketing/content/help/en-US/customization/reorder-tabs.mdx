---
id: reorderTabs
title: Can I change the order of the tabs?
category: customization
---

Yes. The new order applies right away to the bottom bar on phones and the sidebar on tablets.

## Change the order

1. Open **Settings → Features → Tab Order**.
2. Tap the up and down arrows beside a tab. Or press and hold a tab's name to move it to the top or bottom.

## Good to know

- Tabs can be reordered but not hidden.
- WitnessWork still opens on Home.
- Progress only shows for roles that log hours. It keeps its place if you change roles.
- On iPad, the Command-number keyboard shortcuts follow your order.
- The order syncs with your other devices when sync is on, and doesn't change your records.
- To go back, move the tabs to Home, Contacts, Progress, Schedule.

## If something goes wrong

- **The order doesn't update:** restart the app. If it still doesn't, use **Still need help?** in the Help Center.
