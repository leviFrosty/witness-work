id: displaySettingsWeb
title: How do I make text larger or change the display on the web?
category: customization
platforms: [web]
---

Use your browser's zoom to make everything on the page larger or smaller: press Ctrl and Plus or Minus on Windows and Linux, Command and Plus or Minus on a Mac, or use the browser's menu. On a phone or tablet, use the browser's text size or page zoom setting. Zoom applies to app.witnesswork.app and stays for that site.

To choose light or dark mode, open Settings → Personalization → Color Scheme and pick Device, Dark, or Light. Device follows your computer's setting. These settings change only how WitnessWork looks, never your records. If text is cut off or hard to read, report it from the Help Center with a screenshot, your browser, and your zoom level; cover names and addresses first.
