id: cloudSyncFromWeb
title: Why did my phone switch to Cloud Sync after I signed in on the web?
category: backups
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

When you sign in on the web, it asks you to turn on [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync), because the web works only with Cloud Sync. If you choose **Turn On**, your phone switches to Cloud Sync the next time you open WitnessWork on it.

## Nothing is lost

- Your phone keeps every record and photo and combines them with what's already on Cloud Sync.
- Nothing you deleted comes back.
- Your other phones on the same account switch too, each the next time you open WitnessWork on it.
- Your records are private. Only your own devices can read them.

## Switch back

1. On your phone, open **Settings → Sync**.
2. Choose iCloud (Google Drive on Android) or Off.

Your phone stays on the option you choose. The web asks again the next time you use it.

## If it doesn't switch

- Make sure your phone is online and you've finished setting up WitnessWork, then open the app again.
- If the web says **Update WitnessWork on your phone**, update the app from the App Store or Google Play, then open it.
- You can always choose Cloud Sync yourself in **Settings → Sync**.
