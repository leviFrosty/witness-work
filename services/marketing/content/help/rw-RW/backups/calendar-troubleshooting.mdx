title: Ibikorwa bya kalendari birabura cyangwa kuvugurura byarahagaze. Nakora iki?
---

Niba Guhuza kalendari kureka kuvugurura, amenyesha Ivugurura rya kalendari ryahagaritswe kigaragara mu rwego rw'amamenyesha (inzogera iri ku Ahabanza): ako kanya iyo hari ikigusabwa, nko kugera kuri kalendari, cyangwa nyuma y'umunsi ku bibazo bikunze gukemuka ubwabyo, nko kubura interineti. Kanda ugire icyo ukemura, cyangwa uhitemo Zimya niba utagishaka Gusubirayo muri kalendari yawe. Ushobora kandi gufungura Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Guhuza kalendari ugasoma ikosa ryerekanwa. Reba interineti yawe, kwinjira muri iCloud, konti ya kalendari, n'uruhushya rwuzuye rwa kalendari rwa WitnessWork mu Igenamiterere rya iPhone. Koresha Huza ubu ku gikoresho nyamukuru nyuma y'uko konti ya kalendari n'amakuru ya porogaramu birangiza guhuza. Kalendari yabuze igomba kongera guhuzwa cyangwa gusimbuzwa; kalendari zahinduriwe izina hanze ya WitnessWork zishobora gukenera guhitwamo nanone. Iyo igikorwa kizaza cyari cyoherejwe kibuze, kuvugurura birahagarara n'iyo iki gikoresho ari cyo cyakohereje: gutinda kw'guhuza kalendari, gusiba no kwimura igikorwa bishobora kugaragara kimwe. Tegereza ko konti ya kalendari irangiza guhuza kandi wemeze ko wahisemo kalendari imwe yihariye. Subiza inyuma ibikorwa bya WitnessWork wimuriye ahandi mbere yo kongera kugerageza. Garura ibikorwa byabuze ni uburyo bugaragara bwo gukosora nyuma y’ayo igenzura; ishobora gukora kopi zisubiramo niba kopi zikiri gukururwa cyangwa zikiri ahandi. Gusubirayo cyashize ntikongerwaho mu ntangiriro. Ku kwimura igikoresho bitegereje, ongera ufungure igikoresho nyamukuru cya mbere. Niba ikibazo gikomeje, koresha uburyo bw'ubufasha bwihariye bwo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha ushyiremo verisiyo ya porogaramu, igikoresho na verisiyo ya sisitemu y'imikorere, n'ikosa ryerekanwe. Ntushyire amakuru y'abantu, amafoto ya kalendari, inyandiko na backup muri raporo zishyirwa ahagaragara.
