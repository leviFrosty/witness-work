title: "Kuki ikarita itabona aho ndi ubu?"
---

Ikarita ikeneye ko aho uri hafungurwa kuri WitnessWork. Reba igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe, hanyuma wongere ufungure ikarita.

## Intambwe

1. Fungura igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe urebe ko serivisi z'aho uri zifunguye.
2. Shaka WitnessWork wemerere aho uri.
3. Ongera ufungure ikarita ahantu telefoni yawe ishobora kubona umurongo.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Biracyanze:** ushobora kwandika aderesi, cyangwa gushyira ikimenyetso cy'umuntu ukoresheje intoki, nta kureba aho uri ubu. Kugira ngo umenyeshe ikibazo, shyiramo telefoni yawe na verisiyo ya sisitemu, kandi ntushyire amakuru y'ibanga y'abantu mu mafoto ya ecran.
