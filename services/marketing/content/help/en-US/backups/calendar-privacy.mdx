Events in your calendar show only a general Follow-up title, the time and length of the appointment, and a link that opens the visit in WitnessWork. Visit notes and Follow-up topics are never added.

## Names and addresses

Turn on **Include names and addresses** in **Settings → Features → Calendar Sync** to add them to events. This applies to all your devices.

## Good to know

- Your calendar app may share events with your other devices, shared calendars and widgets. Check that calendar's sharing before you add names or addresses.
- WitnessWork only manages its own events. It doesn't read your personal appointments.
