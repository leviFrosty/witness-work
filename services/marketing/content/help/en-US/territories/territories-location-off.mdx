id: territoriesLocationOff
title: Why isn't WitnessWork saving where the doors are?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

WitnessWork saves where each door is only when it can use your location, Save Where Each Door Is is on for the street, and you're at the street. Without location, everything else still works: doors are recorded without a saved place, and the territory map opens without showing where you are.

- **"Location is off for WitnessWork."** The first time something in Territories needs your location, WitnessWork asks. If you said no, the door recorder and the territory map show this notice. Tap Open Settings and allow location for WitnessWork; the notice goes away when you come back. On Android the button may say Allow Location and ask again. On the web, allow this site to use your location in your browser's site settings.
- **"WitnessWork only has your approximate location."** Door locations need your precise location. Tap Open Settings and turn on precise location for WitnessWork.
- **"Location is turned off on this device."** Location is off for the whole device, not only WitnessWork. Tap the button in the notice to turn it on.
- **"You're away from this street, so no pins for now."** When you're far from the street, WitnessWork doesn't pin doors, so they don't all land in one spot. If you really are at the doors, tap the pin icon in the door recorder and tap I'm at the Doors.
- **Save Where Each Door Is is off** for this street. Turn on the switch in the door recorder, or in Edit Street.
- **Territory privacy mode is on.** It keeps no house-by-house records, including door pins (see [What is territory privacy mode?](/help/territories/territories-privacy-mode)).

If none of these notices show, your device may not be able to get a location where you are, for example indoors or with a weak signal.

To pin a door by hand, open the street, tap the house, tap Add Pin, and tap the map where the door is. That works without location.
