title: How do my pet’s hearts work?
category: pet
---

Your pet gains a heart for each day you're active in the ministry, up to 5, and loses hearts when you've been away a while. Patting your pet is just for fun and never adds hearts.

## How hearts fill

- A new pet starts with 3 of 5 hearts, or 4 if you were active the day before you adopted it.
- One heart is added for each active day, however much you did.
- A day counts when you log time (even 0 minutes, if you mark that you shared), save a visit, or make a new plan. Editing a plan doesn't count.
- Visits that came with a contact someone shared with you don't count.

Kingdom Publishers go by the month. An active day fills all 5 hearts, and they stay full through the next month.

## When you're away

- After 10 days without an active day, your pet loses half a heart every 3 days.
- At half a heart, your pet falls asleep.
- After 60 days without an active day, your pet is shown greyed and lying down.

## Wake your pet

Tap a sleeping pet, or tap **Tap to wake**, and it wakes with 1 heart. The next active day adds hearts again.

## Good to know

- Going out also earns coins for the Shop. Only days when you log time or a visit earn coins. A new plan adds a heart but no coins.
- Hearts come from your records, so they're the same on every device.
