id: scribeUsage
title: How do Scribe AI limits work?
category: scribe
---

Scribe AI usage depends on how much it reads and writes, not on how many messages you send. A quick update uses a little; long conversations and, on the phone apps, paper pages use more. To see how much of each limit you’ve used, as a percentage, and when it resets, tap the percentage at the top of Scribe AI or open Settings → Scribe AI → Usage. Without Supporter, you have a weekly limit that starts with your first use and resets 7 days later; it is enough for a few quick updates a week, and your first week has a higher limit so you can bring in your notes. Supporters get much higher limits: a session limit that resets 5 hours after it starts, and a weekly limit that resets 7 days after it starts. If your Supporter subscription ends, the free weekly limit applies again, counting what you’ve already used that week. Before version 1.47.0 the app calls the free limit a monthly limit; it is the same weekly limit, and the reset time shown is correct. When you reach a limit, Scribe AI tells you when you can use it again, and anything it was already working on still finishes. You can always add time, contacts and plans yourself. On the phone apps, reading paper pages counts toward the same limits, and you can read a limited number of pages in any 24 hours. The limits are the same on the web and on your phones.
