title: How do I invite a buddy to a Plan or Follow-up, and answer an invitation?
category: buddies
---

Use Invite Buddies when adding or editing a Plan or scheduled Follow-up, select confirmed buddies, and save. On a Plan's page, or a conversation's page for a scheduled Follow-up, Invite Buddies invites someone right away. You can also tap Invite to a Plan on a buddy's page. A Plan invite shares its date, time, duration, title, location, and note, with the note's formatting and photos. A Follow-up invite shares its date/time, the householder's first name, address or pin, and topic; those details expire a day after the visit. Check that you want to share these details with the people selected. Follow-up invitations are unavailable in Data Protection Mode. To reply, choose Going or Can't Make It in the notification bell on Home, in the Together section if you've added it to Home (from version 1.46.0), on the buddy's page in Buddies, or on the invitation's own page (tap it in the bell, on the buddy's page, or on the day in Schedule to open it); you can change your answer later. A Follow-up invitation's page shows when your buddy is going, the householder's first name, the topic, and the place, with directions. An invitation stays in the bell until you answer it, it is canceled, or its Plan or Follow-up has happened, so it can't be dismissed or cleared by mistake; once answered, you can dismiss it. Going to a Plan adds a linked Plan to your schedule, which follows the inviter's changes or cancellation until the Plan happens; after that it stays on your schedule as it was. From version 1.46.0, the linked Plan's date, time, title, place, and note stay the inviter's, and Edit changes only your hours, reminder, and Type; everyone invited sees who's going under Who's Going, so you can see who'll be there before you answer. A buddy's shared Plan stays open to you for a month after it. Can't Make It takes it off your schedule, and switching back to Going on the Plan's page puts it back. If someone stops being your buddy before an invitation reaches them, WitnessWork tells you, and the Plan shows Not sent beside them until it has happened. A Follow-up invite does not import the contact or visit history.
