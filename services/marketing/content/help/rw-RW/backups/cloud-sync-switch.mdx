title: "Mpindura nte hagati ya Cloud Sync na iCloud cyangwa Google Drive?"
---

Fungura **Igenamiterere → Guhuza** uhitemo Cloud Sync, iCloud (Google Drive kuri Android), cyangwa Zimya. Bitatu byose ni ubuntu.

## Byiza kumenya

- Guhindura bibika ibyose biri kuri iki gikoresho kandi bikabihuriza hamwe n'ibikoresho byawe biri ku buryo bushya. Nta kintu gisimbuzwa, kandi nta kintu wasibye kigaruka.
- Kuva kuri Cloud Sync bikuraho kopi y'iki gikoresho kuri seriveri ya WitnessWork. Ibindi bikoresho byawe bigumana ibyamaze guhuzwa.
- Kuva kuri iCloud cyangwa Google Drive bisiga kopi ya nyuma y'iki gikoresho aho. Ibindi bikoresho byawe bikomeza guhuza hagati yabyo, ariko si n'iki.
- Kugira ngo uhuze ibikoresho byawe byose, hitamo uburyo bumwe kuri buri kimwe.
- Amafoto agenda na Cloud Sync yonyine. Hamwe na iCloud cyangwa Google Drive, fungura **Huza amafoto y'aderesi**.
- WitnessWork kuri murandasi ikoresha Cloud Sync gusa, bityo kuyikoresha bisaba telefoni yawe iri kuri Cloud Sync.
