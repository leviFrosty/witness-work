title: "WitnessWork ibika iki muri mushakisha yanjye?"
---

WitnessWork kuri murandasi ibika amakuru yawe n'uko winjiye muri mushakisha yawe, bityo ifungukira ku makuru yawe kandi igakora nta murandasi. Biguma aho kugeza usohotse.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gusohoka bisiba amakuru yawe n'uko winjiye muri iyo mushakisha. No gukuraho amakuru y'urubuga ni uko. Konti yawe n'amakuru yahuzwe ntibibura, igihe cyose ufite uburyo bwo kongera kwinjira. Reba [Nakora iki niba ntagishoboye kwinjira muri konti yanjye ya WitnessWork?](/help/account/lost-access).
- Nta gufunga kwikora. Umuntu wese ukoresha iyo mushakisha ashobora kuyifungura. Kuri mudasobwa dusangiye, sohoka urangije. Reba [Gusohoka kuri murandasi](/help/account/web-sign-out).
- WitnessWork ikora kuri tab imwe icyarimwe. Reba [Kuki WitnessWork ivuga ko ifunguye kuri indi tab?](/help/general/web-tabs).

## Safari

Safari isiba amakuru y'urubuga nyuma y'iminsi 7 utarusuye. Porogaramu ya murandasi iri ku Home Screen ya iPhone ntibigiraho. Iyo bibaye, ongera winjire, urugero ukoresheje passkey yawe, maze amakuru yawe yahuzwe agaruka. Impinduka zitari zahuzwe zirabura, bityo komeza Cloud Sync ifunguye uyiteze irangize.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Mushakisha yawe ntishobora kubika:** WitnessWork irakomeza gukora ikakubwira rimwe. Niba umwanya wuzuye, shyiraho umwanya ku gikoresho cyawe ugumane tab ifunguye. WitnessWork ibika impinduka zawe vuba bishoboka.
- **Uri mu idirishya ry'ibanga:** ayandi ntiyemerera imbuga kubika ikintu. Impinduka zirabura iyo ufunze tab, bityo koresha idirishya risanzwe.
