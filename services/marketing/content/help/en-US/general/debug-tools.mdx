id: debugTools
title: What does Debugging Tools do?
category: general
---

Debugging Tools helps you and support find and fix problems. It is meant for use when support asks.

## Turn it on or off

1. Open **Settings → About → App Info**.
2. Tap the version number under the app name 7 times. A check mark appears next to the version while it's on.

It applies only to the phone you turn it on for. Turning it on adds a **Tools** tab, shows extra technical details on some screens, and records more detail for support. Turning it on or off doesn't change your data. To hide the tab and details again, turn off the switch at the top of the **Tools** tab, or tap the version in **App Info** 7 times again.

## Good to know

- Some actions in **Tools** add sample data or permanently delete data. Use them only when support asks.
- Back up first under **Settings → Backup & Restore**.
