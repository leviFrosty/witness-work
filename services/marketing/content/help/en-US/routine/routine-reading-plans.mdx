id: routineReadingPlans
title: How do Bible reading plans work in Spiritual Routine?
category: routine
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

From version 1.47.0, Spiritual Routine's Bible reading can be a simple daily check or a reading plan. Plans work the same on iOS and Android.

To choose, open Settings → Features → Spiritual Routine and pick how you read the Bible, or tap Bible reading on the Routine card and then Start a reading plan:

- **Read today** (the default): one check for each day you read, whatever you read.
- **Bible in a year**: all 66 books in order, in 365 daily portions of about three chapters.
- **Bible in a year, in order of events**: the same chapters arranged roughly in the order things happened, for example Job after the early chapters of Genesis and the prophets beside the kings they served under. The order is approximate.
- **My own plan**: choose the book to start with and how many chapters to read a day (1 to 10). The plan goes through to Revelation and then from Genesis back to where you started.

With a plan, the Routine card on Home shows today's portion, like Genesis 1–3. Checking it off marks that portion read and also counts as your Bible reading for the day.

To see where you are, tap Bible reading on the Routine card (the item, not its circle). Its screen shows the plan's progress, like 12 of 365 read, then today's reading and the next few portions. To read ahead, check any of them: each one you check is marked read today. If you check a later portion and leave an earlier one, the earlier one stays first in the list until you read it. Change plan is on the same screen.

Portions you've read move into Completed, below the next portions, so the list always starts with what's next. Tap Completed to open it: it shows what you read and on which day, newest first, and Show earlier readings loads more. Tap a portion there to uncheck it. If it was that day's only portion, the day's Bible reading is unchecked too.

Start and finish dates: under the progress bar the screen shows when you started the plan and, at a reading a day, the day you'd finish. Tap that line to open Plan dates:

- **Started**: change it if you began before you set the plan up in the app, for example on paper.
- **Set a finish date**: turn it on to pick the day you want to finish by. For a plan you're keeping up with, the app first offers the plan's own length from your start date, such as December 31 for a Bible in a year plan started on January 1. The screen then says whether a reading a day gets you there, or how many extra readings or readings a day it takes. Turn it off to go back to the estimate.

Plans never fall behind. They move by portion, not by date: if you're away for a week or a month, the plan simply waits and shows the next portion you haven't read. There's no backlog to catch up on. Without a finish date, the estimate simply moves with you. With one, the date stays where you put it and only the readings a day change; if the date passes, the plan keeps going and you can pick a new date or turn it off.

Switching plans: you can pick another plan at any time. The new one starts at Day 1, and the plan you left keeps its progress, so if you switch back, you continue where you stopped. Choosing Read today keeps your plans' progress too.

Finishing: when you read a plan's last portion, a short celebration plays once. If you have a pet, it also earns 500 coins (see the question about coins). Afterwards you can start the same plan again or choose another.

The reading plans are WitnessWork's own chapter lists. Nothing is downloaded from jw.org, and they don't follow the meeting workbook's weekly Bible reading.

Your plan, its dates and the portions you've read sync between your devices and are saved in your backups, like the rest of Spiritual Routine.
