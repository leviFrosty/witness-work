---
id: scribeOffline
title: Does Scribe AI work offline?
category: scribe
---

No. Scribe AI needs an internet connection to understand what you say. While you’re offline, its button shows a no-internet badge, and tapping it explains why. You can still add time, contacts and plans yourself, and Scribe AI works again as soon as you’re back online. If the connection drops while Scribe AI is answering, it picks up again by itself when you’re back online, unless you’ve typed or sent something since; then tap Try again. If it drops while reading photos of paper pages, Scribe AI keeps the pages it hasn’t read: once you’re back online, tap Try again in the message box to read just those pages without choosing the photos again, or Skip unread pages to keep only what was read.
