title: Nongeraho nte mugenzi, kandi ni nde wemeza icyifuzo?
---

Fungura Buddies uri kuri Gahunda, cyangwa ukande Tumira bagenzi kuri gahunda nshya utaragira mugenzi, hanyuma ukande Tumira mugenzi cyangwa buto + kugira ngo usangize Ubutumire bwa Buddies. Kugira ngo muhuze imbonankubone, koresha buto ya kode kugira ngo werekane Kode yawe ya Buddies undi muntu ayisikane muri WitnessWork. Ushobora no gushyiramo ihuza ukoresheje Andika ihuza ry'ubutumire. Uwakiriye akanda Emera; uwakoze ubutumire akoherezwa gufungura Buddies → Ibisabwa agakanda Emeza. Utangira kubona gahunda za buri wese nyuma y'uko kwemeza gusa. Emeza gusa umuntu wari ugamije gutumira.
