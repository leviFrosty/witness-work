Bible studies are counted per contact, not per day. Each contact you study with counts as 1 study for the month, however many times you meet.

## Count a study

1. Open the contact.
2. Log a conversation.
3. Mark it **Conducted Bible Study**.

## Good to know

- To raise your study count, mark a study with a different contact.
- A contact someone shared with you is theirs, so their visits don't count for you. Once you log your own study with that contact, it counts.
