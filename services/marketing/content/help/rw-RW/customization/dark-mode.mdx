title: "Nahitamo uburyo bw'umwijima cyangwa urumuri buri gihe?"
---

Yego. WitnessWork ishobora gukurikiza igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe, cyangwa igakoresha buri gihe umwijima cyangwa urumuri.

## Hitamo isura

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Personalization → Itsinda ry'amabara**.
2. Hitamo **Igikoresho**, **Umwijima** cyangwa **Urumuri**.

**Igikoresho** gikurikiza igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe.
