Yes, if you turn it on. Hours you're short of your monthly goal can carry over to the next month.

## Turn on rollover

1. Open **Settings → Profile → Advanced**.
2. Turn on **Automatic time rollover**.

![Advanced profile settings with the Automatic time rollover switch](time-rollover.webp)

It shows only if you log hours. The carried hours appear when the new month begins, so you don't need to subtract or re-enter anything.
