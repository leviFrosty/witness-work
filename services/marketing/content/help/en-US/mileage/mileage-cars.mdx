title: What's the difference between archiving and deleting a car?
category: mileage
---

Open Mileage → Settings, tap the car, and choose Archive or Delete…. Archiving hides the car from new trips but keeps its trips in your reports; unarchive it anytime from Archived Cars. Deleting removes the car and all of its trips on every device that syncs with this one, and can't be undone. From version 1.47.0, a shared car can't be deleted, and archiving it hides it from new trips for everyone on the car; to stop using it yourself, leave it (see “What happens when I leave a shared car, I'm removed, or sharing stops?”). Deleting a fuel removes its price history, so cars using it stop showing an estimated cost.
