Yes. Open the contact's address screen and drag the pin to the correct spot. On the Map, you can also press and hold a contact's pin, then drag it. From version 1.47.0, a message with Undo appears after you move a pin there; tap Undo if you moved it by mistake. Directions and route planning use a moved pin instead of the address. You can also place a pin without an address at all, which is helpful in areas without precise geolocation data.
