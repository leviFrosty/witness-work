title: How do I use the Today's Conversations card on Home?
category: contacts
---

The **Today's Conversations** card on Home lists the follow-ups you planned for today. Use it to log each visit as you go.

## Use the card

1. Swipe sideways to move between people.
2. When you've been, tap **Talked** to add notes in the visit form. Or tap **Not at Home** to log the attempt right away. **Undo** appears below the card for a few seconds.
3. To change the date, tap the calendar button.

Tap a person to see their conversation and follow-up. The ••• button, or touching and holding a person, gives call, message, directions, **Open Contact**, and **Dismiss Follow-Up**.

## Good to know

- From 5 PM the card is called **Upcoming Conversations** and adds tomorrow's. You can reschedule those but not log them until their day.
- A follow-up is done once you log any visit with that person on or after its day. The bar at the top fills as you go, and the card shows **All caught up** when every one is done.
- A follow-up still open 4 hours after its time moves to missed follow-ups in the notifications tray.
- **Not at Home** isn't available with data protection mode on.

## Hide the card

- Tap **Done**, or touch and hold the title and choose **Close for Now**. The card returns when you add or reschedule a follow-up.
- To remove it for good, touch and hold the title, choose **Edit Home Screen**, and tap the minus on the card. **Add Sections** brings it back.
