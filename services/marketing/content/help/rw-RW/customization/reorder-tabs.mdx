title: "Nahindura uko tab zikurikirana?"
---

Yego. Uko zikurikirana gushya gukora ako kanya ku murongo wo hasi kuri telefoni no ku murongo w'ibumoso kuri tablet.

## Hindura uko zikurikirana

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Uko utubuto dutondetse**.
2. Kanda imyambi yo hejuru n'iyo hasi iruhande rwa tab. Cyangwa ufate izina rya tab kugira ngo uyimure hejuru cyangwa hasi.

## Byiza kumenya

- Tab zishobora gutondekwa ariko ntizishobora guhishwa.
- WitnessWork igifungukira Ahabanza.
- Aho bigeze higaragara gusa ku ruhare rwandika amasaha. Higumana umwanya wacyo iyo uhinduye uruhare.
- Kuri iPad, utuyoboro twa Command n'umubare dukurikiza uko wazitondetse.
- Uko zikurikirana guhuzwa n'ibindi bikoresho byawe iyo guhuza bifunguye, kandi ntigihindura amakuru yawe.
- Kugira ngo usubire uko byari, shyira tab ku Ahabanza, Contacts, Aho bigeze, Gahunda.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Uko zikurikirana ntibivugururwa:** ongera utangire porogaramu. Niba bikinanirwa, koresha **Uracyakeneye ubufasha?** mu Kigo cy'ubufasha.
