title: Can WitnessWork remind me to plan next week?
category: plans
platforms:
  - ios
  - android
---

Yes. If next week has no plans yet, WitnessWork sends one reminder at the time you choose.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Turn on **Remind Me to Plan Next Week**.
3. Choose **Remind On** and **Remind At**. It's Saturday at 6:00 PM unless you change it.
4. Allow notifications if WitnessWork asks.

## Good to know

- Weeks follow your Start of Week setting.
- Any plan in that week, including a recurring plan, means no reminder. Adding one later clears a reminder you already received.
- If every day is marked as an off day under Availability, you aren't reminded.
- Tap the reminder to open the **Schedule** at that week, where you can tap a day to plan it.
- The reminder also shows in the bell on Home until that week ends.
- It's available for every publisher role.
- Reminders are made on your phone from your own plans. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Open WitnessWork at least every two weeks to keep reminders coming.

## If something goes wrong

- **No reminders arrive:** make sure notifications are allowed for WitnessWork in your phone's settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is off. On Android, also check the Reminders category.
- **You planned on another device:** open WitnessWork on this phone so it can cancel the reminder.
- **Android is a few minutes late:** the system may delay reminders to save battery.
