title: Spiritual Routine ni nshi, elyo nshi nshila ya kuyipanga?
---

Spiritual Routine ifwa imwe ukwingisha ifyo mucita ifya mu mupashi pa mbali ya mulimo wenu wa kushimikila. Mulemba ifyo pa kadi lya Routine pa Home. Ni ya bwamba, ya kusala, elyo ibomba pa iPhone na Android.

## Ipangeni

1. Tinikeni **Set up** pa kadi lya **Spiritual Routine** pa Home. Nelyo mwisule **Ifyo Mwasala → Preferences → Spiritual Routine**.
2. Saleni ifyo mufwaya ukubikamo.
3. Saleni ifyo mubelenga Baibolo, inshiku shenu sha kulongana, ne bushiku bwenu bwa kulambila kwa mu lupwa.

Icibukisho ca cila bushiku cilipo lelo cishala cazimya mpaka mwacayaka. Nga mwatinika **Te nomba** pa kadi lya Home, lyafuma. Kuti mwalipanga pa numa mu Ifyo Mwasala.

## Lembeni ifyo mwacita

Tinikeni mutumba pa mpela ya cintu pa kadi lya Routine. Ilyo fyonse fya lelo fyapwa, kadi lifunya ukuba **All done today**.

Ico kuti mwalemba:

- **Bible reading:** umuku umo pa bushiku. Kuti mwakonka na plan ya kubelenga. Moneni [Ama plan ya kubelenga Baibolo yabomba shani mu Spiritual Routine?](/help/routine/routine-reading-plans)
- **Personal study:** bushiku bumo bumo mwasambilila. Ukufuma pa ntanshi kadi lipapa mpaka mwasambilila umuku umo pa mulungu. Kuti mwacinja icalimo ukuba inshita shimo pa mulungu, nelyo inshiku mwasala.
- **Midweek meeting prep** na **Weekend meeting prep:** umuku umo pa mulungu. Cimo cimo cilaboneka ukufuma pa nshiku shibili pa ntanshi ya kulongana ukufika pa bushiku bwa kulongana.
- **Family worship:** umuku umo pa mulungu, pa bushiku mwasala. Nga tamuli na lupwa, kuti mwalitumbula **Personal worship evening**.

## Moneni ifingi

- Tinikeni icintu ene, te mutumba wa co, ukwisula icikope caco. Cilanga heat map ya nshiku mwalemba, na **Recent days**, uko mwalemba bushiku mwalubile, ukubwelela ku kutendeka kwa mulungu uwapita. Tinikeni bushiku mwalemba ukububwesha.
- Tinikeni ishina lya kadi lya Routine ukumona ifintu fyonse mwayaka ne fyo mwafilemba kangahi pa mweshi uno.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Takuli ukukonkana. Ukubula bushiku tauleta ubwafya.
- Ukufisa kadi, lungikeni Home screen yenu elyo mufumyepo Spiritual Routine. Nelyo muzimye ici mu **Ifyo Mwasala → Preferences → Spiritual Routine**. Takuli ico mwalemba icabula.

## Nga cabipa

- **Icintu tacili pa Home:** ifintu fya pa mulungu fiboneka fye pa nshiku shifyo, elyo personal study ifisa ilyo mwafikila icalimo ca mulungu. Moneni **Ifyo Mwasala → Preferences → Spiritual Routine**.
- **Nacili tacili bwino:** bomfyeni **nomba line kabila afwilisha?** pa nshi ya cakusuka ici ukulembela support email.
