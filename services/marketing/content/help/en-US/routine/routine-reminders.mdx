id: routineReminders
title: Can WitnessWork remind me about my Spiritual Routine?
category: routine
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Yes. WitnessWork can send one reminder a day on iPhone and Android. It's off until you turn it on.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Turn on **Daily Routine Reminder**.
3. Choose a time. It's 7:00 PM unless you change it.
4. Allow notifications if WitnessWork asks.

## Turn it off

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Turn off **Daily Routine Reminder**.

Turning Spiritual Routine off stops the reminders too.

## Good to know

- The reminder lists only what's still open that day, like Bible reading and Midweek meeting prep.
- If everything is checked or nothing is due, no reminder comes.
- Tap the reminder to open WitnessWork and check things off.

## If something goes wrong

- **No reminder arrives:** notifications may be off for WitnessWork in your phone's settings. Turn them back on there.
