title: Mpisha nte mugenzi, nkareka gusangira, cyangwa nkasiba amakuru yanjye ya Buddies?
---

Fungura amakuru ya mugenzi uzimye Erekana kuri kalendari yanjye kugira ngo uhishe gahunda ze kuri iki gikoresho; gusangira birakomeza, kandi Gahunda zawe bwite hamwe na we ziracyagaragara. Kuraho mugenzi irangiza isano ku bantu bombi, kandi kongera guhuza bisaba ubutumire bushya. Ntibamenyeshwa, ariko bashobora kubibona. Niba gukuraho bitegereje udafite interineti, bashobora kubona gahunda zawe zasangijwe za nyuma kugeza WitnessWork yongeye guhuzwa ikarangiza. Igenamiterere rya Buddies → Siba amakuru yanjye ya Buddies ikuraho amasano yose n'ubutumire kandi isiba amakuru yawe ahishwe ya Buddies n'indangamuntu y'ihishwa. Ntishobora gusubirwamo; ongera uhuze kandi wongere ugerageze niba gusiba bitegereje. Inyandiko zawe bwite z'umurimo n'abantu birakomeza.
