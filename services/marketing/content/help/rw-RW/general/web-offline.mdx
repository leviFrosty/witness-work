title: "WitnessWork ikora nta murandasi kuri murandasi?"
---

Yego. WitnessWork kuri murandasi irafunguka nta murandasi kandi ibika impinduka zawe muri mushakisha yawe. Zihuzwa igihe usubiye kuri murandasi.

## Igihe nta murandasi ufite

Ikimenyetso kiravuga ngo: "Nta murandasi ufite. Subira kuri murandasi kugira ngo impinduka zihuzwe. Zibitswe kuri iki gikoresho kugeza icyo gihe." Komeza ukore.

- Guhuza, Scribe AI, no gushakisha ku ikarita n'inzira bitegereza kugeza usubiye kuri murandasi.
- Ururimi utarakoresha bukeneye murandasi ubwa mbere uruhisemo.

## Byiza kumenya

- Impinduka zitari zahuzwa ziba muri iyo mushakisha gusa. Zirabura iyo amakuru ya mushakisha asibwe, urugero iyo usohotse cyangwa ukuyeho amakuru y'urubuga. Reba [WitnessWork ibika iki muri mushakisha yanjye?](/help/general/web-storage).
- Niba umara igihe kirekire ukora nta murandasi, subira kuri murandasi ureke impinduka zihuzwe mbere yo gusohoka cyangwa guhindura mudasobwa.
