WitnessWork doesn't send your report anywhere by itself. How and to whom you submit is up to you and your service committee. Until last month's report is sent, the Service Report card on Home shows a Submit row for it, so you don't send this month's report before the month is over. If you already reported another way, touch and hold that row and choose Already Submitted. Open a month's Field Service Report, choose a method from the dropdown above the Submit button, and tap Submit. You can copy a plain-text summary, share it, or hand it straight to Hourglass or NW Publisher, which opens with your hours, Credit, and Bible studies filled in for you to review and send. NW Publisher only accepts the previous month's report. WitnessWork remembers your choice; you can also change it in Settings → Profile → Submission method. Once you submit, copy, or share a report, or tap Already Submitted on last month's reminder, the report and the month's card show Sent with the date. If that was a mistake, tap Sent at the top of the report, or press and hold the month's card, and choose Mark as Not Sent; last month's reminder then comes back. Opening Hourglass or NW Publisher counts only if it opens. If your device was offline when it opened, WitnessWork asks when you come back whether the report reached it, and marks it Sent only if you choose Mark as Sent. On Android, sharing doesn't mark the report, because the share sheet can't tell whether you sent it: after sending, tap Not Sent at the top of the report and choose Mark as Sent, or tap Already Submitted on the reminder.
