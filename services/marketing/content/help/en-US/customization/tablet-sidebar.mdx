title: How do I resize or hide the sidebar on my tablet?
category: customization
---

On a wide tablet window on iOS or Android, drag the small bar halfway down the sidebar's right edge to resize it. Narrowing the sidebar hides destination names and leaves their icons; widen it to show the names again. From version 1.46.0, the bottom of the sidebar holds your picture and name (tap them for Profile, Settings, and Help Center) above Settings, the page headers leave your picture out while the sidebar shows, and Buddies, when it's on, has its own place in the sidebar. Tap Hide sidebar beside WitnessWork (at the top of the icon-only sidebar) to hide navigation completely. Tap Show sidebar in the page header to restore your last saved width. Your width and visibility are saved on this device across app restarts; they are not synced or included in ministry backups, and resizing does not change your records. The sidebar needs a window at least 1,000 points wide and a supported text size. In a narrower window or with very large text, the bottom bar returns automatically; widen the window or rotate to landscape to use the sidebar again. Screen reader users can adjust the Resize sidebar control to increase or decrease its width. If the navigation still seems missing, restart the app and use Still need help? below; keep contact details and other private data out of public reports.
