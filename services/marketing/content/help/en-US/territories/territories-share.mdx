title: How do I share a territory with my buddies?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
platforms: [ios, android]
---

Open the territory, tap Share, pick what to share and who, and tap Share With. Your buddies can then record doors on it in WitnessWork, as you do, and every door shows who recorded it.

You need Buddies set up with at least one buddy (see [What is WitnessWork Buddies?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview)). If this device hasn't set up Buddies, With Buddies shows Set Up Buddies instead. Then:

1. Open the territory and tap Share, or touch and hold it in the list and tap Share.
2. Under What to Share, choose Whole Territory, or pick the streets to share.
3. Under With Buddies, tap each buddy you want, then tap Share With (for example, Share With Anna). You need a connection.

Each buddy gets an invitation in the notification bell on Home, along with a notification, and taps Join. It then appears in their Territories tab under Shared With Me. An invitation stays open for 7 days.

**What they can do:** record doors, mark streets worked, and add do-not-calls on what you shared, and see everything on it, including other people's doors and the do-not-calls. Someone given the whole territory also gets streets you add later. They can't edit the territory, add or delete streets, share it onward, or return it; only you can. Anything they record stays on the territory, with their name, even after they leave.

**Who it's shared with:** the territory's page lists everyone under Shared With, with what each person has. To stop sharing with someone, tap Stop beside their name. They keep a read-only copy, without its house-by-house records, until they remove it.

A territory holds up to 10 people, counting you and open invitations (see [What are the limits on sharing a territory?](/help/territories/territories-limits)). To share with someone who doesn't have WitnessWork, use a guest link (see [How do I share a territory with someone who doesn't have the app?](/help/territories/territories-guest-link)). To give one street to a buddy to work on their own, hand it off (see [How do I hand off a street to a buddy?](/help/territories/territories-hand-off)).
