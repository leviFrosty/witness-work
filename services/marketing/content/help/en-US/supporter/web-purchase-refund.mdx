---
id: webPurchaseRefund
title: Can I get a refund for a web purchase?
category: supporter
platforms:
  - web
---

Email [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) from the address you used at checkout, and tell us roughly when you paid. We review each request, and an approved refund goes back to the card you paid with; your bank may take several days to show it. You can't refund yourself from the subscription page.

A refund ends what it unlocked right away: refunding a subscription's latest payment ends that Supporter access, and refunding a Tip removes its Donor recognition. Cancelling a subscription is separate and doesn't refund it. To stop future payments too, cancel the subscription with **Manage** (see [How do I manage or cancel a subscription I bought on the web?](/help/supporter/web-manage-subscription)).

App Store purchases are refunded by Apple; ask through your App Store account. For Google Play purchases, ask in Google Play; if Google sends you to us, email support.
