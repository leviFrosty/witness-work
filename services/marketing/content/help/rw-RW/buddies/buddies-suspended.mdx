title: "Kuki Buddies ivuga ko yahagaritswe kuri njye?"
---

WitnessWork ishobora guhagarika Buddies ku muntu nyuma y'ikirego cy'ihohoterwa, nko guhoza mugenzi ku nkeke. Niba utekereza ko ari ikosa, vugana n'ubufasha tuzongera tuyifungure niba ikirego cyari kibeshya.

## Ibibaho igihe ihagaritswe

- Buddies ntishobora kohereza ubutumire cyangwa gusangiza Gahunda.
- Gahunda cyangwa gusubirayo utumiyemo bagenzi ntibyoherezwa. Porogaramu irabikubwira, kandi Gahunda yerekana **Ntibyoherejwe** iruhande rwabo. Ihindure Buddies imaze kongera gufungurwa kugira ngo yoherezwe.
- Ushobora gukomeza gukuraho bagenzi no gusiba amakuru yawe ya Buddies (**Igenamiterere rya Buddies → Siba amakuru yanjye ya Buddies**).
- Bagenzi bawe ntibamenyeshwa.

## Saba ubufasha

1. Kanda akabuto k'ubufasha kari mu mutwe, munsi ya **Uracyakeneye ubufasha?**.
2. Andikira ubufasha ushyiremo verisiyo yawe ya WitnessWork.
