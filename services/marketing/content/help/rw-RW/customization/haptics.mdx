title: Nzimya nte ibinyeganyega?
---

Fungura Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Amajwi n'Ibinyeganyega ukazimya Ibinyeganyega. WitnessWork noneho ireka ibinyeganyega byayo byose, nko gukanda, gukurura ku ikarita y'ibishushanyo, n'ibyishimo byo kugera ku ntego. Igenamiterere rikoreshwa kuri iki gikoresho gusa kandi ntirihuzwa n'ibindi bikoresho byawe. Ibikoresho bimwe bya sisitemu, nka menu na switch, bishobora gukomeza kunyeganyega bitewe n'igenamiterere ry'igikoresho cyawe ubwacyo; ugahindure mu igenamiterere ry'amajwi n'ibinyeganyega ry'igikoresho cyawe.
