"Anna asked to reconnect" means your buddy moved Buddies to a new secure connection. Tap **Reconnect** and you're buddies again once their phone confirms. Nothing else to do.

## Reconnect

1. Open the notification or **Buddies**, and find the request on their row.
2. Tap **Reconnect**.

You keep the nickname, color and sharing settings you had for them.

## Good to know

- Shared Plans and Follow-ups, requests to join and badge reactions with that buddy start fresh. Your own records, contacts and Plans stay as they are.
- If you don't reconnect within about a day, they leave **Buddies**. You can still tap **Reconnect** for 7 days. After that, ask them for a new invite.
- Dismissing the request ends your old connection with that buddy.
- If you changed your own account, your buddies get this request automatically. Each one who reconnects shows up again. The others show as "Waiting for … to reconnect" until they do or the request expires.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This request can't be checked yet":** try again later.
- **Reconnect says the invite isn't available:** it was used or expired. Ask them to send a new one from **Buddies**.
- **You don't see the request:** update WitnessWork. If the buddy simply left, pair again with a new invite.
