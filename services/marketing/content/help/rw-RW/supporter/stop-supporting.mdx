id: stopSupporting
title: "Bigenda bite iyo mpagaritse gufasha?"
---

Ugumana uburenganzira bw'Umufasha kugeza ku iherezo ry'igihe wishyuye. Nyuma yaho, ugumana amakuru yawe yose no guhuza, ibyongeweho by'Umufasha gusa ni byo birangira.

## Hagarika gufasha

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Gucunga ifatabuguzi**.
2. Kanda **Cancel support**.
3. Niba ubishaka, subiza ikibazo kigufi cy'impamvu. Ntikizwi nyirakwo kandi ntikigera kirimo amakuru yawe.

Ushobora kandi guhagarika mu mafatabuguzi yawe ya App Store cyangwa Google Play. Hagarika aho wiyandikishirije. Niba iki gikoresho kidashobora gucunga ifatabuguzi ryawe, koresha ihuza riri mu imeri y'inyemezabwishyu yawe.

## Ibigumaho

- Amakuru yawe n'ibyo wabitse kuri telefoni.
- Guhuza, ukoresheje Cloud Sync, iCloud Sync cyangwa Google Drive Sync, n'amafoto yawe kuri Cloud Sync.

## Ibirangira

- Ibara ryawe n'igishushanyo cya porogaramu bisubira ku bisanzwe. Ibyo wahisemo bibikwa kugeza wongeye gufasha.
- **Gutondeka mu buryo bwihuse** na **Tangira inzira** muri Tegura inzira bikeneye Umufasha nanone.
- Scribe AI isubira ku mupaka w'icyumweru w'ubuntu.

Niba ukeneye ikiruhuko gusa, **Cancel support** ibanza gutanga guhagarika by'igihe kuri iPhone, iPad na Android. Reba [Nahagarika inkunga yanjye by'igihe aho kuyihagarika burundu?](/help/supporter/pause-support).

Murakoze gufasha WitnessWork.
