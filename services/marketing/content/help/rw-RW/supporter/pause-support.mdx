title: Nshobora guhagarika inkunga yanjye by'agateganyo aho kuyisesa?
---

Yego. Fungura Igenamiterere → Gucunga ifatabuguzi. Kuri iPhone na iPad, hitamo guhagarika kwishyura amezi 1, 3, cyangwa 6. Guhagarika bitangira igihe cyawe cy'ubu kirangiye. Ntukatwa amafaranga muri icyo gihe, kandi ibiranga by'Umuterankunga bikomeza, harimo iCloud Sync, ibara ryawe nyamukuru n'ishusho ya porogaramu. Kwishyura bisubukura ubwabyo ku mubare umwe ku itariki yerekanwe, kandi ushobora gusesa igihe icyo ari cyo cyose mu mafatabuguzi yawe ya App Store. Ushobora guhagarika rimwe mu mezi 12; amatariki yo guhagarika abikwa hamwe n'igenamiterere ryawe kugira ngo ibindi bikoresho byawe bibyubahirize. Kuri Android, hitamo Hagarika muri Google Play kugira ngo uhagarike ifatabuguzi ry'ukwezi ku kwezi amezi 1 kugeza kuri 3. Ibiranga by'Umuterankunga nk'ikimenyetso n'ibara nyamukuru na byo birahagarara, hanyuma bigasubira ubwabyo igenamiterere ryawe rikabikwa, kandi ushobora gusubukura hakiri kare muri Google Play. Uburyo bwo guhagarika ntibugaragara mu gihe cy'igeragezwa ry'ubuntu, ikibazo cyo kwishyura, ifatabuguzi rya Family Sharing, cyangwa mu mezi 12 uhereye ku guhagarika kwawe kwa nyuma. Niba guhagarika binaniwe, nta cyahindutse; ongera ugerageze nyuma cyangwa ucunge ifatabuguzi ryawe mu iduka ryawe. Ku bindi, koresha Uracyakeneye ubufasha? kugira ngo uvugane n'ubufasha mu ibanga.
