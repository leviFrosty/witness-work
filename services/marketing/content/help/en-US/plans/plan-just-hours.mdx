---
id: planJustHours
title: Can I plan hours without picking a time?
category: plans
---

Yes. On the Plan form, tap the How long? button next to the date. Choose Just hours to plan the hours for that day without a time, or Time span to pick when you start and finish; the hours add up for you. New Plans open in whichever one you used last, so your usual way is ready next time. A Just hours Plan shows Anytime on your Schedule and counts toward your goals like any other Plan. With Notify Me on, it reminds you at 8:00 AM that day instead of before a start time. Buddies you invite see the day and how long, with no time. To give it a time later, edit the Plan and switch to Time span. If another of your devices still runs an older version of WitnessWork, it shows the Plan at noon until it updates.
