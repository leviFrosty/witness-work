title: Where are the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and open-source licenses?
category: general
---

Open **Settings → About**. It links to the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and WitnessWork's source code.

## Open Source Licenses

1. Tap your profile picture, then **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **About** and tap **Open Source Licenses**.
3. Search by package name, license, or author, then tap a package to see its full license.

## Good to know

- Where Scribe AI is available, **About** also links to the policy that covers what you send to Scribe AI.
- Touch and hold any link to copy or share it.
