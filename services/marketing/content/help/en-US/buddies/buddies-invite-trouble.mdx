title: Why is my Buddies Invite waiting, expired, or saying all spots are in use?
category: buddies
---

An invite waits until you confirm it, works once, and expires after seven days. If yours isn't working, make a new one.

## If something goes wrong

- **Waiting:** open **Buddies → Requests** and tap **Confirm**.
- **Used, canceled or expired:** ask for a new invite link.
- **All your spots are in use:** you can have 50 buddies, pending invites and requests together. Cancel an unused invite or remove a buddy to make room.
- **Making the invite failed:** tap **Try Again**. If the first one went through, WitnessWork shows it instead of making another. It is listed with your pending invites, where you can share or cancel it.
- **The link opens a web page:** install WitnessWork from the App Store or Google Play, then tap the link again. Or copy the link, open **Buddies**, tap **Enter Invite Link** and paste it.
- **You can't scan a code:** allow camera access for WitnessWork in your phone's settings, or paste the invite link instead.

## Good to know

- You can have up to five unused invite links open at once.
