title: "Nandika nte inzu uko mbwiriza umuhanda?"
---

Fungura agace, kanda umuhanda, hanyuma ukoreshe aho wandikira inzu hasi ku ekara. Herekana inzu ikurikira. Nyuma ya buri nzu, kanda ibyabaye.

## Andika inzu

- **Abatari mu rugo:** nta wakinguye. Inzu ijya ku rutonde rw'abatari mu rugo kugira ngo uzongere kugerageza. Kugerageza ku wundi munsi bibara indi nshuro, nka "Wagerageje inshuro 2".
- **Twaganiriye:** waganiriye n'umuntu. Kugira ngo usubire gusura, kanda inzu ku rutonde ukande **Ongeraho nk'umuntu**.
- **Ntibasurwe:** umuntu yasabye ko batamusura. Emeza, maze aderesi yongerwe ku "Ntibasurwe" z'agace n'itariki y'uyu munsi.

Aho wandikira hahita hajya ku nzu ikurikira. Kugira ngo usubize inyuma inzu wanditse, kanda **Subiramo** aho wandikira.

## Genda mu muhanda

- Kugira ngo wandike indi nzu, kanda imyambi (**Nomero ntoya**, **Nomero nini**) cyangwa wandike nomero yayo.
- **Simbukira ku nzu ikurikira** isimbuka inzu utari busure.
- Ku muhanda ufite nomero z'inyamibwa gusa cyangwa z'imibare ibiri, kanda **Hindura ujye ku ruhande rw'imibare ibiri** (cyangwa **Inyamibwa**) iyo ugeze hakurya.
- Kugira ngo urebe urutonde ruri inyuma y'aho wandikira, uhunge hasi cyangwa ukande ikiganza cyaho (**Hisha aho wandikira**). Kanda yongere kugira ngo igaruke.

## Rangiza umuhanda

Kanda **Andika ko umuhanda wabwirijwe**. Aho wandikira hakwereka umuhanda ukurikira, cyangwa inzira isubira ku gace. Ushobora kandi gukanda ufate umuhanda mu gace kugira ngo ugaragaze ko wabwirijwe, cyangwa utabwirijwe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ku muhanda udafite nomero z'amazu, inzu zibarwa (Urugi 1, Urugi 2). "Ntibasurwe" igusaba gusobanura aho hantu, nka "Irembo ry'ubururu ku mfuruka". Sobanura aho hantu, ntuzigere usobanura umuntu.
- Niba mugenzi ari kubwiriza uwo muhanda, inzu ze zigaragara ku rutonde n'izina rye. Iyo yanditse inzu aho wandikira herekana, hahita hajya ku ikurikira.
