On the Contacts map, pick contacts and tap **Plan Route**. They're added to today's route, most urgent first.

## Plan a route from the map

1. On the Contacts map, tap the select button (the dotted lasso in the column of buttons on the left).
2. Tap pins to pick contacts. Or draw a loop around pins with one finger to pick everyone inside it.
3. Tap a square pin with a count to pick everyone in it.
4. To add everyone in view who is overdue or due in the next seven days, tap the button for that in the bar.
5. Tap **Plan Route**.

Then use **Shortest Order**, set how long you'll stay at each stop, and tap **Start Route**. See [How do I plan a route for my service day?](/help/plans/route-planning).

## Good to know

- While you select, one finger draws instead of moving the map. Pinch to zoom, or tap **Done** to move the map again.
- A route holds up to 10 stops. Tap **Clear** to start over.
- Contacts already on today's route aren't added twice.
- **Shortest Order** and **Start Route** are Supporter features. Anyone can pick contacts and see the stops on the map.
- When today has a route, the **Today's Route** chip at the top of the map opens it.
- Picking contacts never changes them or their follow-ups. The route is saved on this phone for today only.

## If something goes wrong

- **Plan Route is dimmed:** pick at least one contact, and no more than the route has room for.
- **A contact you picked isn't on the route:** the route was full. The route builder says how many didn't fit.
- **Anything else:** use the Help Center's private support option, and keep names and addresses out of public reports.
