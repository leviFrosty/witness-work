title: What is WitnessWork Backup?
category: backups
---

WitnessWork Backup keeps a private copy of your records online, so you can restore them on a new phone, even from iPhone to Android. It's free and off until you turn it on. Only you can read it.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings → Account**.
2. Turn on **Back Up to WitnessWork**.

WitnessWork then backs up when your records change, when you leave the app, and at least once a day. Tap **Back Up Now** to back up right away. **Last Backup** shows when it last happened.

## Good to know

- It includes your service records, time, plans, contacts, visits, notes, categories, mileage, profile and shared settings.
- Photos and shared cars aren't included. Photos travel with [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync). Shared cars come back when you set up a new phone with your recovery key.
- This phone's own settings, such as data protection and analytics, aren't included.
- A backup can be up to 20 MB, and an account can keep backups from 8 devices.
- To move to a new phone, see [How do I restore my backup on a new phone?](/help/backups/account-backup-restore).

## If something goes wrong

If **Last Backup** shows a problem instead of a date, read the note under it:

- **Waiting for connection:** it continues on its own once you're online.
- **Update required:** update WitnessWork.
- **Too large:** your records are over 20 MB. Save a backup file in **Settings → Backup & Restore** instead.
- **Too many devices:** delete another device's backup first. See [How do I delete my backup?](/help/backups/account-backup-delete)
