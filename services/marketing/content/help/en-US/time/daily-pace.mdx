Add the Daily Pace card on the Progress tab's Month view (tap Edit Cards, then Add Cards); it's there by default for roles with a monthly goal. It takes what's left of your monthly goal and divides it by the days left in the month after today, the same count the month's report shows as days left. For example, with 25 of 50 hours done and 20 days left, it shows 1.3 hours a day (the figure rounds up slightly, so keeping to it is always enough). On the last day of the month, it shows everything that's left as today's amount. Make the card wide to also see about how much that is a week. Credit time counts up to its monthly limit, as on your report, and your plans aren't counted; the Projected Month card shows where your plans put you. Once you reach your goal, the card says so. For a past month it shows what you averaged a day, and for a future month what each day would need. It doesn't appear when the month has no goal. For the year, the Year Pace card does the same with your annual goal, spread over the months left in the service year.
