---
id: googleDriveSyncTroubleshooting
title: Google Drive Sync tailabomba pa cibombelo candi ca Android. Nshi kuti namona?
---

Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Sync** elyo mubelenge status. Lyonse ilanda ico mwingacita.

## Moneni ifi

1. Ifibombelo fyonse fibomfya Google Drive elyo fyatantika ku Google Account imo.
2. Google Play services yaikwa elyo yakushiwa.
3. Foni yenu ili pa intaneti. Ifyo mwacinja ilyo mwabula intaneti fitumwa ilyo mwabwela pa intaneti elyo WitnessWork yaisulwa.
4. Cibombelo conse cikwata WitnessWork iyapya. Kushani ica mutakusha.

## Nga cabipa

- **Status ilanda ukuti mutantike nakabili:** tinikeni **Tantika Google Drive nakabili** elyo musuminishe ukwingila nakabili. Google ipusha nakabili nga mwafumyepo ukwingila mu Google Account yenu.
- **Ubwikalo bwenu bwa Google bwaisula:** ifyo mwacinja fyashala pa foni iyi. Pangeni apa kuikala nelyo mushite ubwikalo ubwingi, elyo mutinike **Lingana nomba**.
- **Ilanda ukuti Google Drive yaikala:** Google ilecepa ifyo mwapempa pa nshita. Ifyo mwacinja fyashala pa foni iyi, elyo sync ileesha lwa mwine.
- **Ipusha ukwingila kwa Supporter:** kushani WitnessWork. Google Drive Sync ni ya bwamba.
- **Ichishasync:** sungeni ifailo ya backup ne foni ikwata ifyo mwalemba ifya kulekelesha, elyo mubomfye inshila ya support ya fyasuma mu Help Center. Mwisabika ifyebo fya bantu ba mu ng'anda mu fyebo fya bwafya fya bantu bonse.
