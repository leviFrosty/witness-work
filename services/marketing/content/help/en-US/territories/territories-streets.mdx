---
id: territoriesStreets
title: How do I add streets to a territory?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Open the territory and tap Add Street. Start with the first street on the card, then add the others in the order you'll work them.

For each street:

- **Street**: its name.
- **House Numbers**: Both sides (1, 2, 3), Odd side (1, 3, 5), Even side (2, 4, 6), or No house numbers. With no house numbers, doors are counted instead: Door 1, Door 2, Door 3.
- **First Number**: the house number you start at.
- **Numbers Go Down**: turn on when the numbers get smaller as you walk.
- **Save Where Each Door Is**: on by default. It saves where you're standing when you record each door, so it shows on the map.

You can change any of this later: open the street and tap Edit Street, or touch and hold the street and tap Edit Street. Delete Street is there too.

**Find Streets in the Outline** (iPhone, iPad, and Android): if you outlined the territory's area, tap Find Streets in the Outline under Streets. WitnessWork checks the map inside the outline and lists the street names it finds. They're best guesses from the map, so untick any that aren't on the card, then tap Add Streets. If none are found, add the streets from the card by hand. On Android, it asks to use your location to look up street names.
