title: Kuki Guhuza kalendari bikoresha igikoresho nyamukuru?
---

Igikoresho kimwe gusa ni cyo cyohereza Gusubirayo byawe kugira ngo birinde ivugurura rirushanwa. Igikoresho cya mbere gihuza kiba nyamukuru. Ku bikoresho bya iOS bikoresha konti imwe ya iCloud, fungura Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Guhuza kalendari kugira ngo wandike buri gikoresho, urebe urutonde rw'Igikoresho nyamukuru, kandi uhitemo igikoresho nyamukuru. Guhuza kalendari ubwabyo ntibikenera kuba Umuterankunga. Gusubirayo byahinduwe ku kindi gikoresho bigera ku gikoresho nyamukuru bicishijwe mu guhuza amakuru ya porogaramu gusanzwe, bikenera Umuterankunga na iCloud Sync ifunguye; fungura WitnessWork ku gikoresho nyamukuru kugira ngo bibyoherezwe. Nyuma yo kwimura, igikoresho gishya nyamukuru gikeneye kugera kuri konti imwe ya kalendari n'uruhushya rwuzuye rwa kalendari. Niba kwimura bitegereje, ongera ufungure WitnessWork ku gikoresho nyamukuru cya mbere kugira ngo kirangize igikorwa cyahagaze. Nta gufata inshingano ku buryo bwikora hashize igihe niba icyo gikoresho kitazongera kuboneka; koresha ubufasha bwihariye buri munsi ya Uracyakeneye ubufasha? mu Kigo cy'ubufasha. Gukuraho igikoresho cyashaje ku rutonde ntibisiba inyandiko zacyo cyangwa ibikorwa bya kalendari.
