Open the **Progress** tab. The **Month** view shows how many hours and days you have left, and whether you're ahead of or behind your plans.

## See your month

1. Tap **Progress** in the bottom bar.
2. Stay on **Month**. The report shows time so far and hours left.
3. Read the line under it to see if you're ahead of or behind your plans.

The **Daily Pace** card shows how much you need each day. Add it with **Edit Cards** if it isn't there. See [How much do I need each day to reach my goal?](/help/time/daily-pace)

## See your year

Pioneers can switch to the **Year** view.

1. Tap **Progress**, then **Year**.
2. The **Service Year Pace** chart shows how far ahead of or behind your Annual Goal you are today.
3. Each month's bar shows how that month did against its goal.

The **Year Pace** card shows how much you need each month for the rest of the service year. See [How do I read the Service Year Pace chart?](/help/time/service-year-pace)
