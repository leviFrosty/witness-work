---
id: accountBackupPrivacy
title: Can WitnessWork read my backup?
category: backups
---

No. Your backup is locked on your phone before it leaves, and only your own devices can unlock it. WitnessWork never sees your name, email, device name or records.

## Good to know

- Because of this, no one can recover your backup for you. If you lose every phone on your account and your recovery key, nobody can open it, including us.
- Keep your recovery key somewhere safe. To see it, open **Settings → Account → Show Recovery Key**.
