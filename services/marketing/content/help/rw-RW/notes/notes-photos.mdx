title: "Nongeraho nte amafoto ku nyandiko?"
---

Mu gihindura inyandiko, kanda akabuto k'ifoto, hanyuma **Fata ifoto** cyangwa **Hitamo amafoto**. Inyandiko ifata amafoto agera kuri 10.

Kuri murandasi, ushobora kandi kumanika ishusho wakoporoye cyangwa gukurura dosiye y'ishusho ku nyandiko.

## Hindura ubunini bw'ifoto

1. Mu gihe uhindura inyandiko, kanda ifoto.
2. Kurura mfuruka kugira ngo ugabanye cyangwa wongere.

## Reba cyangwa usangize ifoto

Kanda ifoto mu nyandiko kugira ngo uyirebe ku ekara yose cyangwa uyisangize.

## Byiza kumenya

- Amafoto agabanywa ubunini akabikwa ku gikoresho cyawe.
- Kuri murandasi, inyandiko umanika uvuye ku rupapuro zigumana amagambo yazo ariko si amashusho. Koporora ishusho ubwayo kugira ngo uyongereho. Niba murandasi idashobora gufungura dosiye, koresha JPEG cyangwa PNG.
- Urugendo ku modoka isangiwe nta kabuto k'ifoto. Inyandiko yarwo isangizwa na bose bari ku modoka, ariko amafoto si ko.
- Iyo uburyo bwo kurinda amakuru bufunguye, inyandiko z'amasura ntizishobora kugira amafoto, kuko ifoto y'urugo rw'umuntu ni amakuru bwite.
- Kugira ngo urebe amafoto ku bindi bikoresho byawe, harimo murandasi, koresha Cloud Sync, ayohereza yonyine, cyangwa ufungure **Huza amafoto y'aderesi** kuri iCloud (Google Drive kuri Android). Reba [Kuki ifoto ibura mu nyandiko?](/help/notes/notes-sync).
- Dosiye za backup no gusangiza umuntu bisiga amafoto.
- Gahunda utumirira bagenzi isangiza kandi amafoto y'inyandiko yayo. Mugenzi abona ikimenyetso cyo gufata umwanya kugeza ifoto irangije gukururwa. Amafoto asangijwe asibwa ukwezi nyuma ya Gahunda, cyangwa ako kanya iyo uyakuyeho, usibye Gahunda, cyangwa uhagaritse kuyisangiza.
