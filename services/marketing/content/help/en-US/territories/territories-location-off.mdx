title: Why isn't WitnessWork saving where the doors are?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

WitnessWork saves where a door is only when it can use your location, **Save Where Each Door Is** is on for the street, and you're at the street. Without location, everything else still works.

## Fix it

Look for one of these notices in the door recorder or on the territory map:

- **"Location is off for WitnessWork."** Tap **Open Settings** and allow location for WitnessWork. On Android the button may say **Allow Location**. On the web, allow this site to use your location in your browser's settings.
- **"WitnessWork only has your approximate location."** Tap **Open Settings** and turn on precise location.
- **"Location is turned off on this device."** Tap the button in the notice to turn location on for your phone.
- **"You're away from this street, so no pins for now."** If you really are at the doors, tap the pin icon in the door recorder, then **I'm at the Doors**.

## Other reasons

- **Save Where Each Door Is** is off for this street. Turn on the switch in the door recorder, or in **Edit Street**.
- Territory privacy mode is on. It keeps no door pins. See [What is territory privacy mode?](/help/territories/territories-privacy-mode)
- If no notice shows, your phone may not get a signal where you are, such as indoors.

## Pin a door by hand

1. Open the street and tap the house.
2. Tap **Add Pin**.
3. Tap the map where the door is.

This works without location.
