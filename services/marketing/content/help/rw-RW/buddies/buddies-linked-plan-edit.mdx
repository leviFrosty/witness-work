title: "Kuki ntashobora guhindura itariki cyangwa isaha ya Gahunda mugenzi wanjye yantumiyemo?"
---

Uwateguye ni we nyiri itariki, isaha, umutwe, ahantu n'inyandiko ya Gahunda watumiwemo, kugira ngo abazajya bose babone Gahunda imwe. Ushobora guhindura amasaha yawe bwite, inyibutsa n'ubwoko.

## Hindura igice cyawe

1. Fungura Gahunda ukande **Hindura**.
2. Hindura **Amasaha yawe**, **Menyesha njyewe** cyangwa **Ubwoko**.

Paji ya Gahunda ihita yerekana amasaha yawe iruhande y'ay'uwateguye, urugero "Anna yateganyije amasaha 3". Subiza amasaha yawe ku y'uwateguye kugira ngo ukurikize ayo. Gahunda yawe n'aho ugeze bibara amasaha yawe.

## Ukeneye indi tariki cyangwa isaha?

- Saba uwateguye guhindura Gahunda.
- Cyangwa hitamo **Sinshobora** kuri paji ya Gahunda wongereho Gahunda yawe bwite. Gusiba Gahunda ihuje ni kimwe na **Sinshobora**.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Impinduka z'uwateguye ntizakugezeho:** fungura WitnessWork ufite murandasi. Impinduka zigera mu munota mu gihe porogaramu ifunguye.
