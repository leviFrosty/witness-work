title: "WitnessWork ishobora kunyibutsa igihe raporo yanjye yiteguye koherezwa?"
---

Yego. WitnessWork ishobora kukwibutsa ku itariki ya 1 ya buri kwezi ko raporo y'ukwezi gushize yiteguye. Ntiyoherereza raporo yawe.

## Fungura inyibutsa

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Amamenyesha**.
2. Fungura **Unyibutse gutanga raporo yanjye**.
3. Niba ubibajijwe, emera amamenyesha.

Ushobora kandi gukanda **Unyibutse** igihe ekara ya raporo ibitanga nyuma yo kohereza.

## Byiza kumenya

- Inyibutsa iza ku gihe cya **Unyibutse saa**. Ni saa tatu za mu gitondo keretse uhindure.
- Kanda inyibutsa kugira ngo ufungure raporo y'uko kwezi, hanyuma uyohereze nk'uko usanzwe ubikora.
- Nta nyibutsa ku kwezi nta kintu cyanditswemo, cyangwa ku raporo wamaze kohereza.
- Buri gikoresho gishyiraho inyibutsa yacyo. Niba wohereje ku kindi gikoresho, fungura WitnessWork kuri iki kugira ngo kibimenye.
- Kugeza wohereje, raporo y'ukwezi gushize inagaragara ku ikarita ya **Raporo y'umurimo** ya Ahabanza no mu nzogera ya Ahabanza, ndetse inyibutsa zizimije.
- Niba wayohereje ukundi, kanda ⋯ ku ikarita uhitemo **Yandike ko yoherejwe**.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta nyibutsa yaje:** reba ko amamenyesha yemerewe WitnessWork mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe, kandi ko Focus cyangwa Do Not Disturb bitayacecekesha. Kuri Android, reba kandi icyiciro cyayo cya Reminders.
- **Kuri Android yaje yatinze iminota mike:** telefoni yawe ishobora gutinza inyibutsa kugira ngo ibike umuriro.
- **Biracyakomeye:** menyesha ikibazo ukoresheje Ikigo cy'Ubufasha, nta mazina cyangwa andi makuru bwite.
