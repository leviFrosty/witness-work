title: I deleted or returned a territory by mistake. Can I get it back?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

It depends on what you removed.

## A territory you returned by mistake

Yes, you can get it back.

1. In the **Territories** tab, tap **Show returned territories**.
2. Open the territory.
3. Tap **Move Back to Checked Out**.

The streets, which streets were worked, and the do-not-calls come back. The not-at-homes and other house records were deleted when you returned it and can't be recovered. If it was shared, sharing ended. Share it again.

## A door or street removed by mistake

- **A door:** right after you remove it, tap **Undo** in the door recorder.
- **A street:** a deleted street can't be brought back. Add it again.

## A deleted territory

**Delete Territory** can't be undone, and it deletes the territory on all your synced devices. Add it again from the card. If it was shared, deleting it also stopped sharing. Share it again too.

## A territory someone shared with you

If you left it or removed it from your list, ask the owner to share it with you again.
