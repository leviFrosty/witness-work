title: Ni iki kiri ku rupapuro rwa mugenzi, kandi nshobora kumuhindurira izina cyangwa ibara?
---

Fungura Buddies ukande mugenzi. Bikeneye igisubizo cyawe ni urutonde rw'ubutumire bwe utarasubiza; hitamo Ndagenda cyangwa Sinshobora aho cyangwa mu nzogera y'amamenyesha yo ku Ahabanza. Hamwe ni urutonde rwa Gahunda na Gusubirayo bizaza hagati yanyu, uwatumiye uwo ari we wese, hamwe na buri gisubizo; kanda Gahunda kugira ngo ufungure urupapuro rwayo, cyangwa ukoreshe menu iri ku butumire bwa mugenzi kugira ngo uhindure igisubizo cyawe. Ibyumweru bibiri bikurikira bigaragaza iminsi mugenzi wawe ateganya kujya kubwiriza iruhande rw'iyawe; kanda umwe mu minsi ye kugira ngo uteganye igihe kimwe cyangwa usabe kwifatanya. Tumira muri Gahunda ifungura Gahunda nshya na mugenzi uwo asanzwe yatumiwe, naho Reba kuri kalendari ifungura Ingengabihe. Izina ry'irihimbano ni izina ry'ibanga ubona wenyine; mugenzi wawe ntabimenyeshwa, kandi kurikuraho bigarura izina rye bwite. Abaterankunga bashobora guhitamo ibara rya mugenzi muri ibara rimwe n'ibara nyamukuru, cyangwa ibara iryo ari ryo ryose bakoresheje eyedropper; Ibisanzwe bigarura ibara ryatanzwe igihe mwahujwe, ibyo abandi bose bakomeza kugira. Amazina y'irihimbano n'amabara bibikwa hamwe n'amakuru yawe ahishwe ya Buddies, bityo bigaruka iyo Buddies igarurwa ku kindi gikoresho. Gahunda ye yose y'ibyumweru umunani biri imbere iri kuri kalendari yawe mu gihe Erekana kuri kalendari yanjye ifunguye.
