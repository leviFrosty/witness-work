---
id: iCloudSync
title: "Nshobora guhuza amakuru yanjye hagati y'ibikoresho?"
---

Yego. Kuri iPhone na iPad, iCloud Sync ituma inyandiko zawe zingana ku bikoresho byawe bikoresha konti imwe ya iCloud. Ni ubuntu. [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync) ni ubundi buryo bukora kandi kuri Android na murandasi.

## Bifungure

Ibi ubikore kuri buri iPhone cyangwa iPad:

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Guhuza**.
2. Hitamo iCloud.
3. Koresha konti imwe ya iCloud kuri buri gikoresho.

Ihuza inyandiko zawe z'umurimo, abantu, gusura n'ibyo ukunda. Amafoto afite akabuto kayo ka **Huza amafoto y'aderesi**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Igikoresho gihuza n'uburyo bumwe icyarimwe, kandi gusa n'ibikoresho byawe biri ku buryo bumwe. Hitamo bumwe kuri buri kimwe. Reba [Mpindura nte hagati ya Cloud Sync na iCloud cyangwa Google Drive?](/help/backups/cloud-sync-switch).
- Nta murandasi, uko bihagaze bivuga **Utegereje murandasi**. Impinduka zawe zibikwa kandi zihuzwa usubiye kuri murandasi.
- Niba igikoresho cyinjiye muri Apple Account itandukanye, iCloud Sync izima kuri cyo kandi inyandiko zawe zigumya ku gikoresho. Fungura guhuza bundi bushya kugira ngo uhitemo uko uzihuriza n'amakuru y'iyo konti.
- Kuri Android, koresha [Google Drive Sync](/help/backups/google-drive-sync) hagati y'ibikoresho bya Android.
- Guhuza kalendari ni ikindi kintu. Byongera Gusubirayo kwawe kuri kalendari kandi ntibihuza inyandiko zawe.
- Kugira ngo wimukire kuri telefoni nshya, reba [Nimukira nte kuri telefoni nshya cyangwa nkava kuri iPhone nkajya kuri Android?](/help/account/account-handoff).

Niba guhuza bihagaze, reba [Ibikoresho byanjye ntibihuza](/help/backups/sync-troubleshooting).
