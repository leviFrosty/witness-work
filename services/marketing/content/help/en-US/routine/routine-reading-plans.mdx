Your Bible reading can be a simple daily check, or a reading plan that shows you each day's portion. Plans work the same on iPhone and Android.

## Choose a plan

1. Open **Settings → Features → Spiritual Routine** and pick how you read the Bible. Or tap **Bible reading** on the Routine card, then **Start a reading plan**.
2. Choose one:
   - **Daily:** one check for each day you read, whatever you read. This is the default.
   - **Bible in a year:** all 66 books in order, in 365 daily portions of about three chapters.
   - **Bible in a year, in order of events:** the same chapters, arranged roughly in the order things happened.
   - **My own plan:** choose the books and how much to read each day. See below.

## Make your own plan

Use **My own plan** to read part of the Bible, or to read at your own pace.

1. Pick the first book in **From** and the last book in **To**. For example, pick Psalms and Proverbs.
2. In **Read**, pick **Whole chapters** or **A few verses**.
3. In **Each day**, pick how much to read. Chapters go from 1 to 10 a day. Verses go from 2 to 20 a day, and start at 5.

Under **Each day**, the app shows about how many minutes a day takes and how long the plan lasts. Change the books or the amount until it fits your time.

If you read verses, each day stays inside one chapter. A short chapter is one day by itself.

## Read with a plan

The Routine card on Home shows today's portion, like Genesis 1–3. Checking it marks that portion read and counts as your Bible reading for the day.

To open today's portion in JW Library, tap the open button next to it on the Home card. Or tap **Bible reading**, then **Read in JW Library**. JW Library opens at the first chapter or verse of the day. If JW Library is not on your device, the reading opens on jw.org.

To see where you are, tap **Bible reading** on the card. The screen shows your progress, today's reading, and the next few portions. You can read ahead by checking a later one. Portions you've read move into **Completed**. Tap a portion there to uncheck it.

## Set dates

Tap the dates line under the progress bar to open **Plan dates**.

- **Started:** change it if you began before you set up the plan in the app.
- **Set a finish date:** turn it on to pick the day you want to finish by. The screen says whether one reading a day gets you there, or how many extra readings it takes.

## When you finish a plan

On the last day, the app tells you it is the last reading. When you check it, a short celebration plays. Then your Bible reading goes back to **Daily**.

To start another plan, tap **Choose another plan** on the celebration. To read the same plan again, choose it again and tap **Read it again**.

## Good to know

- Plans never fall behind. If you're away, the plan waits and shows the next portion you haven't read.
- You can switch plans any time. The new plan starts at Day 1, and the old one keeps its progress.
- If you have a pet, finishing a plan earns it 500 coins. See [How does Spiritual Routine earn coins for my pet?](/help/routine/routine-coins)
- These are WitnessWork's own reading lists. They don't follow the meeting workbook's weekly reading.
- Your plan and progress are on your other devices and in your backups.
