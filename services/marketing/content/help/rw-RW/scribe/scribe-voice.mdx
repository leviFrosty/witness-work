title: "Kuki ntashobora kuvugisha Scribe AI?"
---

Ijwi rikeneye telefoni yawe gusobanukirwa imvugo mu rurimi rwa porogaramu yawe, kandi rikeneye kugera kuri mikoro. Niba ijwi ridashoboka, Scribe AI ifungukana clavier kugira ngo wandike.

## Ibyo kureba

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere** rya telefoni yawe urebe ko **Mikoro** ifunguye kuri WitnessWork.
2. Niba indi porogaramu ikoresha mikoro, ifunge wongere ugerageze.
3. Ubwa mbere uvuga mu rurimi, tegereza akanya. Telefoni yawe ishobora kuba iri gukurura urwo rurimi.

## Byiza kumenya

- Kuri Android, ijwi rikeneye Android 12 cyangwa irenga hamwe no gusobanukirwa imvugo kuri telefoni yawe ku rurimi rwawe.
- Murandasi nta ijwi ifite, bityo Scribe AI ihora ifungukana clavier aho.
- Niba guhamagara cyangwa guhindura porogaramu bihagaritse kwandika, ibyo Scribe AI yumvise bitegereza mu gasanduku k'ubutumwa. Birangize ukande **Ohereza**.
