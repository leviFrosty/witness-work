id: badgesKeep
title: Can I lose my badges, move them to a new device, or hide them?
category: badges
---

No. Badges are keepsakes. Once you have one, it stays, even if you later delete the time, visits or plans behind it, or a buddy leaves.

## Move badges to a new phone

Restore your backup on the new phone and your badges come back. If sync is on, they also appear on your other devices.

## Hide badges

1. Open **Settings → Features → Streaks & Badges**.
2. Turn off **Show badges**.

This hides them everywhere, including from your buddies and celebrations. Turn it back on and they return.

## Good to know

- Progress toward the next level is kept too when data protection mode suggests deleting old details.
- When imported or restored history reaches new badges, they're added quietly with one summary, not a celebration for each. While you set up WitnessWork, they're added with no summary at all.
