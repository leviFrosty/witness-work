Yes. You can change or remove one day without touching the rest of your recurring plan. It keeps going on every other day.

## Skip one day

1. Open the **Schedule** tab and tap the day.
2. Tap the plan to open it.
3. Tap **More (•••)**, then **Delete**, then **Delete This Plan**.

If the day has more than one plan, do this for each one.

## Change one day

1. Open the **Schedule** tab, tap the day, and tap the plan.
2. Tap **Edit** (the pencil) and make your changes.
3. Save, and choose **Only this date**.

## Good to know

- If you change the Type, title, place, or **Notify Me** reminder for one day, that day becomes its own one-time plan.
