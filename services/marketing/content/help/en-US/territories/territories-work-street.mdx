id: territoriesWorkStreet
title: How do I record doors as I work a street?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Open the territory, tap the street, and use the door recorder at the bottom of the screen. It shows the next house; after each door, tap what happened:

- **Not at Home**: nobody answered. The house goes on the not-at-home list so you can try again. Trying it again on another day counts another try, such as "Tried 2 times".
- **Spoke**: you talked with someone. Tap the house in the list and tap Add as Contact to make a return visit.
- **Do Not Call**: the person asked not to be called on. WitnessWork asks you to confirm, then adds the address to the territory's do-not-calls with today's date.

The recorder then moves to the next house. To record a different house, tap the arrows (Lower number, Higher number) or type its number. Skip to the Next House jumps past a house you aren't calling on. On a street with only odd or even numbers, tap Switch to the Even Side (or Odd Side) when you cross the street. On a street without house numbers, doors are counted (Door 1, Door 2), and a do-not-call asks you to describe the place, such as "Blue gate at the corner"; describe the place, never the person.

Right after you record a door, tap Undo in the recorder to take it back. To see the list behind the recorder, swipe the recorder down or tap its handle (Hide Recorder), and tap it again to bring it back.

When you've finished the street, tap Mark Street Worked. The recorder then offers the next street, or a way back to the territory. Touch and hold a street in the territory to mark it worked, or not worked, without opening it.

If a buddy is working the same street, their doors appear in the list with their name, and if they record the house your recorder is showing, it moves on to the next one.
