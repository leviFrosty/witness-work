---
id: scribeWhatItDoes
title: What can Scribe AI do?
category: scribe
---

Scribe AI can log time, including in a category such as LDC or a new one you name, change or delete time you logged, create Day Plans and change their date, start time or length, add contacts and update their name, phone, email or address, and log visits with follow-ups. It can also answer questions about your time this month or service year, your plans, your contacts and visits, and the plans your buddies share with you. Everything it would change appears as cards for you to review first. Apart from time you logged, it can’t delete anything, change your settings, create or change Recurring Plans, invite buddies, or send anything to anyone. When you plan with a buddy, the plan’s card reminds you to invite them once it’s added. If a visit note reads like instructions to an AI, Scribe AI may skip it and say it couldn’t read it.
