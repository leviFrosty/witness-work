Buddies notifications tell you who did what, such as "Anna invited you to a Plan", "Joe can't make it" or "Levi earned a badge". They use the nickname you gave that buddy.

## Choose which ones you get

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Turn **Buddies Notifications**, **Ask to Join Alerts** and **Badge Alerts** on or off.

For one buddy, open their page, tap the pencil (**Edit Buddy**), then **Sharing & Alerts**.

## Good to know

- A Plan notification shows its day and time, never its title, place or note.
- A Follow-up notification shows only its day and time, never the householder's name, address or topic.
- Right after your phone restarts, before you unlock it, you may get a general notification such as "New invitation". Open the notification bell on Home to see who it's from.
- Notifications can show on your lock screen. To hide what they say, change your phone's lock screen notification settings.
- Everything also appears in the bell. What you read or clear there is cleared on your other devices too.

## If something goes wrong

- **Notifications never arrive:** make sure notifications are allowed for WitnessWork in your phone's settings, then open **Buddies** to refresh. On Android, also check its Buddies categories.
- **Android:** Buddies notifications need Google Play services.
- **Notifications don't name anyone:** update WitnessWork.
- **Still nothing:** report it from the Help Center, without names or other private details.
