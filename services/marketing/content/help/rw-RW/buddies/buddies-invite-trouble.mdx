title: Kuki Ubutumire bwanjye bwa Buddies butegereje, bwararangiye, cyangwa buvuga ko imyanya yose ikoreshwa?
---

Icyifuzo cyakiriwe gitegereza kugeza uwatumiye acyemeje muri Buddies → Ibisabwa. Buri Butumire bwa Buddies bukora rimwe kandi burarangira nyuma y'iminsi irindwi; saba ihuza rishya niba ryarakoreshejwe, ryarahagaritswe cyangwa ryararangiye. Imyanya yawe itanu ibarwamo bagenzi n'ubutumire cyangwa ibyifuzo bitegereje. Hagarika ubutumire budakoreshwa cyangwa ukureho mugenzi kugira ngo habeho umwanya. Umubare muto gusa w'amahuza y'ubutumire adakoreshwa ushobora kuba ufunguye icyarimwe. Niba gukanda ihuza ry'ubutumire bifungura urubuga aho gufungura WitnessWork, shyiraho WitnessWork iva muri App Store cyangwa Google Play ukande ihuza nanone, cyangwa ukoporore ihuza ukarishyira muri Buddies → Andika ihuza ry'ubutumire. Niba gusikana bidashoboka, emerera kamera mu igenamiterere ry'igikoresho cyawe cyangwa usangize kandi ushyiremo ihuza ry'ubutumire.
