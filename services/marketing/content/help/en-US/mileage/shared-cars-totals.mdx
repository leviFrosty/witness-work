Totals on Home, Progress, and Mileage count everyone's trips on a shared car. To see only one person's trips, use the **Whose Trips** menu.

## See only your trips

1. Open **Mileage**.
2. Tap the **Whose Trips** menu.
3. Choose **Everyone**, **Me**, or one person.

The summary, chart, trip list, **Share Report**, and the spreadsheet file all follow your choice. A filtered report says whose trips it holds, such as "Logged by: Anna."

## See who logged a trip

- In the trip list, a trip someone else logged shows their name.
- **Trip Details** shows **Logged By**, and **Edited By** when someone else changed it.
- A trip's share text and the spreadsheet file also say who logged it.

## Good to know

- When two people have the same name, the car's name is added, like "Ben · Family Minivan."
- Estimated costs use the car's own fuels and prices, so everyone sees the same estimate.
