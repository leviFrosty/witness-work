title: How do I switch between Cloud Sync and iCloud or Google Drive?
category: backups
platforms:
  - ios
  - android
---

Open **Settings → Sync** and choose Cloud Sync, iCloud (Google Drive on Android), or Off. All three are free.

## Good to know

- Switching keeps everything on this device and combines it with your devices on the new option. Nothing is replaced, and nothing you deleted comes back.
- Leaving Cloud Sync removes this device's copy from WitnessWork's server. Your other devices keep what they've already synced.
- Leaving iCloud or Google Drive leaves this device's last copy there. Your other devices keep syncing with each other, but not with this one.
- To sync all your devices together, choose the same option on each.
- Photos travel with Cloud Sync on their own. With iCloud or Google Drive, turn on **Include images in sync**.
- WitnessWork on the web uses only Cloud Sync, so using it needs your phone on Cloud Sync.
