---

Suggested is how the Contacts list is sorted to begin with. It puts the people you most likely want to see next at the top, in short sections, so you don't have to search.

## What you'll see

When you aren't searching, the list may start with these sections, each showing up to 5 contacts:

- **Nearby:** contacts within about 250 m of you, closest first.
- **Follow-ups Due:** follow-ups due today, or missed in the last 30 days.
- **Recent:** contacts you visited in the last 2 weeks.
- **Been a While:** people you last talked with 3 weeks to 4 months ago who have no follow-up planned. A **Not at Home** doesn't count as talking.

Everyone else is under **Other Contacts**: favorites first, then Bible studies, then the most recent visit. Empty sections are hidden. Each contact appears only once.

## Change the order

1. Open **Contacts**.
2. Tap **Sort & Filter**.
3. Pick a sort. Tap **Reset** to go back to **Suggested**.

## Take someone out of Been a While

Touch and hold the person and choose **Not Now**. They stay out for 30 days. You can bring them back from **Dismissed Contacts** anytime.

## Good to know

- Your filters and saved views apply first.
- Searching, or choosing another sort, shows one plain list.
- **Nearby** appears only if you've already allowed location for WitnessWork. Your location is used only to sort the list, and it isn't saved or sent anywhere.
- With data protection mode on, **Been a While** stops at 90 days.

## If something goes wrong

- **Nearby is missing:** check that location is allowed for WitnessWork in your phone's settings, and that the contact has a pin on the map.
