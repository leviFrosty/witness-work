id: menuBar
title: Nakoresha nte menu bar kuri iPad?
---

Kuri iPadOS 26 cyangwa irenzeho, koresha Windowed Apps cyangwa Stage Manager maze unyereze urutoki umanuka uvuye ku nkombe yo hejuru kugira ngo ugaragaze menu bar. Nyuma yo kuyitegura, Amahitamo ya WitnessWork afungura igenamiterere ryo muri porogaramu; ikintu gisanzwe Igenamiterere gifungura igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe. Dosiye ifungura Andika gusura…, Ongeraho igihe iyo kwandika amasaha bifunguye, Ongeraho umuntu, Gahunda nshya, na Kubika no Kugarura. Hindura → Shakisha umuntu bifungura urutonde rw'abantu kandi bigashyira ikarita ku gushakisha. Ubufasha bufungura Ikigo cy'ubufasha, Ibishya, porogaramu yawe ya imeri ku bufasha bwa bwite, cyangwa Genzura ibishya. Ibi byoroshyo bikoresha ecran zisanzwe; gufungura fomu nta byo bibika cyangwa ngo bisangize inyandiko zawe. Genzura ibishya igenzura niba hari ivugurura rya porogaramu rihuye kandi ikoresha uburyo busanzwe bwo gukuramo no gutangira bundi bushya iyo ririho. Ntishobora gushyiraho verisiyo nshya ya porogaramu, kandi ntiboneka mu miterere y'iterambere. Gushyigikirwa kwa menu bar bisaba porogaramu yashyizweho irimo ubwo bushobozi kandi ntiboneka kuri iPhone cyangwa Android. Niba ibintu bibura, vugurura ukoresheje iduka rya porogaramu maze ufungure WitnessWork bundi bushya. Ushobora gukoresha ibikorwa bimwe mu porogaramu. Ku bindi bibazo, koresha aho imeri ya bwite iri mu Kigo cy'ubufasha kandi ntushyire amakuru bwite n'ibyabitswe mu bitekerezo rusange.
