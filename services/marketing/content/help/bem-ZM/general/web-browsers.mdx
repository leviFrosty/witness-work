---
id: webBrowsers
title: Nshi browsers WitnessWork ibombela pa web?
---

WitnessWork pa web yeshiwa mu Chrome, Safari, na Firefox iipya, pa makompyuta, foni, ne matablet. Sungeni browser yenu ipya. Ama browsers yambi yapya, nga Microsoft Edge, kuti yabomba lelo tayeshiwa.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ukwingila na passkey kwafwaya browser na password manager nelyo icibombelo icisuminisha passkeys. Moneni [Passkey ni nshi?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
- WitnessWork isunga ifyo mwalemba mu browser. Nga ifyo fyafumishiwapo, mwingila nakabili, elyo ifyo mwacinja ifyashasyncwa fyabula. Moneni [Cinshi WitnessWork isunga mu browser yandi?](/help/general/web-storage).

## Nga cabipa

- **Ukwingila na passkey takupeelwa nelyo kulebwekeshako:** saleni **Use my phone** nelyo **Bomfya Icisokoshi ca Kubwesha** pa cikope ca kwingila.
