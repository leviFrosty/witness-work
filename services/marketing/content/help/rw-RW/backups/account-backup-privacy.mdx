title: "WitnessWork ishobora gusoma backup yanjye?"
---

Oya. Backup yawe ifungirwa kuri telefoni yawe mbere yo kuva, kandi ibikoresho byawe gusa ni byo bishobora kuyifungura. WitnessWork ntibona izina ryawe, imeri, izina ry'igikoresho cyangwa inyandiko zawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ni cyo gituma nta wushobora kugarura backup yawe ngo ayigusubize. Niba utakaje telefoni zose za konti yawe n'urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura, nta wushobora kuyifungura, harimo natwe.
- Bika urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura ahantu hizewe. Kugira ngo ururebe, fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Erekana urufunguzo rwo kugarura**.
