title: Mpindura nte ubugari bw'umurongo w'ibumoso cyangwa nkawuhisha kuri tablet yanjye?
---

Ku idirishya rigari rya tablet kuri iOS cyangwa Android, kurura akabari gato kari hagati mu nkombe y'iburyo y'umurongo w'ibumoso kugira ngo uhindure ubugari bwawo. Kugabanya umurongo bihisha amazina y'aho ujya bigasigaramo amashusho yabyo gusa; wongere ubugari kugira ngo amazina agaruke. Kanda Hisha umurongo w'ibumoso iruhande rwa WitnessWork (hejuru y'umurongo w'ibumoso urimo amashusho gusa) kugira ngo uhishe kujya ahandi burundu. Kanda Erekana umurongo w'ibumoso mu mutwe w'urupapuro kugira ngo ugarure ubugari bwawe bwa nyuma bwabitswe. Ubugari bwawe n'uko bugaragara bibikwa kuri iki gikoresho nubwo wafunga porogaramu; ntibihuzwa kandi ntibishyirwa muri backup z'umurimo, kandi guhindura ubugari ntibihindura inyandiko zawe. Umurongo w'ibumoso ukenera idirishya rya nibura points 1.000 z'ubugari n'ingano y'inyandiko ishyigikiwe. Mu idirishya rito cyangwa inyandiko nini cyane, umurongo wo hasi uragaruka ubwawo; ongera ubugari bw'idirishya cyangwa uhindurire iramburiye kugira ngo wongere ukoreshe umurongo w'ibumoso. Abakoresha porogaramu zisoma ecran bashobora guhindura igikoresho Hindura ubugari bw'umurongo w'ibumoso kugira ngo bongere cyangwa bagabanye ubugari bwawo. Niba kujya ahandi bikibura, ongera utangize porogaramu hanyuma ukoreshe Uracyakeneye ubufasha? hepfo; ntushyire amakuru y'abantu n'andi makuru bwite muri raporo zishyirwa ahagaragara.
