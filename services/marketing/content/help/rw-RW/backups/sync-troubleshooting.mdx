---
id: syncTroubleshooting
title: "Ibikoresho byanjye ntibihuza. Nareba iki?"
---

Tangira ufungura **Igenamiterere → Guhuza**. Ibikoresho bihuza gusa n'ibikoresho byawe biri ku buryo bumwe (Cloud Sync, iCloud cyangwa Google Drive), bityo hitamo bumwe kuri buri kimwe. Hanyuma ukande **Huza ubu**.

## Reba ibi

1. Murandasi yawe, ku bikoresho byombi.
2. Buri gikoresho gifite WitnessWork nshya cyane. Verisiyo ishaje cyangwa dosiye idasomeka bigaragara mu buryo bihagaze.
3. Kuri iCloud ku iPhone cyangwa iPad: koresha Apple Account imwe, fungura iCloud Drive, kandi ufungure WitnessWork munsi ya iCloud kuri buri gikoresho.
4. Kuri Cloud Sync: reka porogaramu ifunguye ku bikoresho byombi.
5. Kuri Android, reba [Google Drive Sync ntikora](/help/backups/google-drive-sync-troubleshooting).

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ivuga ko Cloud Sync yahagaze:** nta kibazo kiri ku ruhande rwawe. Impinduka zawe zitegereza kuri iki gikoresho kandi zihuzwa isubiye.
- **Uko bihagaze bigumya kuri Uploading to iCloud:** bihinduka Apple imaze kwemeza ko yakiriye. **Last synced** ibara gusa ibyoherejwe byemejwe.
- **Ububiko bwawe bwa iCloud bwuzuye:** impinduka zawe zigumya kuri iki gikoresho. Kuraho ibintu cyangwa ugure umwanya muri **Igenamiterere rya iPhone → izina ryawe → iCloud**, maze koherezwa gukomeze.
- **Isaba kuba Umufasha:** vugurura WitnessWork. Guhuza ni ubuntu.
- **Ifoto irabura:** reba [Kuki amafoto y'abantu abura ku kindi gikoresho cyanjye?](/help/backups/sync-photos).
- **Ntibirakosoka:** bika dosiye ya backup n'igikoresho gifite inyandiko zawe nshya. **Ongera wubake amakuru ya iCloud** isimbuza amakuru ya iCloud n'inyandiko z'iki gikoresho. Ibindi bikoresho byawe bihindukira kuri zo ubutaha bihuza, bisimbuza impinduka byakorewemo mbere yaho. Banza uvugurure WitnessWork kuri buri gikoresho. Cyangwa ukoreshe uburyo bw'ubufasha bw'ibanga mu Kigo cy'Ubufasha, kandi ntukohereze amakuru y'abo usura mu bitekerezo by'abantu bose.
