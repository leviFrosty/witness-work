You can leave a shared car any time. You keep a copy of the car and its trips in your own cars, as of your last sync.

## Leave a car

1. Open **Mileage**, then **Mileage Settings** (the gear).
2. Tap the car.
3. Under **People**, tap **Leave Car…**.

Leaving needs an internet connection. If you're offline, try again when you're connected.

## Removed, or sharing stops

Only the owner, the person who shared the car, can use **Remove from Car…** or **Stop Sharing…**. In either case, and when you leave, you keep a copy of the car with the trips others logged. WitnessWork tells you, and you no longer see new changes on the shared car.

## Good to know

- When the owner leaves, the person who joined first becomes the owner.
- When everyone else leaves, the car becomes your own car again.
- **Delete All Mileage Data…** deletes your own cars, fuels, and trips, and leaves every shared car, keeping a copy of each. If it can't leave a car yet, for example while you're offline, the rest is deleted and it tells you. Try again when you're connected.
- **Delete My Buddies Data** in Buddies settings also leaves every shared car first, and won't finish while a car can't be left.
- Removing a buddy doesn't take them off a car, but it cancels your open invitations to them.
