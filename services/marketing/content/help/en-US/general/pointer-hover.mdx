Connect a trackpad or mouse to your iPad, or hold a supported Apple Pencil just above the screen. Buttons, rows, and calendar days highlight as the pointer moves over them.

## What you can do

- Rest the pointer on an icon-only button, such as a narrowed sidebar icon, the map controls, or the bell, to see its name.
- Hover over the activity grid, the Service Year pace chart, or a mileage bar to see its date and value.
- On the map, hover a pin to see the contact's name, or hover a contact in the side list to see its pin.
- Two-finger click to open the same menu as touching and holding.
- Use two-finger scrolling to move a contact photo while you crop it.

## Good to know

- Everything still works by touch.
- Hover changes nothing in your records.
- On Android, a mouse highlights rows and shows names and chart values, but map pins don't show names.
- iPhone has no hover.

## If something goes wrong

- **Nothing responds:** check that the trackpad, mouse, or Pencil is connected, then update and reopen the app.
- **Still stuck:** use the Help Center's private support link, and keep contact details out of public reports.
