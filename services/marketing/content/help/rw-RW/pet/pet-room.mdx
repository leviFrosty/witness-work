title: "Nshyira nte imitako mu cyumba cy'itungo ryanjye?"
---

Fungura amakuru y'itungo ryawe ukande ifoto y'ikintu ufite. Icyumba gihita gihinduka kandi kikabikwa.

## Shyira imitako mu cyumba

1. Kuri **Ahabanza**, kanda ikarita y'itungo ryawe, ariko si itungo ubwaryo. Gukanda itungo birihura.
2. Hitamo igice: **Ingofero**, **Urukuta**, **Hasi**, **Uburiri**, **Etajeri**, **Urumuri**, **Icyapa**, cyangwa **Idirishya**.
3. Kanda ikintu ufite.

Ushobora kandi gukanda ufate izina ry'ikarita ugahitamo **Ingofero n'icyumba…**. Umurongo uri hejuru y'amafoto uvuga icyo ukoresha, nka **Wearing: Cap**. Kuri iPad, kanda **Funga** kugira ngo uve aho.

## Shaka ibindi bintu

Ibindi bintu bifunguye uhereye ku itangiriro. Gura ibindi mu Iduka ukoresheje ibiceri.

1. Kanda **Iduka** iruhande rw'ibiceri byawe, cyangwa ikarita ya **Ibindi biri mu Iduka** ku iherezo ry'igice.
2. Kanda ikintu, hanyuma **Gura**.

Ikintu gishyirwa mu cyumba ako kanya kandi kigumya kuba icyawe iteka. Ikintu ufite utarahitamo kivuga **Gishya**. Ibindi bintu ni ibanga ntibigurishwa. Reba [Ingofero n'ibintu by'icyumba by'ibanga ni ibihe?](/help/pet/pet-secrets) na [Mbona nte ibiceri, kandi nagura iki mu Iduka?](/help/pet/pet-shop).

## Idirishya n'icyapa

- **Idirishya rikurikiza igihe** rikurikira isaha yawe n'igihe cy'umwaka aho uri. Rizimye kugira ngo wihitiremo igihe cy'umunsi n'igihe cy'umwaka.
- Icyapa gishobora kwerekana amasaha yawe y'uku kwezi, amasaha y'uyu mwaka w'umurimo, amezi wabwirije, gahunda yawe ikurikira, ibiganiro n'ugusubira gusura by'uku kwezi, cyangwa nta kintu.

## Ibintu bikora

Buri gice cyo mu Iduka (uretse **Ingofero**) gifite kandi ibintu bine bikora: bibiri bifite ikadiri y'icyatsi, na bibiri bidasanzwe bifite ikadiri y'ubururu. Biza nyuma y'ibisanzwe kandi bigura kuva ku biceri 620 kugeza 2,800.

- Ibyatsi bifite agace kamwe gakora, nka Hourglass, Clouds cyangwa Tall grass.
- Ibidasanzwe bihindura icyumba kibizengurutse kandi bisubiza iyo ukubise itungo ryawe cyangwa ubonye ibiceri. Ingero ni Gramophone, Volcano model, Koi pond, Northern lights na Campfire.
- Hitamo ikintu kidasanzwe mu Iduka urebe uko gisubiza. Utubuto twa **Pat** na **New coins** munsi y'ikarita yacyo dusubiramo igisubizo.
- Ishusho y'urukuta igumana ibara ry'urukuta wahisemo.

## Byiza kumenya

- Kugira ngo ubone ingaruka z'ijoro ku manywa, shyira idirishya kuri **Nijoro** mu makuru y'itungo ryawe.
- Amatara arazima iyo itungo ryawe ritagifite imitima. Ibindi bintu bikora ntibitwa n'imitima.
- Iyo Reduce Motion ifunguye, bigumya bihagaze kandi utubuto twa **Pat** na **New coins** ntitugaragara. Ntibinakora mu mafoto asangijwe cyangwa muri widget. Bagenzi babona itungo ryawe n'ingofero yaryo, si icyumba cyawe.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ikintu ntigikora:** reba ko Reduce Motion izimye mu igenamiterere ry'igikoresho cyawe.
- **Ubona ikintu gitandukanye n'icyo waguze:** vugurura WitnessWork kuri icyo gikoresho.
- **Biracyari bibi:** koresha **Ukeneye ubufasha bundi?** munsi y'iki gisubizo. Shyiramo verisiyo yawe ya WitnessWork, kandi ntushyiremo amazina n'inyandiko bwite.
