title: Bushe territory shandi shisync no kusungwa mu backup?
---

Ee. Territory shenu, ne misebo yashi, ifyo mwafikapo pa miseko, Tamutandalile, na territory shabwelelwa, shisync ku fibombelo fyenu fimbi elyo shaba mu backup yenu, nga fyo abantu benu ba kulanshanya.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Sync ibomfya inshila iyo foni yenu ibomfya: Cloud Sync, iCloud, nelyo Google Drive pa Android. Moneni **Ifyo Mwasala → Sync**.
- Ukucinja no kufumyapo nakonyo kusync. Territory mwafumyepo pa cibombelo cimo ifumapo pa fyonse.
- Icikope ca kadi cishala pa foni apo mwacilundeko. Cilundeni nakabili pa cibombelo cimbi nga mulefwaya ukuti cibeko.
- Territory mwasabankanya shishala shasabankanishiwa pa mafoni yenu yonse ayasync na iyi, ne fyo bambi balemba. Territory shasabankanishiwa nelyo shapelwa kuli imwe shimoneka pa fibombelo fyenu fyonse ifikwata Buddies.
- Ilyo territory privacy mode yaishiba, takuli ifyalembwa fya ng'anda ing'anda ifyasungwa, kanshi takuli ifisync nelyo ukusungwa mu backup. Ukuiwasha kufumyapo na pa fibombelo fyenu fimbi.
- Nga app ya kale tailamona Territories, kushani WitnessWork. Territory shenu shishala bwino pa nshita iyo.
