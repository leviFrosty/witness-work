title: "Nakora konti ya WitnessWork kuri murandasi nta porogaramu ya telefoni?"
---

Yego. Ukeneye mushakisha ibasha kubika passkey. Nta aderesi ya imeri, nimero ya telefoni cyangwa ijambobanga ukeneye.

## Kora konti

1. Fungura app.witnesswork.app uhitemo **Create an Account**.
2. Tegereza igenzura ry'umutekano rirangire, hanyuma uhitemo **Create Account**.
3. Bika passkey mushakisha yawe itanga, yitwa "WitnessWork" ikurikiwe na kode y'inyuguti 4. Emeza ukoresheje Face ID, igikumwe, cyangwa gufunga ecran. Ishobora kubisaba kabiri.
4. Bika urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura ukoresheje **Download**, **Print** cyangwa **Kopi**. Rubike ahantu h'ibanga.
5. Hitamo **I've Saved My Recovery Key**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Bika urufunguzo rwo kugarura nonaha. Murandasi ntiyongera kurwerekana nyuma. Niba utakaje passkey yawe, ni rwo nzira yonyine yo kwinjira, kandi nta muntu ukorera WitnessWork ushobora kurusubiza. Reba [Urufunguzo rwanjye rwo kugarura ni iki, kandi nza kurubika nte?](/help/account/account-recovery-key).
- Bitewe na mushakisha yawe n'ububiko bw'amajambobanga, passkey yawe ishobora kuba iya kwinjira gusa. Noneho kuri indi mudasobwa uzakenera kandi urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura cyangwa telefoni yinjiye. Reba [Passkey ni iki?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
- Igenzura ry'umutekano rikorwa na Cloudflare, ibona aderesi yawe ya murandasi n'amakuru ya mushakisha yawe.
- Amakuru yawe aguma muri iyi mushakisha kandi ahuzwa binyuze kuri Cloud Sync.
- Kugira ngo ukoreshe konti kuri telefoni, andika urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura aho. Reba [Nakoresha nte urufunguzo rwanjye rwo kugarura kuri telefoni nshya?](/help/account/account-recovery-key-use).
- Ntushobora gukora konti muri mushakisha ikibitse amakuru y'indi konti. Ahubwo injira muri iyo konti.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Wafunze icyifuzo cya passkey:** nta konti yakozwe. Hitamo **Create Account** nanone.
- **Igenzura ry'umutekano ntiryarangiye:** hitamo **Ongera ugerageze**. Niba rikomeza kunanirwa, zimya ibyongerwaho bibuza ibirimo, cyangwa ugerageze indi mushakisha.
- **Ntiyashoboye kugera kuri WitnessWork, cyangwa nta murandasi ufite:** reba murandasi yawe.
- **Konti irimo gukorerwa kuri indi tab:** rangirizeyo, cyangwa uyifunge wongere ugerageze.
- **Sign-Up Is Paused:** ongera ugerageze nyuma, cyangwa ukore konti yawe muri porogaramu ya telefoni wunjire kuri murandasi.
- **Konti nyinshi nshya zivuye kuri iyi murandasi:** tegereza igihe cyerekanwe, hanyuma ugerageze nanone.
- **Finish Creating Your Account:** hitamo **Finish with Passkey** uhitemo passkey itangwa. Konti yawe ishobora kuba isanzwe ihari, bityo iyi ni yo nzira yonyine yo gukomeza muri iyi mushakisha.
- **Wasibye passkey utararangiza:** hitamo **I Deleted That Passkey**. Bika urufunguzo rwo kugarura rwerekanwa, hanyuma wunjire ukoresheje rwo.
- **This Browser Holds an Account Key:** bika urufunguzo rwerekanwa, hanyuma wunjire ukoresheje rwo cyangwa passkey y'iyo konti.
- **Mushakisha yawe ntiyashoboye kubika konti yawe:** va mu buryo bw'ibanga bwo gushakisha, cyangwa wemerere uru rubuga kubika amakuru, hanyuma ugerageze nanone.

Ku kindi kintu cyose, koresha ihuza ry'ubufasha riri mu Kigo cy'ubufasha.
