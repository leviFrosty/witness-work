The **Daily Pace** card on the Progress tab shows how much time you need each day to reach your monthly goal.

## Add the card

1. Open the **Progress** tab and go to the **Month** view.
2. Tap **Edit Cards**, then **Add Cards**.
3. Choose **Daily Pace**.

It's already there if your status has a monthly goal.

## Good to know

- It divides what's left of your goal by the days left in the month after today. With 25 of 50 hours done and 20 days left, it shows 1.3 hours a day. The figure rounds up a little, so keeping to it is always enough.
- On the last day of the month, it shows everything that's left as today's amount.
- Make the card wide to also see about how much that is a week.
- Credit counts up to your monthly limit, as on your report. Your plans aren't counted. The **Projected Month** card shows where your plans put you.
- Once you reach your goal, the card says so.
- For a past month it shows what you averaged a day. For a future month it shows what each day would need.
- It doesn't appear when the month has no goal.
- For the whole service year, the **Year Pace** card does the same with your annual goal.
