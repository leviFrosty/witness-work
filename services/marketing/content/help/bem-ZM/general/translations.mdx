WitnessWork iba mu ndimi 19. Ikonka ululimi lwa foni yenu nga mutasala ulumbi.

## Ndimi

Ci-Arabu, Ci-Bemba, Ci-Chinese (Simplified na Traditional), Ci-Dutch, Ci-English, Ci-French, Ci-German, Ci-Italian, Ci-Japanese, Ci-Kinyarwanda, Ci-Korean, Ci-Portuguese (Brazil na Portugal), Ci-Russian, Ci-Spanish, Ci-Swahili, Ci-Ukrainian, na Ci-Vietnamese. Nga ululimi lwenu tulwaba pa lyashi, WitnessWork ilanga Ci-English.

## Cinjeni ululimi

1. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala**.
2. Tinikeni **Ululimi** pa pamulu elyo musale ulumo.

WitnessWork itendeka nakabili mu ululimi ulo. **Icibombelo icabako** ikonka foni yenu. Ululimi mwasala mu WitnessWork lukucila ifyo foni yenu yasala.

Kuti mwabika na ululimi lwa WitnessWork fye mu ifyo mwasala fya foni yenu. Pa iPhone na iPad, isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Apps → WitnessWork → Ululimi**. Ilaboneka ilyo foni yenu yakwata indimi shibili nelyo ukucila. Pa Android 13 nelyo ipya, isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Apps → WitnessWork → Ululimi**.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Amashiwi yamo yafuma ku foni yenu, kanshi ikonka ululimi lwa foni yenu: utwatini twa ifyebo, date pickers, share sheets, ne widget gallery.
- Pa iPhone na iPad, ukulondolola kwa mepusho ya ifyo mwalesha, nga location nelyo camera, kuli lyonse mu Ci-English.
- Ukutalusha kupangwa na AI, kanshi ishiwi nelyo amashiwi kuti yabipa, nga amashiwi yabomfiwa ukwa kwa Nte sha kwa Yehova mu nshila imo.

## Tumeni ukutalusha

Bomfyeni link ya email iya munshi ya **nomba line kabila afwilisha?** elyo mulande ululimi lwenu, icikope, amashiwi mwamona, ne ico cifwile ukulanda. Icikope cilafwa. Fiseni amashina, amaadiresi, ne malembo pa ntanshi. Ifyo mwalungika fyaisa mu cinjeni cimbi.
