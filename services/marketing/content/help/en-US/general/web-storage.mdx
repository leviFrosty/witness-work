---
id: webStorage
title: What does WitnessWork keep in my browser?
category: general
platforms: [web]
---

WitnessWork on the web keeps your records in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) so it opens straight to your data and works offline. It also keeps your account key and your sign-in session there until you sign out. Your account key and session are sealed with a browser key that can't be exported. Signing out, or WitnessWork's server ending your session for security reasons, erases the account key, the session, and all local data from that browser.

There's no "Is this your computer?" question and no automatic lock. Anyone who can use this browser profile can open WitnessWork in it. On a shared computer, sign out when you're done ([Signing out on the web](/help/account/web-sign-out)), or turn on App Lock in Settings → Security once your account has a passkey ([App Lock](/help/general/app-lock)).

**If your browser can't save:** WitnessWork keeps working and tells you once. When the browser is out of space, free up space on your device and keep the tab open: WitnessWork saves your changes as soon as it can. Some private windows don't let sites save at all. Changes you make there are lost when you close the tab, so use a regular window.

WitnessWork works in one tab at a time ([Why does WitnessWork say it's open in another tab?](/help/general/web-tabs)).

**Safari:** Safari erases a site's storage after 7 days without a visit. A web app you added to the Home Screen on an iPhone is exempt. If it happens, sign in again, for example with your passkey, and your synced records come back through Cloud Sync. Changes you made in that browser that hadn't synced are lost, so keep Cloud Sync on and let it finish.

Clearing site data in your browser's settings erases the same things. If you ever lose the browser's copy, your account and your synced records are not lost, as long as you have a way to sign in again ([What if I lose access?](/help/account/lost-access)).
