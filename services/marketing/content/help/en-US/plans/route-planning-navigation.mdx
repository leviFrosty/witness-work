title: How does my route open in Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze?
category: plans
---

Tap Start Route. Your route opens in the Default Navigation App chosen in Settings → Preferences → Navigation, in the order shown, starting with the place you chose as the start when it isn't your current location, and ending where you chose to finish when that isn't the last stop. Google Maps opens the whole route as one trip with every stop in order. Apple Maps does the same on iOS 18.4 or later; on earlier versions it opens one stop at a time. Waze always opens one stop at a time because its links accept only a single destination. When stops open one at a time, come back to WitnessWork and tap Navigate to Stop 2 (and so on) for the next stop. To go to any one stop, open its menu and tap Navigate Here. If Google Maps isn't installed, the route opens in a web browser, which may show fewer stops; install the app or choose another navigation app. On Android, choose Google Maps or Waze. If a map app doesn't open, check that it's installed and try again.
