title: "Mushakisha imara igihe kingana iki yinjiye, kandi nakoresha zingahe?"
---

Mushakisha iguma yinjiye igihe uyikoresha, bityo WitnessWork kuri murandasi ifungukira ku makuru yawe. Kwinjira birangira nyuma y'iminsi 180 nta gukoresha, cyangwa iyo usohotse.

Ushobora kugira mushakisha zigeze kuri 3 zinjiye icyarimwe, zitari zo telefoni zawe. Niba winjije iya kane, iyo wakoresheje kera cyane irasohorwa.

## Reba mushakisha zawe uzisohore

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Ibikoresho byinjiye**, kuri telefoni cyangwa kuri murandasi.
2. Kanda mushakisha, nka "Chrome on macOS".
3. Hitamo **Sohora**.

Mushakisha wasohoye uri ku kindi gikoresho isibwa ubutaha ihuye na murandasi. Ibyo yari itarahuza birabura. Kugira ngo usohore mushakisha urimo gukoresha, reba [Nasohoka nte muri WitnessWork kuri murandasi?](/help/account/web-sign-out).

## Byiza kumenya

- Kwinjira gushya kugaragara mu nzogera y'Ahabanza ku bindi bikoresho byawe, no muri **Igenamiterere → Konti → Ibikorwa kuri konti**. Niba atari wowe, kanda, sohora icyo gikoresho, uhindure urufunguzo rwa konti yawe. Reba [Nakora iki niba telefoni cyangwa mudasobwa yanjye yatakaye cyangwa yibwe?](/help/account/lost-device).
- Mu minota mike nyuma yo kwinjira, ushobora kongeraho cyangwa gukuraho passkey cyangwa kureba urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura utemeje. Nyuma yaho, WitnessWork igusaba gukoresha passkey.
- Nta passkey kuri iyi mudasobwa? Hitamo **Sign In Again** wunjire ukoresheje urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura cyangwa telefoni yawe. Koresha konti imwe. Amakuru yawe aguma muri mushakisha.
- Kugira ngo uhishe WitnessWork iyo uvuye aho, fungura gufunga porogaramu. Reba [Nafunga nte WitnessWork?](/help/general/app-lock).
