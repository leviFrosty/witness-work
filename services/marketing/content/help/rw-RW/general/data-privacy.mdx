title: "Amakuru yanjye ni ay'ibanga kandi ararinzwe?"
---

Yego. WitnessWork nta matangazo ifite, kandi amakuru yawe abitswe kuri telefoni yawe. Nta kintu usangiza WitnessWork gihuzwa n'izina ryawe.

## Ibyo WitnessWork izi kuri wowe

- Porogaramu ya telefoni nta kwiyandikisha ikeneye. Konti yawe nta imeri, nimero ya telefoni cyangwa ijambobanga igira. Ushobora kubika passkey, ni uburyo bwo kwinjira.
- Kuri murandasi, wunjira kuri konti imwe, cyangwa ugakora indi ukoresheje passkey. Igenzura ry'umutekano kuri iyo paji rireba aderesi yawe ya murandasi n'amakuru ya mushakisha yawe.
- Imibare y'ikoreshwa ni iy'ibarura ritazwi nyirabyo. Ntirigera ririmo amazina, Aderesi, raporo cyangwa inyandiko. Ushobora kuyihagarika muri **Igenamiterere → Ibanga**.
- Ibyo waguze muri porogaramu za telefoni bikoresha nimero y'impanuka, si izina ryawe cyangwa imeri.

## Ibiva kuri telefoni yawe n'ababasha kubisoma

- **Backup** na **Cloud Sync** (byombi bimara bihagaritswe kugeza ubifunguye): hakorwa kopi ishobora gusomwa n'ibikoresho byawe bwite gusa. Ndetse WitnessWork ntiyayisoma. Cloud Sync ni na yo ituma porogaramu ya murandasi ibona amakuru yawe.
- **iCloud Sync** na **Google Drive Sync** bijya mu konti yawe bwite ya Apple cyangwa Google, ntibinyura kuri WitnessWork.
- **Buddies** n'**imodoka zisangiwe** muri Ibirometero: ababisoma ni wowe n'abo musangiye gusa.
- **Aderesi zisangiwe** ziba mu ihuza uyoherereje. WitnessWork ntibika kopi.
- **Scribe AI** yohereza ibyo usabye ku batanga serivisi batabibika. Gutegura inzira byohereza aho biri ku ikarita gusa.

## Kuri murandasi

- Amakuru yawe n'uko winjiye bigumana muri mushakisha yawe kugeza usohotse.
- Hafi rimwe muri buri nshuro 10 ukoresha, haba amashusho ya ecran yandikwa, aho inyandiko zose, ibyo wanditse, amafoto n'ikarita bihishwa mbere y'uko hagira ikintu cyoherezwa. Abikwa iminsi 30.
- Aya mashusho ntiyandikwa na rimwe iyo uburyo bwo kurinda amakuru bufunguye. Mu Burayi bw'Ubumwe, mu Bwongereza no mu Busuwisi, yandikwa gusa niba ufunguye **Share Masked Screen Recordings** muri **Igenamiterere → Ibanga**.
- Zimya imibare y'ikoreshwa muri **Igenamiterere → Ibanga** kugira ngo n'aya mashusho ahagarare.

## Byiza kumenya

- Dosiye za backup usohora ni izawe. Zibike mu ibanga, kuko zishobora kuba zirimo amakuru y'abantu usura.
- WitnessWork isiba kopi zayo z'agateganyo za backup zasohowe, dosiye z'aderesi zasangijwe n'amafoto wahisemo, ubutaha itangiye no igihe usibye burundu amakuru y'abantu usura.
- Ngo umenye birenzeho, soma Politiki y'ibanga muri **Igenamiterere → Ibyerekeye**. Ku bibazo by'ibanga, koresha ihuza ry'ubufasha ry'ibanga ryo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha.
