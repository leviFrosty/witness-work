title: "Kuki mugenzi wanjye atabona agace nasangije, cyangwa kuki ntagasangiza?"
---

Mugenzi wawe akeneye Uduce muri porogaramu ye kandi agomba kwemera ubutumire. Fungura agace. Munsi ya **Basangijwe**, "Yatumiwe" iruhande rw'izina rye bivuze ko ataremera.

## Niba mugenzi wawe atabibona

- **Porogaramu ye nta Uduce ifite.** Agomba kuvugurura WitnessWork muri App Store cyangwa Google Play. Uduce turi kugezwa ku bantu buhoro buhoro. Ubutumire bwawe butegereza kugeza ku minsi 7.
- **Ntabwo yemeye.** Ubutumire buri mu nzogera iri kuri Ahabanza, hamwe na **Injira** na **Anga** (**Emera** ku muhanda washyikirijwe). Iyo yinjiye, biba mu gice cye cya Uduce munsi ya **Nasangijwe** (cyangwa **Nashyikirijwe**).
- **Ubutumire bwararangiye cyangwa yarabwanze.** Kanda **Hagarika** iruhande rw'izina rye, hanyuma wongere usangize agace.
- **Ari kureba ku kindi gikoresho.** Uduce dusangijwe tugaragara ku bikoresho bye byose bifite Buddies. Mubwire afungure WitnessWork aho, ategereze akanya.

## Niba gusangiza bitagukorera

Ubutumwa buvuga impamvu:

- **"Shyiraho Buddies":** gusangiza na bagenzi no gushyikiriza bikeneye Buddies kuri iyi telefoni. Kiyikande kugira ngo uyishyireho. Reba [WitnessWork Buddies ni iki?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview) Cyangwa ohereza umurongo w'umushyitsi, utagusaba Buddies. Gusangiza ntibiri kuri murandasi.
- **"Banza wongereho mugenzi":** ushobora gusangiza gusa n'abantu muhujwe muri Buddies. Banza ubatumire, cyangwa wohereze umurongo w'umushyitsi.
- **"Ntibyashobotse kugera kuri WitnessWork"** cyangwa **"Ntibyashobotse gutangira gusangiza. Reba murandasi yawe":** huza murandasi wongere ugerageze.
- **"Ntibyashobotse gutangira gusangiza. Vugurura WitnessWork, hanyuma wongere ugerageze.":** shyiraho ivugurura rivuye muri App Store cyangwa Google Play.
- **"Gusangiza uduce byahagaritswe ubu.":** ongera ugerageze nyuma. Agace kawe kagumya kuri telefoni yawe.
- **"Nyiri agace ni we wenyine ubishobora.":** reba [Kuki ntashobora guhindura cyangwa kugarura agace wansangije?](/help/territories/territories-shared-with-me)
- **"Aka gace ni kanini cyane ku gusangizwa.":** reba [Ni ibihe bipimo byo gusangiza agace?](/help/territories/territories-limits)
- **"Hitamo umuhanda utashyikirijwe.":** umuhanda washyikirijwe ntushobora gusangizwa kugeza usubijwe kuri wowe.
- **"Zimwe mu mpinduka kuri (agace) ntizasangijwe. Uwo muhanda wahawe undi muri icyo gihe.":** umuhanda washyikirijwe cyangwa wisubijwe utari kuri murandasi, bityo impinduka zawe ntizashoboye kubikwa.

Niba ikibazo gikomeje, koresha buto y'ubufasha iri hejuru y'iri kera, urebe munsi ya **Ukeneye ubufasha bundi?**, kandi ushyiremo verisiyo ya porogaramu yawe. Ntukandike nomero z'uduce, aderesi, n'amazina mu bitekerezo by'abantu bose.
