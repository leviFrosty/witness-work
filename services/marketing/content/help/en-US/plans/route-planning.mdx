Route planning puts your day's stops on a map in a good driving order. It's a Supporter feature on iPhone, iPad, and Android. Anyone can open it to see the day on the map, but **Shortest Order** and **Start Route** need Supporter.

## Open the route for a day

1. Tap a day from today on, on Home or in **Schedule**.
2. Tap **Plan Route**.

You can also touch and hold a day and choose **Plan Route**, or choose **Plan Route** in a plan's menu. A day with no follow-ups or plans can still be planned from scratch.

The map shows a card at each stop with when you'd arrive. The box at the top shows when the day starts, when it finishes, and how long that is. Use the arrows beside the date to plan another day.

## Add, move, and remove stops

- Stops start as the day's open follow-ups and plans that have an address or pin.
- Tap **Add Stop** to add Home, your Kingdom Hall, a place from search, one of your contacts, or a pin you dropped on the **Map** tab.
- To change the order, drag a stop by its grip, or use **Move Up** and **Move Down** in its menu.
- Tap **Shortest Order** to put the stops in the fastest driving order. It needs internet.
- Remove a stop from its menu. Removed stops are listed below so you can add them back.
- Set how long you'll spend at a stop with its time chip.

A route can have up to 10 stops, and you can use **Shortest Order** 10 times in any 24 hours. To choose where you start and finish, see [How do I choose where my route starts and finishes?](/help/plans/route-planning-places)

## Start your route

1. Tap **Start Route**.
2. Your route opens in the app chosen under **Settings → Features → Navigation**, in the order shown.

Google Maps opens the whole route as one trip. Apple Maps does too on iOS 18.4 or later. Waze opens one stop at a time. When stops open one at a time, come back to WitnessWork and tap **Navigate to Stop 2** (and so on) for the next one. To go to one stop, open its menu and tap **Navigate Here**.

## Good to know

- Drive times follow the roads and include traffic when you plan. Without internet they are estimates. Check them against fixed appointments.
- A stop shows when you'd be late for its set time. With **Start Visits at Set Times** on, arriving early waits until the set time.
- In **Details**, link the route to one of the day's plans, invite buddies, and add notes. See [How do I plan a route with buddies?](/help/plans/route-planning-buddies)
- Your route is saved on this phone for that day. It isn't synced.
- Tap **Start Over** to rebuild it from the day's follow-ups and plans. Planning a route never changes your contacts, follow-ups, or plans.
- Privacy: see [What's shared when I plan a route?](/help/plans/route-planning-privacy)

## If something goes wrong

- **A map app doesn't open:** make sure it's installed and try again. If Google Maps isn't installed, the route opens in a web browser and may show fewer stops. On Android, choose Google Maps or Waze.
