title: Is my data private and secure?
category: general
---

Yes. WitnessWork has no ads, and your records live on your phone. Nothing you share with WitnessWork is tied to your name.

## What WitnessWork knows about you

- The phone app needs no sign-up. Your account has no email, phone number, or password. You can save a passkey, which is a way to sign in.
- On the web, you sign in to the same account, or create one with a passkey. A security check on that page sees your internet address and browser details.
- Usage statistics are anonymous counts. They never include names, contacts, reports, or notes. Turn them off in **Settings → Privacy**.
- Purchases in the phone apps use a random number, not your name or email.

## What leaves your phone, and who can read it

- **Backup** and **Cloud Sync** (both off until you turn them on): a copy is saved that only your own devices can read. Not even WitnessWork can read it. Cloud Sync is also how the web app gets your records.
- **iCloud Sync** and **Google Drive Sync** go straight to your own Apple or Google account, not through WitnessWork.
- **Buddies** and **shared cars** in Mileage: only you and the people you share with can read them.
- **Shared contacts** travel inside the link you send. WitnessWork doesn't keep a copy.
- **Scribe AI** sends what you ask to providers that don't store it. Route planning sends only map locations.

## On the web

- Your records and sign-in stay in your browser until you sign out.
- About 1 in 10 sessions are recorded as screen replays with all your text, inputs, photos, and the map hidden before anything is sent. They are kept for 30 days.
- Replays are never recorded when data protection mode is on. In the EU, the UK, and Switzerland, they happen only if you turn on **Share Masked Screen Recordings** in **Settings → Privacy**.
- Turn usage statistics off in **Settings → Privacy** to stop replays too.

## Good to know

- Backup files you export belong to you. Keep them somewhere private, because they can include householder details.
- WitnessWork deletes its own temporary copies of exported backups, shared contact files, and picked photos the next time it starts and when you permanently delete householder data.
- For details, read the Privacy Policy under **Settings → About**. For privacy questions, use the private support link in the Help Center.
