title: Why do I see only some of my photos?
category: general

When your iPhone or iPad asked about photos, you chose **Select Photos**, so WitnessWork can see only the photos you shared with it.

## Share more photos

1. In WitnessWork, open the photo picker, for example from a contact picture or a note.
2. Tap **Manage** above the photos.
3. Select the photos you want, or deselect any you'd rather keep private.
4. Confirm. They appear right away.

## Let WitnessWork see all your photos

1. Open your iPhone's **Settings** app.
2. Go to **Apps → WitnessWork → Photos**.
3. Choose **Full Access**.

## Good to know

- WitnessWork copies only the photos you pick. It reads nothing else from your library.

## If something goes wrong

- **A photo won't load:** it may still be downloading from iCloud. Check your connection and try again.
