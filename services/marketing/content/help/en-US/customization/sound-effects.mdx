---
id: soundEffects
title: How do I turn off celebration and reminder sounds?
category: customization
related:
  - 365
  - 486
---

Open Settings → Preferences → Audio & Haptics and turn off Play sounds. This controls celebration chimes and reminders while WitnessWork is open; reminders still appear, just without sound (on Android, as a short message in the app). Reminders received while the app is closed follow your device's notification settings; adjust those separately if needed. From version 1.46.0, it also quiets the sounds your pet's rewards make; to keep other sounds and change or turn off only those, use Settings → Preferences → Rewards (see the question about coins and the Shop).
