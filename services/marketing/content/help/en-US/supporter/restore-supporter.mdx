---
id: restoreSupporter
title: I already paid, but Supporter features or prices are missing. What should I do?
category: supporter
---

Check your connection and that your device's store uses the account you purchased with. If you opened WitnessWork without a connection, prices and your Supporter status load by themselves once you're back online. Open Settings → Become a supporter and use Restore Purchases when it is shown. A one-time Tip does not activate Supporter features. If no purchases are found, prices do not load, or your subscription still is not recognized, email support privately with your app version and what happened. If support needs your account ID, use the support button in this screen's header to open Still need help?, then tap Copy Account ID. Share that ID privately. Restoring purchases restores access, not your service records. From version 1.47.0, syncing your devices doesn't depend on Supporter status.
