title: How do I log time or plan a specific day?
category: time
---

Tap the day on the Schedule tab, in This Week on Home, or under All Days on the Progress tab. The day opens in the bottom half of the screen, so the calendar or list you tapped stays in view above it, and you can tap another day there to switch to it. If the day has no time logged yet, tap Add Time under Time Reports to unfold the time form right there: tap Time, choose how long, add a note or Type if you like, and tap Submit. If the day has no plans yet, tap Plan Day under Plans the same way: add a title if you like, choose One Time or Recurring and how long, and tap the Add button. Tap Add Time or Plan Day again to fold the form away. To add more time or another plan, tap + beside Time Reports or Plans. Tap an entry to change or delete it, or tap a plan to open it. To log a plan you kept, touch and hold it and choose Log as Time; its length and Type are filled in. These open the form in the same place, and its back arrow returns to the day without saving. A note opens in the note editor over the whole screen; tap Done and the day comes back with the form as you left it. To see more of the day or more of the screen above it, drag the bar at the top of the day up or down; WitnessWork remembers the size on this device. To close the day, tap the X or drag it all the way down; on Android, the back button works too. Logging time appears only if your status logs hours; Kingdom Publishers turn on Log my hours in Settings → Profile first.
