title: "Ninjira nte kuri mudasobwa nkoresheje telefoni yanjye?"
---

Erekana QR code muri mushakisha uyemeze kuri telefoni yawe. Ukeneye WitnessWork kuri iPhone cyangwa telefoni ya Android yinjiye muri konti yawe, na murandasi ku bikoresho byombi.

## Injira kuri mudasobwa

1. Kuri mudasobwa, fungura app.witnesswork.app uhitemo **Use my phone**. QR code irigaragaza.
2. Kuri telefoni yawe, fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti → Sign In on a Computer**.
3. Sikana kode muri WitnessWork. Porogaramu ya kamera ntiyayifungura.
4. Reba ko kode y'imibare itandatu iri kuri telefoni yawe ihura n'iri muri mushakisha.
5. Kanda **Emerera**, hanyuma wemeze ukoresheje Face ID, igikumwe cyangwa kode ya telefoni.

Telefoni yawe yerekana **Approved**. Mushakisha iranjira kandi igutanga kongeraho passkey kugira ngo ubutaha ushobore kureka telefoni.

## Byiza kumenya

- Emera gusa mudasobwa iri imbere yawe. Niba telefoni iburira ko mushakisha isa n'iri kure, komeza gusa niba ari mudasobwa yawe.
- Niba utari wowe watangije ibi, kanda **Ntiwemere**.
- Telefoni gusa ni yo ishobora kwemera mushakisha. Nta telefoni, koresha passkey cyangwa urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura. Reba [Nakora iki niba ntagishoboye kwinjira muri konti yanjye ya WitnessWork?](/help/account/lost-access).
- Mushakisha yerekana ko yemewe na telefoni itari iyawe: hitamo **That Wasn't Me**. Reba [Nakora iki niba telefoni cyangwa mudasobwa yanjye yatakaye cyangwa yibwe?](/help/account/lost-device).

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Telefoni ntishobora gusikana:** mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe, emerera WitnessWork gukoresha kamera.
- **Kode si iya WitnessWork, cyangwa ni iy'indi telefoni:** kuri mudasobwa, fungura app.witnesswork.app uhitemo **Use my phone** nanone.
- **Kode yarangiye:** kode zihinduka hafi buri munota kandi zikora rimwe. Hitamo **Show a New Code** muri mushakisha.
- **Telefoni ivuga ko idashobora kwemeza iki gikoresho:** ntishobora kwemera mudasobwa. Koresha passkey cyangwa urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura.
