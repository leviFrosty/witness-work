---
id: updateRequired
title: Why does WitnessWork say Update Required?
category: general
---

Update Required means your version of WitnessWork is too old to work with WitnessWork's servers. You need to update before you can keep using it.

## Update the app

1. Tap **Update WitnessWork**. This opens the App Store, or the Google Play Store app on Android.
2. Install the update.
3. Open WitnessWork again.

If App Lock is on, unlock WitnessWork first. Update Required appears right after.

## Good to know

- Your records, contacts, and notes stay on your phone and are all there after the update.
- Until you update, Cloud Sync, Backup, Buddies, and Scribe AI wait. They catch up a few moments after you open the updated app.
- On the web, reload the page to get the newest version.

## If something goes wrong

- **The store doesn't open:** open it yourself and search for WitnessWork.
- **The store shows no update:** close the store app, open it again, and check for updates there.
