id: streaks
title: How do streaks work?
category: streaks
---

Your streak counts how consistently you keep the plans you make. For pioneers and other roles that log hours, each planned day you log any time on adds one, even if you log less than you planned. Days without a plan don't add to it or end it, so plan the days you mean to go out. For publishers, each month you share in the ministry adds one, whether you mark the month on Home or log time. Your streak shows on Home and your Profile from its first day (before version 1.45.1, once it reached 3). From version 1.46.0, it sits at the end of Home's greeting rather than in Home's header, and on a wide tablet layout it also shows beside your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Before version 1.46.0, your pet's details showed it too, even at 0, and it lit your pet's lamp and opened its hats and room items. From 1.46.0, your pet's lamp follows its hearts and you buy hats and room items in the Shop, so your streak no longer lights the lamp or opens anything for your pet. Instead, while Show streak is on, each milestone your streak reaches since you adopted your pet earns it 10 coins to spend in the Shop (see the question about coins and the Shop). When logging a day takes it to a milestone, like 3, 5, or 10, a short celebration plays; other days, and growth that arrives another way, such as time logged on your watch or another device, make the flame on Home flare instead. From version 1.46.0, tapping it on Home shows when it started, your best streak, and the streaks your buddies share with you, and the ? there opens these answers. When it's about to end, tap it to log the day that's due. To hide your streak, turn off Show streak in Settings → Preferences → Streaks & Badges.
