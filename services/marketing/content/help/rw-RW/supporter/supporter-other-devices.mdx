title: "Kuki ndi Umufasha ku gikoresho kimwe ariko ntari ku kindi?"
---

Uko uri Umufasha kwanditse kuri konti yawe ya WitnessWork, bityo buri gikoresho cyinjiye muri konti imwe kirabisangira, nubwo cyaba cyaraguriweho n'ikindi. Harimo WitnessWork kuri murandasi. Akenshi kigaragara mu munota umwe nyuma yo guhuza.

## Niba igikoresho kimwe kitakwerekana nk'Umufasha

1. Fungura WitnessWork kuri cyo gifite murandasi.
2. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti** urebe ko cyinjiye muri konti imwe.
3. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Sync** urebe ko guhuza bifunguye.

Niba bikibura nyuma y'umunsi, kanda **Garura ibyigeze kugurwa** kuri telefoni cyangwa tablet wagurishijeho (murandasi ntibifite), cyangwa uvugane n'ubufasha.

## Byiza kumenya

- Igikoresho cyinjiye muri indi konti ya WitnessWork cyerekana uko iyo konti ihagaze. Gusubira muri konti yawe bigarura iyawe.
