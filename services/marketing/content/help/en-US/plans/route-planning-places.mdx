title: How do I choose where my route starts and finishes, or save Home and my Kingdom Hall?
category: plans
---

At the top of the route, tap the place under Start to choose your current location, the first stop, Home, your Kingdom Hall, one of the day's plans that has a place, or any place you search for, and tap the time to change when you leave. If a plan with a place starts before your first follow-up, such as a meeting for field service, the route starts there at that time. At the bottom, tap the place under Finish to end at the last stop (the default), go back to where you started, or finish at Home, your Kingdom Hall, or any place you search for. The finish isn't a stop, so there's no time spent there; the drive to it is added, and the finish time is when you'd get there. Start Route takes your navigation app on to the finish. Shortest Order puts the stops in order from the start and keeps the finish last. To save Home or your Kingdom Hall, tap Set Home or Set Kingdom Hall in the Start or Finish menu, or in Add Stop, then search for the address or drop a pin on the Map tab. After that, they're one tap away in those menus and in Add Stop. To change or remove one, touch and hold it in Add Stop. Saved places stay on this device and aren't synced.
