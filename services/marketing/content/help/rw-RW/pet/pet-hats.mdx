title: "Nabona nte ingofero z'itungo ryanjye?"
---

Ugura ingofero mu Iduka ukoresheje ibiceri, ubibona iyo usohotse mu murimo wo kubwiriza. Itungo ryawe rishobora kwambara ingofero imwe cyangwa nta n'imwe.

## Gura ingofero

1. Kuri **Ahabanza**, kanda ikarita y'itungo ryawe, ariko si itungo ubwaryo.
2. Kanda **Iduka**.
3. Hitamo icyiciro cya **Ingofero**.
4. Kanda ingofero urebe uko igukwiriye, hanyuma ukande **Gura**.

Itungo ryawe ririyambara ako kanya. Ingofero zigura kuva ku biceri 100 kuri Bow kugeza kuri 3,200 kuri Little fig tree. Soma [Mbona nte ibiceri, kandi nagura iki mu Iduka?](/help/pet/pet-shop) kugira ngo umenye uko ubibona.

## Hindura ingofero

1. Kanda ikarita y'itungo ryawe.
2. Hitamo igice cya **Ingofero**.
3. Kanda ingofero ufite, cyangwa uhitemo nta n'imwe.

Ingofero ufite igumya kuba iyawe, nubwo wakoresha **Hindura itungo**. Ivuga **Gishya** kugeza uyihisemo. Itungo ryawe ririyambara kuri Ahabanza, mu makuru yaryo, mu mafoto asangijwe, muri widget ya Home Screen (iPhone na iPad), no mu gihe risura bagenzi bawe. Rirayiyambura igihe ryigaragura cyangwa riryamye.

## Ingofero zidasanzwe

Ingofero eshanu zihenze cyane zifite ikadiri y'ubururu. Zirakora, zihindura icyumba kizengurutse itungo ryawe, kandi zisubiza iyo ukubise itungo ryawe cyangwa ubonye ibiceri.

- Fishbowl (ibiceri 1,200): amafi aranyura, rimwe rikavamo igihe ubonye ibiceri.
- Lighthouse (1,600): inyoni z'inyanja zizenguruka ku manywa, kandi urumuri rwerekeza icyumba nyuma y'izuba rirenze.
- Model railway (2,000): gari ya moshi ntoya izenguruka umusozi uri ku mutwe w'itungo ryawe.
- Planetarium (2,600): inyenyeri zuzuza inkuta nyuma y'izuba rirenze, rimwe na rimwe inyenyeri irarasa.
- Little fig tree (3,200): ikurira mu gisigo uko usohoka, mu minsi 50.

Hitamo ingofero idasanzwe mu Iduka urebe uko isubiza. Utubuto twa **Pat** na **New coins** munsi y'ikarita yayo dusubiramo buri gisubizo. Izindi ngofero na zo zirakora, kandi zimwe zifite agace gato kamwe gakora. Vugurura WitnessWork niba utabona izi ngofero.

## Byiza kumenya

- Kugira ngo ubone ingaruka z'ijoro ku manywa, shyira idirishya kuri **Nijoro** mu makuru y'itungo ryawe.
- Iyo Reduce Motion ifunguye, ingofero zikora zigumya zihagaze, kandi utubuto twa **Pat** na **New coins** ntitugaragara. Ntizinakora mu mafoto asangijwe cyangwa muri widget.
- Zimwe mu ngofero ni ibanga. Reba [Ingofero n'ibintu by'icyumba by'ibanga ni ibihe?](/help/pet/pet-secrets).

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ingofero ntikora:** reba ko Reduce Motion izimye mu igenamiterere ry'igikoresho cyawe.
- **Waguze ingofero ku gikoresho gikeneye kuvugururwa:** vugurura WitnessWork. Kugeza icyo gihe, itungo ryawe ryerekanwa ritayifite.
- **Biracyari bibi:** koresha **Ukeneye ubufasha bundi?** munsi y'iki gisubizo kugira ngo uvugane n'ubufasha. Shyiramo verisiyo yawe ya WitnessWork, kandi ntushyiremo amazina n'inyandiko bwite.
