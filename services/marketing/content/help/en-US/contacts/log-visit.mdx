id: logVisit
title: How do I quickly log a visit?
category: contacts
---

Tap + and choose Log Visit, then pick the person. On iPad, Log Visit… is also in the menu bar's File menu. Without searching, the list starts with Nearby (contacts within about 250 m of you, closest first), then Follow-ups Due (today's and missed ones from the last 30 days), then Recent (visited in the last 2 weeks), then All Contacts A–Z. Type to search; if the person isn't there, tap Add as a new contact to create them and log the visit next. After you pick someone, tap Had a Conversation to add notes and a follow-up in the visit form, or Not at Home to log it right away, with Undo at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds. On a contact's page, + Add → Not at Home logs it the same way. In data protection mode, Not at Home isn't offered and the visit form opens directly. Nearby is optional: it appears only if you allow location, and you're never asked unless you tap Turn On. Your location is used on this device only to sort the list; it isn't saved or sent anywhere. If Nearby is missing, check that location is allowed for WitnessWork in your device settings and that the contact has a location on the map. Touch and hold a person to open their contact instead.
