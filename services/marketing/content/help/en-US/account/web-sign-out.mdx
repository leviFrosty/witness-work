---
id: webSignOut
title: How do I sign out of WitnessWork on the web?
category: account
platforms: [web]
---

Sign out in Settings → Account. While the browser is online, it first tries for a few seconds to send Cloud Sync any change that's still waiting. Then it ends this browser's session with WitnessWork and erases the account key, the session, and all WitnessWork data stored in this browser. Changes that haven't synced by then are erased with it, so reconnect and check that Settings → Sync (Device Sync before version 1.47.0) shows everything synced before you sign out.

Signing out doesn't delete your account or your records on WitnessWork's server. It also keeps this browser's Cloud Sync copy there for 30 days, or until the next browser you sign in to has taken in its records. To use WitnessWork in this browser again, sign in with your passkey, your phone, or your recovery key ([How do I sign in on a computer with my phone?](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer)); your records and your Supporter status come back through Cloud Sync.

If you use WitnessWork only in browsers, with no phone on Cloud Sync, that copy is where your records are: sign in again in a browser within 30 days to keep them (that browser keeps its own copy from then on), or download a backup before you sign out.

Sign out whenever you use a computer that isn't yours or that other people share. You can also sign a browser out from another device (see [How long does a browser stay signed in?](/help/account/web-sessions)); anything that browser hasn't synced is lost.
