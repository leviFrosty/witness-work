id: territoriesSharedWithMe
title: Why can't I edit or return a territory someone shared with me?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
platforms: [ios, android]
---

Only the territory's owner, the person who added it and shared it, can edit it, add or delete streets, or return it. A territory a buddy shared or handed to you shows "Shared with you by (name)" or "Handed to you by (name)", and lets you work it.

**When a buddy shared the territory, or some of its streets:** you can record doors, mark streets worked, add do-not-calls, and add results from notes on what they shared. You can't share it onward. To take it off your list, open it and tap Leave; the doors you recorded stay with the owner.

**When a buddy handed you a street:** it's yours to work. You can also share it with your own buddies or make a guest link for it, but you can't hand it off again. When you're done, tap Give Back; the doors you recorded stay on it.

**When it says "No longer shared with you":** the owner stopped sharing it, took the street back, or returned the territory. It stays on your list read-only, keeping which streets were worked and the do-not-calls, until you tap Remove From My List.

If the territory needs a change, such as a missing street, ask its owner. To work a territory you were given on paper on your own, add it yourself from the Territories tab instead.
