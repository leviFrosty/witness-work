---
id: webSignOut
title: How do I sign out of WitnessWork on the web?
category: account
platforms: [web]
---

Sign out in Settings → Account. It ends this browser's session with WitnessWork and then erases the account key, the session, and all WitnessWork data stored in this browser. Anything that hadn't synced yet is erased with it, so let Cloud Sync finish first.

Signing out doesn't delete your account or your records on WitnessWork's server. It also keeps this browser's Cloud Sync copy there for 30 days. To use WitnessWork in this browser again, sign in with your passkey, your phone, or your recovery key ([How do I sign in on a computer with my phone?](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer)).

Sign out whenever you use a computer that isn't yours or that other people share. You can also sign a browser out from another device (see [How long does a browser stay signed in?](/help/account/web-sessions)); anything that browser hasn't synced is lost.
