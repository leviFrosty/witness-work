id: googleDriveSyncPrivacy
title: Google Drive Sync isunga cinshi, elyo nani ungamona?
---

Ifyebo fyenu fya WitnessWork fisungwa mu folda ifisiwa mu Google Drive yenu mwine. WitnessWork fye e ingasula folda iyo, elyo tingamona cimbi mu Drive yenu. Ifyebo fyenu fipita bwino pa pali foni yenu na Google, tafipita pa server sha WitnessWork.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Folda tamoneka mu app nelyo website ya Drive, elyo ama app yambi tayingabelenga.
- Google iyisunga nga fyo isunga amafailo yenu yambi aya Drive. Ipendwa mu bwikalo bwenu bwa Google.
- WitnessWork tasunga imeili yenu.
- Amafailo yenu ya backup ukufuma pa **Ifyo Mwasala → Ukusunga & Ukubwekeshepo** ya kulekanya. Yashala apo mwayasungile.

## Ukuleka ukubomfya Google Drive Sync

- **Pa foni imo:** isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Sync** elyo mufumyepo Google Drive. Ifibombelo fyenu fimbi fyatwalilila ukubomfya folda.
- **Pa fibombelo fyonse:** shimyeni sync pa cila cimo. Pa numa, mu Google Drive pa web, isuleni **Settings → Manage apps → WitnessWork → Options → Delete hidden app data**.
- **Ukufumyapo ukwingila konse:** fumyeni WitnessWork pe pi Third-party apps & services mu Google Account yenu.
