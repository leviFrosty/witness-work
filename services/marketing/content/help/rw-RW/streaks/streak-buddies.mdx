---
id: streakBuddies
title: "Bagenzi banjye bashobora kubona urukurikirane rwanjye?"
---

Yego, niba ururusangije. Bagenzi bawe babona umubare w'urukurikirane rwawe ku ikarita yawe mu rutonde rwabo rwa Buddies, no munsi y'urwabo iyo barukanze Ahabanza. Ntibigera babona igihe cyawe cyangwa raporo.

## Hagarika gusangiza urukurikirane rwawe

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere rya Buddies**.
2. Zimya **Urukurikirane**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ni wowe na bagenzi bawe gusa mushobora kubona ibyo usangiza.
- Urukurikirane rwarangiye ruhagarika kugaragara kuri telefoni za bagenzi bawe, nubwo utafunguye porogaramu.
