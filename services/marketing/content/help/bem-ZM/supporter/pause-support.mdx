title: Bushe kuti naleka ukwafwa pa nshita ukwabula ukukanisha?
---

Ee. Ilyo mwatendeka ukukanisha, WitnessWork ipeela ukuleka pa nshita pa ntanshi.

## Lekeni pa nshita pa iPhone nelyo iPad

1. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Longolokeni ukulipila**.
2. Tinikeni **Cancel support**.
3. Saleni ukuleka ukulipila pa myeshi 1, 3 nelyo 6.

Ukuleka kutendeka ilyo inshita yenu ya nomba yapwa. Tamulipilishiwa pa nshita iyo, elyo ifya Supporter fyashala. Ukulipila kubwelela pa mushuma umo fye pa bushiku bulanga.

## Lekeni pa nshita pa Android

1. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Longolokeni ukulipila**.
2. Tinikeni **Cancel support**.
3. Saleni **Pause** mu Google Play, elyo musale imyeshi 1 ukufika 3. Ici cibomba pa kulipila kwa pa mweshi.

Ifya Supporter nafyo fileka pa nshita, elyo fibwela pa lwafyo na fyo mwasala fyashala. Kuti mwabwelelapo bwangu mu Google Play.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Kuti mwaleka pa nshita umuku umo pa myeshi 12.
- Kuti mwacili mwakanisha inshita iili yonse mu ukulipila kwa store yenu.
- Ukuleka pa nshita tapeelwa ilyo muli mu free trial, ilyo kuli ubwafya bwa kulipila, na Family Sharing, nelyo pa myeshi 12 ya kuleka kwenu kwa kulekelesha.

## Nga cabipa

- **Ukuleka pa nshita kwabula:** takuli ico cicinja. Esheni pa numa, nelyo mulongolole ukulipila kwenu mu store.
- **Ico cimbi conse:** bomfyeni **nomba line kabila afwilisha?** ukulembela support mu cisuma.
