Tap **I already use WitnessWork** when setup starts. The **Welcome back** screen brings your records back from your WitnessWork account through Cloud Sync.

If you tap **Get started** instead and WitnessWork finds your records, it opens **Welcome back** for you.

## Get your records back

- **Your account is already on this phone:** this happens when the phone uses the same Apple Account as your other iPhone or iPad, or the same Google Account as your other Android device. WitnessWork finds your records, downloads them and says **Welcome back! You're ready to go.** Your photos download after that.
- **Your account isn't on this phone yet:** sign in first. Tap **Sign in with a passkey**, **From your other phone** (scan a QR code), or **Use your recovery key**. Cloud Sync then brings your records over.
- **You used iCloud, Google Drive or a backup file:** choose it under **Other ways to restore**.
- **You're new:** tap **I'm new here**. You can turn on Cloud Sync later in **Settings → Sync**.

## Good to know

- Your service records, contacts, visits, plans and shared preferences come back. Settings that belong to one device, like its sync option and data protection mode, stay with each device.
- Only your devices can read your records.
- If this phone already has records, WitnessWork asks first. Tap **Restore & keep in sync**, then choose **Merge** to keep them and add your Cloud Sync records, or **Replace** to use only your Cloud Sync records.
- To bring records from MyTime, notes or paper into a new account, set up as new and use the **Bring in your records** step, or **Settings → Import Data** later.

## If something goes wrong

- **It couldn't reach Cloud Sync:** check your internet connection and tap **Search again**.
- **Still stuck:** use the private support option in the Help Center.
