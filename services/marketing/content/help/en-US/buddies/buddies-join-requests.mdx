title: How do I answer or turn off requests to join my Plans?
category: buddies
---

When a buddy asks to join your Plan, the request appears in the notification bell on Home. Tap **Invite** to add them.

## Answer a request

1. Open the notification bell on Home.
2. Tap **Invite** to send the invitation. The button turns into a check.
3. Or tap **Not Now** to clear the request. Your buddy isn't told.

You can also tap the request to open the Plan and tap **Invite** there.

## Turn off alerts

1. For every buddy: open **Settings → Notifications** and turn off **Ask to Join Alerts**.
2. For one buddy: open their page, tap the pencil (**Edit Buddy**), tap **Sharing & Alerts**, and turn off **Ask to Join Alerts**. Or touch and hold their request and choose **Turn Off Alerts**.

Requests still appear in the bell either way.

## Good to know

- If the Plan came from someone else's invitation, only the organizer can invite.
- Inviting someone to one day of a recurring plan makes that day its own Plan. Your other days stay the same.
- If your Plans that day changed since they asked, open the Plan you're keeping and invite them there.
- **Not Now** and alert settings apply to this phone only.
