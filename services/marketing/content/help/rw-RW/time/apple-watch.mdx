id: appleWatch
title: "Hari porogaramu ya Apple Watch?"
---

Yego. WitnessWork ifite porogaramu ya Apple Watch ku iPhone ihujwe na Apple Watch ifite watchOS 26 cyangwa irenga.

## Yishyireho

1. Niba Automatic App Install ifunguye, porogaramu yishyiraho yonyine. Naho ubundi, fungura porogaramu ya Watch kuri iPhone yawe.
2. Shaka WitnessWork munsi ya **Available Apps** ukande **Install**.
3. Fungura WitnessWork kuri iPhone yawe rimwe, kugira ngo isaha yawe ibone aho ugeze.

## Icyo ushobora gukora

- Reba amasaha y'uku kwezi n'niba uri imbere cyangwa inyuma ya gahunda zawe.
- Ongeraho igihe cy'uyu munsi, ufite Ubwoko ushobora kongeraho.
- Tangiza kandi uhagarike by'agateganyo igihe kibarwa cya iPhone yawe, ubike igihe cyacyo nk'inyandiko.
- Andika ko wabwirije muri uku kwezi, niba utandika amasaha nk'Umubwiriza.
- Ongeraho complication ya **Iterambere ry'ukwezi** ku isura y'isaha kugira ngo ubone amasaha yawe agana ku ntego y'uku kwezi, na **Ibikurikira** kugira ngo ubone Gusubirayo kwawe gukurikira cyangwa gahunda. Reba [Ibikurikira ku Apple Watch yanjye byerekana iki?](/help/time/apple-watch-up-next)
- Koresha Siri ku isaha yawe. Reba [Nshobora gukoresha Siri cyangwa Shortcuts na WitnessWork?](/help/time/siri)

## Byiza kumenya

- iPhone yawe ibika inyandiko zawe. Isaha ntishobora guhindura cyangwa gusiba ibyanditswe.
- Isaha ntiyakira inyandiko zawe cyangwa amakuru y'abantu. Kuri Ibikurikira yakira amazina, aderesi n'insanganyamatsiko bya Gusubirayo kwawe n'imitwe n'aho gahunda zawe ziri mu byumweru bibiri biri imbere.
- Porogaramu ikeneye iPhone yawe, bityo ntiboneka ku Apple Watch yashyizweho na Family Setup.
- Ufite telefoni ya Android, koresha porogaramu ya Wear OS ahubwo.
- Niba isaha ivuga **Guhuza na iPhone**, reba [Kuki Apple Watch yanjye ivuga Guhuza na iPhone?](/help/time/apple-watch-sync)
