id: android
title: Is WitnessWork on Android?
category: general
---

Yes. WitnessWork is back on Google Play, and it's free. Time and reports, the timer, contacts and follow-ups, plans, the map (with Google Maps), mileage, reminders, and backups all work on Android. Buddies works on Android too, including with buddies on iPhone. You can keep your devices in sync with Cloud Sync, which also syncs with an iPhone and the web, or with Google Drive Sync between Android devices; from version 1.47.0, sync is included for everyone and a new install starts on Cloud Sync. A few features rely on Apple services and stay on iPhone and iPad: iCloud Sync, widgets, Live Activities, and app icons. Scribe AI works on Android too. You can also use WitnessWork in your browser at app.witnesswork.app, on the same account through Cloud Sync, including without the app (see "Can I use WitnessWork in a web browser?"). To move to a new phone on either platform, use Set Up Another Device on the old phone (Settings → Account); with Cloud Sync, the new phone gets your records without a restore step, or it offers to restore your Backup if you turned it on in Settings → Account. You can also create a backup file (Settings → Backup & Restore → Create Backup), copy it to the new phone, then choose From a backup file on the “Pick up where you left off” setup screen. Between Android devices you can also choose From Google Drive there. Keep backup files private; they can contain householder details.
