---

Fungura umuntu hanyuma ukande "Reka." Uzahitamo igihe uzamureka (icyumweru 1, ukwezi 1, amezi 3, amezi 6, cyangwa umwaka 1). Umuntu avanwa ku nyandiko zawe zikora muri icyo gihe, hanyuma agasubira ubwabyo igihe kirangiye. Niba amamenyesha afunguye, uzabona kandi urwibutso igihe agarutse, bityo abantu warekeye ntibazibagirana bucece. Ushobora kubona cyangwa kugarura abantu warekeye igihe icyo ari cyo cyose ukoresheje ihuza riri hasi ku rutonde rw'Abantu bawe, ibikubiyemo bya ••• muri Abantu, cyangwa Igenamiterere → Abantu warekeye.
