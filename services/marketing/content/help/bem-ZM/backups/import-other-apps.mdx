id: importOtherApps
title: Bushe kuti naleta ifyebo ukufuma ku MyTime, Ministry Assistant, nelyo app imbi?
---

Kuti mwaleta backup ya MyTime. Amafailo ya ma app yambi tayabako, lelo kuti mwabika amalembo ayabelengwa mu Scribe AI.

## Ukuleta ukufuma ku MyTime

1. Mu MyTime, yeni ku **More → Settings → Backup** elyo mwitumine backup pa imeili.
2. Sungeni ifailo ya .mytimedb iyaishile pa cibombelo cenu. Pa Android nelyo web, itumeni ukufuma ku MyTime pa iPhone yenu elyo mukopolole ifailo pa cibombelo ico.
3. Mu WitnessWork, isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Leteni Ifyebo → ukufuma MyTime**. Ilyo mulepanga, saleni **ukufuma MyTime** pa cifulo.
4. Saleni ifailo.
5. Moneni ico yaleta, elyo muilete.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Pa web, ifailo ishala mu browser yenu elyo tayatumwa. Ifailo iyakulu ukucila pa 25 MB yakanwa.
- Pa app imbi, eseni ukutuma amalembo ayabelengwa elyo mukayabike mu Scribe AI. Moneni ico ipanga pa ntanshi ya kusunga.
- Amafailo ya backup ya WitnessWork yaya mu **Ifyo Mwasala → Ukusunga & Ukubwekeshepo**, te apa.
- Ukuleta ifyebo ukufuma mu Notes nelyo pa pepala pa numa, bomfyeni **Ifyo Mwasala → Leteni Ifyebo**.

## Nga ilanda ukuti ifailo te database

Ukuleta kufwaya ifailo ya .mytimedb iya ntanshi iyapangwa ne MyTime. PDF, amalembo yatumwa, backup ya WitnessWork, nelyo ifailo iyapyaniwa ishina tafibomba. Sungeni ifailo ya ntanshi ukwabula ukucinja. Nga backup iisuma ichili ukulebula, tumeni imeili kuli support mu fyasuma ne bwafya ne nshita ya WitnessWork yenu. Mwisabika backup ku bantu bonse.
