---
id: territoryHouseNumbers
title: Can WitnessWork fill in the house numbers on a territory's streets?
category: map
platforms: [ios, android]
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

Yes, where the map has them. Draw the territory's outline, then open the territory and tap **Find Streets in the Outline** at the bottom of its Streets list. Once the territory has streets without house numbers, the same row is called **Find House Numbers**. While it looks up street names, WitnessWork also asks for the house numbers inside the outline. Streets that have them show how many houses were found ("24 houses"). When you add the streets, their house numbers come with them. If a street you already added gets house numbers, the screen offers **Add House Numbers** for it. A list you already have is never replaced.

On a street with house numbers, the **Houses** card shows every house as a button, colored by what you recorded there, with how many you've visited and the percentage. Tap a house to put it in the recorder, then tap Not at Home, Talked, or Do Not Call. After each house, the recorder moves to the next house on the list that you haven't recorded, keeping to the street's side if you number one side at a time. The Streets list shows each street's progress and the percentage of houses visited across the whole territory, which helps you pace the territory and decide which streets to hand off.

House numbers come from OpenStreetMap, a map made by volunteers, so coverage depends on the area. Many cities have every house, and many suburbs and rural areas have none yet. When a street has no house numbers, everything works as before: type the first number and the recorder counts up from there. A house the list doesn't have can still be typed in and recorded.

To look up house numbers you need to be signed in to your WitnessWork account and online. WitnessWork sends only the territory's outline to our server, which looks it up in OpenStreetMap; no names, notes, or door records are sent. The house list is saved with the street and shared with anyone you share the street with. Finding streets and house numbers works on iPhone, iPad, and Android.

If no house numbers come back, the screen says why: the area isn't mapped yet, you're signed out, the outline is too large, or the lookup is busy. If the lookup is busy, try **Find Streets in the Outline** again in a minute. For other problems, use the Help Center's private support option and keep addresses out of public reports.
