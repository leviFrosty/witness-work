id: appInfo
title: "Nasanga hehe verisiyo ya porogaramu n'indangamuntu ya konti yanjye?"
---

Fungura **Igenamiterere → Ibyerekeye → App Info**. Verisiyo ya porogaramu iri hejuru, naho indangamuntu ya konti iri munsi ya **Konti**.

## Kopora amakuru y'ubufasha

1. Kanda ifoto y'umwirondoro wawe, hanyuma **Igenamiterere**.
2. Fungura **Ibyerekeye → App Info**.
3. Kanda **Copy All Details**, cyangwa ukande umurongo uwo ariwo wose kugira ngo ukopore agaciro kawo gusa.
4. Ukabihambire mu imeri y'ibanga yoherezwa ku bufasha.

## Ibiriho

- Verisiyo na build ya WitnessWork, igikoresho cyawe na verisiyo ya sisitemu yacyo, na verisiyo ya seriveri ya WitnessWork.
- **Account ID** ni konti yawe ya WitnessWork. Handitse **No account yet** kugeza igihe iki gikoresho cyinjiriye.
- **Purchases ID** ni yo ihuza ibyo waguze kuri App Store cyangwa Google Play.

## Byiza kumenya

- Gukopora nta kintu byohereza. Ni wowe uhitamo aho ubihambira.
- Sangiza izi ndangamuntu gusa mu imeri y'ibanga yoherezwa ku bufasha, ntuzigeze uzishyira muri raporo rusange y'ikibazo cyangwa mu ifoto ya ecran.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Verisiyo ya seriveri handitse Unavailable:** iki gikoresho nticyashoboye kugera kuri WitnessWork. Reba murandasi yawe ufungure **App Info** nanone.
