id: buddiesRestore
title: Nshobora kugarura bagenzi banjye ku kindi gikoresho cyangwa mu backup?
---

Kuri iPhone na iPad, urufunguzo rwawe rwo guhisha rwa Buddies rubikwa muri iCloud Keychain, bityo Buddies igarura amasano yayo ku kindi gikoresho gikoresha Apple Account imwe, cyangwa nyuma yo kongera gushyiraho WitnessWork, iyo ihuza. Kuri Android, serivisi za Google Play zibika urufunguzo: rugaruka iyo wongeye gushyiraho WitnessWork kuri telefoni imwe mu gihe backup ya Google ifunguye (Igenamiterere → Google → Backup), kandi iyo ufite gufunga ecran, runabikwa, rwahishwe impande zombi, muri backup yawe ya Google, bityo rushobora kugaruka iyo utegura telefoni nshya uvuye kuri iyo backup. Nta gufunga ecran, urufunguzo rukomeza kuba kuri iyo telefoni. Ibikoresho bya Android ntibisangira urufunguzo rumwe nk'uko iCloud Keychain ibikora, bityo buri gikoresho cya Android, na iPhone na telefoni ya Android, bifite amasano yabyo bwite ya Buddies. Dosiye zisanzwe za backup ya WitnessWork ntizirimo urufunguzo rwo guhisha. Kugarura Buddies bitandukanye na iCloud Sync y'Umuterankunga ku nyandiko z'umurimo. Niba urufunguzo rutaboneka, cyangwa wakoresheje Siba amakuru yanjye ya Buddies, ongera uhuze ukoresheje ubutumire bushya. Bika inyandiko zawe bwite muri backup ukwazo.
