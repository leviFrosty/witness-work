id: progressStats
title: What do the Year and All-time cards count?
category: customization
---

Each card counts from your own records, worked out on your phone. Nothing new is stored or shared.

## Time

- **Year:** time counts as each month's report counts it, so credit time counts up to that month's limit.
- **All-time:** time counts as you logged it, before monthly credit limits. This matches your lifetime hours and Year by Year.

## Averages and comparisons

- **Average on Year:** runs from your first month with time to the last month that's ended. The month under way joins only while it's the only one.
- **Average on All-time:** covers the service years that have ended, or the one under way if it's your first.
- **Last Year:** compares this service year so far with last year at the same point, or the whole of both once the year is over.

## Months, studies and visits

- **Months Out:** counts months you shared in the ministry, with or without hours, from your first record. The month under way counts once you've shared in it, and is never shown as missed. All-time also shows your longest run of months in a row.
- **Studies:** counts each contact you marked as a Bible study once, and shows the most any one month had. That's the figure for your report. Deleted contacts aren't counted.
- **Visits:** counts visits where you talked with someone. Not-at-homes are counted separately.
- Visits that came with a contact someone shared with you are theirs, so Studies and Visits leave them out.

## If something goes wrong

- **A figure looks wrong:** check the month's report and the visits on the contact. You can contact support from the Help Center, leaving out names and notes.
