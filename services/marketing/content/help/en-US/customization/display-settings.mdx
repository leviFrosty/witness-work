---
id: displaySettings
title: Does WitnessWork support Bold Text, larger text, and other display settings?
category: customization
platforms:
  - ios
  - android
---

Yes. WitnessWork follows your device's display settings; there's nothing to turn on in the app. Text size: it uses the size you choose (Larger Text on iPhone and iPad, Font size on Android), up to the largest accessibility sizes, and wraps or stacks rows so words stay whole; on a tablet, very large text swaps the sidebar for the bottom bar. Bold Text on iPhone and iPad, and Bold text on Android 12 or later, make WitnessWork's text heavier too; on Android, the change shows when you come back to WitnessWork. Reduce Transparency (iPhone and iPad) makes blurred headers and bars solid. Increase Contrast (iPhone and iPad) makes gray text and borders stand out more, in light and dark mode; on Android, High contrast text is applied by the system. Reduce Motion (Remove animations on Android) skips confetti and keeps celebrations still. To choose light or dark mode, use Settings → Personalization → Color Scheme. These settings change only how WitnessWork looks, never your records. If text is cut off or hard to read, report it from the Help Center with a screenshot, your device, and your text size; cover names and addresses first.
