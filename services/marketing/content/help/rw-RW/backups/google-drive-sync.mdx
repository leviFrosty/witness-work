---
id: googleDriveSync
title: Nahuza nte amakuru yanjye hagati y'ibikoresho bya Android?
---

Google Drive Sync ni ikiranga cy'Umuterankunga kuri Android, kikiri mu igeragezwa (beta). Kuri buri telefoni cyangwa tablet ya Android, fungura Igenamiterere → Google Drive Sync ufungure Guhuza na Google Drive, hanyuma uhitemo Konti imwe ya Google mu idirishya rya Google, wemere ko WitnessWork ibika amakuru yayo muri Google Drive yawe. Ihuza inyandiko z'umurimo, gahunda, abantu, gusura, ibyiciro, ibirometero, umwirondoro n'amahitamo asangiwe; amafoto afite akuma gatandukanye kitwa Shyiramo amafoto mu guhuza. Buri gikoresho gifite akuma kacyo, kandi ibintu nk'uburyo bwo kurinda amakuru, uruhushya rw'isesengura n'intangiriro (onboarding) bikomeza kuba kuri buri gikoresho. Mu gihe WitnessWork ifunguye, impinduka ziturutse ku bindi bikoresho byawe zihagera mu gihe cy'umunota umwe cyangwa hafi, naho ubundi ubutaha uyifunguye; kanda Huza ubu kugira ngo urebe ako kanya. Ku gikoresho gishya cya Android, hitamo Kuri Google Drive ku rupapuro rwo gutangira rwitwa “Ngaho nimukomeze mujye aho mwasize” kugira ngo ugarure amakuru yawe, ibyo bikazana n'imimerere yawe y'Umuterankunga igihe iyo Konti ya Google ihujwe. Niba igikoresho gihuje Konti ya Google itandukanye, guhuza birazima kuri cyo mbere y'uko hagira icyo gisomwa cyangwa cyoherezwa, kandi inyandiko zawe zikomeza kuba ku gikoresho. Guhuza hagati ya iPhone n'igikoresho cya Android ntibishoboka; koresha Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura kugira ngo wimure backup hagati yabyo.
