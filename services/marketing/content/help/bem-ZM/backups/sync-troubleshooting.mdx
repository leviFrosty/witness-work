id: syncTroubleshooting
title: Ifibombelo fyandi tafilesync. Nshi kuti namona?
---

Tendekeni ne kusula **Ifyo Mwasala → Sync**. Ifibombelo fisync fye na fibombelo fyenu ifili pa nshila imo (Cloud Sync, iCloud nelyo Google Drive), kanshi saleni imo pa fyonse. Elyo mutinike **Lingana nomba**.

## Moneni ifi

1. Intaneti yenu, pa fibombelo fyonse fibili.
2. Cibombelo conse cikwata WitnessWork iyapya. Inshita ya kale nelyo ifailo iyashabelengwa filanga mu status.
3. Pa iCloud pa iPhone nelyo iPad: bomfyeni Apple Account imo, washeni iCloud Drive, elyo mwashe WitnessWork pe pi iCloud pa cibombelo conse.
4. Pa Cloud Sync: leseni app yaisulwa pa fibombelo fyonse fibili.
5. Pa Android, moneni [Google Drive Sync tailabomba](/help/backups/google-drive-sync-troubleshooting).

## Nga cabipa

- **Ilanda ukuti Cloud Sync yaleka:** takuli bwafya kuli imwe. Ifyo mwacinja filolela pa cibombelo ici elyo fisync ilyo yabwela.
- **Status yashala pa Ukutuma ku iCloud:** ilacinja ilyo Apple yasuminisha ukutuma. **Sync ya kulekelesha** ipenda fye ukutuma ukwasuminishiwa.
- **Ubwikalo bwenu bwa iCloud bwaisula:** ifyo mwacinja fyashala pa cibombelo ici. Pangeni apa kuikala nelyo mukushe mu **iPhone Settings → ishina lyenu → iCloud**, elyo ukutuma kutwalilila.
- **Ipusha ukwingila kwa Supporter:** kushani WitnessWork. Ukusync kwa bwamba.
- **Icikope cabula:** moneni [Mulandu nshi ifikope fya bantu ba kulanshanya fyabula pa cibombelo candi cimbi?](/help/backups/sync-photos).
- **Icili ukubula:** sungeni ifailo ya backup ne cibombelo cikwata ifyo mwalemba ifya kulekelesha. **Pangeni nakabili ifyebo fya iCloud** ipyana ifyebo fya iCloud ne fyalembwa fya cibombelo ici. Ifibombelo fyenu fimbi fyacinja ku fyo ilyo fyasync ilyo ikonkapo, ukupyana ifyo mwacinja pa fyo pa ntanshi iyo. Kushani WitnessWork pa cibombelo conse pa ntanshi. Nelyo mubomfye inshila ya support ya fyasuma mu Help Center, elyo mwisabika ifyebo fya bantu ba mu ng'anda mu fyebo fya bwafya fya bantu bonse.
