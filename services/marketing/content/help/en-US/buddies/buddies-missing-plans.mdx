title: Why are a buddy's plans or invitations missing or out of date?
category: buddies
---

A buddy's plans may be missing because the request isn't confirmed, their sharing is off, or your phone is offline. Pull down on **Buddies** to refresh.

## Things to check

1. Make sure the request is confirmed in **Buddies → Requests**.
2. On **Schedule**, tap the day. It lists every buddy's plans for that day.
3. Open **Buddies** and pull down to refresh. Both of you need an internet connection.

## Good to know

- Dots under days show only buddies with **Show on my calendar** turned on. It starts off for a new buddy.
- Your buddy may have paused sharing or stopped sharing plans with you. WitnessWork doesn't tell you.
- A buddy who shares only part of the time shows **Morning**, **Afternoon**, **Evening** or **Planned** in place of a time.
- You see plans for the next eight weeks.
- Plans you already downloaded stay visible offline. New changes need a connection.
- Seeing a buddy's plans doesn't add them to yours.

## If something goes wrong

- **No invitation notification:** turn on Buddies notifications in WitnessWork and in your phone's settings. The Home notification bell still shows invitations. If it says it couldn't check, reconnect and tap **Try Again**.
- **Android:** Buddies notifications need Google Play services. Without them, changes arrive when you open WitnessWork.
- **Buddies says it can't verify this device:** see [Why does WitnessWork say it can't verify this device?](/help/account/account-verification).
