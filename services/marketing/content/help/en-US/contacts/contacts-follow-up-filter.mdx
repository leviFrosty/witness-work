Open **Sort & Filter** in Contacts, tap **Add filter**, and choose **Follow-up**. You can then pick contacts that have a return visit planned, or none.

## Find contacts by follow-up

1. Open **Contacts**.
2. Tap **Sort & Filter** at the start of the chips under the search field.
3. Tap **Add filter**, then choose **Follow-up**.
4. Pick a value and tap **Apply**.

The values mean:

- **Upcoming:** a follow-up still ahead, with no visit since.
- **None upcoming:** no return visit planned.
- **Missed:** a follow-up that passed more than 4 hours ago, within the last 30 days, with no visit since. These are the same people as your missed follow-up reminders.

## See who is next

In **Sort & Filter**, choose **Next follow-up** under **Sort**. Each contact then shows when their follow-up is due. The soonest comes first with **Descending**, the latest with **Ascending**. Contacts with no follow-up go last.

## Good to know

- Dismissed follow-ups don't count.
- The filter applies to the list and the map, and can be part of a saved view.
- Nothing is shared.

## If something goes wrong

- **A contact appears where you don't expect:** open their most recent visit and check its follow-up date, or whether a later visit already answered it.
