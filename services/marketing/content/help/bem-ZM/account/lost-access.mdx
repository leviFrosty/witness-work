title: Nga naloba ukwingila mu akaunti yandi ya WitnessWork, nshi nshingacita?
---

Esheni ifi pa nshila. Abengi babwelela na cimo pa fitatu fya ntanshi.

## Inshila sha kubwelela

1. **Foni ipya iyakwata kale key yenu.** iPhone nelyo iPad iyaingila mu Apple Account imo na iCloud Keychain iyaiyakwa ilayisanga pa lwakwe. Pa Android, ukupanga foni ipya ukufuma ku backup yenu ya Google kuti kwabwesha.
2. **Passkey.** Isuleni app.witnesswork.app elyo musale **Sign in with a passkey**. Pa foni ipya, isaleni mu kupanga (**Naliibomfya kale WitnessWork**) nelyo mu **Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti → Sign In with a Passkey**. Nga tacingapwa pa lwakwe, WitnessWork ipusha foni yenu imbi nelyo icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha.
3. **Foni imbi iyacili yaingila.** Langeni code ya **Pangeni Icibombelo Cimbi** pa foni ipya, nelyo musuminishe kompyuta na **Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti → Sign In on a Computer**. Browser tayingacita cimo pa fyo. Moneni [Nshi nshila ya kuya ku foni ipya nelyo ukucinja pa iPhone na Android?](/help/account/account-handoff) na [Nshi nshila ya kwingila pa kompyuta na foni yandi?](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer).
4. **Icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha.** Saleni **Bomfya Icisokoshi ca Kubwesha**. Moneni [Nshi nshila ya kubomfya icisokoshi candi ca kubwesha pa foni ipya?](/help/account/account-recovery-key-use).

## Nga fyonse tafyabomba

Ukwabula icisokoshi ca kubwesha, akaunti tayingabwekeshiwa. Takuli uwa ku WitnessWork uwingaibelenga nelyo ukuibwekeshapo, elyo support tayingacinja ici. Kuti mwatendeka akaunti ipya pa cibombelo cipya, lelo ifyo mwalemba ifya kale tafingabwelela ukufuma pa server.

## Mwishilike ica ku ntanshi

- Sungeni icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha mu password manager, nelyo mucilembe pa mpepa. Moneni [Icisokoshi candi ca kubwesha ni nshi, elyo nshi nshila ya kucisunga bwino?](/help/account/account-recovery-key).
- Lekeni mwasunge na passkey. Moneni [Passkey ni nshi?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
