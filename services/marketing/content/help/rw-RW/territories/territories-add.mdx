title: "Nakora iki ngo nongere agace nafashe?"
---

Fungura igice cya **Uduce**, kanda **Ongeraho agace**, wuzuze amakuru y'ikarita, hanyuma ukande **Bika**. Nyuma uzongeraho imihanda.

## Ongeraho agace

1. Andika **Nomero** y'agace iri ku ikarita. **Akarere cyangwa izina** si ngombwa, urugero "Umusozi wo mu Majyaruguru".
2. Reba **Itariki warikuyeho**, ni umunsi bakuhaye ikarita. **Rigomba kugarurwa bitarenze** ishyirwaho nyuma y'amezi ane. Rihindure niba bakuhaye indi tariki.
3. Munsi ya **Ikarita (si ngombwa)**, ongeraho icyagufasha kubona agace, cyangwa ubyihorere. Reba [Ni ubuhe buryo bwo kongera ikarita ku gace?](/help/territories/territories-map-options)
4. Kanda **Bika**. Agace karafunguka, kiteguye kwakira imihanda. Reba [Nakora iki ngo nongere imihanda ku gace?](/help/territories/territories-streets)

## Byiza kumenya

- Nyuma y'itariki yo **Rigomba kugarurwa bitarenze**, agace kagaragara nk'akarengeje igihe.
- Kugira ngo uhindure ikintu nyuma, fungura agace ukande **Hindura**. Cyangwa ukande kandi ufate agace ku rutonde, hanyuma ukande **Hindura agace**. **Siba agace** na yo iri aho.
