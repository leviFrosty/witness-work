---
id: dataProtectionDevices
title: Does EU / GDPR mode apply to all my devices?
category: general
minAppVersion: "1.50.0"
---

Yes. When you turn **EU / GDPR mode** on or off on one device, your other devices that sync with it ask before they do the same.

## Turn it on everywhere

1. Open **Settings → Privacy**.
2. Turn on **EU / GDPR mode**, then tap **Turn on**.
3. The next time you open WitnessWork on each of your other devices, it says **Moving to EU / GDPR mode**. Tap **Turn on**.

To keep it off on one device, tap **Not on this device** there instead.

## Turn it off

Turn off **EU / GDPR mode** on one device. Your other devices ask **Turn off EU / GDPR mode here too?** Tap **Turn off**, or **Keep it on**.

## Good to know

- A device you kept out shows **On for your other devices** under the switch. To match your other devices, turn the switch on there.
- In a web browser, choose **OK** to follow your other devices or **Cancel** to keep this one as it is.
- A device that doesn't sync keeps its own setting.
- While EU / GDPR mode is on for your account, Calendar Sync leaves names and addresses out of events.
