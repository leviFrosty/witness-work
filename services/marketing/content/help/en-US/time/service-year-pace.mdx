title: How do I read the Service Year Pace chart?
category: time
related:

  - 196
---

Open the Progress tab and switch to Year. If your status has an Annual Goal, such as regular pioneer, the Service Year Pace card appears below your milestones. The dashed gray line is where your Annual Goal puts you by the end of each month. It is built from each month's own goal, so a mid-year appointment or an auxiliary pioneer month changes it, but a Monthly Goal you changed yourself does not. The green line is your time so far, counted the same way as your Service Report, including each month's credit limit. The dashed green line adds the time you have planned. The faint line is last service year, shown when you logged time in it, and the number at its end is last year's total. If a line runs well past your Annual Goal, the space above the goal is drawn on a compressed (logarithmic) scale so the months below stay readable. Press and hold the chart, then slide your finger, to see any month's totals. "Ahead of pace" compares your time with where the goal line is today. Below the card, each month has a bar: solid for logged time, lighter for credit time, and striped for plans, with a tick at that month's goal. The key above the months explains these marks, and the Hours and vs Goal columns give the numbers. Every bar uses the same scale, so you can compare months. Without an Annual Goal you see the month bars but not the chart. If a month looks wrong, press and hold it to change its status or goal. The chart is drawn on your device from your own entries and isn't shared.
