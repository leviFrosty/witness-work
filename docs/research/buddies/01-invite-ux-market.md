# WitnessWork Buddies: invite and relationship-management UX research

_Research date: 2026-09-23. Apple support pages fetched this session describe iOS 27 (for example, the Personal Safety guide says it "applies primarily to … iOS 27"). Every fact below has an inline source. Anything I did not confirm on a primary source this session is labeled **UNVERIFIED** and listed again in §7._

> **Scope note.** The research sub-agents hit an account rate limit before returning anything, and the session's web-search budget ran out. This report is therefore built on primary pages I fetched myself. I verified the Apple-native products, Figma, Excalidraw, Google Drive/Docs, Google Calendar, Google Maps location sharing, Signal group links, Day One Shared Journals (an extra E2E analog), Bitwarden Send, 1Password item sharing, and link-preview behavior (Mysk, Apple TN3156). **I did not verify this session:** Life360, WhatsApp, Snap Map, Strava, Duolingo, BeReal, Locket, TimeTree, Cupla, Howbout, Notion, Linear, tldraw, Telegram, Discord. Where I mention them it's from memory and marked UNVERIFIED.

## 0. TL;DR

- **Links are the norm; codes and QR are the fallbacks.** Newer E2E products put the secret in the URL **fragment** (`#…`), which the browser never sends to the server: Excalidraw ([blog](https://blog.excalidraw.com/end-to-end-encryption/)) and Signal (`https://signal.group/#` + group master key + link password, per [Signal-Android source](https://github.com/signalapp/Signal-Android/blob/main/app/src/main/java/org/thoughtcrime/securesms/groups/v2/GroupInviteLinkUrl.java)). Good links expire and can be revoked: Day One invite links last 14 days ([Day One](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/shared-journals/shared-journals/)), Bitwarden Send defaults to 7 days with a 30-day maximum ([Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/help/send-lifespan/)), and Signal has a "Reset link" option.
- **Link previews hit your URL.** iMessage, WhatsApp, Signal (when previews are on) and Viber generate previews **on the sender's device**. Slack, Discord, LINE, LinkedIn, Zoom and Facebook Messenger fetch them **server-side**, and those services receive the full link text ([Mysk](https://mysk.blog/2020/10/25/link-previews/)). Two rules follow: an invite URL must never be consumed by a GET, and it must never put personal data in its Open Graph tags. Apple itself added "Remove Background Preview" to Invites for privacy ([Apple Invites](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-invites/change-event-settings-dev7e7cef88f/ios)).
- **No app installed, no attribution SDK:** the reliable options are (1) "install, then tap the same link again", (2) paste the link using a user-initiated paste (UIPasteControl avoids the iOS 16+ paste alert, [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uipastecontrol)), and (3) later, an App Clip that hands the invite to the full app through a shared container or keychain ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/sharing-data-between-your-app-clip-and-your-full-app)). Firebase Dynamic Links shut down on **August 25, 2025** ([Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/dynamic-links-faq)).
- **Consent models:** Apple Fitness is mutual on accept ("you can see their activity and they can see yours"). Find My, Health and Google Calendar share one direction at a time. E2E group products often add an **owner-approval** step: Day One ("Opening an invite link sends a request to join – it does not add you"), Signal "Require an admin to approve", and Apple Invites "Approve Guests".
- **Ending a connection:** Apple's current behavior is to stop immediately and send no push. People "may notice", and older OS versions posted a message ([Personal Safety guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/find-my-and-location-sharing-ips05ede4573/web)). In Find My, removal is symmetric. When a Notes owner stops sharing, the note is deleted from participants' devices ([Notes](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/share-and-collaborate-iphe4d04f674/ios)). Apple is also honest that removed people "may still have access" to what they already saw ([Shared Passwords](https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/manage-shared-password-and-passkeys-ips3ce9f6e15/web)).
- **Safety:** iOS Safety Check has Emergency Reset, review by People or by Information, and Quick Exit. It **cannot reach third-party apps** ([Safety Check](https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/safety-check-iphone-ios-16-ips2aad835e1/web)). WitnessWork therefore needs its own "stop sharing with everyone" control. Abusers are often "UI-bound adversaries" who misuse legitimate sharing features ([Freed et al., CHI 2018](https://rist.tech.cornell.edu/papers/stalkers-paradise-intimate.pdf)). Design for visibility, a quiet exit, and minimal data.

## 2. Findings

### 2.1 Invite mechanics

**Link vs code vs QR vs contact picker**

- **Links sent through the share sheet or Messages dominate.** Apple's own guidance is built around them: "Messages allows people to send collaboration invitations using existing conversations. No need to ask for email addresses" ([WWDC22 10015](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/10015/)). Apple Invites uses links ([guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-invites/invite-guests-dev851dd16db/ios)). Family Sharing sends through "AirDrop, Messages, or Mail" and also offers **Invite in Person** ([guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/add-family-sharing-members-iph8f958ab3f/ios)).
- **Contact-based invites** (Fitness, Health, Find My, Google Calendar) assume an account identity such as an Apple Account or Google Account. WitnessWork has no accounts, so it can't use this model. An Apple-native alternative exists: Messages collaboration can hand the app "a set of cryptographic identities representing the recipients". The server then checks a signed identity proof when the link is opened, "all without exchanging account information" ([WWDC22 10093](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/10093/)). This only works for iMessage recipients who already have the app, and it needs native Swift. It could be a later hardening step, not a v1 feature.
- **QR codes** cover the in-person case. Signal: "People who scan this code will be able to join your group. Admins will still need to approve new members if you have that setting turned on" ([strings](https://github.com/signalapp/Signal-Android/blob/main/app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml)).
- **Codes to type or paste** are the lowest-tech fallback (Life360-style, U). They matter most for getting an invite across an App Store install.

**Single-use vs multi-use; default expiry**

- Multi-use "anyone with the link" is common for groups (Apple Invites, Notes, Figma, Google Drive, Signal). Each pairs it with something that limits damage: approval (Apple Invites "Approve Guests", Signal, Day One), reset (Signal), or a password (Figma).
- Apple Invites also offers **per-person unique links**, and "Guests you invite individually can automatically join the event" ([guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-invites/approve-or-deny-rsvp-requests-dev48e9e39e0/ios)). The pattern: _personal links skip approval, public links need it._
- Expiry examples: Day One 14 days; Bitwarden Send 7 days by default, 30 max, plus a max access count; Google Maps link sharing up to 24 h; Figma link expiry on Enterprise only; Google Drive expiry for work/school accounts only; 1Password lets the sender choose and remembers that choice.
- **Communicate the cap and the expiry on the invite sheet itself.** Day One's sheet says: "Share this invite link so they can request to join. You can approve requests for up to 30 members. Link expires in 14 days." ([Day One](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/shared-journals/shared-journals/)).

**How the invite renders in iMessage and other messengers (and who hits the URL)**

- Preview generation, per [Mysk (2020)](https://mysk.blog/2020/10/25/link-previews/):
  - **Sender generates it:** iMessage, Signal (if link previews are on), Viber, WhatsApp.
  - **A server generates it:** Discord, Facebook Messenger, LINE, LinkedIn, Slack, Zoom. Instagram and Messenger servers were found to download entire files.
  - Mysk found apps "exposing links sent in end-to-end encrypted chats". A link's fragment stays hidden from _your_ server and from HTTP fetchers, but a messaging platform that previews server-side receives the whole link from its own client.
- Apple's TN3156 (TN2444 now redirects there) says previews use `og:title`, `og:image` and similar tags, and "do not follow meta redirects, nor run JavaScript; metadata must be available directly on the linked page. Server-side redirects are followed" ([TN3156](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3156-create-rich-previews-for-messages)). So the preview can never see the fragment or decrypt the invite. It should be generic ("WitnessWork buddy invite" plus the icon).
- **Precedent for suppressing personal preview detail:** Apple Invites' "Remove Background Preview" setting will "only show the background image to guests after they've opened the invitation with a verified email address or from a private link" ([Invites settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-invites/change-event-settings-dev7e7cef88f/ios)).
- **Implications:** GET requests on invite URLs must be side-effect-free and must not consume tokens. Acceptance should be an explicit POST from the app. OG tags should carry no personal data.

**When the recipient doesn't have the app (no third-party attribution SDK)**

- **Fragment-carried secrets survive the web fallback.** "Anything that's added after the # doesn't get sent to the server, but is readable from the client-side JavaScript code" ([Excalidraw](https://blog.excalidraw.com/end-to-end-encryption/)). The landing page can offer "Copy invite link" (full URL including the fragment) or even decrypt and personalize the page on the client.
- **Universal-link matching supports fragments.** Apple's AASA `components` dictionaries include a `#` (fragment) key ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/applinks/details-swift.dictionary/components-swift.dictionary)). Once the app is installed, **tapping the same link again** opens it in the app with the whole URL. For iMessage and WhatsApp users this is the simplest "deferred deep link" there is, because the message is still sitting in the thread.
- **Clipboard:** "In iOS 16 and later, programmatic pasting raises a user alert … Use this class [UIPasteControl] to paste without a user prompt" ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uipastecontrol)). Reading the clipboard silently on first launch is visible to users and feels invasive. A user-initiated "Paste invite" is fine.
- **App Clip handoff:** "Store App Clip data and assets in a shared container … Store sensitive App Clip data in the keychain and access it in your full app" ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/sharing-data-between-your-app-clip-and-your-full-app)). An App Clip could show the invite card and keep the invite through install. It is heavier to build in Expo, and the details (App Clip cards for Messages links, size limits) are UNVERIFIED here.
- **SDKs:** Firebase Dynamic Links: "On August 25th, 2025, Firebase Dynamic Links will shut down" ([Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/dynamic-links-faq)). Commercial deferred-deep-link SDKs rely on device matching, which conflicts with WitnessWork's privacy stance. Apple's specific fingerprinting rules were not re-verified this session (U).
- **Web-only fallback precedent:** Apple Invites lets people "RSVP on iCloud.com" without an Apple Account ([guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-invites/rsvp-to-an-event-devc9d9cdbd5/ios)). For WitnessWork, acceptance has to happen in the app, where the keys are, so the web page is only a bridge to the App Store.

**What the pre-accept confirmation shows**

- Signal: "When you request to join, your name and photo will be shared with its members."
- Check In tells the recipient what they'll receive and when: "your contact is informed about … What they can expect if you don't respond".
- The Fitness safety page states what is _not_ shared: "It doesn't give them any information about your location" ([PS guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/manage-activity-sharing-on-apple-watch-ips91d58b7ba/web)).
- Apple Invites controls what an invitee sees before replying: "By default, guests can see who's invited after they reply."
- Apple's collaboration HIG: "Write succinct phrases that summarize the sharing permissions … 'Only invited people can edit'" ([HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/collaboration-and-sharing)).

### 2.2 Consent

- **Mutual on accept:** Fitness ("you can see their activity and they can see yours"). This is the closest match to Buddies' "see each other's plans".
- **Separate one-way directions:** Find My (share yours; ask to follow theirs), Health (the recipient of "Ask Someone to Share" "control[s] what data they share and when to stop sharing"), Google Calendar.
- **Invite + accept + owner approval:** Day One ("Opening an invite link sends a request to join – it does not add you to the journal. The entries only download once the owner approves"), Signal (optional), Apple Invites (optional, public links only). This matters for E2E: approval is the natural moment for the owner's device to hand keys to the new member.
- **Declining:** Google Maps lets you ignore or block a request: "If someone blocks your request, you can no longer ask for their location" ([help](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/15437054)). Signal and Apple Invites turn a deny into a block on re-requests. No product I checked tells the requester "X declined". Pending simply stays pending, or expires.
- **Acceptance feedback to the inviter:** Health says "You receive a notification when your invitation is accepted". Fitness moves the friend out of "Invited".
- **Consent for watcher features:** In Find My, "Your friend gets an alert after you set the notification", and recurring or "is not at" alerts need approval. In Google Maps the sharer is emailed when someone adds an alert, and turning alerts off is silent ("They won't be notified that you turned off notifications").

### 2.3 Management

- **Connection lists** exist everywhere: Fitness "Sharing" (with an "Invited" area), Find My People, Notes "Manage Shared Note", Invites "Manage Guests", Day One members, Google Calendar "Shared with".
- **Per-connection granularity** is usually two or three toggles, not a matrix:
  - Fitness: Hide, Mute, Remove
  - iCloud Calendar: Allow Editing per person
  - Notes: Edit or View per person, plus a global Sharing Options
  - Health: topic-level
  - Google Calendar: four levels
  - Apple's HIG says: "Keep the number of custom choices to a minimum and group them in ways that help people understand them at a glance."
- **Pause or hide:** Fitness "Hide My Activity" hides one direction indefinitely. Find My "Hide Location" auto-resumes "the next day" (new in Sept 2026, per [What's new](https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/whats-new-in-personal-safety-ips16d2b1802/1.0/web/1.0)). On the viewer side, Google Maps has "Hide from map".
- **Per-connection mute:** Fitness "Mute Notifications". Invites and Google offer per-type push and email toggles.
- **Removal and existing data:**
  - Find My is symmetric ("removed from your People list and you're removed from theirs").
  - Notes deletes the note from others' devices when the owner stops sharing.
  - Day One: a revoked member's journal "will no longer appear in your Day One app at all".
  - Family Sharing leaves shared content on the device.
  - Shared Passwords: the removed person "may still have access to the accounts and passwords you shared while they were in the group".
  - Day One's privacy FAQ: members "will be able to view, save, and even export all entries … once it's viewed by others, it's outside your control" ([FAQ](https://dayoneapp.com/shared-journals/privacy/)).
- **Is the other side notified?**
  - Find My: no; people "may notice", and older OS versions posted a message.
  - iCloud Calendar: "may be notified".
  - Apple Invites: yes, softened to "the event has been removed".
  - Fitness: not documented.
- **Blocking** is mostly about stopping _re-requests_: Apple Invites deny-and-block (with Unblock), Signal deny, Google Maps block.
- **Caps and how they're shown:**
  - Fitness 40: found only in a troubleshooting article, so people discover it by hitting an error.
  - Family Sharing: organizer + 5, stated in docs.
  - Day One 30: stated **on the invite sheet**, with a rationale ("designed to maintain an intimate and manageable group size").
  - CKShare 100; Find My Notify Me 25; Google Maps alerts 5 per person.
- **Pending invites:** Fitness has "Invite Again" and swipe-to-Delete. Notes has "Cancel Invitation". Family Sharing lets you "follow up with reminders". Apple Invites lets you copy and resend a person's unique link. Day One links auto-expire after 14 days.

### 2.4 Transparency and safety

- **Safety Check** ([guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/safety-check-iphone-ios-16-ips2aad835e1/web), [FAQ](https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/safety-check-faq-ips5f85e089f/web)):
  - Two paths: **Emergency Reset** ("Immediate and universal reset of sharing for all people and apps") and **Manage Sharing & Access** (review by **People** or by **Information**).
  - **Quick Exit**: "Individuals in abusive situations may need to rapidly hide their use of Safety Check."
  - "Progress is saved as you go", followed by a verification step.
  - **Scope limit:** "Safety Check can't review or change … Non-Apple accounts … Social media sharing." Apple tells users that "Managing relationships within the Apple ecosystem does not extend to … a third-party app", and to look in each app's "safety, security, privacy, data sharing, and discoverability settings" ([third-party](https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/third-party-app-settings-ips37f0fef41/web)). **WitnessWork has to provide this itself.**
  - Apple's cautions: "Changes you make to sharing or access may be noticed by others" and "When you change a sharing relationship, you may lose access to tools and information that could be important to you" ([considerations](https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/safety-considerations-ips84b821dd3/1.0/web/1.0)).
- **Reminders that you're sharing:**
  - iOS reminds users "from time to time" about background location use ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102515)).
  - Apple advises using Safety Check "at a regular cadence" ([Stay safe](https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/stay-safe-guide-ips3b57ceba0/web)).
  - The HIG says the Collaboration button "reminds people that the content is shared and identifies who's sharing it".
  - Check In shows a lock-screen indicator while it runs.
  - Day One shows "a banner indicating the people who will see it" when you write in a shared journal.
- **Coercive control:**
  - [Freed et al. (CHI 2018)](https://rist.tech.cornell.edu/papers/stalkers-paradise-intimate.pdf) describe the "UI-bound adversary": an authenticated but adversarial user of the victim's device or account who uses the normal UI.
  - [Chatterjee et al. (IEEE S&P 2018)](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8418618/) found most intimate-partner surveillance tools are **"dual-use"** apps with a legitimate purpose.
  - IBM's principles ([IBM Policy Lab, 2020](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ibm-policy-lab-5-technology-design-principles-combat-domestic); [PDF](https://www.ibm.com/blogs/policy/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Design-Principles-to-Combat-Domestic-Abuse.pdf)): diversity ("consider all potential users … not just the assumed user"), privacy and choice, combating gaslighting (a digital record), security and data (joint control, "only collecting and sharing necessary information"), and intuitive tech.
  - The UK regulator Ofcom's Nov 2025 guidance asks firms to do "abusability testing and using personas" ([Refuge](https://refuge.org.uk/news/what-does-ofccoms-vawg-guidance-mean-for-women-and-girls/)). Secondary summaries say it also covers time-limited location sharing (U).
  - **Applied to Buddies:** a controlling spouse or parent can take the phone and accept an invite, or turn on sharing. Mitigations: sharing is always visible, each person controls only their own side, leaving is quick and quiet, there is no presence or "last seen", addresses (location data) are shared only per item, and watcher-style alerts about you are disclosed and approval-gated.
- **Automatic verification by default, manual verification optional:** iMessage Contact Key Verification "automatically verifies" and offers optional code comparison ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118246)). For Buddies, the secret in the link can authenticate the key exchange, so users never have to compare codes.

### 2.5 Anti-patterns to avoid

1. **Contact-book upload and friend suggestions.** Apple's direction is **Limited** contacts access (iOS 18) ([WWDC24](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10121/)). The share sheet needs no contacts permission at all.
2. **Growth hacks:** invite rewards, "invite 3 to unlock", nag pushes to invitees, sending SMS on the user's behalf.
3. **Social-feed mechanics:** likes, public profiles, follower counts, "last seen" or presence, and **leaderboards, competitions or streak guilt** about ministry hours. Duolingo and Fitness competitions are the reference points (U for Duolingo).
4. **Unsafe links:** permanent multi-use links; tokens consumed on GET (preview fetchers will burn them); personal data in OG tags; links that stay valid after being revoked.
5. **Exit friction:** confirmshaming, removal buried in menus, notifying the other party, or needing their consent to leave.
6. **Reading the clipboard on launch** without a tap.
7. **Attribution and fingerprinting SDKs.**
8. **Silent reciprocity:** sharing your data back without saying so before accept.

## 4. Proposed end-to-end flow

_(Copy examples are illustrative. Put every string through i18n / `en-US.json`.)_

**A. The inviter creates an invite**

1. Entry points: **Buddies** (Profile/Settings), an empty-state card on the calendar ("Plan together"), and from a Follow-up, "Invite a buddy along". That last one only lists existing buddies; if there are none, it offers to create a buddy invite first.
2. **Invite sheet:**
   - "Buddies see each other's planned service days. Nothing else — not your contacts, visits, notes, or hours."
   - "You can have up to 5 buddies · 3 spots left."
   - "This link works once and expires in 7 days (Oct 1)."
   - Primary button **Share invite link** opens the share sheet. Secondary button **Show QR code** for in person.
3. Link `https://ww-proxy.leviwilkerson.com/b/<inviteId>#<secret>`. The server stores only the ID, the encrypted invite card (inviter name and avatar, encrypted with a key derived from the secret), the expiry and a status.
4. Prefilled message: "Let's be WitnessWork buddies so we can see each other's service plans. Tap to accept (works once, expires Oct 1): <link>"
5. The preview is generic ("WitnessWork buddy invite" plus the icon). A GET never changes state.

**B. The invitee doesn't have the app**

1. The link opens Safari to a small static, localized page: "You've been invited to be buddies on WitnessWork", **Get WitnessWork**, and "After installing, come back to this message and tap the link again."
2. **Copy invite link** copies the full URL including the fragment. In the app, a "Have an invite? Paste it" field (user-initiated paste) reads it.
3. Optionally, JavaScript on the page decrypts the name to show "Maria invited you" (the fragment stays on the device).
4. The app never reads the clipboard on launch and uses no attribution SDK.
5. First run: after minimal onboarding (a name and avatar, so the inviter recognizes you), the pending invite is shown. The App Clip version is phase 2.

**C. The invitee opens the invite with the app installed**

1. The universal link opens the **invite sheet**:
   - "Maria Lopez wants to be buddies"
   - "Maria will see: your planned service days (date, time, length)"
   - "You'll see: Maria's planned service days"
   - "Not shared: contacts, visits, notes, hours"
   - "Expires Oct 1"
2. Buttons: **Accept**, **Not now**, and a small **Decline**, which is silent.
3. Edge cases:
   - Already buddies.
   - Expired, used or canceled: "Ask Maria for a new link".
   - At the limit: "You have 5 buddies. Remove one to accept."
   - Your own link: blocked.

**D. Accept**

1. The E2E key exchange is authenticated by the link secret. The server marks the invite consumed.
2. Both lists update. Maria's plans appear faded on the invitee's month calendar with a legend.
3. Confirmation: "You and Maria are buddies. You can hide your plans or remove Maria anytime in Buddies."

**E. The inviter is told**

1. Push: "Sarah accepted your buddy invite." The content is E2E and decrypted on the device.
2. Sarah's buddy page shows "Connected Sep 23", and for the first days a **"Not who you expected? Remove"** action.

**F. Managing**

1. The **Buddies** list (5 or fewer, plus pending) shows statuses such as "Sharing plans both ways", "You're hiding your plans from Sarah", or "Invite pending · expires Oct 1" (with Resend and Cancel).
2. **Buddy page:**
   - What Sarah sees from you (Plans; later Activity and Awards)
   - What you see from Sarah
   - Notifications (types, Mute)
   - **Hide my plans from Sarah** (stays on until you turn it off; Sarah just sees "No shared plans", with no push)
   - **Remove Sarah**
3. **Privacy & Safety:** a "Who can see my plans" summary and **Stop sharing with everyone**.
4. A calendar chip "Shared with 2". An occasional review card: "You're sharing your plans with Maria and Sarah. [Review] [Looks good]".
5. **Follow-up invite:** pick a buddy, see a preview of exactly what they'll get (date/time by default; address, first name and topic as toggles; notes off), then send. The buddy answers "I'll come" or "Can't make it", and the inviter is notified. Details disappear after the visit or if the invite is canceled. Canceling an invite sends a neutral "A shared return visit on Tue was canceled".

**G. Ending the connection (either person)**

1. The sheet says: "Remove Sarah? You'll both stop seeing each other's plans and shared return visits. Sarah won't get a notification but may notice. Plans she already saw can't be unseen." If there are upcoming shared follow-ups: "1 upcoming shared visit will be canceled."
2. Effects:
   - The server deletes the relationship, stored ciphertexts and pending follow-up invites, and keys are discarded.
   - The remover's device purges the other person's data immediately.
   - The other device purges on its next sync, and the row simply disappears.
   - Reconnecting needs a new invite.
   - The same thing happens whichever person removes.
3. **Stop sharing with everyone** runs this for every buddy in one confirmed step.

## 5. Open questions to validate

- 7 vs 14-day expiry for older users who may install slowly; consider "Resend" re-sharing the same valid link.
- Should "Hide my plans" auto-resume (like Find My's next-day resume) or stay on until turned off? The recommendation is to stay on, because calendars are longer-lived than location.
- What happens on reinstall or a new device if the identity keys change: silent reconnect vs. "Maria needs to re-invite you". Also uninstalling: Find My warns that deleting the app doesn't stop sharing ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105104)). Buddies needs a stale-data rule (e.g., show "last updated").
- Whether any cultural or community dynamics (family members, ministry partners) call for extra quiet-exit protections beyond the above. Test with abusability personas (Ofcom).

## 6. Additional Apple precedents worth copying verbatim (as patterns)

- "Share a Link" vs "Send a Copy". Messages link invites have a Send-a-Copy analog for follow-ups: an **iCal/.ics or plain text "send details" copy** as a no-relationship alternative.
- Health: "simply removing them from Contacts doesn't stop the information from being shared". Buddies should never depend on the Contacts app.
- Find My "Allow Friend Requests" as a global switch. Not needed while invites stay link-only; revisit if in-app requests are ever added.
