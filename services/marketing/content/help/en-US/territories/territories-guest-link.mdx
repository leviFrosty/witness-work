Make a guest link. The person opens it in their web browser, with no app and no sign-in.

## Make a link

1. Open the territory and tap **Share**.
2. Choose **Whole Territory**, or pick the streets to share.
3. Tap **A Link or QR Code**.
4. Send the link, or let them scan the QR code.

You need an internet connection. You don't need Buddies, and they don't need an account.

One link is enough for a whole car group. Everyone scans the same QR code or opens the same link on their own phone.

## What they can do

- Type their name once, so the group sees who recorded each door. Then pick the street they are on.
- Record doors as **Not at Home**, **Spoke**, or **Do Not Call**, and mark streets worked.
- See everything on the link's streets, including do-not-calls and doors others recorded.
- Come back later. Opening the link again brings back their name, street, and records.

They never see your other streets or the card's photo. If they lose their connection, their phone keeps what they record and sends it when they're back online. With territory privacy mode on, they can only mark streets worked and note do-not-calls.

## How long a link lasts

A new link lasts 1 day. To keep it longer, change **Link Lasts** on the link screen, up to 30 days. The same link and QR code keep working. The link screen also shows who has joined and how long is left.

To end a link sooner, open it under **Shared With** and tap **End Link Now**. Doors already recorded stay. Returning the territory ends all its links.

## Good to know

- A link covers up to 30 streets. A territory can have 10 open links at a time.
- Handed-off streets aren't included in your links.
- Share a link only with people you want working the territory.
