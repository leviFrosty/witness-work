title: "Nahindurira hehe amamenyesha yanjye?"
---

Fungura **Igenamiterere → Amamenyesha**. Inyibutsa zose n'imenyesha WitnessWork ishobora kohereza biri kuri uru rupapuro rumwe.

## Ibyo wahindura

- **Emera kwibutswa na Notification:** yerekana niba telefoni yawe yemerera WitnessWork kohereza ikintu. Niba izimye, kanda kugira ngo uyifungure. Niba ifunguye, kanda kugira ngo ufungure igenamiterere rya telefoni rya majwi, ibimenyetso bigaragara n'ibyerekanwa kuri ecran yo gufunga.
- **Gahunda n'ibyo gusubirayo:** niba gahunda nshya n'ibyo gusubirayo bitangirana na **Menyesha njyewe** ifunguye, n'igihe mbere yabyo inyibutsa iziramo. Ushobora kubihindura kuri buri kimwe.
- **Inyibutsa z'umurimo:** inyibutsa zo gutegura icyumweru gitaha, kwandika igihe cyawe no gutanga raporo yawe.
- **Urukurikirane n'Imigenzo:** inyibutsa z'urukurikirane rwawe n'iz'imigenzo ya buri munsi. Zigaragara gusa igihe ibyo bikorwa bifunguye.
- **Buddies:** imenyesha ryerekeye bagenzi bawe, aho Buddies iboneka. Kuri mugenzi umwe, fungura paji ye, kanda ikaramu (**Hindura mugenzi**), hanyuma **Gusangiza n'imenyesha**.
- **Backup:** inyibutsa iza iyo hashize igihe kinini utakora backup.

## Byiza kumenya

- Kuzimya inyibutsa nta cyo bihindura ku bintu biri mu nzogera y'Ahabanza.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Inyibutsa ntiza:** reba ko **Emera kwibutswa na Notification** ifunguye kandi ko Focus cyangwa Ntugire ikikubangamira itayicecekesha.
- **Biracyabura:** vugana n'ubufasha ukoresheje Ikigo cy'ubufasha ukavuga inyibutsa irebwa. Ntushyiremo amazina cyangwa aderesi by'abantu usura.
