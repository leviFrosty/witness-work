id: shareApp
title: How do I share WitnessWork with a friend?
category: general
---

Open **Settings → Share WitnessWork** and show your friend a code or send them a link. WitnessWork is free on both iPhone and Android.

## Share with a code

1. Tap your profile picture, then **Settings → Share WitnessWork**.
2. Pick **Android** or **iPhone**.
3. Your friend scans the code with their phone's camera to open WitnessWork in Google Play or the App Store.

## Share with a link

Tap **Share Link**. The message has both store links, so it works for anyone.

## Good to know

- The message has only the store links. It says nothing about you or your records.

## If something goes wrong

- **A code won't scan:** turn up your screen brightness, or use **Share Link** instead.
