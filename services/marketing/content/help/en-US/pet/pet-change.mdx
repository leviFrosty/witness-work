id: petChange
title: Can I change or rename my pet?
category: pet
---

Yes. You can rename your pet or switch to another animal, and it keeps everything.

## Rename your pet

1. On **Home**, tap your pet's card, but not the pet itself.
2. Tap the settings button at the top right, then **Name**.
3. Type a name of up to 20 letters and tap **Save**.

Clear the name and save to use the animal's name instead.

## Change the animal

1. Tap your pet's card, then the settings button at the top right.
2. Tap **Change pet**.
3. Pick another animal or coat.

You can also touch and hold the card's name and choose **Rename…** or **Change Pet…**.

## Good to know

- Changing the animal keeps its name, hearts, hat, room, keepsakes and everything you bought in the Shop.
- Names are yours and are never translated.
