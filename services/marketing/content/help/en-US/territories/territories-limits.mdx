"This territory is too large to share" usually means the territory has reached one of these limits.

## Limits when sharing

- **10 people per territory.** This counts you, everyone it's shared with, open invitations, and people a buddy shared a handed-off street with. Guest link users don't count.
- **10 open guest links per territory,** and up to 30 new links a day.
- **A very large territory.** Rarely, a territory with a great deal of history is too large to share at all. It stays on your phone as before.

## Make room

- **For people:** open the territory and tap **Stop** beside someone who has finished, or beside an invitation nobody accepted. It's under **Shared With**.
- **For links:** tap the link under **Shared With**, then **End Link Now**.

## Other limits

- A guest link covers up to 30 streets. For more, make a second link for the rest.
- Guest links last 1 day unless you change **Link Lasts**, up to 30 days.
- Invitations last 7 days. After that, share the territory again.
- Notes for Scribe AI can be up to about 8,000 characters or 300 lines. Longer notes are read on your phone instead, so split them to have Scribe AI read them.

Territories are free, with no limit on how many you add.
