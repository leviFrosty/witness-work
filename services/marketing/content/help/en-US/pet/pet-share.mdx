title: How do I share a picture of my pet?
category: pet
---

Yes. Your pet's share card is a picture of your pet in its room that you can send with any app.

## Share the picture

1. On **Home**, tap your pet's card, but not the pet itself.
2. Tap **Share** at the top right. Or touch and hold the card's name and choose **Share…**.
3. Tap **Share** again and pick an app.

## Good to know

- The card shows your pet, its name and hearts, a few facts (days together, hats, and your Service Streak if it's above 0 and streaks are on), and the WitnessWork name.
- Nothing is sent until you choose where it goes. The picture holds nothing else about you or your records.

## If something goes wrong

- **Sharing fails:** try again. If it keeps failing, close and reopen WitnessWork.
