title: How do I add streets to a territory?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Open the territory and tap **Add Street**. Start with the first street on the card, then add the others in the order you'll work them.

## Add a street

1. Enter the **Street** name.
2. Under **House Numbers**, choose **Both sides** (1, 2, 3), **Odd side** (1, 3, 5), **Even side** (2, 4, 6), or **No house numbers**. With no house numbers, doors are counted: Door 1, Door 2, Door 3.
3. Enter the **First Number**, where you start.
4. Turn on **Numbers Go Down** if the numbers get smaller as you walk.
5. Leave **Save Where Each Door Is** on if you want doors to show on the map. It's on by default.

To change a street later, open it and tap **Edit Street**. Or touch and hold it and tap **Edit Street**. **Delete Street** is there too.

## Find streets from an outline

This is in the phone apps only. If you outlined the territory's area:

1. Tap **Find Streets in the Outline**. It's the first button under **Streets** on a territory with no streets yet.
2. Untick any street that isn't on the card. These are best guesses from the map.
3. Tap **Add Streets**.

If none are found, add the streets by hand. On Android, it asks to use your location to look up street names.
