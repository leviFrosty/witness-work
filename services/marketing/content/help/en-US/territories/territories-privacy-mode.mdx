---
id: territoriesPrivacyMode
title: What is territory privacy mode, and what does data protection mode change?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Territory privacy mode keeps no house-by-house records. With it on, WitnessWork keeps only which streets you've worked and the do-not-calls, with their address or pin and the date. There are no not-at-homes, Spoke results, or door pins, and Add Results From Notes is hidden.

Turn it on or off in Settings → Privacy → Territory privacy mode. Turning it on deletes the house-by-house records already on this device, after you confirm, and on your other synced devices as they sync; do-not-calls are kept. Turning it off doesn't bring them back.

**Data protection mode:** territory privacy mode follows data protection mode until you change it yourself. So turning on data protection mode (Settings → Privacy) also turns on territory privacy mode. To keep house-by-house records with data protection mode on, turn territory privacy mode off.

**With shared territories:**

- **If you own the territory,** your setting applies to everyone on it, guests included: nobody records doors, and people can only mark streets worked and add do-not-calls. Sharing it carries only those.
- **If someone shared it with you,** your setting applies only to your device: it doesn't keep or show other people's doors, and you can still mark streets worked and add do-not-calls.

When it's on, a territory's page shows "Territory privacy mode is on · no house-by-house records", and Privacy Settings opens the switch.
