title: How do I remove an old device from sync?
category: backups
---

Open **Settings → Sync → Devices** on a device that still syncs and tap **Remove** beside the old one. Do this for a phone you sold, replaced or reset.

## Remove a device

1. Open **Settings → Sync → Devices**.
2. Find the old device. Devices that haven't synced in 90 days or more are marked.
3. Tap **Remove** beside it.

## Good to know

- Removing deletes only that device's copy of your records from iCloud, Google Drive or Cloud Sync. This device and your other devices keep everything.
- If the removed device is still in use, it adds its records back the next time it syncs. Turn sync off on it first to stop that.
- Photos aren't tied to a device, so removing a device doesn't remove them. For iCloud photos, use **Remove iCloud photos**. Cloud Sync photos are deleted when you remove them or when none of your devices uses Cloud Sync.

## If something goes wrong

- **It asks you to sync first:** go back, tap **Sync now**, and fix any problem the status shows.
- **The old device uses a newer WitnessWork:** update this device first.
- **Still stuck:** use the private support option in the Help Center. Keep householder details out of public reports.
