Turn off Haptics in settings. WitnessWork then stops its vibration feedback, such as taps, scrubbing a chart, and goal celebrations.

## Turn off vibrations

1. Open **Settings → Features → Audio & Haptics**.
2. Turn off **Haptics**.

## Good to know

- This applies only to this device. It isn't synced to your other devices.
- Some system controls, like menus and switches, may still vibrate. Change those in your phone's sound and haptics settings.
