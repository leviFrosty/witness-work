title: How do I return a territory, and what happens to its records?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

When you hand the card back, open the territory, tap **Return Territory**, and confirm. The territory moves to your returned territories.

## What happens to its records

Nothing is deleted. The streets, which streets were worked, the house-by-house records (not-at-homes, **Spoke** results, and door pins), and the do-not-calls all stay. You can look at them, but you can't change them.

If some streets aren't marked worked, the confirmation says how many, so you can mark them first. When every street is worked, the page asks "Is that the whole card?" and offers **Return Territory** or **Add Another Street**.

## Pass the do-not-calls on

1. Open the returned territory.
2. Tap **Copy List** under **Do Not Call**.
3. Paste the list wherever your congregation keeps them.

## Find it again

In the **Territories** tab, tap **Show returned territories** and open one.

- **Move Back to Checked Out** reopens a territory you returned by mistake, with all its records.
- **Check Out Again** is for when you're given the territory again. It starts fresh from today: its house-by-house records and worked dates are cleared. Streets and do-not-calls are kept.

## Good to know

- If it was shared, returning stops sharing for everyone and ends its guest links. People you shared it with keep a read-only copy, with which streets were worked and the do-not-calls but not the house-by-house records, until they remove it.
- A street you handed off stays with your buddy, so they can finish it. It moves to their own territories, with its records.
- To remove a territory completely, for example to free up space, open it, tap **Edit**, then **Delete Territory**. This deletes its streets, records, and do-not-calls from your phone.
