title: WitnessWork ishobora kuntegurira ukwezi kwanjye?
---

Yego. Mu gihe cyo gutegura, niba wandika amasaha kandi ufite intego y'ukwezi, ubona incamake yihuse y'Umufasha: hitamo iminsi ujyaho kubwiriza kenshi, ugabanye intego yawe kuri iyo minsi y'uku kwezi, cyangwa ukwezi gukurikira niba hasigaye iminsi itarenze irindwi. Kanda umunsi uwo ari wo wose kugira ngo uhindure gahunda yawo ukoresheje slider, cyangwa uteganye umunsi wowe ubwawe. Kanda Ongeraho kuri ingengabihe yanjye kugira ngo uzibike nka gahunda ku Ingengabihe, aho ushobora guhindura cyangwa gusiba iyo ari yo yose, cyangwa ukande Nzateganya nyuma kugira ngo ukomeze nta gahunda. Iminsi y'icyumweru utahisemo iba iminsi y'ikiruhuko, bityo Umufasha ntazagusaba gahunda kuri yo; hindura ibi igihe icyo ari cyo cyose muri Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Gahunda → Kuboneka. Nyuma yo gutegura, uteganya ku Ingengabihe ukanda umunsi uwo ari wo wose, kandi Umufasha uriho agusaba gahunda igihe cyose ubuze ku ntego yawe. Gahunda zibikwa hamwe n'izindi nyandiko zawe kandi ntizibarwa ku raporo yawe; andika igihe ukoresha koko. Ababwiriza b'Ubwami batandika amasaha, n'umuntu wese udafite intego y'ukwezi, basimbuka iki gice ariko bashobora kongeraho gahunda bakoresheje Ingengabihe.
