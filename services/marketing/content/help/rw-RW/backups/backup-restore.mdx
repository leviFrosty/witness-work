title: "Nkora nte backup kandi nkagarura amakuru yanjye?"
---

Bika dosiye ya backup muri **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura**, uyigarure aho hamwe. Dosiye ikora kuri iPhone, Android na murandasi, bityo ushobora kuyikoresha no kwimura inyandiko zawe uva ku kimwe ujya ku kindi.

Ku backup yikora, reba [WitnessWork Backup ni iki?](/help/backups/account-backup).

## Kora dosiye ya backup

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura**.
2. Kanda **Kora Backup**.
3. Hitamo uko ufunga dosiye. **Konti yanjye** ifungurwa ku bikoresho byawe byinjiye muri konti yawe, cyangwa ahandi hose ukoresheje urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura. **Ijambo ry'ibanga** (nibura inyuguti 8) rifungurwa ku gikoresho icyo ari cyo cyose gifite iryo jambo, ukarikoresha rero ngo usangize kopi.
4. Bika dosiye, yitwa witness-work-backup.json.

Kuri murandasi, dosiye ijya mu bubiko bw'ibyo wakuye muri murandasi yawe.

## Garura ukoresheje dosiye ya backup

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura**.
2. Kanda **Garura ukoresheje backup**.
3. Hitamo dosiye yawe ya backup. Andika ijambo ryayo ry'ibanga niba ririmo.

Kuri telefoni nshya, ushobora ahubwo gukanda **Nari nsanzwe nkoresha WitnessWork** igihe gutangira kugeze, hanyuma ugahitamo **Uvuye muri dosiye ya backup** munsi ya **Ubundi buryo bwo kugarura** ku ekara ya **Urakaza neza**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Kugarura bisimbuza inyandiko zo kuri iki gikoresho ziri muri backup. Banza ubike backup iriho ubu.
- Dosiye ikubiyemo inyandiko z'umurimo, gahunda, abantu, gusura, ibyiciro, ibirometero, itungo ryawe, umwirondoro n'igenamiterere risangiwe. Ntirimo amafoto, imodoka zisangiwe, n'igenamiterere bwite ry'iki gikoresho.
- Nta wushobora gufungura dosiye ifunze atazi ijambo ryayo ry'ibanga cyangwa adafite konti yawe, kandi WitnessWork ntishobora kugarura ijambo ry'ibanga wibagiwe.
- Backup yakozwe na WitnessWork nshya cyane ntishobora kugarurwa kugeza uvuguruye.
- Bika dosiye n'ijambo ryayo ry'ibanga mu ibanga. Ishobora kubamo amakuru y'abo usura.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ivuga ko banza kwinjira:** injira muri konti yawe kuri iyi telefoni ukoresheje **Injira ukoresheje QR code** cyangwa urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura, hanyuma wongere ugerageze.
- **Ivuga ko dosiye ifunze kuri indi konti:** uyigarure ku gikoresho cyinjiye muri iyo konti, cyangwa ukore aho backup ifunze n'ijambo ry'ibanga.
- **Ibintu bimwe ntibyashoboye gusomwa:** ibisigaye biragarurwa, kandi WitnessWork ikubwira umubare w'ibyasizwe inyuma.
- **Biracyakomeye:** koresha uburyo bw'ubufasha bw'ibanga mu Kigo cy'Ubufasha.
