Yes. With Cloud Sync, your profile photo, contact photos and photos in notes sync between your phones. You don't need to turn anything on. Your account has 2 GB of space for them.

## Good to know

- Photos are private. Only your own devices can see them.
- A phone downloads the photos its records use after it syncs.
- The web syncs photos in notes, both ways. Profile and contact photos don't show there yet: you see initials, and editing a contact on the web leaves its photo as it is.
- Update WitnessWork on every device. An older version shows initials or a placeholder instead of a synced photo.
- If none of your devices uses Cloud Sync any more, its copy of your photos is deleted from WitnessWork's server about a day later. They stay on your devices.
- Photo space applies to Cloud Sync only. iCloud and Google Drive photos use your own iCloud or Google storage.

## Check how much space you've used

Open **Settings → Account** and look under **Photo Storage**, on a device that uses Cloud Sync.

## If a photo doesn't appear on another device

1. On the device that added the photo, open WitnessWork.
2. Open **Settings → Sync** and tap **Sync now**.

## If storage is full

**Settings → Account** says **Storage full**, and new photos show **Not synced: storage full** and stay on the phone that added them. Photos already synced aren't affected.

1. Remove or replace photos you don't need: a contact's photo, your profile photo, or photos in notes.
2. Open WitnessWork again. Photos that didn't sync upload on their own.

A deleted contact's photo keeps its space while the contact can still be restored.

## If a photo is too large

One photo can be up to 25 MB. A larger one shows **Not synced: photo too large**. Replace it with a smaller photo.
