---
id: webSignOut
title: Nshi nshila ya kufuma mu WitnessWork pa web?
---

Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti** elyo mufume. WitnessWork itendeka ukwesha pa masekondi yantu ukutuma ifyo mwacinja ifyalolela ku Cloud Sync. Pa numa ifumyapo akaunti yenu ne data yonse ya WitnessWork mu browser iyi.

## Fumeni

1. Moneni ukuti **Ifyo Mwasala → Sync** ilanga ifintu fyonse fyasync.
2. Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Akaunti**.
3. Saleni **Fumya**.

Ifyo mwacinja ifyashasync fyabula.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Ukufuma tekufumyapo akaunti yenu nelyo ifyo mwalemba ukufuma kuli WitnessWork.
- Kopi ya Cloud Sync ya browser iyi yashala inshiku 30, nelyo mpaka browser imbi mwaingila yapokelela ifyo mwalemba.
- Ukubomfya browser iyi nakabili, ingileni na passkey yenu, foni yenu, nelyo icisokoshi cenu ca kubwesha. Moneni [Nshi nshila ya kwingila pa kompyuta na foni yandi?](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer). Ifyo mwalemba fyabwela.
- Mulebomfya WitnessWork fye mu ama browsers, ukwabula foni pa Cloud Sync? Ingileni nakabili pa nshiku 30 ukusunga ifyo mwalemba, nelyo mutulule backup pa ntanshi ya kufuma.
- Fumeni lyonse ilyo mulebomfya kompyuta iishili yenu nelyo iyo abantu abengi babomfya.
- Kuti mwafumya na browser ukufuma pa cibombelo cimbi. Moneni [Browser ishala yaingila inshita ndeshi, elyo shinga shinshi kuti nabomfya?](/help/account/web-sessions).
