title: "Mpuza nte amakuru yanjye hagati y'ibikoresho bya Android?"
---

Google Drive Sync ituma inyandiko zawe zingana ku matelefoni na tablet byawe bya Android. Ni ubuntu kandi iracyari ku igerageza.

## Bifungure

Ibi ubikore kuri buri gikoresho cya Android:

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Guhuza**.
2. Hitamo **Google Drive**.
3. Fungura **Huza na Google Drive**.
4. Mu idirishya rya Google, hitamo Google Account imwe kuri buri gikoresho, wemerere WitnessWork kubika amakuru yayo muri Google Drive yawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ihuza inyandiko zawe z'umurimo, gahunda, abantu, gusura, ibyiciro, ibirometero, umwirondoro n'igenamiterere risangiwe. Amafoto afite akabuto kayo ka **Huza amafoto y'aderesi**. Imodoka zisangiwe zihuzwa zonyine.
- Igenamiterere nko kurinda amakuru na analytics bigumya kuri buri gikoresho.
- Mu gihe WitnessWork ifunguye, impinduka ziva ku bindi bikoresho byawe zigera mu munota nk'umwe. Ubundi zigera ubutaha uyifunguye. Kanda **Huza ubu** kugira ngo urebe ako kanya.
- Niba igikoresho gihuza indi Google Account, guhuza kuzima kuri cyo kandi inyandiko zawe zigumya ku gikoresho.
- Ihuza gusa n'ibindi bikoresho byawe bya Android. Kugira ngo uhuze iPhone na telefoni ya Android, koresha [Cloud Sync](/help/backups/cloud-sync).

## Shyiraho telefoni nshya ya Android

Igihe gutangira kigeze, kanda **Nari nsanzwe nkoresha WitnessWork**, hanyuma uhitemo **Uvuye kuri Google Drive** ku ekara ya **Urakaza neza**. Inyandiko zawe zirazana, kandi n'uko uri Umufasha uzana iyo Google Account imaze guhuzwa.

Ku bibazo, reba [Google Drive Sync ntikora](/help/backups/google-drive-sync-troubleshooting).
