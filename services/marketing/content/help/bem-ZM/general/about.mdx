Isuleni **Ifyo Mwasala → Pa Mulandu**. Ilekanya ku Privacy Policy, Terms of Use ne code ya WitnessWork.

## Open Source Licenses

1. Tinikeni icikope cenu, elyo **Ifyo Mwasala**.
2. Shilukeni ukufika ku **Pa Mulandu** elyo mutinike **Open Source Licenses**.
3. Fwayeni ku shina lya package, license, nelyo uwalembele, elyo mutinike package ukumona license yonse.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Apo Scribe AI iba, **Pa Mulandu** ilekanya nankwe ku mulandu uulanda pa fyo mutuma kuli Scribe AI.
- Tinikisheni link iili yonse ukuyikopolola nelyo ukuyisabankanya.
