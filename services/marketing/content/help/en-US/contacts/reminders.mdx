id: reminders
title: How do follow-up, plan, and contact reminders work?
category: contacts
---

Turn on **Notify Me** when you add or edit a follow-up or a plan, then choose how long before it starts the reminder should arrive.

## Set a reminder

1. Add or edit a follow-up or plan.
2. Turn on **Notify Me**.
3. Choose how long before it starts.

If notifications aren't allowed, turning it on asks for permission. If you said no before, it opens your phone's settings.

## Good to know

- A repeating plan reminds you the same amount of time before each date. Dates you deleted, and dates after its end date, don't remind you.
- A phone schedules a repeating plan's reminders two weeks ahead, so open WitnessWork at least every two weeks.
- If the reminder time has already passed, the form says so and no reminder is sent.
- Dismissing a contact also reminds you when they're available again.
- Each reminder also appears in the bell on Home, and tapping it opens the follow-up, plan, or contact.
- After a sync or restore, open WitnessWork once on that phone to set up its reminders.
- On the web, no notifications are sent. Plans and follow-ups you set there reach your phones, which remind you once you've opened WitnessWork on each.
- Deleting a visit, plan, or contact removes its reminders.
- With data protection mode on, reminders leave out names and topics.
- On Android, a reminder may arrive a few minutes late to save battery.

## If something goes wrong

- **Reminders don't arrive:** check that notifications are allowed for WitnessWork in **Settings → Notifications** and in your phone's settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them.
- **On Android:** check that the **Reminders** category is on in WitnessWork's notification settings.
- **Still nothing:** report it from the Help Center, without names or other private details.
