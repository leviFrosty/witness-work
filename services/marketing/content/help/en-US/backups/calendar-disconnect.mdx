title: What happens when I turn off Calendar Sync or uninstall WitnessWork?
category: backups
platforms:
  - ios
  - android
---

Open **Settings → Features → Calendar Sync** and tap **Turn Off Calendar Sync**. WitnessWork asks whether to keep the events already in your calendar or remove them.

## Choose what happens to events

- **Keep Events:** the events stay in your calendar, but they're no longer updated.
- **Remove Events:** WitnessWork removes its own events from the calendar. Your other events and the calendar itself stay.

You can also tap **Turn Off** on a "Calendar updates paused" message.

## Good to know

- On iPhone and iPad, turning it off on the device that updates your calendar stops updates from all your devices. On your other devices, it turns off for that device only, and the events stay.
- Uninstalling WitnessWork doesn't remove calendar events. Remove them first if you don't want them.
- You can delete a WitnessWork calendar you no longer use in your calendar app, after checking it holds nothing you want.
- Turning it off never deletes your Contacts, Visits or Follow-ups in WitnessWork.
