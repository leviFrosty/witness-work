title: How do I see which contacts are due for a visit?
category: contacts
---

Tap a chip under the search field in Contacts to show only the people in that group: **Stale**, **This month**, **This week**, or **Never contacted**. Each chip shows how many contacts it holds.

## Use the chips

1. Open **Contacts**.
2. Under the search field, tap a chip. Swipe the row sideways to see them all.
3. To show everyone again, tap the chip once more, or tap **All**.

## Good to know

- The groups follow your most recent visit. A **Not at Home** counts as a visit.
- A chip's color matches the contact's pin on the map. The same chips appear at the top of the map and filter the pins there.
- To change when a contact moves between groups, or the colors, tap the info button at the end of the chips, then the pencil.
- To change the order of the chips, touch one, pause briefly, then drag it along the row. **All** stays first.
- A chip adds a filter you can see and remove in **Sort & Filter**.
- Dismissed contacts aren't counted. Nothing is shared.
