title: How does WitnessWork save where each door is, and how do I use the territory map?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

When Save Where Each Door Is is on for a street, which it is by default, WitnessWork saves where you're standing each time you record a door, so the door shows as a pin on the territory map. Turn it on or off with the switch in the door recorder, or in Edit Street.

Doors are still recorded without a pin when WitnessWork can't use your location or you're away from the street, so pins don't all land in one spot. If you really are at the doors, tap the pin icon in the recorder and tap I'm at the Doors. To place or fix a pin by hand, tap the house in the street's list, tap Add Pin or Move Pin, and tap the map where the door is.

**The territory map:** open the territory and tap Map (or Map & Card). It shows the territory's pin and outline, each door with a saved location, and the do-not-calls. Tap a pin to see the house, its result and date, and who recorded it; tap Open and the street's name to go to that street. Show My Location shows where you are, and from a street's menu, Show on Map focuses on that street; tap Show the Whole Territory to zoom back out. If you added a photo of the card, switch between Map and Card.

Directions, on the territory's page, opens your navigation app to the territory's pin, or to the middle of its outline.

Territory privacy mode keeps no door pins, though a do-not-call keeps its pin. If pins aren't being saved, see [Why isn't WitnessWork saving where the doors are?](/help/territories/territories-location-off)
