title: How do I hide a buddy, stop sharing, or delete my Buddies data?
category: buddies
---

Open a buddy's page, tap Sharing & Alerts (from version 1.47.0, tap Edit Buddy, the pencil at the top, first), and turn off Show on my calendar to stop marking their days on your calendar on all your devices with Buddies; sharing continues, their plans still list when you tap a day, and your own Plans with them still show. Remove Buddy (on their page, or from version 1.47.0 under Edit Buddy) ends the connection for both people, and reconnecting takes a new invite. They are not notified, though they may notice. From version 1.47.0, removing a buddy doesn't take them off a shared car, but it cancels your open invitations to them. If removal is pending while offline, they may still see your last shared plans until WitnessWork reconnects and completes it. Buddies Settings → Delete My Buddies Data removes all connections and invites and erases your encrypted Buddies data and encryption identity. Because it gives you a new account and recovery key, it also deletes your WitnessWork backup and your Cloud Sync copy on our server. It's only in the phone app; a browser can't do it. It cannot be undone; reconnect and retry if deletion is pending. From version 1.47.0, it first leaves every shared car, and you keep a copy of each in your cars; if a car can't be left yet, for example while you're offline, nothing is deleted, so try again when you're connected. Your personal service records and contacts remain.
