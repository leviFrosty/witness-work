title: How is the estimated fuel cost calculated, and what happens when fuel prices change?
category: mileage
---

WitnessWork estimates fuel cost as distance, divided by the car's fuel economy, times the fuel's price. It uses the values in effect on each trip's date.

## When a price or fuel economy changes

When you change a fuel's price or a car's fuel economy, choose how it applies:

- **From a Date:** applies from that day forward. Earlier trips keep the old value.
- **Fix Current Value:** corrects a mistake, and also updates past trips that used it.

To remove a past entry, touch and hold it in the history.

## Good to know

- The estimate appears only once the car has a fuel economy and its fuel has a price. Trips without both are left out, and the report says so.
- Every car that uses a fuel shares its price. A shared car has its own fuels, so changing your own fuel's price doesn't change the shared car's.
- It's only an estimate. If you're reimbursed, the amount is worked out by whoever reimburses you.
