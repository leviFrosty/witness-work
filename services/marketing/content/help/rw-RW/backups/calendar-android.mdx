title: "Guhuza kalendari bikora bite kuri Android?"
---

Guhuza kalendari byongera Gusubirayo kwawe kuri kalendari yo kuri telefoni yawe ya Android. Ni ubuntu.

## Bifungure

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Guhuza kalendari**. Mu gihe cyo gutangira, cyangwa ukoresheje ubutumire buri mu menyesha ryawe, kanda **Ongeraho kuri kalendari** ahubwo.
2. Kanda **Ongeraho kuri kalendari** cyangwa **Hitamo kalendari**, hanyuma wemere kugera kuri kalendari.
3. Kora kalendari ya WitnessWork kuri iyi telefoni, cyangwa uhitemo kalendari ukoresha kuri WitnessWork gusa, nk'iyakozwe muri Google Calendar.

Kalendari yawe nkuru ntishobora guhitamo. Kalendari yakorewe kuri telefoni igumya kuri iyi telefoni. Kalendari ya Google igaragara no ku bindi bikoresho byawe no kuri murandasi.

## Byiza kumenya

- Buri Gusubirayo kuzaza kongerwaho nk'ibyabaye by'iminota 30, bifite imenyesha rimwe n'irya **Menyesha njyewe**. Gusubirayo kwahise na gahunda ntibyongerwaho.
- Kuvugurura bibaho iyo ufunguye WitnessWork no nyuma yo kubika impinduka. Kanda **Huza ubu** kugira ngo uvugurure ako kanya.
- Hindurira muri WitnessWork. Niba usibye ikintu muri porogaramu ya kalendari, kigaruka ku ivugurura rikurikira. Reka umurongo uri mu bisobanuro bya buri kintu nk'uko uri.
- Amazina na aderesi ntibijya mu byabaye keretse ufunguye **Shyiramo amazina na aderesi**.
- **Zimya guhuza kalendari** ikwemerera kubika cyangwa gukuraho ibyabaye. Ntisiba kalendari cyangwa ibindi byabaye byawe.
- Gukuraho WitnessWork bisiga ibyabaye byayo muri kalendari yawe. Ubikureho mbere niba utabishaka.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ubutumwa "Kuvugurura kalendari byahagaze" bugaragara:** kibukande urebe igisubizo.
- **Kugera kuri kalendari byazimijwe:** kanda **Fungura Igenamiterere**, hanyuma **Uburenganzira → Kalendari → Emera**. Subira kuri WitnessWork.
- **Ikindi kibazo:** kanda **Ongera ugerageze**.
- **Biracyakomeye:** koresha uburyo bw'ubufasha bw'ibanga munsi ya Ukeneye ubufasha bundi? mu Kigo cy'Ubufasha. Ntukohereze amakuru y'abantu cyangwa amafoto y'ekara ya kalendari mu bitekerezo by'abantu bose.
