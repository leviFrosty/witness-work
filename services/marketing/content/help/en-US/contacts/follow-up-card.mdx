title: How do I use the Today's Conversations card on Home?
category: contacts
---

The card shows the follow-ups you scheduled for today. From 5 PM it shows Upcoming Conversations, which adds tomorrow's; those can be rescheduled but not logged until their day. Swipe sideways to move between people. When you've been, tap Talked to log the conversation in the visit form, or tap Not at Home to log the attempt right away. Undo appears below the card for a few seconds after Not at Home. The calendar button reschedules the follow-up. Tap a person to see that conversation and its follow-up in a sheet. Use the ••• button or touch and hold a person for call, message, directions, Open Contact, and Dismiss Follow-Up. A follow-up counts as done once you log any visit with that person on or after its day. The bar at the top fills as you go, and the card shows All caught up when every one is done. Tap Done, or touch and hold the title and choose Close for Now, to hide the card until you add or reschedule a follow-up. A follow-up still open 4 hours after its time moves to missed follow-ups in the notifications tray. Not at Home is unavailable while data protection mode is on. Visits are saved like any other visit on this device. To remove the card for good, touch and hold its title, choose Edit Home Screen, and tap the minus on the card. Add Sections brings it back while you edit Home. If something looks wrong, contact support from the Help Center and leave out names, addresses, and notes.
