Set them in **Settings → Features → Spiritual Routine**. This works the same on iPhone and Android.

## Set your meeting days

1. Open **Settings → Features → Spiritual Routine**.
2. Turn on **Midweek meeting prep**. Under it, choose your **Midweek meeting** day (Monday to Friday).
3. Turn on **Weekend meeting prep**. Under it, choose your **Weekend meeting** day (Saturday or Sunday).

If you don't prepare for a meeting in Spiritual Routine, choose its day under **Meeting days** at the bottom of the screen.

## Choose when meeting prep starts

1. Open **Settings → Features → Spiritual Routine**.
2. Under **Midweek meeting prep** or **Weekend meeting prep**, tap **Prep starts**. Each meeting has its own.
3. Choose **Meeting day**, or how many days before the meeting you want to start. It's 2 days before unless you change it.

## Good to know

- **Midweek meeting prep** and **Weekend meeting prep** show on Home's Routine card from the day you chose in that meeting's **Prep starts** through the meeting day. They don't show before the Monday of that week. Without meeting days, both show all week until you check them.
- If you turned on the daily reminder, it reminds you about meeting prep on the same days.
- The Plans assistant uses the same days and keeps its suggestions lighter on them. You only set them once.
- If your meeting schedule changes, for example during a circuit overseer's visit, you can check off your prep any day that week from the Spiritual Routine screen.
- Your meeting days are on all your devices if you use sync.
