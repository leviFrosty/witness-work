id: timezoneShift
title: Will my entries, plans, or reminders move if I travel to another time zone?
category: time
related:
  - 309
  - 261
---

No. Your entries and plans stay on the day you gave them, wherever you travel, including across daylight saving changes.

## Good to know

- A plan's time stays on the clock. A 10:00 AM plan still reads 10:00 AM on the day clocks change.
- When you open WitnessWork in a new time zone, **Home**, **Schedule** and **Progress** switch to today in that zone.
- Reminders set for a time of day move to that same time where you are. This covers a plan's reminder and the reminders to log time, plan next week or submit your report.
- Follow-ups keep their exact moment, so they show at the matching local time.

## If something goes wrong

- **An entry is on the wrong day:** please send a bug report from the Help Center and we'll take a look.
