id: syncPhotos
title: Why are contact photos missing on my other device?
category: backups
---

Which fix applies depends on the sync option you use. Open **Settings → Sync** to see it.

## If you use Cloud Sync

Photos sync on their own. See [Do photos sync with Cloud Sync?](/help/backups/cloud-sync-photos).

1. Update WitnessWork on every device.
2. On the device that added the photo, open **Settings → Sync** and tap **Sync now**.
3. If the photo shows **Not synced: storage full**, your 2 GB of photo space is full. Remove photos you don't need and it uploads by itself.
4. If it shows **Not synced: photo too large**, replace it with a smaller photo.

The web syncs photos in notes, but not profile or contact photos yet. Contacts show initials there.

## If you use iCloud (Google Drive on Android)

Photos have their own switch. On each device, open **Settings → Sync** and turn on **Include images in sync**. Photos count against your iCloud or Google storage. Update WitnessWork on every device.

To remove uploaded photos, pause photo sync on your other devices first, then choose **Remove iCloud photos**. Otherwise they can upload their copies again.

## Still missing?

Keep the device with the original photo, check storage and permissions, then tap **Sync now**. If it still fails, use the private support option in the Help Center. Keep householder details out of public reports.
