---
id: scribeTurnOff
title: Can I turn off Scribe AI?
category: scribe
minAppVersion: "1.46.0"
---

Yes. Go to Settings → Scribe AI and turn off Use Scribe AI. Scribe AI then disappears from the app: the microphone in Quick Actions, Speak an update in the + menu (the chat button and Type an update on the web), and its other buttons are hidden, and nothing is sent to it. Turning it off doesn't delete your conversations or Scribe AI's memory; to clear the memory, use Settings → Scribe AI → Memory before you turn it off. The switch follows you to your other devices when sync is on. Turn it back on in the same place anytime.

The first time you use Scribe AI on a device, it says what Scribe AI does and how it handles your data, and asks you to agree to WitnessWork's [Terms of Use](/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy). If you're not ready, swipe the sheet away: Scribe AI stays available and asks again next time. To read how it handles your data later, go to Settings → Scribe AI → Zero Data Retention and tap Learn more.
