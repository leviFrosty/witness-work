title: "Kuki isaha yanjye ya Wear OS ivuga Guhuza na telefoni?"
---

Isaha yawe yerekana **Guhuza na telefoni** mu gihe igihe wongeyeho ku isaha kikiri mu nzira ijya kuri telefoni yawe. Birangira iyo telefoni yawe iri hafi kandi wafunguye WitnessWork kuri yo.

## Icyo gukora

1. Reba ko isaha yawe ihujwe na telefoni yawe muri porogaramu ya mugenzi w'isaha yawe.
2. Gumana telefoni yawe hafi.
3. Fungura WitnessWork kuri telefoni yawe.
4. Fungura WitnessWork ku isaha yawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Igiteranyo cy'isaha gihita kirimo igihe gishya. Igihe cya Credit cyongerwaho telefoni yawe imaze kukibika, kuko telefoni yawe ari yo ishyira ku mupaka wawe wa buri kwezi wa Credit.
- Buri gihe cyanditswe kigumana umunsi wacyongeyeho, kandi kibikwa rimwe gusa.
- Gutangiza, guhagarika by'agateganyo no guhagarika igihe kibarwa bikeneye telefoni yawe hafi. WitnessWork ntikeneye kuba ifunguye kuri yo.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Isaha ivuga ko igihe kibarwa cyahindutse kuri telefoni yawe:** reba igihe, hanyuma wongere ugerageze.
- **Isaha ivuga ko telefoni yawe itashoboye kubika igihe:** igihe ntikiongeweho. Ongera ugerageze.
- **Isaha igusaba kuvugurura WitnessWork kuri telefoni yawe:** yivugurure, hanyuma wongere ugerageze.
- **Isaha igumya idakurikije igihe, cyangwa complications zayo zerekana akarongo:** kurikiza intambwe enye zo hejuru.
- **Biracyakomeye:** koresha umurongo w'ubufasha bw'ibanga w'Ikigo cy'Ubufasha, kandi ntukohereze inyandiko zawe mu bitekerezo by'abantu bose.
