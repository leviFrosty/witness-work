---
id: webUpdates
title: How do I get the latest version of WitnessWork on the web?
category: general
platforms: [web]
---

There's nothing to download. When a new version is ready, WitnessWork on the web shows **Update available** with a **Reload** button.

## Get the new version

1. Choose **Reload**.
2. If you installed the web app, close it and open it again.

## Good to know

- WitnessWork never reloads by itself while you're working. If you wait, the message comes back when you return to the tab.
- A tab left open a long time can reload once by itself.
- Reloading doesn't erase your records. Anything that hadn't synced yet syncs afterward.

## If something goes wrong

- **It says Update Required:** reload the page. If that doesn't help, close the tab, open app.witnesswork.app again, and check your connection.
