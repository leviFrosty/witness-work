id: buddiesPause
title: Bushe kuti naleka ukusabankanya nelyo ukufisa inshita naya?
---

Ee. Kuti mwaleka ukusabankanya kuli bonse, nelyo ukucinja ifyo munyina umo umo amona.

## Lekeni ukusabankanya

1. Isuleni **Buddies**, tinikeni akatini ka menu, elyo musale **Ifyo Mwasala fya Buddies**.
2. Yakeni **Leka ukusabankanya**.
3. Saleni umulungu, umweshi, nelyo mpaka mwaiyaka nakabili.

**Buddies** ilanga **Sharing is paused** na katini ka **Tendeka nakabili**. Ukuleka ukwa nshita kupwa pa lwakwe.

Ilyo kwaleka, banyina tabamona Plans, streak, amabaji nelyo pet yenu. Bacamona ishina ne cikope cenu. Ukwita mwatuma kwacili kuya.

## Fisheni inshita mutendeka

Munshi ya **Ubupange Bwenu**, tinikeni **Inshita sha kutendeka** elyo musale **Inshita ishininkishiwa**, **Inshita ya bushiku** (**Ilyo bwacibela**, **Pa Kasuba** nelyo **Pa Mulalilo**), nelyo **Mwipeela**. Banyina bacamona inshiku mulepanga ne ndeshi.

## Lekeni ukusabankanya Plans na munyina umo

1. Isuleni icikope cakwe elyo mutinike pencil (**Edit Buddy**).
2. Tinikeni **Ukusabankanya & Ifyebo**.
3. Zimyeni **Sabankanya ubupange bwandi**.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Banyina benu tabalebebwa pa fyo mwasala ifi, nangu kuti bamona ukuti Plans shenu shabula.
- Ifyo mwasala fyabomba pa fibombelo fyenu fyonse ifikwata Buddies.
- Icikope cenu, umusango wa pioneer, streak, amabaji ne pet fyonse fikwata switch yaibo munshi ya **Ifyo banyina bamumona**.
