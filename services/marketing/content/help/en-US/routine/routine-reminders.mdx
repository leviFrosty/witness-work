---
id: routineReminders
title: Can WitnessWork remind me about my Spiritual Routine?
category: routine
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

From version 1.47.0, Spiritual Routine can send one reminder a day on iOS and Android. It's off unless you turn it on.

To turn it on, open Settings → Spiritual Routine, turn on Daily reminder, and choose a time (7:00 PM unless you change it). WitnessWork asks for permission to send notifications if it doesn't have it yet. If notifications are off for WitnessWork in your device's settings, the reminder can't arrive; turn them back on there.

The reminder lists only what's still open for that day, like Bible reading and Midweek meeting prep. On a day when everything is already checked, or nothing is due, no reminder comes. Tapping the reminder opens WitnessWork so you can check things off.

To stop the reminders, turn off Daily reminder in Settings → Spiritual Routine. Turning Spiritual Routine off stops them too.
