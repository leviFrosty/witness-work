category: streaks

Your streak counts how steadily you keep the plans you make. It shows on Home and on your Profile.

## How it grows

- **If you log hours:** each planned day you log any time on adds one, even if you log less than you planned.
- **Days without a plan** don't add to your streak or end it, so plan the days you mean to go out.
- **For Publishers:** each month you share in the ministry adds one, whether you mark the month on Home or log time.

## Good to know

- When logging a day reaches a milestone like 3, 5 or 10, a short celebration plays. Other days, and time logged on your watch or another device, make the flame on Home flare instead.
- Tap the flame on Home to see when your streak started, your best streak, and the streaks your buddies share with you.
- When it's about to end, tap it to log the day that's due. See [When does my streak end, and how do I keep it?](/help/streaks/streak-ends)
- Each milestone your streak reaches earns your pet 100 coins to spend in the Shop (see [the pet shop answer](/help/pet/pet-shop)), while **Show streak** is on. Milestones reached before you adopted your pet don't count.
- To hide your streak, see [Can I turn off streaks?](/help/streaks/streaks-hide)
