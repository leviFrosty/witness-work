Spiritual Routine helps you keep up personal spiritual activities beside your ministry. You check them off on a Routine card on Home. It's free, optional, and works on iPhone and Android.

## Set it up

1. Tap **Set up** on the **Spiritual Routine** card on Home. Or open **Settings → Features → Spiritual Routine**.
2. Choose which activities to include.
3. Choose how you read the Bible, your meeting days, and your family worship evening.

A daily reminder is available but stays off unless you turn it on. If you tap **Not now** on the Home card, it goes away. You can set it up later in Settings.

## Check things off

Tap the circle at the end of an item on the Routine card. When everything for today is done, the card shrinks to **All done today**.

What you can check off:

- **Bible reading:** once a day. You can also follow a reading plan. See [How do Bible reading plans work in Spiritual Routine?](/help/routine/routine-reading-plans)
- **Personal study:** any day you study. By default the card asks for it until you've studied once that week. You can change the goal to a number of times a week, or to chosen days.
- **Midweek meeting prep** and **Weekend meeting prep:** once each week. Each shows from two days before the meeting through the meeting day.
- **Family worship:** once a week, on the evening you choose. If you aren't with family, you can call it Personal worship evening.

## See more

- Tap an item itself, not its circle, to open its screen. It shows a heat map of the days you checked, and **Recent days**, where you can check off a day you forgot, back to the start of last week. Tap a checked day to undo it.
- Tap the Routine card's title to see every activity you've turned on and how often you checked it this month.

## Good to know

- There are no streaks. Missing a day never counts against you.
- To hide the card, edit your Home screen and remove Spiritual Routine. Or turn the feature off in **Settings → Features → Spiritual Routine**. Nothing you checked is lost.

## If something goes wrong

- **An activity isn't on Home:** weekly activities only show on their days, and personal study hides once you've met your goal for the week. Check **Settings → Features → Spiritual Routine**.
- **Still not right:** use **Still need help?** below this answer to email support.
