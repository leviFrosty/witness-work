title: "Nerekana igihe nka \"1h 30m\" aho kuba \"1.5\"?"
---

Yego. Hitamo Duration Format mu igenamiterere, kandi ikoreshwa hose igihe cyerekanwa.

## Hindura imiterere

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Akarere n’imiterere**.
2. Kanda **Duration Format**.
3. Hitamo imiterere ukunda.
