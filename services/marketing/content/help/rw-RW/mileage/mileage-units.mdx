title: "Nakoresha kilometero, litiro cyangwa L/100 km?"
---

Yego. Intera ishobora kuba mile cyangwa kilometero, kandi ingano ya lisansi ikoreshwa ishobora kuba mpg (US), mpg (UK), L/100 km cyangwa km/L.

## Hindura ibipimo

1. Fungura **Ibirometero**, hanyuma ukande uruziga rw'igenamiterere.
2. Kanda **Ibipimo**.
3. Hitamo ibipimo by'intera n'iby'ingano ya lisansi ikoreshwa.

**Byikora** ikurikiza akarere ka telefoni yawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Guhindura ibipimo bihindura uko imibare yerekanwa gusa. Ingendo zawe ntizihindurwa.
- Lisansi igurishwa ku gaganda iyo ukoresheje mpg (US), no ku kilitiro mu zindi zose.
- **Electric** (n'indi lisansi wongeyeho igurishwa kuri kWh) igurishwa kuri kWh. Ingano ikoreshwa yerekanwa nka mi/kWh, km/kWh cyangwa kWh/100 km bihuje n'ibyo wahisemo.
- Ifaranga rikurikiza akarere ka telefoni yawe.
