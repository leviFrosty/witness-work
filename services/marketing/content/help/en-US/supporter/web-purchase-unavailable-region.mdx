title: Why can't I buy on the web in my country or province?
category: supporter

For now, WitnessWork on the web doesn't sell Supporter subscriptions or Tips in the European Union, the United Kingdom, a number of other countries where tax applies from the first sale, or the Canadian provinces of Manitoba and Saskatchewan. Selling there brings tax obligations we aren't set up for yet. The [Terms of Use](/terms) list every place. The rest of Canada can buy on the web.

If WitnessWork thinks you're in one of those places, **Settings → Become a supporter** shows a notice titled "Not available where you are yet" instead of web prices and buy buttons. The Supporter benefits are still listed. The notice has App Store and Google Play buttons that open WitnessWork in the stores. The stores sell Supporter subscriptions and Tips there and handle the tax.

- **A store purchase shows on the web too.** Sign in to the same WitnessWork account on the web, and your Supporter features appear there (WitnessWork 1.47.0 or later on the phone; open it once after updating).
- **Your location comes from your connection.** If you're traveling, or using a VPN or a work or school network that routes through one of these places, you may see the notice even though you live elsewhere. Turn the VPN off or reconnect, then reload WitnessWork.
- **In Canada, WitnessWork needs to tell your province.** When your connection doesn't show which province you're in, the notice shows anywhere in Canada. Reconnect or try another network, then reload WitnessWork, or buy in the app.
- **Checkout checks your card's country too.** Payments are declined when the card or the billing address is from one of these countries, even if you're browsing from somewhere else. The decline happens before anything is charged.

If you're somewhere the Terms don't list and still can't buy, email [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) with what you see.
