title: "Nimukira nte kuri telefoni nshya cyangwa nkahindura hagati ya iPhone na Android?"
---

Erekana kode kuri telefoni yawe ishaje ayisikane na nshya. Ibi bikora hagati ya telefoni ebyiri zose, harimo iPhone ijya kuri Android n'ukundi. Mushakisha ntishobora gushyiraho ikindi gikoresho.

## Kwimukira kuri telefoni nshya

1. Kuri telefoni ishaje, jya kuri **Igenamiterere → Konti → Shyiraho ikindi gikoresho**. QR code irigaragaza.
2. Kuri telefoni nshya, shyiraho WitnessWork.
3. Mu gihe cyo gutegura, kanda **Wari usanzwe ukoresha? Subiza amakuru**, hanyuma **Uvuye ku yindi telefoni yawe**. Nyuma yo gutegura, koresha **Injira ukoresheje QR code** muri **Igenamiterere → Konti**.
4. Sikana QR code.
5. Reba ko telefoni zombi zerekana kode imwe y'imibare itandatu.
6. Kuri telefoni ishaje, kanda **Emerera**, hanyuma wemeze ukoresheje Face ID, igikumwe, kode cyangwa gufunga ecran.

Konti yawe na bagenzi bawe bimukira kuri telefoni nshya. Telefoni yawe ishaje ikomeza kuba yinjiye.

## Zana n'amakuru yawe

Amakuru y'umurimo wawe, Aderesi n'inyandiko ntibirimo muri iyi ntambwe. Telefoni nshya itangirana na Cloud Sync ifunguye, bityo biza ubwabyo umaze kwinjira. Cyangwa banza ufungure **Backup** muri **Igenamiterere → Konti** kuri telefoni ishaje, maze telefoni nshya igusabe kuyigarura. Ushobora no gukoresha dosiye iva muri **Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Telefoni zombi zikeneye murandasi, kandi kode imara iminota 10.
- Niba telefoni nshya yarakoresheje Buddies ifite indi konti, ikubaza mbere yo kuyisimbuza.
- Passkey ishobora gukora ibi nta telefoni yawe ishaje. Reba [Passkey ni iki?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
- Kode ntikora? Reba [Bigenda bite iyo kwinjira ukoresheje QR code bidakora?](/help/account/account-handoff-trouble).
