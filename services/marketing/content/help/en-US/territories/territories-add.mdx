title: How do I add a territory I've checked out?
category: territories
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Open the Territories tab and tap Add Territory, fill in the card's details, and tap Save. You add the streets next.

1. Enter the territory's **Number** from the card. **Area or Name** is optional, such as "North Hill".
2. Check **Checked Out On**, the day you were given the card. **Return By** is set to four months later; change it if you were given another date. After that date, the territory shows as overdue.
3. Under **Map (Optional)**, add whatever helps you find the territory: scan the card's QR code, enter a map link, pin it on a map, outline the area, or add a photo of the card. You can add more than one, or none. See [Which map options can I add to a territory?](/help/territories/territories-map-options)
4. Tap Save. The territory opens, ready for its streets (see [How do I add streets to a territory?](/help/territories/territories-streets)).

To change any of this later, open the territory and tap Edit, or touch and hold it in the list and tap Edit Territory. Edit is also where you'll find Delete Territory.
