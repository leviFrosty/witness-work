Yes, on iPhone. Touch and hold your home or lock screen, tap **+**, search for WitnessWork, and pick a widget.

## Which widgets

- **Service Report** (home or lock screen): this month's hours toward your goal and how much a day it takes to reach it. If you don't log hours, it shows this month's conversations and studies.
- **Calendar**, **Appointments**, **Contacts**, and **Pet Companion**: home screen.

## Good to know

- Widgets move on to the new day at midnight even if you don't open WitnessWork.

## If something goes wrong

- **A widget looks out of date:** open WitnessWork.
