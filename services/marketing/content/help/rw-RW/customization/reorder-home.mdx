title: "Nahindura nte uko ibice by'Ahabanza bitondetse cyangwa nkabihisha?"
---

Hindura ecran yawe y'Ahabanza kugira ngo wimure ibice cyangwa uhishe ibyo udakoresha. Guhisha igice ntibisiba amakuru yawe.

## Hindura uko bitondetse cyangwa uhishe ibice

1. Ku **Ahabanza**, fata ku gice, hanyuma uhitemo **Hindura Ekara y'ahabanza**. Cyangwa ukande **Hindura Ekara y'ahabanza** nyuma y'igice cya nyuma.
2. Ibice bitangira kunyeganyega. Fata igice ukurure kugira ngo ukimure. Kuri iPad, ibice bishobora kwimuka hagati y'inkingi ebyiri.
3. Kugira ngo uhishe igice, kanda akamenyetso ka minus k'ibara ry'ivu ku nguni yacyo yo hejuru. Kanda **Subiza inyuma** niba utabishakaga.
4. Kanda **Byarangiye**. Kuri Android, akabuto ko gusubira inyuma na ko karakora.

## Ongera werekane igice cyahishwe

1. Tangira guhindura nk'uko byavuzwe haruguru.
2. Kanda **Ongeraho ibice** hejuru.
3. Hitamo igice. Kigaruka ari icya mbere Ahabanza.

Ushobora kandi kwerekana, guhisha no gutondeka ibice muri **Igenamiterere → Igenamiterere → Ekara y'ahabanza**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Igice kidafite icyo kwerekana ubu, nka Ibiganiro byegereza nta byo gusubirayo uyu munsi, kigaragara nk'umurongo mu gihe uhindura, bityo ushobora kucyimura cyangwa kugihisha.
- Getting Started kiguma hejuru kugeza ukirangije cyangwa ukigafunga.
- Ibice bimwe bishingira ku ruhare rwawe cyangwa igikoresho. Kubara amasaha n'iminota kigaragara ku ruhare rwandika igihe, Ibirometero iyo gukurikirana ibirometero bifunguye, na Incamake y'umwaka w'umurimo kuri iPad ifite intego y'umwaka.
- Iyo Buddies ifunguye, **Ongeraho ibice** kandi itanga Hamwe, cyerekana ubutumire bwa bagenzi na gahunda hamwe na bagenzi mu byumweru bibiri biri imbere. Ntikiri Ahabanza kugeza ukongeye.
- Uko wateguye guhuzwa n'ibindi bikoresho byawe iyo guhuza bifunguye, kandi kurimo muri backup.
