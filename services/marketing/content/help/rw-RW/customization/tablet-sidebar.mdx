title: "Mpindura nte ubugari bw'umurongo w'ibumoso kuri tablet yanjye cyangwa nkawuhisha?"
---

Mu idirishya rinini rya tablet, ushobora gukurura umurongo w'ibumoso kugira ngo uwugabanye cyangwa uwugure, cyangwa uwuhishe.

## Hindura ubugari bw'umurongo w'ibumoso

1. Shaka akabari gato kari hagati ku nkombe y'iburyo y'umurongo w'ibumoso.
2. Gakurure ibumoso cyangwa iburyo.

Umurongo mugufi werekana ibishushanyo gusa. Wongere uwugure kugira ngo ubone amazina nanone.

## Hisha umurongo w'ibumoso

1. Kanda **Hisha umurongo w'ibumoso** iruhande rwa WitnessWork hejuru y'umurongo. Niba mugari, banza uwugabanye.
2. Kugira ngo uwugarure, kanda **Erekana umurongo w'ibumoso** mu mutwe w'urupapuro.

## Byiza kumenya

- WitnessWork yibuka ubugari bwawe n'uko wawuhishe kuri iki gikoresho. Ntibihuzwa cyangwa ngo bibikwe muri backup, kandi guhindura ubugari nta cyo bihindura ku makuru yawe.
- Umurongo w'ibumoso ukeneye idirishya rifite nibura ubugari bwa points 1.000. Mu idirishya rito, cyangwa n'inyandiko nini cyane, umurongo wo hasi uragaruka. Agura idirishya cyangwa utere tablet yawe kuruhande kugira ngo wongere gukoresha umurongo w'ibumoso.
- Hasi y'umurongo w'ibumoso, kanda ifoto n'izina ryawe kugira ngo ubone Umwirondoro, Igenamiterere n'Ikigo cy'ubufasha.
- Ukoresheje gusoma kwa ecran, hindura akabuto ka **Hindura ubugari bw'umurongo w'ibumoso** kugira ngo ube mugari cyangwa mugufi.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Imiyoboro isa n'ibura:** ongera utangire porogaramu. Niba ikibura, koresha **Uracyakeneye ubufasha?** mu Kigo cy'ubufasha, kandi ntushyire amakuru y'abantu muri raporo rusange.
