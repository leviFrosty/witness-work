---
id: scribeMistakes
title: Scribe AI got something wrong. What do I do?
category: scribe
---

Before you tap Add, uncheck any card you don’t want, or tap Say a change (or type) to tell Scribe AI what to fix, like “Make that Saturday at ten.” It replaces the cards with new ones. After you tap Add, tap Undo in the message that appears, or for up to 30 days open the conversation from History in Scribe AI and tap Undo there. Undo removes what was added and puts back what was changed or deleted, except a change you’ve edited again since, or a contact or type that something else now uses; Scribe AI says how many stayed. You can also edit any record yourself as usual. If Scribe AI keeps getting something wrong, email support from Still need help? in the Help Center, and describe what happened without sharing personal details.
