id: wearOsSync
title: Kuki isaha yanjye ya Wear OS igira iti Guhuza na telefoni?
---

Igihe wongeraho ku isaha yawe cyoherezwa kuri telefoni yawe ako kanya iyo iri hafi, cyangwa ubutaha zihuye, kandi kikabikwa ubutaha ufunguye WitnessWork kuri telefoni yawe. Kugeza icyo gihe isaha yawe igaragaza Guhuza na telefoni. Igiteranyo cyayo, tile na complication zazo birimo icyo gihe ako kanya, uretse Igihe cy'inyongera, cyongerwaho telefoni yawe imaze kukibika kandi gishyirwaho imbibi z'ukwezi z'inyongera. Buri nyandiko igumana umunsi wayongeyeho ku isaha, ibikwa rimwe gusa n'iyo yoherejwe kabiri, kandi ntigarurwa niba wari warayisibye kuri telefoni yawe. Gutangiza, guhagarika by'agateganyo, guhagarika no kubika igihe kibarwa bikenera telefoni yawe iri hafi, n'iyo WitnessWork itaba ifunguye kuri yo. Niba isaha ivuga ko igihe kibarwa cyahindutse kuri telefoni yawe, reba igihe hanyuma ugerageze nanone. Niba isaha yawe ikomeza kutavugururwa, reba ko ihujwe na telefoni yawe muri porogaramu yayo ihuza (companion app), komeza telefoni yawe hafi, fungura WitnessWork kuri telefoni yawe, hanyuma uyifungure ku isaha yawe. Ku bindi bibazo, koresha ihuza ry'ubufasha bwihariye ryo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha kandi ntushyire inyandiko zawe muri raporo zishyirwa ahagaragara.
