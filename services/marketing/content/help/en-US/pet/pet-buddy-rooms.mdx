id: petBuddyRooms
title: Can I see my buddies' pets and their rooms?
category: pet
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Yes. Open **Buddies**, tap a buddy, and if they share their pet with you, you see it in its room, with its hat. You can pat it just like yours.

It's only for fun. Nothing you do there reaches your buddy or changes their pet's hearts.

## Good to know

- A buddy's pet shows when they share it with you and your own pet section is on Home.
- Their sign stays blank, because it would show their service time. You never see their hearts or coins, and their pet always looks happy on your screen.
- Changes they make show up the next time WitnessWork refreshes **Buddies**.
- If their app is old, their pet shows in the default room until they update.

## Bring back your pet section

If you removed the pet from Home, buddies' pets are hidden too.

1. On **Home**, tap **Edit Home Screen** after the last section.
2. Tap **Add Sections**.
3. Tap **Pet**.

## Stop sharing your pet

- To stop for everyone, turn off **Pet** in Buddies Settings.
- To stop for one buddy, open their page, tap **Edit Buddy** (the pencil at the top), then **Sharing & Alerts**.
