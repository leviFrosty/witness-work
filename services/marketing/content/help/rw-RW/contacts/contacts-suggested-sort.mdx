title: "Gutondeka kwa Byatanzwe muri Aderesi bikora iki?"
---

Byatanzwe ni uko urutonde rwa Aderesi rutondekwa mu itangiriro. Rushyira hejuru abantu ushobora kuba wifuza kubona nyuma, mu bice bigufi, kugira ngo utagomba gushakisha.

## Ibyo uzabona

Iyo udashakisha, urutonde rushobora gutangirana n'ibi bice, buri kimwe cyerekana abantu bagera kuri 5:

- **Hafi:** abantu bari muri metero 250 hafi yawe, uwegereye mbere.
- **Gusubirayo kwageze:** gusubirayo kuri uyu munsi, cyangwa kwasibye mu minsi 30 ishize.
- **Vuba aha:** abantu wasuye mu byumweru 2 bishize.
- **Hashize igihe:** abantu uherutse kuganira na bo hashize ibyumweru 3 kugeza ku mezi 4 badafite gusubirayo giteganyijwe. **Abatari mu rugo** ntibibarwa nk'ikiganiro.

Abandi bose bari munsi ya **Abandi bantu**: abakunzwe mbere, hanyuma ibyigisho bya Bibiliya, hanyuma gusura kwa vuba. Ibice bitarimo ikintu bihishwa. Buri muntu agaragara rimwe gusa.

## Hindura uko bikurikirana

1. Fungura **Aderesi**.
2. Kanda **Tondeka & Shungura**.
3. Hitamo uburyo bwo gutondeka. Kanda **Subiza** kugira ngo usubire kuri **Byatanzwe**.

## Kuramo umuntu muri Hashize igihe

Kanda ufate umuntu uhitemo **Si ubu**. Agumya hanze iminsi 30. Ushobora kumugarura muri **Abantu bavanyweho** igihe icyo ari cyo cyose.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ishungura ryawe n'uburyo bwabitswe bikora mbere.
- Gushakisha, cyangwa guhitamo ubundi buryo bwo gutondeka, byerekana urutonde rumwe rworoshye.
- **Hafi** igaragara gusa niba waramaze kwemerera WitnessWork aho uri. Aho uri hakoreshwa gusa mu gutondeka urutonde, kandi ntihabikwa cyangwa ngo hoherezwe ahandi.
- Iyo uburyo bwo kurinda amakuru bufunguye, **Hashize igihe** ihagarara ku minsi 90.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Hafi irabura:** reba ko aho uri hemerewe WitnessWork mu igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe, kandi ko umuntu afite akadomo ku ikarita.
