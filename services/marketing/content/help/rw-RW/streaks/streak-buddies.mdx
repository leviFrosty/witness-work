---
id: streakBuddies
title: Bagenzi banjye bashobora kubona urukurikirane rwanjye?
---

Yego, iyo rugeze kuri 3 kandi urusangiza. Bagenzi bawe babona umubare ku ikarita yawe mu rutonde rwa Buddies rwabo, ariko ntibabona igihe cyawe cyangwa raporo zawe. Rurarinzwe n'ibanga kuva ku gikoresho kimwe kugeza ku kindi (end-to-end) nk'ibindi usangiza. Kugira ngo uhagarike kurusangiza, fungura Igenamiterere rya Buddies ukuzimye Urukurikirane. Urukurikirane rwarangiye ruhagarara kugaragara kuri telefoni za bagenzi bawe nubwo porogaramu yawe itafunguwe kuva icyo gihe.
