---
id: restoreSupporter
title: I already paid, but Supporter features or prices are missing. What should I do?
category: supporter
---

From version 1.47.0, your Supporter status belongs to your WitnessWork account, so it shows on every device signed in to the same account, including WitnessWork on the web, which has no Restore Purchases button. Check your connection, that you're signed in to the same account in Settings → Account, and that your device's store uses the account you purchased with. If you opened WitnessWork without a connection, prices and your Supporter status load by themselves once you're back online. Open Settings → Become a supporter and use Restore Purchases when it is shown. If buying or Restore Purchases says "Purchases Aren't Ready Yet", check your connection and try again; it can show for a moment after a device signs in to a different account. If you bought on this device while it was signed in to another WitnessWork account, use Restore Purchases after signing in to the account you want it on. A one-time Tip does not activate Supporter features. If no purchases are found, prices do not load, or your subscription still is not recognized, email support privately with your app version and what happened. If support needs your account ID, open Settings → About → App Info and tap Copy All Details. Before version 1.47.0, use the support button in this screen's header to open Still need help?, then tap Copy Account ID. Share that ID privately. Restoring purchases restores access, not your service records. From version 1.47.0, syncing your devices doesn't depend on Supporter status.
