title: "Kuki WitnessWork ivuga ngo Update Required?"
---

Update Required bisobanura ko verisiyo yawe ya WitnessWork ishaje cyane ku buryo idakorana na seriveri za WitnessWork. Ugomba kuyivugurura mbere yo gukomeza kuyikoresha.

## Vugurura porogaramu

1. Kanda **Vugurura WitnessWork**. Ibi bifungura App Store, cyangwa porogaramu ya Google Play Store kuri Android.
2. Shyiraho ivugurura.
3. Fungura WitnessWork nanone.

Niba gufunga porogaramu bifunguye, banza ufungure WitnessWork. Update Required igaragara ako kanya nyuma yaho.

## Byiza kumenya

- Amakuru yawe, Aderesi n'inyandiko bigumana kuri telefoni yawe kandi biriho byose nyuma y'ivugurura.
- Kugeza ubwo uvugurura, Cloud Sync, Backup, Buddies na Scribe AI birategereza. Bigeraho nyuma y'akanya gato ufunguye porogaramu yavuguruwe.
- Kuri murandasi, ongera upakire paji kugira ngo ubone verisiyo nshya.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Ububiko ntibufunguka:** fungura ububiko ubwawe ushakishe WitnessWork.
- **Ububiko ntibwerekana ivugurura:** funga porogaramu y'ububiko, uyifungure nanone, hanyuma urebe amavugurura aho.
