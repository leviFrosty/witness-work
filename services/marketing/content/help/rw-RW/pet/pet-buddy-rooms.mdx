title: "Nshobora kubona amatungo ya bagenzi banjye n'ibyumba byayo?"
---

Yego. Fungura **Buddies**, kanda mugenzi, kandi niba asangiza itungo rye nawe, ubona ririmo mu cyumba cyaryo, rifite ingofero. Ushobora kuririhura nk'iryawe.

Ni iby'ibyishimo gusa. Nta kintu ukora aho kigera kuri mugenzi wawe cyangwa gihindura imitima y'itungo rye.

## Byiza kumenya

- Itungo rya mugenzi ryerekana iyo aririsangiza nawe kandi igice cy'itungo ryawe cyo kuri Ahabanza gifunguye.
- Icyapa cyaryo kigumya ubusa, kuko cyerekana igihe cye cy'umurimo. Ntuzigera ubona imitima cyangwa ibiceri bye, kandi itungo rye risa n'iryishimye ku ekara yawe buri gihe.
- Impinduka akora zigaragara ubutaha WitnessWork ivuguruye **Buddies**.
- Niba porogaramu ye ishaje, itungo rye ryerekanwa mu cyumba gisanzwe kugeza avuguruye.

## Garura igice cy'itungo ryawe

Niba wakuyeho itungo kuri Ahabanza, amatungo ya bagenzi na yo arahishwa.

1. Kuri **Ahabanza**, kanda **Hindura Ekara y'ahabanza** nyuma y'igice cya nyuma.
2. Kanda **Ongeraho ibice**.
3. Kanda **Itungo**.

## Reka gusangiza itungo ryawe

- Kugira ngo uhagarike kuri bose, zimya **Itungo** mu Igenamiterere rya Buddies.
- Kugira ngo uhagarike kuri mugenzi umwe, fungura urupapuro rwe, kanda **Hindura mugenzi** (ikaramu iri hejuru), hanyuma **Gusangiza n'imenyesha**.
