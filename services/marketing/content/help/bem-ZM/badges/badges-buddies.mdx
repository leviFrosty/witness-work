title: Bushe banyina bandi bamona finshi, ukwasuka kubomba shani, kabili ningaleka shani ukusabankanya amabaji nelyo ifyakwishibisha fya mabaji?
---

Banyina benu bamona amabaji mukwete, cila baji pa cipimo cayo ca pa mulu, ilyo baisula lupapa lwenu mu Buddies. Tabamona ifyo mwakula, inambala, inshiku, nelyo amabaji mushakwata. Mu data protection mode, Isambililo lya Baibolo lya Ntanshi tayasabankanishiwa. Pa kuleka ukusabankanya amabaji, shimyeni Amabaji mu Ifyo Mwasala fya Buddies. Ilyo munyina apokelela baji ipya, mupokelela icishibisho cikwata ishina lyakwe na baji, nga “Levi apokelela baji” na “Umwaka Onse, Silivere” panshi; tinikeni pa kwisula lupapa lwakwe. Mupokelela icishibisho cimo fye pa munyina pa bushiku, kabili amabaji yambi aya pa bushiku bumo yaisa mu kwisa mu belu ya fyakwishibisha. Amabaji ayasangwa mu fyacitika fyaletwa nelyo fyabweshiwa, na mabaji aya umuku umo, tayalondololwa. Pa kukoselesha munyina, isuleni lupapa lwakwe mu Buddies, tinikeni baji imo yakwe, no kusala ukwasuka: 🎉, 🎊, 🔥, 👏, 👍, nelyo 🙌. Kuti mwasuka umuku umo pa cila baji; ukusala ukwasuka ukumbi kucinja ukwa ntanshi. Munyina uyo fye e wamona uwasuka, kabili tapali icintu cimbi icitumwa: tapali butumwa, kabili tapali baji yenu iili yonse. Nga tamuli pa intaneti, ukwasuka kwenu kutumwa ilyo mwabwela pa intaneti. Ilyo munyina asuka ku baji imo yenu, mupokelela icishibisho nga “Anna asuka 🔥 ku baji yenu,” umuku umo fye pa munyina pa bushiku, kabili ukwasuka kumoneka mu belu ya fyakwishibisha na ilyo mwaisula baji iyo. Pa kuleka ifyakwishibisha fya mabaji apya ne fya kwasuka pa cibombelo, shimyeni Ifyakwishibisha fya Mabaji mu Ifyo Mwasala fya Buddies; fyonse fibili fimoneka mu belu ya fyakwishibisha. Ilyo Langa amabaji yashimiwa, tamwingasuka, kabili ukwasuka ku mabaji yenu tekumoneka.
