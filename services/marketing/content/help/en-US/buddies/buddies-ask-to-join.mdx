title: How do I ask to join a buddy's Plan?
category: buddies
---

Tap a day on your **Schedule**, then tap **Ask to Join** next to a buddy who is going out that day. Your buddy decides whether to invite you.

## Ask to join

1. On **Schedule**, tap the day.
2. Tap **Ask to Join** beside your buddy's Plan.

You can also tap one of their days under **Next two weeks** on their page.

The request shows **Asked** until your buddy invites you, you tap **Asked** to take it back, or the Plan starts. If your buddy invites you, answer in the notification bell on Home.

## Good to know

- Your buddy sees only the day, time and length of their own Plan.
- You can ask until two hours before the Plan starts.
- You can have three requests waiting with each buddy.
- Your buddy is alerted the first time you ask about a Plan, and for no more than three requests a day.
- If your buddy moves or removes the Plan, your request is withdrawn.
- **Asked** shows only on the phone you asked from.
- If you answer **Going** while you already have your own Plan at that time, you can replace it for that day so your time isn't counted twice.
