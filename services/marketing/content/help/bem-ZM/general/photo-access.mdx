id: photoAccess
title: Cinshi cimpelele ukumona fye ifikope fimbi?
---

Ilyo iPhone nelyo iPad yenu yapushishe pa fikope, mwasalile **Select Photos**, kanshi WitnessWork yamona fye ifikope mwayisabankanya.

## Sabankanyeni ifikope ifingi

1. Mu WitnessWork, isuleni photo picker, ukwabula, ukufuma pa cikope ca muntu wa kulanshanya nelyo mu note.
2. Tinikeni **Manage** pa pamulu pa fikope.
3. Saleni ifikope mufwaya, nelyo mufumyepo ifyo mufwaya ukushala fya cisuma.
4. Tinikisheni. Fyaboneka ilyo line.

## Leseni WitnessWork imone ifikope fyenu fyonse

1. Isuleni app ya **Ifyo Mwasala** pa iPhone yenu.
2. Yeni ku **Apps → WitnessWork → Photos**.
3. Saleni **Full Access**.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- WitnessWork ikopolola fye ifikope mwasala. Tabelenga ico cili conse cimbi mu library yenu.

## Nga cabipa

- **Icikope tacipoka:** kuti cacili ukutululwa ukufuma ku iCloud. Moneni intaneti yenu elyo mwesheko nakabili.
