title: "Kuki hari umubare ku gishushanyo cya WitnessWork?"
---

Kuri iPhone, umubare uri ku gishushanyo cya porogaramu ni uw'ibintu bitarasomwa biri mu nzogera y'Ahabanza. Amakuru ya bagenzi bawe, nk'ikimenyetso gishya cya mugenzi wawe cyangwa uko yakiriye icyawe, ntabwo abarwa.

## Gabanya umubare

1. Fungura WitnessWork ukande inzogera iri Ahabanza.
2. Kurikira ibintu byose, cyangwa ubikureho cyangwa ubihanagure.

## Hisha umubare

Fungura igenamiterere ry'amamenyesha rya telefoni yawe kuri WitnessWork ukuremo **Ibimenyetso**. Ibi bihisha umubare gusa. Ntibigira icyo bihindura ku bimenyetso wunguka mu mwirondoro wawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Umubare ukenera uruhushya rw'amamenyesha.
- Uvugururwa WitnessWork ifunguye, no mu gihe haje inyibutsa yo gusubirayo, ya gahunda cyangwa y'umuntu. Ibindi bintu, nka raporo y'ukwezi gushize, bibarwa ubutaha ufunguye WitnessWork.
- Buri gikoresho kigira umubare wacyo.
- Kuri Android, igishushanyo cya porogaramu nta mubare kigaragaza. Ahari uzabona akadomo.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Umubare usa n'utari wo:** fungura WitnessWork uyiveho akanya gato kugira ngo ivugururwe. Niba utarahura n'inzogera, vugana n'ubufasha ukoresheje Ikigo cy'ubufasha.
