id: calendarSetup
title: Nashyira nte Gusubirayo muri kalendari yanjye?
---

Guhuza kalendari ni ubuntu kuri iPhone, iPad na Android. Iki gisubizo kivuga kuri iPhone na iPad, naho Guhuza kalendari bikora bite kuri Android? kivuga kuri Android. Mu gihe cyo gutangira bwa mbere, cyangwa ukoresheje ubutumire buri mu rwego rw'amamenyesha nyuma yo kuvugurura, kanda Ongeraho kuri kalendari kugira ngo ukore kalendari ya WitnessWork (muri iCloud iyo iboneka). Ushobora no kubikora igihe icyo ari cyo cyose muri Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Guhuza kalendari: emera kugera kuri kalendari yose, hanyuma ukore kalendari ya WitnessWork cyangwa uhitemo kalendari ikoreshwa kuri WitnessWork gusa. Kuvugurura bikenera kwinjira muri iCloud na interineti, no kuri kalendari za Google cyangwa Outlook. Kugira ngo ukoreshe Google cyangwa Outlook, kora kalendari kuri uwo mutanga serivisi, wongere konti muri Igenamiterere rya iPhone → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts, ufungure kalendari zayo. Kalendari yawe isanzwe na kalendari zibitswe kuri iki gikoresho gusa ntizishobora guhitwamo. Kalendari imaze guhitwamo, buri Gusubirayo kuzaza kongerwaho ubwako, harimo ibyo wari warabitse, nk'igikorwa cy'iminota 30. Guhuza kalendari byongeraho Gusubirayo byose bizaza cyangwa nta na kimwe; kugira ngo uhagarike kubyongeraho, bizimye muri Igenamiterere → Amahitamo → Guhuza kalendari. Gusubirayo byashize, Gahunda z'umurimo na Gahunda zisubiramo ntibyongerwaho. Buri gikorwa gihabwa imenyesha rimwe n'urwibutso rwa Menyesha njyewe rwa Gusubirayo kwacyo, kandi nta menyesha iyo Menyesha njyewe izimye. Utanga kalendari yawe ashobora kongeraho amamenyesha ye asanzwe; kuri Google, ubikureho muri Event notifications ya kalendari muri igenamiterere rya Google Calendar. Hindurira muri WitnessWork, kuko impinduka zakozwe muri kalendari, harimo amamenyesha, zishobora gusimbuzwa ku vugurura rikurikira.
