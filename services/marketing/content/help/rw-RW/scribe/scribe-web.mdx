---

Yego, wandika. Scribe AI kuri murandasi yerekana ibyo yumvise nk'amakarita, kandi nta kintu cyongerwa kugeza ukanze **Ongera**.

## Koresha Scribe AI kuri murandasi

1. Injira muri WitnessWork kuri murandasi.
2. Kuri **Ahabanza**, kanda akabuto ko kuganira mu Ibikorwa byihuse. Cyangwa ukande **+** uhitemo **Andika ibigezweho**.
3. Andika ibyo wakoze cyangwa uteganya gukora, nka "Amasaha abiri uyu munsi mu gitondo, kandi naganiriye na John ku muhanda wa Oak 42, gusubirayo ku wa kabiri utaha."
4. Kanda **Ohereza**.
5. Kuraho ikimenyetso ku kintu cyose udashaka, hanyuma ukande **Ongera**.

Subiramo ikora nk'uko ikora kuri telefoni yawe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Scribe AI ikora ibintu bimwe n'ibya kuri telefoni yawe ([Scribe AI ishobora gukora iki?](/help/scribe/scribe-what-it-does)) ifite imipaka imwe ([Imipaka ya Scribe AI ikora ite?](/help/scribe/scribe-usage)).
- Kuvuga, gusuzuma inyandiko no gusoma amafoto y'impapuro ntibiri kuri murandasi. Koresha porogaramu ya telefoni yawe kuri ibyo.
- Ubwa mbere, Scribe AI igusaba kwemera [Amabwiriza y'imikoreshereze](/terms) na [Politiki y'ibanga](/privacy) bya WitnessWork.
- Ibiganiro byawe na Kwibuka kwa Scribe AI bibikwa gusa muri iyo murandasi. Ntibihuzwa cyangwa ngo bibikwe muri backup, kandi gusohoka birabisiba. Reba [Scribe AI yohereza iki, kandi ni iki kibikwa?](/help/scribe/scribe-privacy).

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Nta murandasi ufite:** Scribe AI irabivuga. Ushobora gukomeza kongeraho igihe, abantu na gahunda ubwawe. Reba [Scribe AI ikora nta murandasi?](/help/scribe/scribe-offline).
- **Ivuga ko uvugurura WitnessWork:** ongera ufungure urupapuro.
- **Wageze ku mupaka:** ikubwira igihe ushobora kongera kuyikoresha.
- **Biracyakomeye:** andikira ubufasha imeri ukoresheje **Ukeneye ubufasha bundi?** mu Kigo cy'Ubufasha. Sobanura ibyabaye utanga amakuru bwite.
