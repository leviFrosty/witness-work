title: How do I format a note?
category: notes
---

Open the note, and use the bar above the keyboard.

## Format a note

1. Tap the note in a visit, time entry, plan, or trip.
2. Tap a button on the bar above the keyboard.
3. Tap **Save** or **Add** when you're done.

The bar has headings, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, bulleted and numbered lists, checklists, and links. Swipe the bar sideways to see every button. The button at its right end puts the keyboard away.

## Good to know

- Links show a preview card under the note.
- You can tick off checklist items right where the note is shown.
- Service report comments and follow-up topics stay plain text.
