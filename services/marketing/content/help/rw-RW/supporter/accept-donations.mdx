id: acceptDonations
title: Kuki wemera gutanga amafaranga?
---

Kubera ko ndi umuvandimwe wo mu Ngoro, mbona ari iby'agaciro gukoresha ubuhanga bwanjye mu gufasha abandi bo mu itorero. N'ubwo bimeze gutyo ariko, buri wese akoresha uburyo bwo kugenzura no kubungabunga ibidukikije, bitwara amafaranga menshi n'igihe. Njye mbona gutanga ari kimwe n'uburyo umuntu ashobora kwishyura gaz y'undi nyuma yo kujya mu murimo - ni igikorwa cy'urukundo gishimwa cyane.
