title: Why can't I install WitnessWork on my phone?
category: general
---

Most often, the page hasn't finished loading, or the browser can't install web apps. Open [app.witnesswork.app](https://app.witnesswork.app) in Chrome on Android, or Safari on iPhone, and wait for the **Sign In to WitnessWork** screen before you open the menu.

## If something goes wrong

- **Chrome's menu has no Add to Home screen or Install app:** reload the page and wait for it to finish. Check your connection. Update Chrome from the Play Store if you can.
- **You use Samsung Internet, Firefox, or another browser:** look in its menu for **Install**, **Add to Home screen**, or **Add page to**. If it isn't there, use Chrome.
- **You're in an Incognito or private tab:** web apps can't be installed there. Open a regular tab.
- **The icon opens a browser tab with an address bar:** you added a shortcut, not the app. Remove the icon, then install again and choose **Install**.
- **It says it's already installed:** look for **WitnessWork** in your list of apps. To install again, remove it first.
- **You removed WitnessWork by accident:** install it again and sign in. Your synced records come back.

For the full steps, see [getting WitnessWork on Android without Google Play](/help/general/android-web-app) or [on iPhone without the App Store](/help/general/iphone-web-app).
