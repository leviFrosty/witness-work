---
id: webOffline
title: Does WitnessWork work offline on the web?
category: general
platforms: [web]
---

Yes. WitnessWork on the web opens without a connection and saves your changes in your browser. They sync when you're back online.

## When you're offline

A banner says: "You're offline. Reconnect to sync your changes. They're saved on this device until then." Keep working.

- Syncing, Scribe AI, and map search and directions wait until you're back online.
- A language you haven't used yet needs a connection the first time you pick it.

## Good to know

- Changes that haven't synced exist only in that browser. They're lost if the browser's data is erased, for example when you sign out or clear site data. See [What does WitnessWork keep in my browser?](/help/general/web-storage).
- If you work offline for long, reconnect and let your changes sync before you sign out or switch computers.
