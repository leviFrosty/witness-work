id: badgesMonths
title: Why do badges count months instead of hours?
category: badges
---

So everyone grows at the same pace, whatever their circumstances. Every badge counts calendar months, never hours or goals.

## Good to know

- A month counts once, whether you shared for a few minutes or all day, and whether you mark that you shared or log hours as a pioneer.
- Badges never reset and don't need an unbroken run of months. Time off for illness, travel or a hard season takes nothing away. The next month you share simply counts.
- **Year Round** counts service years with at least 10 months of sharing, so two months off in a service year never count against you.
- Your streak is separate and never affects your badges.
- The highest level, Pearl, takes years, because each month counts only once. That's on purpose.
