Each badge counts calendar months. This list says what makes a month count for each one.

## What each badge counts

- **Sharing the Good News:** months where you logged time or marked that you shared.
- **Year Round:** service years with at least 10 of those months.
- **Reports Sent:** months whose report you sent from WitnessWork or marked as already sent.
- **Ready to Go:** months with a Plan, once the Plan's day arrives.
- **Kind Words:** months with a visit where you talked with someone. Not at Home visits don't count.
- **Return Visits:** months you went back to someone you had visited before.
- **Next Time:** months with a Follow-up that has a topic.
- **Keeping in Touch:** the most different months you've talked with one person, in person, by phone or by letter.
- **Two by Two:** months a buddy went with you on a Plan or Follow-up. It shows only where Buddies is available.

One-time badges mark a first: your first Bible study, and your first buddy.

## If a month didn't count

- Hours carried over by rollover don't count on their own.
- Visits that came with a contact someone shared with you are theirs, so they don't count.
- A Plan counts only once its day arrives.
- Two by Two needs a buddy who answered **Going**.
