The **Service Year Pace** chart shows how your time so far compares with where your Annual Goal puts you.

## Find it

1. Open the **Progress** tab.
2. Tap **Year**.
3. Scroll below your milestones to **Service Year Pace**.

It appears only if your status has an Annual Goal, such as Regular Pioneer.

## Read the chart

- **Dashed gray line:** where your Annual Goal puts you by the end of each month.
- **Green line:** your time so far, counted like your Service Report, with each month's Credit limit.
- **Dashed green line:** adds the time you've planned.
- **Faint line:** last service year, with its total at the end.
- **Ahead of pace:** compares your time with where the gray line is today.

Press and hold the chart, then slide your finger, to see any month's totals.

## Month bars

Below the chart, each month has a bar. Solid is logged time, lighter is Credit, and striped is plans. A tick marks that month's goal. Every bar uses the same scale, so you can compare months.

## Good to know

- Without an Annual Goal you see the month bars but not the chart.
- If a month looks wrong, press and hold it to change its status or goal.
