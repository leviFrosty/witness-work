title: Gutondeka kwa Byatanzwe muri Abantu bikora iki?
---

Byatanzwe ni uburyo busanzwe bwo gutondeka urutonde rw'Abantu kuri iOS na Android. Iyo udashakisha, urutonde rutangira n'ibice bigufi bigera kuri bitatu: Hafi (abantu bari muri metero 250 hafi yawe, uwa hafi akabanza, hamwe n'umuhanda wabo), Gusubirayo bigeze (gusubirayo bigeze uyu munsi cyangwa byatinze mu minsi 30 ishize, ibyatinze cyane bikabanza), na Vuba aha (abasuwe mu byumweru 2 bishize). Buri gice kigaragaza abantu bagera kuri 5, kandi buri muntu agaragara rimwe gusa. Abandi bose bakurikira munsi ya Abandi bantu: abakunzwe babanza, hanyuma ibyigisho bya Bibiliya bikorwa, hanyuma ibyigisho byashize, hanyuma ukurikije gusura kwa vuba. Imyunguruzi yawe n'Uburyo bubitswe bikoreshwa mbere, bityo ibice birimo gusa abantu urutonde rwagaragaza. Ibice ubusa bihishwa, kandi niba bitatu byose ari ubusa urutonde ruba urutonde rumwe rworoshye. Gushakisha cyangwa guhitamo ubundi buryo bwo gutondeka na byo bigaragaza urutonde rumwe rworoshye. Hafi ni ubushake. Kigaragara gusa niba warimaze kwemera aho uri kuri WitnessWork; urutonde rw'Abantu ntirubisaba. Gikoresha akamenyetso ku ikarita ka buri muntu, kava kuri aderesi ye cyangwa akamenyetso washyizeho, bityo umuntu udafite aho aherereye ntashobora kuba Hafi. Aho uri hasomwa ku gikoresho cyawe iyo ufunguye Abantu, gusa kugira ngo hatondeke urutonde, kandi ntihabikwa cyangwa ngo hoherezwe ahandi. Kugira ngo imirongo itimuka mu gihe usoma, aho uri hashya hagaragara ubutaha ufunguye Abantu. Kugira ngo ukoreshe indi gahunda yo gutondeka, kanda buto y'umuyunguruzi iruhande rw'ishakisha (cyangwa ••• → Tondeka & Shungura…) uhitemo uburyo; Subizaho isubira kuri Byatanzwe. Niba Hafi ibura, reba ko aho uri hemewe kuri WitnessWork mu igenamiterere ry'igikoresho cyawe kandi ko umuntu afite akamenyetso ku ikarita. Ku bindi, vugana n'ubufasha uciye mu Kigo cy'ubufasha kandi udashyiramo amazina na aderesi.
