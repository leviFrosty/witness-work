title: "Nakora iki niba ntagishoboye kwinjira muri konti yanjye ya WitnessWork?"
---

Gerageza ibi ukurikije uko bikurikirana. Abantu benshi bongera kwinjira bakoresheje kimwe muri bitatu bya mbere.

## Inzira zo kongera kwinjira

1. **Telefoni nshya isanzwe ifite urufunguzo rwawe.** iPhone cyangwa iPad yinjiye muri konti imwe ya Apple ifite iCloud Keychain ifunguye irubona ubwayo. Kuri Android, gutegura telefoni nshya ukoresheje backup yawe ya Google bishobora kurugarura.
2. **Passkey.** Fungura app.witnesswork.app uhitemo **Sign in with a passkey**. Kuri telefoni nshya, uyihitemo mu gutegura (**Wari usanzwe ukoresha? Subiza amakuru**) cyangwa muri **Igenamiterere → Konti → Sign In with a Passkey**. Niba idashobora kurangiza yonyine, WitnessWork igusaba indi telefoni yawe cyangwa urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura.
3. **Indi telefoni ikiri yinjiye.** Erekana kode ya **Shyiraho ikindi gikoresho** kuri telefoni nshya, cyangwa wemere mudasobwa ukoresheje **Igenamiterere → Konti → Sign In on a Computer**. Mushakisha ntishobora gukora ibyo byombi. Reba [Nimukira nte kuri telefoni nshya cyangwa nkahindura hagati ya iPhone na Android?](/help/account/account-handoff) na [Ninjira nte kuri mudasobwa nkoresheje telefoni yanjye?](/help/account/sign-in-on-computer).
4. **Urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura.** Hitamo **Koresha urufunguzo rwo kugarura**. Reba [Nakoresha nte urufunguzo rwanjye rwo kugarura kuri telefoni nshya?](/help/account/account-recovery-key-use).

## Niba nta na kimwe gikora

Nta rufunguzo rwo kugarura, konti ntishobora kugarurwa. Nta muntu ukorera WitnessWork ushobora kuyisoma cyangwa kuyisubiza uko yari, kandi ubufasha ntibushobora guhindura ibyo. Ushobora gutangira konti nshya ku gikoresho gishya, ariko amakuru yawe ashaje ntashobora kugarurwa avuye kuri seriveri.

## Irinde ubutaha

- Bika urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura mu bubiko bw'amajambobanga, cyangwa ucape. Reba [Urufunguzo rwanjye rwo kugarura ni iki, kandi nza kurubika nte?](/help/account/account-recovery-key).
- Tekereza no kubika passkey. Reba [Passkey ni iki?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
