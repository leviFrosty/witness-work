title: How do I use the menu bar on iPad?
category: general
platforms:
  - ios
---

On iPadOS 26 or later, use Windowed Apps or Stage Manager and swipe down from the top edge to reveal the menu bar. After setup, WitnessWork Preferences opens in-app settings; the standard Settings item opens your device's settings. File opens Log Visit…, Add Time when hours logging is enabled, Add Contact, New Plan, and Backup & Restore. Edit → Find Contact opens the Contacts list and focuses search. Help opens the Help Center, What's New, your email app for private support, or Check for Update. These shortcuts reuse the existing screens; opening a form does not save or share your records. Check for Update checks for a compatible app update and uses the existing download and restart flow when one is available. It cannot install a new native app version, and is unavailable in development builds. Menu-bar support requires an installed app build that includes it and is unavailable on iPhone and Android. If items are missing, update from your app store and reopen WitnessWork. You can still use the same actions inside the app. For other problems, use the Help Center's private email link and keep contact details and backups out of public reports.
