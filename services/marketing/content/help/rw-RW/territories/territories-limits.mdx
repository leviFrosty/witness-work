title: "Ni ibihe bipimo byo gusangiza agace, kandi kuki bivuga ko ari kanini cyane ku gusangizwa?"
---

"Aka gace ni kanini cyane ku gusangizwa" akenshi bivuze ko agace kageze ku kimwe muri ibi bipimo.

## Ibipimo byo gusangiza

- **Abantu 10 kuri buri gace.** Ibi bibara wowe, abo musangiye, ubutumire bufunguye, n'abo mugenzi yasangije umuhanda yashyikirijwe. Abakoresha umurongo w'umushyitsi ntibabarwa.
- **Imirongo 10 y'abashyitsi ifunguye kuri buri gace,** n'imirongo mishya igera kuri 30 ku munsi.
- **Agace kanini cyane.** Rimwe na rimwe, agace gafite amateka menshi cyane ni kanini cyane ku buryo katasangizwa na gato. Gagumya kuri telefoni yawe nk'uko byari bimeze.

## Tegura umwanya

- **Ku bantu:** fungura agace ukande **Hagarika** iruhande rw'uwarangije, cyangwa iruhande rw'ubutumire ntawabwemeye. Biri munsi ya **Basangijwe**.
- **Ku mirongo:** kanda umurongo munsi ya **Basangijwe**, hanyuma **Rangiza umurongo ubu**.

## Ibindi bipimo

- Umurongo w'umushyitsi ukubiyemo imihanda igera kuri 30. Ushaka myinshi, kora umurongo wa kabiri w'isigaye.
- Imirongo y'abashyitsi imara umunsi 1 keretse uhinduye **Umurongo umara**, kugeza ku minsi 30.
- Ubutumire bumara iminsi 7. Nyuma yaho, ongera usangize agace.
- Inyandiko za Scribe AI zishobora kugera ku nyuguti 8,000 cyangwa imirongo 300. Inyandiko ndende zisomerwa kuri telefoni yawe, bityo ugabanye mo ibice kugira ngo Scribe AI izisome.

Uduce ni ubuntu, nta mubare ntarengwa w'ushobora kongera.
