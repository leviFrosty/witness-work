id: buddiesNotSent
title: Why does my answer say Sending in or Not sent yet, or my request say Waiting to Send?
category: buddies
---

Going is sent to your buddy right away. Can't Make It waits 15 seconds first, in case you tapped it by mistake: tap Change to fix it before it's sent. Leaving the app sends it right away. Not sent yet or Waiting to Send means your answer or Ask to Join request hasn't reached your buddy, usually because you're offline or Buddies is temporarily unavailable. You don't need to answer or ask again: it's saved on this device and sends automatically the next time Buddies refreshes, such as when you open Buddies or return to WitnessWork while connected. While WitnessWork is open, it tries once more on its own shortly after. To send it sooner, reconnect and tap Try Again on the Plan or Follow-up's page, or open Buddies and pull down to refresh. Your buddy won't see the answer or request until it's sent.
