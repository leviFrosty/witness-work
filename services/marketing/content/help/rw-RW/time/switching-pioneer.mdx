Hindura uko uhagaze muri **Igenamiterere → Umwirondoro → Uko uhagaze**. Intego n'ibiteranyo bihinduka bijyanye.

## Hindura uko uhagaze

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Umwirondoro → Uko uhagaze**.
2. Hitamo icyo gikwiye ubu: Umubwiriza w'Ubwami, Umupayiniya wungirije w'igihe cyose, Umupayiniya w'igihe cyose, Umupayiniya wa bwite, Umugenzuzi usura amatorero, cyangwa Yihariye.

![Igenamiterere ry'umwirondoro rifite menu ya Uko uhagaze](switching-pioneer.webp)

Subira inyuma urangije. Amezi yahise agumana igihe wanditse, bityo amateka yawe agumya kuba nyayo.
