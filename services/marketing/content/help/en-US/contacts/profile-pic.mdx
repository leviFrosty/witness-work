Yes. Open a contact, tap the picture, and choose a photo from your library or take a new one. It helps when contacts share names, or to spot the right door.

## Add a photo

1. Open the contact.
2. Tap the picture.
3. Pick a photo or take a new one.

## Photos on your other devices

- **Cloud Sync:** contact photos and your profile photo sync between your phones on their own, within your account's 2 GB of photo storage. See [Do photos sync with Cloud Sync?](/help/backups/cloud-sync-photos). Replacing or removing a photo frees its space right away.
- **iCloud (Google Drive on Android):** turn on **Include images in sync** in **Settings → Sync**.
- **On the web:** photos don't sync yet. You can't add one there, and contacts show their initials. Editing a contact on the web leaves its photo on your phones as it is.

If a photo is missing on another device, see [Why are contact photos missing on my other device?](/help/backups/sync-photos). Update WitnessWork on that device too.
