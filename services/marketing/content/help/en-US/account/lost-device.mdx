---
id: lostDevice
title: What should I do if my phone or computer is lost or stolen?
category: account
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Anyone who can unlock the device may be able to open WitnessWork on it, so act soon, from another device. Do the same if the bell on Home tells you about a new sign-in, a browser approved, or a passkey added that wasn't you. WitnessWork's server holds only end-to-end encrypted copies.

If you have no other signed-in device, first sign in on a computer or another phone with a passkey or your recovery key.

1. **Sign out the lost device.** On a phone or in a browser that's still signed in, go to Settings → Account → Signed-In Devices and tap the lost phone or browser to sign it out. That ends its connection to your account on WitnessWork's server. A browser you sign out is erased the next time it connects. Anything it hasn't synced is lost. Sign it out before you change your account key: a browser still signed in when the key changes keeps its records. A phone that still has your account on it can sign back in the next time it's used, so don't stop here if someone else may have it.
2. **If it's an Apple device, remove it from your Apple Account first.** Your account key syncs through iCloud Keychain, so a device still signed in there would receive the new key.
3. **Change your account key** in Settings → Account → Change Account Key. It gives your account a new account key and recovery key, signs out your other devices, and asks your buddies to reconnect. The old key then stops working. In a browser, it needs a passkey and a browser signed in for at least a day. See [How do I change my account key?](/help/account/change-account-key).
4. Check Settings → Account → Account Activity for sign-ins and devices you don't recognize, and Settings → Account → Passkeys for a passkey that isn't yours: tap it and choose Remove.

Your recovery key is your account: whoever has it can sign in, and no one at WitnessWork can override that. Keep it somewhere only you can reach. If your recovery key was saved on the lost device, changing your account key replaces it. If you lost the recovery key too, see [What if I lose access?](/help/account/lost-access).
