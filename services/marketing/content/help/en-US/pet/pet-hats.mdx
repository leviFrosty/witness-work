You buy hats in the Shop with coins, which you earn by going out in the ministry. Your pet can wear one hat or none.

## Buy a hat

1. On **Home**, tap your pet's card, but not the pet itself.
2. Tap **Shop**.
3. Pick the **Hat** category.
4. Tap a hat to try it on, then tap **Buy**.

Your pet wears it right away. Hats cost from 30 coins for the Bow up to 960 for the Lion and lamb. Read [How do I earn coins, and what can I buy in the Shop?](/help/pet/pet-shop) to learn how to earn coins.

## Change hats

1. Tap your pet's card.
2. Pick the **Hat** tab.
3. Tap a hat you own, or choose none.

A hat you own stays yours, even after **Change pet**. It says **New** until you pick it. Your pet wears it on Home, in its details, in shared pictures, in the Home Screen widget (iPhone and iPad), and when it visits your buddies. It takes the hat off while it rolls over or lies down.

## Rare hats

The six dearest hats have a blue frame. They move, change the room around your pet, and react when you pat your pet or earn coins.

- Fishbowl (360 coins): fish swim, and one leaps when you earn coins.
- Carousel (480): little animals ride around on your pet's head, and colored lights circle the room after dusk.
- Little fig tree (540): it grows from a sprout as you go out, over 50 days.
- Model railway (600): a little train loops the hill on your pet's head.
- Planetarium (780): stars fill the walls after dusk, with a shooting star now and then.
- Lion and lamb (960): they play when you pat your pet, and butterflies fill the room. After dusk they fall asleep, and a pat wakes them for a little while.

Pick a rare hat in the Shop to watch it react. The **Pat** and **New coins** buttons under its card play each reaction again. Under the Little fig tree, tap **Watch it grow** to see it grow from a sprout to a full tree in a few seconds. Other hats move too, and some have one small moving part. Update WitnessWork if you don't see these hats.

## Good to know

- To see night effects in the daytime, set the window to **Night** in your pet's details.
- With Reduce Motion on, moving hats stay still, and the **Pat**, **New coins** and **Watch it grow** buttons don't show. They also don't move in shared pictures or the widget.
- Some hats are secrets. See [What are the secret hats and room items?](/help/pet/pet-secrets).

## If something goes wrong

- **A hat doesn't move:** check that Reduce Motion is off in your device's settings.
- **You bought a hat on a device that needs updating:** update WitnessWork. Until then, your pet shows without it.
- **Still wrong:** use **Still need help?** below this answer to contact support. Include your WitnessWork version, and leave out names and private notes.
