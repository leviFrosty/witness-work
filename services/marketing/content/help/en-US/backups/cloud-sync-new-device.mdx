id: cloudSyncNewDevice
title: How do I get my records back on a new phone?
category: backups
platforms: [ios, android]
minAppVersion: '1.47.0'
---

From version 1.47.0, tap I already use WitnessWork when setup starts. The Welcome back screen brings your records back from your WitnessWork account through Cloud Sync. If you tap Get started instead and WitnessWork finds your records, it opens Welcome back for you.

- **Your account is already on this phone.** This happens when the phone uses the same Apple Account as your other iPhone or iPad (iCloud Keychain), or the same Google Account as your other Android device. WitnessWork looks for your records in Cloud Sync right away. If it finds them, it downloads them, finishes setup, and shows “Welcome back! You're ready to go.” Your photos download after that.
- **Your account isn't on this phone yet.** Sign in first: tap Sign in with a passkey (your phone asks for the passkey), From your other phone (scan a QR code), or Use your recovery key. Cloud Sync then brings your records over.
- **Other ways to restore.** Your iCloud backup (Google Drive on Android) and a WitnessWork backup file are under Other ways to restore. A device that syncs with iCloud or Google Drive keeps its records there, not in Cloud Sync.
- **You're new.** Tap I'm new here to set up WitnessWork. Nothing is downloaded, and you can turn on Cloud Sync later in Settings → Sync.

What comes back: your service records, contacts, visits, plans, and shared preferences from your other devices on Cloud Sync. Device settings, like your sync option and data protection mode, stay with each device. Your data is end-to-end encrypted, so only your devices can read it, and WitnessWork can't.

If this phone already has records, WitnessWork doesn't download by itself. Tap Restore & keep in sync, then choose Merge (keep them and add your Cloud Sync records) or Replace (use only your Cloud Sync records).

If WitnessWork says it couldn't reach Cloud Sync, check your internet connection and tap Search again. To bring records from MyTime, Notes or paper into a new account instead, set up as new and use the Bring in your records step, or Settings → Import Data later.
