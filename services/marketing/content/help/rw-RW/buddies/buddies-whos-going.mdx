title: "Ni nde ushobora kubona ko nzajya muri Gahunda ya mugenzi?"
---

Buri wese watumiwe muri Gahunda abona izina ryawe munsi ya **Abazajyayo** umaze gusubiza **Ndagenda**, nubwo atari bagenzi bawe. Nta kindi kuri wowe basangizwa.

## Reba abazajya

1. Fungura Gahunda uvuye ku munsi muri **Gahunda**, cyangwa mu nzogera y'amamenyesha y'Ahabanza.
2. Reba munsi ya **Abazajyayo**. Herekana amafoto n'umubare w'abazajya.
3. Kanda kugira ngo ubone urutonde rwose.

Kugira ngo usubize cyangwa uhindure igisubizo cyawe, kanda ikimenyetso cyo kwemeza (**Ndagenda**) cyangwa X (**Sinshobora**) iruhande rwa **Wowe**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Ifoto yawe nayo igaragara, iyo **Ifoto** ifunguye kuri **Bagenzi bose** muri **Igenamiterere rya Buddies**.
- Hagaragara gusa abasubije **Ndagenda**. Utarasubiza, cyangwa udashoboye, abonwa n'uwateguye gusa.
- **Sinshobora** ikuraho izina ryawe ku rutonde kuri bose. Guhitamo **Ndagenda** nanone rirugarura.
- Utumirwa nyuma na we abona urutonde.

## Iyo hari ikitagenze neza

- **Hari umuntu utari mugenzi wawe:** kanda izina rye, hanyuma **Erekana kode yanjye ya Buddies**, umusabe kuyisikana muri kumwe.
- **Hari umuntu ugaragara gusa nka "1 more going":** WitnessWork ye irashaje. Musabe kuyivugurura.
- **Ubona gusa uwateguye:** WitnessWork y'uwateguye ikeneye ivugurura.
