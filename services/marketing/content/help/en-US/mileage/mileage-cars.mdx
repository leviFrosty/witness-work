title: What's the difference between archiving and deleting a car?
category: mileage
---

Archiving hides a car from new trips but keeps its trips in your reports. Deleting removes the car and all its trips, and can't be undone.

## Archive or delete a car

1. Open **Mileage**, then **Mileage Settings** (the gear).
2. Tap the car.
3. Choose **Archive** or **Delete…**.

To use an archived car again, open **Archived Cars** and unarchive it.

## Good to know

- Deleting a car also removes it on every device that syncs with this one.
- A shared car can't be deleted. Archiving it hides it from new trips for everyone on the car. To stop using it yourself, leave it. See [What happens when I leave a shared car, I'm removed, or sharing stops?](/help/mileage/shared-cars-leave).
- Deleting a fuel removes its price history, so cars using it stop showing an estimated cost.
