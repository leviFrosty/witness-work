title: "Konti yanjye ya WitnessWork ni iki?"
---

Ni konti itazwi nyirayo ituma Buddies, Scribe AI, Backup no kwinjira ku bindi bikoresho bikora. Nta imeri, nta nimero ya telefoni, nta ijambobanga.

Telefoni yawe iyikorera ubwa mbere ukoresheje kimwe muri ibyo, cyangwa iyo urebye urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura.

## Aho wayisanga

Fungura **Igenamiterere → Konti**. Uko ihagaze kuhagaragara:

- **Nta murandasi:** iki gikoresho nta murandasi gifite. Byose bikomeza gukora, kandi uko ihagaze kuvugururwa usubiye kuri murandasi.
- **Ntisubiza:** WitnessWork ntiyabonetse. Tegereza iminota mike ukande **Ongera ugerageze**.

## Byiza kumenya

- Amakuru y'umurimo wawe, Aderesi n'inyandiko biguma ku gikoresho cyawe. Niba ufunguye **Backup**, hakorwa kopi ishobora gusomwa na wowe wenyine.
- Buddies ntabwo irebera izina ryawe cyangwa ikindi kintu utasangije.
- Ushobora kandi kwinjira ukoresheje passkey, cyangwa ugakoresha konti yawe muri mushakisha kuri app.witnesswork.app imaze gushyirwaho kuri telefoni yawe. Reba [Passkey ni iki?](/help/account/passkeys-overview).
- Urufunguzo rwawe rwo kugarura ni rwo rukugarura muri konti yawe. Reba [Urufunguzo rwanjye rwo kugarura ni iki, kandi nza kurubika nte?](/help/account/account-recovery-key).
- Niba igikoresho kidashobora kwerekana ko ari porogaramu nyayo, reba [Kuki WitnessWork ivuga ko idashobora kwemeza iki gikoresho?](/help/account/account-verification).
