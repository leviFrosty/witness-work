Open the territory (or one of its streets) and tap **Add Results From Notes**. Paste or type the notes, read them, check what was found, and tap **Add Entries**.

## Add results from notes

1. Type or paste the notes. Put a street's name on its own line, then one house and what happened per line, like "12 NH" or "14 spoke". A range like 12-20 covers each house in between.
2. Tap **Read With Scribe AI**, or **Read Notes** if Scribe AI is off. Scribe AI understands your own words in any language. Otherwise your phone reads common English shorthand: NH or not home, spoke, DNC or do not call. See [Does Scribe AI read my territory notes?](/help/territories/territories-notes-scribe)
3. Check each entry. Fix a house number, change a result, or remove an entry. Tap **Choose Street** for entries without one.
4. Set **When Were These Worked?** Under **Who Worked These?**, choose **Me** or **Someone Else** and type their name.
5. Tap **Add Entries**. Nothing is saved until you do.

## Good to know

- **Handwritten notes:** on iPhone and iPad, tap **Scan Notes** to copy them with the camera. On Android, copy the text from a photo with Google Lens, then paste it.
- Lines that couldn't be read are listed under **Couldn't Read**. Tap **Add as Entry** to fix one by hand.
- If a house was already recorded, its entry says whether saving replaces the result or adds another try.
- Do-not-calls in the notes are added to the territory's list.
- A name you type is kept only with this territory.
- This is hidden on returned territories and while territory privacy mode is on.
