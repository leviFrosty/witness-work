The organizer owns the date, time, title, place and note of a Plan you're invited to, so everyone going sees the same Plan. You can change your own hours, reminder and type.

## Change your part

1. Open the Plan and tap **Edit**.
2. Change **Your Hours**, **Notify Me** or **Type**.

The Plan's page then shows your hours beside the organizer's, for example "Anna planned 3 Hrs". Set your hours back to the organizer's to follow theirs again. Your schedule and progress count your hours.

## Need a different day or time?

- Ask the organizer to change the Plan.
- Or choose **Can't Make It** on the Plan's page and add your own Plan. Deleting a linked Plan is the same as **Can't Make It**.

## If something goes wrong

- **The organizer's change hasn't reached you:** open WitnessWork with an internet connection. Changes arrive within a minute while the app is open.
