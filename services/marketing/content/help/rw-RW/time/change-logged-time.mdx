---

Fungura **Shyiramo igihe**, hitamo umunsi, ukande igihe wanditse ushaka guhindura cyangwa gusiba.

## Hindura igihe cyanditswe

1. Kuri **Ahabanza**, fungura **Shyiramo igihe**. Ushobora kandi gukoresha akabuto ka **+**.
2. Kanda umunsi mu mirongo y'iminsi iri hejuru. Kugira ngo uhitemo undi munsi, kanda itariki iri hejuru yayo.
3. Kanda igihe cyanditswe ku rutonde ruri munsi y'iminsi.
4. Hindura igihe, Ubwoko cyangwa inyandiko. Kugira ngo ukimure, kanda undi munsi.
5. Kanda **Bika**.

Kugira ngo uve ku gihe cyanditswe utabitse, ongera ukande. Niba hari ikintu wahinduye, WitnessWork ibaza mbere yo gutakaza impinduka.

## Siba igihe cyanditswe

1. Fungura **Shyiramo igihe** uhitemo umunsi.
2. Kanda ufate igihe cyanditswe, hanyuma uhitemo **Siba**. Kuri murandasi, kanda iburyo.

Gusiba ntibishobora gusubizwa inyuma.

## Byiza kumenya

- Umunsi ufite ibyanditswe birenga bitatu werekana bibiri bya mbere. Kanda **Erekana byinshi** kugira ngo ubone ibisigaye.
- Kugira ngo wandike igihe ku minsi myinshi, ongeraho icya mbere, hanyuma ukande **Ongeraho ikindi** ku butumwa buyemeza.
- Kwandika igihe kigaragara gusa niba uko uhagaze kwandika amasaha. Ababwiriza b'Ubwami babanza gufungura **Andika amasaha yanjye** muri **Igenamiterere → Umwirondoro**.
- Ushobora kandi guhindura igihe cyanditswe uhereye ku munsi wacyo. Reba [Nandika nte igihe cyangwa ngateganya umunsi runaka?](/help/time/log-time-for-day)
