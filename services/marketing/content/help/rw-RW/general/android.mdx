title: WitnessWork iri kuri Android?
---

Yego. WitnessWork yagarutse kuri Google Play, kandi ni ubuntu. Igihe na raporo, igihe kibarwa, abantu na gusubirayo, gahunda, ikarita (hamwe na Google Maps), ibirometero, ibyibutso, na backup byose bikora kuri Android. Buddies na yo ikora kuri Android, harimo na bagenzi bakoresha iPhone. Abaterankunga bashobora guhuza ibikoresho byabo bya Android bakoresheje Google Drive Sync. Ibiranga bimwe bishingiye kuri serivisi za Apple bigume kuri iPhone na iPad: iCloud Sync, widget, Live Activities, n'amashusho ya porogaramu. Kwinjiza inyandiko ukoresheje Scribe AI biri gutangwa buhoro buhoro kuri Android. Kugira ngo wimukire kuri telefoni nshya ku rubuga urwo ari rwo rwose, kora backup (Igenamiterere → Kubika & Kugarura → Kora Backup), wimure dosiye kuri telefoni nshya, hanyuma uhitemo Kuri dosiye y'ububiko ku rupapuro rwo gutangira rwitwa “Ngaho nimukomeze mujye aho mwasize”. Hagati y'ibikoresho bya Android ushobora kandi guhitamo Kuri Google Drive aho. Bika dosiye za backup mu ibanga; zishobora kuba zirimo amakuru ya ba nyir'inzu.
