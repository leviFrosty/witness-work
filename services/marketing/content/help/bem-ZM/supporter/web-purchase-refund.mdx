title: Bushe kuti napokelela indalama shandi pa fyo nashitile pa web?
---

Kuti mwapapa. Lembeleni email ku [support@witnesswork.app](mailto:support@witnesswork.app) ukufuma ku diresi mwabomfeshe ilyo mwalipila, elyo mulande pa nshita iyo mwalipilileko.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- Tulangalila ico mwapapa conse. Indalama shabwekeshiwa shibwelela ku card mwalipilileko, elyo bank yenu kuti yaisha inshiku imo ukuishimona.
- Tamwingaibwekesha imwe pa cikope ca ukulipila.
- Ukubwekeshiwa indalama kupwisha ico cayakile pa lwakwe. Ukubwekeshiwa indalama sha ukulipila kwa kulekelesha kwa subscription kupwisha ukwingila ko kwa Supporter. Ukubwekeshiwa Tip kufumyapo ukutotelwa kwa Donor.
- Ukukanisha subscription te kubwekeshiwa indalama. Ukwimya ukulipila ukwa ku ntanshi, moneni [Nshi nshila ya kulongolola nelyo ukukanisha ukulipila nashitile pa web?](/help/supporter/web-manage-subscription).
- Ifyashitilwa mu App Store fibwekeshiwa na Apple. Papeni mu akaunti yenu ya App Store.
- Pa fyashitilwa mu Google Play, papeni mu Google Play. Nga Google yamutuma kuli ifwe, lembeleni support email.
