title: How do I see a conversation and its follow-up, or bring a buddy along?
category: contacts
---

Tap a conversation in a contact's Conversation History, the Up Next card on a contact, or a person on Home's conversations card, and it opens in a sheet without leaving where you are. Tapping a follow-up reminder opens it there too. Across the top is who it was with: tap them to open the contact, or use Call, Text, and Navigate. Below, a line runs from what happened ("You talked with…", when, your note, and its fields) to the follow-up, with a Today marker in between, so you can see at a glance whether the return visit is ahead or behind. The follow-up says when you're going back and how far off it is, the topic, who's going, and the reminder. Log Visit appears once its day comes, and Reschedule while it's still open. A missed follow-up turns amber, one you kept links to the visit that kept it, and a dismissed one can be planned again. With no follow-up, the sheet links to your next visit with that person, or offers Plan the next visit after the latest one. Tap the pencil to edit, or ••• to reschedule, copy the note or topic, dismiss the follow-up, or delete the conversation; deleting can't be undone. If you use Buddies, invited buddies and their answers show inside the follow-up. While it's still ahead, the dashed + invites someone right away and shares only its date and time, the householder's first name, the address, and the topic. To take someone off, edit the conversation. Follow-up invitations are unavailable in Data Protection Mode. Opening a conversation changes nothing, and only inviting a buddy shares anything. If something looks wrong, contact support from the Help Center and leave out names, addresses, and notes.
