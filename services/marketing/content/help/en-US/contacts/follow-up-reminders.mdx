---
id: followUpReminders
title: Can I keep visit notes without scheduling a return visit or reminder?
category: contacts
related:
  - 480
---

Yes. When adding or editing a visit, turn off Follow Up and save. The visit and its notes remain, but that visit has no scheduled follow-up or reminder and will not appear as overdue. You can turn Follow Up on later and choose a date and time. To stop an older follow-up reminder, edit that visit and turn Follow Up off.
