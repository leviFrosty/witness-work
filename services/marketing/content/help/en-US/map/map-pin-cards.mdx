Each contact on the map is a round pin with their initials. Its color shows how long it's been since your last visit. A card beside it shows the contact's name and one detail.

## Read the pins

- Tap the ⓘ button on the map to see what each color means. To change the colors, open **Settings → Personalization → Color Key**.
- A small mark on the corner shows an overdue follow-up (red clock), a follow-up today (green clock), or a favorite (star).
- Pins close together gather into one square pin with a count. Tap it. Contacts at the same address, such as an apartment building, open in a list. Tap a name to select that contact. Contacts that are only near each other zoom the map in.
- The chip at the top counts contacts in view who are overdue or due in the next seven days. Tap it to keep only them bright, and tap it again to see everyone.

## Choose what cards show

1. Tap the layers button on the map.
2. Choose **Pin cards show**.
3. Pick **Next visit**, **Last visit**, **Follow-up topic**, **Name only**, or one of your contact fields.

You can also use **Settings → Features → Map**.

## Good to know

- By default a card shows the next visit: an overdue follow-up, an upcoming follow-up, or when you last visited.
- Cards that would cover another pin wait until you zoom in.
- The selected contact's card turns green.
- A card shows only the name when the contact has no topic or no value in the field you chose.
- Nothing is sent anywhere to draw pins and cards.

## If something goes wrong

- **A contact is missing:** see [Why are some contacts missing from the map?](/help/map/map-filters).
- **A card shows the wrong detail:** check the contact's follow-up or field value.
- **Anything else:** use the Help Center's private support option, and keep names and addresses out of public reports.
