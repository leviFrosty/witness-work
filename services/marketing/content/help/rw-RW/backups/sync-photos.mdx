id: syncPhotos
title: Kuki amafoto y'abantu abura ku kindi gikoresho cyanjye?
---

Guhuza amafoto ni ikiranga cy'ubushake cy'Umuterankunga kuri iOS na Android. Fungura Igenamiterere → iCloud Sync (Google Drive Sync kuri Android) ufungure Shyiramo amafoto mu guhuza. Ibi byohereza amafoto y'umwirondoro n'ay'abantu mu bubiko bwawe bwite bwa iCloud (cyangwa mu bubiko buhishwe bwa WitnessWork muri Google Drive) kandi bigakurura amafoto yifashishwa; abarwa mu bubiko bwawe bwa iCloud cyangwa Google. Vugurura WitnessWork ku bikoresho byose kugira ngo ukoreshe ivugurura ry'amafoto neza. Guhagarika guhuza amafoto ku gikoresho kimwe bigumana dosiye zoherejwe na kopi zo ku gikoresho. Kugira ngo ukureho amafoto yoherejwe, hitamo Kuraho amafoto ya iCloud kandi banza uhagarike guhuza amafoto ku bindi bikoresho, bitabaye ibyo bishobora kongera kohereza kopi zabyo. Gukuraho umuntu cyangwa ifoto ye bikuraho kopi zakuruwe iyo iyo mpinduka ihagereye; kopi zishaje zo mu ikusanyirizo zisukurwa nyuma y'igihe cy'umunsi umwe cyo gutegereza. Niba ifoto ibuze, komeza igikoresho gifite iy'umwimerere, reba ububiko n'uburenganzira, hanyuma ukoreshe Huza ubu. Koresha uburyo bw'ubufasha bwihariye bwo mu Kigo cy'ubufasha niba bikomeje kunanirwa; ntushyiremo amakuru ya ba nyir'inzu muri raporo zishyirwa ahagaragara.
