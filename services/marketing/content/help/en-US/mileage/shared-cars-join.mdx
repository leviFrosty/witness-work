When a buddy shares a car with you, you get a notification. The invitation also waits in the bell on Home, with **Join** and **Decline**.

## Join or decline

1. On Home, tap the bell.
2. Find the invitation, such as "Anna shared Family Minivan with you."
3. Tap **Join** or **Decline**.

**Join** adds the car to Mileage with its whole history, including trips from before you joined. Your own cars stay your own. The person who invited you is told you joined.

## Good to know

- Everyone on the car sees all its trips, who logged them, and their notes. People who aren't your buddies see you by the name and photo you share.
- An invitation stays open for 7 days.
- You need Buddies to get an invitation, because only a buddy can invite you. See [What is WitnessWork Buddies, and why don't I see it?](/help/buddies/buddies-overview).

## If something goes wrong

- **The invitation expired or disappeared:** ask someone on the car to invite you again.
- **"This car already has the most people it can.":** a car holds 10 people, counting open invitations. Someone on the car needs to cancel an open invitation, or the owner needs to remove someone.
