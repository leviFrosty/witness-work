title: Mulandu nshi icikope cibulile mu lilembo, nelyo ilembo lyaba lya fye pa cibombelo cimbi?
---

Ilembo lyalanda ukuti **This photo isn't on this device** nga icikope tacilafika pa cibombelo ico.

## Ukulete icikope

1. Isuleni WitnessWork pa cibombelo cabikilepo icikope.
2. Yeni ku **Ifyo Mwasala → Sync** elyo mutinike **Lingana nomba**.
3. Lolelesheni ukufika ilyo ifibombelo fyenu fimbi fyalunganikanya.

## Ifyo mwafwile ukwishiba

- **Cloud Sync** itumina ifikope yonse. Nga icibombelo ca ntanshi cilanda ukuti **Not synced: storage full**, apali ifikope pa akaunti yenu pa isula. Moneni [Bushe ifikope fisync na Cloud Sync?](/help/backups/cloud-sync-photos).
- **iCloud Sync** (Google Drive Sync pa Android): washeni **Ingiya ifikope mu sync** pa fibombelo fyonse fibili.
- Ukubwesha ifailo ya backup tabwesha ifikope.

## Nga cabipa

- **Ilembo lyaba lya fye pa cibombelo cimbi:** icibombelo ico cikwata WitnessWork ya kale, elyo cilanga amalembo ukwabula ukulungika nelyo ifikope. Ukulungika ilembo kulya kufumyapo ukulungika kwalyo pa fibombelo fyonse. Kushani WitnessWork pa cibombelo conse.
