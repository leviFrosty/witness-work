title: How do I switch between Publisher and Auxiliary Pioneer mid-month?
category: time
pinned: true
related:
  - 287
  - 302
---

Open Settings → Profile → Status and choose what fits right now: Kingdom Publisher, Regular Auxiliary, Regular Pioneer, Special Pioneer, Circuit Overseer, or Custom. Goals and totals adjust to match. Switch back when you're done. Past months keep the time you entered, so your history stays accurate.
