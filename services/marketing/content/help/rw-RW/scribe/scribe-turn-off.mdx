title: "Nshobora kuzimya Scribe AI?"
---

Yego. Yizimye maze Scribe AI ikavanwa muri porogaramu, kandi nta kintu kiyoherezwa.

## Yizimye

1. Fungura **Igenamiterere → Scribe AI**.
2. Zimya **Koresha Scribe AI**.

Kugira ngo uyifungure bundi bushya, koresha akabuto kamwe.

## Byiza kumenya

- Mikoro mu Ibikorwa byihuse na **Vuga ibigezweho** muri menu ya **+** birahishwa. Kuri murandasi, akabuto ko kuganira na **Andika ibigezweho** birahishwa.
- Kuyizimya ntibisiba ibiganiro byawe cyangwa Kwibuka kwa Scribe AI. Kugira ngo ubanze usibe Kwibuka, koresha **Igenamiterere → Scribe AI → Kwibuka**.
- Akabuto gakurikira ku bindi bikoresho byawe iyo guhuza bifunguye.

## Ubwa mbere uyikoresheje

Scribe AI isobanura icyo ikora ikagusaba kwemera [Amabwiriza y'imikoreshereze](/terms) na [Politiki y'ibanga](/privacy) bya WitnessWork. Niba utiteguye, kurura urupapuro. Scribe AI igumya iboneka kandi ikongera kubaza ubutaha. Kugira ngo usome nyuma uko ikoresha amakuru yawe, fungura **Igenamiterere → Scribe AI → Nta kubika amakuru** ukande **Menya byinshi**.
