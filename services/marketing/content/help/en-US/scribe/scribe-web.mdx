id: scribeWeb
title: Can I use Scribe AI on the web?
category: scribe
minAppVersion: "1.47.0"
---

Yes, by typing. Sign in to WitnessWork on the web, then tap the chat button in Quick Actions on Home, or tap + and choose Type an update. Type what you did or plan to do, like “Two hours this morning, and I talked with John at 42 Oak St, follow up next Tuesday,” and tap Send. Scribe AI answers as it writes and shows what it understood as cards: time, plans, and contacts with their visits and follow-ups. Uncheck anything you don’t want and tap Add. Nothing is added until you do, and Undo works the same as on your phones.

Scribe AI can do the same things on the web as on your phone (see [What can Scribe AI do?](/help/scribe/scribe-what-it-does)), with the same limits (see [How do Scribe AI limits work?](/help/scribe/scribe-usage)).

What isn’t on the web: speaking to Scribe AI, and scanning notes or reading photos of paper pages. Their buttons are hidden there, and Scribe AI always opens with the keyboard. Use the WitnessWork app on your phone for those.

The first time you use Scribe AI in a browser, it says what Scribe AI does and how it handles your data, and asks you to agree to WitnessWork’s [Terms of Use](/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy). Your conversations and Scribe AI’s memory are saved only in that browser. They aren’t synced or backed up, and signing out of the browser erases them. What you type is handled the same way as on your phone: see [What does Scribe AI send, and what’s kept?](/help/scribe/scribe-privacy)

Scribe AI needs a connection. When you’re offline, it says so, and you can still add time, contacts and plans yourself; see [Does Scribe AI work offline?](/help/scribe/scribe-offline) If it says to update WitnessWork, reload the page to get the latest version. If it says you’ve reached your limit, it tells you when you can use it again. If you’re still stuck, email support from Still need help? in the Help Center, and describe what happened without sharing personal details.
