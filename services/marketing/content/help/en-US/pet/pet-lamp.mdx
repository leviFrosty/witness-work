id: petLamp
title: What does the lamp in the room mean?
category: pet
---

The lamp shows how your pet is doing. It burns one level brighter for each whole heart.

- With less than one heart, it's unlit. A sleeping pet is at half a heart.
- At five hearts, it's at its brightest.
- If your pet died, it goes out.

## Good to know

- It doesn't depend on your Service Streak or the light you picked in the **Light** tab.
- See [How do my pet's hearts work?](/help/pet/pet-hearts).
